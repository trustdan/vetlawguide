# Playbook 19: Extraschedular Referrals (`§ 3.321(b)(1)`), Analogous Code Selection (`§ 4.20` / *Webb*) & Temporary `100%` Hospital/Convalescent Audits (`§§ 4.28–4.30`)

When standard VASRD body-system tables appear to trap a severely impaired veteran at a low percentage—or when inpatient admissions, surgeries, or cast immobilizations punctuate the medical timeline—three cross-cutting `Part 3` / `Part 4 Subpart A` mechanisms unlock higher evaluations and retroactive back pay: **(Track A)** `38 C.F.R. § 3.321(b)(1)` extraschedular referrals, **(Track B)** `38 C.F.R. § 4.20` analogous code re-engineering under *Webb v. McDonough*, and **(Track C)** `38 C.F.R. §§ 4.28–4.30` temporary `100%` awards paired with automatic *Bradley* `SMC(s)`.

## 1. Design-by-Contract Preconditions & Invariants (`kill_switches`)

### A. Preconditions (VBMS / C-File Form, Clinical & Code-Sheet Triggers)

Screen the VBMS eFolder, Rating Code Sheets ([VA Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md)), and VistA/CAPRI medical files for these exact triggers:
1. **Forms & Filings:** `VA Form 21-526EZ` (post-Mar. 24, 2015 claim for increase / temporary total), `VA Form 21-0966` (Intent to File within 1 year of hospital admission or surgery), `VA Form 20-0995` / `20-0996` / `10182` (AMA review lanes), `VA Form 21-8940` (TDIU pivot), and legacy `VA Form 10-7131` / `10-564-D` VAMC hospital exchange slips (`< Mar. 24, 2015`).
2. **Code-Sheet Notation Triggers:**
   * **Hyphenated `xx99-xxxx` Analogous Codes (`§ 4.27`):** E.g., `7599-7522`, `8599-8520`, `5299-5257`, `7399-7346`, or `6899-6845` capped at `0%`, `10%`, or `20%`.
   * **`Paragraph 28` / `Paragraph 29` / `Paragraph 30` Entries:** Internal code-sheet shorthand for `§§ 4.28, 4.29, and 4.30` awards—audit start dates, end dates, absence of `SMC (S-1)`, and post-expiration percentage drops!
3. **Clinical Record Triggers:**
   * Prescription of **wheelchair, walker, crutches, cane, AFO brace**, frequent falls, or dizziness/syncope during orthopedic/hearing/neurological claims where the assigned DC evaluates only goniometric range of motion or audiometric thresholds (*Spellers v. Wilkie*).
   * **VAMC or VA-approved inpatient discharge summaries exceeding `21` continuous days** for an SC physical or psychiatric condition (`§ 4.29`).
   * **Operative reports** (inpatient or outpatient ambulatory surgery) or **orthopedic cast-application notes** immobilizing at least one major joint (`shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle`) for an SC disability (`§ 4.30`).

## 2. Track A — Extraschedular Referrals (`38 C.F.R. § 3.321(b)(1)`) Litigation Recipe

### Step 0: Exhaust All Schedular Maximization Tools First (*Morgan v. Wilkie*)

Under ***Morgan v. Wilkie*, `31 Vet. App. 162, 166–68 (2019)`**, extraschedular referral under `§ 3.321(b)(1)` is an exceptional safety valve triggered *only* after VA has exhausted every standard schedular mechanism to compensate the veteran's actual symptoms:
1. Check **separate non-pyramiding Diagnostic Codes (`§ 4.14` / *Esteban*)** (e.g., rating neurological radiculopathy, gait instability `DC 5257`, flexion `DC 5260`, extension `DC 5261`, or painful scars `DC 7804` separately).
2. Check **unmedicated baseline functional impairment (`§ 4.10` / *Ingram v. Collins*, `38 Vet. App. 130 (2025)`)** (and screen out any decision tainted by VA's rescinded 10-day Feb. 17–27, 2026 IFR, `91 FR 7118`, rescinded at `91 FR 9712`).
3. Check **`§ 4.7` higher-of-two-evaluations** and **`§ 4.20` higher-ceiling analogous codes** (Track B below).
4. Plead **schedular maximization as Primary Relief** and **`§ 3.321(b)(1)` referral in the Alternative**!

### Step 1: Execute the Three-Step *Thun* Referral Framework (*Thun*, *Spellers*, *Anderson*)

Under ***Thun v. Peake*, `22 Vet. App. 111, 115–16 (2008)`, *aff'd sub nom. Thun v. Shinseki*, `572 F.3d 1366 (Fed. Cir. 2009)`** and [`VAOPGCPREC 6-96`](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md), neither the RO nor the Board has jurisdiction to assign an initial extraschedular rating in the first instance; instead, they must refer the claim to the **Director, Compensation Service** whenever Steps 1 and 2 are met:

* ***Thun* Step 1 (Schedular Inadequacy Comparison — *Spellers v. Wilkie*, `30 Vet. App. 211 (2018)`):**
  * Compare the **severity and type** of the veteran's actual SC symptoms against the specific criteria of all applicable Diagnostic Codes.
  * **Winning CAVC Remand Pattern (*Spellers*):** In *Spellers*, `30 Vet. App. at 216–18`, the Board denied `§ 3.321(b)(1)` referral for a back/hip disability rated on range of motion despite record evidence that the veteran required a **cane/walker, could not walk more than one block, and had difficulty standing/sitting**. The CAVC vacated: goniometric ROM tables measure *joint excursion*, **not** reliance on assistive ambulation devices or inability to sit/stand! Always isolate functional impacts *wholly absent* from the DC text (e.g., wheelchair/cane reliance prior to diagnostic codes expressly adding assistive devices, frequent syncope, incontinence, medication sedation, or episodic bedrest in non-incapacitating-episode codes).
* ***Thun* Step 2 (Exceptional Factors — Disjunctive `"OR"` Test!):**
  * Show that this unusual disability picture exhibits governing norms such as **(a) marked interference with employment** (missed workdays, demotion, inability to perform physical/cognitive tasks, workplace accommodations—*even if the veteran is still employed and NOT totally unemployable!*) **OR (b) frequent periods of hospitalization**.
* ***Thun* Step 3 (Director Award & De Novo Board Review — *Anderson v. Shinseki*, `22 Vet. App. 423 (2009)`):**
  * Once Steps 1 and 2 are satisfied (or reasonably raised without adequate Board reasons or bases), the RO/Board **must refer** the file to the Director, Compensation Service (`§ 3.321(b)(1)`).
  * **Crucial Post-Referral Appellate Rule (*Anderson*, `22 Vet. App. at 427–29`; *cf. Wages v. McDonald*, `27 Vet. App. 233, 238–39 (2015)` on `§ 4.16(b)`):** While the Board cannot *bypass* the Director initially, **once the Director issues a decision denying extraschedular relief (or assigning an inadequate percentage), the Board reviews the Director's decision *de novo***—owing zero deference to the Director and possessing full authority to award or increase the extraschedular rating itself!

### Step 2: Navigate the `January 8, 2018` (`82 FR 57830`) Collective-Impact Split

| Adjudication Era / Posture | Controlling Law | Litigation Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pre-`January 8, 2018` Period** *(e.g., Legacy Board appeal or CAVC remand covering pre-Jan. 8, 2018 staged periods where raised)* | ***Johnson v. McDonald*, `762 F.3d 1362 (Fed. Cir. 2014)`**; ***Yancy v. McDonald*, `27 Vet. App. 484 (2016)`** | Former `§ 3.321(b)(1)` used the plural *"service-connected disability or disabilities"*, requiring collective extraschedular consideration when the synergistic effect of multiple SC conditions was argued or reasonably raised (*Yancy*) for staged periods prior to Jan. 8, 2018. |
| **On or After `January 8, 2018`** *(`82 FR 57830`, pub. Dec. 8, 2017, **eff. Jan. 8, 2018**)* | ***Thurlow v. Wilkie*, `30 Vet. App. 231 (2018)`**; ***NOVA v. Sec'y*, `927 F.3d 1263 (Fed. Cir. 2019)`** | Amended `§ 3.321(b)(1)` restricts extraschedular evaluations strictly to **"an individual"** single SC disability (`Thurlow` held applying the amended rule to claims pending on/after Jan. 8, 2018 is lawful for post-amendment periods).<br/>**MANDATORY PIVOT:** Whenever multiple `<60%/<70%` SC disabilities *collectively* destroy employability post-Jan. 8, 2018, pivot immediately to **`38 C.F.R. § 4.16(b)` Extraschedular TDIU** (`VA Form 21-8940` + vocational expert report — see [`tdiu-digestive-respiratory-diffs.md`](../cfr-diffs/part-4-policy/tdiu-digestive-respiratory-diffs.md)), which *expressly* aggregates all SC disabilities! |

## 3. Track B — `38 C.F.R. § 4.20` Analogous Code Selection & *Webb v. McDonough* Playbook

Unlisted conditions (peripheral dysautonomia/POTS, rare autoimmune/connective-tissue disorders, post-surgical visceral syndromes, pelvic floor dysfunction, complex regional pain) are frequently parked by raters under a dead-end analogous code (`xx99-xxxx` under `§ 4.27`) that caps out at `10%` or `20%`.

### Four-Step Analogous Re-Engineering Protocol:

1. **Decode the Hyphenated Code Sheet Entry (`§ 4.27`):** Pull the internal Rating Code Sheet ([VA Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md)). Locate every `xx99-xxxx` code and check the **maximum schedular ceiling** of the second 4-digit code.
2. **Build a Comparative *Lendenmann* Matrix (`3 Vet. App. 345, 350–51 (1992)`):** Identify an alternative VASRD Diagnostic Code with `30%`, `50%`, `60%`, or `100%` tiers and map all three mandatory factors side-by-side in your brief/IMO:
   * **Factor 1 — Functions Affected:** Does the candidate DC measure the exact physiological/occupational function impaired (e.g., orthostatic syncope/cardiac output vs. peripheral nerve paralysis vs. voiding frequency)?
   * **Factor 2 — Anatomical Localization:** Does the candidate DC sit in the same organ system or anatomical region?
   * **Factor 3 — Symptomatology:** Do the candidate DC's symptom descriptors match the veteran's actual clinical manifestations?
   * *(Appellate Hook: Whenever a claimant proposes a plausible alternative analogous DC or the record shows the assigned DC poorly matches one of the three factors, the Board commits reversible `§ 7104(d)(1)` error if it fails to compare both codes across all three *Lendenmann* factors!)*
3. **Defeat Literal Prerequisite Denials Under *Webb v. McDonough* (`71 F.4th 1377 (Fed. Cir. 2023)`):**
   * **The Trap:** VA agrees to rate an unlisted disorder by analogy to `DC XXXX`, but then denies the `20%`, `30%`, or `60%` tier because the veteran lacks an **organic/anatomical hallmark unique to the named disease** (just as VA denied `20%` under `7599-7522` in *Webb* because medication-induced erectile dysfunction lacked physical *"penile deformity"*, or denies `DC 7206` GERD analogy because non-GERD esophageal dysmotility lacks acid-induced `"esophageal stricture"`).
   * **The *Webb* Strike:** Cite *Webb*, `71 F.4th at 1381–83`: under `38 C.F.R. § 4.20` (and `§§ 4.7, 4.21`), an unlisted disability is rated by analogy *precisely because it is not the listed condition*. Requiring literal satisfaction of a disease-specific structural prerequisite renders `§ 4.20` a nullity; once `DC XXXX` is selected as closely related, VA must evaluate whether the unlisted disorder's **functional impairment and symptomatology** match (or `§ 4.7` more nearly approximate) the compensable tier!

## 4. Track C — Temporary `100%` Hospitalization (`§ 4.29`) & Convalescence (`§ 4.30`) Audit Engine

Thousands of dollars in tax-free `100%` + `SMC(s)` back pay go unclaimed every year because veterans undergo VAMC or private surgeries, joint replacements, cast immobilizations, or `22+`-day inpatient psychiatric/medical admissions for already-service-connected conditions without filing a timely `§§ 4.29/4.30` audit.

### Step 1: Verify Objective Substantive Triggers (`§§ 4.28, 4.29, 4.30` & *Hudgens*)

1. **Inpatient Admission Exceeding `21` Continuous Days (`38 C.F.R. § 4.29`):**
   * Count exact admission-to-discharge calendar days in a **VA medical center, VA domiciliary hospital ward, or VA-approved/contracted hospital** (`22` days minimum!).
   * Verify from the admission note and discharge summary that **active treatment or observation of a service-connected disability** (e.g., inpatient PTSD residential trauma program, heart failure admission, osteomyelitis/cellulitis IV antibiotics) began on day 1 (or identify the exact later hospital day SC treatment was instituted and continued `>21` days following an NSC admission under `§ 4.29(b)`).
   * **Payment Span:** `100%` runs from the **first day of continuous SC hospitalization** through the **last day of the month of hospital discharge (regular discharge or release to non-bed care) or the last day of the month of termination of SC treatment/observation** (`§ 4.29(a)`), plus optional `1–3` months post-discharge convalescence (`§ 4.29(e)`) and up to `3` further months upon VSCM approval (`§ 4.29(f)`).
2. **Post-Surgical or Non-Surgical Cast Convalescence (`38 C.F.R. § 4.30`):**
   * Unlike `§ 4.29`, **`§ 4.30` covers surgeries or casts performed in ANY licensed private OR VA hospital or outpatient ambulatory surgical center** (`<21` days or same-day outpatient!) for an SC disability, provided **any ONE** of three triggers is met:
     1. **`§ 4.30(a)(1)`:** Surgery necessitating **at least `1 month` (`30+` days) of convalescence** (documented by surgeon return-to-work / post-op restriction notes: rotator cuff repair, spinal fusion/laminectomy, meniscectomy/ACL reconstruction, hernia/bowel resection, radical prostatectomy);
     2. **`§ 4.30(a)(2)`:** Surgery resulting in **severe postoperative residuals** (incompletely healed surgical wounds/drains, therapeutic immobilizing casts/splints, required crutches/wheelchair preventing navigation, or strict therapeutic confinement to the home); **OR**
     3. **`§ 4.30(a)(3)`:** **Immobilization by cast, without surgery, of one or more major joints** (`shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle` — note that rigid prescribed walking boots/splints that functionally immobilize a major joint like a cast should be briefed with physician certification of cast-equivalent immobilization!).
   * **Payment Span & Extensions:** `100%` begins on the **exact date of hospital admission or outpatient surgery/cast** and continues for **`1, 2, or 3 months` from the first day of the month following hospital discharge or outpatient release** (`§ 4.30(a)`), plus **`1–3` additional months** (`up to 6 mos`) upon medical proof of ongoing convalescence (`§ 4.30(b)(1)`).
   * **Prosthetic Joint Replacement Check (*Hudgens v. McDonald*, `823 F.3d 630 (Fed. Cir. 2016)` & `85 FR 76453`):** Always check whether surgery implanted a hip/knee/shoulder/ankle prosthesis (`DC 5051–5056` — [`orthopedic-spine-joints.md`](../cfr-diffs/vasrd/orthopedic-spine-joints.md)):
     * Before **February 7, 2021** (`85 FR 76453`), `DC 5054/5055` mandated **`1 full year` (`12–13 months`) at `100%`**—and under *Hudgens*, **partial knee replacements** also unlocked `DC 5055` for claims filed/pending prior to July 16, 2015 (`80 FR 42040`)!
     * On or after **February 7, 2021**, total joint replacements under `DC 5054/5055` receive **`4 months` at `100%`** following surgery (extendable via `§ 4.30(b)` if severe residuals persist!), while partial replacements/resurfacing procedures qualify directly under **`§ 4.30`**.

### Step 2: Lock In the Effective Date (`§ 3.400(o)(2)` vs. Legacy `§ 3.157(b)`)

* **Modern Rule (Treatment On or After `March 24, 2015`, `79 FR 57660`):**
  * Because VA repealed `former 38 C.F.R. § 3.157(b)` effective March 24, 2015, even a surgery performed *inside a VAMC* **does NOT automatically file a claim**!
  * Under **`38 U.S.C. § 5110(b)(2)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.400(o)(2)`**, the veteran **MUST submit an Intent to File (`VA Form 21-0966`) or formal claim (`VA Form 21-526EZ` / `20-0995`) within `1 year` of the date of hospital admission or surgery** to capture the day-1 retroactive `100%` effective date! *(Practice Tip: If a client had a 3-month convalescence starting `11` months ago, file an immediate `21-0966` TODAY before the 1-year `§ 3.400(o)(2)` window closes!)*
* **Legacy Goldmine (VAMC Admission/Surgery Before `March 24, 2015` — `Former 38 C.F.R. § 3.157(b)`):**
  * For any **VA hospital admission or VA surgical report** prior to March 24, 2015 for an **already-service-connected disability** (even `0%` SC!), `former § 3.157(b)` (`26 FR 1571`) treated the VAMC medical report itself as a **mandatory informal claim for increase/temporary total benefits on the date of admission** ([Playbook 04](04-unadjudicated-tdiu-informal-claims-smc.md); [`38-cfr-3.155-3.157b-informal-claims-pre-2015.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.155-3.157b-informal-claims-pre-2015.md))! If VA never adjudicated `§ 4.29`/`§ 4.30` on that pre-2015 VAMC surgery/admission, demand immediate retroactive payment via **pending unadjudicated claim** or **RO CUE (`§ 3.105(a)`)**!

## 5. Postconditions: Automatic *Bradley* `SMC(s)` & Void Post-Convalescent Reductions

Never close a `§§ 4.28–4.30` (or `DC 5054/5055/7528`) audit without running both postcondition checks:

### Postcondition 1: Mandatory Temporary Statutory `SMC(s)` (*Bradley v. Peake*, `22 Vet. App. 280 (2008)`)

* **The Rule (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(s)(1)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(i)(1)`):** Statutory Housebound `SMC(s)` (`$4,408.53/mo` vs. `$3,938.58/mo` in 2026 = **`+$469.95/month` net gain**) does **NOT** require that the `100%` disability be permanent! Whenever a veteran receives a temporary `100%` evaluation for **one single SC disability** under `§ 4.28`, `§ 4.29`, `§ 4.30`, `DC 5054/5055`, or active cancer (`DC 7528`), check the Rating Code Sheet for the veteran's *other* permanent SC disabilities ([Master SMC Treatise & Step Math](../docs/smc-complete-treatise-and-step-math.md); [`SMC & Ancillary Audit Worksheet`](../checklists/smc-and-ancillary-audit-worksheet.md)).
* **Automatic Trigger (`cue-and-open-claim`):** If those separate, independent permanent SC disabilities combine to **`60%` or more** under `§ 4.25` (including any `§ 4.26` bilateral factor on paired non-surgical extremities), **VA is statutorily required to pay `SMC(s)` for every single month the temporary `100%` evaluation is in effect** (*Bradley*, `22 Vet. App. at 293–94`)! Raters routinely pay `100%` under `Paragraph 29/30` while forgetting to code `SMC (S-1)`—creating an effortless, pure-math **RO CUE (`§ 3.105(a)`)** on any final award!

### Postcondition 2: The Post-Convalescent Unlawful Reduction Trap (`38 C.F.R. § 3.105(e)` & *Foster*)

Audit the exact rating percentage VA assigned **the day after** the `§ 4.29` or `§ 4.30` temporary `100%` period expired:
1. **Lawful Expiration to Pre-Surgical Baseline (Exempt from `§ 3.105(e)`):** Suppose a veteran held a running **`20%`** right shoulder evaluation, underwent rotator cuff surgery, received `100%` under `§ 4.30` for `3` months, and automatically reverted to **`20%`** on month `4`. Under the concluding text of `§§ 4.29` and `4.30`, reverting to the pre-existing `20%` baseline at the end of a fixed temporary period is **exempt** from `38 C.F.R. § 3.105(e)` 60-day advance notice (though the veteran can still appeal for a `30%+` post-surgical residual rating!).
2. **VOID Reduction *Below* Pre-Surgical Running Baseline (`§ 3.105(e)` & `§ 3.344` Violated!):** Suppose that same veteran held a running **`30%`** knee or **`40%`** lumbar spine rating before surgery (especially if in effect `5+` years under `§ 3.344(c)`!), received `3` months of `§ 4.30` `100%` convalescence, and at month `4` VA dropped the rating from `100%` straight down to **`10%`** (*below* the `30%/40%` pre-surgical running award!) **without** issuing a `§ 3.105(e)` proposal-to-reduce letter, affording 60 days to submit evidence, and waiting 60 days post-final notice (*or* discontinued a `DC 7528` post-treatment cancer `100%` without the mandatory 6-month C&P exam and `§ 3.105(e)` 60-day notice enforced in ***Foster v. McDonough*, `34 Vet. App. 338, 346–49 (2021)`**, noting *Foster* distinguishes `§ 3.105(e)` notice compliance from `§§ 3.343/3.344` stabilized-rating improvement rules):
   * **Remedy ([Playbook 05](05-void-rating-reductions-stabilized-ratings.md)):** That cut below the pre-surgical baseline is **void *ab initio***! Demand immediate **outright reinstatement** of the `30%/40%` baseline (or `100%` under *Foster* until lawful `§ 3.105(e)` effective date) retroactive to the unlawful reduction date—**with zero CUE burden**!
