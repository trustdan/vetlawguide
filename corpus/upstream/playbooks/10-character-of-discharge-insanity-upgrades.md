# Playbook 10: Character of Discharge (`§ 3.12` Overhaul), Conditional Discharges (`§ 3.13`), Insanity (`§ 3.354(b)`) & DRB/BCMR Upgrades

Threshold **"veteran" status** under `38 U.S.C. § 101(2)` requires active military, naval, air, or space service terminated *"under conditions other than dishonorable"* (*Holmes v. Brown*, `10 Vet. App. 38, 40 (1997)`; *D'Amico v. West*, `209 F.3d 1322 (Fed. Cir. 2000)`; ***Newman v. McDonough*, `35 Vet. App. 310 (2022)`**—abrogating the pre-VCAA *Aguilar*/*Holmes* preponderance hurdle and holding that **`38 U.S.C. § 5107(b)` benefit-of-the-doubt equipoise** governs threshold veteran-status and COD/insanity determinations; see [Three-Era Title 38 Statutory Crosswalk](../docs/title-38-statutory-crosswalk.md)). Veterans separated with an **Other Than Honorable (`OTH`)** (historically *"Undesirable"*), **Bad Conduct (`BCD`)**, **Dishonorable (`DD`)**, or **Uncharacterized** discharge are routinely turned away at intake—yet **five distinct statutory and regulatory pathways** allow counsel to establish full compensation eligibility and, in four of those five pathways, capture **multi-decade retroactive effective dates** pre-dating VA's **June 25, 2024** Character of Discharge (`COD`) overhaul (`89 FR 32361`).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Paragraph Redesignation Alert (`89 FR 32361`, pub. Apr. 26, 2024, eff. June 25, 2024):** VA's 2024 Final Rule inserted a new **`38 C.F.R. § 3.12(e)` (*Compelling circumstances exception*)** and **redesignated former paragraphs `(e)–(k)` as `(f)–(l)`** (`§ 3.12(f)` `[former (e)]` BCMR upgrades; `§ 3.12(g)` `[former (f)]` pre-Oct. 8, 1977 DRB upgrades; `§ 3.12(h)` `[former (g)]` post-Oct. 8, 1977 DRB upgrades; `§ 3.12(i)` `[former (h)]` special review programs; `§ 3.12(l)` `[former (k)]` uncharacterized separations). Always cite **both** current and `[former ...]` paragraph letters when briefing cross-era claims. For verbatim pre- and post-June 25, 2024 regulatory text and structural diffs, see [`38-cfr-3.12-3.13-3.354-cod-insanity.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.12-3.13-3.354-cod-insanity.md) and [`character-of-discharge-3.12-evolution.md`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/character-of-discharge-3.12-evolution.md). Run the [`COD & Guard/Reserve Intake Audit`](../checklists/cod-and-guard-reserve-intake-audit.md) at initial screening, and pair Guard/Reserve duty-status issues with [Playbook 11](11-guard-reserve-adt-iadt-and-line-of-duty.md).

## 1. Preconditions: `DD-214`, OMPF & VBMS Administrative Triggers

Before accepting a prior VA COD denial at face value, obtain the complete **Official Military Personnel File (`OMPF`)** (enlistment contracts `DD Form 4`, separation packet, court-martial promulgating orders, Article 15s) and VBMS **Administrative Decision** ([VA Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md)):

* **`DD Form 214` Block-by-Block Audit (`1979–Present` Format; Equivalent Boxes on Older `DD-214` / `NAVPERS 553`):**
  * **Block `12` (`Record of Service`) & Block `18` (`Remarks`):** Look for total prior active service exceeding the final enlistment contract, or remarks stating ***"Immediate Reenlistment,"*** ***"Continuous Honorable Active Service From [Date] to [Date],"*** or ***"SOLDIER HAS COMPLETED FIRST FULL TERM OF SERVICE."*** A single consolidated `DD-214` masking an earlier honorable reenlistment is the **#1 CUE goldmine in COD law (`38 C.F.R. § 3.13`)**!
  * **Block `24` (`Character of Service`):** `Honorable` or `Under Honorable Conditions (General)` binds VA favorably (`§ 3.12(a)`), *unless* issued via a post-Oct. 8, 1977 DRB upgrade where a `§ 3.12(c)` statutory bar remains (`§ 3.12(h)`). `Under Other Than Honorable Conditions`, `Undesirable`, `Bad Conduct`, `Dishonorable`, or `Uncharacterized` (`§ 3.12(l)`) triggers a formal VA COD determination.
  * **Blocks `25` (`Separation Authority`), `26` (`Separation Code / SPD`), `28` (`Narrative Reason`) & `29` (`Dates of Time Lost Under 10 U.S.C. § 972`):**
    * Distinguish **Special Court-Martial (`SPCM`) BCD** from **General Court-Martial (`GCM`) BCD/DD** via the Court-Martial Order (`CMO`)—`DD-214` Block `24` says `"Bad Conduct"` for both!
    * Distinguish administrative **OTH in lieu of trial by GCM** (e.g., Army `AR 635-200, Ch. 10` / `SPD KFS`, which triggers non-waivable `§ 3.12(d)(1)(i)`) from misconduct/pattern-of-misconduct separations (`AR 635-200, Ch. 14` / `MILPERSMAN 1910-140`, which fall under waivable `§ 3.12(d)(2)`!).
    * Audit **Block `29` (`Time Lost`)**: Verify whether `180+` lost days reflect a **single continuous AWOL (`>= 180` consecutive days)** versus *multiple shorter AWOLs* or **military/civilian confinement (`CONF`)**!
* **Clinical & Procedural Triggers in STRs / VBMS:**
  * Post-deployment behavioral collapse, brig/stockade psychiatric evaluations, emergency room overdoses, MST markers, TBI blast exposures, or Axis I diagnoses (PTSD, schizophrenia, bipolar I/II, major depression) preceding or contemporaneous with the misconduct (`§ 3.354(b)` & `§ 3.12(e)(2)`).
  * Post-February 19, 2019 (`AMA`) `VA Form 20-0995` or `21-526EZ` requesting COD reopening where VA issued a letter or decision that failed to explicitly adjudicate the COD issue under `38 U.S.C. § 5104(b)` (*Hamill v. Collins*, `166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. 2026)`).

## 2. Bitemporal Pathway Gate (Escaping the `June 25, 2024` `§ 3.114(a)` Floor)

Reopening a denied COD determination solely under the **June 25, 2024 liberalizing rule (`89 FR 32361`)** caps the earliest possible effective date at **June 25, 2024** (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(g)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.114(a)`). ## 3. Proof Elements & Doctrinal Mechanics

### A. Statutory Bars (`§ 5303(a)` / `§ 3.12(c)`) vs. Regulatory Bars (`§ 3.12(d)`) & The `2024` Overhaul (`89 FR 32361`)

*(Link to verbatim text in [`38-cfr-3.12-3.13-3.354-cod-insanity.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.12-3.13-3.354-cod-insanity.md) and side-by-side comparison in [`character-of-discharge-3.12-evolution.md`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/character-of-discharge-3.12-evolution.md)).*

1. **The Six Statutory Bars (`38 U.S.C. § 5303(a)` [`former § 3103(a)`]; `38 C.F.R. § 3.12(c)(1)–(6)`):**
   * **`(c)(1)` Conscientious Objector:** Refused to perform military duty, wear the uniform, or comply with lawful orders. *(Non-waivable under `§ 3.12(e)`).*
   * **`(c)(2)` Sentence of a General Court-Martial (`GCM`):** Absolute statutory bar unless overcome by `§ 3.13` prior period, `§ 3.354(b)` insanity, or `10 U.S.C. § 1552` BCMR upgrade (`§ 3.12(f)`).
     * **CRITICAL `GCM` vs. `SPCM` Distinction:** A **Bad Conduct Discharge (`BCD`)** adjudged by a **Special Court-Martial (`SPCM`)** is **NOT** a statutory bar under `§ 5303(a)` / `§ 3.12(c)(2)`! An SPCM BCD must be adjudicated under the **regulatory bars of `§ 3.12(d)`**—meaning an SPCM BCD for drug possession or minor offenses **IS eligible for the `§ 3.12(e)` compelling-circumstances exception**! Whenever an RO summarily labels any `"BCD"` a `"statutory court-martial bar"` without pulling the Court-Martial Order (`SPCMO` vs. `GCMO`), reverse on HLR/appeal (or RO CUE if `SPCM` was on the face of the record)!
   * **`(c)(3)` Officer Resignation for the Good of the Service:** Statutory bar applicable strictly to commissioned/warrant officers (e.g., `AR 600-8-24` resignation in lieu of GCM—enlisted `Ch. 10` discharges fall under regulatory `§ 3.12(d)(1)(i)` instead). *(Non-waivable under `§ 3.12(e)`).*
   * **`(c)(4)` Deserter:** Requires affirmative military record proof of desertion (`10 U.S.C. § 885`—intent to remain away permanently or shirk hazardous/important service), not mere administrative "dropped from rolls" (`DFR`) shorthand without a desertion finding. *(Non-waivable under `§ 3.12(e)`).*
   * **`(c)(5)` Alien During Hostilities:** Affirmatively shown that the alien requested release during hostilities (`§ 3.7(b)`). *(Non-waivable under `§ 3.12(e)`).*
   * **`(c)(6)` Continuous AWOL `>= 180` Days Resulting in OTH (`38 U.S.C. § 5303(a)`):**
     * **Strict Computation Rules:** The `180` days must be **continuous** (consecutive). **Exclude** authorized leave, periods after return to military control, travel time authorized upon apprehension/surrender, and **military or civilian confinement** (once apprehended into custody, the member is no longer AWOL). Two separate 110-day AWOLs separated by 3 days of military control equal `220` lost days in `DD-214` Block `29`—**but `0` continuous 180-day bars under `§ 3.12(c)(6)`**!
     * **Compelling Circumstances Eligible (`§ 3.12(c)(6)(i)`):** Unlike `(c)(1)–(5)`, Congress expressly built a compelling-circumstances escape valve into `§ 5303(a)` for `180+`-day AWOL, now governed by **`§ 3.12(e)`**!

2. **Restructured Regulatory Bars (`38 C.F.R. § 3.12(d)(1)–(2)`, eff. June 25, 2024; Validity Upheld in *Garvey v. Wilkie*, `972 F.3d 1333 (Fed. Cir. 2020)`):**
   * `89 FR 32361` eliminated the archaic *"homosexual acts involving aggravating circumstances"* bar (`former § 3.12(d)(5)`) and split remaining regulatory bars into two strict categories:
   * **`§ 3.12(d)(1)` — Compelling Circumstances NOT Applicable:**
     1. **`(d)(1)(i)` Discharge in Lieu of Trial by GCM:** Acceptance of an OTH (or equivalent) discharge in lieu of trial by **General Court-Martial** (note: discharge in lieu of *Special* Court-Martial does **not** trigger `(d)(1)(i)`!).
     2. **`(d)(1)(ii)` Mutiny or Spying:** Mutiny or espionage.
   * **`§ 3.12(d)(2)` — Compelling Circumstances (`§ 3.12(e)`) IS Applicable:**
     1. **`(d)(2)(i)` Offense Involving Moral Turpitude:** Clarified to include, generally, conviction of a **felony** (or civilian/UCMJ equivalent carrying `>1 year` confinement / dishonorable discharge).
     2. **`(d)(2)(ii)` Willful and Persistent Misconduct:** Replaced vague subjective standards with **bright-line temporal & *Manual for Courts-Martial* (`MCM`) thresholds**:
        * *One-time isolated misconduct* is **never** "persistent" (preserving *Cropper v. Brown*, `6 Vet. App. 450 (1994)`).
        * *"Minor misconduct"* means an offense whose maximum *MCM* sentence if tried by GCM would **not** include a dishonorable discharge or confinement `> 1 year`.
        * Conduct is **"persistent"** ONLY if: `(A)` two+ instances of *minor* misconduct occur within **2 years** of each other; `(B)` an instance of *minor* misconduct occurs within **2 years** of *more serious* misconduct; or `(C)` two+ instances of *more serious* misconduct occur within **5 years** of each other.

3. **The Three-Factor Compelling Circumstances Matrix (`38 C.F.R. § 3.12(e)(1)–(3)` — Applies ONLY to `(c)(6)` & `(d)(2)`):**
   * **`(e)(1)` Length & Character of Service** exclusive of the period of prolonged AWOL or misconduct (honest, faithful, meritorious service, deployments, medals, promotions);
   * **`(e)(2)(i)–(vii)` Reasons for Prolonged AWOL or Misconduct:**
     * `(i)` **Mental or cognitive impairment** at the time of conduct—explicitly listing **PTSD, depression, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, substance use disorder (`SUD`), ADHD, impulsive behavior, or cognitive disabilities** *(Crucial: a formal clinical diagnosis at the time of service is NOT required if lay/post-service evidence demonstrates impairment!)*;
     * `(ii)` **Physical health**, including physical trauma and medication side effects;
     * `(iii)` **Combat-related or overseas-related hardship**;
     * `(iv)` **Sexual abuse / assault (`MST`)** or harassment;
     * `(v)` **Duress, coercion, or desperation**;
     * `(vi)` **Family obligations** or comparable obligations to third parties; and
     * `(vii)` **Age, education, cultural background, and judgmental maturity**.
   * **`(e)(3)` Valid Substantive UCMJ Legal Defense** that would have precluded conviction (going to guilt/innocence rather than technicalities).

### B. Pathway 1 — Unbroken Reenlistment & Conditional Discharges (`38 U.S.C. § 101(18)(B)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.13(a)–(c)`)

Prior to 1979, and frequently in modern Navy/Army administrative practice, servicemembers who reenlisted early ("short-term discharge" to accept a new contract or commission) without a physical return to civilian life received **no `DD-214`** at the end of Contract #1. Years later, when the servicemember received an OTH or GCM BCD at the end of Contract #2, DoD issued **one consolidated `DD-214`** covering the entire multi-contract span with Block `24: Other Than Honorable`.

* **The Statutory Rescue (`38 U.S.C. § 101(18)(B)`, added by `Pub. L. 95-126`, Oct. 8, 1977; `38 C.F.R. § 3.13(c)`):**
  * Even where no unconditional discharge certificate was issued (`§ 3.13(a)–(b)`), a servicemember **shall be considered to have been unconditionally discharged or released** from active service upon completing the period of service for which they were originally obligated (`§ 3.13(c)(1)`), where non-issuance resulted from an intervening enlistment/reenlistment (`§ 3.13(c)(2)`), and the member was eligible for a discharge under conditions other than dishonorable at that point (`§ 3.13(c)(3)`).
  * Furthermore, in post-Vietnam peacetime/all-volunteer service (`§ 3.13(a)(3)`), an immediate discharge issued *to reenlist* when eligible for honorable separation is **already an unconditional honorable discharge** locking in the date of early reenlistment!
* **Textbook CUE Weapon (`cue-and-open-claim`):** Any disability incurred or aggravated during Enlistment #1 is **100% immune** from even a **General Court-Martial Dishonorable Discharge** in Enlistment #2! If `DD-214` Block `18` (*"Continuous Honorable Active Service..."*) or `DD Form 4` enlistment contracts were in the C-file when an RO or Board barred *all* active service, file **`38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)` / `38 U.S.C. § 7111` CUE** (or, if `DD Form 4` contracts were never obtained by VA, submit them under **`38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)`** [Playbook 03](03-service-department-records-38-cfr-3-156c.md) to capture the original claim effective date without the CUE standard!).

### C. Pathway 2 — VA Insanity at Time of Offense (`38 U.S.C. § 5303(b)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.354(a)–(b)`)

Under `38 U.S.C. § 5303(b)` (`former § 3103(b)`) and `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.12(b), 3.354(b)`, if it is established to the satisfaction of the Secretary that the veteran was **insane** at the time of committing the offense(s) leading to the court-martial, discharge, or resignation, **every statutory bar (`§ 3.12(c)`, including GCM and desertion!) and every regulatory bar (`§ 3.12(d)`) is completely eliminated**.

1. **VA's Broad, Non-Criminal Definition (`§ 3.354(a)` — *Zang*, *Gardner*, *Struck* & `VAOPGCPREC 20-97`):**
   * VA insanity is **NOT** criminal *M'Naghten* / UCMJ Article `50a` lack-of-mental-responsibility, and a military court-martial's finding of mental competency to stand trial **does NOT bind VA**!
   * Under ***Zang v. Brown*, 8 Vet. App. 246, 252–54 (1995)**, ***Struck v. Brown*, 9 Vet. App. 145, 152–54 (1996)**, and [`VAOPGCPREC 20-97`](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md), a person is insane under `§ 3.354(a)` if, **due to a disease** (excluding constitutional psychopathic / pure personality disorder unless psychosis is engrafted, and excluding mere voluntary intoxication *absent* an underlying mental disease), the person:
     1. Exhibits *"a more or less prolonged deviation from his normal method of behavior"*; **OR**
     2. *"Interferes with the peace of society"*; **OR**
     3. *"Has so departed (become antisocial) from the accepted standards of the community to which by birth and education he belongs as to lack the adaptability to make further adjustment to the social customs of the community in which he resides."*
   * **No Strict Direct-Causation Requirement (*Gardner v. Shinseki*, `22 Vet. App. 415, 419–21 (2009)`):** Overturning restrictive BVA practice, *Gardner* held that VA insanity is **markedly broader than the Model Penal Code**—*"due to disease"* modifies the *behavioral state* (`prolonged deviation`), **not** a criminal-law requirement that the mental disease directly compelled the specific criminal act, so long as the veteran was insane at the time of the misconduct leading to separation!
2. **Duty to Obtain All Procurable Records & Adequate Psychiatric Opinions (*Cropper*, *Stringham*):**
   * Under `§ 3.354(b)`, VA must base its decision on *"all the evidence procurable relating to the period involved"* (including STRs, brig/stockade logs, pre-enlistment records showing baseline "normal method of behavior," and post-service VAMC notes), provide a medical nexus/insanity opinion applying `§ 3.354(a)` when reasonably raised, and state adequate reasons or bases (*Cropper v. Brown*, `6 Vet. App. 450, 452–54 (1994)`; *Stringham v. Brown*, `8 Vet. App. 445, 448–49 (1995)`). *(Note: Under Paulson v. Brown, 7 Vet. App. 466 (1995) and McKnight v. Gober, 131 F.3d 1483 (Fed. Cir. 1997), pre-VCAA notice/status hurdles historically tripped pro se claimants, whereas post-VCAA D'Amico overruling and 38 U.S.C. § 5103A mandate full VA record procurement on COD!).*

### D. Pathway 3 — AMA Explicit-Notice Non-Finality (*Hamill v. Collins*, `166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. 2026)`)

As documented in [Procedural Vehicles for Piercing Finality](../docs/procedural-vehicles-piercing-finality.md), the Federal Circuit's landmark opinion abolishing the implicit-denial doctrine under the AMA—***Hamill v. Collins*, 166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. Feb. 4, 2026)**—**literally arose out of an unaddressed AMA request to reopen a Character of Discharge determination!**
* Whenever a claimant submitted an AMA `VA Form 20-0995` or `21-526EZ` on or after **February 19, 2019** contesting or reopening a COD bar and VA issued an administrative letter or rating decision that failed to satisfy all explicit-notice elements of `38 U.S.C. § 5104(b)` on the COD issue, **the AMA COD claim never became final and remains legally pending**.

### E. Pathway 4 — DRB (`10 U.S.C. § 1553`) vs. BCMR/BCNR (`10 U.S.C. § 1552`) Upgrades & Effective Dates

1. **Forum & Liberal Consideration Selection (`DD Form 293` vs. `DD Form 149`):**
   * **Discharge Review Board (`DRB`, `10 U.S.C. § 1553`, `DD Form 293`):** **15-year statute of limitations** from discharge; reviews propriety and equity (right to personal/video hearing); **cannot** upgrade a **General Court-Martial (`GCM`)** discharge (can review SPCM BCDs granted via clemency/administrative channels depending on branch rules, not GCM sentences).
   * **Board for Correction of Military/Naval Records (`BCMR`/`BCNR`, `10 U.S.C. § 1552`, `DD Form 149`):** **3-year limit** from discovery of error/injustice, **routinely waived "in the interest of justice"**; full authority to upgrade **any** discharge (including setting aside/upgrading GCM characterizations via clemency, changing narrative reasons to **Medical Retirement (`Chapter 61`)**, or erasing AWOL lost-time entries!).
   * **Mandatory Statutory & DoD "Liberal Consideration" (`10 U.S.C. §§ 1552(h), 1553(d)(3)`):** Leverage the four foundational DoD memoranda—**Hagel** (`Sept. 3, 2014`, PTSD), **Carson** (`Feb. 24, 2016`, TBI & *de novo* waivers), **Kurta** (`Aug. 25, 2017`, MST, sexual harassment, broader mental health, and civilian/post-service evidence sufficiency), and **Wilkie** (`July 25, 2018`, equity/clemency/rehabilitation)—now reinforced by statutory mandates requiring clinical/mental-health advisor review!
2. **THE `Pub. L. 95-126` UPGRADE TRAP (`§ 3.12(f)` `[former (e)]` vs. `§ 3.12(h)` `[former (g)]`):**
   * **`BCMR`/`BCNR` Upgrade (`10 U.S.C. § 1552` -> `38 C.F.R. § 3.12(f)` `[former (e)]`):** An upgrade to **Honorable OR General (Under Honorable Conditions)** issued by a **`BCMR`/`BCNR`** is **final and conclusive on VA** and **sets aside BOTH `(c)` statutory bars AND `(d)` regulatory bars**!
   * **Post-October 8, 1977 `DRB` Upgrade (`10 U.S.C. § 1553` -> `38 U.S.C. § 5303(e)(1)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.12(h)(1)–(3)` `[former (g)]`):** An Honorable or General discharge issued on or after October 8, 1977 by a **DRB** sets aside **`(d)` regulatory bars** (provided `(h)(1)–(3)` individual case review + uniform published standards are met, which modern standard DRBs satisfy—unlike 1977 Carter Special Discharge Review Program upgrades under `§ 3.12(i)` `[former (h)]`), **BUT DOES NOT SET ASIDE A `§ 3.12(c)` STATUTORY BAR!** Thus, if the veteran had **`180+` continuous days AWOL (`§ 3.12(c)(6)`)** or **desertion (`§ 3.12(c)(4)`)**, a DRB upgrade alone leaves the statutory bar standing unless counsel **(a) petitions the `BCMR`/`BCNR` under `10 U.S.C. § 1552` (`§ 3.12(f)`)**, **(b) proves `§ 3.12(e)` compelling circumstances** (for `(c)(6)` AWOL), or **(c) proves `§ 3.354(b)` insanity**!
   * **The `10 U.S.C. § 874(b)` Clemency Trap ([`VAOPGCPREC 10-96`](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md)):** A substituted administrative discharge granted as executive clemency by a Service Secretary under **`10 U.S.C. § 874(b)`** does **NOT** lift a `§ 5303(a)` **General Court-Martial** bar; you must obtain a **`10 U.S.C. § 1552` BCMR correction** (`§ 3.12(f)`) or establish `§ 3.354(b)` insanity!
3. **Effective Dates After Upgrade (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(i)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.400(g)` vs. `§ 3.156(c)` *Spencer*):**
   * **Standard Upgrade Rule (`§ 5110(i)`; `§ 3.400(g)`):** Where VA entitlement arises *because of* a `10 U.S.C. § 1552` or `§ 1553` discharge upgrade, the effective date is the **latest** of: `(1)` **the date the application for upgrade/correction was filed with the service department**, `(2)` the date VA received a previously disallowed claim, or `(3)` **1 year prior to the date of reopening** of the disallowed VA claim! *(Practice Imperative: **Always file a VA claim / Supplemental Claim within 1 year of the DRB/BCMR upgrade decision**—otherwise the `(3)` 1-year lookback forfeits the entire multi-year DRB/BCMR processing period!)*
   * **Super-Retroactive `§ 3.156(c)` Override (*Spencer v. West*, `13 Vet. App. 376 (2000)`):** If the BCMR did not merely grant equitable clemency on character of discharge, but **corrected underlying military facts** (e.g., voided an unlawful separation to grant constructive active duty as in *Spencer*, expunged erroneous AWOL days, or issued a `DD-215` corroborating combat/MST stressors) alongside newly associated OMPF records, invoke **`38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)`** ([Playbook 03](03-service-department-records-38-cfr-3-156c.md)) to demand the **original VA claim effective date**!

## 4. Invariants / Kill-Switches

* **The `June 25, 2024` `89 FR 32361` Non-Retroactivity & *George* CUE Bar:** Never plead CUE (`§ 3.105(a)` / `§ 7111`; see [`Board CUE Pre-Filing Gate`](../checklists/board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md)) against a pre-June 25, 2024 final COD decision by citing the 2024 `§ 3.12(d)(2)` 2-year/5-year misconduct metrics or `§ 3.12(e)` compelling circumstances (*George v. McDonough*, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)`; `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`). Pre-2024 CUE requires proving undebatable error under **pre-2024 law** (e.g., ignoring `§ 3.13` prior honorable service, miscounting non-continuous AWOL, mislabeling an SPCM BCD as a GCM bar, or treating a single isolated minor infraction as "persistent" contrary to *Cropper*).
* **Non-Waivable Bars Under `§ 3.12(e)`:** `§ 3.12(e)` compelling circumstances **cannot** waive `§ 3.12(c)(1)–(5)` (conscientious objector, GCM, officer resignation for good of service, deserter, alien release) or `§ 3.12(d)(1)(i)–(ii)` (OTH in lieu of GCM, mutiny/spying). Pivot immediately to **`§ 3.13` prior enlistment**, **`§ 3.354(b)` insanity**, or **`10 U.S.C. § 1552` BCMR upgrade (`§ 3.12(f)`)**.
* **Pure Personality Disorder / Standalone Voluntary Intoxication Exclusion (`§ 3.354(a)`):** Under *Zang*, *Struck*, and `VAOPGCPREC 20-97`, antisocial/borderline personality disorder alone—or acute voluntary intoxication without an underlying Axis I/organic mental disease—cannot establish `§ 3.354(b)` insanity (though **substance use disorder (`SUD`), ADHD, and impulsive behavior DO qualify as `§ 3.12(e)(2)(i)` compelling circumstances** on post-June 25, 2024 COD claims!).
* **Uncharacterized Entry-Level Separations (`§ 3.12(l)` `[former (k)]`):** For separations initiated `>= Oct. 1, 1982`, an **Entry Level Separation (`ELS`, `<180` days)** is treated as **under conditions other than dishonorable** (`§ 3.12(l)(1)`)! Only *Void Enlistments* (`§ 3.14`) or *Dropped From Rolls* (`§ 3.12(l)(2)–(3)`) require special scrutiny (and watch `24-month` minimum-active-duty rules under `38 U.S.C. § 5303A` / `38 C.F.R. § 3.12a`, which **exempt** any veteran discharged for a disability incurred/aggravated in line of duty OR holding a compensable SC disability!).

## 5. Postconditions: Chapter 17 Healthcare Carve-Outs, Effective Dates & Fees

1. **Immediate Chapter 17 VHA Healthcare Access (Even While Compensation COD Is Denied or Pending!):**
   * **`38 U.S.C. § 1720D` (MST Care):** Free VHA counseling and medical/mental treatment for **any** physical or mental condition related to **Military Sexual Trauma (`MST`)**, **frequently available regardless of OTH/BCD character of discharge or `§ 5303A` length of service**!
   * **`38 U.S.C. § 1720I` (Mental & Behavioral Health for OTH Veterans):** Urgent and ongoing VHA mental/behavioral healthcare for former servicemembers with an **OTH** administrative discharge (excluding GCM or statutory bars) who served `>100` active days and either deployed to a combat theater/hostile-fire area, operated unmanned aerial vehicles in combat, or experienced sexual assault/harassment.
   * **`38 U.S.C. § 1702` (`former § 602` Psychosis / Mental Illness Presumption), `§ 1710(e)` Toxic-Exposure Care & `38 C.F.R. § 3.360` (`Pub. L. 95-126, § 2`):** `§ 1702` presumes service connection **for Chapter 17 medical treatment purposes** if a veteran of wartime/Persian Gulf service develops an active psychosis (or post-Gulf War mental illness) within **2 years after discharge** (and before statutory end dates). Moreover, under **`38 C.F.R. § 3.360`**, even when a `§ 3.12(d)` regulatory bar blocks *monetary compensation*, VA **must** still authorize **Chapter 17 healthcare** for disabilities incurred or aggravated in line of duty during active service (`38 U.S.C. § 5303(a)` statutory bars and BCDs block `§ 3.360` care unless `§ 1720D`, `§ 1720J` acute suicidal crisis stabilization, or `§ 3.354(b)` insanity applies!).
   * **`38 C.F.R. § 17.34` Tentative Eligibility:** Authorizes immediate emergent/provisional VHA mental and medical care while a formal VBA COD determination is pending!
2. **Effective-Date & Ancillary Audit Upon Winning COD:**
   * Verify the RO did not reflexively assign **June 25, 2024** (`§ 3.114(a)`) if entitlement was independently established via **`§ 3.13` prior honorable period**, **`§ 3.354(b)` insanity**, **`§ 3.400(g)` DRB/BCMR application date**, **`§ 3.156(c)` newly associated OMPF/STRs**, or **`§ 5104(b)` *Hamill* pending AMA status**.
   * Audit restoration of **Chapter 31 VR&E**, **Chapter 33 GI Bill** *(note: `38 U.S.C. §§ 3011, 3311` strictly require a **full `"Honorable"`** discharge—neither a `General` discharge nor a VA `§ 3.12` COD grant unlocks GI Bill without a DRB/BCMR upgrade to full **Honorable**, unless `§ 3.13` establishes a prior unconditional Honorable period!)*, **Chapter 37 VA Home Loan**, and **`Chapter 61` / `CRSC` ([Playbook 06](06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md))**.
3. **Attorney Fees (`38 U.S.C. § 5904` vs. Pro Bono / Hourly DRB-BCMR Rules):**
   * A prior VA administrative decision denying benefits on COD grounds constitutes an **initial AOJ decision** under `38 U.S.C. § 5904(c)(1)`—meaning `20%–33.3%` contingency fees attach immediately upon filing a `VA Form 20-0995` Supplemental Claim, `20-0996` HLR, `10182` Board appeal, or CUE motion before VA!
   * Separate **Title 10 DRB/BCMR** petitions (`10 U.S.C. §§ 1552, 1553`) are *not* governed by Title 38 `§ 5904(d)` VA direct-pay withholding; structure engagement agreements cleanly to separate Title 10 military-board work from Title 38 VA representation, and offset any CAVC `EAJA` (`28 U.S.C. § 2412(d)`) award dollar-for-dollar against `§ 5904` fees for the same appellate work ([Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).
