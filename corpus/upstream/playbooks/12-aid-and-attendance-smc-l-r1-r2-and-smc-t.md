# Playbook 12: Regular Aid & Attendance (`SMC(l)`), Higher-Level Care (`SMC(r)(1)/(r)(2)`), TBI `SMC(t)` (*Laska v. McDonough*) & `§ 3.552` Hospitalization Defense

Aid and Attendance (`A&A`) tiers represent the single largest monthly compensation multipliers in Title 38—scaling from **`$4,900.83/month`** at `SMC(l)` (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(l)`) to **`$9,826.88/month`** at `SMC(r)(1)` (`§ 1114(r)(1)`) and **`$11,271.67/month`** (veteran alone, effective Dec. 1, 2025) at `SMC(r)(2)` (`§ 1114(r)(2)`) and Traumatic Brain Injury **`SMC(t)` (`§ 1114(t)`)**. Two watershed 2024 judicial decisions—***Barry v. McDonough*, `101 F.4th 1348 (Fed. Cir. May 16, 2024)`** (multi-step bumps into the `SMC(r)(1)` gateway) and ***Laska v. McDonough*, `37 Vet. App. 460 (Sept. 6, 2024)`** (striking down `38 C.F.R. § 3.352(b)(2)`'s licensed-professional barrier to `SMC(t)`)—have unlocked thousands of previously blocked `~$10,000–$11,271+/month` claims.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Pair Doctrinal Architecture, Verbatim Text & Ancillary Unlocks:** Read this operational playbook alongside the [Master SMC Treatise & Step Math](../docs/smc-complete-treatise-and-step-math.md), verbatim snapshots in [`38-cfr-3.350-3.352-smc-evolution.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.350-3.352-smc-evolution.md), chronological diffs in [`smc-statutory-and-regulatory-evolution.md`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/smc-statutory-and-regulatory-evolution.md), [Playbook 04](04-unadjudicated-tdiu-informal-claims-smc.md) (inferred SMC & AMA explicit notice), [Playbook 13](13-ancillary-grants-sah-auto-clothing-dea-spina-bifida.md) (`SAH`/`SHA`, `Auto`, `Clothing`, `DEA`), and the [`SMC & Ancillary Audit Worksheet`](../checklists/smc-and-ancillary-audit-worksheet.md).

## 1. Preconditions: `VA Form 21-2680`, `PCAFC` Caregiver Files & Code-Sheet Triggers

Before pleading any `A&A` tier, pull the complete **VBMS eFolder**, **CAPRI/VistA VHA clinical records** (including Geriatrics & Extended Care and Caregiver Support Program notes), and **every internal Rating Code Sheet** ([VA Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md)):

* **Clinical & VHA Caregiver Triggers (`Bell` Constructive Possession Goldmines):**
  * **`VA Form 21-2680`** (*Examination for Housebound Status or Permanent Need for Regular Aid and Attendance*) or Neurological/PTSD/TBI DBQs checking assistance with bathing, dressing, feeding, toileting, prosthetic adjustment, medication management, mobility/transfers, or supervision against environmental hazards.
  * **VHA `PCAFC` Stipend Files (`38 U.S.C. § 1720G`; `38 C.F.R. § 71.20`):** Enrollment in VHA's *Program of Comprehensive Assistance for Family Caregivers* (`PCAFC`) requires a clinical VHA interdisciplinary finding that the veteran needs personal care services for `>= 6` continuous months due to an inability to perform one or more `ADL`s or a need for **supervision, protection, or instruction**. Because VHA `PCAFC` evaluations sit inside VA medical records, they are **constructively before VBA raters** (*Bell v. Derwinski*; [`VAOPGCPREC 12-95`](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md)) and **reasonably raise `SMC(l)` / `SMC(t)`** (*Akles v. Derwinski*, `1 Vet. App. 118 (1991)`)!
  * **VAMC `HBPC`, `H/HHA`, Bowel/Bladder & Polytrauma Notes:** Home-Based Primary Care (`HBPC`), Homemaker/Home Health Aide (`H/HHA`) authorizations, `BRADEN` fall/ulcer scores, neurogenic bowel/bladder catheterization regimens, or Polytrauma/TBI Clinic notes (`DC 8045`) documenting executive dysfunction, wandering, stove/driving hazards, impulsivity, or memory cueing.
* **Rating Code Sheet SMC Designator Audit:**
  * Inspect the `SMC` block at the bottom of the Rating Code Sheet:
    * `SMC (L-1)` (`§ 1114(l)` Regular A&A) paired with uncounted independent `50%+` or `100%` schedular disabilities (`Barry` `SMC(r)(1)` candidate!).
    * `SMC (O-1)` / `(O-2)` (`§ 3.350(e)(2)` paraplegia + anal/bladder sphincter loss) or `P-1`/`P-2`/`P-3` intermediate steps without `R-1` or `R-2` despite `A&A`.
    * `10%`, `40%`, `70%`, or `100%` `DC 8045` Traumatic Brain Injury (or co-morbid `TBI + PTSD/dementia` rated as `100%`) receiving only `100%`, `SMC(s)`, or `SMC(l)` despite spousal caregiving that prevents nursing-home or assisted-living placement (`SMC(t)` candidate!).
    * Award lines showing `"Hospitalized — SMC Reduced"` (`38 C.F.R. § 3.552`) that persisted *after* the veteran was discharged home!

## 3. Proof Elements & Doctrinal Mechanics

### A. `SMC(l)` Regular Aid & Attendance (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(l)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.352(a), (c)`)

*(Link to verbatim text in [`38-cfr-3.350-3.352-smc-evolution.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.350-3.352-smc-evolution.md)).*

1. **Holistic, Disjunctive `ADL` Standard (*Turco v. Brown*, `9 Vet. App. 222, 224 (1996)`):**
   * `38 C.F.R. § 3.352(a)` enumerates six core indicators: `(1)` inability to dress or undress; `(2)` inability to keep oneself ordinarily clean and presentable; `(3)` frequent need of adjustment of special prosthetic or orthopedic appliances; `(4)` inability to feed oneself through upper-extremity incoordination or extreme weakness; `(5)` inability to attend to the wants of nature (toileting/continence); **OR** `(6)` **physical or mental incapacity requiring care or assistance on a regular basis to protect the claimant from hazards or dangers incident to his or her daily environment**.
   * **The *Turco* Rule:** In *Turco*, `9 Vet. App. at 224`, the CAVC held that `§ 3.352(a)` is explicitly **disjunctive and holistic**—a claimant **need NOT exhibit every listed inability**, and **"regular" aid and attendance does NOT require "constant" (`24/7`) need**! Requiring assistance with bathing and dressing three or four times per week, or regular daily cognitive hazard monitoring, fully satisfies `"regular"` A&A when viewed in connection with the veteran's condition as a whole.
   * **Permanently Bedridden Alternative (`§ 3.352(a)`):** Requires that the essential character of the SC disease/injury *actually requires* remaining in bed (excluding voluntary bedrest or physician-prescribed convalescent rest). Never let an examiner conflate `"bedridden"` with `"regular A&A"`—`§ 3.352(a)` expressly prohibits basing A&A on whether the veteran stays in bed!
2. **Cognitive & Psychiatric `"Hazards of Daily Environment"` (`§ 3.352(a)`):**
   * Raters routinely fixate on physical paralysis while overlooking cognitive/mental A&A. Document concrete safety incidents in spouse/caregiver declarations: leaving burners/stoves on, forgetting or double-dosing insulin/medications, wandering/getting lost, falling on stairs due to vertigo/ataxia/orthostatic hypotension, or severe psychotic/dissociative/dementia disorientation.
3. **Unlicensed Spouse / Family Caregiving (`38 C.F.R. § 3.352(c)`) & Medical Nexus Rigor (*Prejean* & *Martinez*):**
   * Under **`38 C.F.R. § 3.352(c)`**, performance of A&A by a **spouse, child, or household relative** fully qualifies—zero professional licensing or out-of-pocket payment is required for `SMC(l)`!
   * **Sole-SC Causation Rule:** Unlike NSC Pension A&A, compensation `SMC(l)` requires that **service-connected disability(ies) alone** necessitate regular A&A (excluding NSC aging/arthritis). Under ***Prejean v. West*, `13 Vet. App. 444, 448–49 (2000)`**, any private `VA Form 21-2680` or IMO **must** provide a **thorough, reasoned medical rationale** connecting objective clinical findings on SC conditions directly to the specific `§ 3.352(a)` functional deficits (conclusory check-boxes without clinical reasoning will be discounted). *(Procedural note: Under *Martinez v. Wilkie*, `31 Vet. App. 170 (2019)`, VA is not statutorily required to proactively mail an unfavorable C&P copy prior to decision, so counsel must actively pull VBMS `21-2680` C&P uploads immediately after exam completion to submit rebuttal IMOs before rating!).*

### B. Unlocking `SMC(r)(1)` (`$9,826.88/mo`) & `SMC(r)(2)` (`$11,271.67/mo`) (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(r)`; `§§ 3.350(h), 3.352(b)`)

A veteran needing Regular A&A (`§ 3.352(a)`) cannot jump straight from `SMC(l)` (`$4,900.83`) to `SMC(r)(1)` (`$9,826.88`) without **first** crossing one of **three statutory gateways** in `38 U.S.C. § 1114(r)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(h)(1)–(2)` (see full math in [Master SMC Treatise § 4](../docs/smc-complete-treatise-and-step-math.md)):

1. **Gateway 1 — Dual `(l)–(n)` Entitlements (`§ 1114(o)` / `§ 3.350(e)(1)(ii)` & `(e)(2)`):**
   * **Paraplegia `Code O-2` (`§ 3.350(e)(2)`):** Bilateral lower-extremity LOU **+** loss of anal and bladder sphincter control = automatic `SMC(o)` + A&A -> **`SMC(r)(1)`** (and **immune** from `§ 3.552` hospitalization reduction!).
   * **Paired Independent `(l)–(n)` Conditions (*Breniser* Boundary):** E.g., bilateral foot LOU (`SMC(l)` #1) **+** Regular A&A (`SMC(l)` #2) caused **solely by a separate, distinct SC condition** (such as SC Parkinson's upper-extremity/cognitive impairment or SC heart failure). Under ***Breniser v. Shinseki*, `25 Vet. App. 64, 74–77 (2011)`**, if the A&A need relies *in whole or in part* on the bilateral foot LOU, `(e)(1)(ii)` fails—the IMO **must** isolate the non-extremity SC condition as independently sufficient for `§ 3.352(a)` A&A!
2. **Gateway 2 — Post-*Barry* Multi-Step Bumps to Max `§ 1114(p)` (`$6,877.12`):**
   * Under ***Barry v. McDonough*, `101 F.4th 1348 (Fed. Cir. 2024)`**, a veteran at base **`SMC(l)` Regular A&A** who stacks **6 half-steps** (`§ 3.350(f)(3)` `>= 50%` units), **3 full-steps** (`§ 3.350(f)(4)` `100%` schedular units—*not TDIU*), or any equivalent mix (`e.g., two 100%s + two 50%s`) reaches **"the maximum rate authorized under subsection (p)" (`$6,877.12`)**, which under the plain text of **`38 U.S.C. § 1114(r)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(h)(1)` automatically converts into `SMC(r)(1)` (`$9,826.88/mo`)**!
3. **Gateway 3 — The `SMC(n½) + SMC(k)` Special Bridge (`§ 1114(r)` & `§ 3.350(h)(2)`):**
   * Even **one half-step short of `SMC(o)`**, a veteran at **`SMC(n½)` (`$6,514.44`) + at least one `SMC(k)` (`$139.87`)** (e.g., `ED` `DC 7522`) who needs Regular A&A **immediately qualifies for `SMC(r)(1)` (`$9,826.88/mo`)**!

#### Escalating From `SMC(r)(1)` to `SMC(r)(2)` (`$11,271.67/mo` — `§ 1114(r)(2)`; `§ 3.352(b)(1), (3)–(6)`):
Once any of the three gateways above is met, advancing from `(r)(1)` to **`(r)(2)` (`+$1,444.79/month`)** requires proving **all three** strictly construed (`§ 3.352(b)(6)`) elements:
1. **Daily In-Home `"Personal Health-Care Services"` (`§ 3.352(b)(3)`):** Services requiring professional health-care training/skills—such as **administration of injections, placement/management of indwelling or intermittent catheters, sterile wound/ulcer dressings, physical/occupational therapy, tracheostomy/ventilator suctioning, PEG tube feeding, or digital bowel disimpaction programs**—performed **daily** in the veteran's home.
2. **Licensed Provider OR `"Regular Supervision"` of a Spouse/Relative (`§ 3.352(b)(4)–(5)` — Critical Practitioner Lever!):**
   * A spouse or household relative performing those daily health-care services **QUALIFIES for `SMC(r)(2)`** under `§ 3.352(b)(4)–(5)` **if** `(a)` the relative follows a **prescribed regimen** of personal health-care services ordered by a licensed health-care professional (`MD`, `DO`, `RN`, `NP`, `PA`, `PT`), **AND** `(b)` **the licensed health-care professional consults with the unlicensed caregiver at least ONCE EACH MONTH to monitor the regimen (`"The consultation need not be in person; a telephone call will suffice"` — `§ 3.352(b)(4)`)**!
   * *Action Step:* Enlist the veteran's VAMC `HBPC` nurse or private treating physician to write the daily home-care regimen into the medical chart and log a scheduled **monthly telephone check-in** with the caregiving spouse!
3. **Institutionalization-Avoidance Nexus (`§ 3.352(b)(1)(iii)`):** Medical certification that, **in the absence of** such daily higher-level care, the veteran **would require hospitalization, nursing home care, or other residential institutional care**.

### C. Traumatic Brain Injury `SMC(t)` (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(t)`) & ***Laska v. McDonough*, `37 Vet. App. 460 (2024)`**

Enacted by **`Pub. L. 111-275, Title VI, § 601`** (`124 Stat. 2864, 2884`, Oct. 13, 2010, **effective October 1, 2011**) and codified nearly seven years later at **`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.350(j), 3.352(b)(2)`** (`83 FR 20735`, pub. May 8, 2018, **eff. June 7, 2018**), **`SMC(t)` pays the full `SMC(r)(2)` monthly rate (`$11,271.67/month` in 2026)** to TBI veterans **without** requiring `SMC(o)`, limb loss, or multiple ratings!

#### How *Laska v. McDonough* (`37 Vet. App. 460 (Sept. 6, 2024)`) Demolished VA's `SMC(t)` Roadblock:
* **VA's Unlawful 2018 Regulation (`38 C.F.R. § 3.352(b)(2)(ii)`):** When VA promulgated `83 FR 20735` in 2018, VA inserted an extra-statutory requirement into `§ 3.352(b)(2)(ii)` demanding that TBI veterans prove a need for `"higher level of care"` (`§ 3.352(b)(3)`—daily skilled medical procedures by or under monthly supervision of a licensed health-care professional). Because severe TBI residuals typically demand **cognitive/behavioral supervision, cueing, and safety monitoring** by a spouse rather than sterile surgical dressings or injections, VA denied `95%+` of `SMC(t)` claims!
* **The Landmark Holding in *Laska v. McDonough*, `37 Vet. App. 460 (2024)` (`No. 22-1018`, decided Sept. 6, 2024; originally docketed and argued as *Haskell v. McDonough* before surviving spouse Margaret Laska substituted):**
  * A precedential CAVC panel **invalidated `38 C.F.R. § 3.352(b)(2)`'s `"higher level of care"` requirement**, holding it manifestly contrary to the plain text of **`38 U.S.C. § 1114(t)`**!
  * Under `§ 1114(t)` and *Laska*, a veteran wins **`SMC(t)` (`$11,271.67/mo`)** upon proving **three plain statutory elements**:
    1. **Regular Aid & Attendance for SC TBI Residuals:** As a result of service-connected **`TBI` residuals (`DC 8045`)**, the veteran needs **Regular Aid & Attendance under `38 C.F.R. § 3.352(a)`**—explicitly including **unlicensed spouse or family caregiving (`§ 3.352(c)`)** for cognitive supervision, memory prompting, medication management, behavioral/impulse safety, fall prevention, or ADL assistance! *(Note: Where SC TBI and co-morbid SC PTSD/dementia cannot be clinically disentangled by an examiner, apply *Mittleider v. West*, `11 Vet. App. 181 (1998)` to attribute inseparable cognitive symptoms to the SC TBI!)*
    2. **Ineligibility for `SMC(r)(2)`:** The veteran is not otherwise eligible for `§ 1114(r)(2)` (e.g., lacks `SMC(o)`/`(p)` or lacks skilled `§ 3.352(b)(3)` nursing care).
    3. **Institutionalization Avoidance Absent Regular A&A:** **In the absence of such regular aid and attendance**, the veteran **would require hospitalization, nursing home care, or other residential institutional care** (including assisted-living facilities, memory-care units, or residential brain-injury group homes—the veteran could **not** safely live independently if the family caregiver were removed!).

### D. `38 U.S.C. § 5503(c)` & `38 C.F.R. § 3.552` VA Hospitalization Reduction Defense

To prevent duplicate federal expenditure when VA itself furnishes inpatient nursing/medical care, `38 U.S.C. § 5503(c)` (`former § 3203`) and `38 C.F.R. § 3.552` reduce A&A awards during prolonged federal hospitalization—creating **four high-frequency RO errors** that counsel must audit:

1. **The `"First Day of the Second Calendar Month"` Grace Window (`§ 3.552(b)(1)–(2)`):**
   * Discontinuance of `SMC(l)` A&A, `SMC(r)(1)`, `SMC(r)(2)`, or `SMC(t)` is **NEVER immediate upon admission** (except readmission within `<6 months` after an **against-medical-advice [`AMA`] or disciplinary** discharge under `§ 3.552(b)(3)`!).
   * Reduction takes effect on the **last day of the month following the month of admission**—meaning the reduced rate begins on the **first day of the SECOND calendar month after admission** at VA / U.S. Government expense!
   * *Calendar Math:* Admitted to a VAMC or VA Community Living Center (`CLC`) on **March 2** -> **100% full `SMC(r)/(t)` (`$11,271.67`) is payable for ALL of March AND ALL of April** -> reduction takes effect **May 1**! If discharged on **April 29**, **zero reduction ever occurs**! *(Note: Under `§ 3.552(k)(2)`, if initial A&A entitlement arises *on or after* hospital admission, A&A payment is deferred until hospital release unless exempt under `(a)(2)`).*
2. **Exact Hospitalization Fallback Rates (`§ 3.552(d)–(i)`):**
   * **`SMC(r)(1)` / `(r)(2)`:** Drops to the underlying `SMC(o)` / max `SMC(p)` (`$6,877.12`)—*unless* that `SMC(o)` itself depended on a second `SMC(l)` A&A rate, in which case **`§ 3.552(g)(1)–(3)`** governs: base `(l)` + separate A&A drops to **`SMC(m)`** (`(g)(1)`); base `(m)` + separate A&A drops to **`SMC(n)`** (`(g)(2)`); base `(n)` + separate A&A **retains `SMC(o)`** (`(g)(3)`)!
   * **`SMC(l)` A&A + `§ 3.350(f)` Step Bumps (`§ 3.552(i)`):** Reduces during hospitalization to **`SMC(s)` (`$4,408.53`)** (including `SMC(r)(1)` achieved solely via `SMC(l)` A&A + *Barry* `(f)(3)–(4)` bumps, or `SMC(t)` where underlying TBI + co-morbidities equal `SMC(s)` or `100%`).
3. **Absolute Statutory/Regulatory Exemptions (`§ 3.552(a)(1)–(3)`):**
   * **Paraplegia + Sphincter Loss (`§ 3.552(a)(2)` — `Code O-2`):** **ZERO hospitalization reduction** ever applies when A&A is based on **paralysis of both lower extremities together with loss of anal and bladder sphincter control**, OR **Hansen's disease**! *(Trap Warning: `§ 3.552(a)(2)`'s `5/200` blindness exemption applies **only to NSC Pension**, NOT to SMC compensation!)*
   * **Non-VA Expense Care:** Private hospital or private nursing-home stays paid by **Medicare, private insurance, or personal funds** (not under VA contract/expense) **do NOT trigger `§ 3.552`**!
4. **Mandatory Immediate Restoration on Hospital Release (`§ 3.552` — Classic RO CUE):**
   * Full `SMC(l)` / `(r)(1)` / `(r)(2)` / `(t)` **must be restored effective the exact date of release/discharge** from VA hospitalization (including authorized `30+`-day convalescent leaves). Raters routinely forget to lift the hospitalization withholding code for months or years after discharge—a textbook **RO CUE (`§ 3.105(a)`)**!

## 4. Invariants / Kill-Switches

* **Hard `October 1, 2011` Statutory Floor on `SMC(t)` (`Pub. L. 111-275, § 601(c)`):** Never request `SMC(t)` for any period prior to **October 1, 2011**, even on an open 2007 Iraq/Afghanistan TBI appeal—stage the award as `SMC(l)` (or `SMC(r)(1)` if *Barry* bumps apply) prior to Oct. 1, 2011, stepping up to `SMC(t)` on **October 1, 2011**!
* **The *Breniser* Double-Counting Bar (`25 Vet. App. 64`):** Never plead `SMC(o)` / `SMC(r)(1)` under `§ 3.350(e)(1)(ii)` when the veteran's A&A need is caused in whole or in part by the exact same extremity/blindness condition establishing base `SMC(l)`–`(n)`. Pivot to **Gateway 2 (*Barry* `(f)(3)–(4)` bumps from distinct ratings)** or **`§ 3.350(e)(2)` bowel/bladder loss (`Code O-2`)** instead!
* **The *George v. McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) / `§ 20.1403(e)` CUE Bar:** Never file a CUE motion attacking a final pre-May 16, 2024 single-bump rating under *Barry* or a final pre-Sept. 6, 2024 `SMC(t)` denial under *Laska*. Unlock non-finality via `§ 5104(b)` *Hamill*, `§ 3.156(b)` *Lang*, or `§ 3.156(c)`, or file an immediate `VA Form 20-0995` Supplemental Claim!
* **TDIU Exclusion Under `§ 3.350(f)(4)`:** Remember `§ 3.350(f)(4)`'s full-step bump requires a **`100%` schedular** evaluation (`"apart from any consideration of individual unemployability"`). If an independent `70%` disability underpins TDIU, use it as a **`+½` step under `§ 3.350(f)(3)`** instead!

## 5. Postconditions: Effective Dates, `PCAFC` Non-Offset, Ancillary Unlocks & Fees

1. **Zero Offset Between `SMC(l)/(r)/(t)` & VHA `PCAFC` Family Caregiver Stipends (`38 U.S.C. § 1720G`):**
   * **Massive Household Multiplier:** Monthly VHA `PCAFC` stipends paid directly to a spouse/family caregiver under `38 U.S.C. § 1720G` (`38 C.F.R. pt. 71`) are **separate VHA health-care support payments—NOT Title 38 duplicate disability compensation**! A qualifying TBI or polytrauma household legally collects **100% of tax-free `SMC(t)` or `SMC(r)(2)` (`$11,271.67+/month`) PLUS 100% of the monthly `PCAFC` Level 1 or Level 2 caregiver stipend** with **zero dollar-for-dollar offset** (only VHA *in-home contract aide hours* may be coordinated with clinical need, never VBA `SMC` cash pay!).
2. **Automatic High-Dollar Ancillary Unlocks ([Playbook 13](13-ancillary-grants-sah-auto-clothing-dea-spina-bifida.md)):**
   * Upon winning `SMC(l)`–`(t)` or extremity/vision LOU, immediately trigger: **`~\$121,000+` `Ch. 21` Specially Adapted Housing (`SAH`)** (for bilateral lower-extremity LOU, blindness, severe burns, or certain single-extremity + A&A/balance combinations), **`~\$26,000+` `Ch. 39` Automobile + unlimited adaptive equipment grants** (including the 10-year second auto grant under `Pub. L. 117-333`), **multiple annual `§ 3.810` Clothing Allowances** (*Sursely*), and **`P&T` `Ch. 35` DEA / `CHAMPVA`** retroactive effective-date audits!
3. **Military Retirement (`CRDP` / `CRSC`) & Attorney Fees (`38 U.S.C. § 5904` & `EAJA`):**
   * For military retirees (`Ch. 61` medical retirees especially!), stepping up from `100%` (`$3,938.58`) to `SMC(r)(2)`/`SMC(t)` (`$11,271.67`) increases VA waiver against military retired pay—immediately file/amend **`CRSC` (`10 U.S.C. § 1413a`)** for combat/instrumentality TBI or polytrauma to recover **uncapped retroactive retired-pay offsets under *Soto v. United States*, `605 U.S. 360 (2025)`** ([Playbook 06](06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md))!
   * Because SMC is part and parcel of underlying compensation claims (*Akles*), any prior AOJ rating decision on the underlying TBI or SC disabilities satisfies `38 U.S.C. § 5904(c)(1)` for contingency fees on Supplemental, HLR, or Board SMC upgrades (offsetting any CAVC `EAJA` award dollar-for-dollar for the same work; see [Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).
