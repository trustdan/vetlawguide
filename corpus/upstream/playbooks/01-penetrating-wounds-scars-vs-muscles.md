# Playbook 01: Gunshot, Shrapnel & Penetrating Wounds (`0%` Scar vs. Muscle Group Injury)

One of the highest-yielding retroactive patterns in WWII, Korean War, Vietnam, and early Gulf/OIF/OEF C-files occurs when a combat veteran suffered a penetrating gunshot (`GSW`) or shell/grenade fragment wound (`SFW`/`MFW`) and VA assigned `0%` (or `10%`) under **skin scar codes (`DC 7800–7805`)** while completely ignoring **underlying muscle group damage (`DC 5301–5323`)** and **peripheral nerve injury (`DC 8510–8730`)**.

---

## 1. C-File Red Flags (Audit Triggers)

Scan the earliest post-discharge Rating Code Sheet and clinical records for:
* **Rating Code Sheet Notations:**
  * `7804` / `7805` / `7801` / `7802` — `0%` or `10%` — `"Residuals, GSW right thigh"` or `"Scars, multiple fragment wounds"` (noting pre-Jan. 1, 1959 sheets cite `Vet. Reg. No. 1(a)` and `1959–Aug. 5, 1991` sheets cite `38 U.S.C. § 310` prior to `Pub. L. 85-857` / `Pub. L. 102-83` renumbering).
  * Hyphenated scar codes (`7805-7804`) with zero `53xx` muscle group code anywhere on the sheet.
* **Service Medical / Separation Physical Keywords (`SF 88`, `SF 600`, Field Medical Card `DD Form 1380`, Evac Hospital Summary):**
  * `"T&T"` or `"through and through"` (entrance and exit wounds!).
  * `"Debridement"` (surgical excision of devitalized muscle/deep tissue).
  * `"Retained MFB"` / `"retained metallic fragments"` / `"shrapnel on X-ray"` inside muscle compartments.
  * `"Loss of deep fascia"`, `"muscle hernia"`, `"depressed/adherent to underlying muscle"`, `"atrophy"`, `"weakness"`, or `"lowered threshold of fatigue"`.

---

## 2. Substantive Mechanics: Why Separate & Higher Ratings Were Mandatory

### A. Muscle Groups (`38 C.F.R. § 4.73`, `DC 5301–5323`) & Verbatim `§ 4.56` Grading
Always pull the exact edition of `38 C.F.R. § 4.56` in force on the rating date from [`38-cfr-4.56-muscle-injuries-1946-to-present.md`](../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.56-muscle-injuries-1946-to-present.md) (`62 FR 30235`, published June 3, 1997, **effective July 3, 1997**):
* **Slight Muscle Disability (often `0%`, or `10%` in Group XIII `DC 5313` Posterior Thigh/Hamstrings):** Simple wound without debridement, no infection, clean healing, no retained intramuscular fragments, trivial functional effect.
* **Moderate Muscle Disability (`10%` or `20%` depending on Muscle Group):**
  * **Pre-July 3, 1997 `§ 4.56(b)`:** **Through-and-through (`T&T`) or deep penetrating wound** of relatively short track by single bullet or small shell/shrapnel fragment, *without* explosive effect of high-velocity missile, residuals of debridement, or prolonged infection, accompanied by lowered fatigue threshold or impaired tonus/power.
  * **Post-July 3, 1997 `§ 4.56(b)` & `(d)(2)`:** `§ 4.56(d)(2)` carries forward the short-track `T&T` / deep penetrating wound criteria (*without* explosive effect, debridement, or prolonged infection) plus cardinal signs (`loss of power`, `weakness`, `lowered threshold of fatigue`, `fatigue-pain`, `impairment of coordination`), **AND `§ 4.56(b)` establishes an explicit floor:** *"A through-and-through injury with muscle damage shall be evaluated as no less than a moderate injury for each group of muscles damaged."*
* **Moderately Severe (`20%`, `30%`, or `40%`) & Severe (`30%`, `40%`, or `50%`):**
  * **Surgical debridement**, prolonged infection, sloughing of soft parts, and intermuscular scarring sit in **Moderately Severe** (`pre-1997 § 4.56(c)`; `post-1997 § 4.56(d)(3)`), while explosive high-velocity wounds, shattering/open comminuted fractures (`post-1997 § 4.56(a)`), extensive fascia/muscle substance loss, and scattered intramuscular metallic fragments sit in **Severe** (`pre-1997 § 4.56(d)`; `post-1997 § 4.56(d)(4)`).

### B. Anti-Pyramiding & Separate Ratings (`38 C.F.R. § 4.14`; *Esteban v. Brown*)
Under *Esteban v. Brown*, 6 Vet. App. 259, 261–62 (1994) (construing the unambiguous plain text of `38 C.F.R. § 4.14` since 1946—making it **`cue-and-open-claim`** safe without triggering *George v. McDonough*, 596 U.S. 740 (2022) / `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`), anti-pyramiding bars compensating the **exact same manifestation** twice, NOT separate manifestations of the same combat wound! A single GSW or shrapnel blast routinely supports **three independent, stackable ratings**:
1. **Cutaneous Scar (`DC 7800–7805`):** Superficial skin tenderness/pain on touch or instability (`DC 7804`), nonlinear surface area (`DC 7801/7802`), or head/face/neck disfigurement (`DC 7800`) — check the exact scar era in [`38-cfr-4.118-scars-evolution.md`](../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.118-scars-evolution.md) (and note the Sept. 29, 2025 `90 FR 46542` `DC 7804` objective-pain proposal remains **proposed / unfinalized**).
2. **Muscle Group Injury (`DC 5301–5323`):** Loss of muscle power, lowered fatigue threshold, deep muscular ache on use. (Under `38 C.F.R. § 4.55(c)–(e)`, injuries to **different anatomical muscle groups** rate separately unless acting on the *same* joint in the *same* plane—and two injured muscle groups acting on the same joint combine by elevating the higher group to the next higher grade; *see also* *Tropf v. Nicholson*, 20 Vet. App. 317 (2006) and *Lyles v. Shulkin*, 29 Vet. App. 107 (2017)).
3. **Peripheral Nerve Injury (`DC 8510–8730`):** Numbness, paresthesia, sensory deficit, or motor denervation (`38 C.F.R. § 4.55(a)`).

## 3. Legal Pathway Selection Decision Tree

1. **Were the inpatient surgical / evac hospital records MISSING when VA originally rated `0%` scar?**
   * **YES -> `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)` Reconsideration!** Request the inpatient clinical packet from NPRC (distinct from the outpatient STR jacket). Once associated, VA **must** reconsider the original claim, order a contemporary Muscle Injuries DBQ, and grant staged ratings back to the original claim date—**no CUE standard required**!
2. **Were the STRs / separation exam showing T&T wound, debridement, or muscle damage ALREADY IN THE C-FILE when the RO assigned `0%` under `DC 7804/7805`?**
   * **YES (and never appealed to BVA on that issue) -> RO CUE (`38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)`):** Argue the rater committed undebatable legal/factual error by failing to apply `38 C.F.R. §§ 4.55, 4.56, 4.73` to undisputed record facts (e.g., documented `T&T` wound through Muscle Group XIII `DC 5313` or Group XVII `DC 5317`, where a *Moderate* or *Moderately Severe* rating yielded `10%–40%`, manifestly changing the veteran's combined compensable evaluation under *Russell v. Principi*, 3 Vet. App. 310 (1992) (en banc)).
3. **Did the veteran get rated `0%` for the wound, and later VAMC clinic notes (pre-March 24, 2015) documented muscle weakness/retained-shrapnel flare-ups without a rating decision?**
   * **YES -> Unadjudicated Informal Claim for Increase (`historical 38 C.F.R. § 3.157(b)`)!**

---

## 4. Evidentiary Burden & Kill-Switches

* **Combined-Rating Math Check ("Manifestly Changed Outcome"):** Before pleading CUE, verify the **Combined Ratings Table (`38 C.F.R. § 4.25`)** on the date of the challenged RO decision! If the veteran was `0%` overall, moving one muscle group from `0%` to `10%` manifestly changed payment. If the veteran was already `60%` (`58%` raw), adding `10%` (`58 + 4 = 62 -> 60%`) **did NOT change the monthly check** at that exact date unless it later pushed a combined threshold or `SMC(s)` over the line!
* **Temporal `§ 4.56` Text Match:** Always quote the **pre-July 3, 1997** `38 C.F.R. § 4.56` text for decisions rendered before July 3, 1997 (note `62 FR 30235` was published June 3, 1997, so a June 20, 1997 decision is still governed by the pre-1997 text!), and the **post-July 3, 1997** text for later decisions. See [Muscle Injuries Historical Diff](../cfr-diffs/vasrd/muscle-injuries-dc-5301-5323.md).
