# Playbook 21: VA Overpayment Debt Defense & Waivers (`§ 5302` / `§ 5112(b)(10)`), Incompetency & Fiduciary Defense (`§ 3.353`) & Wartime Aid & Attendance Pension (`Ch. 15`)

Three urgent financial-protection and elder-care emergencies routinely land on a Veterans Law firm's desk—often involving existing compensation clients whose livelihoods or autonomy are suddenly threatened: **(1) VA Debt Management Center (`DMC`) demand letters** threatening `100%` or partial monthly benefit recoupment over alleged dependency, drill-pay, severance, or reduction overpayments; **(2) proposed `38 C.F.R. § 3.353` incompetency findings** triggered when a `70%`/`100%` PTSD or TBI examiner casually checks `"No"` on financial capability; and **(3) Wartime Veterans & Survivors Aid & Attendance (`A&A`) Pension (`38 U.S.C. ch. 15`)** claims where memory-care or assisted-living costs (`$5,000–$8,500+/month`) wipe out a wartime household's retirement income.

## 1. Preconditions: VBMS, DMC & Clinical Intake Triggers (`c_file_triggers`)

Immediately pull the complete **VBMS eFolder**, **DMC accounting ledger**, **Rating Code Sheets**, and **CAPRI/VistA clinical notes** ([VA Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md)) whenever intake reveals any of these triggers:

* **Overpayment & Debt-Collection Triggers (`Domain A`):**
  * **VA Debt Management Center (`DMC`) `First Demand Letter`** (`St. Paul, MN`) or Regional Office notice citing `38 U.S.C. § 5314` and scheduling benefit offset or Treasury Offset Program (`TOP`) referral.
  * **`VA Form 5655`** (*Financial Status Report*) packet, `VA Form 21-686c` (*Declaration of Status of Dependents*) divorce/stepchild removal lag, Reserve drill-pay audit (`VA Form 21-8951`), or severance/separation recoupment adjustment ([Playbook 20](20-backpay-offsets-crdp-crsc-severance-recoupment.md)).
  * **Committee on Waivers and Compromises (`COWC`) Decision** issued after **December 29, 2022** dismissing a waiver request as `"untimely (> 180 days)"` when Congress had already expanded `38 U.S.C. § 5302(a)` to **`1 year`**!
* **Incompetency & Fiduciary Triggers (`Domain B`):**
  * **C&P Mental Disorders or TBI DBQ** checking **`"No"`** to *"Is the veteran capable of managing his or her financial affairs?"*—especially following an award of `70%` or `100%` PTSD, schizophrenia, dementia, or `DC 8045` TBI.
  * **Proposed Incompetency Letter** citing `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.105(h), 3.353(e)` (starts the critical **`60-day` evidence / `30-day` predetermination hearing** clock!), Fiduciary Hub field-examination notice, `up to 4%` fiduciary fee authorization (`38 U.S.C. § 5502(b)`), or allegations that an appointed fiduciary diverted client funds (`38 U.S.C. §§ 6106–6107`).
* **Wartime Pension & Aid & Attendance Triggers (`Domain C`):**
  * **`VA Form 21P-527EZ`** (*Application for Veterans Pension*), **`VA Form 21P-534EZ`** (*Application for DIC, Survivors Pension, and/or Accrued Benefits*), **`VA Form 21-2680`** (*Examination for Housebound Status or Permanent Need for Regular Aid and Attendance*), **`VA Form 21P-8416`** (*Medical Expense Report*), and **`VA Form 21P-0969`** (*Income and Asset Statement*).
  * **Assisted-Living, Memory-Care, or Home-Care Contracts** documenting recurring custodial/`ADL` care expenses alongside `DD-214` wartime service (`>= 1 day` during WWII, Korea, Vietnam, or Gulf War) or an existing `10%–50%` service-connected evaluation where `Ch. 15` A&A Pension pays more (`38 C.F.R. § 3.701(a)–(c)`; `M21-1, Part VI, Subpart ii, 1.A.2.a`).

## 3. Domain A — VA Overpayment Debt Defense (`38 U.S.C. §§ 5302, 5112(b)(10)`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 1.911–1.970, 3.500(b)(2)`)

*(Link to verbatim text in [`38-cfr-3.274-3.279-3.353-1.965-pension-competency-waivers.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.274-3.279-3.353-1.965-pension-competency-waivers.md)).*

### A. The Mandatory Two-Track Pleading Rule (*Schaper*, *Farless*, *Narron* & *Edwards*)

Under **`38 U.S.C. § 5314`**, VA cannot lawfully offset compensation or pension benefits until it affords the beneficiary written predisposition notice of **both** the right to **dispute the existence/amount of the debt** and the right to **request an equitable waiver** (*Schaper v. Derwinski*, `1 Vet. App. 430, 435–36 (1991)`; *Farless v. Derwinski*, `2 Vet. App. 555, 558 (1992)`). In ***Narron v. West*, `13 Vet. App. 223, 227–28 (1999)`**, the CAVC held that debt-validity disputes and timely waiver requests are procedurally intertwined conditions precedent to final recoupment, and in ***Edwards v. Peake*, `22 Vet. App. 57, 63–65 (2008)`**, the Court required VA and the Board to sympathetically read *pro se* debt correspondence to recognize implicit waiver requests.

* **Ironclad Firm Rule:** **NEVER file a `VA Form 5655` / `§ 5302` waiver request in isolation!** Filing a waiver alone implicitly concedes that the debt was lawfully created. Always file a **simultaneous Two-Track Response**: **(Track 1)** an AMA `VA Form 20-0995` / `20-0996` / `10182` (or `§ 3.105(a)` invalidity challenge) attacking the **creation and calculation** of the debt at the Regional Office, **AND (Track 2)** a protective **`§ 5302(a)` equitable waiver + oral hearing request** with `DMC`/`COWC`.

### B. Track 1 — Attack Debt Creation, Validity & Calculation (No `1-Year` Waiver Bar!)

1. **Void *Ab Initio* Underlying Reduction or Severance (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.105(d)–(e), 3.343, 3.344` — [`Playbook 05`](05-void-rating-reductions-stabilized-ratings.md)):**
   * Whenever an overpayment stems from a rating reduction, severance of service connection, or discontinuance of pension/dependency executed **without** strict `60-day` predetermination notice, `30-day` hearing rights (`§ 3.105(i)`), and delayed effective-date calculation (`last day of the month in which a 60-day period after final notice expires` under `38 U.S.C. § 5112(b)(6), (b)(9)`), the underlying reduction is **void *ab initio*** (*Brown v. Brown*, `5 Vet. App. 413 (1993)`; *Murphy v. Shinseki*, `26 Vet. App. 510 (2014)`). Reinstating the prior award **extinguishes `100%` of the resulting debt as a matter of law**.
2. **Sole Administrative Error (`38 U.S.C. § 5112(b)(10)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.500(b)(2)`; *Jordan v. Brown*, `10 Vet. App. 171 (1997)`):**
   * Under `38 U.S.C. § 5112(b)(10)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.500(b)(2)`, when an erroneous award is based **solely on administrative error** by VA (and not an act of commission or omission by the beneficiary), reduction or discontinuance takes effect on the **date of last payment**—meaning the adjustment is **strictly prospective and ZERO retroactive overpayment debt can be created**!
   * **The Two-Prong *Jordan* Test (*Jordan v. Brown*, `10 Vet. App. 171, 174–75 (1997)`):** Counsel must prove:
     1. **Zero Payee Omission / Timely Disclosure:** The veteran or survivor timely reported the change (e.g., uploaded `VA Form 21-686c`, `VA Form 21-4138`, or divorce decree in VBMS, or disclosed Reserve status/severance pay on `DD-214` / `21-526EZ`, yet VA waited `3` years to process the dependency removal); **AND**
     2. **Absence of Actual or Constructive Knowledge:** The payee neither knew nor reasonably should have known that the continuing direct deposits were erroneous (e.g., complex blended combined-rating/COLA award tables, obscured award letters lacking `§ 5104(b)` itemization, or cognitive impairment).
3. **Audit Statutory Calculation & Recoupment Errors ([`Playbook 20`](20-backpay-offsets-crdp-crsc-severance-recoupment.md)):**
   * Verify whether VA illegally created a "debt" by recouping **combat-zone Disability Severance Pay** barred by `10 U.S.C. § 1212(d)(2)` (eff. Jan. 28, 2008), recouping **pre-tax gross** rather than **after-tax net** readjustment, severance, or separation/`SSB` pay (`38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(2), (a)(3) & (a)(5)`), forfeiting an entire year's compensation instead of **daily pro-rata Reserve drill-pay days** (`38 C.F.R. § 3.654` — typically `63` training days/year), or applying the wrong end-of-month vs. end-of-year dependency discontinuance rule (`38 U.S.C. § 5112(b)(2)` / `38 C.F.R. § 3.501`).

### C. Track 2 — Equitable Waiver (`38 U.S.C. § 5302(a)`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 1.963, 1.965`)

1. **Critical Two-Clock Architecture (`30-Day` Freeze vs. Two-Era `180-Day -> 1-Year` Waiver Deadline):**
   * **Clock 1 — The `30-Day` Recoupment Freeze (`38 C.F.R. § 1.912a(c)`):** Submitting a written waiver request (or written dispute of existence/amount or oral hearing request) within **`30 days`** of the DMC `First Demand Letter` **automatically stays all benefit withholding** until the `COWC` conducts any requested hearing and issues a written decision! *(If filed on Day `31–365`, the waiver is still timely, but VA may begin monthly withholding pending decision—ask DMC immediately for a hardship compromise/reduced monthly installment plan while the waiver is adjudicated!)*
   * **Clock 2 — The Bitemporal `180-Day -> 1-Year` Statutory Expansion (`Pub. L. 117-328, Div. U, § 254` & `90 FR 54234`):**
     * **Before December 29, 2022:** `38 U.S.C. § 5302(a)` imposed a strict **`180-day`** deadline from the date of notification of indebtedness.
     * **Enacted December 29, 2022 (`Pub. L. 117-328, Div. U, § 254`, Cleland-Dole Act):** Congress amended `38 U.S.C. § 5302(a)` to expand the waiver filing window to **`1 year`** (`365 days`) from the date of notification of indebtedness.
     * **Regulatory Alignment (`90 FR 54234`, pub. Nov. 26, 2025, eff. Jan. 26, 2026, `RIN 2900-AS18`):** Nearly three years later, VA consolidated `former § 1.963(b)(1)–(2)` into undivided **`38 C.F.R. § 1.963(b)`**, updating the regulatory text from `"180 days"` to **`"one year"`** while preserving the vital **actual-receipt rescue clause**: if the payee establishes that **actual receipt** of the debt notice did not occur within a reasonable period after mailing through no fault of the payee (wrong address, hospital stay, postal failure), the **`1-year` clock runs from the date of actual receipt**!
     * **High-Yield `2023–2025` Audit Alert:** Whenever a client's waiver request on a post-Dec. 29, 2022 debt notice was summarily rejected by `DMC`/`COWC` between **2023 and January 2026** because a clerk relied on the unamended `"180-day"` `eCFR` text, challenge that dismissal immediately—the **self-executing statutory `1-year` text of `38 U.S.C. § 5302(a)` controlled over the stale regulation**!
2. **Proving the `6` `"Equity and Good Conscience"` Factors (`38 C.F.R. § 1.965(a)`) & *Cullen* Reasons-or-Bases Mandate:**
   * First clear the **`§ 5302(c)` / `§ 1.965(b)` Gate:** Establish zero **fraud, misrepresentation, or bad faith** (unfair or deceptive dealing design; mere negligence or misunderstanding of VA rules is *not* bad faith!).
   * Under ***Cullen v. Brown*, `5 Vet. App. 510, 512–13 (1993)`**, ***Smith v. Derwinski*, `1 Vet. App. 267, 279–80 (1991)`**, and ***Kaplan v. Brown*, `9 Vet. App. 116, 119–20 (1996)`** (reviewing Board waiver denials under `38 U.S.C. § 7261(a)(3)(A)`'s arbitrary-and-capricious / abuse-of-discretion standard), the Board **must explicitly analyze all six `§ 1.965(a)` factors** with adequate reasons or bases (`38 U.S.C. § 7104(d)(1)`):
     1. **Fault of Debtor (`§ 1.965(a)(1)`):** Document timely reporting (`21-686c`/`21-4138` fax/VBMS receipts), mental/cognitive limitations, or confusing consolidated direct deposits.
     2. **Balancing of Faults (`§ 1.965(a)(2)`):** Weigh debtor fault directly against **VA's multi-year processing delay**—even partial debtor fault justifies partial or total waiver when VA sat on reported dependency/income data for years!
     3. **Undue Hardship (`§ 1.965(a)(3)`):** Complete **`VA Form 5655`** line-by-line with verified rent/mortgage, utilities, groceries, prescriptions, co-pays, transportation, and minimum debt service—proving collection would deprive the veteran and family of **basic necessities** (*Cullen*, `5 Vet. App. at 513`).
     4. **Defeat the Purpose (`§ 1.965(a)(4)`):** Show recoupment undermines the very subsistence/rehabilitative purpose for which Title 38 compensation or pension was awarded.
     5. **Unjust Enrichment (`§ 1.965(a)(5)`):** Establish absence of windfall where funds were consumed in ordinary good-faith household living expenses.
     6. **Changing Position to One's Detriment (`§ 1.965(a)(6)`):** Document irreversible financial commitments made in reliance on VA's award (e.g., signing an assisted-living lease, retiring from work, or purchasing home medical adaptations).
   * **Demand an Oral `COWC` Hearing (`§ 1.911a` / `§ 1.966`):** Debtor credibility on good faith and financial hardship wins waivers—always request a personal/virtual `COWC` hearing.
   * **Attorney Fee Trap (*Gumpenberger v. Wilkie*, `973 F.3d 1379 (Fed. Cir. 2020)`):** Under *Gumpenberger*, invalidating or waiving an *uncollected* `$50,000` debt yields **`$0` in `"past-due benefits"`** for `38 U.S.C. § 5904(d)` direct-pay withholding; direct-pay `20%` contingency fees attach **only to cash already withheld/recouped by VA and restored to the client** (plus CAVC `EAJA` fees if won on judicial remand). Draft compliant non-direct-pay or fixed/flat fee agreements when defending *unrecouped* debt balances!

## 4. Domain B — Incompetency & Fiduciary Defense (`38 U.S.C. §§ 5502, 5507, 6107`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.353, 3.105(h)`)

*(Link to verbatim text in [`38-cfr-3.274-3.279-3.353-1.965-pension-competency-waivers.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.274-3.279-3.353-1.965-pension-competency-waivers.md)).*

### A. The Clinical DBQ Trap & Collateral Stakes (`4%` Commissions, Retro Locks & `Pub. L. 118-42` `NICS` Rules)

During `70%` or `100%` PTSD, Major Depressive Disorder, or `DC 8045` TBI C&P exams, examiners routinely equate psychiatric occupational impairment or impulsivity with financial incapacity and check **`"No"`** on the DBQ financial-competency box—triggering three severe consequences:
1. **Involuntary Fiduciary Control & `Up to 4%` Commissions (`38 U.S.C. § 5502(b)`; `38 C.F.R. pt. 13`):** VA strips the veteran of direct control over monthly compensation and **freezes retroactive back-pay lump sums** pending a Fiduciary Hub field exam; if VA appoints a paid professional fiduciary instead of a spouse/family member, that fiduciary can skim **up to `4%` of the veteran's monthly VA benefits**!
2. **Brady Act (`NICS`) Reporting & the March 9, 2024 `Pub. L. 118-42` Judicial-Order Shield:**
   * Historically, a final `§ 3.353` administrative incompetency rating caused VA to report the beneficiary to the FBI's National Instant Criminal Background Check System (`NICS`) as `"adjudicated as a mental defective"` (`18 U.S.C. § 922(g)(4)`), stripping Second Amendment firearm rights without any court hearing.
   * **Current Statutory Restriction (`Pub. L. 118-42`, *Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2024*, enacted Mar. 9, 2024, continued in subsequent appropriations riders):** Congress prohibited VA from using appropriated funds to report a beneficiary to `NICS` based on a VA administrative incompetency/fiduciary determination **unless there is an order or finding of a judge, magistrate, or other judicial authority of competent jurisdiction that such person is a danger to themselves or others**!
   * *Practitioner Warning:* Never grow complacent because of the `Pub. L. 118-42` appropriations rider—appropriations restrictions must be renewed unless permanently codified in Title 38, pre-2024 legacy `NICS` entries may require affirmative relief petitions, and **fiduciary seizure of retroactive back pay + `4%` commissions remain in full force**!

### B. Due-Process Shield (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.105(h), 3.353(e)`) & Benefit-of-the-Doubt Rebuttal (*Sanders*)

1. **Mandatory `60-Day` Predetermination Notice & `30-Day` Hearing Stay (`§§ 3.105(h), 3.105(i), 3.353(e)`):**
   * VA **cannot** finalize an incompetency rating without first issuing a **Proposed Incompetency Notice** detailing the evidence and affording **`60 days`** to submit rebuttal evidence and **`30 days`** to request a **predetermination oral hearing**.
   * **Immediate Action within `30 Days`:** File a written demand for a **`§ 3.105(h)`/`(i)` predetermination hearing** within **`30 days`** of the proposal letter—this **legally bars VA from finalizing incompetency** until the hearing is held and all rebuttal evidence is weighed!
2. **Substantive Standard & Explicit Presumption of Competency (`38 C.F.R. § 3.353(a)–(e)`; *Sanders v. Principi*, `17 Vet. App. 329 (2003)`):**
   * **Definition (`§ 3.353(a)`):** A mentally incompetent person is one who, because of injury or disease, **lacks the mental capacity to contract or to manage his or her own affairs, including disbursement of funds without limitation**. Physical disability alone (quadriplegia, blindness, ALS speech loss) **never** constitutes incompetency!
   * **Sole Rating-Agency Authority (`§ 3.353(b)(1)`):** Rating agencies hold sole authority to make official determinations of competency and incompetency for VA benefit disbursement and insurance purposes—Fiduciary Hub personnel cannot invent incompetency (and advance due-process notice is excused under `§ 3.353(e)` only where a court of competent jurisdiction has already declared the beneficiary incompetent).
   * **Definite Medical Opinion & Consistency Mandate (`§ 3.353(c)`):** Unless medical evidence is *"clear, convincing and leaves no doubt as to the person's incompetency,"* the rating agency **cannot** find incompetency without a *"definite expression regarding the question by the responsible medical authorities,"* weighed across **all evidence of record** with a consistent relationship between disability percentage, commitment/hospitalization facts, and incompetency.
   * **Presumption in Favor of Competency (`§ 3.353(d)`):** Under the plain text of **`38 C.F.R. § 3.353(d)`** and ***Sanders v. Principi*, `17 Vet. App. 329, 336–37 (2003)`**, ***"Where reasonable doubt arises regarding a beneficiary's mental capacity to contract or to manage his or her own affairs, including the disbursement of funds without limitation, such doubt will be resolved in favor of competency."*** Even a `50/50` equipoise between a C&P DBQ check-box and positive real-world financial evidence **compels a finding of competency as a matter of law**!
   * **The Rebuttal Evidentiary Pack:** Counter a `20-minute` C&P check-box by submitting: **(a)** a private treating psychiatrist/psychologist letter specifically addressing `§ 3.353(a)` contractual/financial capacity; **(b)** `6–12` months of clean bank statements, autopay utility/mortgage ledgers, and a clean credit report (`Equifax`/`Experian`/`TransUnion`) showing zero delinquencies; and **(c)** lay declarations from the veteran, spouse, CPA, or banker confirming independent budgeting (note: voluntarily delegating bill-paying mechanics to a spouse while retaining contractual comprehension *proves prudent management*, not incompetency!).
3. **Restoring Competency Anytime (*Sanders* New-Claim Rule) & Mandatory `§ 6107` Misuse Reissuance:**
   * **Restoring Competency (`Sanders`, `17 Vet. App. at 332–33`):** A request to lift an existing incompetency rating and restore competency is **legally treated as an initial/new claim** (analogous to an increased-rating claim)—meaning the veteran can file to restore competency at any time with updated clinical/financial proof!
   * **Temporary Fiduciaries (`38 U.S.C. § 5507(d)`) & Mandatory Reissuance of Misused Funds (`38 U.S.C. §§ 6106–6107`):** Under `§ 5507(d)`, temporary protective fiduciary appointments cannot exceed **`120 days`**. Crucially, if a VA-appointed fiduciary misappropriates or misuses *any* part of a beneficiary's VA benefits (`§ 6106`), **`38 U.S.C. § 6107` (as broadened by `Pub. L. 116-315, § 7005`, `134 Stat. 4932, 5059`, enacted Jan. 5, 2021)** mandates that **VA MUST reissue and pay the full misused amount directly to the beneficiary or successor fiduciary—regardless of whether VA was negligent in vetting or monitoring the rogue fiduciary**!

## 5. Domain C — Needs-Based Wartime Veterans Pension & Survivors Pension with Aid & Attendance (`38 U.S.C. §§ 1502, 1513, 1521, 1541`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.2, 3.3, 3.271–3.279`)

*(Link to verbatim text in [`38-cfr-3.274-3.279-3.353-1.965-pension-competency-waivers.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.274-3.279-3.353-1.965-pension-competency-waivers.md) and pre-/post-2018 regulatory diffs in [`pension-net-worth-and-effective-date-diffs.md`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/pension-net-worth-and-effective-date-diffs.md)).*

### Gate 1 — Qualifying Wartime Active Duty (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.2, 3.3(a)(3)`; `38 U.S.C. § 5303A`)

The veteran must hold a discharge **other than dishonorable** ([Playbook 10](10-character-of-discharge-insanity-upgrades.md)), **no combat or overseas deployment is required**, and active service ([Playbook 11](11-guard-reserve-adt-iadt-and-line-of-duty.md)) must satisfy:
* **Duration:** **`>= 90` days** of active duty (or, if enlisted after **Sept. 7, 1980** / entered as an officer after **Oct. 16, 1981**, completion of the **`24-month`** continuous active-duty minimum or the full period for which called/ordered to active duty under `38 U.S.C. § 5303A`, unless discharged early for disability/hardship); **AND**
* **At Least `1 Day` Within a Statutory Wartime Period (`38 C.F.R. § 3.2`):**
  * **World War II:** `December 7, 1941` – `December 31, 1946`
  * **Korean Conflict:** `June 27, 1950` – `January 31, 1955`
  * **Vietnam Era:** `February 28, 1961` – `May 7, 1975` *(for veterans who served **in the Republic of Vietnam**)*; **`August 5, 1964` – `May 7, 1975`** *(for all other active-duty service anywhere in the world)*
  * **Persian Gulf War:** **`August 2, 1990` – `present`** *(open-ended until terminated by Presidential proclamation or law)*

### Gate 2 — Age `65+` Statutory `P&T` Shortcut (`38 U.S.C. § 1513(a)`), *Chandler* Boundary & Survivors `A&A`

1. **Veterans Age `65 or Older` (`38 U.S.C. § 1513(a)`, enacted by `Pub. L. 107-103, § 207(a)` [2001]):**
   * Wartime veterans **age `65 or older`** are **statutorily exempted** from proving permanent and total (`P&T`) non-service-connected disability or unemployability to qualify for **basic Veterans Pension (`§ 1521(a)`)**! *(Note: Under `Pub. L. 110-157` [2007], VA cross-matches IRS/SSA and National Directory of New Hires [`NDNH`] wage databases to verify income).*
   * **Pairing Age `65+` with Regular Aid & Attendance (`§ 1521(d)`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.351(b)–(c), 3.352(a)`):** Once a `65+` wartime veteran submits **`VA Form 21-2680`** proving a need for **Regular Aid & Attendance** (`>= 2` `ADL` deficits, daily cognitive/dementia safety supervision against hazards of the daily environment, `5/200` blindness, **OR** status as a **patient in a nursing home** for mental/physical incapacity under `§ 3.351(c)(2)`), the veteran unlocks the **maximum Veterans `A&A` `MAPR` tier** (`$29,093/yr` = **`$2,424/mo`** single veteran; `$34,488/yr` = **`$2,874/mo`** veteran with `1` dependent, effective Dec. 1, 2025)!
   * **The *Hartness* -> *Chandler* Trap on `§ 1521(e)` Housebound SMP:** While *Hartness v. Nicholson*, `20 Vet. App. 216 (2006)` had briefly allowed `65+` veterans to claim `§ 1521(e)` Housebound Pension without a single `100%` disability, the Federal Circuit **overruled *Hartness* in *Chandler v. Shinseki*, `676 F.3d 1045, 1050–52 (Fed. Cir. 2012)`**, holding that `§ 1513(a)` waives `P&T` only for `§ 1521(a)`—meaning `§ 1521(e)` Housebound SMP still requires **either** a single permanent `100%` disability (`+ 60%` separate OR permanent confinement to the dwelling/premises). Whenever an elderly client cannot meet `§ 1521(e)`'s single-`100%` hurdle, target **`§ 1521(d)` Regular Aid & Attendance** instead!
2. **Surviving Spouses (`38 U.S.C. § 1541`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.351(b)–(c)`):**
   * An unremarried surviving spouse of a wartime veteran needs **zero disability and zero minimum age** to qualify for basic Survivors Pension (`$11,699/yr` eff. Dec. 1, 2025), and qualifies for the **maximum Survivors `A&A` `MAPR` (`$18,697/yr` = `$1,558/mo` eff. Dec. 1, 2025)** upon **`VA Form 21-2680`** proof of Regular A&A or nursing-home patient status!

### Gate 3 — Bright-Line Net Worth & `36-Month` Transfer Lookback (`83 FR 47246`, eff. Oct. 18, 2018; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.274–3.276`)

Effective **October 18, 2018** (`83 FR 47246`, pub. Sept. 18, 2018), VA replaced subjective net-worth adjudication with an objective Medicaid-indexed formula:

$$\text{VA Net Worth} = \text{Covered Assets (Veteran + Spouse)} + \text{Annual Income (IVAP after § 3.278 UME Deductions!)}$$

* **Annual COLA-Indexed Bright-Line Limit (`§ 3.274(a)–(b)`):** Tied to the Medicaid maximum Community Spouse Resource Allowance (`CSRA`) and adjusted every **December 1** by the Social Security COLA: `$123,600` (`Oct. 18, 2018`), `$159,240` (`Dec. 1, 2024 – Nov. 30, 2025`), and **`$163,699` (`Dec. 1, 2025 – Nov. 30, 2026`)**.
* **Exempt Assets Excluded From Net Worth (`38 C.F.R. § 3.275(b)`):**
  1. **Primary Residence + Marketable Lot up to `2 Acres` (`87,120 sq. ft.`):** Remains **`100%` exempt regardless of dollar value**—and **stays `100%` exempt even after the veteran or surviving spouse moves into an assisted-living facility or nursing home**! *(Crucial Trap: Rental income from the home counts toward gross income, and if the home is **sold**, the net cash proceeds immediately convert into countable assets unless reinvested into another primary residence within the **same calendar year** under `§ 3.275(b)(1)`!)* Acreage exceeding `2 acres` is countable *unless* unmarketable due to zoning restrictions, lack of access, or conservation easements.
  2. **Personal Effects & Family Transportation Vehicles:** Household goods, clothing, jewelry, and vehicles used for family transportation (excluding speculative collectibles, boats, or RVs).
* **The `36-Month` Asset-Transfer Lookback & Penalty Formula (`38 C.F.R. § 3.276`):**
  * Applies **only** to transfers for **less than fair market value (`< FMV`)**—gifts to children, transfers into irrevocable trusts, or purchases of non-liquid annuities where the claimant cannot liquidate principal—executed **within `36 months` prior to claim receipt AND on or after October 18, 2018**.
  * **The `"Covered Asset"` Exemption Shield (`§ 3.276(a)(2), (b)` — Huge Practitioner Win!):** VA **does NOT penalize every gift**! A penalty attaches **only to `"covered assets"`**—the portion of transferred assets that, had they been retained, **would have pushed total Net Worth over the `$163,699` bright-line cap**! *(Example: Widow has `$50,000` in savings, `$0` IVAP after memory-care UMEs, and gifted `$40,000` to her daughter `12 months` ago. Had she kept the `$40,000`, her Net Worth would be `$90,000 < $163,699` -> **Covered Asset = `$0` -> ZERO transfer penalty**!)*
  * **Penalty Divisor & `5-Year` Cap (`§ 3.276(e)`):** Divide excess transferred covered assets by the **Monthly Penalty Divisor** = **`MAPR for a veteran with 1 dependent in need of A&A ÷ 12`** (**`$2,874/month`** eff. Dec. 1, 2025 [`$34,488 ÷ 12`]; `$2,795/month` in the 2025 rate year), **drop fractional months** (round down!), cap at **`60 months` (`5 years`)**, and start the penalty on the **first day of the month following the last transfer**. Under `§ 3.276(e)(5)`, returning all or part of the gifted assets within **`60 days`** of penalty notice (with `90 days` to submit proof) recalculates or extinguishes the penalty!

### Gate 4 — Unreimbursed Medical Expense (`UME`) Zeroing of `IVAP` (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.278–3.279`)

Many middle-class wartime families mistakenly assume `$40,000–$60,000/year` in Social Security and retirement pensions disqualifies them from VA Pension. Walk every family through the **`§ 3.278` `UME` Dual-Zeroing Math** (using **Dec. 1, 2025 – Nov. 30, 2026** rates):

1. **Facts:** Widowed Korean/Vietnam wartime veteran (single, no dependents) has **`$48,000/year`** (`$4,000/month`) in Social Security + pension income and **`$130,000`** in bank savings. Without care costs, `$48,000` income + `$130,000` savings = **`$178,000` Net Worth** (`> $163,699` cap -> **denied**!) and `$48,000 > $29,093` `MAPR` (**`$0` benefit**!).
2. **Enter Memory Care / Assisted Living (`VA Form 21-2680` + `VA Form 21P-8416`):** Veteran moves into memory care costing **`$66,000/year`** (`$5,500/month`). Under **`38 C.F.R. § 3.278(d)(3)`**, **`100%` of assisted-living / memory-care room, board, and care charges count as deductible `UME`s** whenever the facility provides or contracts for health care or custodial care (`§ 3.278(d)(3)(iv)(A)`)—or a physician, PA, certified nurse practitioner (`CNP`), or clinical nurse specialist (`CNS`) certifies in writing on `VA Form 21-2680` / clinical statement that the claimant must reside in the protected facility to obtain third-party/family care (`§ 3.278(d)(3)(iii)–(iv)`)! Medicare Part B/D & Medigap premiums also count (`+$2,400/yr` -> `$68,400` total UMEs; even using `$66,000` alone):
   * **`5%` Basic `MAPR` Deductible (`§ 3.272(g)`):** `5% × $17,441` (single veteran basic `MAPR`) = **`$872`**.
   * **Deductible `UME`s:** `$66,000 - $872` = **`$65,128`**.
   * **Countable Income (`IVAP`):** `$48,000 (Gross Income) - $65,128 (Deductible UMEs)` = **`-$17,128` -> floored at `$0` `IVAP`!**
3. **Dual Unlock Result:**
   * **Net Worth Drops Below Cap:** `$130,000 (Assets) + $0 (IVAP)` = **`$130,000 < $163,699`** (**Net Worth PASSED!**).
   * **`100%` Maximum Tax-Free `A&A` `MAPR` Payable:** `$29,093 (Max A&A MAPR) - $0 (IVAP)` = **`$29,093/year` (`$2,424/month` tax-free!)** paid every month!

### E. Compensation vs. Pension Auto-Switching (`38 U.S.C. § 5304(a)`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.700(a)(4), 3.701`)

Under `38 U.S.C. § 5304(a)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(4)`, a veteran cannot receive Disability Compensation and Wartime Pension concurrently—**but NEVER needs to forfeit or sever service connection to collect the higher `A&A` Pension**!
* **Assumed Greater-Benefit Payment (`38 C.F.R. § 3.701(a)–(c)`; `M21-1, Part VI, Subpart ii, 1.A.2.a`):** While `§ 3.701(a)–(c)` preserves a beneficiary's right to elect either benefit in writing (`(b)`) and bars retroactive switching prior to election receipt except as otherwise provided (`(c)`), under `M21-1, Part VI, Subpart ii, 1.A.2.a` when entitlement to both Compensation and Pension is established VA **assumes election of and pays the greater benefit**—e.g., paying **`$2,424/mo` (`$2,874/mo` if married) in Wartime `A&A` Pension** over a `20%` (`~$347/mo`) or `40%` (`~$774/mo`) SC award while keeping all service-connected ratings intact on the Rating Code Sheet! *(Best Practice: Always include an explicit written statement electing the greater benefit alongside `VA Form 21P-527EZ` to satisfy `§ 3.701(b)–(c)` strictly).*
* **Two-Stage Bridge Strategy:** File **`VA Form 21P-527EZ` + `21-2680` + `21P-8416`** immediately to start `$2,424–$2,874/month` cashflow within months while simultaneously prosecuting a PACT Act / secondary / `100%` + `SMC(l)` compensation claim ([Playbook 12](12-aid-and-attendance-smc-l-r1-r2-and-smc-t.md)). The moment `100%` SC + `SMC(l)` (`$4,900.83+/mo`) grants, VA automatically switches to the higher `SMC` award and pays the **net retroactive difference** above the Pension dollars already paid!

## 6. Invariants / Kill-Switches

* **Never Plead Waiver Without Contesting Debt Creation (*Schaper* / *Narron*):** Always pair Track 1 (`§ 3.105(e)` voidness + `§ 5112(b)(10)` sole administrative error) with Track 2 (`§ 5302(a)` waiver). If the `1-year` waiver clock already expired, Track 1 debt-invalidity remains alive!
* **Hard `§ 5302(c)` / `§ 1.965(b)` Fraud & Bad-Faith Bar:** Any finding of fraud, intentional misrepresentation, or bad faith kills equitable waiver outright—anchor every `VA Form 5655` submission in documentary proof of good-faith disclosure or honest confusion.
* **`§ 5904(c)(1)` Initial-Claim Fee Prohibition on Pension & *Gumpenberger* Debt-Fee Limit:** Under `38 U.S.C. § 5904(c)(1)`, **attorneys and agents are strictly prohibited from charging a fee to prepare or file an initial `VA Form 21P-527EZ` or `21P-534EZ` Pension application** prior to an initial AOJ decision on that claim (fees attach only to post-AOJ-decision `0995`/`0996`/`10182` appeals!). And under *Gumpenberger*, `973 F.3d at 1384–85`, direct-pay `§ 5904(d)` contingency fees never attach to unwithheld debt balances.
* **Post-Oct. 18, 2018 Asset-Protection Trusts & Annuities Trap (`§ 3.276`):** Transferring excess covered assets (`> $163,699` net worth) into an irrevocable trust or immediate annuity within `36 months` before filing `21P-527EZ`/`534EZ` triggers up to a **`60-month` penalty**! Always run the `UME -> IVAP -> Net Worth` worksheet *before* any family executes a gift or trust transfer.

## 7. Postconditions: Effective Dates (`§ 5110(b)(4)` / `§ 5110(d)` / `§ 3.31`), Medicaid `$90` Cap & Post-Grant Audit

1. **Exact Pension Effective-Date & `§ 3.31` Payment-Date Rules ([`effective-dates-and-staged-ratings-treatise.md`](../docs/effective-dates-and-staged-ratings-treatise.md)):**
   * **Veterans Pension (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(a), (b)(4)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.400(b)(1)(ii)`):** Defaults to **date of receipt of `ITF` (`VA Form 21-0966`) or `21P-527EZ`**—*except* under **`§ 5110(b)(4)` / `§ 3.400(b)(1)(ii)(B)`**, where a veteran who files within **`1 year`** of becoming `P&T` disabled AND proves that an **incapacitating disability** (stroke, dementia crisis, ICU stay) **prevented filing a claim for at least the first `30 days`** wins up to a **`1-year` retroactive effective date**! *(Remember `38 C.F.R. § 3.31`: actual monthly cash payment commences on the **first day of the calendar month following** the effective date).*
   * **Survivors Pension `1-Year` Post-Death Lookback (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(d)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.400(c)(2)`):** If `VA Form 21P-534EZ` (or an `ITF` perfected within `1 year`) is received within **`1 year` of the veteran's death**, the effective date reaches all the way back to the **first day of the month in which the veteran died**!
2. **Medicaid Nursing-Home `$90/Month` Reduction (`38 U.S.C. § 5503(d)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.551(i)`):**
   * If a **single** veteran having **neither spouse nor child** (or a surviving spouse having **no child**) resides in a **Medicaid-approved nursing facility** where Medicaid pays part/all of the stay, VA Pension (including `A&A`) reduces to **`$90/month`** (retained by the resident as a personal-needs allowance, exempt from Medicaid nursing-home share-of-cost!) effective no sooner than the last day of the calendar month following the month Medicaid coverage begins. *(Private-pay assisted living / memory care **never** triggers the `$90` cap!)*
3. **Mandatory Post-Grant Audit ([`award-letter-and-withholding-audit-checklist.md`](../checklists/award-letter-and-withholding-audit-checklist.md)):**
   * Upon any debt-cancellation/refund, restored competency release of withheld funds, `§ 6107` misuse reissuance, or retroactive `Ch. 15` `A&A` award, verify dollar-for-dollar restoration of previously recouped installments, termination of Treasury (`TOP`) referrals, removal of fiduciary `4%` fee codes, accurate `UME` projection carry-forwards (`§ 3.272(g)`), and compliant `§ 5904` / `EAJA` fee accounting ([Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).
