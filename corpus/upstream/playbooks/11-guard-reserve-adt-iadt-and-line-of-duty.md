# Playbook 11: Guard & Reserve Duty Status (`ADT` vs. `IADT`, `§ 3.6`), `Title 10`/`32` Orders, Line of Duty (`§ 3.1(m)`) & Secondary Substance Abuse (*Allen v. Principi*)

National Guard and Reserve claims fail at the Regional Office (`AOJ`) far more often than Regular Component claims—not because entitlement is absent, but because raters misread **`NGB Form 22`** state separation summaries as proof of *"zero active service,"* conflate **`Title 32` full-time operational Guard duty (`32 U.S.C. § 502(f)`)** with non-federal **State Active Duty (`SAD`)**, overlook favorable binding **Line of Duty (`LOD`)** determinations (`38 C.F.R. § 3.1(m)`), or unlawfully reject secondary alcohol/drug-related organ damage caused by service-connected PTSD or chronic pain (*Allen v. Principi*, `237 F.3d 1368 (Fed. Cir. 2001)`).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Pair With COD Screening & `§ 3.156(c)` Reconsideration:** Whenever a Guard/Reserve file also involves an `OTH`/`BCD` or multi-period active/reserve service, run [Playbook 10](10-character-of-discharge-insanity-upgrades.md), [`38-cfr-3.12-3.13-3.354-cod-insanity.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.12-3.13-3.354-cod-insanity.md), [`character-of-discharge-3.12-evolution.md`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/character-of-discharge-3.12-evolution.md), and the [`COD & Guard/Reserve Intake Audit`](../checklists/cod-and-guard-reserve-intake-audit.md). Crucially, because Guard/Reserve unit orders and `LOD` packets are notoriously absent from initial VA STR pulls, **associating missing `LOD` or `Title 32` orders decades later triggers `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)` reconsideration** back to the original denied claim ([Playbook 03](03-service-department-records-38-cfr-3-156c.md))!

## 1. Preconditions: The `NGB-22` C-File Trap & Guard/Reserve Record Triggers

Before evaluating the medical merits of any Guard or Reserve claim, audit the VBMS eFolder against the member's state Adjutant General (`TAG`) / Reserve Personnel Center (`ARPC`, `HQ RIO`, `PERS-9`, `CG PSC`) holdings ([VA Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md)):

* **The Classic `NGB Form 22` C-File Trap:**
  * **`NGB Form 22`** (*Report of Separation and Record of Service*) documents **only termination from a state National Guard enlistment**—it is **NOT** a federal `DD Form 214` and frequently omits individual `ADT` school tours, `Title 32 § 502(f)` operational orders, `IADT` drill injuries, and short `Title 10` call-ups!
  * Whenever a prior VA denial states *"No qualifying active duty shown on NGB-22,"* immediately FOIA/request:
    1. All **`DD Form 214`s** (issued for `IADT`/`AIT` basic training `>= 90` days, `Title 10` federal mobilizations, and `>= 90`-day `Title 32` active-duty tours);
    2. Complete **`Title 10` (`10 U.S.C. §§ 12301–12304`)** and **`Title 32` (`32 U.S.C. §§ 316, 502–505`)** movement/activation orders, travel vouchers (`DD Form 1351-2`), and **Retirement Point Accounting (`NGB Form 23` / `ARPC Form 249-2-E` / `LES` pay stubs)** proving exact `ADT` vs. `IADT` drill dates; and
    3. Formal and informal **Line of Duty (`LOD`) Investigation Packets**: Army **`DA Form 2173`** (*Statement of Medical Examination and Duty Status*), Air Force **`AF Form 348`**, DoD formal **`DD Form 261`** (*Report of Investigation — Line of Duty and Misconduct Status*), or Navy/USMC **`JAGMAN` LOD** endorsements, plus civilian emergency room transport records from drill weekends!
* **Line-of-Duty & Substance-Use Red Flags:**
  * Any post-October 31, 1990 denial citing `38 U.S.C. § 105(a)` or `38 C.F.R. § 3.301(d)` (*"alcohol/drug abuse is willful misconduct"*) where the veteran **already held (or simultaneously established) service connection** for PTSD, major depression, TBI, MST, or chronic orthopedic/neuropathic pain!

## 3. Proof Elements & Doctrinal Mechanics

### A. The Three-Tier Duty Matrix (`38 U.S.C. §§ 101(21)–(24), 106(d)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(a)–(e)`)

Under `38 U.S.C. § 101(2)` and `§ 101(24)` (see [Three-Era Title 38 Statutory Crosswalk](../docs/title-38-statutory-crosswalk.md)), *"active military, naval, air, or space service"* encompasses **three distinct tiers** with dramatically different disease, injury, and presumption rules:

| Duty Tier & Statute / Regulation | Qualifying Orders / Service | Compensable Events Covered | Presumption of Soundness (`38 U.S.C. § 1111`)? | Portal-to-Portal Travel (`38 U.S.C. § 106(d)`; `§ 3.6(e)`)? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: `Active Duty`** (`38 U.S.C. § 101(21)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(b)`) | Regular Component full-time duty; **Reserve/Guard `Title 10` federal call-up** (`10 U.S.C. §§ 12301–12304`, *excluding* training-only orders); **AGR (`Title 10`)**; Academy cadets/midshipmen. | **Injury OR Disease** incurred or aggravated in line of duty + full `§ 3.309` / PACT presumptions (subject to minimum service days). | **YES (`§ 1111` & *Wagner*)** — VA must prove *both* preexistence AND non-aggravation by clear & unmistakable evidence! | Covered round-the-clock (`24/7`) from induction to separation (unless AWOL/confined under `§ 3.1(m)`). |
| **Tier 2: `Active Duty for Training (ADT / ACDUTRA)`** (`38 U.S.C. §§ 101(22), 101(24)(B)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(c)`) | Reserve `AT` (2-week annual training), **`IADT`/`AIT` basic/initial skill schools**, service schools, and **National Guard full-time `Title 32 U.S.C. §§ 316, 502, 503, 504, 505` duty** (`§ 3.6(c)(3)`), including **`Title 32 § 502(f)`** operational missions & `Title 32 AGR`! | **Injury OR Disease** incurred or aggravated in line of duty during the `ADT` window. | **NO (`Biggins`; `Paulson`; `Smith`)!** Claimant must first prove `§ 101(24)` veteran status by proving in-`ADT` incurrence or `§ 3.306` aggravation! | **YES (`§ 3.6(e)`)** — Authorized direct travel to/from `ADT` covers **injury** OR acute MI, cardiac arrest, or stroke! |
| **Tier 3: `Inactive Duty Training (IADT / INACDUTRA)`** (`38 U.S.C. §§ 101(23), 101(24)(C), 106(d)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(a)(3), (d), (e)`) | Weekend drills / Battle Assemblies (`37 U.S.C. § 206`), unit training assemblies (`UTA`s), prescribed additional training/flight drills, and **National Guard `32 U.S.C. §§ 316, 502–505` drills**. *(Excludes work/study in connection with correspondence courses & muster duty).* | **STRICTLY:** **(1) `"Injury"`** incurred/aggravated in line of duty, **OR (2) Acute myocardial infarction, cardiac arrest, or cerebrovascular accident (`stroke`)** (`§§ 101(24)(C), 106(d)`, `§ 3.6(a)(3), (e)(3)`, eff. Nov. 1, 2000). **NO general chronic disease!** | **NO** — Claimant bears burden of proving in-`IADT` injury (or `§ 101(24)(C)`/`§ 106(d)` cardiac/stroke event) or aggravation. | **YES (`§ 3.6(e)(1)–(3)`)** — Authorized direct travel to/from `IADT` covers **injury** OR acute MI, cardiac arrest, or stroke! |

#### Critical Doctrinal Nuances Inside the Duty Matrix:

1. **The `ADT` Presumption-of-Soundness Catch-22 (*Biggins*, *Paulson*, *Smith*, *Donnellan* & *Acciola*):**
   * Because `38 U.S.C. § 1111` grants the presumption of soundness only to a *"veteran,"* and a Guard/Reserve member with **pure `ADT` service** becomes a *"veteran"` under `38 U.S.C. §§ 101(2), 101(24)(B)` **only if disabled by an injury or disease incurred or aggravated during `ADT`**, the CAVC holds that **`§ 1111` soundness does NOT apply to `ADT` claims unless veteran status is first independently established** (*Biggins v. Derwinski*, `1 Vet. App. 474, 477–78 (1991)`; *Paulson v. Brown*, `7 Vet. App. 466, 469–70 (1995)`; *Acciola v. Peake*, `22 Vet. App. 320, 324 (2008)`; *Smith v. Shinseki*, `24 Vet. App. 40, 46–48 (2010)`).
   * **The Aggravation Trap (*Donnellan v. Shinseki*, `24 Vet. App. 167, 173–75 (2010)`):** For a preexisting condition claimed as aggravated during `ADT` (`38 U.S.C. § 1153`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.306(a)`), the claimant cannot rely on `§ 3.306(b)`'s clear-and-unmistakable rebuttal shield; instead, **the claimant must affirmatively prove** (under standard equipoise `§ 5107(b)`) **permanent worsening during `ADT` beyond natural progression** (not a mere temporary flare-up of symptoms!).
   * **Practitioner Escape Hatch:** Always check whether the client **also** had a separate period of **Tier 1 `Title 10` Active Duty**! Once a claimant establishes `ADT` incurrence/aggravation (or analyzes a Tier 1 `Title 10` tour), full `§ 1111` *Wagner v. Principi*, `370 F.3d 1089 (Fed. Cir. 2004)` protections attach to every Tier 1 `Active Duty` period!
2. **`IADT` Cardiac/Stroke Expansion (`Pub. L. 106-419, § 301`, eff. Nov. 1, 2000; `38 U.S.C. §§ 101(24)(C), 106(d)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(a)(3), (e)(2)–(3)`):**
   * Prior to November 1, 2000 (`66 FR 48558, 48560`, Sept. 21, 2001; `67 FR 49584, 49585`, July 31, 2002), a Guardsman who suffered a massive heart attack or stroke during a drill PT test was denied because atherosclerotic heart/cerebrovascular disease was classified as a `"disease"` rather than an `"injury."` Under `38 U.S.C. §§ 101(24)(C), 106(d)(1), (4)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(a)(3), (e)(2)–(3)`, **acute myocardial infarction, cardiac arrest, and cerebrovascular accident (`stroke`)** occurring during `IADT` (or portal-to-portal travel) are statutorily compensable!
   * **Framing Orthopedic, Acoustic, Assault (`MST`), & Toxic Traumas on `IADT` as `"Injury"`:** Remember that under `§ 3.6(a)(3)` and `(d)`, physical trauma, **acoustic trauma / noise-induced sensorineural damage**, heatstroke/frostbite, lightning/electrical shock, **Military Sexual Trauma (`MST` physical/sexual assault — `M21-1` recognizes personal/sexual assault trauma during `IADT` as an `"injury"` causing PTSD!)**, and acute chemical/toxic cellular trauma (e.g., *C-123* Agent Orange reserve aircrew `38 C.F.R. § 3.307(a)(6)(v)`) can establish an in-`IADT` **"injury"**!
3. **Portal-to-Portal Direct Travel Rules (`38 U.S.C. § 106(d)(1)–(4)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(e)(1)–(3)`):**
   * Covers injury (or `§ 3.6(e)(3)` `"covered disease"`: acute MI / cardiac arrest / stroke) incurred **while proceeding directly to or returning directly from** authorized/required `ADT` or `IADT` (`§ 3.6(e)(1)–(2)`).
   * Under **`38 U.S.C. § 106(d)(2)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(e)(2)`**, VA weighs six factors: `(i)` hour of departure, `(ii)` scheduled arrival/release hour, `(iii)` mode of travel, `(iv)` itinerary (no major personal detours/frolics!), `(v)` manner of travel, and `(vi)` immediate cause of disability/death. **Statutory Burden Warning (`38 U.S.C. § 106(d)(3)`):** Unlike regular in-camp LOD presumptions, **`38 U.S.C. § 106(d)(3)` places the affirmative burden of proof on the claimant** in portal-to-portal travel claims—document exact Google Maps route miles, drill schedule sign-in/out rosters, and police crash timestamps!

### B. National Guard `Title 10` vs. `Title 32` (`§ 502(f)`) vs. State Active Duty (`SAD`) & The PACT Act

1. **Decoding the Three National Guard Authorities:**
   * **Federal Mobilization (`Title 10 U.S.C. §§ 12301(a), 12302, 12304`):** Federalized **Tier 1 `Active Duty`** (`§ 3.6(b)`). Confers full veteran status, `DD-214`, `§ 1111` presumption of soundness, and overseas `§ 1119` / `§ 1120` Gulf War / post-9/11 burn-pit presumptions.
   * **Full-Time National Guard Duty (`Title 32 U.S.C. §§ 316, 502, 503, 504, 505`):** Federally funded duty under state Governor command—classified by **`38 U.S.C. § 101(22)(C)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(c)(3)` as Tier 2 `Active Duty for Training (ADT)`** (even when operational rather than classroom training!).
     * **`32 U.S.C. § 502(f)` Operational Missions:** Post-9/11 airport security (`Operation Noble Eagle`), Southwest Border missions, FEMA hurricane/disaster response (Katrina, Maria), DC Capitol security, and **2020–2022 COVID-19 pandemic response** were overwhelmingly executed on **`Title 32 U.S.C. § 502(f)`** orders! Raters who mistake `§ 502(f)` orders for "State Active Duty" commit clear legal error—**both injuries AND diseases** (including severe COVID-19 residuals, orthopedic injuries, and PTSD) incurred or aggravated on `Title 32 § 502(f)` orders establish full service connection (`§ 3.6(c)(3)`)! *(Also check `38 C.F.R. § 3.7(o)` for Guard service where a disability was incurred).*
   * **Pure State Active Duty (`SAD`):** Activated strictly by a state Governor under **state law and 100% state funds** (e.g., local blizzard/riot call-up not converted to federal `Title 32 § 502(f)` FEMA reimbursement). **Not** qualifying federal service under Title 38 (remedy lies in state workers' compensation)—**always verify whether FEMA/DoD retroactively converted initial `SAD` orders into `Title 32 § 502(f)` orders** (as happened nationwide in March 2020 for COVID-19!).
2. **PACT Act (`Pub. L. 117-168`) Toxic Exposure Expansions for Guard & Reserve (`38 U.S.C. §§ 1119, 1168`):**
   * **Presumptive Burn-Pit / Herbicide Deployments (`§§ 1116, 1119, 1120`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.320`):** Guard/Reserve units deployed on `Title 10` (or qualifying `ADT` in covered Thailand/Guam/Johnston Atoll/SW Asia/Djibouti airspace or waters) receive identical `23+` burn-pit and expanded herbicide presumptions ([Playbook 06](06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md)).
   * **Toxic Exposure Risk Activity (`TERA`) Participation (`38 U.S.C. § 1168`):** Section `303` of the PACT Act expanded `TERA` lookup (` DoD ILER` Individual Longitudinal Exposure Record) to Guard and Reserve members who were exposed to PFAS firefighting foam (`AFFF`) at Guard airbases, jet fuel (`JP-8`), solvents (`TCE`), depleted uranium, burn pits, or radiation during **`Active Duty`, `ADT`, or `IADT`**, mandating a `§ 1168` VA toxic-exposure medical nexus opinion when disability + `TERA` participation are shown (while still respecting `§ 3.6(d)` `IADT` injury vs. `ADT` injury/disease thresholds!).

### C. Line of Duty (`38 U.S.C. § 105(a)`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.1(m)–(n), 3.301`) & Secondary Substance Abuse (*Allen v. Principi*)

1. **Asymmetric Binding Force of Service-Department `LOD` Determinations (`38 C.F.R. § 3.1(m)–(n)`):**
   * **Presumption of Line of Duty (`38 U.S.C. § 105(a)`):** Every injury or disease incurred during active military, naval, air, or space service is **presumed** to have been incurred in line of duty unless caused by the veteran's own **willful misconduct** or (for claims filed after October 31, 1990) **abuse of alcohol or drugs**, or incurred while deserting / materially AWOL (`§ 3.1(m)(1)`) or confined under court-martial/civil sentence (`§ 3.1(m)(2)`).
   * **THE ASYMMETRIC BINDING RULE (`§ 3.1(m)` & `§ 3.1(n)` — Massive Litigation Weapon!):**
     * **Favorable Military `LOD` BINDS VA:** Under the plain text of `38 C.F.R. § 3.1(m)` and `§ 3.1(n)`, a service-department finding that an injury, disease, or death **occurred `"in line of duty"`** (or was **not** due to misconduct) ***"will be binding on the Department of Veterans Affairs unless it is patently inconsistent with the requirements of laws administered by the Department of Veterans Affairs"*** (e.g., where the military found `"In LOD"` despite undisputed `0.22` BAC single-car intoxication prohibited by `38 U.S.C. § 105(a)`). Ignoring a valid favorable `DA Form 2173` or `AF Form 348` in the C-file is **CUE**!
     * **Unfavorable Military `"Not in LOD"` Does NOT Bind VA:** Notice the one-way wording of `§ 3.1(m)–(n)`! If a Guard commander summarily checked `"Not in Line of Duty"` (or failed to complete an `LOD` packet at all), VA is **NOT** bound by that adverse/missing finding and **must** independently adjudicate line of duty under `38 U.S.C. § 105(a)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.301`!

2. **Willful Misconduct vs. The Complete Bitemporal History of Substance-Abuse Claims (`1944–Present`):**
   * **General Willful Misconduct (`§ 3.1(n)(1)–(3)`; `§ 3.301(a)–(c)`):** Requires deliberate or intentional wrongdoing with knowledge of—or **wanton and reckless disregard** of—its probable consequences, which is the **proximate cause** of the injury (`§ 3.1(n)(3)`). Mere negligence, carelessness, poor judgment, or technical violation of traffic/police ordinances (`§ 3.1(n)(2)`) is **NOT** willful misconduct! Simple drinking of alcohol is not willful misconduct unless acute intoxication proximately and immediately causes the accident (`§ 3.301(c)(2)`).
   * **Era 1 (`Pre-November 1, 1990` Claims):** Under `former § 3.301(c)(2)`, while *primary* alcoholism was treated as misconduct for standalone ratings (*Traynor v. Turnage*, `485 U.S. 535 (1988)`), **organic diseases resulting from chronic use of alcohol** (e.g., alcoholic cirrhosis incurred in service!) were expressly **compensable** (`"will not be considered of willful misconduct origin"`) on claims filed **on or before October 31, 1990**!
   * **Era 2 (`November 1, 1990` `OBRA 1990` Bar — `Pub. L. 101-508, § 8052`):** Effective for **claims filed after October 31, 1990**, Congress amended `38 U.S.C. §§ 105(a), 1110, 1131` (implemented at `38 C.F.R. § 3.301(d)`, `60 FR 27407, 27408`, May 24, 1995; gender-neutral wording update at `87 FR 26125`, May 3, 2022) to bar line-of-duty / direct service connection for **primary** abuse of alcohol or drugs (and organic diseases caused by *primary* voluntary substance abuse). *(Note: `66 FR 18195, 18198` [Apr. 6, 2001] added the `§ 3.300(c)` tobacco cross-reference to `§ 3.310(a)`, while `71 FR 44913, 44918` [Aug. 8, 2006] was an unrelated omnibus rulemaking that did not amend `§ 3.301` or `§ 3.310`).*
   * **Era 3 (`1999–2001` Overreach & *Allen v. Principi* Breakthrough):**
     * In [`VAOPGCPREC 7-99`](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md) (`June 9, 1999`), VA OGC unlawfully overextended `OBRA 1990` to bar **secondary** service connection even when service-connected PTSD caused alcohol abuse and fatal cirrhosis.
     * In ***Allen v. Principi*, `237 F.3d 1368, 1376–81 (Fed. Cir. 2001)`**, *reh'g denied*, `268 F.3d 1340`, the Federal Circuit **invalidated `VAOPGCPREC 7-99` in core part**, holding that `38 U.S.C. § 1110` precludes compensation **only** for willful-misconduct / **primary** alcohol or drug abuse—it **does NOT bar compensation when alcohol or drug abuse is a secondary disability (or symptom) proximately caused or aggravated (`Allen v. Brown`, `7 Vet. App. 439 (1995) (en banc)`) by a service-connected mental or physical condition under `38 C.F.R. § 3.310(a)–(b)`** (`71 FR 52744`, pub. Sept. 7, 2006, eff. Oct. 10, 2006)!
   * **How to Plead *Allen v. Principi* Secondary Substance-Use Chains Today:**
     1. **Mental-Disorder Rating Incorporation:** Where an SC mental disorder (`PTSD`, `MDD`, `TBI`) causes self-medication (`Alcohol Use Disorder` / `Opioid Use Disorder`), the substance use disorder cannot be rated separately from the mental disorder (`§ 4.130` pyramiding rule), **but its occupational/social impairment merges directly into a higher `70%` or `100%` `§ 4.130` evaluation**!
     2. **High-Value Physical Organ & Intermediate-Step Chains (`§ 3.310(a)–(b)`):** Once medical nexus evidence shows an SC condition (`PTSD`, `anxiety`, **or chronic intractable SC spine/radiculopathy pain!**) caused or aggravated alcohol, opioid, or nicotine dependence (`38 C.F.R. § 3.300(b)` for secondary tobacco claims!), **every resulting physical organ disability is separately compensable**, including:
        * **Alcoholic cirrhosis / chronic liver disease / portal hypertension / hepatocellular carcinoma**;
        * **Chronic pancreatitis & secondary diabetes mellitus**;
        * **Alcoholic cardiomyopathy, atrial fibrillation, & hypertension**;
        * **Alcoholic peripheral neuropathy & cerebellar ataxia**;
        * **Obesity / weight-gain intermediate chains (`VAOPGCPREC 1-2017`) -> Obstructive Sleep Apnea (`OSA`) or GERD**; and
        * **Survivor DIC (`38 U.S.C. § 1310`)** for fatal cirrhosis or accidental overdose secondary to SC PTSD/pain ([Playbook 08](08-survivor-dic-direct-and-1318-hypothetical.md))!

## 4. Invariants / Kill-Switches

* **Routine `IADT` Chronic-Disease Kill-Switch (`38 U.S.C. § 101(24)(C)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(a)(3), (d), (e)`):** Never plead direct chronic disease onset (e.g., essential hypertension, hypothyroidism, idiopathic MS, or osteoarthritis without trauma) during a 2-day `IADT` drill weekend—`§ 3.6(a)(3)` and `(e)(3)` cover **only `"injury"`** or **`§ 101(24)(C)` / `§ 106(d)` acute myocardial infarction, cardiac arrest, or stroke**. Check immediately whether symptoms manifested or worsened during a 2-week **`ADT` (`AT`)** tour or **`Title 32 § 502(f)`** order (`§ 3.6(a)(2), (c)` covers diseases!), or arose from an acute drill **injury**!
* **Pure `ADT` Soundness Mispleading (*Smith* & *Donnellan*):** Never argue in a CAVC or Board brief that VA failed to rebut the `§ 1111` presumption of soundness on a pure `ADT` claim where the claimant lacks independent veteran status—you will lose under *Smith v. Shinseki*, `24 Vet. App. 40 (2010)` and *Donnellan v. Shinseki*, `24 Vet. App. 167 (2010)`. Instead, develop an affirmative private IMO proving permanent `§ 3.306(a)` aggravation beyond natural progression during the `ADT` tour!
* **Pure Governor-Funded State Active Duty (`SAD`):** Confirm federal funding/statutory authority (`Title 10` or `Title 32 U.S.C. §§ 316, 502–505`) before litigating; pure state-funded `SAD` without federal conversion cannot confer Title 38 eligibility.
* **Primary Post-1990 Substance Abuse & The `VAOPGCPREC 7-99` / *George* CUE Bar:**
  * Standalone primary alcohol/drug abuse (not caused or aggravated by an SC disorder) remains statutorily barred for claims filed after October 31, 1990 (`38 U.S.C. §§ 105(a), 1110`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.301(d)`).
  * Under *George v. McDonough*, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)` and `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`, **do NOT file a CUE motion** ([`Board CUE Pre-Filing Gate`](../checklists/board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md)) attacking a final `June 1999 – 2001` RO/Board decision that faithfully applied [`VAOPGCPREC 7-99`](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md) before *Allen v. Principi* invalidated it—reopen via `§ 3.156(c)` missing service records or a Supplemental Claim (`VA Form 20-0995`) instead!

## 5. Postconditions: Effective Dates, Drill-Pay / Severance Offsets & Fees

1. **Effective-Date Verification:**
   * **Late-Associated `LOD` (`DA Form 2173` / `AF Form 348` / `DD Form 261`), `Title 10`/`32` Orders, or Guard STRs (`38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)(3)`):** Audit every Guard/Reserve grant! If VA previously denied for *"no active service"* or *"no in-service injury"* while holding only an `NGB-22`, and later granted after associating `LOD` or `Title 32` orders, **demand the original denied claim's effective date** under `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)(3)` ([Playbook 03](03-service-department-records-38-cfr-3-156c.md); *Vigil v. Peake*, `22 Vet. App. 63 (2008)`).
   * **1-Year Post-`ADT`/`Title 10` Filing (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(b)(1)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.400(b)(2)`):** A claim received within **1 year after release from a qualifying `Active Duty` or `ADT` tour** where disability was incurred/aggravated takes the **day following release from that tour** as its effective date!
2. **Guard/Reserve Drill-Pay Waiver, Combat Severance & Retirement Offsets:**
   * **Annual Drill-Pay Waiver Math (`38 U.S.C. § 5304(c)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(3)` — `VA Form 21-8951` / `21-8951-2`):** Drilling Guardsmen and Reservists **cannot** receive VA disability compensation and military drill/AT pay **for the exact same day**—**NOT** the entire year! A standard drilling reservist performs ~`48` `UTA`s (`4` drill days = `1` day of VA pay per `UTA`) + `15` `AT` days = **`~63 days/year`**, waiving **only `63/360`ths (~2 months) of VA compensation** while keeping **nearly 10 months of tax-free VA pay PLUS 100% of military drill pay**! Always counsel drilling clients **never** to waive their entire VA award!
   * **Combat-Zone Disability Severance Non-Recoupment (`10 U.S.C. § 1212(d)(2)`):** If a Guard/Reserve member was medically separated (`IDES`/`MEB`) with disability severance pay for a disability incurred in a **combat zone** or during **combat-related operations** (post-Jan. 28, 2008 NDAA FY 2008 `Pub. L. 110-181, § 1646`), VA is **statutorily prohibited from recouping** that severance pay—and under `10 U.S.C. § 1212(d)(1)`, VA also cannot recoup severance pay if the severed condition is `0%` or non-contributory to the combined rating!
   * **`20-Year` ("Gray Area" Age `60`) & `Chapter 61` Reserve Retirees (`CRDP` `10 U.S.C. § 1414` & `CRSC` `10 U.S.C. § 1413a` / *Soto*):** Once a Guard/Reserve retiree reaches retirement pay receipt age (typically age `60`, or earlier reduced age for post-2008 deployments) with `>= 50%` VA combined rating, verify automatic **`CRDP`** restoration—or, for `Chapter 61` medical retirees (`<20` good years) or combat-injured retirees, file **`CRSC`** with **uncapped pre-6-year retroactivity under *Soto v. United States*, `605 U.S. 360 (2025)`** ([Playbook 06](06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md))!
3. **Attorney Fees (`38 U.S.C. § 5904` & `EAJA`):**
   * Prior VA denials for *"lack of veteran status / no active duty on NGB-22"* or *"willful misconduct under § 3.301(d)"* satisfy the initial-AOJ-decision prerequisite of `38 U.S.C. § 5904(c)(1)`, enabling immediate contingency fee agreements on `§ 3.156(c)` reconsiderations, Supplemental Claims, HLRs, and Board appeals (subject to dollar-for-dollar `EAJA` same-work offset; see [Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).
