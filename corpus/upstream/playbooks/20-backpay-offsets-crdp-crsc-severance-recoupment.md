# Playbook 20: Net Back-Pay Offsets, Concurrent Receipt (`CRDP` vs. `CRSC` / *Soto*), Severance & Separation Pay Recoupment (`§ 3.700`), Drill Pay, Incarceration & `§ 5904(d)` Fee-Base Engineering

Winning a `100%` rating or a multi-decade retroactive effective date is only half of the representation contract: **unlawful VA/DFAS withholdings, Chapter 61 concurrent-receipt traps, gross-vs.-net severance recoupments, and `38 U.S.C. § 5904(d)` fee-base errors** routinely siphon tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars out of a favorable award. Every grant must pass a post-decision offset and fee-withholding audit.

*(Cross-references: Verbatim offset & effective-date rules in [`38-cfr-3.114-3.400-3.700-effective-dates-offsets.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.114-3.400-3.700-effective-dates-offsets.md); verbatim waiver/pension/competency rules in [`38-cfr-3.274-3.279-3.353-1.965-pension-competency-waivers.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.274-3.279-3.353-1.965-pension-competency-waivers.md); [Master Effective-Date & Staged-Rating Treatise](../docs/effective-dates-and-staged-ratings-treatise.md); [`Part 3 Pension Net-Worth & Effective-Date Diffs`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/pension-net-worth-and-effective-date-diffs.md); [Playbook 21 (Overpayment Waivers `§ 5302`, Incompetency `§ 3.353` & Wartime Pension)](21-overpayment-waivers-incompetency-and-wartime-pension.md); [Playbook 06 (*Nehmer* & Uncapped `CRSC` / *Soto*)](06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md); [Playbook 11 (Guard/Reserve `ADT`/`IADT`)](11-guard-reserve-adt-iadt-and-line-of-duty.md); [Playbook 17 (`§ 1151` & `FTCA` `§ 3.362` Gross Offset)](17-section-1151-va-malpractice-and-ftca-interplay.md); [Master SMC Treatise](../docs/smc-complete-treatise-and-step-math.md); [`Post-Grant Award Letter & Withholding Audit Checklist`](../checklists/award-letter-and-withholding-audit-checklist.md); [Master `VAOPGCPREC` Digest](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md); [Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md); and [Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).*

## 1. Preconditions: VBMS, `DD-214`, `DFAS` & Award-Letter Triggers

Pull these exact documents from VBMS and the client before closing any retroactive grant:

* **Separation & Retirement Source Records:**
  * **`DD Form 214` (`Blocks 18, 24, 26 & 28`)** and any **`DD Form 215`** correction: Screen Separation Program Designator (`SPD`) codes—**`JFL` / `JFM` / `SFJ`** (Disability Severance Pay under `10 U.S.C. § 1212`), **`JEB` / `SEB` / `SFK`** (Permanent/Temporary Disability Retired List `PDRL`/`TDRL` under `10 U.S.C. ch. 61`), or **`JBK` / `JGH` / `JCC` / `KBK`** (Involuntary Separation Pay `ISP`, Special Separation Benefit `SSB` `10 U.S.C. § 1174a`, or Voluntary Separation Incentive `VSI` `10 U.S.C. § 1175`). Record the exact separation date and exact dollar entry in Block 18 (`Remarks`), plus **`DA Form 199` / `AF Form 356` / Navy PEB** combat-related (`V1`/`V3`/`10a`/`10c`) findings!
  * **`DFAS Retiree Account Statement (RAS)`** (or Coast Guard `PPC` statement): Check Gross Retired Pay, `VA Waiver` (`38 U.S.C. § 5305`), `CRDP` restoration amount, `SBP` premium deductions, and federal tax withholding.
* **VBMS Financial & Withholding Artifacts:**
  * **VA Award Letter (`Payment Summary` & `Withholding` table):** Flag every line showing `"Withholding for Military Retired Pay"`, `"Disability Severance Pay"`, `"Separation Pay"`, `"Readjustment Pay"`, `"Drill Pay Adjustment"`, or `"Incarceration Adjustment"`.
  * **`DFAS Audit Error Worksheet (AEW)`:** Electronic reconciliation worksheet transmitted between DFAS (or Coast Guard PPC) and VA's Hines ITC whenever a military retiree wins retroactive VA benefits.
  * **`VA Form 21-8951` / `21-8951-2`:** Annual *Notice of Waiver of VA Compensation or Pension to Receive Military Pay and Allowances* (Reserve/National Guard drill-pay audit).

## 3. Military Retiree Concurrent Receipt Matrix (`38 U.S.C. §§ 5304–5305` vs. `CRDP` vs. `CRSC`)

Under **`38 U.S.C. §§ 5304(a)(1) & 5305`** and **`38 C.F.R. § 3.750`**, a military retiree cannot receive full DoD retired pay and VA disability compensation concurrently *unless* an explicit Title 10 exception restores the waived retired pay (**`CRDP`**, `10 U.S.C. § 1414`) or pays a special tax-free combat supplement (**`CRSC`**, `10 U.S.C. § 1413a`).

| Retiree Category | Combined VA Rating `< 50%` (`10%–40%`) | Combined VA Rating `>= 50%` (Including `100%` `TDIU`!) | Optimal Practitioner Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Regular `20+` Year Longevity Retiree, `TERA` (`15–19` Yr, `10 U.S.C. § 1293 note`), or Reserve "Gray-Area" Retiree Reaching Retirement Pay Age (`60` or reduced early age)** | **No `CRDP`!** Dollar-for-dollar `§ 5305` waiver reduces taxable DoD retired pay by the VA award (**tax-free swap** only), *unless* disabilities are combat-related -> **file `CRSC` (`>= 10%`)**! | **Automatic `CRDP` (`10 U.S.C. § 1414(a)`)!** Restores `100%` of waived longevity retired pay automatically (no application required). Under **`Pub. L. 110-181, § 642`** (`eff. Dec. 31, 2004`), **`100%` `TDIU` (`§ 4.16`) fully qualifies** for `CRDP`! | Compare taxable `CRDP` vs. tax-free `CRSC` during **DFAS Annual Open Season** (`Jan.`): evaluate marginal tax bracket **PLUS** `USFSPA` former-spouse divorce division (`10 U.S.C. § 1408` divides disposable retired pay/`CRDP`, *not* `CRSC`!) and `SBP` premium deductions. |
| **Chapter 61 Medical Retiree (`PDRL`/`TDRL`) With `20+` Years Qualifying Service** | **No `CRDP`!** File **`CRSC` (`DD Form 2860`)** for any `>= 10%` combat-related disability. | **`CRDP` Capped at Longevity Portion (`10 U.S.C. § 1414(b)(1)`):** Restores retired pay *only* up to the `2.5% × YOS` (or `2.0%` Blended Retirement) longevity ceiling; any excess Chapter 61 disability-percentage multiplier remains offset! | Verify DFAS applied the full `2.5% × YOS` ceiling; model `CRSC` election if combat-related ratings match the longevity cap tax-free. |
| **Chapter 61 Medical Retiree (`PDRL`/`TDRL`) With `< 20` Years of Service** *(THE #1 CONCURRENT-RECEIPT TRAP!)* | **ZERO `CRDP` (`10 U.S.C. § 1414(b)(2)`)!** Dollar-for-dollar `§ 5305` offset wipes out DoD retired pay up to the VA rate. | **ZERO `CRDP` (`10 U.S.C. § 1414(b)(2)`)!** Even at `100%` P&T or `SMC`, `< 20-yr` Chapter 61 retirees are **categorically barred from `CRDP`** (unless `TERA`-retired)! | **MANDATORY `CRSC` FILING (`DD Form 2860`, `10 U.S.C. § 1413a`):** Expanded to `< 20-yr` Ch. 61 retirees eff. **`Jan. 1, 2008`** (`Pub. L. 110-181, § 641`). Pays tax-free monthly `CRSC` (capped at the **`2.5% × YOS` longevity ceiling**, `10 U.S.C. § 1413a(b)(3)`) for Purple Heart, armed conflict, hazardous service, instrumentality of war (including **Agent Orange, burn-pit/Gulf War & radiation presumptions** when tied to qualifying exposure!), or simulated war—with **uncapped arrears** under ***Soto v. United States*, `605 U.S. 360 (2025)`** ([Playbook 06](06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md))! |

## 4. Disability Severance Pay (`10 U.S.C. § 1212`) vs. Separation Pay (`§ 3.700`) Three-Gate Recoupment Audit

Never assume a VA severance/separation withholding on an award letter is lawful. Run **all three statutory gates** against verbatim [`38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(2), (a)(3) & (a)(5)`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.114-3.400-3.700-effective-dates-offsets.md):

### Audit Gate A: Combat-Zone / Combat-Related Non-Recoupment (`10 U.S.C. § 1212(d)(2)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(3)`)
* **The Rule (`Pub. L. 110-181, § 1646`, `122 Stat. 472`; `74 FR 26956`, pub. & eff. June 5, 2009, **retroactively applicable to separations `>= Jan. 28, 2008`**):** VA is **strictly prohibited (`$0` recoupment)** from deducting Disability Severance Pay (`DSP`) from VA compensation if:
  1. The member separated from the Armed Forces under Chapter 61 **on or after `January 28, 2008`** *(Fatal Cutoff: `Pub. L. 110-181, § 1646(c)` bars this exemption for separations on or before Jan. 27, 2008)*; **AND**
  2. The disability for which `DSP` was awarded was incurred in line of duty in a **combat zone** (designated by Executive Order) **OR** incurred during the performance of duty in **combat-related operations** as designated by the Secretary of Defense (armed conflict, hazardous service, instrumentality of war, or simulated war—broader than geographic combat deployment!).
* **Remedial Action When DoD/VA Missed the Combat Code:** Inspect `DD-214 Block 18` and PEB `DA Form 199` (`V1`/`V3` codes). If the severance condition resulted from parachute jump, tactical field training (`simulated war`), armored vehicle/artillery (`instrumentality of war`), or combat theater deployment on/after Jan. 28, 2008—and VA recouped `DSP` because `DD-214` omitted the combat-related remark—petition the service branch **BCMR/BCNR (`DD Form 149`)** for a `DD Form 215` combat-related determination, then file an RO audit / `§ 3.105(a)` CUE claim forcing VA to **refund 100% of every dollar previously withheld**!

### Audit Gate B: Severance-Disability Isolation Math (`§ 3.700(a)(3)`) vs. Total-Award Separation Pay (`§ 3.700(a)(5)`)
* **Critical Program Divide (`VAOPGCPREC 12-96` & `38 C.F.R. § 3.700`):**
  * **Non-Disability Separation Pay (`ISP`, `10 U.S.C. § 1174`), `SSB` (`10 U.S.C. § 1174a`, `§ 3.700(a)(5)`), & Lump-Sum Readjustment Pay (`§ 3.700(a)(2)`):** Recouped from **100% of the veteran's monthly VA disability compensation** across **ALL** service-connected conditions until the net balance is repaid *(whereas **`VSI`**, `10 U.S.C. § 1175(e)(4)`, is offset directly by **DFAS** from the annual `VSI` installment, NOT withheld by VA!)*.
  * **Disability Severance Pay (`10 U.S.C. § 1212`, `§ 3.700(a)(3)`):** VA may recoup **ONLY** from compensation payable for **the exact disability (or disabilities) for which military `DSP` was granted**—VA **cannot** raid compensation payable independently for non-severance SC disabilities!
* **Exact `§ 3.700(a)(3)` & `M21-1` Two-Method Incremental Recoupment Cap:**
  * Once VA makes its *"initial determination of the degree of disability"* (first regular Part 4 schedular rating, excluding temporary `§§ 4.29/4.30` hospital/convalescent ratings), monthly recoupment **cannot exceed** the monthly rate payable for that initial severance rating, and when non-severance SC disabilities coexist (or ratings later increase), VA must compare **(Method 1) standalone rate for the initial severance rating** against **(Method 2) incremental difference (`Total Combined Rate` minus `Combined Rate of Non-Severance Disabilities Alone`)** and withhold the **lesser** amount (most advantageous to the veteran):
    * *Zero-Increment Example (`70% + 10% = 70%`):* Veteran has `70%` PTSD (non-severance) and `10%` lumbar strain (severance disability). Under `38 C.F.R. § 4.25`, `70% + 10% = 73% -> rounds to 70%`. Because the veteran already receives the full `70%` rate from non-severance PTSD alone, the `10%` severance disability adds **`$0.00` incremental compensation**—VA must withhold **`$0/month`** while paying the full `70%` check!
    * *Partial-Increment Example (`50% + 20% = 60%`):* Veteran has `50%` PTSD (non-severance) and `20%` knee (initial severance rating). `50% + 20% = 60%` combined. Compare standalone `20%` rate vs. `(60% rate minus 50% rate)`: VA must pay at least the full `50%` non-severance rate and withhold **at most** the lesser of the `20%` standalone rate or the `60% - 50%` incremental difference! And if that severance knee later worsens from `20%` to `60%`, `§ 3.700(a)(3)` **caps monthly recoupment at the initial `20%` schedular severance rate**!

### Audit Gate C: Post-Sept. 30, 1996 After-Tax Net Cap & *St. Clair* / `Pub. L. 114-292` Tax Refunds
1. **Federal Income Tax Exclusion (`Pub. L. 104-201, § 653`, `110 Stat. 2583`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(2), (a)(3) & (a)(5)`):** For any Readjustment Pay, Disability Severance Pay, Separation Pay (`ISP`), or `SSB` **paid after `September 30, 1996`**, VA can recoup **ONLY the after-tax net amount** (`gross payout minus flat federal income tax withheld by DFAS`, typically `20%`–`28%`!). Auditing old awards where VA keyed the gross `DD-214 Block 18` figure instead of deducting federal tax withholding immediately cuts `20%+` off the recoupment debt!
2. ***St. Clair* (`26 U.S.C. § 104(a)(4)`) & Combat-Injured Veterans Tax Fairness Act (`Pub. L. 114-292`):** Under ***St. Clair v. United States*, `778 F. Supp. 894 (E.D. Va. 1991)`** *(frequently mis-referred to in informal practice notes as "Stinson," which is an unrelated `508 U.S. 36` Sentencing Guidelines case!)*, lump-sum military `DSP` becomes tax-exempt under `26 U.S.C. § 104(a)(4)` when VA awards service connection for the same injury. Between `Jan. 17, 1991` and `2016`, DFAS computers erroneously withheld federal taxes on `130,000+` tax-exempt `DSP` payouts, prompting Congress to enact **`Pub. L. 114-292` (`Dec. 16, 2016`)** reopening IRS `Form 1040-X` refunds. **Recoupment Rule:** Even if a veteran separately recovered an IRS tax refund under *St. Clair* or `Pub. L. 114-292`, `38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(3)` still measures VA recoupment strictly by *"the total amount of the severance pay less the amount of Federal income tax withheld from such pay"* at separation (unless zero tax was withheld at separation because DFAS coded it tax-exempt up front)!

## 5. Reserve Drill Pay (`§ 3.654`), `OWCP`/`FECA` (`§ 3.708`) & Felony Incarceration (`§ 5313` / `§ 3.665`)

### A. Reserve & National Guard Drill-Pay Pro-Rata Math (`38 U.S.C. § 5304(c)`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.654, 3.700(a)(1)(iii)`)
* **The `1/30th` Per-Day Rule (`VA Form 21-8951`):** Reservists and Guardsmen cannot receive VA disability compensation and active/inactive-duty training pay for the **exact same day**, *but* they only waive **`1/30th` of one month's VA compensation for each day of active duty or each Inactive Duty Training (`IDT`/`UTA`) drill period**!
  * One standard weekend drill (`4 UTAs` — Saturday morning/afternoon + Sunday morning/afternoon) = **`4 days`** of VA compensation waived; a standard Reserve year (`48 UTAs` + `15 days` Annual Training) = **`63 days`** (`2.1 months`) of VA compensation waived!
  * **Practitioner Audit:** Because 1 day of military drill/AT base pay almost always exceeds `1/30th` of monthly VA compensation (even at `100%`!), **always elect to waive VA compensation for the `63` training days** rather than waiving military pay—and immediately file an HLR/audit whenever VA mistakenly suspends the veteran's **entire `365-day` award** as an "active-duty return" instead of posting a `63-day` pro-rata debt!

### B. Federal Employees' Compensation Act / `OWCP` Election (`5 U.S.C. § 8116`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.708`)
* **Same-Injury Bar vs. Distinct-Condition Dual Receipt:** Under `38 C.F.R. § 3.708(a)–(b)` (for civilian federal employment injuries `>= Sept. 13, 1960`), a veteran **may concurrently receive `100%` of both `OWCP`/`FECA` wage-loss/schedule awards AND VA disability compensation** whenever they compensate **different injuries or anatomical conditions** (e.g., VA `70%` combat PTSD + Postal Service `OWCP` torn rotator cuff)!
* **Aggravation Increment Rule:** An irrevocable election is triggered **only** when `OWCP` and VA compensate the **same disability**—and if a civilian federal workplace injury *aggravates* a preexisting SC disability (e.g., `20%` SC back aggravated to `60%`), the veteran can retain the preexisting `20%` VA baseline while electing between `OWCP` and only the incremental `40%` VA increase!

### C. Felony Incarceration Reductions, Halfway Houses & Family Apportionment (`38 U.S.C. § 5313`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.665–3.666`)
1. **Trigger & Conviction-Date Anchor (`§ 3.665(a), (c)–(d)`):** Reduction begins on the **`61st day`** of incarceration in a federal, state, local, or state-contracted private penal institution (*Wanless v. Shinseki*, `618 F.3d 1333 (Fed. Cir. 2010)`) **following conviction of a felony**:
   * **Disability Compensation (`§ 3.665(d)`):** Ratings of **`20%–100%`** (including `TDIU`/`SMC`) drop to the **`10%` statutory rate**; a **`10%`** rating drops to **one-half of the `10%` rate** (`DIC` similarly drops to half the `10%` compensation rate). *Contrast Wartime/Survivors Pension (`38 C.F.R. § 3.666`):* Pension terminates **completely (`$0`)** on the `61st day` of imprisonment for **either a felony OR a misdemeanor**!
   * **Pre-Conviction vs. Post-Verdict Trap (*Mulder*):** Pre-trial detention before a guilty plea/verdict does **NOT** trigger `§ 5313`, *but* under ***Mulder v. McDonald*, `805 F.3d 1342 (Fed. Cir. 2015)`** (*aff'g Mulder v. Gibson*, `27 Vet. App. 10 (2014)`), the 60-day clock starts on the **date the guilty plea or verdict is accepted**, NOT the later sentencing date!
   * **Exemptions (No Reduction):** Misdemeanor convictions (for compensation!), civil commitment / forensic psychiatric hospitalization prior to felony conviction, parole, work-release, or **residing in a residential reentry center / halfway house (`38 U.S.C. § 5313(a)(2)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.665(b)`)**!
2. **Mandatory Family Rescue — Dependent Apportionment (`38 U.S.C. § 5313(b)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.665(e)–(h)`):**
   * All or part of the withheld `90%+` compensation (`full awarded rate minus the 10% rate paid to the veteran`) **may be apportioned (`VA Form 21-0788`) to the veteran's spouse, children, or dependent parents** on the basis of individual financial need! VA is legally required (`§ 3.665(a)`) to notify both the veteran and dependents of this right; if dependents apply within **1 year** of notice, the apportionment relates back to the **`61st day`** of incarceration (`§ 3.665(f)`).
3. **Day-of-Release Full Restoration (`§ 3.665(i)`):** Full `100%` compensation resumes on the **exact date of release** (including transfer to a halfway house or work-release!) **if** VA is notified within **1 year** following release!

## 6. Postconditions: Engineering `38 U.S.C. § 5904(d)` `20%` Direct-Pay Fee Withholdings Across Offsets

Under **`38 U.S.C. § 5904(d)(1)–(3)`** and **`38 C.F.R. § 14.636(g)–(h)`**, VA directly withholds and pays an accredited attorney/agent up to **`20%` of `"the total amount of any past-due benefits awarded on the basis of the claim"`** (*contingent on filing the fee agreement within the **strict `30-day` window** of **`§ 14.636(g)(3)`**—missing that `30-day` filing rule forfeits direct VA payment even on a valid retainer under ***Ravin v. Wilkie*, `956 F.3d 1346, 1350–52 (Fed. Cir. 2020)`**!*). Audit every fee notice against these four Federal Circuit rules:

1. **Incarceration Reductions Do NOT Shrink the Statutory `"Award"` (*Snyder v. Nicholson*, `489 F.3d 1213, 1217–20 (Fed. Cir. 2007)`):**
   * VA historically tried to calculate an attorney's `20%` fee on the tiny `$100+/month` `10%` incarcerated payout (`§ 5313`) rather than the `70%` or `100%` rating won on appeal.
   * In *Snyder*, the Federal Circuit **reversed**, holding that `"past-due benefits awarded"` under `§ 5904(d)` means the **unpaid sum of the monthly ratings assigned from the effective date through the award decision** (less any prior ratings already in effect), *before* `§ 5313` payment withholding—so VA must calculate `20%` on the **full awarded rating** and pay it out of the non-apportioned retroactive pool! *(Note: In ***Jackson v. McDonald*, `635 F. App'x 858 (Fed. Cir. 2015) (nonprecedential)`**, rev'g `26 Vet. App. 460`, the Federal Circuit noted in a nonprecedential order that *Snyder* distinguishes the gross "award" from net "payable" cash where `§ 14.636(h)(3)` applies; always anchor briefs on binding *Snyder*, *Ravin*, and *Gumpenberger*!)*
2. **Overpayment / Debt Invalidation Fees Apply ONLY to Actually Withheld Cash (*Gumpenberger v. Wilkie*, `973 F.3d 1379, 1382–85 (Fed. Cir. 2020)`):**
   * When counsel invalidates an unlawful `$196,000` VA debt ([Playbook 21](21-overpayment-waivers-incompetency-and-wartime-pension.md)) after VA had recouped `$38,000` from monthly benefits, `"past-due benefits"` under `§ 5904(d)` equals **ONLY the `$38,000` actually withheld and retroactively restored in cash** (`20% = $7,600`), **NOT** the `$158,000` uncollected debt balance that VA merely canceled on paper! Structure debt-defense retainers (`non-direct-pay` flat/hourly or hybrid post-decision clauses complying with `§ 14.636(e)–(h)`) accordingly.
3. **Military Retiree `DFAS AEW` Holds & `38 C.F.R. § 14.636(h)(3)` Cash-Payable Trap:**
   * Under `38 C.F.R. § 14.636(h)(3)(i) & (iii)`, direct-pay `20%` withholding requires an award resulting in **cash payment** from which the fee can be deducted, and explicitly flags awards subject to military retired-pay waivers (`§ 5305`) or severance recoupments.
   * **Mandatory `AEW` Audit:** When a `20+` year retiree jumps from `< 50%` (or `0%`) to **`>= 50%` / `100%` (`CRDP`-eligible)**, VA initially withholds `100%` of the retroactive VA award pending DFAS's **`Audit Error Worksheet (AEW)`**. Once DFAS confirms `CRDP` entitlement (`10 U.S.C. § 1414`), the `§ 5305` waiver lifts and VA owes the full net retroactive cash payout—**calendar a `45-day` post-grant `AEW` check** to verify VA ran its *Fee Allocation / Release* calculation on the restored post-`AEW` retroactive benefits rather than leaving counsel's `20%` fee at `$0` based on the temporary pre-`AEW` withholding! *(Conversely, for a `< 20-yr` Chapter 61 retiree whose VA award is completely offset dollar-for-dollar by prior DoD retired pay and who recovers arrears separately from DoD via `CRSC`, `§ 14.636(h)(3)` yields `$0` VA cash withholding because `CRSC` is a Title 10 DoD payment!)*
4. **Prior-Rating Step Deductions, `§ 1151` `FTCA` Offsets & `EAJA` Same-Work Refunds:**
   * **Step Increases (`§ 14.636(h)(3)(ii)`):** Past-due benefits equal the difference between the newly awarded combined rate (including `SMC` and dependents!) and the prior combined rate actually paid during each historical month.
   * **`§ 1151` `FTCA` Tort Offset ([Playbook 17](17-section-1151-va-malpractice-and-ftca-interplay.md)):** If an `FTCA` judgment became final *before* the `§ 1151` grant and absorbs `100%` of retroactive `§ 1151` payments under `38 C.F.R. § 3.362(b)`, no net cash is payable by VA for the post-judgment months—secure `§ 1151` effective dates predating the FTCA judgment or account for the FTCA fee (`28 U.S.C. §§ 2672/2678`).
   * **Mandatory `EAJA` + `§ 5904` Lesser-of-Two Client Refund (`Pub. L. 102-572, § 506(c)`, `106 Stat. 4513`):** Whenever counsel recovers CAVC `EAJA` fees (`28 U.S.C. § 2412(d)`) for court remand work **AND** later collects a `§ 5904(d)` contingency fee covering **the same work** on remand, counsel **must refund the smaller of the two fees dollar-for-dollar to the veteran**!
