# Playbook 06: *Nehmer* Herbicide Retroactivity (`§ 3.816`), 1-Year Chronic Presumptions & Uncapped CRSC (*Soto*)

This playbook covers three statutory/class mechanisms that routinely produce high-six-figure awards by overriding ordinary effective-date rules or abolishing statutory time caps.

---

## 1. *Nehmer* Class Retroactive Effective Dates (`38 C.F.R. § 3.816`)

Under the *Nehmer* consent decree and `38 C.F.R. § 3.816`, veterans who served in the **Republic of Vietnam** (including **Blue Water Navy** offshore territorial seas under *Procopio* / `Pub. L. 116-23` / 2020 N.D. Cal. *Nehmer* enforcement order!) and their survivors enjoy extraordinary retroactive effective dates for **covered herbicide diseases** (`§ 3.816(b)(2)` / `§ 3.309(e)`).

### A. The Two *Nehmer* Retroactive Pathways (`§ 3.816(c)(1) & (2)`)
1. **Previously Denied Claims (`§ 3.816(c)(1)` — "Footnote 1" Claims):**
   * If VA **denied** compensation for a covered herbicide disease in a claim **received on or after September 25, 1985** (or pending on Sept. 25, 1985), the effective date of a later *Nehmer* grant is the **date VA received that prior denied claim** (or the date the disability arose, whichever is later)—**even if decades passed and the veteran never appealed**!
2. **Coding / Diagnostic Lookback Rule ("Footnote 1" Medical Evidence Trap):**
   * Even if the veteran did **not** name the exact *Nehmer* condition on a post-Sept. 25, 1985 claim (e.g., veteran claimed "shortness of breath / chest pain" or "hypertension" in 1994), if **medical records in the file at the time of that claim or prior to the new presumptive rule** showed a diagnosis of **Ischemic Heart Disease (`CAD`/`IHD`)**, VA **must** treat it as a previously denied *Nehmer* claim!
3. **Claims Filed Before Regulatory Effective Date (`§ 3.816(c)(2)`):**
   * Pending claims when a new herbicide presumption took effect relate back to claim receipt or date disability arose.
4. ***Nehmer* Survivor & Estate Payouts (`§ 3.816(f)`):**
   * Uniquely in all of Veterans Law, when a *Nehmer* class member dies before receiving owed *Nehmer* retroactive benefits, VA must pay **100% of the unpaid retroactive compensation** (without the 1-year `§ 5121(c)` filing bar or pre-Dec. 16, 2003 2-year accrued-benefits cap of `38 U.S.C. § 5121`!) to the surviving spouse, children (**including adult independent children**), parents, or **even the veteran's estate**! See [`Playbook 09: Accrued Benefits, Substitution & Nehmer Estates`](09-accrued-benefits-substitution-nehmer-estates.md) and verbatim [`38-cfr-3.1000-3.1010-accrued-substitution.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.1000-3.1010-accrued-substitution.md).
5. **Mandatory *Nehmer* Exclusions, Survivor `§ 1305` DIC Escape Hatch & PACT Act OIG Audit Check:**
   * **`M21-1 VIII.i.2.B.1.c` Exclusions:** **Hypertension**, **MGUS**, and **chloracne** are **excluded** from `38 C.F.R. § 3.816` *Nehmer* class retroactivity! For living veterans, PACT Act hypertension and MGUS claims are governed by PACT Act statutory effective-date rules (`Aug. 10, 2022` or `38 C.F.R. § 3.114` 1-year lookbacks)—unless an independent finality-piercing rule (`§ 3.156(c)` newly associated service records, `§ 3.156(b)` *Lang* non-finality, or `§ 3.309(a)` 1-year post-discharge CUE) applies. **HOWEVER, for surviving spouses/children**, if DIC was *previously denied* before August 10, 2022 and the death is now presumptive under PACT Act `§ 404` (hypertension/MGUS), **`38 U.S.C. § 1305` (`PACT Act § 204`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.33`) mandates retroactive DIC back to the original denied DIC claim**—see [`Playbook 08: Survivor DIC & § 1318`](08-survivor-dic-direct-and-1318-hypothetical.md)!
   * **VA OIG \$20.4 Million PACT Error Pattern:** Audit every 2022–2026 PACT Act award to verify the rater did not overlook a pre-August 14, 2023 Intent to File or `§ 3.114(a)` 1-year retroactive lookback back to **August 10, 2022**.

## 2. Overlooked 1-Year Post-Discharge Chronic Diseases (`38 U.S.C. § 1112`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.307, 3.309(a)`)

> [!TIP]
> **Expanded Presumptions Playbook:** For full coverage of `§ 3.309(a)` chronic diseases, `§ 3.309(b)` tropical diseases, `§ 3.309(c)` Former POW presumptions, `§ 3.318` ALS (`20%` minimum), radiation (`§§ 3.309(d), 3.311`), Camp Lejeune, and *Shedden*/*Hickson* continuity traps, see **[`Playbook 07: Statutory & Regulatory Presumptions`](07-statutory-presumptions-chronic-tropical-pow-als.md)**.

Historically, RO raters summarily denied post-service claims whenever **Service Treatment Records (STRs) were silent**, forgetting that **40+ enumerated "chronic diseases" in `38 C.F.R. § 3.309(a)`** require **ZERO in-service manifestations** if they manifest to a compensable degree (`10%` or more) within **1 year of separation** (3 years for Hansen's disease / TB; 7 years for MS)!

### Classic Overlooked `§ 3.309(a)` Conditions
* **Arthritis** (degenerative/osteoarthritis or rheumatoid manifesting to `10%` within 1 year of discharge)
* **Cardiovascular-renal disease** (including **hypertension** and arteriosclerosis!)
* **Organic diseases of the nervous system** (including **sensorineural hearing loss**, **tinnitus**, **epilepsy**, **peripheral neuropathy**, and **migraines** when organically rooted—*see Walker v. Shinseki*, 708 F.3d 1331 (Fed. Cir. 2013) restricting continuity-of-symptomatology under `§ 3.303(b)` strictly to `§ 3.309(a)` chronic diseases!)
* **Peptic / duodenal ulcers**, **calculi** (kidney/bladder/gallbladder), **malignant tumors**, **diabetes mellitus**, **psychoses**.

**CUE Trigger:** If private or VAMC records in the C-file at the time of an early denial undisputedly showed a `§ 3.309(a)` chronic disease rated at `10%+` severity within 12 months of separation (after 90+ days active wartime/post-1946 service) and no affirmative clear evidence rebutted the presumption (`§ 3.307(d)`), denying for "negative STRs" is classic **RO CUE (`38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)`)**!

## 3. Uncapped CRSC Arrears After *Soto v. United States*, 605 U.S. 360 (2025)

Whenever your client is a **military retiree** (20+ year regular/reserve retiree OR **Chapter 61 medical retiree**—even with <20 years of service!) and holds combat-related VA disabilities (Purple Heart, armed conflict, hazardous service, instrumentality of war, or **presumptive Agent Orange / Gulf War / radiation combat-related conditions**):

1. **The Historical Trap:** Until June 12, 2025, Navy/Army/Air Force CRSC boards invoked the Barring Act (`31 U.S.C. § 3702(b)`) to confiscate all retroactive CRSC older than **6 years** prior to the veteran's CRSC application.
2. **The *Soto* Holding (`605 U.S. 360 (2025)`):** The U.S. Supreme Court unanimously held that `10 U.S.C. § 1413a` provides independent settlement authority, **completely eliminating the 6-year Barring Act time bar** on CRSC claims.
3. **Practice Checklist & Forum Split (DoD Guidance vs. Court of Federal Claims):**
   * **Aug. 20, 2025 DoD Guidance & Past Partial CRSC Grants:** Did the retiree apply for CRSC in 2015–2025 and get cut off at 6 years of back pay despite holding VA combat-related ratings back to 2003 (when CRSC began) or retirement? Under DoD's **August 20, 2025 implementation guidance**, demand full retroactive recalculation from the military department CRSC board / DFAS; for disputed or delayed pre-6-year arrears exceeding **\$10,000**, evaluate parallel Tucker Act (`28 U.S.C. § 1491`) litigation in the U.S. Court of Federal Claims (e.g., *Christopher Carey v. United States*).
   * **Pair Every VA Retroactive Win With a Follow-On CRSC Application:** When a Chapter 61 or 20-year retiree wins a 15- or 20-year VA effective date via CUE, `§ 3.156(c)`, or *Nehmer*, immediately file/amend their branch CRSC packet to unlock a **second uncapped retroactive payout** from DFAS! *(Note: Remember `CRDP` vs. `CRSC` tax-free optimization—Chapter 61 retirees with <20 years do NOT get CRDP, making CRSC pure net new tax-free recovery up to their longevity retirement cap!)*
