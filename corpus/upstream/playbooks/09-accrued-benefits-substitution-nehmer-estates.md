# Playbook 09: Accrued Benefits (`§ 5121`), Post-Mortem Substitution (`§ 5121A`), CAVC `Rule 43` & *Nehmer* Class Estates (`§ 3.816(f)`)

Under general Veterans Law principles, a veteran's claim for lifetime disability compensation extinguishes upon death unless saved by statute (*Zevalkink v. Brown*, `102 F.3d 1236, 1243–44 (Fed. Cir. 1996)`). Yet when a veteran dies with a multi-year appeal pending, an unadjudicated informal/TDIU/AMA claim sitting in the C-file, or a previously denied Agent Orange condition, **hundreds of thousands of dollars in unpaid lifetime compensation** hang in the balance.

Capturing those funds—while simultaneously protecting the survivor's prospective monthly DIC ([Playbook 08](08-survivor-dic-direct-and-1318-hypothetical.md))—requires choosing with surgical precision among **three distinct statutory/regulatory post-mortem vehicles**: **(A) `38 U.S.C. § 5121A` Substitution**, **(B) `38 U.S.C. § 5121` Accrued Benefits**, and **(C) `38 C.F.R. § 3.816(f)` *Nehmer* Class Survivor/Estate Payouts**.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Immediate 48-Hour Post-Death Intake Rule:** A veteran's Power of Attorney (`VA Form 21-22a`) and representation authority **terminate by operation of law at the moment of death**. Execute the [`Survivor Post-Mortem Triage Gate`](../checklists/survivor-post-mortem-triage-gate.md) immediately: sign a **new `VA Form 21-22a`** (and updated fee agreement) with the eligible survivor/executor, calendar the **strict 1-year post-death statutory bar** (`§§ 5121(c), 5121A(a)(1)`), and check for running **`120-day` CAVC Notice of Appeal (`NOA`)** deadlines!

## 1. Preconditions: VBMS, Docket & C-File Post-Mortem Triggers

*(See verbatim primary-source regulations in [`38-cfr-3.1000-3.1010-accrued-substitution.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.1000-3.1010-accrued-substitution.md) and form/code-sheet conventions in [`forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md`](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md)).*

* **Prescribed Post-Mortem VA Forms:**
  * **`VA Form 21P-0847`** (*Request for Substitution of Claimant Upon Death of Claimant*) — primary form to step into a pending claim, HLR, or Board appeal within **1 year of death**.
  * **`VA Form 21P-534EZ`** (*Application for DIC, Survivors Pension, and/or Accrued Benefits*) — simultaneously claims DIC **and** `§ 5121` accrued benefits (and triggers VA's duty under *Reliford* to address `§ 5121A` substitution if a claim was pending!).
  * **`VA Form 21P-601`** (*Application for Accrued Amounts Due a Deceased Beneficiary*) — used by non-spouse/parent/adult-child payers seeking **`§ 5121(a)(6)` last-sickness and burial expense reimbursement**, or when claiming accrued amounts without DIC.
* **Pending-Claim & Docket Audit Triggers (What Counts as "Pending at Death"?):**
  * Any active initial claim (`21-526EZ`), AMA Supplemental Claim (`20-0995`), Higher-Level Review (`20-0996`), Board appeal (`10182` or Legacy `VA Form 9` / unissued `SOC` after post-1962 `NOD`), or CAVC appeal pending on the date of death.
  * **Hidden Unadjudicated Claims Open at Death:** Even if VBMS shows *"No Active Claims"* on the date of death, a claim was **legally pending at death** if the C-file contains:
    1. A pre-March 24, 2015 informal claim (`former § 3.155(a)` / `§ 3.157(b)` VAMC hospitalization report) or pro se symptom entry (`Shea v. Wilkie`) never adjudicated ([Playbook 04](04-unadjudicated-tdiu-informal-claims-smc.md));
    2. Reasonably raised `Roberson`/`Rice` **TDIU** during an open rating stream;
    3. A defective-address decision that never became final under `§ 3.103` / *Mindenhall* ([Playbook 02](02-wrong-address-defective-notice-cp-exams.md)); or
    4. A post-February 19, 2019 AMA claim/issue omitted from explicit `38 U.S.C. § 5104(b)` decision notice under ***Hamill v. Collins*, 166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. 2026)** ([Procedural Vehicles Piercing Finality](../docs/procedural-vehicles-piercing-finality.md))!
  * ***Nehmer* Class Audit Trigger (`§ 3.816(f)`):** Service in the Republic of Vietnam (land or `12-nm` Blue Water Navy territorial seas, Jan. 9, 1962 – May 7, 1975) or Korean DMZ + **any** post-Sept. 25, 1985 denied claim or in-file diagnosis ("Footnote 1") for a `§ 3.309(e)` covered herbicide disease ([Playbook 06](06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md)).

## 3. Proof Elements & Doctrinal Mechanics

### A. Standalone Accrued Benefits (`38 U.S.C. § 5121` [`former § 3021`]; `38 C.F.R. § 3.1000`)

1. **The "Pending Claim or Existing Decisions" Prerequisite (*Zevalkink* & *Jones*):**
   * Under `38 U.S.C. § 5121(a)` (`former 38 U.S.C. § 3021(a)`, [Three-Era Statutory Crosswalk](../docs/title-38-statutory-crosswalk.md)), periodic monetary benefits to which an individual was entitled at death `"under existing ratings or decisions or... based on evidence in the file at date of death"` and `"due and unpaid"` are paid to the highest living `§ 5121(a)(2)` class.
   * In ***Zevalkink v. Brown*, 102 F.3d 1236, 1241–42 (Fed. Cir. 1996)** and ***Jones v. West*, 136 F.3d 1296, 1299–1300 (Fed. Cir. 1998)**, the Federal Circuit held that an accrued-benefits claim is **derivative** of the veteran's claim: unless unpaid benefits were already awarded before death, **the veteran must have had a claim pending (or a non-final appeal window open) on the date of death**.
   * **What Counts as `"Evidence in the File at Date of Death"` (`§ 3.1000(d)(4)`):** Includes all records physically in the C-file plus records in **constructive VA possession** (VAMC clinical records, military service records, SSA records already held by VA, etc.), **excluding** post-mortem medical nexus opinions unless `§ 5121A` substitution is invoked.
2. **Repeal of the Historical 2-Year Payment Cap (`Pub. L. 108-183, § 104`):**
   * Prior to December 16, 2003, `38 U.S.C. § 5121(a)` brutally capped accrued recoveries at **2 years** of retroactive benefits—forfeiting 8 years of back pay if a veteran died during a 10-year appeal!
   * Section `104(a)` & `(d)` of the *Veterans Benefits Act of 2003* (`Pub. L. 108-183`, `117 Stat. 2656`) **repealed the 2-year cap in full for claimants dying on or after December 16, 2003**, unlocking 100% of uncapped past-due lifetime compensation. *(If auditing an older death occurring **before Dec. 16, 2003**, the 2-year cap still applies to `§ 5121`—pivot immediately to `38 C.F.R. § 3.816(f)` if herbicide-related!)*
3. **The Three-Way Survivor CUE Matrix (*Haines v. West*, `154 F.3d 1298 (Fed. Cir. 1998)`):**
   * **Rule 1 — Survivor CANNOT Initiate CUE for `§ 5121` Accrued Back Pay:** In *Haines v. West*, `154 F.3d at 1300–02`, the Federal Circuit held that `38 U.S.C. §§ 5109A` and `7111` do **not** authorize a survivor to initiate a post-mortem CUE attack on a final lifetime rating decision in order to manufacture `"due and unpaid"` accrued benefits under `§ 5121`.
   * **Rule 2 — Survivor CAN Pursue a CUE Motion Filed by the Veteran Before Death:** If the *veteran* filed a valid `§ 5109A` or `§ 7111` CUE motion during life and died before final resolution, that CUE claim was **pending at death**, allowing an eligible `§ 5121(a)(2)` survivor to substitute (`§ 5121A`) or take accrued benefits (`§ 5121`) upon proving CUE!
   * **Rule 3 — Survivor CAN Initiate Lifetime CUE Under `38 C.F.R. § 3.22(b)(1)` for `§ 1318` DIC:** *Haines* bars CUE **only** for `§ 5121` lifetime back pay; `38 C.F.R. § 3.22(b)(1)` explicitly authorizes a survivor to prove CUE in a lifetime decision to establish `10-year` total disability for the survivor's own monthly `§ 1318` DIC ([Playbook 08](08-survivor-dic-direct-and-1318-hypothetical.md))!

### B. Post-Mortem Substitution (`38 U.S.C. § 5121A`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.1010`) & The *Reliford* Waiver Choice

Enacted by **`Pub. L. 110-389, § 212` (`122 Stat. 4151`, Oct. 10, 2008)** for deaths **on or after October 10, 2008**, and implemented at **`38 C.F.R. § 3.1010`** (`79 FR 52977`, published Sept. 5, 2014, eff. **Oct. 6, 2014**; upheld in ***Nat'l Org. of Veterans' Advocates, Inc. (NOVA) v. Sec'y of Veterans Affairs*, 809 F.3d 1359 (Fed. Cir. 2016)**), Section `5121A` revolutionized survivor litigation:

1. **Open-Record Shoes of the Veteran (*Reeves* & *Breedlove*):**
   * By filing **`VA Form 21P-0847`** (or `21P-534EZ` requesting accrued/substitution) within **1 year of the veteran's death**, an eligible `§ 5121(a)(2)` survivor steps directly into the pending claim or appeal at its exact procedural stage (*Breedlove v. Shinseki*, `24 Vet. App. 7, 18–20 (2010)` (per curiam)).
   * Under ***Reeves v. Shinseki*, 682 F.3d 988, 993–94 (Fed. Cir. 2012)**, an eligible survivor has standing to substitute on appeal without having first prosecuted a separate standalone `§ 5121` accrued claim, inheriting the veteran's evidentiary presumptions (such as `38 U.S.C. § 1154(b)` combat relaxation) and full open-record development rights—including submitting **new post-mortem private medical nexus / staging opinions**!
2. **Tactical Gem — When to WAIVE Substitution Under *Reliford v. McDonald*, `27 Vet. App. 297 (2015)`:**
   * In *Reliford*, the veteran died with a positive nexus opinion already placing the record in `§ 5107(b)` equipoise; instead of granting `§ 5121` accrued benefits on the closed date-of-death record, VA automatically treated the widow's `21-534` as a `§ 5121A` substitution, ordered a **new post-mortem VA medical opinion that came back negative**, and denied the claim!
   * The CAVC reversed, holding that `§ 5121` and `§ 5121A` are distinct statutory pathways and **VA must notify an accrued claimant of the right to WAIVE `§ 5121A` substitution** (`27 Vet. App. at 303–05`).
   * **Practitioner Decision Rule:**
     * **Substitute (`§ 5121A` — 95% of cases):** Whenever the date-of-death record lacks a medical nexus, diagnosis confirmation, stressor proof, or higher-rating staging evidence that you can supply via a post-mortem private IMO/DBQ.
     * **Waive Substitution & Demand Closed-Record `§ 5121` Adjudication (*Reliford* — 5% of cases):** Whenever the file **on the date of death** already contains uncontroverted or equiponderant favorable medical evidence entitling the claimant to win under `§ 5107(b)`, and reopening the record risks VA shopping for a hostile post-mortem C&P opinion!

### C. The CAVC & Board Death-Timing Matrix (`CAVC Rule 43`, *Smith*, *Breedlove*, *Padgett* & *Phillips*)

When a client dies near or during appellate review, the **exact date of death relative to the BVA decision and CAVC docket** dictates the mandatory litigation motion:

| Timing of Veteran's Death | Legal Status of BVA Decision | Controlling Precedent & Rule | Mandatory Practitioner Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Died *BEFORE* BVA Decision Issued** *(without prior substitution)* | **Legal Nullity (*ultra vires*):** BVA lacked jurisdiction over a deceased appellant (`38 C.F.R. § 20.1302(a)`) | *Smith v. Brown*, `10 Vet. App. 330 (1997)`; *Landicho v. Brown*, `7 Vet. App. 42 (1994)`; *NOVA*, `809 F.3d at 1364` | **(a)** Move BVA (or appeal to CAVC if needed) to **vacate the void BVA decision** so it poses zero res judicata bar; **AND**<br/>**(b)** File **`VA Form 21P-0847`** within **1 year of death** to substitute into the still-pending appeal! *(Remember [`VAOPGCPREC 2-2007`](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md) was withdrawn at `75 FR 11229`!)* |
| **2. Died *AFTER* BVA Decision During `120-Day` NOA Window** (`38 U.S.C. § 7266`) | Valid BVA decision that will become **final roadblock** unless timely appealed | *Breedlove v. Shinseki*, `24 Vet. App. 7 (2010)`; *Pekular v. Mansfield*, `21 Vet. App. 495 (2007)`; *Merritt v. Wilkie*, `965 F.3d 1357 (Fed. Cir. 2020)`; **`CAVC Rule 43(a)(2)`** | **Mandatory Dual-Forum Filing:**<br/>**(a)** File **NOA + Motion to Substitute + new survivor `21-22a`/fee agreement** in **CAVC** within 120 days of BVA decision (plead equitable tolling if death delayed filing); **AND**<br/>**(b)** File **`VA Form 21P-0847` + `21P-534EZ`** at the **VA AOJ** within **1 year of death** (judicial substitution alone moots on remand under *Merritt* if no timely formal VA claim was filed!). |
| **3. Died *AFTER* CAVC NOA Docketed (Pre-Submission)** | Live CAVC appeal | *Phillips v. Shinseki*, `581 F.3d 1358 (Fed. Cir. 2009)`; *Reeves*, `682 F.3d 988`; *Merritt*, `965 F.3d 1357`; **`CAVC Rule 43(a)(1)`** | File **`CAVC Rule 43(a)(1)` Motion to Substitute** (attaching death certificate, marriage/dependency proof, and new survivor `21-22a`) **AND** file **`21P-0847` + `21P-534EZ`** at VA within **1 year of death** (*Merritt*). Preserves merits remand **and EAJA fee standing** (*Phillips*)! |
| **4. Died *AFTER* Case Submitted to CAVC / Fed. Cir. (or No `§ 5121` Payee Exists Post-Submission)** | Live submitted judicial appeal | ***Padgett v. Nicholson*, 473 F.3d 1364 (Fed. Cir. 2007) (en banc)** | Substitute under `Rule 43(a)(1)` **AND/OR** move the Court to enter judgment ***nunc pro tunc* effective the day before the veteran's death**! Crucial if no `§ 5121(a)(2)` payee exists (crystallizing `§ 5121(a)(6)` reimbursement & EAJA standing) or for pre-Oct. 10, 2008 deaths. |

### D. *Nehmer* Class Retroactive Survivor & Estate Payouts (`38 C.F.R. § 3.816(f)`)

Whenever a deceased veteran served in the **Republic of Vietnam** (including `12-nm` **Blue Water Navy** offshore waters under `Pub. L. 116-23` / `VAOPGCPREC 3-2019`) or covered *Nehmer* cohorts and had a post-September 25, 1985 denied claim or in-file "Footnote 1" diagnosis for a covered herbicide condition ([Playbook 06](06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md)), **`38 C.F.R. § 3.816(f)` completely supersedes the harsh restrictions of `§§ 5121` and `5121A`**:

1. **No 1-Year Filing Bar & Unlimited Retroactivity:** Even if the veteran died in 1995 and the family contacts your firm 30 years later, VA **must** audit the C-file and pay **100% of the unpaid retroactive compensation** from the prior post-Sept. 25, 1985 claim/diagnosis date to the veteran's death (`§ 3.816(c), (f)`).
2. **Dramatically Broader Payee Chain (`38 C.F.R. § 3.816(f)(1)(i)–(iv)`):** VA must pay the first living class in order:
   * **`(i)` Surviving Spouse at Death:** Payable **regardless of current marital status** (even if the widow remarried at age 35 and lost `§ 1310` DIC!).
   * **`(ii)` Child(ren) in Equal Shares:** Payable **regardless of age or marital status**—meaning **40- or 60-year-old independent adult children** (natural, adopted, or household stepchildren at death) recover full six-figure *Nehmer* awards despite being completely barred under `§ 5121(a)(2)`!
   * **`(iii)` Parent(s) in Equal Shares:** Payable **regardless of financial dependency**.
   * **`(iv)` The Deceased Class Member's Estate:** If no spouse, child, or parent survives at the time of payment, VA **must pay the deceased class member's probate estate** (via court-appointed executor/administrator or state small-estate affidavit procedures where accepted)!
3. **Mandatory Exclusion Screen (`M21-1 VIII.i.2.B.1.c`):** Remember that PACT Act **hypertension**, **MGUS**, and **chloracne** are **excluded** from `38 C.F.R. § 3.816` *Nehmer* class status ([Playbook 06](06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md)). An adult child or estate **cannot** use `§ 3.816(f)` to collect unpaid lifetime hypertension compensation (though a surviving spouse **can** use `38 U.S.C. § 1305` to win multi-decade retroactive **DIC** if hypertension caused/contributed to death and a prior DIC claim was denied; see [Playbook 08](08-survivor-dic-direct-and-1318-hypothetical.md)!).

## 4. Invariants / Kill-Switches

* **Expired Power of Attorney Trap:** Filing an NOA, `20-0995`, or `21P-0847` signed by counsel under the *deceased veteran's* `VA Form 21-22a` without obtaining a **new `VA Form 21-22a` signed by the survivor/claimant** results in jurisdictional dismissal for unauthorized filing!
* **Confusing the `§ 5110(d)` 1-Year DIC Effective-Date Rule With the `§§ 5121(c) / 5121A` Absolute Bar:** Filing a DIC claim (`21P-534EZ`) `14 months` after death merely forfeits month-of-death DIC retroactivity (paying DIC from claim receipt forward). Filing a `§ 5121` accrued claim or `§ 5121A` substitution request `14 months` after death **permanently extinguishes the entire accrued/substitution claim forever**!
* ***Haines v. West* Post-Mortem CUE Trap:** Never file a new post-mortem `§ 5109A` or `§ 7111` CUE motion expecting `§ 5121` accrued back pay (*Haines*, `154 F.3d 1298`). Check instead for **(1)** an unadjudicated lifetime claim (`Shea`, `Roberson`, *Hamill* `§ 5104(b)`), **(2)** `§ 3.816(f)` *Nehmer* class status, or **(3)** `§ 3.22(b)(1)` CUE solely as the predicate for `§ 1318` DIC ([Playbook 08](08-survivor-dic-direct-and-1318-hypothetical.md)).
* **Single Substitute Representative, Preferred-Class Forfeiture & Successive Substitution (`38 U.S.C. § 5121A(a)(1)–(2)`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.1000(a)(1)(ii), 3.1000(c)(2), 3.1010(a), 3.1010(g)(4)–(5)`):** Upon the death of a veteran with no surviving spouse and multiple eligible minor/helpless/school children, only **one** person may be treated as the formal substitute to prosecute the claim (`§ 5121A(a)(2)`; `§ 3.1010(a)`), while resulting accrued benefits are paid in equal shares to all eligible children (`§ 3.1000(a)(1)(ii)`—*note: `§ 3.1000(a)(2)` governs death of a surviving spouse*). If a preferred-tier survivor waives or misses the 1-year deadline, lower-tier survivors are barred (`§§ 3.1000(c)(2), 3.1010(g)(4)`); **however**, if a timely-substituted survivor **dies while the substituted claim/appeal is still pending**, **`§ 3.1010(g)(5)`** grants the next eligible class member a fresh 1-year window from the *substitute's* death for successive substitution!

## 5. Postconditions: Payment Cutoffs, Retiree Arrears (`Soto`) & Attorney Fees

1. **Exact Payment Boundary & Seamless DIC Handoff (`38 U.S.C. § 5112(b)(1)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.500(g)(1)`):**
   * Lifetime compensation won via `§ 5121`, `§ 5121A`, or `§ 3.816(f)` pays from the veteran's effective date **through the last day of the calendar month PRECEDING the month of death** (veterans do not receive compensation for the month in which they die).
   * If the surviving spouse files `21P-534EZ` within 1 year of death, **DIC begins on the first day of the month OF death** (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(d)`), leaving **zero unpaid gap days**! *(Audit the veteran's bank account for any Treasury reclamation of a posthumously deposited month-of-death VA check vs. `38 U.S.C. § 5122` month-of-death surviving-spouse check rules).*
2. **Parallel DFAS Retiree Arrears & Uncapped CRSC (*Soto v. United States*, `605 U.S. 360 (2025)`):**
   * Whenever a `§ 5121A`, `§ 5121`, or `§ 3.816(f)` award retroactively increases a **military retiree's** lifetime VA compensation, audit whether VA withheld money for military retired-pay offset (`38 U.S.C. §§ 5304–5305`). File a follow-on **Arrears of Pay (`AOP`, `10 U.S.C. § 2771`, `SF 1174`)** and **uncapped `CRSC` (`10 U.S.C. § 1413a`)** claim with DFAS / the service branch CRSC board under *Soto* ([Playbook 06](06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md))—remarkably, **`10 U.S.C. § 2771` DFAS arrears pay out to designated beneficiaries, spouses, adult children, parents, or legal representatives without `§ 5121` minor-child restrictions**!
3. **Attorney Fees & EAJA Alignment (`38 U.S.C. § 5904` & `28 U.S.C. § 2412(d)`):**
   * **Execute a New Fee Agreement Immediately:** Sign a new `38 U.S.C. § 5904` contingency fee agreement (and `VA Form 21-22a`) with the substituting survivor and file within 30 days at VA (`38 C.F.R. § 14.636(g)`). When substituting under `§ 5121A` into a claim that already had an initial AOJ decision during the veteran's life, `§ 5904(c)(1)` fee eligibility carries forward seamlessly; if filing an original standalone `§ 5121` or `§ 3.816(f)` claim with no prior decision on that issue, paid `§ 5904(c)(1)` representation attaches only after an initial AOJ decision on that claim.
   * **EAJA Survivorship & Offset:** Upon `CAVC Rule 43` substitution (or *Padgett* *nunc pro tunc* relief), the substituted survivor maintains full standing to recover **`28 U.S.C. § 2412(d)` EAJA fees** (*Phillips*, `581 F.3d at 1368`), subject to the mandatory dollar-for-dollar `§ 5904` same-work offset ([Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).
