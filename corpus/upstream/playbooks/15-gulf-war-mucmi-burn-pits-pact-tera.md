# Playbook 15: Gulf War Illness (`§ 3.317` `MUCMIs`), PACT Act Burn Pits (`§§ 1119–1120` / `§§ 3.320–3.320b`) & `§ 1168` `TERA` Nexus Strategy

Modern Southwest Asia, post-9/11, and occupational toxic-exposure litigation turns on **three legally distinct statutory engines** that VA examiners and Regional Office (`AOJ`) raters routinely conflate:
1. **Engine 1 (`38 U.S.C. § 1117`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.317`):** **Undiagnosed Illnesses & Medically Unexplained Chronic Multisymptom Illnesses (`MUCMIs`)** + `9` infectious diseases (`6-month` chronicity, **zero nexus required**; pre-Aug. 10, 2022 `10%` floor superseded eff. Aug. 10, 2022 by `Pub. L. 117-168, § 405(a)` `"to any degree at any time"`!).
2. **Engine 2 (`38 U.S.C. §§ 1119–1120`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.320, 3.320a, 3.320b`):** **Burn Pit & Particulate Matter (`PM2.5`) Presumptive Cancers & Respiratory/Hematologic Diseases** (`0%` floor, **zero manifestation deadline**, **zero nexus required**).
3. **Engine 3 (`38 U.S.C. §§ 1168 & 5103A(d)(2)`):** **Non-Presumptive Toxic Exposure Risk Activity (`TERA`) Direct Causation** (mandatory `ILER` audit + synergistic `§ 1168(a)` medical nexus opinion governed by `38 U.S.C. § 5107(b)` equipoise).

## 1. Preconditions: VBMS, Deployment & `ILER` Audit Triggers

Pull the complete VBMS eFolder and screen these exact records before selecting a pleading track:

* **Qualifying Deployment & Exposure Proof:**
  * **`DD Form 214` / `DD Form 215`**, Guard/Reserve **`Title 10` mobilization orders** ([Playbook 11](11-guard-reserve-adt-iadt-and-line-of-duty.md)), **`DA Form 2-1`**, flight logs, ship deck logs (Persian Gulf, Red Sea, Arabian Sea, Gulf of Aden, Gulf of Oman), Hostile Fire / Imminent Danger Pay (`HFP`/`IDP`) LES entries, Post-Deployment Health Assessments (**`DD Form 2796`** / **`DD Form 2900` `PDHRA`**), and DoD Burn Pit Registry questionnaires.
  * **VBMS `TERA` Memorandum & `ILER` (`Individual Longitudinal Exposure Record`) Extract:** Audit whether the RO generated the mandatory **Toxic Exposure Risk Activity (`TERA`) Memorandum** and queried `ILER` + the Defense Occupational and Environmental Health Readiness System (`DOEHRS`). Missing deployment locations (e.g., classified temporary duty at **Karshi-Khanabad (`K2`), Uzbekistan** or **Syria**) or omitted MOS chemical hazards make any downstream `§ 1168` medical opinion legally defective.
* **High-Value C-File Audit Red Flags:**
  * Prior denial of **IBS (`DC 7319`)**, **fibromyalgia (`DC 5025`)**, **CFS (`DC 6354`)**, or chronic multi-site joint/muscle pain/headaches/fatigue where the C&P examiner wrote *"less likely than not related to Gulf War service"* (*Gutierrez* / *Stewart* legal error!).
  * Pre-August 10, 2022 denial of **asthma, chronic rhinitis, or chronic sinusitis** because symptoms manifested `> 10 years` after separation (`86 FR 42724` interim trap eliminated by `38 U.S.C. § 1120`!) or rated `0%` noncompensable (`10%` floor eliminated!).
  * Post-2022 diagnosis of **leukemia, multiple myeloma, `MGUS`, `MDS`, myelofibrosis (`§ 3.320b`)**, **bladder/ureter cancer (`§ 3.320a`)**, or **urethral/paraurethral/male breast cancer (`89 FR 51224`)**.

## 3. Engine 1: Persian Gulf War Undiagnosed Illnesses & `MUCMIs` (`38 U.S.C. § 1117`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.317`)

### A. Geographic Scope & The Pre- vs. Post-PACT Afghanistan Trap
1. **Classic `§ 3.317(e)(2)` Southwest Asia Theater of Operations (`>= Aug. 2, 1990`):** Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, the neutral zone between Iraq and Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Qatar, the United Arab Emirates (`UAE`), Oman, the Gulf of Aden, the Gulf of Oman, the Persian Gulf, the Arabian Sea, the Red Sea, and the airspace above these locations.
2. **PACT Act `§ 405` Statutory Expansion (`38 U.S.C. § 1117(f)`, eff. Aug. 10, 2022) vs. `38 C.F.R. § 3.317` Lag:**
   * **Pre-August 10, 2022 Trap:** Prior to the PACT Act, **Afghanistan** was covered **only** under `38 C.F.R. § 3.317(c)` for `9` specific infectious diseases (`75 FR 59970`, Sept. 29, 2010: brucellosis, *Campylobacter jejuni*, Q fever, malaria, *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*, nontyphoid *Salmonella*, *Shigella*, visceral leishmaniasis, and West Nile virus)—**NOT** for `§ 3.317(a)` undiagnosed illnesses or `MUCMIs`!
   * **On/After August 10, 2022 (`Pub. L. 117-168, § 405(b)`, `136 Stat. 1783`):** Congress enacted **`38 U.S.C. § 1117(f)`**, expanding `§ 1117` `MUCMI` and undiagnosed-illness eligibility (`>= Aug. 2, 1990` or `>= Sept. 11, 2001` as applicable) to active service in **Afghanistan, Israel, Egypt, Turkey, Syria, and Jordan**!
   * **Statute Trumps Stale CFR Text (`0%` Floor + `6` New Countries + No Sunset):** Although VA's conforming revision to `38 C.F.R. § 3.317` (`89 FR 79815`, Oct. 1, 2024, `RIN 2900-AR75` — *proposed / not finalized*) lagged behind the statute—leaving stale `10 percent or more` and pre-PACT country text in `eCFR`—**positive statutory text in `38 U.S.C. § 1117(a)(1) & (f)` controls immediately from August 10, 2022**: `(a)` replacing the legacy `10%` manifestation threshold with **"became manifest to any degree at any time"** (`0%` floor!), `(b)` adding those `6` countries, and `(c)` **permanently abolishing the former December 31, 2026 manifestation sunset** (`87 FR 6038`)!

### B. Qualifying `§ 3.317(a)` Disabilities, Thresholds & Appellate Weapons
To establish presumptive service connection under `38 U.S.C. § 1117` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.317(a)(1)`:
* **Severity & Chronicity Floor (Pre- vs. Post-Aug. 10, 2022 Trap):** Under `§ 3.317(a)(4)`, a qualifying chronic disability must have persisted for **`6 months` or more** without affirmative evidence attributing it to an intercurrent post-service cause (`§ 3.317(a)(7)`). **Watch the bitemporal `10%` trap:** for decisions prior to **August 10, 2022**, `38 U.S.C. § 1117(a)(1)` required manifestation to a **compensable degree of `10%` or more**; effective **August 10, 2022**, `Pub. L. 117-168, § 405(a)` amended `§ 1117(a)(1)` to cover disabilities manifesting **"to any degree at any time"**—making any post-Aug. 10, 2022 denial that relies on stale `eCFR` `§ 3.317(a)(1)(i)`'s `10%` threshold unlawful!
* **Two Substantive Prongs (`§ 3.317(a)(2)(i)(A)` vs. `(B)`):**
  1. **Undiagnosed Illness (`§ 3.317(a)(2)(i)(A)`):** Objective or lay-observable chronic signs/symptoms (`§ 3.317(b)` — fatigue, skin symptoms, headache, muscle pain, joint pain, neurological signs, neuropsychological signs, upper/lower respiratory signs, sleep disturbances, GI signs, cardiovascular signs, abnormal weight loss, menstrual disorders) that cannot be attributed to any known clinical diagnosis.
     * ***Joyner v. McDonald*, `766 F.3d 1393, 1395 (Fed. Cir. 2014)`:** **Pain alone** (such as chronic multi-joint, neck, or muscle pain without somatic pathology on imaging) qualifies as an undiagnosed illness under `§ 3.317`!
  2. **Medically Unexplained Chronic Multisymptom Illness (`MUCMI`, `§ 3.317(a)(2)(i)(B)`):** A **diagnosed** illness defined by a cluster of signs or symptoms without conclusive pathophysiology or etiology, **expressly including by law**:
     * **Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (`CFS`, `DC 6354`)**;
     * **Fibromyalgia (`DC 5025`)**; and
     * **Functional Gastrointestinal Disorders (`FGIDs`)** (`76 FR 41696`, pub. July 15, 2011, **eff. Aug. 15, 2011**), including **Irritable Bowel Syndrome (`IBS`, `DC 7319` — up to `30%` under the May 19, 2024 Digestive VASRD `89 FR 19735`!)**, functional dyspepsia, functional vomiting, functional constipation, functional abdominal pain syndrome, and functional bloating (*excluding structural GI diseases like IBD/Crohn's, ulcerative colitis, or celiac disease*).
* **Three Controlling Precedents That Destroy Defective `§ 3.317` Denials:**
  * ***Gutierrez v. Principi*, `19 Vet. App. 1, 8–9 (2004)`:** A claimant under `§ 1117` / `§ 3.317` is **NEVER required to prove a medical nexus** to service or to a specific environmental toxin! Whenever a C&P examiner opines *"fibromyalgia/IBS is less likely than not caused by Gulf War service,"* the opinion is legally irrelevant and non-responsive—once service in a covered location and a `6-month` chronic diagnosis of `CFS`, `fibromyalgia`, or `IBS`/`FGID` (at `>= 10%` pre-Aug. 10, 2022, or to *any degree* `>= Aug. 10, 2022` under `§ 1117(a)(1)`) are shown, **entitlement is automatic by operation of law** (absent affirmative proof of an intercurrent supervening cause like traumatic abdominal surgery).
  * ***Goodman v. Shulkin*, `870 F.3d 1383, 1386–88 (Fed. Cir. 2017)` & *Stewart v. Wilkie*, `30 Vet. App. 383, 390–93 (2018)`:** For conditions *outside* the three per se `MUCMI` categories, whether a diagnosed multisymptom illness has "inconclusive pathophysiology or etiology" is a **case-specific medical question**—an examiner cannot reject `MUCMI` status merely because *risk factors* exist if neither the exact etiology nor pathophysiology in *this veteran* is conclusive (*contrast conditions with partially understood pathology like diabetes, MS, or OSA excluded under `§ 3.317(a)(2)(ii)`—plead those under Engine 3 `§ 1168` TERA!*).

## 4. Engine 2: Burn Pits, `PM2.5` & PACT Act Presumptions (`38 U.S.C. §§ 1119–1120`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.320, 3.320a, 3.320b`)

### A. Two Covered Location & Date Windows (`38 U.S.C. § 1119(c)(1)`)
1. **Window A (`On or after August 2, 1990`):** Active military, naval, air, or space service in **Bahrain, Iraq, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Somalia, or the United Arab Emirates (`UAE`)**, **plus the airspace above** any of those locations *(Note: unlike `§ 3.317(e)(2)`, `§ 1119(c)(1)(A)` omits surrounding open seas unless the servicemember entered territorial land/airspace—use `§ 1168` TERA for shipboard exhaust/incinerators on open seas!)*.
2. **Window B (`On or after September 11, 2001`):** Active military, naval, air, or space service in **Afghanistan, Djibouti, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, Yemen, or Uzbekistan** (explicitly covering **Karshi-Khanabad (`K2`) Air Base**), **plus the airspace above** any of those locations.

### B. Complete Presumptive Disease Architecture (`0%` Floor & Zero Time Limit!)
Under **`38 U.S.C. § 1120(b)`** and **`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.320, 3.320a, 3.320b`** (`88 FR 60336`, pub. Sept. 1, 2023, **eff. Oct. 31, 2023**), Congress **eliminated both the `10%` severity floor and the `10-year` post-service manifestation cap** that had crippled VA's `2021` `PM2.5` interim rule (`86 FR 42724`). Even a **`0%` noncompensable diagnosis** (e.g., `0%` chronic rhinitis without polyps or `<50%` nasal obstruction under `DC 6522`) **must be granted service connection**, unlocking lifetime VHA health care, future increased ratings, and secondary service connection (`§ 3.310`, e.g., secondary obstructive sleep apnea `DC 6847`!).

## 5. Engine 3: Non-Presumptive `38 U.S.C. § 1168` `TERA` Nexus Litigation

For *every* non-presumptive condition—such as **Obstructive Sleep Apnea (`OSA`, `DC 6847`)**, **hypertension** (in non-herbicide veterans), **autoimmune/neurological/hepatic/thyroid disorders**, or **CONUS/shipboard/MOS exposures**—PACT Act Section `303` enacted **`38 U.S.C. § 1168`** and **`38 U.S.C. § 5103A(d)(2)`**:

### A. Triggering Mandatory `§ 1168(a)` TERA Examinations Beyond Overseas Burn Pits
Whenever `(1)` the veteran has a current disability, `(2)` the record reflects participation in **any Toxic Exposure Risk Activity (`TERA`, `38 U.S.C. § 1710(e)(4)(C)`)**, and `(3)` record evidence is insufficient to grant outright, VA **must** order a medical examination and nexus opinion that **explicitly considers the total potential exposure through all applicable deployments AND the synergistic, combined effect of all toxic exposure risk activities** (`38 U.S.C. § 1168(a)(2)`):
* **High-Yield Occupational / CONUS `TERA` Pathways:**
  * **PFAS / `AFFF` Aqueous Film-Forming Foam:** Military firefighters (`DoD` crash-rescue MOS), flight-deck crews, and airbases with documented `PFAS` groundwater plumes (linked to kidney/testicular cancer, thyroid disease, ulcerative colitis, dyslipidemia).
  * **CONUS & Overseas Base Contamination:** **Fort McClellan, Alabama** (`PCBs` / Monsanto plume, chemical-corps training—no statutory presumption yet, making `§ 1168` + `ILER`/MOS proof essential!), **Camp Lejeune** non-presumptive conditions ([Playbook 07](07-statutory-presumptions-chronic-tropical-pow-als.md)), **George AFB / Kelly AFB / Wurtsmith AFB** (`TCE`/perchloroethylene degreasers).
  * **Jet Fuels (`JP-8` / `JP-4` / `JP-5`), Solvents, Shipboard Asbestos (`VAOPGCPREC 4-2000`), Heavy Metals/Lead (indoor firing ranges / artillery / mechanics), & Depleted Uranium / Radar.**
* **Narrow `§ 1168(b)` Exception:** VA may decline a `§ 1168(a)` exam **only** if it determines there is *no indication of an association* (e.g., pure acute orthopedic trauma or standalone `§ 4.130` psychiatric disorders without neurotoxicological/secondary linkage)—and VA **must submit a written report to Congress** justifying exemptions (`§ 1168(b)`).

### B. Destroying Boilerplate Negative `§ 1168` C&P Opinions on Appeal
Contract C&P examiners routinely rubber-stamp negative `TERA` opinions citing generic `National Academies` (`NASEM`) tables and claiming *"scientific literature does not conclusively prove a causal link."* Attack these opinions on three precedential grounds (`VA Form 20-0996` HLR or `VA Form 10182` Board appeal):
1. **Impermissible Elevation of the `§ 5107(b)` Standard of Proof:** Requiring epidemiological certainty (`"conclusive proof"` or `"established positive association"` in population studies) conflates the standard for Congress creating a *nationwide statutory presumption* with individual direct service connection under **`38 U.S.C. § 5107(b)` ("at least as likely as not" equipoise)** (*Stefl v. Nicholson*, `21 Vet. App. 120, 124 (2007)`; *Polovick v. Shinseki*, `23 Vet. App. 48, 53 (2009)`).
2. **Violation of Statutory Synergistic-Exposure Mandate (`38 U.S.C. § 1168(a)(2)`):** If the veteran had *multiple* exposures (e.g., Iraq burn pits + `JP-8` fuel handling + `AFFF` foam) and the examiner analyzed only burn pits in isolation—or ignored the `ILER` / `TERA` memo—the exam violates `§ 1168(a)(2)` as a matter of law.
3. **Dual-Track Pleading for Sleep Apnea (`DC 6847`):** Never rely on a bare `TERA` theory for `OSA` alone! Always plead **both** `(a)` **secondary service connection (`38 C.F.R. § 3.310`)** to presumptive **chronic rhinitis (`DC 6522`) / chronic sinusitis (`DC 6510–6514`) / asthma (`DC 6602`)** (upper-airway nasal resistance collapsing pharyngeal patency during sleep, or corticosteroid/weight-gain intermediate link under `VAOPGCPREC 1-2017`) **AND** `(b)` direct `§ 1168` `TERA` particulate airway inflammation! *(Watchlist Guardrail: VA's Feb. 15, 2022 proposal to curtail `50%` CPAP ratings under `DC 6847`, `RIN 2900-AQ72` / `87 FR 8474`, remains **proposed / not finalized**—current `50%` CPAP criteria govern!)*
