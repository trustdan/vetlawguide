# Playbook 22: Unauthorized Emergency Medical Reimbursement (`§§ 1728` vs. `1725`, *Staab* & *Wolfe* Coinsurance) & `PCAFC` Family Caregiver Stipend Appeals (*Beaudette*)

Veterans Law firms routinely leave six-figure non-Part-4 recoveries on the table—or forfeit CAVC `EAJA` fees—when VHA's Office of Community Care (`OCC`) denies `$50,000–$500,000+` private emergency hospital and air-ambulance bills under **`38 U.S.C. §§ 1725 / 1728`**, or when VHA's Caregiver Support Program (`CSP`) denies or slashes monthly **`PCAFC` (`38 U.S.C. § 1720G`)** caregiver stipends. Following ***Wolfe v. McDonough*, `28 F.4th 1348 (Fed. Cir. 2022)`** and ***Beaudette v. McDonough*, `93 F.4th 1361 (Fed. Cir. 2024)`**, both domains are prime BVA and CAVC litigation dockets.

*(Cross-references: Verbatim `§§ 17.1002, 17.4000` & related regulatory snapshots in [`38-cfr-3.159-17.1002-17.4000-21.282-evidence-emergency-pcafc-vre.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.159-17.1002-17.4000-21.282-evidence-emergency-pcafc-vre.md); AMA & fee evolution in [`ama-part-19-20-and-part-14-fee-evolution.md`](../cfr-diffs/part-20/ama-part-19-20-and-part-14-fee-evolution.md); [CAVC, Federal Circuit, Writs & Class Actions Treatise](../docs/cavc-federal-circuit-writs-and-class-actions.md); [AMA Lanes & BVA Litigation Manual](../docs/ama-lanes-and-bva-litigation-manual.md); [Attorney Fees (`§ 5904`) & `EAJA` Practice Treatise](../docs/attorney-fees-5904-and-eaja-practice.md); [`CAVC Appeal, EAJA & Fee Compliance Gate`](../checklists/cavc-appeal-eaja-and-fee-compliance-gate.md); [Playbook 23 (`Ch. 31` VR&E & *Rudisill*)](23-vre-chapter-31-retroactive-induction-and-rudisill.md); [Playbook 12 (`SMC(l)`, `SMC(r)` & `SMC(t)`)](12-aid-and-attendance-smc-l-r1-r2-and-smc-t.md); [Playbook 20 (Offsets & `§ 5904(d)` Fee Base)](20-backpay-offsets-crdp-crsc-severance-recoupment.md); [Playbook 21 (Overpayment Waivers)](21-overpayment-waivers-incompetency-and-wartime-pension.md); [Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md); and [Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).*

## 1. Preconditions: VHA `OCC`, Billing, Ambulance & `PCAFC` Intake Triggers

Demand both the **VBMS Compensation eFolder** and the **VHA Office of Community Care (`OCC`) / Caregiver Support Program (`CSP`) administrative record** (`FOIA/Privacy Act` to the local VAMC Privacy Officer + VHA Payment Operations in St. Paul/Denver) whenever intake reveals:

* **Emergency Medical & Ambulance Billing Artifacts (`§§ 1728, 1725, 1703`):**
  * **`VA Form 10-583`** (*Claim for Payment of Cost of Unauthorized Medical Services*), institutional hospital **`UB-04` (`CMS-1450`)**, physician/air-ambulance **`CMS-1500`**, itemized billing ledgers, and third-party **Explanation of Benefits (`EOB`)** or Medicare Remittance Advice (`MSN`/`RA`) breaking down billed charges, contractual adjustments, **deductibles**, **copayments**, and **percentage-based coinsurance**.
  * **VHA `OCC` / `Optum` / `TriWest` Denial Codes:** Boilerplate denials citing *"Other Health Insurance (`OHI`) made payment"*, *"Veteran not an active VA health-care participant within 24 months"*, *"72-hour emergency notification not received"*, *"Condition not service-connected"*, *"Does not meet prudent layperson criteria"*, *"Post-stabilization care unauthorized"*, or *"Timely filing limit exceeded"*.
  * **ED Triage & Hospital Transfer-Center Logs:** Emergency Department intake nursing triage vitals/chief complaint (establishing symptoms *at presentation* under `§ 17.1002(b)`), physician stabilization notes, and hospital transfer-center phone logs showing VAMC bed unavailability (`"VAMC on diversion / no ICU or telemetry bed available"`).
* **PCAFC Caregiver Stipend Artifacts (`38 U.S.C. § 1720G`; `38 C.F.R. Part 71`):**
  * **`VA Form 10-10CG`** (*Application for the Program of Comprehensive Assistance for Family Caregivers*), **Centralized Eligibility and Appeals Team (`CEAT`)** decision letters, `Katz ADL` / clinical functional assessments, VHA Clinical Appeals (`VHA Directive 1041`) records, and reassessment tier-reduction or `60-day` advance revocation/discharge notices (`38 C.F.R. § 71.45`).

## 3. Emergency Medical Reimbursement Matrix (`§ 1728` vs. `§ 1725` vs. `§ 1703` & `§ 1720J`)

### A. Rule #1 of Intake: Always Screen `38 U.S.C. § 1728` BEFORE `38 U.S.C. § 1725`!

Practitioners and VHA payment clerks routinely default to `§ 1725` and walk straight into fatal `24-month` VHA-user, `90-day` filing, and OHI deductible/copayment traps that **do not exist under `38 U.S.C. § 1728`** (see [`38-cfr-3.159-17.1002-17.4000-21.282-evidence-emergency-pcafc-vre.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.159-17.1002-17.4000-21.282-evidence-emergency-pcafc-vre.md)):

| Statutory Element | `38 U.S.C. § 1728` (`38 C.F.R. §§ 17.120–17.132`) | `38 U.S.C. § 1725` (`38 C.F.R. §§ 17.1000–17.1008`) | `38 U.S.C. § 1703` (`38 C.F.R. § 17.4020(c)`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Qualifying Clinical / Disability Basis** | **(1)** Adjudicated **SC disability** (`§ 17.120(a)(1)`); **(2)** **Adjunct** condition aggravating an SC disability (`§ 17.120(a)(2)`); **(3)** **ANY disability (SC or non-SC!)** of a veteran rated **`100% P&T`** (`§ 1728(a)(2)(D)`; `§ 17.120(a)(3)`); OR **(4)** **ANY care** needed by an active **`Ch. 31` VR&E** participant (`§ 1728(a)(2)(C)`; `§ 17.120(a)(4)`)! | **Non-service-connected (`NSC`)** emergency medical condition where no other VA authority (`§ 1728`, `§ 1703`, `§ 1720J`) covers the care (`§ 17.1002(a)–(f)`). | Authorized Community Care emergency treatment for VHA-enrolled/eligible veterans (`§ 17.4010`) when VA is notified within **`72 hours`** (`§ 17.4020(c)`). *(Also check **`38 U.S.C. § 1720J` [`COMPACT Act`, `§§ 17.1200–17.1230`]** for `$0`-cost acute suicidal crisis care regardless of VHA enrollment!)* |
| **`24-Month` Prior VHA Healthcare Rule** | **NONE!** Veteran need **never** have used VHA healthcare within the prior 24 months! | **STRICTLY REQUIRED (`§ 1725(b)(2)(B)`; `§ 17.1002(d)`):** Must be VHA-enrolled AND have received VA care within **`24 months`** before the emergency! | VHA enrollment (or statutory exemption under `§ 17.4005`/`§ 17.4010`); **no** strict `24-month` visit bar if `72-hr` notice is met! |
| **OHI Cost-Sharing (`Deductible`, `Copay`, `Coinsurance`)** | **PAYS ALL THREE (`§ 1728(b)`; `38 C.F.R. § 17.129`)!** Reimburses **deductibles, fixed copayments, AND coinsurance** in full! | **PAYS COINSURANCE ONLY (*Wolfe*; `§ 17.1005(a)(5)`)!** **Deductibles & fixed copayments are BARRED** (`§ 1725(c)(4)(D)`). | VA pays the community provider directly at contract/Medicare rates; **zero** OHI cost-share owed by the veteran (only standard VA copay, if any). |
| **Notice / Claim Filing Deadline** | **`2 YEARS` (`38 C.F.R. § 17.126`):** Within `2 years` of care (`§ 17.126(a)`) OR within **`2 years` of VBA SC/`P&T` grant notice** (`§ 17.126(b)`). | **`90 DAYS` (`38 C.F.R. § 17.1004(d)`):** Within `90 days` after the *latest* of discharge, death in stay/transport, OR **final exhaustion of third-party/OHI payment (`§ 17.1004(d)(3)`)**! | **`72 HOURS` (`38 C.F.R. § 17.4020(c)`):** Hospital, veteran, or family must notify VHA centralized portal/hotline within **`72 hours`** of start of care! |

### B. Six Substantive Rules Governing `§ 1725` & `§ 1728` Litigation

1. **The `"Prudent Layperson"` Standard (`§ 1725(f)(1)`; `§ 17.1002(b)` & `§ 17.120(b)`):**
   * Emergency status is judged **exclusively by acute symptoms at initial presentation**—whether a *prudent layperson with an average knowledge of health and medicine* could reasonably expect the absence of immediate medical attention to place health in serious jeopardy or cause serious impairment to bodily functions or organs.
   * **Reversible VHA/Board Error:** Denying reimbursement because the *final discharge diagnosis* proved non-lethal (e.g., veteran rushed to ER with crushing substernal chest pain and diaphoresis, discharged 18 hours later with `"esophageal spasm / panic attack"`). Pull the **ED nursing triage note** to win outright!
2. **Stabilization Cutoff & The `"No Available VA Bed"` Exception (`38 C.F.R. § 17.1002(c)` & `§ 17.120(c)`):**
   * Unauthorized reimbursement normally stops at the point of **clinical stabilization** (when transfer to a VAMC can occur without material clinical deterioration).
   * **Crucial Exception (`§ 17.1002(c)`):** VHA **must continue paying post-stabilization inpatient care** if a VAMC or contracted facility **was not feasibly available or could not accept the transfer** (e.g., VAMC lacked an ICU/cardiac bed or declined transfer), *provided* the non-VA facility contacted VA reasonably promptly upon stabilization! Always subpoena/request the private hospital's **Transfer Center log**!
3. **Satisfying the `24-Month` Active VHA User Gate (`§ 1725(b)(2)(B)`; `§ 17.1002(d)`):**
   * Audit VAMC `VistA`/`CPRS` records for *any* qualifying healthcare episode within the `730 days` preceding ER presentation: VAMC primary-care/specialty visits, **telephone/video telehealth clinical encounters**, **VA mail-order pharmacy prescription fills/renewals** managed by a VA provider, VA-authorized `§ 1703` Community Care appointments, or Vet Center readjustment counseling!
4. **Never Conflate `72-Hour` `§ 1703` Notice With `90-Day` `§ 1725` or `2-Year` `§ 1728` Filing:**
   * Missing VHA's **`72-hour` centralized notification (`38 C.F.R. § 17.4020(c)`)** only prevents VHA from *pre-authorizing* the admission under the `§ 1703` Veterans Community Care Program—it **NEVER** bars a timely **`90-day` `§ 1725` claim (`§ 17.1004(d)`)** or **`2-year` `§ 1728` claim (`§ 17.126`)**! And under **`§ 17.1004(d)(3)`**, when Medicare or private OHI adjudicates or appeals the hospital claim for months after discharge, the `90-day` `§ 1725` clock **does not start** until the veteran *finally exhausts* third-party recovery!
5. **Surgical OHI Cost-Sharing Precision (*Staab*, *Wolfe*, *Kimmel* & `88 FR 10835`):**
   * ***Staab v. McDonald*, `28 Vet. App. 50 (2016)`** struck down VA's total bar against veterans whose OHI paid *any* fraction of an emergency bill (codified by Interim Final Rule **`83 FR 974`**, pub. & eff. `2018-01-09`). VA responded in `2018` by amending `38 C.F.R. § 17.1005(a)(5)` to bar **all** `"copayment[s], deductible[s], coinsurance, or similar payment[s]"`.
   * ***Wolfe v. Wilkie*, `32 Vet. App. 1 (2019)`**, ***rev'd*, *Wolfe v. McDonough*, `28 F.4th 1348 (Fed. Cir. 2022)`**: Plead the Federal Circuit's three-part holding with exact precision:
     1. *Mandamus Reversed:* CAVC erred in issuing a class-wide writ of mandamus because AMA/BVA administrative appeals (`-> CAVC Direct Review`) provided an adequate ordinary remedy.
     2. *Deductibles & Fixed Copayments Excluded:* Under `38 U.S.C. § 1725(c)(4)(D)`, both **fixed copayments** (flat dollar fee per visit) and **annual deductibles** ARE excluded `"copayment[s] or similar payment[s]"`—VA **cannot** reimburse them under `§ 1725`!
     3. *Coinsurance MUST Be Paid:* **Percentage-based coinsurance** (e.g., `20%` Medicare Part A/B or private insurance share of a `$150,000` surgical/air-ambulance bill = `$30,000`!) is **NOT** a `"similar payment"` and **MUST be reimbursed by VA**!
   * Following *Wolfe* and the Federal Circuit's `38 U.S.C. § 502` mandate in ***Kimmel v. Secretary of Veterans Affairs*, `No. 22-1754, 2022 WL 16337653 (Fed. Cir. Oct. 25, 2022) (nonprecedential order)`**, VA promulgated **`88 FR 10835` (pub. `2023-02-22`, eff. `2023-04-24`)**, amending **`38 C.F.R. § 17.1005(a)(5)`** to pay **coinsurance** (and opening a 1-year `§ 17.1004(f)` retroactive filing window through Feb. 22, 2024 for emergency care rendered between Feb. 1, 2010 and Feb. 22, 2023).
6. **Provider Balance-Billing Extinguishment (`§ 1725(a)(2)(A)` & `(e)`) & Delayed Air-Ambulance Cap (`88 FR 10032` -> `2029`):**
   * **Liability Extinguished (`§ 1725(a)(2)(A), (e)`, `§ 17.1005(a)(4)`):** Payment by VA under `§ 1725` (typically at `70%` Medicare rates when VA is primary payer) **legally extinguishes the veteran's entire personal liability** to the hospital or ambulance company (apart from non-reimbursable OHI deductibles/copayments)—stopping illegal balance-billing in its tracks!
   * **Emergency Air/Ground Ambulance Watchlist (`38 U.S.C. §§ 111(b)(3), 1725, 1728`; `38 C.F.R. § 70.30`):** On Feb. 16, 2023, VA published **`88 FR 10032`** to slash non-contracted Beneficiary Travel (`Part 70`) special-mode ambulance payments to the lesser of actual charges or the **Medicare Part B Ambulance Fee Schedule**. **CRITICAL DELAY:** VA delayed `88 FR 10032` first to Feb. 16, 2025 (**`88 FR 90120`**, Dec. 29, 2023) and then **delayed its effective date until `February 16, 2029` (`89 FR 88888`, pub. Nov. 12, 2024)**! Reject any premature VHA attempt to apply the delayed `88 FR 10032` `Part 70` Medicare cap prior to **Feb. 16, 2029**!

## 4. `PCAFC` Family Caregiver Stipend Litigation (`38 U.S.C. § 1720G`; `38 C.F.R. Part 71`)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **`CFR` Citation Trap (`38 C.F.R. Part 71` vs. `Part 17`):** `PCAFC` is codified in **`38 C.F.R. Part 71` (`§§ 71.10–71.60`)** (`85 FR 46226`, pub. `2020-07-31`, eff. `2020-10-01`)—**NOT** `38 C.F.R. §§ 17.4000–17.4040` (which governs the `§ 1703` Veterans Community Care Program, including `§ 17.4010` community-care eligibility and `§ 17.4020(c)` `72-hour` emergency notice)! Always cite **`§§ 71.15, 71.20, 71.40 & 71.45`** in `PCAFC` briefs!

1. **Judicial Review Unlocked (*Beaudette v. McDonough*, `93 F.4th 1361 (Fed. Cir. 2024)`, *aff'g* `34 Vet. App. 95 (2021)`):**
   * For over a decade (`2011–2021`), VA refused to allow BVA appeals on `PCAFC` claims, claiming `38 U.S.C. § 1720G(c)(1)` (*"decisions ... affecting the furnishing of assistance or support ... shall be deemed to be medical determinations"*) barred Board and CAVC jurisdiction.
   * The Federal Circuit **unanimously affirmed CAVC's class-wide mandamus order**, holding that `§ 1720G(c)(1)` insulates only narrow clinical choices regarding *how* support is furnished, whereas **all `PCAFC` eligibility, stipend tier-level, effective-date, reassessment, and revocation determinations are fully reviewable through the AMA (`VHA HLR / Supplemental / Board Form 10182 -> CAVC`)**!
2. **Substantive `PCAFC` Eligibility & Tier-2 Proof Contract (`85 FR 46226`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 71.15, 71.20, 71.40`):**
   * **Threshold Rating (`§ 71.20(a)(2)`):** Single or combined SC disability rating of **`70%` or higher** (for all service eras as of Oct. 1, 2022).
   * **`6-Month` Personal Care Requirement (`§ 71.20(a)(3)` & *Veteran Warriors*):** Veteran must need in-person personal care services for a **minimum of `6 continuous months`** based on *either*:
     * **(a) Inability to perform an `ADL` (`§ 71.15`):** Dressing/undressing, bathing, grooming, adjusting prosthetics/orthotics, toileting, feeding, or mobility *(Note: In ***Veteran Warriors, Inc. v. Secretary of Veterans Affairs*, `29 F.4th 1320 (Fed. Cir. 2022)`**, the Federal Circuit **invalidated** VA's ultra-restrictive `"each and every time"` ADL definition in `§ 71.15`—reject any `CEAT` denial demanding 100%-of-the-time physical dependence on a single ADL!)*; **OR**
     * **(b) Need for Supervision, Protection, or Instruction (`§ 71.15`):** Functional impairment at home impacting ability to maintain personal safety on a **daily basis** (dementia, severe TBI, active psychosis/dissociation, seizures, fall risk).
   * **Level 1 (`62.5%`) vs. Level 2 (`100%`) Monthly Stipend Math (`38 C.F.R. § 71.40(c)(4)`):** Calculated from the annual **`OPM` `GS-4, Step 1`** rate (including **locality pay** for the veteran's residence ZIP code!) divided by 12:
     * **Level 1 (`62.5%` of `GS-4, Step 1`):** Meets baseline `§ 71.20` eligibility (`~$1,800–$2,300+/month` tax-free depending on locality).
     * **Level 2 (`100%` of `GS-4, Step 1`):** Veteran is **`"unable to self-sustain in the community"`** (`§ 71.15` — requires personal care services *each time* completing **3 or more ADLs** with full caregiver dependence, OR requires supervision, protection, or instruction on a **continuous daily basis**, paying `~$2,900–$3,700+/month` tax-free + **`CHAMPVA`** health coverage for uninsured Primary Caregivers!).
3. **`60-Day` Advance Notice + `90-Day` Post-Discharge Continuation (`§ 71.45`) & Legacy Cohort Protection Through `Sept. 30, 2028` (`90 FR 46477`):**
   * Under **`38 C.F.R. § 71.45(a)(1)(iii) & (b)(1)(ii)(A)`**, VA-initiated revocations (noncompliance) or discharges (clinical `§ 71.20` non-eligibility, absent fraud or safety emergencies) require **`60-day` advance written notice prior to the effective date of revocation or discharge**, **PLUS `90 days` of post-discharge stipend/benefit continuation** (`§ 71.45(b)(1)(iii)`) following the effective date of qualifying discharges (reassessment non-eligibility, veteran death, or institutionalization).
   * **Legacy Cohort Freeze (`90 FR 46477`, pub. Sept. 29, 2025, eff. Sept. 30, 2025):** For **Legacy Participants and Legacy Applicants** (approved or applying before Oct. 1, 2020, `§ 71.20(b)–(c)`), VA extended transition and reassessment protections (`87 FR 57602` -> **`90 FR 46477`**) through **`September 30, 2028`**—barring VHA from discharging or reducing a legacy caregiver's stipend based on reassessment before **Sept. 30, 2028**! *(Quarantine Note: VA's Dec. 6, 2024 `PCAFC` NPRM at **`89 FR 97404` [`RIN 2900-AR96`]** remains **proposed / not finalized**—never cite its proposed criteria as enacted law!)*
   * **Zero Offset With `SMC(l)` / `SMC(r)(1)` / `SMC(t)` ([Playbook 12](12-aid-and-attendance-smc-l-r1-r2-and-smc-t.md)):** `PCAFC` stipends (`Ch. 17`, paid to the caregiver) **stack `100%` concurrently** with `SMC(l)`, `SMC(r)(1)`, and TBI `SMC(t)` (`Ch. 11`, paid to the veteran)!

## 5. Invariants (`Kill-Switches`)

* **`§ 1725` Deductible / Copay Pleading Trap:** Never plead for reimbursement of OHI **deductibles** or **fixed copayments** under `§ 1725` (*Wolfe*, `28 F.4th at 1358–62`); isolate **percentage coinsurance** under `§ 17.1005(a)(5)` or establish SC / `100% P&T` / `Ch. 31` status to unlock full deductible + copay + coinsurance recovery under **`§ 1728` (`§ 17.129`)**.
* **Missing the `2-Year` Post-SC-Grant Clock (`§ 17.126(b)`):** Whenever a firm wins a retroactive SC or `100% P&T` award in Compensation, immediately audit the prior period for unpaid/out-of-pocket ER or ambulance bills and file `VA Form 10-583` under `§ 1728` within **`2 years` of the rating notification letter**.
* **Single-Signatory `PCAFC` Retainer Error:** Signing only the Veteran on a `PCAFC` appeal when the **Primary Family Caregiver** is the legal payee of the `§ 71.40(c)(4)` monthly stipend!

## 6. Postconditions: Arrears Payees & Structuring `§ 5904` + `EAJA` Fee Recovery

1. **Exact Payee & Cash-Flow Mechanics (`§§ 1725/1728` vs. `PCAFC`):**
   * **`§§ 1725 / 1728` Awards:** If the veteran already paid the hospital or OHI coinsurance out-of-pocket, VHA disburses a **lump-sum cash reimbursement directly to the veteran**; if the hospital/ambulance bill remains unpaid, VHA pays the **non-VA provider directly**, extinguishing the veteran's debt (`§ 1725(a)(2)(A)`).
   * **`PCAFC` Stipend Arrears (`38 U.S.C. § 1720G(a)(3)(A)(ii)(V)`; `38 C.F.R. § 71.40(c)(4)`):** Monthly stipends and retroactive tier-increase/grant arrears (running back to the `VA Form 10-10CG` receipt date!) are paid **directly to the Primary Family Caregiver**, *not* the veteran. Always execute **`VA Form 21-22a`** and a representation agreement signed by **BOTH the Veteran AND the Primary Family Caregiver**!
2. **Firm Fee Engineering (`28 U.S.C. § 2412(d)` `EAJA` + `38 U.S.C. § 5904` Non-Withheld Clauses):**
   * **Why `§ 5904(d)` `20%` Direct-Pay Withholding Often Fails in VHA:** VHA (`OCC` / `CSP`) disburses medical and caregiver payments outside VBA's `VBMS-Awards` check-splitting engine, and when VHA pays an unpaid `$180,000` hospital bill directly to a provider, **zero cash is payable to the veteran** from which VA could withhold a direct-pay fee (*cf. Gumpenberger v. Wilkie*, `973 F.3d 1379 (Fed. Cir. 2020)` & `38 C.F.R. § 14.636(h)(3)`).
   * **Compliant Two-Pillar Fee Contract ([Attorney Fees & `EAJA` Treatise](../docs/attorney-fees-5904-and-eaja-practice.md)):**
     1. **CAVC `EAJA` Fee Shifting (`28 U.S.C. § 2412(d)`):** Primary revenue engine on BVA denials of `§§ 1725/1728` (especially provider-paid hospital/ambulance bills!) and `PCAFC` appeals—recovering full CPI-U hourly fees from the United States without reducing the client's medical reimbursement or caregiver stipend by a single cent!
     2. **Non-Withheld Contingency Clause (`<= 20%` or `<= 33.3%` under `38 C.F.R. § 14.636(e)–(f)`):** Draft the retainer to attach strictly to **retroactive cash lump sums actually disbursed to the Veteran or Primary Family Caregiver** (e.g., `$55,000` retroactive `PCAFC` Level 2 stipend arrears or `$32,000` out-of-pocket coinsurance refund), file within the mandatory **`30-day` window** (`§ 14.636(g)(3)`; *Ravin v. Wilkie*, `956 F.3d 1346 (Fed. Cir. 2020)`), and enforce the **`Pub. L. 102-572, § 506(c)`** lesser-of-two client refund whenever both `EAJA` and `§ 5904` fees cover the same CAVC representation phase!
