# Playbook 07: Overlooked Chronic, Tropical, ALS & Former POW Statutory Presumptions (`38 U.S.C. § 1112`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.307, 3.309, 3.318`)

Across the pre-VJRA (`1946–1988`) and early post-VJRA eras, RO rating boards routinely denied claims with a rubber-stamped line—*"Not shown in Service Medical Records"*—completely overlooking that **`38 U.S.C. § 1112` and `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.307, 3.309, 3.318` require ZERO in-service clinical documentation** when a qualifying disease manifests to a compensable degree (`10%+`) within its statutory presumptive window.

---

## 1. Presumptive Categories & Statutory Manifestation Windows

| Presumptive Track | Governing Authority | Service Prerequisite | Post-Separation Window & Covered Conditions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1-Year Chronic Diseases** | `38 U.S.C. § 1112(a)(1)`<br/>`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.307(a)(3), 3.309(a)` | `>= 90` days continuous active service | **`>= 10%` within 1 year of separation:** Arthritis (degenerative/rheumatoid), cardiovascular-renal disease (incl. hypertension, arteriosclerosis, myocarditis), diabetes mellitus, peptic/duodenal ulcers, psychoses, epilepsies, organic diseases of the nervous system (sensorineural hearing loss, tinnitus, neuropathy, migraines if organic—*Fountain*), calculi (kidney/bladder/gallbladder), malignant tumors, lupus, sarcoidosis, Hodgkin's, leukemias, etc. |
| **3-Year & 7-Year Chronic Diseases** | `38 U.S.C. § 1112(a)(2)–(4)`<br/>`38 C.F.R. § 3.307(a)(3)` | `>= 90` days continuous active service | • **Within 3 years (`>= 10%`):** Hansen's disease (leprosy) and active tuberculous disease.<br/>• **Within 7 years (`>= 10%`):** **Multiple sclerosis (`MS`)**. |
| **ALS (Lou Gehrig's Disease)** | `38 C.F.R. § 3.318` *(eff. Sept. 23, 2008, `73 FR 54693`)* | `>= 90` days continuous active service | **At ANY time after separation** (even 40+ years later!), unless affirmative evidence establishes a separate intercurrent cause (`100%` minimum evaluation under `DC 8017`!). |
| **Tropical Diseases** | `38 U.S.C. § 1112(a)(2)`<br/>`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.307(a)(4), 3.309(b)` | `>= 90` days active service (incl. tropical locality/exposure) | **`>= 10%` within 1 year** (or standard incubation period): Malaria, bacillary/amebic dysentery, amebiasis, blackwater fever, cholera, dracontiasis, filariasis, leishmaniasis, loiasis, onchocerciasis, oroya fever, pinta, plague, schistosomiasis, yaws, yellow fever, and resultant therapy complications. |
| **Former Prisoners of War (`FPOW`) — Tier 1 (Any Duration)** | `38 U.S.C. § 1112(b)(2)`<br/>`38 C.F.R. § 3.309(c)(1)` | **Any period** of captivity/internment as an FPOW | **`>= 10%` at ANY time post-service:** Psychosis, **any of the anxiety states**, dysthymic disorder (depressive neurosis), organic residuals of frostbite, post-traumatic osteoarthritis, **atherosclerotic / hypertensive heart disease** and complications, **stroke** and complications, and *(eff. Oct. 10, 2008)* osteoporosis if PTSD is diagnosed. |
| **Former Prisoners of War (`FPOW`) — Tier 2 (`>= 30` Days)** | `38 U.S.C. § 1112(b)(3)`<br/>`38 C.F.R. § 3.309(c)(2)` | **`>= 30` days** total FPOW internment | **`>= 10%` at ANY time post-service:** Avitaminosis, beriberi (incl. beriberi heart disease), chronic dysentery, helminthiasis, malnutrition (incl. optic atrophy), pellagra, other nutritional deficiencies, **irritable bowel syndrome (`IBS`)**, **peptic ulcer disease**, **peripheral neuropathy** (unless due to infectious disease), **cirrhosis of the liver**, and *(eff. Sept. 28, 2009)* osteoporosis. |

## 2. Evidentiary Mechanics That Unlock Retroactive Wins

1. **Symptoms Count Even Before Formal Diagnostic Label (`§ 3.307(b)`):**
   * Under `38 C.F.R. § 3.307(b)`, manifestation within the 1-year / 3-year / 7-year window **does not require a contemporaneous textbook diagnosis** if factual, clinical, or competent lay evidence shows **characteristic symptoms of `>= 10%` severity** within the presumptive period that a later medical examiner identifies as the onset of the chronic disease (e.g., documented optic neuritis, paresthesias, or foot drop within 7 years of discharge later diagnosed as **Multiple Sclerosis**!).
2. **`§ 3.303(b)` Chronicity & Continuity of Symptomatology (*Savage* & *Walker*):**
   * Under *Walker v. Shinseki*, 708 F.3d 1331 (Fed. Cir. 2013), `38 C.F.R. § 3.303(b)` (in-service chronicity OR continuous post-service symptomatology without needing an Etiology/Nexus medical opinion) applies **exclusively to the chronic diseases enumerated in `38 C.F.R. § 3.309(a)`**.
   * For non-`§ 3.309(a)` orthopedic conditions (e.g., an ankle sprain or lumbar strain prior to radiographically confirmed degenerative arthritis), claimants must proceed under `§ 3.303(a)` or `§ 3.303(d)` direct service connection using lay symptom continuity as factual foundation for a medical nexus opinion!
3. **High Threshold for Government Rebuttal (`38 U.S.C. § 1113`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.307(d)`):**
   * Once presumptive service and manifestation within the statutory window are shown, VA can rebut the presumption **only** via *"affirmative evidence to the contrary, or evidence to establish that an intercurrent injury or disease which is a recognized cause of any of the diseases or disabilities has been suffered between the date of separation from service and the onset of any such diseases or disabilities"* (`§ 3.307(d)`). Speculative examiner statements cannot rebut a statutory presumption.

## 3. Pathway Selection: Closed-Record CUE vs. `§ 3.156(c)` vs. Supplemental Claim

1. **Evidence of Presumptive Manifestation Was Already in the Closed Record -> File CUE (`38 U.S.C. §§ 5109A / 7111`):**
   * Example: Veteran discharged after 3 years active duty in June 1971; VAMC or private records **already in the C-file** (or constructively possessed in VAMC files on/after July 21, 1992 per *Bell*) documented blood pressure readings meeting `10%` under `DC 7101`, an active duodenal ulcer (`DC 7305`), or X-ray degenerative arthritis (`DC 5003`) in February 1972; RO denied in 1973 solely because *"STRs negative."* **Textbook RO CUE (`38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)`)!**
2. **Missing Separation / TDRL / Military Hospital Records Associated Later -> File `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)` Reconsideration:**
   * If Temporary Disability Retired List (`TDRL`) exams, military inpatient charts, or FPOW repatriation records (`VA Form 10-564-D` / military debriefings) are newly associated, demand reconsideration back to the original denied claim under [Playbook 03](03-service-department-records-38-cfr-3-156c.md).
3. **Private First-Year Records Located Today -> File Supplemental Claim (`§ 3.2501`):**
   * Private post-service medical records do **not** trigger `§ 3.156(c)` effective-date retroactivity, nor can unsubmitted private records establish CUE, though they establish prospective service connection immediately.
