# Playbook 04: Unadjudicated TDIU, Pre-2015 Informal Claims (`§ 3.157(b)`), Mandatory SMC & AMA Explicit Notice

Massive retroactive awards frequently hide in plain sight when VA **received a valid informal or inferred claim**—or had **statutory facts on its own code sheet requiring SMC** (`38 U.S.C. § 1114`, `former § 314` prior to `Pub. L. 102-83`, Aug. 6, 1991)—and simply failed to adjudicate it.

---

## 1. Archetype A: Reasonably Raised Unadjudicated TDIU (*Roberson* & *Rice*)

Under *Roberson v. Principi*, 251 F.3d 1378 (Fed. Cir. 2001) and *Rice v. Shinseki*, 22 Vet. App. 447 (2009), a request for Total Disability based on Individual Unemployability (`38 C.F.R. § 4.16` — `40 FR 42535`, Sept. 15, 1975; `54 FR 4281`, pub. Jan. 30, 1989, eff. Mar. 1, 1989; `55 FR 31579`, pub. Aug. 3, 1990, eff. Sept. 4, 1990) is **part and parcel** of an initial or increased-rating claim whenever:
1. A veteran is seeking service connection or a higher rating, AND
2. The record contains **cognitive, clinical, or lay evidence of unemployability** attributable to the disability(ies).

### C-File Red Flags
* Veteran wrote on a `21-4138`, `21-526`, or C&P intake sheet: *"Lost my job because of my back/PTSD"*, *"Haven't worked since 1998"*, or *"On Social Security Disability (`SSDI`)"*.
* VAMC notes or Chapter 31 (`VR&E`) records state *"Employment not currently feasible"*.
* RO granted `70%` PTSD in 2004 (meeting `§ 4.16(a)` schedular threshold!) while C&P exam stated `"Unemployed x 5 years due to PTSD"`, and RO neither awarded TDIU, deferred TDIU, nor sent `VA Form 21-8940`!
  * **Trap Check (Legacy vs. AMA Implicit Denial):** In **Legacy (pre-Feb. 19, 2019)** decisions, check whether the rating decision denied a schedular `100%` increase under circumstances that implicitly denied reasonably raised TDIU (*Deshotel v. Nicholson*, 457 F.3d 1258 (Fed. Cir. 2006); *Hampton v. McDonough*, 68 F.4th 1376 (Fed. Cir. 2023); *Steele v. Collins*, 135 F.4th 1353 (Fed. Cir. 2025); *cf.* *Comer v. Peake*, 552 F.3d 1362 (Fed. Cir. 2009)). In **AMA (on/after Feb. 19, 2019)** decisions, under ***Hamill v. Collins*, 166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. Feb. 4, 2026) (`No. 24-1543`)**, silent omission **never** denies TDIU—it **remains legally pending**!

## 2. Archetype B: Historical `38 C.F.R. § 3.157(b)` VAMC Reports & `§ 3.156(b)` Post-Decision VAMC Abatement (*Lang*)

### Part 1: Pre-March 24, 2015 `§ 3.157(b)` Informal Claims for Increase
Before **March 24, 2015** (`79 FR 57660`, published Sept. 25, 2014 — see [`38-cfr-3.155-3.157b-informal-claims-pre-2015.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.155-3.157b-informal-claims-pre-2015.md)), `38 C.F.R. § 3.157(b)(1)` (`26 FR 1571`, Feb. 24, 1961) mandated that once service connection was in effect (even at `0%`!), **VA's receipt of a VA hospital admission report or VA outpatient examination report** showing worsening **operated as an informal claim for increased compensation** on the date of admission/examination.
1. Identify the date service connection was **first granted** (e.g., `10%` knee or `0%` scar/sinusitis in 1974).
2. Pull VAMC treatment/admission records between 1974 and **March 23, 2015** showing objective worsening (also checking pre-2015 written filings pointing to VAMC records under *Shea v. Wilkie*, 926 F.3d 1362 (Fed. Cir. 2019)).
3. Check whether **any rating decision** was issued on that condition after that VAMC report:
   * **If NO subsequent rating decision adjudicated that condition until decades later:** That `§ 3.157(b)` informal claim remained **unadjudicated and pending**!
   * **If a subsequent Legacy rating decision adjudicated that exact condition:** Normally, a later final adjudication terminates an earlier pending informal claim for increase (*Williams v. Peake*, 461 F.3d 1349 (Fed. Cir. 2006)), UNLESS the later grant failed to apply the mandatory 1-year lookback under `38 U.S.C. § 5110(b)(2)` (`former § 3010(b)(2)`) / `38 C.F.R. § 3.400(o)(2)` / `§ 3.157(b)` (potential **RO CUE**).

### Part 2: `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(b)` 1-Year *Post*-Decision VAMC Finality Abatement (*Lang v. Wilkie*, 2020)
Even for **initial service-connection denials** (where `§ 3.157(b)` never applied because SC was not yet granted!) and even **between March 24, 2015 and February 18, 2019**:
* Under *Beraud v. McDonald*, 766 F.3d 1402 (Fed. Cir. 2014) and ***Lang v. Wilkie*, 971 F.3d 1348 (Fed. Cir. 2020)**, if a **VAMC generated clinical treatment records** relevant to the denied/rated issue within **1 year *after*** a post-July 21, 1992 Legacy RO decision, VA constructively received those records during the 1-year appeal window under `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(b)`.
* Unless VA issued a formal determination deciding whether those post-decision VAMC notes constituted new and material evidence, **the Legacy RO decision NEVER BECAME FINAL**—meaning a grant 10 or 25 years later must assign an effective date anchored to that original non-final claim!

## 3. Archetype C: Mandatory Statutory SMC (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(k), (l), (s)`) & Multi-Step Bumps (*Barry*)

> [!TIP]
> **Full `SMC(k)–(t)` & Ancillary Suite:** For complete multi-step math, *Breniser*/*Amberman* anti-pyramiding rules, `SMC(l)`/`(r)(1)`/`(r)(2)`/`(t)` (*Laska v. McDonough*, `37 Vet. App. 460 (2024)`), `§ 3.552` hospitalization reductions, and `SAH`/`Auto`/`Clothing`/`DEA` grants, see the **[Master SMC Legal Treatise & Step Math](../docs/smc-complete-treatise-and-step-math.md)**, **[Playbook 12 (A&A, `SMC(r)` & `SMC(t)`)](12-aid-and-attendance-smc-l-r1-r2-and-smc-t.md)**, **[Playbook 13 (Ancillary Grants)](13-ancillary-grants-sah-auto-clothing-dea-spina-bifida.md)**, and the **[SMC & Ancillary Audit Worksheet](../checklists/smc-and-ancillary-audit-worksheet.md)**.

VA must consider and award SMC **even when never claimed by the veteran** whenever record facts establish entitlement (*Akles v. Derwinski*, 1 Vet. App. 118 (1991); *Buie v. Shinseki*, 24 Vet. App. 242 (2010)).

### High-Probability SMC Audits
1. **`SMC(s)` Statutory Housebound (`100% + 60%` Rule — `38 U.S.C. § 1114(s)` / `former § 314(s)`):**
   * Veteran has **one single disability rated 100%** (either schedular `100%`, OR **TDIU attributable solely to ONE single service-connected disability** under *Bradley v. Peake*, 22 Vet. App. 280 (2008)!), **PLUS** additional separate/independent service-connected disabilities combining to **`60%` or more**.
   * **Pure Math CUE (`cue-and-open-claim`):** When the Rating Code Sheet itself shows schedular `100%` PTSD + `50%` Sleep Apnea + `20%` Lumbar (`50 + 20 = 60%` combined!) and omits `SMC(s-1)`, that is an airtight, mathematical **RO CUE**!
   * **The *Bradley* Angle & *George v. McDonough* Guardrail (`open-claim-only-george-restricted` pre-2008):** If VA granted TDIU citing multiple disabilities, check whether one disability *alone* caused unemployability. *(Bitemporal Caution: Attacking a final **pre-Nov. 26, 2008** decision via CUE under *Bradley* faces a `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)` / *George v. McDonough*, 596 U.S. 740 (2022) change-in-interpretation defense; always check first for open/pending claims, `§ 3.156(c)` service records, or *Lang* `§ 3.156(b)` non-finality!)*
2. **Multi-Step Intermediate & Full-Step SMC Increases (`38 C.F.R. § 3.350(f)(3)–(4)` — *Barry v. McDonough*, 2024):**
   * Under ***Barry v. McDonough*, 101 F.4th 1348 (Fed. Cir. May 16, 2024)**, veterans receiving base `SMC(l)–(n)` are entitled to **multiple** half-step (`§ 3.350(f)(3)`, per independent `50%+` disability) and full-step (`§ 3.350(f)(4)`, per independent `100%` disability) rate bumps, capped at `SMC(o)`. Audit every catastrophic injury / A&A code sheet for suppressed multi-step bumps (noting the same `§ 20.1403(e)` / *George* CUE bar on final pre-May 16, 2024 ratings vs. full retroactive application on open, pending, or `§ 3.156(b)–(c)` claims).
3. **`SMC(k)` Loss of Use of Creative Organ / Extremity (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(k)`):**
   * Radical prostatectomy (e.g., Agent Orange prostate cancer!) or erectile dysfunction documented as secondary to diabetes/PTSD SSRIs on the C&P exam itself while rater granted `DC 7528` or `DC 7522` at `0%` and forgot to check the `SMC(k)` award box!
4. **`SMC(l)` Aid & Attendance / Housebound-in-Fact:**
   * VAMC nursing-home/homebound documentation or C&P exam certifying inability to dress, bathe, manage prosthetics, or protect oneself from hazards of daily environment (`38 C.F.R. § 3.352(a)`).

## 4. Archetype D: Post-Feb. 19, 2019 AMA Silent Omissions (*Hamill v. Collins*, 2026)

Under ***Hamill v. Collins*, 166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. Feb. 4, 2026) (`No. 24-1543`)**, audit every AMA-era rating decision (dated **February 19, 2019 or later**) side-by-side against:
* Every box/row listed on the veteran's `VA Form 21-526EZ` or `20-0995`, AND
* Every secondary condition or extraschedular/TDIU/SMC issue reasonably raised on the DBQ/C&P exam (e.g., DBQ checks *"Radiculopathy: Yes — Mild right sciatic nerve"* during a lumbar spine claim, and the AMA rating decision grants `20%` lumbar spine while saying **zero words** about radiculopathy!).
* **Result under *Hamill*:** Because `38 U.S.C. § 5104(b)` prohibits denial by silence under the AMA, that radiculopathy or TDIU claim **was never denied**—demand immediate adjudication with an effective date anchored to the original AMA `21-526EZ` / ITF! *(Practice Note: If within 1 year of the silent AMA decision, pursue HLR or Board appeal per *Hamill* fn. 6 rather than a mandamus petition; see [Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).*
