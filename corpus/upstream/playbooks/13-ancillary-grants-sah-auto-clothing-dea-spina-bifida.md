# Playbook 13: High-Dollar Ancillary Grants — `SAH`/`SHA`/`TRA` (`Ch. 21`), Auto & Adaptive Equipment (`Ch. 39`), Multiple Clothing Allowances (`§ 3.810`), Retroactive `DEA`/`CHAMPVA` & Spina Bifida (`Ch. 18`)

Winning a `100%` Permanent and Total (`P&T`), Loss of Use (`LOU`), blindness, burn, `ALS`, or Special Monthly Compensation (`SMC`) award is only half of catastrophic-injury representation. Properly audited **ancillary grants and derivative family benefits** routinely deliver **`$150,000–$300,000+` in tax-free lump sums, home/vehicle adaptations, recurring annual stipends, and retroactive family education/healthcare reimbursements** that general practitioners leave unclaimed on the table.

## 1. Preconditions: VBMS / VHA / Loan Guaranty Forms & Code-Sheet Triggers

Audit every `100%`, `TDIU`, `SMC`, `ALS`, orthopedic bracing, burn, or herbicide C-file for these exact forms and triggers ([VA Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md)):

## 2. Bitemporal Pathway Gate (`Inferred Subordinate Issues` vs. `Application-Gated Grants`)

1. **Mandatory Subordinate / Inferred Ancillary Issues (`cue-and-open-claim` / `ama-explicit-notice-5104b`):**
   * Whenever a Rating Decision grants `100%` schedular or `TDIU` with no routine future examination (`38 C.F.R. § 3.327`), VA **must** adjudicate `Ch. 35` DEA basic `P&T` eligibility (`38 C.F.R. § 3.807`). Likewise, when VA grants `SMC` for extremity `LOU`, qualifying blindness, or `ALS` (`38 C.F.R. § 3.318`), VA is operationally required to address subordinate `Ch. 39` (`§ 3.808`) and `Ch. 21` (`§§ 3.809–3.809a`) certification.
   * Under **AMA (`>= Feb. 19, 2019`)**, if a claimant checked or reasonably raised `P&T`/`SAH`/`Auto` and the decision notice stays silent, the ancillary issue **remains legally pending** under `38 U.S.C. § 5104(b)` (*Hamill v. Collins*, `166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. 2026)`).
2. **Application-Gated Lump Sums & Liberalizing Statutes (`prospective-liberalizing-3.114`):**
   * Actual disbursement of `SAH`/`SHA` (`26-4555`), `Ch. 39` Conveyance (`21-4502`), `Clothing Allowance` (`10-8678`), and `Ch. 18` (`21-0304`) requires a formal application. Statutory expansions (`Pub. L. 111-275` eff. Oct. 1, 2011; `Pub. L. 116-23` eff. Jan. 1, 2020; `Pub. L. 116-154` eff. Oct. 1, 2020; `Pub. L. 117-333` eff. Jan. 5, 2023) cannot generate CUE or payments predating their statutory effective dates (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(g)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.114(a)`).
3. ***George v. McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) CUE Bar (`open-claim-only-george-restricted`):**
   * Judicial invalidations of restrictive VA ancillary interpretations—such as *Sursely v. Peake* (`2009`, multiple clothing allowances), *Kilpatrick v. Principi* (`2003`, `§ 1151` `SAH`/`Auto`), and *Jensen v. Shulkin* (`2017`, `§ 3.809` lower-extremity `LOU`)—apply fully to open/pending appeals and new annual/supplemental filings, **not** as `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)` CUE against final pre-precedent denials that faithfully applied extant regulations.

## 3. Program-by-Program Merits & Pleading Playbook

### Program 1: `Chapter 21` Specially Adapted Housing (`SAH`), `SHA` & `TRA` (`38 U.S.C. §§ 2101–2102B`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.809–3.809a`)

1. **Qualifying Medical Pathways (`SAH` `§ 2101(a)` vs. `SHA` `§ 2101(b)`):**
   * **Full `SAH` Grant (`38 U.S.C. § 2101(a)(2)(B)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.809` — CPI-indexed past `~$117,000+` in FY 2024–2026):** Permanent and total SC (or `§ 1151`) disability due to:
     1. Loss or loss of use (`LOU`) of **both lower extremities** precluding locomotion without braces, crutches, canes, or a wheelchair;
     2. **Blindness in both eyes having central visual acuity of `20/200` or less** in the better eye with corrective lenses (**expanded by `Pub. L. 116-154`, eff. Oct. 1, 2020**—eliminating the harsh pre-Oct. 1, 2020 rule that required *either* light-perception-only/5-degree concentric contraction *or* blindness plus a missing leg!);
     3. Loss or `LOU` of **one lower extremity** together with `(a)` **residuals of organic disease or injury** affecting balance or propulsion so as to preclude locomotion without braces/crutches/canes/wheelchair, OR `(b)` loss/`LOU` of **one upper extremity** affecting balance/propulsion;
     4. Loss or `LOU` of **one lower extremity** occurring after Sept. 11, 2001 making it medically infeasible to ambulate without a wheelchair (capped at **`120` grants per fiscal year** post-Oct. 1, 2020 under `Pub. L. 116-154`, up from `30`);
     5. **Severe burn injuries** (full-thickness or deep partial-thickness burns with scar contractures); OR
     6. **Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (`ALS`)** (`38 C.F.R. § 3.809(d)` — **automatic `SAH` eligibility** upon service connection under `38 C.F.R. § 3.318` without waiting for end-stage paralysis; see [Playbook 07](07-statutory-presumptions-chronic-tropical-pow-als.md)!).
   * **`SHA` Grant (`38 U.S.C. § 2101(b)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.809a` — CPI-indexed `~$23,000+`):** Anatomical loss or `LOU` of **both hands**, certain severe respiratory injuries (`Pub. L. 116-154`), or qualifying deep partial-thickness burns not meeting `SAH`.
   * **`TRA` Grant (`38 U.S.C. § 2102A`):** Temporary adaptation grant (`~$47,000+` `SAH` / `~$8,400+` `SHA`) when the veteran resides **temporarily** in a home owned by a **family member**—and under `38 U.S.C. § 2102A(c)`, `TRA` funds **do NOT reduce** the veteran's aggregate lifetime `SAH`/`SHA` grant pool!
2. **The *Jensen v. Shulkin* Assistive-Device Breakthrough (`29 Vet. App. 66 (2017)`):**
   * Raters routinely commit legal error by denying `SAH` unless the veteran already holds `SMC(l)`/`(m)` amputation-equivalent `LOU` under `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(a)(2)`.
   * In ***Jensen v. Shulkin*, `29 Vet. App. 66, 73–76 (2017)`**, the CAVC **rejected** importing `§ 3.350`'s strict `SMC` `LOU` standard into `38 U.S.C. § 2101(a)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.809`! Under *Jensen* and `§ 3.809(c)`, lower-extremity "loss of use" for `SAH` exists whenever bilateral lower-extremity pathology deprives the veteran of perambulation **without the regular and constant use of braces (`AFOs`), crutches, canes, or a wheelchair as their normal mode of locomotion**—even if **"occasional locomotion by other methods"** (such as shuffling a few steps unassisted inside a bathroom) remains possible!
   * *(Citator Guardrail: Never confuse *Jensen v. Shulkin*, `29 Vet. App. 66 (2017)` on `§ 3.809` assistive-device locomotion with *Walker v. Shinseki*, `708 F.3d 1331 (Fed. Cir. 2013)` on `§ 3.303(b)` chronicity!)*
3. **Ryan Kules Act Lifetime Uses (`6x`) & `2030` Supplemental Refresh (`Pub. L. 116-154`, `134 Stat. 690`, eff. Oct. 1, 2020):**
   * Veterans may split their `SAH`/`SHA` cap across **up to `6` separate lifetime grant uses** (`38 U.S.C. § 2102(d)(3)`), capturing biennial/annual construction-cost escalations on unused balances.
   * Under **`38 U.S.C. § 2102(f)`** (enacted by `Pub. L. 116-154, § 3`, **phased in starting October 1, 2030**), `10` years after receiving a prior grant, veterans who exhausted their aggregate cap will unlock a **supplemental `SAH`/`SHA` refresh up to `50%` of the prevailing statutory maximum**!

### Program 2: `Chapter 39` Automobile Purchase & Unlimited Adaptive Equipment (`38 U.S.C. §§ 3901–3904`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.808`)

1. **Qualifying Conditions (`Full Vehicle Purchase + Equipment` vs. `Adaptive Equipment Only`):**
   * **Full Conveyance Purchase Grant (`VA Form 21-4502`, `~$25,000+` paid to seller) + Unlimited Adaptive Equipment (`38 U.S.C. § 3901(1)(A)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.808(b)(1)–(4), (6)`):**
     * Loss or **permanent loss of use (`LOU`)** of **one or both feet**, OR **one or both hands** (*Tucker v. West*, `11 Vet. App. 369 (1998)`—foot drop / peroneal paralysis with `AFO` brace qualifying for `SMC(k)` `LOU` **simultaneously unlocks the `$25,000+` `Ch. 39` Auto Grant**!);
     * Permanent bilateral visual impairment (`<= 20/200` central visual acuity in both eyes, OR widest visual field `<= 20 degrees` in both eyes) — *note: the veteran does **not** have to drive personally; a caregiver/spouse may drive the vehicle for the blind/disabled veteran!*;
     * **Severe burn injuries** (`Pub. L. 111-275, § 803`, eff. **Oct. 1, 2011**; `§ 3.808(b)(4)` — deep partial-thickness or full-thickness burns causing scar contractures that preclude effective vehicle operation); OR
     * **Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (`ALS`)** (`38 C.F.R. § 3.808(b)(6)` — automatic eligibility!).
   * **Adaptive Equipment ONLY (`38 U.S.C. § 3902(b)(2)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.808(b)(5)` — `VA Form 10-1394`):**
     * **Ankylosis of one or both knees, or one or both hips** (without full `SMC(k)` foot `LOU`) unlocks **unlimited VHA-paid adaptive equipment** (power seats, hand controls, left-foot accelerators, lifts, power steering/brakes, air conditioning) on up to `2` vehicles within any `4-year` period (`38 U.S.C. § 3903(b)`), though **not** the vehicle purchase price itself unless counsel proves functional `LOU` of the foot!
2. **Second & Recurring `10-Year` Full Auto Purchase Grants (`AUTO for Veterans Act`, `Pub. L. 117-333, § 2`, `136 Stat. 6121`, enacted Jan. 5, 2023; `38 U.S.C. § 3903(a)`):**
   * Congress ended the 75-year "once-in-a-lifetime" conveyance limit in **two statutory phases** (`38 U.S.C. § 3903(a)(2)–(3)`):
     * **Phase 1 (`Jan. 5, 2023 – Jan. 4, 2033` — Immediate `30-Year` Bridge):** Any eligible veteran whose prior `Ch. 39` vehicle purchase grant was paid **`30 years` or more ago** (e.g., Vietnam, Gulf War, or early peacetime amputees/`LOU` veterans paid on or before `1996`!) is **immediately eligible right now** for a brand-new `$25,000+` vehicle purchase grant!
     * **Phase 2 (`Beginning January 5, 2033` — Recurring `10-Year` Cycle):** Starting `10 years` after enactment of `Pub. L. 117-333`, **every** eligible veteran can receive a new full vehicle purchase grant **every `10 years`** once a decade has elapsed since their prior grant!

### Program 3: Multiple & Recurring Annual Clothing Allowances (`38 U.S.C. § 1162`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.810`; `VA Form 10-8678`)

1. **Stacking Multiple Annual Allowances (*Sursely v. Peake*, `551 F.3d 1351 (Fed. Cir. 2009)` & `76 FR 70883`, eff. Dec. 16, 2011):**
   * Under **`38 C.F.R. § 3.810(a)(2)`**, a veteran earns **separate annual tax-free lump-sum allowances** (`~$1,000+` *each* per year) for every qualifying prosthetic/orthopedic appliance (including wheelchairs) or physician-prescribed topical SC skin medication that independently damages a **distinct type of outergarment** (e.g., `Allowance #1` rigid/hinged knee brace tearing trousers + `Allowance #2` shoulder/back brace or wheelchair tearing shirts/jackets + `Allowance #3` prescribed corticosteroid/calcipotriene ointment staining collars/sleeves!).
   * Under **`38 C.F.R. § 3.810(a)(3)`**, multiple qualifying appliances/medications affecting a **single type of garment** (e.g., bilateral `AFO`s/knee braces + wheelchair both destroying trousers) **also** unlock multiple allowances when together they wear/tear that garment at an **accelerated rate** requiring faster replacement than a single appliance!
2. **Defeating VHA Prosthetics' (`PSAS`) Unlawful "Rigid Appliance" Denials (`38 C.F.R. § 20.105`):**
   * Local VHA `PSAS` offices routinely deny `VA Form 10-8678` by citing internal `VHA Handbook 1173.15` lists and labeling velcro-strapped, carbon-fiber, or hinged braces "flexible / non-rigid."
   * **Appellate Cure:** Neither `38 U.S.C. § 1162` nor `38 C.F.R. § 3.810(a)(1)` contains the word **"rigid"**—the sole statutory standard is whether the appliance **"tends to wear out or tear"** the veteran's clothing! Appeal VHA denials to the Board (`VA Form 10182`, attaching photos of frayed trousers/shirts and a brief invoking `38 C.F.R. § 20.105`, under which internal VHA manuals **do not bind the Board**!).
3. **`August 1` Filing Cutoff & `Pub. L. 117-328` Automatic Renewal (`Div. U, § 201`, Dec. 29, 2022):**
   * File initial or *additional-allowance* `VA Form 10-8678` claims with the local VAMC `PSAS` on or before **August 1** of that benefit year *(Exception: under `38 C.F.R. § 3.810(c)(1)`, if filed within `1 year` of the rating grant establishing SC for the underlying disability, VA pays the prior benefit year retroactively!)*. Under `Pub. L. 117-328, Div. U, § 201`, once approved for static devices/skin medications, annual clothing allowances **renew automatically each year** without re-filing!

### Program 4: Retroactive `P&T` Unlocks for `Ch. 35` DEA (`§ 3.807`, `§ 21.4131`) & `CHAMPVA` (`§ 1781`)

1. **The `1-Year` Post-Notice Election Goldmine for Retroactive `Ch. 35` DEA (`38 C.F.R. § 21.4131(d)` / `(f)`):**
   * Suppose a veteran wins a `100%` `P&T` (or `TDIU P&T`) appeal in `2026` with an effective date of `2018`, and their spouse or child attended college/trade school between `2018` and `2025`.
   * If each dependent files **`VA Form 22-5490` within `1 year` of the date of VA's `P&T` notification letter**, `38 C.F.R. § 21.4131` lets them elect a **retroactive `Ch. 35` DEA commencing date all the way back to the `2018` `P&T` effective date**—generating up to `36` (`45` pre-Aug. 1, 2018) months of lump-sum tax-free stipends (**`$35,000–$55,000+` per dependent**)! Missing the `1-year` post-notice window caps DEA retroactivity to at most `1 year` prior to receipt of `22-5490`!
   * **No `Age-26` / `10-Year` Expiration Post-Aug. 1, 2023 (`Pub. L. 117-328, Div. U, § 234`; `38 U.S.C. § 3512(g)`):** Dependents who first become eligible, turn `18`, or finish high school **on or after August 1, 2023** have **zero delimiting-date expiration or age-26 cutoff**!
   * **Helpless Child Sequencing Warning (`38 U.S.C. § 3562`; `38 C.F.R. § 21.3023`):** Never let a disabled `18–23` child cash a `Ch. 35` DEA payment *before* securing `38 C.F.R. § 3.356` helpless-child status—an adjudicated `§ 3.356` helpless child receives `Ch. 35` DEA **concurrently** with dependency/DIC (`§ 21.3023(b)`), whereas a non-`§ 3.356` schoolchild triggers an **irrevocable lifetime bar** to schoolchild dependency/DIC upon negotiating their first DEA payment (`§ 21.3023(c)(1)`; see [`survivor-marriage-dependency-evolution.md`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/survivor-marriage-dependency-evolution.md))!
2. **Retroactive `CHAMPVA` Medical Reimbursement (`38 U.S.C. § 1781`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 17.271–17.276`):**
   * Non-TRICARE-eligible spouses and children of `100%` `P&T` veterans should file **`VA Form 10-10d`** immediately upon receipt of the `P&T` award, then submit **`VA Form 10-7959a`** with itemized provider bills/EOBs from the entire retroactive `P&T` period to recover out-of-pocket hospital, surgical, deductible, and prescription payments!

## 4. Postconditions: Effective Dates, Offsets & `§ 5904` Fee Structuring

1. **Attorney Fees on Denied Ancillary & VHA Appeals (`38 U.S.C. § 5904(c)(1)`; `38 C.F.R. § 14.636`):**
   * Initial preparation of `VA Form 26-4555`, `21-4502`, `10-8678`, `22-5490`, or `21-0304` before an initial AOJ/VHA decision is **pro bono (`$0` fee)**.
   * Once VBA, Loan Guaranty, or VHA (`PSAS` Clothing Allowance / `CHAMPVA`) issues an **initial denial** (or under-evaluates a `Ch. 18` Spina Bifida level at Level `I` instead of Level `III`), a compliant `§ 5904` contingency fee agreement (`<= 20%` standard reasonable presumption) attaches to post-decision AMA Supplemental (`20-0995`), HLR (`20-0996`), or Board (`10182`) review!
2. **Direct-Pay (`§ 5904(d)`) Disbursement Nuance:**
   * While VBA withholds `20%` direct-pay fees from cash lump-sum compensation, `Ch. 18`, and `Ch. 35` DEA back pay paid to the claimant, **VHA `PSAS` Clothing Allowances** are disbursed through VHA fiscal channels without automated `VBMS` fee withholding, and **`Ch. 39` Auto (`21-4502`) / `Ch. 21` `SAH` (`26-4555`)** funds are paid to auto dealers, escrow agents, or construction builders rather than as unrestricted cash back pay to the veteran. Draft fee agreements explicitly excluding third-party vendor/escrow disbursements where required (or relying on **CAVC `EAJA` fees (`28 U.S.C. § 2412(d)`)** on judicial remand of denied `SAH`/`Auto`/`Clothing` appeals!).
