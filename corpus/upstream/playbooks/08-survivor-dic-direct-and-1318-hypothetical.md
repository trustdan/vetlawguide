# Playbook 08: Survivor DIC — Direct/Contributory Death (`§ 1310`), Hypothetical `§ 1318` Entitlement (`§ 3.22`) & PACT Act `§ 1305` Re-Evaluations

Dependency and Indemnity Compensation (`DIC`, `38 U.S.C. ch. 13`, `former ch. 13 §§ 401–423`; see [Three-Era Title 38 Statutory Crosswalk](../docs/title-38-statutory-crosswalk.md)) is the cornerstone tax-free monthly survivor benefit for surviving spouses, minor/school-age/helpless children, and low-income dependent parents (`38 U.S.C. § 1315`). Regional Office (`AOJ`) raters routinely deny valid DIC claims by treating the four-line state **Death Certificate** as conclusive proof of etiology, overlooking **contributory** vital-organ impairment under `38 C.F.R. § 3.312(c)`, ignoring **`38 C.F.R. § 3.22(b)` CUE / `§ 3.156(c)` hypothetical `10-year` total-disability reconstruction**, or misapplying **`38 U.S.C. § 1305` (`PACT Act § 204`)** retroactive effective dates.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Run Parallel Post-Mortem Triage:** Every deceased-veteran intake involves **two distinct claim tracks** that must be screened simultaneously within **1 year of death**: **(1) the survivor's own Chapter 13 DIC claim** (this Playbook) and **(2) the deceased veteran's unpaid lifetime compensation** via `38 U.S.C. § 5121A` Substitution, `§ 5121` Accrued Benefits, or `38 C.F.R. § 3.816(f)` *Nehmer* Estate payouts ([Playbook 09](09-accrued-benefits-substitution-nehmer-estates.md) and [`Survivor Post-Mortem Triage Gate`](../checklists/survivor-post-mortem-triage-gate.md)).

## 1. Preconditions: VBMS, Death Certificate & C-File Audit Triggers

Before selecting a DIC theory, pull the complete deceased veteran's VBMS eFolder, **every historical Rating Code Sheet** ([VA Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md)), and terminal medical records:

* **Core Survivor Claim & Ancillary Forms:**
  * **`VA Form 21P-534EZ`** (*Application for DIC, Survivors Pension, and/or Accrued Benefits*, or legacy `VA Form 21-534` / `21P-534`), **`VA Form 21P-535`** (dependent parents DIC under `38 U.S.C. § 1315`), **`VA Form 21-0966`** (Intent to File), and **`VA Form 21-2680`** (Surviving Spouse Aid & Attendance / Housebound under `§ 1311(c)–(d)`).
  * **Marriage & Dependency Proof (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.50–3.57, 3.356`):** Marriage certificate, prior divorce/annulment decrees, continuous cohabitation or `§ 3.52` deemed-valid marriage affidavits, post-57 / post-55 remarriage dates, and pre-age-18 helpless-child medical proof (see [`Survivor Marriage, Remarriage & Dependency Evolution`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/survivor-marriage-dependency-evolution.md)).
* **Terminal Clinical vs. Death-Certificate Triggers (`§ 1310` / `§ 3.312`):**
  * State **Certificate of Death** listing only an immediate terminal mechanism (*"cardiopulmonary arrest," "respiratory failure," "sepsis," "natural causes"*) in **Part I** while omitting or burying service-connected diabetes, COPD, ischemic heart disease (`IHD`), hypertension, chronic kidney disease, cirrhosis, or PTSD in **Part II ("Other Significant Conditions")**.
  * Terminal **VAMC / ICU / Hospice Discharge Summaries**, **Autopsy Reports**, **`DD Form 1300`** (*Report of Casualty*), or toxicology/psychiatry notes documenting multi-organ decompensation or service-connected mental unsoundness (`38 C.F.R. § 3.302`).
* **Rating Code Sheet Triggers (`§ 1318` / `§ 1311` / `§ 1305`):**
  * Veteran died of a **purely non-service-connected cause** (e.g., motor vehicle accident, non-presumptive malignancy, acute infection) after holding `100%` schedular or `TDIU` (`§ 4.16`) for **fewer than 10 years on paper** (e.g., `7`–`9.5` years), **OR** holding `70%–90%` combined while factually unemployable (`VA Form 21-8940` / VAMC vocational notes / SSA disability records in file).
  * Any **previously denied DIC claim** in the eFolder where the veteran had herbicide exposure (including Thailand, Laos, Cambodia, Guam, Blue Water Navy, or **PACT Act `§ 404` hypertension / MGUS**), burn-pit / Southwest Asia deployment (`38 U.S.C. § 1120`), Camp Lejeune water exposure (`38 C.F.R. § 3.307(a)(7)`), or Former POW captivity (`38 U.S.C. § 1318(b)(3)`).
  * Veteran died **before January 1, 1993** at pay grade **`E-7` through `O-10`** (`§ 1311(a)(3)` higher pay-grade rate audit), or held `100%`/`TDIU` for **`>= 8` continuous years** while married to the surviving spouse (`§ 1311(a)(2)` kicker audit).

## 3. Proof Elements & Doctrinal Mechanics

### A. Primary vs. Contributory Service-Connected Death (`38 U.S.C. § 1310`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.312(a)–(c)`)

Under `38 U.S.C. § 1310(a)` (`former 38 U.S.C. § 410(a)`), DIC is payable whenever a veteran dies from a service-connected (or compensable `§ 1151`) disability. Under `38 C.F.R. § 3.312(a)`, death is service-connected if a service-connected disability was **either the principal OR a contributory cause of death**.

1. **Principal Cause of Death (`§ 3.312(b)`):**
   * The service-connected disease or injury, singly or jointly with some other condition, was the **immediate or underlying cause of death** or was **etiologically related** thereto (e.g., service-connected diabetes mellitus causing end-stage renal disease or fatal myocardial infarction; service-connected hepatitis C causing hepatocellular carcinoma).
2. **Contributory Cause of Death (`§ 3.312(c)(1)–(4)` — The High-Yield Litigation Engine):**
   * Even when a **non-service-connected** event is the immediate cause of death (e.g., pneumonia, surgical shock, COVID-19, stroke, or non-SC cancer), DIC must be granted under `§ 3.312(c)(1)` if a service-connected disability:
     * **"Contributed substantially or materially"** to death;
     * **"Combined to cause death"**; or
     * **"Aided or lent assistance to the production of death."** *(Showing merely that it "casually shared" in producing death is insufficient).*
   * **Vital Organ Impairment (`§ 3.312(c)(3)`) vs. Static Musculoskeletal Conditions (`§ 3.312(c)(2)`):** Service-connected disabilities involving active processes affecting **vital organs** (cardiovascular, pulmonary, renal, hepatic, central nervous system) receive heightened probative weight when evaluating whether they debilitated the veteran or lowered physiological reserve against an acute non-SC insult—and where such an active vital-organ condition is rated **`100%` disabling**, ***"debilitation may be assumed"*** (`§ 3.312(c)(3)`). Conversely, minor or quiescent/static musculoskeletal disabilities (`§ 3.312(c)(2)`, even if rated `100%`) generally do not contribute unless medical proof demonstrates they materially accelerated fatal decline (e.g., severe service-connected paraplegia/immobility causing fatal pulmonary embolism, decubitus sepsis, or obesity-mediated cardiopulmonary failure under `VAOPGCPREC 1-2017`).
3. **Overcoming Terse Death Certificates (*Lathan* & *Dyment*):**
   * Attending physicians and county coroners complete death certificates within minutes to authorize burial, routinely listing only terminal cardiac/respiratory arrest rather than underlying chronic pathophysiology.
   * Under ***Lathan v. Brown*, 7 Vet. App. 359, 365–67 (1995)** and ***Dyment v. Principi*, 287 F.3d 1377, 1380–82 (Fed. Cir. 2002)**, VA **cannot** rely on the omission of a service-connected condition from the death certificate to deny DIC without evaluating the totality of terminal clinical records—and where record evidence indicates a service-connected vital-organ condition may have contributed materially to death, VA's duty to assist requires an adequate medical nexus opinion analyzing the mechanics of `§ 3.312(c)` (which need **not** be expressed with absolute medical certainty; `"at least as likely as not"` satisfies `§ 5107(b)`!).
4. ***De Novo* Survivor Merits Independence, Combat Relaxation & Secondary Suicide/Substance Deaths:**
   * **Prior Lifetime Denials Do Not Bind the Survivor (*Hupp v. Nicholson*, `21 Vet. App. 342, 352 (2007)`):** A survivor's `§ 1310` DIC claim is an **original, independent claim**. Even if the veteran applied for service connection for heart disease or lung cancer during life and let a denial become final, the survivor establishes service connection for the cause of death ***de novo*** (without needing CUE or `new and relevant` evidence to reopen the veteran's dead lifetime claim!), armed with `38 U.S.C. § 1154(a)–(b)` combat/service consideration and `§ 5107(b)` Benefit of the Doubt.
   * **Suicide & Secondary Substance-Abuse Deaths (*Myore* & *Allen*):** Under `38 U.S.C. § 105(a)`, willful misconduct bars `§ 1310` DIC (*Myore v. Nicholson*, `489 F.3d 1207, 1213–14 (Fed. Cir. 2007)`). **Two major exceptions defeat the bar:**
     1. **Mental Unsoundness Suicide (`38 C.F.R. § 3.302`):** Suicide resulting from "mental unsoundness" caused by a service-connected disorder (PTSD, major depression, TBI, chronic intractable SC pain syndrome) is **not** willful misconduct and establishes service-connected death!
     2. **Secondary Substance Abuse (*Allen v. Principi*, `237 F.3d 1368 (Fed. Cir. 2001)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.310(a)`):** Fatal cirrhosis, pancreatitis, cardiomyopathy, or accidental overdose caused by alcohol or substance use disorder **proximately secondary to service-connected PTSD, depression, or chronic SC pain** is compensable under `§ 1310` (see [`VAOPGCPREC 7-99` citator note](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md)).

### B. Non-SC Death & Hypothetical `10-Year` Total Disability (`38 U.S.C. § 1318`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.22`)

*(See verbatim historical and current regulatory text in [`38-cfr-3.22-dic-hypothetical-evolution.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.22-dic-hypothetical-evolution.md)).*

Enacted originally as `former 38 U.S.C. § 410(b)` (`Pub. L. 95-479`, Oct. 18, 1978), recodified at `former § 418` (`Pub. L. 100-687, § 1403`, Nov. 18, 1988), and renumbered **`38 U.S.C. § 1318`** (`Pub. L. 102-83`, Aug. 6, 1991), Section `1318` mandates payment of DIC **"in the same manner as if the veteran's death were service connected"** even when the veteran died of a **100% non-service-connected cause**, provided the death was *not* the result of the veteran's own willful misconduct (`§ 1318(b)`) and the veteran was `"in receipt of or entitled to receive"` compensation at death for a service-connected disability **rated totally disabling** (`100%` schedular **or** `TDIU` under `38 C.F.R. § 4.16`!) under any of **three duration tracks**:

1. **The `10-Year` Rule (`§ 1318(b)(1)`; `§ 3.22(a)(2)(i)`):** Continuously rated totally disabling for **10 or more years immediately preceding death**;
2. **The `5-Year` Post-Discharge Rule (`§ 1318(b)(2)`; `§ 3.22(a)(2)(ii)`):** Continuously rated totally disabling from the **date of discharge or release from active duty** and for **not less than 5 years** immediately preceding death; or
3. **The `1-Year` Former POW Rule (`§ 1318(b)(3)`; `§ 3.22(a)(2)(iii)`):** Added by the *John William Rolen Act* (`Pub. L. 106-117, Title V, § 501`, `113 Stat. 1573`, enacted & eff. **Nov. 30, 1999**)—veteran was a **Former Prisoner of War (`FPOW`)** whose SC disability was continuously rated totally disabling for **not less than 1 year immediately preceding death**.

#### The Doctrinal Arc of `"Entitled to Receive"` (`1997–2009` Bitemporal History)

Understanding why open-ended "hypothetical entitlement" died—and how `§ 3.22(b)(1)–(2)` replaced it—is essential to avoiding malpractice:

1. **Era 1 — Open-Ended *De Novo* Hypothetical Entitlement (`1997–Jan. 20, 2000`):** In ***Green v. Brown*, 10 Vet. App. 111 (1997)**, ***Wingo v. West*, 11 Vet. App. 307 (1998)**, and ***Cole v. West*, 13 Vet. App. 268 (1999)**, the CAVC construed `"entitled to receive"` in `§ 1318(b)` to allow a survivor to prove *de novo*—from records in the C-file or VA custody—that the veteran *hypothetically should have been rated* `100%` or `TDIU` for 10 years prior to death, **without proving CUE** in a prior final rating decision. *(See also Wanner v. Principi, 17 Vet. App. 4 (2003), rev'd in part on jurisdictional VASRD grounds, 370 F.3d 1124 (Fed. Cir. 2004)).*
2. **Era 2 — The January 21, 2000 Rulemaking (`65 FR 3388`):** Published and effective **January 21, 2000** (`65 FR 3388`), VA overhauled `38 C.F.R. § 3.22(b)` expressly to overturn *Green*, *Wingo*, and *Cole*. Amended `§ 3.22(b)` restricts `"entitled to receive"` strictly to cases where **the veteran filed a disability compensation claim during his or her lifetime** AND satisfies an enumerated regulatory basis—chiefly:
   * **`§ 3.22(b)(1)` (Lifetime CUE):** The veteran would have received total disability compensation for the `10`/`5`/`1`-year period **but for Clear and Unmistakable Error (`CUE`)** committed by VA in a decision on a claim filed during the veteran's lifetime;
   * **`§ 3.22(b)(2)` (`§ 3.156(c)` Service Department Records):** VA reopens/reconsiders a lifetime claim after death based **solely on newly associated service department records** (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.156(c), 3.400(q)(2)`), yielding a retroactive total rating spanning the required period; or
   * **Administrative Nonpayment / Withholding Tracks (`§ 3.22(b)(3)(i)–(vi)`) & Pending Claims (`§ 5121`/`§ 5121A`):** Under `§ 3.22(b)(3)(i)–(vi)` (`70 FR 72220`), `"entitled to receive"` also covers cases where the veteran held a total disability rating for the required period that was unpaid solely due to `(i)` dependents' apportionment, `(ii)` `§ 5314` overpayment/indebtedness offset, `(iii)` unwaived military retired pay, `(iv)` `10 U.S.C. § 1174(h)(2)` separation-pay recoupment, `(v)` unknown whereabouts, or `(vi)` `§§ 5308–5309` alien withholding. Separately, winning a retroactive `10-year` total rating post-mortem on a claim pending at death via `§ 5121` accrued benefits or `§ 5121A` substitution establishes `§ 3.22(a)(2)` entitlement directly (`70 FR 72213–14`).
3. **Era 3 — Facial Validity & Retroactive Application (*NOVA*, *Rodriguez* & *Tarver*):**
   * In ***Nat'l Org. of Veterans' Advocates, Inc. (NOVA) v. Sec'y of Veterans Affairs*, 314 F.3d 1373 (Fed. Cir. 2003)** (following an earlier remand at `260 F.3d 1365 (Fed. Cir. 2001)` requiring VA to harmonize `§ 3.22` with `38 C.F.R. § 20.1106`), the Federal Circuit upheld `65 FR 3388` as a valid interpretation of `§ 1318(b)`.
   * In ***Rodriguez v. Peake*, 511 F.3d 1147 (Fed. Cir. 2008)** and ***Tarver v. Shinseki*, 557 F.3d 1371 (Fed. Cir. 2009)**, the Federal Circuit held that amended `§ 3.22(b)` **applies retroactively even to DIC claims filed BEFORE January 21, 2000**, foreclosing open-ended *Green*/*Cole* hypothetical arguments across all eras.

#### Three Winning `§ 3.22(b)` Reconstruction Plays Today

* **Play 1 — Unadjudicated `Roberson` / `Rice` TDIU CUE (`§ 3.22(b)(1)`):** Veteran died in 2024 of a car accident; held `100%` from 2018–2024 (`6` years), and held `70%` PTSD from 2011–2018 while VAMC records in the C-file (post-July 21, 1992 *Bell* constructive possession) undebatably established inability to work due to PTSD during a 2012 increased-rating adjudication where the RO failed to adjudicate TDIU ([Playbook 04](04-unadjudicated-tdiu-informal-claims-smc.md)). Proving CUE in the 2012 RO decision under `§ 3.22(b)(1)` extends total disability from `2012 -> 2024` (`12` continuous years), **unlocking both `§ 1318` DIC and the `§ 1311(a)(2)` 8-year kicker**!
* **Play 2 — Late-Arriving `§ 3.156(c)` Service Records (`§ 3.22(b)(2)`):** Veteran filed a PTSD or orthopedic claim in 2009 that was denied for lack of stressor/in-service documentation, was granted `100%` in 2019 (`5` years before a 2024 non-SC death), and post-mortem NPRC/JSRRC unit records corroborate the 2009 stressor ([Playbook 03](03-service-department-records-38-cfr-3-156c.md)). Under `§ 3.22(b)(2)`, reconsidering the 2009 claim with `100%`/`TDIU` medical staging back to `<= 2014` satisfies the `10-year` clock **without proving CUE**!
* **Play 3 — Void Rating Reduction Restoration ([Playbook 05](05-void-rating-reductions-stabilized-ratings.md)):** If VA unlawfully reduced a `100%` or `TDIU` evaluation within 10 years of death in violation of `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.105(e), 3.343, or 3.344`, restoring the void reduction *ab initio* bridges the gap and establishes `10` continuous years of total disability!

### C. DIC Rate Architecture (`38 U.S.C. § 1311`), SBP Repeal & PACT Act `§ 1305` Re-Evaluations

1. **Flat Rate vs. Pre-1993 Pay-Grade Election (`§ 1311(a)(1)` vs. `§ 1311(a)(3)`):**
   * Under the *Veterans' Benefits Act of 1992* (`Pub. L. 102-568, § 102`, eff. **Jan. 1, 1993**), Congress replaced military pay-grade DIC tables with a uniform flat monthly base rate (`§ 1311(a)(1)`) for deaths occurring **on or after January 1, 1993**.
   * **Pre-1993 Death Audit (`§ 1311(a)(3)`):** Whenever a veteran died **before January 1, 1993** (e.g., in a *Nehmer*, `§ 1305`, or `§ 3.156(c)` retroactive DIC grant, or an ongoing widow's award), VA **must** pay whichever is **greater**: `(i)` the legacy military pay-grade table rate (`E-7` through `O-10` routinely pays **hundreds of dollars more per month**!) **or** `(ii)` the modern `§ 1311(a)(1) + (a)(2)` flat + kicker rate!
2. **The `"8-Year Kicker"` & Vigilant Marriage-Overlap Audit (`38 U.S.C. § 1311(a)(2)`):**
   * Adds a substantial COLA-indexed monthly supplement (`~$330+/month` in 2025–2026) if the veteran was `"in receipt of or was entitled to receive"` compensation for a SC disability **rated totally disabling (`100%` or `TDIU`) for a continuous period of at least 8 years immediately preceding death**.
   * **CRITICAL MARRIAGE-OVERLAP RULE:** The final sentence of `38 U.S.C. § 1311(a)(2)` mandates: ***"In determining such period, only periods in which the veteran was married to the surviving spouse shall be considered."*** Both conditions—total disability **AND** legal/deemed-valid marriage to *that* surviving spouse—must overlap continuously for the **8 years immediately preceding death**!
3. **Mandatory DIC Monthly Add-Ons (`§ 1311(b), (c), (d), (f)`):**
   * **`§ 1311(b)` (Dependent Children):** Monthly add-on for **each** child of the veteran under age 18 (or helpless child / schoolchild where applicable).
   * **`§ 1311(c)` (Surviving Spouse Aid & Attendance) & `§ 1311(d)` (Housebound):** Monthly supplements (`~$400+` for A&A; `~$195+` for Housebound) when the surviving spouse resides in a nursing home, is blind, or requires regular assistance with activities of daily living (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.351, 3.352` — always screen elderly surviving spouses with **`VA Form 21-2680`**!).
   * **`§ 1311(f)` (2-Year Transitional Child Benefit):** Enacted by `Pub. L. 108-454, § 301` (eff. Jan. 1, 2005), pays an **automatic extra monthly flat benefit** (`~$350+/month`, COLA-indexed) on top of `§ 1311(b)` for the **first 2 years** following commencement of DIC entitlement whenever the surviving spouse has **one or more children under age 18** (terminating after 24 months or when the youngest child turns 18, whichever occurs first).
4. **Complete Repeal of the SBP-DIC `"Widow's Tax"` (`Pub. L. 116-92, § 622`; `10 U.S.C. § 1450(c)`):**
   * Historically, `10 U.S.C. § 1450(c)` (and `38 U.S.C. § 1318(e)` equating `§ 1318` DIC with `§ 1311(a)` DIC) forced DFAS to confiscate military Survivor Benefit Plan (`SBP`) annuities dollar-for-dollar by the amount of VA DIC.
   * Section `622` of the **NDAA for FY 2020 (`Pub. L. 116-92`)** repealed the offset in three steps: **(i) `Jan. 1, 2021`:** SBP offset capped at `2/3` of DIC + Special Survivor Indemnity Allowance (`SSIA`); **(ii) `Jan. 1, 2022`:** SBP offset capped at `1/3` of DIC + `SSIA`; **(iii) `January 1, 2023 – Present`:** **100% complete repeal**—surviving spouses receive **100% of military SBP AND 100% of VA DIC concurrently** with zero offset! *(When winning retroactive pre-2023 DIC for a retiree spouse, audit DFAS recoupment math across the pre-2021, 2021, 2022, and post-Jan. 1, 2023 windows and verify employee SBP-premium refund credits!)*
5. **PACT Act DIC Re-Evaluations & Original-Claim Effective Dates (`38 U.S.C. § 1305` & `38 C.F.R. § 3.33`):**
   * **Statutory Precision (`§ 204` vs. `§ 404` of `Pub. L. 117-168`):** Practitioners frequently refer colloquially to "PACT Act § 404 DIC re-elections" because **Section `404`** of `Pub. L. 117-168` (`136 Stat. 1782`, Aug. 10, 2022) added **hypertension and MGUS** as herbicide presumptions (`38 U.S.C. § 1116(a)(2)(L)–(M)`—which are excluded from `§ 3.816` *Nehmer*!). However, the overarching statutory DIC re-evaluation mandate was enacted by **Section `204`** of `Pub. L. 117-168` (`136 Stat. 1776`, eff. Aug. 10, 2022), codified at **`38 U.S.C. § 1305`**, and implemented at **`38 C.F.R. § 3.33`** (`88 FR 82264`, published Nov. 24, 2023, eff. **Jan. 23, 2024**).
   * **Super-Retroactive Effective-Date Mandate (`§ 1305(a)(3)`; `§ 3.33(d)(1)`):** Whenever a previously submitted DIC claim was evaluated and **denied before** a law, regulation, or federal court decision/settlement taking effect **on or after August 10, 2022** established or modified a presumption of service connection (covering PACT Act `§ 404` hypertension/MGUS, `§§ 401–403` Thailand/Laos/Cambodia/Guam herbicides, `§ 406` / `§ 1120` burn-pit cancers/COPD/interstitial lung disease, and future presumptions), the survivor may elect re-evaluation (e.g., via `VA Form 20-0995` or `21P-534EZ`), and ***"notwithstanding section 5110 of this title,"*** VA **must** grant DIC **"effective as if the establishment or modification of the presumption of service connection had been in effect on the date of the submission of the original claim"**!
   * **Audit Goldmine:** Unlike living-veteran PACT Act hypertension claims (capped at Aug. 10, 2022 absent an independent finality exception; [Playbook 06](06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md)), a widow whose Vietnam-veteran husband died of hypertensive heart/renal disease or stroke and whose DIC claim was denied in **1998** wins **nearly 30 years of retroactive DIC back to 1998** under `38 U.S.C. § 1305(a)(3)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.33(d)(1)`!

## 4. Invariants / Kill-Switches

* **Dead *Green* / *Cole* Open-Ended Hypothetical Entitlement:** Never argue that `§ 1318` DIC is warranted simply because C-file evidence could support a *de novo* retrospective `100%` rating without a lifetime claim + `§ 3.22(b)(1)` CUE or `§ 3.22(b)(2)` `§ 3.156(c)` records (*NOVA*, `314 F.3d 1373`; *Rodriguez*, `511 F.3d 1147`; *Tarver*, `557 F.3d 1371`).
* **The *George v. McDonough*, *Cook* & *Damrel* CUE Bars Inside `§ 3.22(b)(1)`:**
  * Under *George v. McDonough*, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)` and `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`, you **cannot** establish `§ 3.22(b)(1)` CUE in a final lifetime decision by invoking a *post-decision* judicial reinterpretation (`open-claim-only-george-restricted`), nor can an inadequate lifetime examination or `§ 5103A` duty-to-assist failure establish `§ 3.22(b)(1)` CUE (*Cook v. Principi*, `318 F.3d 1334 (Fed. Cir. 2002) (en banc)`; `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(d)(1)`). Always check `§ 3.156(c)` (`§ 3.22(b)(2)`), void reduction restoration ([Playbook 05](05-void-rating-reductions-stabilized-ratings.md)), or `§ 1310` contributory death instead!
  * Under *Damrel v. Brown*, `6 Vet. App. 242, 246 (1994)` and `VAOPGCPREC 12-95`, *Bell v. Derwinski* constructive possession of VAMC records applies **only to lifetime AOJ/Board decisions rendered on or after July 21, 1992** ([Procedural Vehicles Piercing Finality](../docs/procedural-vehicles-piercing-finality.md)). Also complete the [`Board CUE Pre-Filing Gate`](../checklists/board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md) to avoid BVA subsumption (`§ 20.1400(b)`) or one-shot res judicata (`§ 20.1409(c)`).
* **The `§ 1318(d)` Wrongful-Death Tort Offset Trap (`VAOPGCPREC 3-97`):** Under `38 U.S.C. § 1318(d)` and [`VAOPGCPREC 3-97`](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md), if a surviving spouse or child receives money/property from **any judicial award, FTCA judgment, or private tort settlement** for the veteran's death (e.g., asbestos, medical malpractice, auto crash), **`§ 1318` DIC is suspended dollar-for-dollar** until the entire settlement is recouped—regardless of state-law "pain and suffering" labels! By contrast, **`§ 1310` direct/contributory SC death carries NO `§ 1318(d)` private-tort offset** (offsets apply to `§ 1310`-style awards *only* if entitlement rests on `38 U.S.C. § 1151(b)` malpractice). Always prove `§ 1310` contributory death whenever a wrongful-death settlement exists!
* **Marriage Duration & Remarriage Cutoffs (`38 U.S.C. §§ 103(d), 1304, 1318(c)`):** Verify 1-year marriage (or child born of/before marriage, or marriage `<15` years after the period of service in which the fatal disability arose under `§ 1304(2)` for `§ 1310` DIC!) and remarriage age thresholds (**age `57`** eff. Jan. 1, 2004 via `Pub. L. 108-183`; **age `55`** eff. Jan. 5, 2021 via `Pub. L. 116-315`) in [`Survivor Marriage, Remarriage & Dependency Evolution`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/survivor-marriage-dependency-evolution.md).

## 5. Postconditions: Effective Dates, Ancillaries, Offsets & Fees

1. **Effective-Date Verification Matrix:**
   * **Filed Within 1 Year of Death (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(d)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.400(c)(2)`):** Effective date is the **first day of the calendar month in which the veteran died**! *(Verify whether an Intent to File `VA Form 21-0966` or Social Security survivorship application filed under `38 U.S.C. § 5105` / `38 C.F.R. § 3.153` locked in the 1-year post-death window!)*
   * **Previously Denied DIC Re-Evaluated Under New Post-Aug. 10, 2022 Presumption (`38 U.S.C. § 1305(a)(3)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.33(d)(1)`):** Effective **as if the presumption had been in effect when the original denied DIC claim was submitted** (reaching back to the month of death if that original denied DIC claim was filed within 1 year of death!).
   * ***Nehmer* Herbicide Death (`38 C.F.R. § 3.816(d)`):** Relates back to the prior post-Sept. 25, 1985 denied DIC claim (or month of death if filed within 1 year) per [Playbook 06](06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md); unpaid *Nehmer* DIC if the surviving spouse subsequently dies is payable down the `§ 3.816(f)` chain ([Playbook 09](09-accrued-benefits-substitution-nehmer-estates.md)).
   * **First-Time Liberalizing DIC Claim (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(g)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.114(a)`):** Up to **1 year prior** to receipt of claim/ITF, no earlier than the liberalizing law's effective date (e.g., **August 10, 2022** for PACT Act claims backed by a pre-Aug. 14, 2023 ITF).
2. **Mandatory Ancillary Survivor Unlocks (Audit Every Grant Letter!):**
   * **Chapter 35 Survivors' & Dependents' Educational Assistance (`DEA`, `38 U.S.C. §§ 3500–3566`):** Up to 36 months of tax-free college/vocational stipends for the surviving spouse and eligible children (with modernized post-Aug. 1, 2023 delimiting-date repeal under `38 U.S.C. § 3512`!).
   * **CHAMPVA Comprehensive Health Care (`38 U.S.C. § 1781`)** (if not TRICARE-eligible).
   * **Chapter 37 Surviving Spouse VA Home Loan Guaranty & Funding-Fee Exemption (`38 U.S.C. § 3701(b)(2)`).**
   * **Service-Connected Burial Allowance Upgrade (`38 U.S.C. § 2307`):** Whenever DIC is granted under **`§ 1310`** (service-connected death—*note: `§ 2307` requires `§ 1310` SC death rather than `§ 1318` non-SC death!*), claim the statutory SC burial reimbursement (`$2,000+`) and National Cemetery plot/transportation benefits.
3. **Attorney Fees (`38 U.S.C. § 5904` & `EAJA`):**
   * Because a survivor's first `VA Form 21P-534EZ` is an **original claim** separate from the veteran's lifetime claims (*Hupp*), paid `§ 5904(c)(1)` contingency fees attach **only after VA issues an initial AOJ decision on the DIC claim**—**EXCEPT** where the survivor is pursuing a Supplemental Claim / HLR / Board appeal / `§ 1305` re-evaluation of a **previously denied DIC claim**, in which case an initial AOJ decision already exists and `§ 5904` contingency representation attaches immediately! Always offset any `28 U.S.C. § 2412(d)` EAJA award dollar-for-dollar against `§ 5904` fees for the same appellate work ([Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).
