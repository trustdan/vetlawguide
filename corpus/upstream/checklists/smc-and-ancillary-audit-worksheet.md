# Zero-PII 5-Gate Special Monthly Compensation (`SMC(k)`–`SMC(t)`), *Barry* Step-Math & Ancillary Grants Audit Worksheet

> [!CAUTION]
> **ZERO CLIENT PII RULE:** Never enter veteran names, SSNs, claim numbers, or unredacted medical/award text inside this Git repository—copy this worksheet to your secure offline case management folder before screening! Because SMC is **inferred by law** inside every evaluation claim whenever record evidence reasonably raises it (*Akles v. Derwinski*, `1 Vet. App. 118 (1991)`; *Buie v. Shinseki*, `24 Vet. App. 242 (2010)`), running all **five sequential gates** below routinely uncovers **`$2,000–$7,000+/month` rate upgrades** plus **`$150,000+` in unclaimed ancillary grants**.

*(Cross-references: Verbatim `SMC` regulations [`38-cfr-3.350-3.352-smc-evolution.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.350-3.352-smc-evolution.md); [`Part 3 SMC Evolution Diff`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/smc-statutory-and-regulatory-evolution.md); [Master SMC Treatise & Step Math](../docs/smc-complete-treatise-and-step-math.md); [Playbook 12 (`SMC(l)`, `SMC(r)(1)–(2)` & `SMC(t)`)](../playbooks/12-aid-and-attendance-smc-l-r1-r2-and-smc-t.md); [Playbook 13 (Ancillary Grants)](../playbooks/13-ancillary-grants-sah-auto-clothing-dea-spina-bifida.md); [Playbook 04 (Unadjudicated TDIU & Mandatory SMC)](../playbooks/04-unadjudicated-tdiu-informal-claims-smc.md); [`Surviving Spouse & Helpless Child Diff`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/survivor-marriage-dependency-evolution.md); [Procedural Vehicles for Piercing Finality](../docs/procedural-vehicles-piercing-finality.md); [Board CUE Pre-Filing Gate](board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md); [Master `VAOPGCPREC` Digest](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md); [Three-Era Statutory Crosswalk](../docs/title-38-statutory-crosswalk.md); [Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md); and [Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).*

## Preconditions (Mandatory C-File / VBMS Intake Packet)

- [ ] **Pull Every Historical & Current Rating Code Sheet:** Notification letters omit internal SMC designators (`K-1`, `S-1`, `L-1`, `M½`, `R-1`, `T`), diagnostic codes, and subordinate ancillary blocks!
- [ ] **Pull All `VA Form 21-2680`s, C&P DBQs, VAMC `PSAS` Prosthetics Logs & Caregiver (`PCAFC`) Assessments:** Essential for proving functional Loss of Use (`LOU`), bracing, ADL deficits, and TBI supervision.

## Gate 1: `SMC(k)` (`x3` Stacking) & Statutory Rate-Ceiling Audit (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(k)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(a)`)

Audit whether the veteran qualifies for **up to `3` concurrent `SMC(k)` awards (`K-1`, `K-2`, `K-3`)**:

- [ ] **1. Creative Organ (`§ 3.350(a)(1)`, eff. `Aug. 1, 1952`):** SC erectile dysfunction (`DC 7522`, e.g., secondary to diabetes, hypertension, radical prostatectomy `DC 7528`, spinal/radicular nerve injury, or SSRI/PTSD medication!), female sexual arousal disorder (`FSAD`), orchiectomy, hysterectomy, or oophorectomy.
  - **`VAOPGCPREC 5-89` Surgical Rule (`>= Mar. 23, 1989`):** Prior elective noncompensable sterilization (vasectomy / tubal ligation) **does NOT bar `SMC(k)`** for subsequent SC **anatomical** removal of a creative organ!
- [ ] **2. Single Hand or Single Foot (`§ 3.350(a)(2)`):** Anatomical loss or functional `LOU` (proceed to **Gate 2**).
- [ ] **3. Both Buttocks (`§ 3.350(a)(3)`, eff. `Aug. 1, 1956`):** Severe bilateral gluteal muscle damage preventing the veteran from rising from a seated or stooping position and maintaining postural stability without assistance ([Playbook 01](../playbooks/01-penetrating-wounds-scars-vs-muscles.md)).
- [ ] **4. Single Eye Blindness (`§ 3.350(a)(4)`):** Light perception only (`LPO`) or anatomical enucleation.
- [ ] **5. Total Bilateral Deafness (`§ 3.350(a)(5)`, eff. `July 1, 1963`, `Pub. L. 88-20`):** Absence of air and bone conduction in both ears.
- [ ] **6. Complete Organic Aphonia (`§ 3.350(a)(6)`, eff. `July 1, 1963`, `Pub. L. 88-22`):** Constant organic inability to communicate by speech (e.g., post-laryngectomy `DC 6518` or vocal-cord paralysis `DC 6519`).
- [ ] **7. Breast Tissue Loss or Radiation (`§ 3.350(a)` intro; `former § 3.350(a)(7)` removed at `68 FR 55467`):** Complete mastectomy (`>= Nov. 1, 2000`, `Pub. L. 106-419`), OR **`>= 25%` tissue loss** (single breast or combined lumpectomy/excision) **OR radiation treatment of breast tissue** (`>= Dec. 6, 2002`, `Pub. L. 107-330`, `68 FR 55467`).
- [ ] **Statutory Rate-Ceiling Check (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(k)`):**
  - Combined `0%–100%` schedular/TDIU (`§ 1114(a)–(j)`) or `SMC(s)` (`§ 1114(s)`): Each `SMC(k)` (`up to 3`) adds on top, provided `§ 1114(a)–(j)` or `§ 1114(s)` + `k`(s) does not exceed the `SMC(l)` dollar rate (capped at `(l)`).
  - Base `SMC(l)` through `SMC(n½)`: `SMC(k)` (`up to 3`) adds on top, **strictly capped at the `SMC(o)` / `§ 1114(p)` dollar ceiling** (`zero` `SMC(k)` add-ons pay above `SMC(o)`, `SMC(r)(1)`, `SMC(r)(2)`, or `SMC(t)`!).

## Gate 2: Extremity Loss of Use (`LOU`) Screen (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.350(a)(2), 4.63` — *Tucker v. West*)

Never wait for physical amputation—audit every severe radiculopathy, peripheral neuropathy, spinal cord, `ALS`, stroke, or orthopedic file under ***Tucker v. West*, `11 Vet. App. 369 (1998)`**:

- [ ] **Lower Extremity `LOU` (`Foot`):** Would remaining function be **equally well served by an amputation with prosthesis**? Check for **complete foot drop** (common peroneal nerve paralysis), inability to balance/propel (`push-off`) without a rigid ankle-foot orthosis (`AFO`), unfavorable ankylosis, non-union of tibia/fibula, or severe trophic/vascular ulceration.
- [ ] **Upper Extremity `LOU` (`Hand`):** No more effective function than an amputation stump with suitable prosthetic appliance—e.g., inability to grasp, pinch, manipulate, or oppose thumb and fingers (`median`/`ulnar`/`radial` paralysis or contractures).
- [ ] **Paired `LOU` Upgrade Check:** `2 feet` = **`SMC(l)`**; `2 hands` = **`SMC(m)`**; `1 hand + 1 foot` = **`SMC(l)`**; higher anatomical/above-knee/above-elbow pairings = **`SMC(l½)`–`SMC(o)`** *(Note: under `VAOPGCPREC 30-97`, preexisting NSC paralysis never bars anatomical-amputation SMC!).*

## Gate 3: `SMC(s)` Housebound Screen — Statutory (`100% + 60%`) vs. Factual

Whenever the veteran is **not** yet receiving `SMC(l)+`, test both `38 U.S.C. § 1114(s)` (`eff. Sept. 1, 1960`) prongs:

- [ ] **Prong 3A — Statutory `SMC(s)` (`100% + 60%`):**
  - Does the veteran have **ONE single SC disability rated `100%` schedular** OR **TDIU (`§ 4.16`) attributable solely to ONE single SC disability** (*Bradley v. Peake*, `22 Vet. App. 280 (2008)`; *Buie v. Shinseki*, `24 Vet. App. 242 (2010)`)? *(Trap: Multi-disability TDIU fails this prong under *Gazelle v. Shulkin*, `868 F.3d 1006 (Fed. Cir. 2017)`—get an IMO isolating unemployability to a single `70%` PTSD or `60%` back condition!)*
  - Do the **remaining separate/independent SC disabilities** combine via `38 C.F.R. § 4.25` (not simple addition, *Gazelle*) to **`>= 60%`** (rounded, e.g., `55% -> 60%`)?
  - **Bitemporal CUE Gate:** Omission of `SMC(s)` on a **schedular `100% + 60%`** code sheet (`>= Sept. 1, 1960`) is **pure math CUE (`cue-and-open-claim`)**! For pre-Nov. 26, 2008 TDIU-based denials (`VAOPGCPREC 6-99` era), *George v. McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) bars CUE—use `§ 3.156(b)–(c)` or pending claims ([Playbook 04](../playbooks/04-unadjudicated-tdiu-informal-claims-smc.md)).
- [ ] **Prong 3B — Factual Housebound (`38 C.F.R. § 3.350(i)(2)`):**
  - Single `100%` (or TDIU) disability + **substantially confined** as a direct result of SC disability(ies) to the veteran's dwelling and immediate premises (or ward/clinical area) with reasonable certainty of persistence throughout life.
  - ***Howell v. Nicholson*, `19 Vet. App. 535 (2006)`:** Leaving home **for medical appointments** does **NOT** defeat factual housebound!

## Gate 4: `SMC(l)` A&A, *Barry* Multi-Step Calculator, Anti-Pyramiding, `SMC(r)`/`(t)` & `§ 3.552` Trap

Run this 5-step catastrophic calculator on every polytrauma, `A&A`, or `TBI` file ([Playbook 12](../playbooks/12-aid-and-attendance-smc-l-r1-r2-and-smc-t.md)):

- [ ] **Step 4.1 — Establish Base `SMC(l)`–`SMC(n)`:**
  - **Regular `A&A` (`SMC(l)`, `38 C.F.R. § 3.352(a)`):** Under *Turco v. Brown*, `9 Vet. App. 222 (1996)`, the veteran **need NOT satisfy every listed factor**—inability to dress, bathe, feed, toilet, adjust prosthetics, **OR physical/mental incapacity requiring regular care/supervision to protect from hazards of the daily environment** establishes `SMC(l)`!
- [ ] **Step 4.2 — Cross Out Base Disabilities (*Breniser* / *Amberman* Anti-Double-Counting Gate!):**
  - List the exact SC disability(ies) used to establish base `SMC(l)–(n)` in Step 4.1. Under ***Breniser v. Shinseki*, `25 Vet. App. 64 (2011)`** and ***Amberman v. Shinseki*, `570 F.3d 1377 (Fed. Cir. 2009)`**, those base disabilities are **"spent"** and **CANNOT** also be counted in Step 4.3! *(Practice Tip: If a veteran has BOTH bilateral foot `LOU` AND `A&A` due to a separate `100%` Parkinson's/PTSD condition, ground base `SMC(l)` on bilateral foot `LOU` so the separate `100%` condition remains free to power a full-step `(f)(4)` bump—or vice versa!)*
- [ ] **Step 4.3 — Apply Multi-Step `§ 3.350(f)(3)–(4)` Stacking (*Barry v. McDonough*, `101 F.4th 1348 (Fed. Cir. May 16, 2024)`):**
  - Count every **separate, independent ("unspent") permanent SC disability rated `50%`–`90%`** (plus any `§ 4.25` **combination** of leftover `< 50%` permanent SC disabilities combining to `>= 50%`) -> add **`+ ½` step EACH** (`§ 3.350(f)(3)`).
  - Count every **separate, independent ("unspent") permanent SC disability rated `100%` schedular** (*excluding `§ 4.16` TDIU*) -> add **`+ 1` full step EACH** (`§ 3.350(f)(4)`).
  - Sum steps along `L -> L½ -> M -> M½ -> N -> N½ -> O` (capped at **`SMC(o)`**; add any `SMC(k)` up to the `SMC(o)` dollar cap). *(Note: *George* bars standalone CUE on final pre-May 16, 2024 single-step ratings—use pending appeals, `§ 3.156(b)–(c)`, *Hamill* AMA silent omissions, or file an immediate Supplemental/increase claim!)*
- [ ] **Step 4.4 — Check `SMC(r)(1)`, `SMC(r)(2)` & TBI `SMC(t)` (`~$11,000+/month` Top Tiers!):**
  - **`SMC(r)(1)` (`§ 1114(r)(1)`; `§ 3.350(h)`):** Did *Barry* step-stacking (or paired `LOU`s) push the veteran to **`SMC(o)`** (or **`SMC(n½) + SMC(k)`** hitting the `(p)` ceiling) **WHILE** also needing regular `A&A`? If yes, **`SMC(r)(1)` unlocks automatically** (provided `A&A` itself wasn't the sole basis without independent paired losses/bumps satisfying `§ 3.350(h)`)!
  - **`SMC(r)(2)` (`§ 1114(r)(2)`; `§ 3.352(b)(1)`):** Qualifies for `(r)(1)` **PLUS** requires daily personal health-care services by (or under regular supervision of) a **licensed health-care professional** absent which institutionalization would be required.
  - **`SMC(t)` for Traumatic Brain Injury (`§ 1114(t)`, `Pub. L. 111-275, § 601`, eff. `Oct. 1, 2011` — *Laska v. McDonough*, `37 Vet. App. 460 (Sept. 6, 2024)`):**
    - Does the veteran have **SC TBI residuals** requiring **regular `A&A` (`§ 3.352(a)`)** (including lay/spousal cognitive supervision, medication management, wandering/safety protection) without which they would require hospitalization, nursing home, or residential institutional care?
    - **Post-*Laska* Goldmine:** *Laska* **invalidated `38 C.F.R. § 3.352(b)(2)`'s "higher level of care" hurdle**—paying the **full `SMC(r)(2)` rate** (`>= Oct. 1, 2011` statutory floor) **WITHOUT** needing `SMC(o)` and **WITHOUT** needing skilled nursing care!
- [ ] **Step 4.5 — Audit `38 C.F.R. § 3.552` VA Hospitalization Reductions:**
  - If VA reduced `SMC(l)`, `(r)(1)`, `(r)(2)`, or `(t)` during inpatient care, verify: `(1)` was care actually **at VA/government expense**? `(2)` did VA wait until at least the **`1st` day of the `2nd` calendar month following the month of admission** (`§ 3.552(b)(1)`)? and `(3)` did VA overlook the **`§ 3.552(a)(2)` continuation exception** (paraplegia involving both lower extremities + bowel/bladder sphincter loss, or Hansen's disease) or fail to restore full A&A immediately on hospital release?

## Gate 5: High-Dollar Ancillary Grants & Fee/Offset Sweep ([Playbook 13](../playbooks/13-ancillary-grants-sah-auto-clothing-dea-spina-bifida.md))

- [ ] **`Ch. 21` `SAH` / `SHA` / `TRA` (`VA Form 26-4555`, `~$117,000+`):** Screen bilateral LE bracing/crutches/wheelchair under ***Jensen v. Shulkin*, `29 Vet. App. 66 (2017)`** (`§ 3.809(c)` — does *not* require `SMC` `LOU`!), `<= 20/200` bilateral blindness (`Pub. L. 116-154`, eff. Oct. 1, 2020), `1 LE + organic balance/propulsion`, severe burns, `ALS` (`§ 3.809(d)`), or `§ 1151` (*Kilpatrick*); check `6` lifetime grant uses (`§ 2102(d)(3)`) + `TRA` family-home grant (`§ 2102A`).
- [ ] **`Ch. 39` Auto Purchase + Adaptive Equipment (`VA Form 21-4502` & `10-1394`, `~$25,000+` + Unlimited Equip.):** Screen `1+` hand/foot `LOU`, `<= 20/200` or `20°` blindness, severe burns (`Pub. L. 111-275, § 803`, eff. Oct. 1, 2011), `ALS`, knee/hip ankylosis (`10-1394` adaptive equip.), and **prior recipients from `>= 30 years` ago** (immediately eligible for a **second full vehicle grant** under `Pub. L. 117-333`, `38 U.S.C. § 3903(a)`, shifting to every `10 years` on Jan. 5, 2033!).
- [ ] **Multiple Annual Clothing Allowances (`VA Form 10-8678`, `§ 3.810`, `Aug. 1` Deadline):** Count **every** qualifying brace, prosthesis, wheelchair, or SC skin cream damaging distinct garments (`§ 3.810(a)(2)`, *Sursely*) or accelerating damage to a single garment (`§ 3.810(a)(3)`); appeal VHA `Handbook 1173.15` `"non-rigid"` denials to BVA (`§ 20.105`); verify automatic annual renewal (`Pub. L. 117-328, Div. U, § 201`).
- [ ] **Retroactive `Ch. 35` DEA (`VA Form 22-5490`) & `CHAMPVA` (`VA Form 10-10d` / `10-7959a`):** Calendar the **strict `1-year` post-`P&T`-notice deadline (`38 C.F.R. § 21.4131`)** to capture multi-year retroactive DEA tuition/stipends; verify post-Aug. 1, 2023 elimination of DEA age-26/10-year expiration (`Pub. L. 117-328, Div. U, § 234`); **never** let an unadjudicated `18–23` disabled child cash a DEA payment before establishing `§ 3.356` helpless-child status (`38 U.S.C. § 3562`).
- [ ] **`Ch. 18` Spina Bifida & Birth Defects (`VA Form 21-0304`, Denver RO):** Screen biological children of Vietnam, Korea DMZ (`Sept. 1, 1967 – Aug. 31, 1971`), and **Thailand (`Jan. 9, 1962 – May 7, 1975`, `38 U.S.C. § 1822`)** herbicide veterans for spina bifida (non-occulta), and biological children of female Vietnam veterans (`§ 3.815`)—zero offset against VA compensation, DIC, DEA, or SSI (`§ 1834`)!
- [ ] **Offsets & `§ 5904` Fee Check:** Audit `CRDP`/`CRSC`/*Soto* concurrent receipt ([Playbook 06](../playbooks/06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md)), verify post-decision `§ 5904(c)(1)` fee posture on denied `SMC`/ancillary/VHA appeals, and structure retainers to account for non-withheld VHA/vendor disbursements + CAVC `EAJA` fee offsets.
