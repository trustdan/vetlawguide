# Retroactive Pathway Comparison & Selection Matrix

> **Golden Rule of Veterans Appellate Strategy:** **CUE is your weapon of LAST resort, not first resort.** Always screen for non-CUE vehicles (`§ 3.156(c)`, Void Reduction, *Nehmer*, Defective Notice, `§ 3.156(b)` *Lang*, Pending Informal/AMA-Omitted Claim) *first* because they give the veteran the **Benefit of the Doubt (`38 U.S.C. § 5107(b)`)**, allow **new medical evidence**, and bypass both CUE's "undebatable" trapdoor and the *George v. McDonough*, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)` / `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)` change-in-interpretation bar! See [`procedural-vehicles-piercing-finality.md`](../docs/procedural-vehicles-piercing-finality.md).

## Side-by-Side Comparison of Retroactive Vehicles

| Pathway | Governing Law | Standard of Proof | New Evidence Allowed? | Duty to Assist Applies? | Effective Date Unlocked | Fatal Trap / Bitemporal Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Service Dept. Records Reconsideration** | `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)(1) & (3)` | Equipoise (`§ 5107(b)`) | **YES** (incl. new nexus opinions!) | **YES** | Later of **original denied claim date** or **date entitlement arose** | Record must be an *official service dept. record* relevant to a disputed issue (*Blubaugh* / *Kisor*); **bypasses *George* CUE bar** |
| **2. *Nehmer* Class Retroactivity** | `38 C.F.R. § 3.816` | Equipoise + presumptive exposure/disease | **YES** | **YES** | Prior denied/coded claim (post-`9/25/1985`) or date disability arose; **pays estate/survivors 100%**! | Applies to covered RVN/Blue Water *Nehmer* class claims (`§ 3.816`), excluding chloracne, hypertension & MGUS (`M21-1 VIII.i.2.B.1.c`) |
| **3. Void Rating Reduction** | `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.105(d)–(e), 3.343, 3.344, 3.951, 3.957`; *Brown v. Brown* | Strict regulatory compliance by VA (Void *ab initio*) | Record of reduction scrutinized | N/A — Remedy is **reversal/reinstatement** | **Exact effective date of the unlawful reduction** | Does not apply to initial staged ratings (*Fenderson v. West*, `12 Vet. App. 119 (1999)`) unless 5-year stabilization attached |
| **4. Defective Notice / Wrong Address** | `38 U.S.C. § 5104(a)`; `§ 3.655`; *Ashley* / *Mindenhall* / *Romero* | "Clear evidence" bursts regularity -> VA must prove receipt; merits = Equipoise | **YES** (claim never became final!) | **YES** | **Original open claim date** | Bare assertion of non-receipt fails (*Mindenhall*, `7 Vet. App. 271 (1994)`; *cf.* nonprecedential *Marion v. Collins*, `No. 24-6964` (`mem. dec.` — `CAVC Rule 30(a)`)); requires C-file address/systemic proof |
| **4B. Legacy 1-Yr Post-Decision VAMC Notes** | `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(b)`; *Beraud* (2014) / *Lang v. Wilkie* (2020) | Equipoise (`§ 5107(b)`) once non-finality proven | **YES** (Legacy decision never became final!) | **YES** | **Original Legacy claim date** | Applies to post-`7/21/1992`, pre-`2/19/2019` Legacy RO decisions where VA never made a N&M determination on VAMC records generated within 1 year |
| **5. AMA Silent Omission** | `38 U.S.C. § 5104(b)`; *Hamill v. Collins*, `166 F.4th 1030 (2026)` | Equipoise (`§ 5107(b)`) | **YES** | **YES** | **Original AMA claim / ITF date** (`>= 2/19/2019`) | Applies **only** to AMA decisions (`>= 2/19/2019`), NOT Legacy decisions |
| **6. Pre-2015 Pending Informal / `§ 3.157(b)` / TDIU** | Historical `§§ 3.155, 3.157(b)` (`79 FR 57660`); *Roberson* / *Rice* | Equipoise (`§ 5107(b)`) | **YES** | **YES** | **Date of pre-`3/24/2015` informal claim or VAMC report** | Extinguished if a later pre-AMA decision explicitly or implicitly (*Deshotel* / *Hampton* / *Steele*) decided the issue; dead after `3/24/2015` |
| **7. RO CUE** | `38 U.S.C. § 5109A`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)` | **Undebatable** error + **manifestly changed outcome** | **NO** — Strictly closed record at time (+ *Bell* post-`7/21/1992`) | **NO** (*Cook v. Principi*) | **Effective date of the erroneous RO decision** | Barred if a later BVA decision **subsumed** the RO decision OR if premised on a post-decision judicial reinterpretation (*George v. McDonough*; `§ 20.1403(e)`)! |
| **8. Board (BVA) CUE** | `38 U.S.C. § 7111`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 20.1400–20.1411` | **Undebatable** error + **manifestly changed outcome** | **NO** — Strictly closed record before BVA | **NO** | **Effective date of the erroneous BVA decision** | **ONE-SHOT FINALITY (`§ 20.1409(c)`)** + *George* `§ 20.1403(e)` bar: Clear [`board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md`](board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md) first! |
| **9. CRSC Arrears Recalculation** | `10 U.S.C. § 1413a`; *Soto v. United States*, `605 U.S. 360 (2025)` | Combat-related nexus + retiree status | **YES** (submit to military branch CRSC board) | Branch CRSC process | **Uncapped back to CRSC entitlement / retirement** (no 6-yr Barring Act limit!) | Requires military retiree status (20-yr or Ch. 61 medical retirement) + combat-related qualifier |
