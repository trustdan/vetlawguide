## Gate 1: Board Finality, `120-Day` NOA Clock, *Rosler* vs. `§ 5110(a)(2)(D)–(E)` Parallel Track & Equitable Tolling Audit

- [ ] **1.1 Jurisdictional Finality & Adverse-Ruling Check (`38 U.S.C. §§ 7252(a), 7266(a)`):**
  - Confirm the BVA issued a **final denial** (or partial grant denying an earlier effective date, higher staged rating, `TDIU`, or `SMC`). **Trap:** Purely remanded BVA issues are non-final and non-appealable to the CAVC (*Breeden v. Principi*, `17 Vet. App. 475 (2004)`)—appeal *only* the finally denied/under-granted issues!
- [ ] **1.2 Exact `120-Day` Calendar Math (`38 U.S.C. § 7266(a)`; `CAVC Rules 4 & 26`):**
  - Count **`120 calendar days`** from the stamped mailing date on the BVA decision (`38 U.S.C. § 7266(a)`). Verify whether a defective BVA mailing address (or failure to mail a copy to accredited POA) prevented the `120-day` clock from starting under *Ashley v. Derwinski*, `2 Vet. App. 307 (1992)`.
- [ ] **1.3 *Rosler* Chairman Reconsideration Tolling vs. AMA Supplemental Parallel Track:**
  - ***Rosler* Tolling (*Rosler v. Derwinski*, `1 Vet. App. 241, 249 (1991)`):** Filing a motion for BVA Chairman Reconsideration (`38 U.S.C. § 7103`; `38 C.F.R. § 20.1001`) **within `120 days`** of the BVA decision abates finality and restarts a fresh `120-day` CAVC NOA clock once the Chairman denies reconsideration *(filing reconsideration on Day `121+` does **NOT** revive CAVC jurisdiction!)*.
  - **AMA Post-BVA (`§ 5110(a)(2)(D)`) & Post-CAVC (`§ 5110(a)(2)(E)`) Supplemental Track (`38 C.F.R. § 3.2500(c)–(d)`):** Remember `38 C.F.R. § 3.2500(d)` bars pursuing two AMA lanes *simultaneously* on the exact same issue—file the CAVC `NOA` within `120 days` first (preserving `EAJA` + judicial error review!), knowing that even if CAVC later *affirms* an **AMA** BVA decision, **`38 U.S.C. § 5110(a)(2)(E)`** still grants **`1 year` after the CAVC decision** to file a `VA Form 20-0995` Supplemental Claim with new & relevant evidence while keeping the original effective date! *(Legacy Trap: `§ 5110(a)(2)(E)` does **NOT** save a Legacy CAVC affirmance!)*
- [ ] **1.4 Equitable Tolling Audit When Day `120` Passed (*Henderson v. Shinseki*, `562 U.S. 428 (2011)`):**
  - Because `§ 7266(a)` is non-jurisdictional (*Henderson*), immediately assemble sworn declarations + corroborating records if the claimant satisfied **both** `(a)` **extraordinary circumstances** beyond control *and* `(b)` **reasonable diligence**:
    1. **Mental or Physical Incapacity (***Barrett v. Principi*, `363 F.3d 1316, 1321 (Fed. Cir. 2004)`**; *Arbas v. Nicholson*, `403 F.3d 1379 (Fed. Cir. 2005)`):** Medical/lay proof that psychiatric or physical illness rendered the veteran incapable of rational thought/deliberate decision-making or physically unable to handle affairs during the window.
    2. **Stop-Clock vs. Full-Period Diligence (***Checo v. Shinseki*, `748 F.3d 1373, 1378–80 (Fed. Cir. 2014)`**):** Under *Checo*'s **"stop-clock"** rule (homelessness, hospitalization, disaster), the veteran need only prove causation and diligence **during the extraordinary impediment period** (not the remaining unimpeded days)!
    3. **Case-Specific Postal / Filing Obstacles (***James v. Wilkie*, `917 F.3d 1368, 1373–75 (Fed. Cir. 2019)`**):** Rejects rigid categorical "garden-variety neglect" bars (e.g., *pro se* veteran placing timely NOA in residential mailbox with flag raised on Day 120 where USPS failed to pick up mail).

## Gate 2: Favorable-Finding Lock (*Medrano* / `§ 5104A`) & Issue-Preservation Gate (*Francway*; *Maggitt*)

- [ ] **2.1 Lock Every Binding Favorable Finding (*Medrano v. Nicholson*, `21 Vet. App. 165, 170 (2007)`; `38 U.S.C. § 5104A`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.104(c)`):**
  - Catalog every explicit or implicit favorable factual finding in the RO/HLR/BVA decisions (e.g., conceded stressor/exposure, in-service injury, current diagnosis, lay credibility). Under *Medrano*, **the CAVC will not—and the Board on remand cannot—disturb favorable findings** (and under AMA `§ 5104A` / `§ 3.104(c)`, AOJ/BVA favorable findings bind all subsequent VA adjudicators unless rebutted by **clear and unmistakable evidence**!).
- [ ] **2.2 C&P Examiner Competency Preservation Check (*Francway v. Wilkie*, `940 F.3d 1304, 1307–09 (Fed. Cir. 2019) (en banc)`):**
  - Did prior counsel or the claimant challenge the VA C&P examiner's professional qualifications/competence **before the Board**? Under *Francway*, the presumption of competency attaches **unless challenged at the agency/BVA level** (absent independent record evidence of irregularity)—if unpreserved below, pivot your CAVC attack to **inadequate medical rationale / inaccurate factual premise** (*Nieves-Rodriguez* / *Barr* / *Dalton*), which requires **no** competency challenge!
- [ ] **2.3 Issue-Exhaustion & *Robinson* `EAJA` Preservation Gate (*Maggitt v. West*, `202 F.3d 1370, 1377–79 (Fed. Cir. 2000)`):**
  - Distinguish `(a)` arguments demonstrating **Board error on the record** (`§ 7104(d)(1)` failure to address reasonably raised record theories under *Robinson v. Peake*, `21 Vet. App. 526 (2008)`, *aff'd sub nom. Robinson v. Shinseki*, `557 F.3d 1355 (Fed. Cir. 2009)`) from `(b)` brand-new discretionary arguments never raised below (*Maggitt*; *Scott v. McDonald*, `789 F.3d 1375 (Fed. Cir. 2015)`).
  - **EAJA Trap (*Robinson v. O'Rourke*, `891 F.3d 976 (Fed. Cir. 2018)`):** Remember a *Maggitt* remand solely for the Board to address a **brand-new argument raised for the first time at CAVC** (where the Board committed zero error below) yields **`$0` EAJA fees**!

## Gate 3: Outright Reversal (*Deloach* / *Horn* / *Gutierrez*) vs. `Rule 33` `JMPR` Drafting Gate

- [ ] **3.1 Screen First for Outright Reversal (`38 U.S.C. § 7261(a)(4)`; *Bufkin v. Collins*, `604 U.S. 369 (2025)`):**
  - Never reflexively sign a remand when the undisputed record mandates **outright CAVC reversal**! Screen for:
    1. **Unrebutted Favorable Medical Evidence (***Deloach v. Shinseki*, `704 F.3d 1370, 1380–81 (Fed. Cir. 2013)`**):** All competent medical opinions of record favor the veteran (or the sole negative VA C&P exam is legally worthless/non-probative while a positive, adequate nexus/TDIU opinion stands uncontradicted), leaving the Board's denial *"clearly erroneous"*!
    2. **Failed Government Burden on Presumption of Soundness (`§ 1111`) or Rating Reduction (`§§ 3.343–3.344`) (***Horn v. Shinseki*, `25 Vet. App. 231, 243–44 (2012)`**; *Brown v. Brown*, `5 Vet. App. 413 (1993)`):** When VA fails its affirmative clear-and-unmistakable rebuttal burden under `§ 1111` (*Horn*) or reduces a protected rating unlawfully, **outright reversal and restoration—NOT remand to give VA a second bite to manufacture negative medical opinions—is mandatory**!
    3. **Uncontroverted Schedular / `SMC` / `TDIU` Criteria Met (***Gutierrez v. Principi*, `19 Vet. App. 1, 9–10 (2004)`**):** Undisputed Board/clinical findings already satisfy `100%` of the higher Diagnostic Code or `SMC` criteria as a matter of law.
- [ ] **3.2 Mandatory 4-Clause `CAVC Rule 33` `JMPR` Drafting Checklist:**
  - Before signing any Joint Motion for Remand (`JMR` / `JMPR`), verify all **four protective clauses** appear verbatim or in substance in the joint motion:
    - [ ] **Clause A — Explicit Administrative-Error Predicate (*Sumner v. Principi*, `15 Vet. App. 256 (2001) (en banc)`; *Kelly*; *Zuberi*):** State explicitly that *"Vacatur and remand are warranted because the Board erred under `38 U.S.C. § 7104(d)(1)` [and/or `§ 5103A`] by failing to provide adequate reasons or bases..."*—**locking in `EAJA` `"prevailing party"` status!**
    - [ ] **Clause B — *Medrano* Favorable-Finding Lock (*Medrano*, `21 Vet. App. at 170`; `38 U.S.C. § 5104A`):** Explicitly list and preserve every favorable Board/AOJ finding (`"The parties agree that the Board's favorable findings that [X, Y, Z] shall not be disturbed on remand"`).
    - [ ] **Clause C — Anti-*Carter* Non-Waiver & Open-Submission Clause (*Carter v. Shinseki*, `26 Vet. App. 534 (2014)`, *vacated on other grounds sub nom. Carter v. McDonald*, `794 F.3d 1342 (Fed. Cir. 2015)`):** State explicitly that *"The enumeration of specific errors herein shall not limit the scope of the Board's review on remand, Appellant waives no additional arguments, theories, or issues raised by the record, and Appellant retains the right to submit additional argument and evidence on remand"* (`Quarles v. Derwinski`, `3 Vet. App. 129 (1992)`; `Kutscherousky v. West`, `12 Vet. App. 369 (1999)` / `38 C.F.R. § 20.802`).
    - [ ] **Clause D — *Stegall* Compliance Directive (*Stegall v. West*, `11 Vet. App. 268, 271 (1998)`; `38 U.S.C. § 7112`):** Require expedited remand adjudication (`38 U.S.C. § 7112`) and explicit compliance with the JMPR's terms (creating automatic *Stegall* error if the Board ignores any directive on remand!).

## Gate 4: `EAJA` (`28 U.S.C. § 2412(d)`) `30-Day` Post-Mandate Filing, Net-Worth, `CPI-U` & *Richlin* Billing Gate

- [ ] **4.1 Calendar the Exact `30-Day` EAJA Deadline Immediately Upon Disposition (`28 U.S.C. § 2412(d)(1)(B)`; `CAVC Rules 36, 39, 41`):**
  - **Consent `JMPR` / Stipulated Immediate Mandate (`CAVC Rule 41(b)`):** **ZERO `60-day` wait!** File the complete EAJA application **within `30 calendar days` of the `JMPR` order date**!
  - **Contested Single-Judge (`nonprecedential, CAVC Rule 30(a)`) or Panel Judgment (`CAVC Rule 36`):** Calendar **`60 days` (`§ 7292(a)` appeal period -> `Rule 41(a)` mandate) + `30 days` (`Rule 39(a)`) = `90 days` from judgment** *(knowing premature post-judgment EAJA filings are held and deemed timely at finality, and timely filings may be amended under **Scarborough v. Principi**, `541 U.S. 401 (2004)`).*
- [ ] **4.2 Mandatory 4-Prong Pleading & *Ratliff* Assignment Packet:**
  - [ ] Allege `(1)` **Prevailing Party** status, `(2)` Government position **not substantially justified** at both BVA and CAVC stages (*Patrick v. Shinseki*, `668 F.3d 1325`), `(3)` **Net Worth `<= $2,000,000`** declaration (or cite docketed CAVC `IFP` grant), and `(4)` **Itemized `0.1-hr` billing statement** + **executed Client `EAJA` Fee Assignment** (*Astrue v. Ratliff*, `560 U.S. 586 (2010)`).
- [ ] **4.3 *Mannino* `CPI-U` Midpoint Math, *Richlin* Paralegal Rates & *Jean* Fees-on-Fees:**
  - [ ] Compute attorney rate = `$125.00 × (Midpoint Regional/National CPI-U ÷ March 1996 Base CPI-U)` under ***Mannino v. West*, `12 Vet. App. 242 (1999)`** (verify midpoint date = exact halfway date between first and last billed appellate entries!).
  - [ ] Bill supervised paralegal and law-clerk hours at **prevailing market rates** under ***Richlin Security Service Co. v. Chertoff*, `553 U.S. 571 (2008)`** (never payroll cost!), include `1.5–3.0 hrs` for preparing the EAJA application itself (***Jean*, `496 U.S. 154`**), and claim `$50` CAVC filing fee expense (if not waived via `IFP`).

## Gate 5: Federal Circuit Jurisdictional Gate (`38 U.S.C. § 7292`) vs. `Rule 23` Class & `TRAC` Mandamus Triage

- [ ] **5.1 Strict `38 U.S.C. § 7292(a) & (d)(2)` Pure-Law Framing Audit (`60-Day` NOA Clock):**
  - Unless raising a **bona fide constitutional claim**, **`38 U.S.C. § 7292(d)(2)` strips the Federal Circuit of jurisdiction** to review any challenge to a **factual determination** OR **application of law to the facts of a particular case** (including clear-error `§ 5107(b)` equipoise weighing after *Bufkin v. Collins*, `604 U.S. 369 (2025)`)!
  - **Pre-Filing Test:** Can the Question Presented be stated purely as *"Whether the CAVC misinterpreted the legal standard of `38 U.S.C. § [X]` / `38 C.F.R. § [Y]` by requiring [legal element Z]..."* without disputing how the Board weighed evidence?
  - **CRITICAL AMA EFFECTIVE-DATE SAFETY NET (*MVA v. Sec'y*, `7 F.4th 1110, 1131–35 (Fed. Cir. 2021)`):** Because `38 U.S.C. § 5110(a)(2)(E)` preserves continuous-pursuit effective dates for `1 year` after a **CAVC** decision—and does **NOT** reset a new `1-year` Supplemental Claim window after an unsuccessful **Federal Circuit** appeal—calendar a hard **`330-day` post-CAVC-decision stop-date** to evaluate filing a protective AMA `VA Form 20-0995` Supplemental Claim if the Federal Circuit appeal remains pending or risky!
- [ ] **5.2 `CAVC Rule 23` Class Action & All Writs Act (`28 U.S.C. § 1651(a)`) Mandamus Triage:**
  - **Unreasonable Agency Delay (`TRAC` Six-Factor Test — *Martin v. O'Rourke*, `891 F.3d 1338, 1344–49 (Fed. McDonough*, `93 F.4th 1361 (Fed. Cir. 2024)`** (opening BVA/CAVC review to `PCAFC` caregiver appeals)!
