# 48-Hour Survivor Post-Mortem Triage & Pre-Filing Gate Checklist

> [!CAUTION]
> **ZERO CLIENT PII RULE & IMMEDIATE DEADLINE ALERT:** Never enter veteran names, SSNs, VA file numbers, or unredacted death certificates into repository files—execute case-specific checklists offline! Upon notice of a veteran's death, **stop treating the file as a living-veteran Chapter 11 claim immediately**: the veteran's POA extinguishes at death (`38 C.F.R. § 14.631(g)`), appellate jurisdiction shifts overnight, and **missing the strict `365-day` statutory clock under `38 U.S.C. §§ 5121(c), 5121A` permanently destroys non-*Nehmer* accrued and substitution back pay!**

*(Cross-references: Verbatim snapshots [`38-cfr-3.22-dic-hypothetical-evolution.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.22-dic-hypothetical-evolution.md) and [`38-cfr-3.1000-3.1010-accrued-substitution.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.1000-3.1010-accrued-substitution.md); [Playbook 08 (DIC & `§ 1318`)](../playbooks/08-survivor-dic-direct-and-1318-hypothetical.md); [Playbook 09 (Accrued, Substitution & *Nehmer*)](../playbooks/09-accrued-benefits-substitution-nehmer-estates.md); [Playbook 06 (*Nehmer* `§ 3.816`)](../playbooks/06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md); [Marriage & Dependency Diff (`§§ 3.50–3.57, 3.356`)](../cfr-diffs/part-3/survivor-marriage-dependency-evolution.md); [Forms Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md); [Title 38 Statutory Crosswalk](../docs/title-38-statutory-crosswalk.md); and [Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).*

## Preconditions (Intake Packet & VBMS Snapshot — Hours 0–24)

- [ ] **Exact Date & Cause of Death Verified:** Obtain the long-form **Death Certificate** (checking *both* Part I Immediate/Underlying Causes and Part II "Other Significant Conditions Contributing to Death") plus terminal hospitalization / autopsy / hospice summaries.
- [ ] **Freeze Complete VBMS eFolder & VACOLS/Caseflow Snapshot:** Download the latest **Rating Code Sheet**, all pending `526EZ` / `0995` / `0996` / `10182` / Legacy `NOD`–`Form 9` filings, active `VA Form 21-0966` Intents to File, and any pending CAVC / Federal Circuit docket entries.

---

## Gate 1: Immediate Appellate & Forum Posture Check (Hours 0–48)

Identify **where every live issue stood on the exact date of death** and execute the matching forum preservation move:

- [ ] **Scenario 1A — Appeal Pending at CAVC or Federal Circuit:**
  - File a **`CAVC Rule 43(a)`** (or `Fed. R. App. P. 43(a)`) **Motion to Substitute** an eligible `§ 5121(a)` accrued-benefits claimant (*Breedlove v. Shinseki*, `24 Vet. App. 7 (2010)`).
  - If the veteran died **after** briefing/submission was complete at the CAVC or Federal Circuit, move simultaneously for ***nunc pro tunc* relief** back to the date of death (*Padgett v. Nicholson*, `473 F.3d 1364 (Fed. Cir. 2007) (en banc)`).
  - **CRITICAL *MERRITT* TRAP (*Merritt v. Wilkie*, `965 F.3d 1357 (Fed. Cir. 2020)`):** Judicial substitution at CAVC/Fed. Cir. **DOES NOT** satisfy VA's 1-year administrative filing deadline if the court vacates/remands rather than reversing outright! **Always file `VA Form 21P-0847` AND `VA Form 21P-534EZ` (or `21P-535`/`21P-601`) directly with the VA AOJ within `1 year` of death** while the court appeal is pending!
- [ ] **Scenario 1B — Veteran Died During the `120-Day` BVA Notice of Appeal (`NOA`) Window:**
  - File a timely **protective CAVC Notice of Appeal** + `CAVC Rule 43(a)` motion to substitute within `120 days` of the BVA decision mailing date (*Pekular v. Mansfield*, `21 Vet. App. 495 (2007)`; *Breedlove*, `24 Vet. App. at 20–21`), **AND** file `VA Form 21P-0847` + `VA Form 21P-534EZ` at the VA AOJ within `1 year` of death!
- [ ] **Scenario 1C — Veteran Died *Before* the BVA Issued Its Decision (Post-Death BVA Decision):**
  - Under *Smith v. Brown*, `10 Vet. App. 330 (1997)` and *Landicho v. Brown*, `7 Vet. App. 42 (1994)`, the Board lost jurisdiction over the veteran at the moment of death—making the post-death BVA decision **ultra vires and a legal nullity**!
  - Move the Board (via Motion to Vacate) or appeal to CAVC within `120 days` to **vacate the void BVA decision** so it carries zero adverse preclusive effect, and file **`VA Form 21P-0847`** within `1 year` of death so the survivor substitutes directly into the still-pending BVA appeal!
- [ ] **Scenario 1D — Claim/Review Pending at RO, HLR, Supplemental Claim, or BVA Docket (`38 U.S.C. § 5121A`):**
  - File **`VA Form 21P-0847`** (*Request for Substitution of Claimant Upon Death of Claimant*) within **`1 year` of death** (`38 C.F.R. § 3.1010(b)`).
  - Confirm whether open-record substitution (keeping Duty to Assist, new nexus opinions, and `§ 1154(b)` combat presumptions under *Reeves v. Shinseki*, `682 F.3d 988 (Fed. Cir. 2012)`) beats waiving substitution for immediate closed-record `§ 5121` adjudication (*Reliford v. McDonald*, `27 Vet. App. 297 (2015)`).

## Gate 2: Strict 1-Year Statutory Bars vs. Effective-Date Relation-Back Windows

> [!WARNING]
> **NEVER CONFUSE `§ 5110(d)` WITH `§§ 5121(c) & 5121A`:** Missing `1 year` on DIC only shifts the DIC effective date forward; missing `1 year` on Accrued Benefits or Substitution **kills the entire back-pay claim forever** (unless *Nehmer* `§ 3.816(f)` applies)! Do **not** rely on an unperfected `VA Form 21-0966` Intent to File to toll `§ 5121(c)` or `§ 5121A` past 365 days—file the formal application inside 1 year of death!

| Benefit / Vehicle | Statute & CFR | Within `1 Year` of Death | Filed *After* `1 Year` from Death |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DIC (`§§ 1310, 1318`)** | `38 U.S.C. § 5110(d)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.400(c)(2)` | Effective **first day of the month in which death occurred**! | **Still eligible!** Payable from date of claim (or perfected `VA Form 21-0966` ITF / `§ 3.114` / `§ 1305` date). |
| **Substitution (`§ 5121A`)** | `38 U.S.C. § 5121A(a)(1)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.1010(b)` | Steps into veteran's pending claim/appeal back to **veteran's original effective date** (uncapped!). | **PERMANENT STATUTORY BAR — substitution forfeited!** |
| **Accrued Benefits (`§ 5121`)** | `38 U.S.C. § 5121(c)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.1000(c)` | Unpaid benefits "due and unpaid" on file at death (uncapped for deaths on/after Dec. 16, 2003 per `Pub. L. 108-183, § 104`). | **PERMANENT STATUTORY BAR — accrued claim forfeited!** |
| ***Nehmer* Class Payout (`§ 3.816(f)`)** | `38 C.F.R. § 3.816(f)` | 100% of unpaid *Nehmer* retroactive compensation paid to survivor or **estate**. | **NO 1-YEAR BAR!** *Nehmer* mandate overrides `§ 5121(c)` time and payee bars! |

- [ ] Calendar **300-day** and **350-day** hard internal firm stop-dates measured from the exact date of death.

## Gate 3: Statutory Payee Hierarchy & Estate Eligibility Audit

Verify the client qualifies in the highest surviving payee tier (see full status matrices in [`survivor-marriage-dependency-evolution.md`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/survivor-marriage-dependency-evolution.md)):

- [ ] **Tier 1 — Surviving Spouse (`38 U.S.C. §§ 101(3), 103, 5121(a)(2)(A)`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.50–3.55`):**
  - Valid marriage (`§ 103(c)` / `§ 3.1(j)`, including nationwide post-*Obergefell* recognition and Same-Sex Duration Relief under Instruction `01-22`, `87 FR 68904`), OR **`§ 3.52` Deemed Valid Marriage** (*Dedicatoria* / *Colon* ignorance at marriage + no entitled rival legal spouse).
  - **Continuous Cohabitation (`§ 3.53`):** If living apart at death, document **absence of surviving spouse's fault at the time of separation** (*Gregory v. Brown*, `5 Vet. App. 108 (1993)` — e.g., veteran's PTSD, abuse, desertion, or nursing-home care).
  - **Marriage Duration (`§ 1304` vs. `§ 1318(c)`):** Confirm `>= 1 year` marriage OR child born (`§ 3.54(d)`). *(Remember: the `15-year` post-service marriage path in `§ 1304` applies ONLY to `§ 1310` SC-death DIC, NOT `§ 1318` total-disability DIC!)*
  - **Remarriage Audit (`§ 103(d)(2)` / `§ 3.55(a)`):** Terminated remarriage restores DIC (`Pub. L. 105-178`); ongoing remarriage retains DIC if entered **at age `57+` (`Dec. 16, 2003 – Jan. 4, 2021`)** OR **at age `55+` on or after `January 5, 2021` (`Pub. L. 116-315, § 2009`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.55(a)(9)(iii)`, `87 FR 68360` eff. Nov. 15, 2022)**—audit `2021–2022` denials where raters wrongly applied pre-Nov. 2022 `§ 3.55(a)(10)` age-57 text, and remember `Ch. 35 DEA` / `Ch. 37 Home Loan` remain at age `57+`!
- [ ] **Tier 2 — Surviving Child(ren) (`38 U.S.C. §§ 101(4)(A), 5121(a)(2)(B)`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.57, 3.356`):**
  - Unmarried child under `18`, schoolchild `18–23` (*note*: schoolchild `18–23` qualifies for DIC under `§ 1314(b)`, whereas `§ 5121` accrued tracks `§ 101(4)(A)` definition!), OR **pre-age-`18` permanently incapable Helpless Child (`§ 3.356`)** (confirm no post-Oct. 31, 1990 non-void/non-annulled marriage under `§ 103(e)` / `§ 3.55(b)`!).
- [ ] **Tier 3 — Dependent Parent(s) (`38 U.S.C. §§ 1315, 5121(a)(2)(C)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.250`):**
  - Screen low-income parents for income-tested Parents' DIC (`VA Form 21P-535`) and dependency-at-death accrued benefits.
- [ ] **Tier 4 — Adult Non-Helpless Child / Deceased Veteran's Estate (`§ 3.816(f)` or `§ 5121(a)(6)` ONLY):**
  - Confirm **either** (a) **`38 C.F.R. § 3.816(f)` *Nehmer* class entitlement** (pays 100% unpaid retro to adult children or the **estate** when no Tier 1–3 *Nehmer* payee survives!) **or** (b) **`38 U.S.C. § 5121(a)(6)` (`§ 3.1000(a)(5)`)** reimbursement capped at documented **last-sickness and burial expenses** paid by the claimant (`VA Form 21P-601`).

## Gate 4: Merits Track & Prescribed Form Selection Matrix

Select **all** applicable tracks simultaneously (filing **`VA Form 21P-534EZ`** for a spouse/child automatically claims DIC, Survivors Pension, *and* `§ 5121` Accrued Benefits, while **`VA Form 21P-0847`** is additionally required to substitute under `§ 5121A`!):

- [ ] **Track A — Direct or Contributory Cause-of-Death DIC (`38 U.S.C. § 1310`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.312(a)–(c)`):**
  - Screen primary cause (`§ 3.312(b)`) AND **contributory cause (`§ 3.312(c)`)**: did an SC disability debilitate a **vital organ (`§ 3.312(c)(3)`, where `100%` vital-organ evaluation assumes debilitation)** or combine substantially/materially (`§ 3.312(c)(1)`) to hasten death even if omitted from the Death Certificate?
  - **PACT Act DIC Re-Evaluation (`38 U.S.C. § 1305`, enacted by `Pub. L. 117-168, § 204`) & New Herbicide/Burn-Pit Presumptions (`Pub. L. 117-168, §§ 403–406`):** If DIC was *previously denied* and the cause of death is now presumptive (e.g., hypertension or MGUS under PACT `§ 404`, expanded herbicide locations under `§ 403`, or burn-pit cancers/COPD under `§ 406`), file a **`§ 1305` re-evaluation election** on `21P-534EZ` / `20-0995`!
- [ ] **Track B — `§ 1318` Actual (`§ 3.22(a)(2)`) or Hypothetical (`§ 3.22(b)(1)–(2)`) `10-Year` Total-Disability DIC:**
  - Did the veteran hold (or **hypothetically qualify for**) continuous `100%` schedular or `TDIU` evaluations for **`10+` years** before death (`5+` years from discharge, or `1+` year if a Former POW under `§ 3.22(a)(2)(i)–(iii)`)?
  - **Hypothetical `§ 3.22(b)(1)–(2)` Audit:** If the veteran was under-rated during life, screen historical Rating Code Sheets for **RO/BVA CUE (`§ 3.22(b)(1)` via `§ 3.105(a)` / `§ 7111`)** or **newly associated service department records (`§ 3.22(b)(2)` via `§ 3.156(c)`)** on a lifetime claim that push combined/TDIU ratings to `100%` across the 10-year window!
  - ***Haines* vs. `§ 3.22(b)(1)` Rule (*Haines v. West*, `154 F.3d 1298 (Fed. Cir. 1998)`):** Remember that a survivor-initiated CUE motion **cannot** recover `§ 5121` accrued back pay unless the veteran's own CUE motion was pending at death, **but CAN** establish prospective/month-of-death `§ 1318` DIC under `§ 3.22(b)(1)`! *(Run the [BVA CUE Pre-Filing Gate](board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md) and exclude George v. McDonough `§ 20.1403(e)` change-in-interpretation theories before filing any Subpart O motion).*
- [ ] **Track C — `§ 5121A` Substitution (`VA Form 21P-0847`) + `§ 5121` Accrued (`21P-534EZ` / `21P-535` / `21P-601`):**
  - File `VA Form 21P-0847` alongside `21P-534EZ` (spouse/child), `21P-535` (dependent parent), or `21P-601` (reimbursement / non-spouse accrued).
- [ ] **Track D — `38 C.F.R. § 3.816(f)` *Nehmer* Footnote 1 & Estate Audit:**
  - If the veteran served in the Republic of Vietnam or qualifying Blue Water Navy waters, audit the entire C-file back to **September 25, 1985** for any prior denied claim or contemporaneous medical diagnosis of a covered `§ 3.816(b)(2)` herbicide condition (*excluding* hypertension, MGUS, and chloracne per [Playbook 06](../playbooks/06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md)).

## Gate 5: Postconditions — Rate Add-Ons, Concurrent-Receipt Offsets & Representation Hygiene

Verify every dollar of survivor entitlement and lock in compliant representation **before** submitting the filing packet:

- [ ] **`38 U.S.C. § 1311` DIC Rate Add-Ons Screened:**
  - **`§ 1311(a)(2)` ("8-Year Kicker"):** Extra monthly allowance if the veteran was `100%`/`TDIU` continuously for `8+` years immediately preceding death *and* married to the surviving spouse for those same `8+` years (applies to hypothetical `§ 1318` grants too!).
  - **`§ 1311(c)` Aid & Attendance / `§ 1311(d)` Housebound:** Submit **`VA Form 21-2680`** (or nursing-home proof) if the surviving spouse or dependent parent has functional/ADL limitations!
  - **`§ 1311(b)` Minor-Child Add-On & `§ 1311(f)` 2-Year Transitional Benefit:** Verify VA pays *both* the per-child increment (`§ 1311(b)`) and the automatic 2-year Transitional Benefit (`§ 1311(f)`) whenever `>= 1` child under age `18` is on the surviving spouse's award!
- [ ] **SBP–DIC Full Concurrent Receipt Verified (`Pub. L. 116-92, § 622`):**
  - Confirm DFAS pays **100% of military Survivor Benefit Plan (`SBP`)** concurrently with **100% of VA DIC** for all periods on or after **January 1, 2023** (zero "Widow's Tax" offset!).
- [ ] **Ancillary Benefits & Helpless-Child `Ch. 35` DEA Safeguard:**
  - Trigger **`CHAMPVA` (`38 U.S.C. § 1781`)**, **`Ch. 37` VA Home Loan COE**, **`Ch. 35` DEA**, and **Burial / Plot / Service-Connected Death Burial Allowance (`38 U.S.C. §§ 2302–2308`; `VA Form 21P-530EZ`)**.
  - **Helpless Child `Ch. 35` Gate (`38 U.S.C. § 3562`; `38 C.F.R. § 21.3023(a)–(c)`):** Ensure any disabled 18-year-old child files for and secures **`§ 3.356` helpless-child status BEFORE** negotiating a first `Ch. 35` DEA check/EFT (protecting concurrent receipt under `§ 21.3023(b)` and avoiding an irrevocable `§ 3562` schoolchild forfeiture if `§ 3.356` were denied!).
- [ ] **Fresh Survivor POA & `§ 5904` Fee Agreement Executed (`38 C.F.R. §§ 14.631(g), 14.636`):**
  - Execute a **brand-new `VA Form 21-22a`** signed by the **surviving claimant** (or court-appointed estate executor for `§ 3.816(f)` *Nehmer* estate claims) AND a **new `38 U.S.C. § 5904` fee agreement** in the survivor's own name—never rely on the deceased veteran's extinguished POA or fee contract!
