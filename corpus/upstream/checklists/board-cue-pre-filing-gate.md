# Board (BVA) CUE Motion Pre-Filing Gate Checklist (`38 U.S.C. § 7111`; `38 C.F.R. Part 20, Subpart O`)

> [!CAUTION]
> **THE `38 C.F.R. § 20.1409(c)` SINGLE-SHOT FINALITY TRAP (*Hillyard v. Shinseki*, 24 Vet. App. 343 (2011)):**
> Unlike Regional Office CUE motions under `38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)` (where distinct new CUE theories can be brought later), **`38 C.F.R. § 20.1409(c)` gives a veteran ONLY ONE merits Board CUE motion per Board decision issue—forever.** If you file an incomplete or poorly researched BVA CUE motion and the Board denies it on the merits, **every stronger CUE theory you overlooked in that Board decision is permanently extinguished by res judicata!**
>
> **NEVER file a Subpart O Board CUE motion until every box below is verified.**

---

## Gate 1: Exhaust Non-CUE Retroactive Alternatives First!
Before touching `38 U.S.C. § 7111`, verify whether you can achieve the **exact same retroactive effective date** on an **open record under equipoise (`§ 5107(b)`)** without burning your one BVA CUE bullet:
- [ ] **Are there newly associated or retrievable official Service Department Records (`38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)`)?** Under *George v. Shulkin* and `§ 20.1411(b)`, a `§ 3.156(c)` reconsideration request goes to the **AOJ/RO**, overrides Board finality, allows new medical opinions, and **does not** consume `§ 20.1409(c)`!
- [ ] **Is the veteran a *Nehmer* class member (`38 C.F.R. § 3.816`)?** *Nehmer* readjudication bypasses prior final Board denials of covered herbicide conditions automatically.
- [ ] **Was the Board decision an unlawful rating reduction (`§§ 3.343, 3.344, 3.951, 3.957`)?** Check *Brown v. Brown* / *Kitchens* void *ab initio* challenge rules alongside CUE.
- [ ] **Was the Board decision never validly mailed to the veteran or representative (`38 U.S.C. § 7104(e)`)?** Rebutting the Presumption of Regularity abates finality so the CAVC appeal window never started!

---

## Gate 2: Jurisdictional Eligibility (`38 C.F.R. §§ 20.1400, 20.1409(c)`)
- [ ] **No Prior Merits Subpart O Decision (`§ 20.1409(c)`):** Search the entire VBMS eFolder / C-file to confirm neither the veteran (pro se!) nor a prior VSO/attorney previously had a Subpart O CUE motion **decided on the merits** as to this specific issue in this Board decision. *(Note: A prior CUE motion **dismissed without prejudice** for lack of pleading specificity under `§ 20.1404(b)` does NOT trigger the `§ 20.1409(c)` bar!)*
- [ ] **Not Decided by a Court (`§ 20.1400(b)(1)`):** Confirm the specific Board issue was **not** appealed to and decided on the merits by the CAVC or Federal Circuit (including via Joint Motion for Partial Summary Affirmance, though a **Joint Motion for Remand (`JMR`)** that vacated the Board decision means that vacated decision is no longer a final decision—check the *post-remand* decision instead!).
- [ ] **Correct Target Decision Identified (Subsumption Check):** Confirm whether a *later* Board decision reopened and readjudicated the exact effective-date/rating period or whether the target Board decision remains the final operative adjudication for that period.

---

## Gate 3: Exclude the Five Categorical Non-CUE Traps (`38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(d)–(e)`)
Confirm your motion does **NOT** rely on any of the five regulatory CUE exclusions:
- [ ] **NOT a Delayed/Changed Medical Diagnosis (`§ 20.1403(d)(1)`):** A new 2026 nexus opinion or corrected diagnosis cannot prove CUE in a 1998 Board decision.
- [ ] **NOT a Duty-to-Assist Failure (`§ 20.1403(d)(2)`; *Cook v. Principi*):** Do not argue the Board failed to obtain records or relied on an inadequate C&P exam.
- [ ] **NOT a Weighing-of-Evidence Dispute (`§ 20.1403(d)(3)`):** If the historical record contained *conflicting* competent evidence on the disputed element, reasonable minds could differ—CUE fails.
- [ ] **NOT Inadequate "Reasons or Bases" (`38 U.S.C. § 7104(d)(1)`; *Fugo*):** Failure to explain why evidence was rejected is a direct-appeal error, **never** CUE.
- [ ] **NOT a Subsequent Change in Interpretation of Law (`§ 20.1403(e)`; *George v. McDonough*, 596 U.S. 740 (2022)):** Confirm the statutory/regulatory rule you invoke was settled and binding **on the date of the Board decision** (not created years later by a judicial reinterpretation or invalidated regulation).

---

## Gate 4: Temporal Record & Law-of-the-Era Audit
- [ ] **Verbatim Historical `38 C.F.R.` Pulled:** Attach or quote verbatim the **exact annual edition of `38 C.F.R.`** (checking Federal Register *effective* date vs. *publication* date in [`authorities/`](../authorities)) in force on the day the Board Chair/VLJ signed the decision.
- [ ] **Closed-Record / *Bell* Check (`July 21, 1992`):**
  - If the Board decision was issued **before July 21, 1992**, confirm every relied-upon document was **physically inside the C-file / before the Board** when decided (`VAOPGCPREC 12-95`; *Damrel*).
  - If the Board decision was issued **on or after July 21, 1992**, confirm any unassociated VA medical record preexisted the Board decision and bore a reasonably apparent relationship to the claim (*Bell v. Derwinski*).
- [ ] **Manifestly Changed Payment Math (`§ 20.1403(a),(c)`):** Compute the `38 C.F.R. § 4.25` combined rating table (and `§ 4.26` bilateral factor / `SMC`) to confirm that winning this issue **actually increases the monthly dollar payment** (or unlocks SMC/TDIU/ancillary benefits) during the historical period!

---

## Gate 5: Strict Pleading Specificity (`38 C.F.R. § 20.1404(b)`; *Andrews v. Nicholson*)
Because pro-claimant sympathetic reading does **not** excuse omitting distinct CUE theories when represented by counsel (*Andrews v. Nicholson*, 421 F.3d 1278 (Fed. Cir. 2005)), confirm the motion explicitly sets forth **every viable CUE theory in one comprehensive filing**:
- [ ] Claimant's full name, file number, and **exact date of the Board decision** (`§ 20.1404(a)`).
- [ ] Exact issue(s) in the Board decision challenged (`§ 20.1404(b)`).
- [ ] Clear, specific identification of **(a) the exact factual or legal error**, **(b) why the error was undebatable under extant law/record**, and **(c) persuasive reasons why the outcome would have manifestly changed** (`§ 20.1404(b)`).
- [ ] Claimant's or accredited representative's signature (`§ 20.1404(c)`), filed directly with the **Board of Veterans' Appeals** (`§ 20.1404(d)`).
