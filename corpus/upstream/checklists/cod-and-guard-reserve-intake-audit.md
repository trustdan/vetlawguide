# Character of Discharge (`OTH` / `BCD` / `Uncharacterized`) & National Guard / Reserve Active-Service Intake Audit Checklist

> [!CAUTION]
> **ZERO CLIENT PII RULE:** Never type veteran names, SSNs, VA file numbers, service numbers, or unredacted `DD-214` / `NGB-22` blocks into repository files—execute all case-specific intake audits offline! General practitioners and VSOs routinely turn away disabled veterans with "bad paper" or `NGB-22`-only National Guard records; running the five sequential gates below separates dead statutory bars from **high-value `§ 3.13`, `§ 3.12(e)`, `§ 3.354(b)`, *Hamill*, and `ADT`/`LOD` `§ 3.156(c)` fee cases** in under 30 minutes.

*(Cross-references: Verbatim snapshot [`38-cfr-3.12-3.13-3.354-cod-insanity.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.12-3.13-3.354-cod-insanity.md); [`Part 3 COD Evolution Diff (89 FR 32361)`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/character-of-discharge-3.12-evolution.md); [Playbook 10 (COD, Insanity, `§ 3.13` & Upgrades)](../playbooks/10-character-of-discharge-insanity-upgrades.md); [Playbook 11 (Guard/Reserve ADT/IADT & LOD)](../playbooks/11-guard-reserve-adt-iadt-and-line-of-duty.md); [Playbook 03 (`§ 3.156(c)` Service Records)](../playbooks/03-service-department-records-38-cfr-3-156c.md); [Procedural Vehicles for Piercing Finality](../docs/procedural-vehicles-piercing-finality.md); [Master `VAOPGCPREC` Digest](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md); [Three-Era Statutory Crosswalk](../docs/title-38-statutory-crosswalk.md); [Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md); and [Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).*

## Preconditions (Mandatory Intake Packet — Never Rely on `Member-1` Short Copy!)

- [ ] **Obtain `DD Form 214` (`Member-4` or `Service-2` Long Copy) for *Every* Period of Service:** `Member-1` deliberately omits Blocks `23–30` (Character of Service, Separation Authority, `SPD` Code, Reentry Code, and Narrative Reason)!
- [ ] **Order Full `OMPF` + Military Justice / Separation Packet (`SF-180` / `milConnect` / Archival FOIA):** Pull enlistment/reenlistment contracts (`DD Form 4`), Administrative Separation Board (`AdSep`) notification/waiver memos, `UCMJ Art. 15` (`NJP`) logs, charge sheets (`DD Form 458`), court-martial promulgating orders, `CID`/`NCIS`/`OSI` reports, and inpatient/brig clinical jackets.
- [ ] **Pull Complete VBMS eFolder:** Inspect every prior VA Administrative Decision ("Character of Discharge Determination"), Rating Code Sheet, and post-Feb. 19, 2019 AMA decision notice.

## Gate 1: Forensic `DD-214` (`Member-4`) & `NGB-22` Block-by-Block Decode

Audit six exact blocks on **every** `DD-214` (and compare against any `NGB Form 22` *Report of Separation and Record of Service*):

- [ ] **Blocks `12a–12h` (*Record of Service*):**
  - Check `12c` (*Net Active Service This Period*), `12d` (*Total Prior Active Service*), `12e` (*Total Prior Inactive Service*), and `12f` (*Foreign and/or Sea Service* — combat/TERA trigger!).
  - **CRITICAL `NGB-22` TRAP:** An `NGB-22` proves separation from a state's Army/Air National Guard (`ANG`), **NOT** Title 38 federal active duty (`38 C.F.R. § 3.6(a)–(b)`). Always cross-check whether federal mobilizations or `IADT`/`ADT` basic training generated separate `DD-214`s!
- [ ] **Block `18` (*Remarks*):**
  - Scan immediately for **`"IMMEDIATE REENLISTMENT"`** or **`"CONTINUOUS HONORABLE ACTIVE SERVICE FROM [YYYYMMDD] TO [YYYYMMDD]"`** (triggers **Gate 2**!).
  - Audit any **`"LOST TIME"` / `"TIME LOST UNDER 10 USC 972"`** date ranges: count whether any single unauthorized absence (`AWOL`) reached **`>= 180` *continuous* days** (`38 U.S.C. § 5303(a)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.12(c)(6)`). Multiple shorter AWOLs totaling `180+` cumulative days **do NOT** trigger the `(c)(6)` statutory bar!
- [ ] **Block `24` (*Character of Service*):**
  - `Honorable` or `Under Honorable Conditions (General)` -> **Binding pass** under `§ 3.12(a)` (unless a rare `(c)` statutory bar applies or post-Oct. 8, 1977 `DRB` upgrade requires `§ 3.12(h)` verification).
  - `Under Other Than Honorable Conditions` (`OTH` / pre-1977 `Undesirable`) -> Triggers VA `§ 3.12` COD review.
  - **`Bad Conduct` (`BCD`) — CRITICAL COURT-MARTIAL SPLIT:** Determine from the promulgating order / Block `25`/`28` whether the `BCD` was adjudged by a **Special Court-Martial (`SPCM`)** (NOT a statutory bar under `§ 3.12(c)(2)`—evaluated under `(d)(2)` and eligible for `§ 3.12(e)` compelling circumstances!) vs. a **General Court-Martial (`GCM`)** (hard statutory bar under `§ 3.12(c)(2)`!).
  - `Dishonorable` (Enlisted) or `Dismissal` (Officer) -> Adjudged by `GCM` (`§ 3.12(c)(2)` statutory bar).
  - `Uncharacterized` (separations initiated `>= Oct. 1, 1982`) -> Check Block `28` under **`38 C.F.R. § 3.12(l)(1)–(3)` `[former § 3.12(k)(1)–(3)]`**:
    - **`Entry Level Separation` (`ELS`, typically `< 180` days):** **AUTOMATICALLY "other than dishonorable" under `§ 3.12(l)(1)`!** (If VA denied an `ELS` on COD grounds, file immediate CUE!).
    - `Void Enlistment` (`§ 3.12(l)(2)`) or `Dropped From the Rolls` (`§ 3.12(l)(3)`): Reviewed under `(c)` and `(d)`.
- [ ] **Blocks `25`, `26` & `28` (*Separation Authority, `SPD` Code & Narrative Reason*):**
  - **`In Lieu of Trial by Court-Martial` (`AR 635-200 Ch. 10` / `USMC SOTS` / `SPD KFS` or `JFS`):** Verify in the separation packet whether charges were actually **referred to (or threatened with referral to) a General Court-Martial** (`§ 3.12(d)(1)(i)` non-exempt bar) vs. only a **Special Court-Martial**! If the OMPF lacks proof of pending GCM charges, VA **cannot** apply `§ 3.12(d)(1)(i)`!
  - **`Pattern of Misconduct` / `Minor Disciplinary Infractions` / `Commission of a Serious Offense` / `Drug Rehabilitation Failure` (`AR 635-200 Ch. 14` / `SPD JKA, JKB, JKQ, JKN, JPC, KKC`):** Governed post-June 25, 2024 by **`§ 3.12(d)(2)(i)–(ii)`** and **fully eligible for `§ 3.12(e)` compelling circumstances**!
  - **`Personality Disorder` / `Adjustment Disorder` (`AR 635-200 Ch. 5-13 / 5-17`, `SPD JFX / JFV`):** Classic DoD administrative label frequently masking undiagnosed in-service **PTSD, MST, TBI, or bipolar/psychotic illness**—prime evidence for `§ 3.12(e)(2)(i)` or `§ 3.354(b)` insanity!

## Gate 2: Multi-Enlistment `38 C.F.R. § 3.13` Unconditional-Discharge Screen *(Check BEFORE Litigating `§ 3.12`!)*

- [ ] **Audit Every Enlistment Contract (`DD Form 4`) & `DD-214` Block `18`:** Did the veteran complete the obligated term of an earlier enlistment (`§ 3.13(c)`)—or discharge early in post-Vietnam service to reenlist when eligible for honorable separation (`§ 3.13(a)(3)`)—and **immediately reenlist without a physical break in active service**?
- [ ] **Apply `38 U.S.C. § 101(18)(B)` & `38 C.F.R. § 3.13(a)–(c)` (*Holmes v. Brown*, `10 Vet. App. 38 (1997)`; `§ 5107(b)` equipoise confirmed in *Newman v. McDonough*, `35 Vet. App. 310 (2022)`):**
  - **Rule:** Instead of suffering an adverse `"conditional discharge"` stitch under `§ 3.13(a)–(b)`, Period 1 is deemed **unconditionally discharged or released under conditions other than dishonorable (`§ 3.13(c)` / `(a)(3)`)** for all disabilities incurred or aggravated during Period 1—**even if Period 2 ended in an `OTH`, `BCD`, or `General Court-Martial` Dishonorable Discharge** (absent `38 U.S.C. § 6104` treason/subversion/mutiny/spying forfeiture)!
  - **CUE Check (`cue-ro-3.105a` / `cue-bva-7111`):** If a prior RO or Board decision barred benefits across *all* service based on a composite single `DD-214` displaying an `OTH` in Block `24` while ignoring `"CONTINUOUS HONORABLE ACTIVE SERVICE"` in Block `18`, plead **CUE** for multi-decade back pay on all Period 1 disabilities (complete the [Board CUE Pre-Filing Gate](board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md) if attacking a BVA decision).

## Gate 3: Statutory vs. Regulatory Bar Triage & Dual-Track VA + DoD Upgrade Strategy

Select the highest-yield VA pathway(s) **and** launch a parallel DoD military-board track where warranted (see full clause-by-clause table in [`character-of-discharge-3.12-evolution.md`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/character-of-discharge-3.12-evolution.md)):

- [ ] **Track 3A — Objective Defeat of `§ 3.12(d)(2)(ii)` ("Not Persistent" Defense):**
  - Pull the *Manual for Courts-Martial* (`MCM`) maximum punishment table for every documented misconduct incident:
    - Is the offense **"minor misconduct"** (max MCM punishment does **not** include dishonorable discharge or confinement `> 1 year`)?
    - **Single Offense:** Never "persistent" under `(d)(2)(ii)`!
    - **Spacing Check:** Were two minor offenses separated by **`> 2 years`**, a minor and serious offense separated by **`> 2 years`**, or two serious offenses separated by **`> 5 years`**? If outside those windows, `(d)(2)(ii)` **fails objectively** even before reaching compelling circumstances!
- [ ] **Track 3B — `38 C.F.R. § 3.12(e)` Compelling Circumstances Exception (`Eff. June 25, 2024`, `89 FR 32361`):**
  - **Eligible Bars:** `§ 3.12(c)(6)` (`>= 180`-day continuous AWOL), `§ 3.12(d)(2)(i)` (moral turpitude), and `§ 3.12(d)(2)(ii)` (willful & persistent misconduct). *(Ineligible: `(c)(1)–(5)` including `GCM`, and `(d)(1)(i)–(ii)` discharge in lieu of `GCM` / mutiny).*
  - Build a 3-part evidentiary brief + private Nexus/IMO addressing: `(1)` `§ 3.12(e)(1)` honest/meritorious service outside the misconduct window; `(2)` `§ 3.12(e)(2)(i)–(vii)` mitigating factors (mental/cognitive impairment **including PTSD, TBI, ADHD, or substance use disorder**, physical trauma/medication side effects, combat/overseas hardship, **MST**, duress/desperation, family obligations, youth/immaturity); and `(3)` `§ 3.12(e)(3)` substantive UCMJ defenses.
  - **Effective-Date Ceiling Warning:** Standalone `89 FR 32361` reopenings (`VA Form 20-0995`) **cannot pay earlier than June 25, 2024** (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(g)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.114(a)`). Always screen Tracks **3C–3E** below if pre-2024 back pay is at stake!
- [ ] **Track 3C — `38 U.S.C. § 5303(b)` / `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.12(b), 3.354(b)` Insanity Exception *(Defeats ALL Bars + Retroactive!)*:**
  - Mandatory whenever facing a **General Court-Martial (`(c)(2)`)**, **discharge in lieu of GCM (`(d)(1)(i)`)**, **deserter (`(c)(4)`)**, or **pre-June 25, 2024 back-pay** claim!
  - Obtain a forensic psychiatric opinion applying VA's **non-criminal** `§ 3.354(a)` standard (*Zang v. Brown*, `8 Vet. App. 246 (1995)`; *Gardner v. Shinseki*, `22 Vet. App. 415 (2009)`; [`VAOPGCPREC 20-97`](../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md))—proving disease-based prolonged behavioral deviation concurrent with the offense(s) (never let an examiner apply criminal *M'Naghten* "right vs. wrong" tests, and pair newly obtained brig/inpatient STRs with **`38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)`**!).
- [ ] **Track 3D — *Hamill* AMA Explicit-Notice Audit & Pre-2024 *Cropper* CUE:**
  - ***Hamill v. Collins*, `166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. 2026)` (`38 U.S.C. § 5104(b)`):** Did the veteran submit any AMA (`>= Feb. 19, 2019`) claim or COD reopening request that VA failed to address with explicit `§ 5104(b)` findings? If so, the AMA claim **remains legally pending and unadjudicated**!
  - ***Cropper v. Brown*, `6 Vet. App. 450 (1994)`:** Did a pre-2024 final COD denial misclassify a *Special* Court-Martial `BCD` as a `GCM` statutory bar, ignore `ELS` status under `former § 3.12(k)(1)`, or violate extant pre-2024 law? *(Never cite `89 FR 32361` for pre-2024 CUE per *George v. McDonough*, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)` / `§ 20.1403(e)`).*
- [ ] **Track 3E — Parallel DoD Discharge Upgrade (`DRB` vs. `BCMR`/`BCNR`) & `§ 3.400(g)` Effective Dates:**
  - **`DRB` (`DD Form 293`, `10 U.S.C. § 1553`):** Strict **`15-year`** deadline from discharge; **cannot** upgrade a `GCM` sentence; **TRAP (`Pub. L. 95-126` / `38 C.F.R. § 3.12(h)`):** a post-Oct. 8, 1977 `DRB` upgrade to *General (Under Honorable Conditions)* lifts `(d)` bars **only** if issued under uniform historical standards and **never** overrides an original `(c)` statutory bar!
  - **`BCMR` / `BCNR` (`DD Form 149`, `10 U.S.C. § 1552`):** `3-year` deadline routinely waived "in the interest of justice"; **CAN** upgrade `GCM` discharges, change Narrative Reason (`Personality Disorder -> Secretarial Authority / Medical Retirement`), and **conclusively binds VA under `38 C.F.R. § 3.12(f)`**! Plead DoD **Liberal Consideration** (*Hagel* `2014`, *Carson* `2016`, *Kurta* `2017`, and *Wilkie* `2018` memoranda for PTSD, TBI, mental health, and MST).
  - **`10 U.S.C. § 874(b)` Clemency Trap (`VAOPGCPREC 10-96`):** A Service Secretary's `§ 874(b)` clemency administrative substitution **does NOT** lift a `§ 5303(a)` `GCM` bar—use `10 U.S.C. § 1552` `BCMR` instead!
  - **Post-Upgrade Filing Clock (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(i)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.400(g)(1)–(3)`):** File an original VA claim within **`1 year` of the military upgrade taking effect** to lock in the `DD-293`/`DD-149` filing date (`§ 5110(i)` / `§ 3.400(g)(1)`), or reopen a previously disallowed VA claim promptly (watching `§ 3.400(g)(3)`'s 1-year-prior-to-reopening cap, and pairing any newly uncovered STRs with **`38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)`** to reach the original VA claim date!).

## Gate 4: National Guard / Reserve Active-Service & Line-of-Duty (`LOD`) Audit (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.6, 3.301`)

When a prospect served in the Army/Air National Guard or Reserves, verify the **exact statutory duty status on the date of the injury, exposure, or symptom onset** ([Playbook 11](../playbooks/11-guard-reserve-adt-iadt-and-line-of-duty.md)):

| Duty Classification | Governing Authority | Covered Conditions | Presumption of Soundness (`§ 1111`) & Key Traps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Federal Active Duty (`Title 10`)** | `38 U.S.C. § 101(21)(A)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(b)` | **Any** in-service injury or disease + full statutory presumptions | **YES (`§ 1111` applies):** VA must prove preexistence *and* non-aggravation by clear & unmistakable evidence (*Wagner v. Principi*, `370 F.3d 1089 (Fed. Cir. 2004)`). |
| **2. Full-Time National Guard Operational / `502(f)` Orders (`Title 32`)** | `32 U.S.C. §§ 316, 502–505`; `38 U.S.C. § 101(22)(C)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(c)(3)` | Normally classified as **`ADT`** (`§ 3.6(a)(2), (c)(3)`)—injury *or* disease (plus COVID-19 / Homeland Defense / Southwest Border `32 U.S.C. § 502(f)` federalized protections) | Treated as **`ADT`** unless converted by statute (`e.g., 38 U.S.C. § 1164` COVID-19 presumption) or disabled in line of duty under `§ 101(24)`. |
| **3. Active Duty for Training (`ADT` / `ACDUTRA`, incl. `IADT` Basic/AIT & Annual Training)** | `38 U.S.C. §§ 101(22), 101(24)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(a)(2), (c), (e)` | In-service **injury OR disease** incurred or aggravated in line of duty (`+ § 3.6(e)` authorized portal-to-portal travel) | **CRITICAL TRAP (*Biggins* / *Paulson* / *Smith* / *Donnellan*):** **NO `§ 1111` presumption of soundness or `§ 1153` presumption of aggravation** unless the claimant *first* proves at least in equipoise (`38 U.S.C. § 5107(b)` benefit of the doubt; *Newman v. McDonough*, `35 Vet. App. 310 (2022)`) that a disability was incurred or aggravated during `ADT` (`24 Vet. App. 40`; `24 Vet. App. 167`)! |
| **4. Inactive Duty Training (`IADT` — Weekend `UTA` Drills)** | `38 U.S.C. §§ 101(23), 101(24)(C), 106(d)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.6(a)(3), (d), (e)` | **ONLY:** `(1)` an **injury** incurred/aggravated in line of duty, OR `(2)` **`§ 106(d)` acute myocardial infarction, cardiac arrest, or stroke (`CVA`)** (`+ § 3.6(e)(1)–(3)` direct portal-to-portal travel) | **FATAL DISEASE BAR:** Ordinary diseases (e.g., non-stroke sleep apnea, diabetes, non-traumatic psychiatric onset without injury/assault) during weekend `IADT` **cannot** establish `§ 101(24)` active service unless tied to an `IADT` **injury** (e.g., orthopedic trauma, acoustic trauma, TBI, or physical MST assault) or `TERA` exam! |
| **5. Pure State Active Duty (`SAD` — Governor's State Funds)** | State law | **NONE under Title 38** (state workers' compensation only) | Verify funding/authority code on orders—many Guardsmen confuse `Title 32 § 502(f)` federally funded disaster/border duty (qualifying!) with pure state-funded `SAD`! |

- [ ] **Demand Missing Guard/Reserve Records for `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)` Reconsideration:** Request all `Title 10`/`32` orders, `NGB-23` / Reserve retirement point sheets, unit drill muster rosters, **`DA Form 2173` (Army) / `AF Form 348` (Air Force) / `DD Form 261` Formal Line of Duty (`LOD`) determinations**, and `1973` St. Louis fire / state Adjutant General (`TAG`) archives. Associating an unobtained `DA Form 2173` or `Title 32` order unlocks retroactive compensation back to a prior `"no active service"` denial under **`38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)`**!
- [ ] **Audit Line of Duty & Secondary Substance Use (`38 U.S.C. § 105(a)`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.1(m)–(n), 3.301, 3.310(a)–(b)`):** Under **`38 C.F.R. § 3.1(m)` and `(n)`**, favorable military `LOD` and non-misconduct findings **are binding on VA** **`"unless it is patently inconsistent with the facts and the requirements of laws administered by the Department of Veterans Affairs"`**—whereas adverse `"Not in LOD"` military findings do *not* bind VA! For alcohol/substance-related disabilities, plead **secondary service connection under *Allen v. Principi*, `237 F.3d 1368 (Fed. Cir. 2001)` (`§ 3.310(a)–(b)`)** whenever SC PTSD, depression, or chronic orthopedic pain caused or aggravated the substance use disorder and downstream organ damage!
- [ ] **Screen `PACT Act` Guard/Reserve Toxic Exposures (`38 U.S.C. § 1168` `TERA`):** Under `Pub. ## Gate 5: Immediate `Chapter 17` Healthcare Access, Ancillaries & `§ 5904` Fee Check

Never leave a disabled `OTH` client untreated while a 12-month COD or `BCMR` docket runs—immediate VHA care protects the veteran's life **and** generates contemporaneous clinical diagnoses/nexus records (`Bell` constructive possession):

- [ ] **Enroll Immediately in Statutory `Chapter 17` Healthcare Carve-Outs (`VA Form 10-10EZ` / VHA Eligibility):**
  - **`38 U.S.C. § 1720D` (`MST` Counseling & Care):** Furnished **regardless of character of discharge** and **without requiring any in-service report** of the sexual assault or sexual harassment!
  - **`38 U.S.C. § 1720I` (`OTH` Mental & Behavioral Health Care):** Available to former service members with an administrative `OTH` (excluding `GCM`/dishonorable) who either `(A)` served **`> 100` cumulative days** with deployment in a combat theater / contingency / hostility / drone operation **OR** `(B)` experienced **`MST`**.
  - **`38 U.S.C. § 1720J` (`COMPACT Act` Acute Suicidal Crisis Care):** Zero-cost emergency inpatient/outpatient stabilization at any VA or community facility regardless of enrollment or discharge status (subject to statutory exclusion thresholds).
  - **`38 C.F.R. § 3.360` (`Pub. L. 95-126, § 2` Line-of-Duty Healthcare) & `38 C.F.R. § 17.34` (Tentative Eligibility):** Unlocks Chapter 17 medical treatment for specific service-connected/line-of-duty disabilities for administrative `OTH` veterans whenever no `§ 5303(a)` / `§ 3.12(c)` statutory bar or `BCD` applies!
- [ ] **Verify `38 U.S.C. § 5904(c)(1)` / `38 C.F.R. § 14.636` Contingency-Fee Posture:**
  - **Initial Original Claim / Pre-Decision COD Review:** Representation before VA issues an initial AOJ decision on the claim is **pro bono (`$0` fee)** under `§ 5904(c)(1)`.
  - **Post-AOJ COD Denial (`VA Form 20-0995` Supplemental, `20-0996` HLR, or `10182` Board NOD):** A prior AOJ Administrative Decision denying eligibility on `COD` or `"no qualifying active service"` grounds **satisfies `§ 5904(c)(1)`**, authorizing a compliant contingency fee (`<= 20%` direct-pay withholding) on all past-due Title 38 compensation unlocked once the COD/service gateway and disability ratings are granted!
  - **DoD `DRB`/`BCMR` Fee Boundary:** `VA` will **not** withhold `§ 5904(d)` direct-pay fees from DoD proceedings (`DD-293`/`DD-149`); ensure any parallel DoD upgrade retainer is separately structured and compliant, with Title 38 contingency fees attaching only to continuous/post-AOJ-decision VA reviews.
