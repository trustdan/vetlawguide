# Zero-PII 6-Gate Post-Grant VA Award Letter, Offset, Ancillary & `§ 5904(d)` Fee-Withholding Audit Checklist

> [!CAUTION]
> **MANDATORY 7-DAY POST-GRANT AUDIT & ZERO-PII RULE:** Copy this checklist to your secure, encrypted firm case-management workspace before entering case numbers—**never** put client PII in Git! Run all **six sequential gates within `7 calendar days`** of every favorable RO, HLR, or BVA implementation decision. Over `40%` of high-dollar grants contain at least one effective-date truncation, omitted `SMC`/bilateral calculation, unlawful severance/retiree withholding, or miscalculated `20%` fee base.

*(Cross-references: Verbatim [`38-cfr-3.114-3.400-3.700-effective-dates-offsets.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.114-3.400-3.700-effective-dates-offsets.md); verbatim [`38-cfr-3.274-3.279-3.353-1.965-pension-competency-waivers.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.274-3.279-3.353-1.965-pension-competency-waivers.md); [Master Effective-Date & Staged-Rating Treatise](../docs/effective-dates-and-staged-ratings-treatise.md); [`Part 3 Pension Net-Worth & Effective-Date Diffs`](../cfr-diffs/part-3/pension-net-worth-and-effective-date-diffs.md); [Playbook 20 (Back-Pay Offsets, `CRDP`/`CRSC`, Severance & `§ 5904` Fees)](../playbooks/20-backpay-offsets-crdp-crsc-severance-recoupment.md); [Playbook 21 (Overpayment Waivers, Incompetency & Wartime Pension)](../playbooks/21-overpayment-waivers-incompetency-and-wartime-pension.md); [Playbook 06 (*Nehmer* & `CRSC` / *Soto*)](../playbooks/06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md); [Playbook 13 (Ancillary Grants)](../playbooks/13-ancillary-grants-sah-auto-clothing-dea-spina-bifida.md); [Playbook 17 (`§ 1151` & `FTCA` Offsets)](../playbooks/17-section-1151-va-malpractice-and-ftca-interplay.md); [Master SMC Treatise](../docs/smc-complete-treatise-and-step-math.md); [`5-Gate SMC & Ancillary Audit Worksheet`](smc-and-ancillary-audit-worksheet.md); [Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md); and [Authority Verification Log](../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).*

## Preconditions (Pull From VBMS Before Auditing the Award Letter)

- [ ] **1. Internal Rating Code Sheet** *(never rely solely on the claimant notification letter!)*
- [ ] **2. VA Award Letter + `Payment Summary` / `Withholding` Schedule** *(monthly rate table from effective date to present)*
- [ ] **3. `DFAS Audit Error Worksheet (AEW)` / `DD-214 Block 18` / `DFAS RAS`** *(if military retiree or separated with severance/separation pay)*
- [ ] **4. RO Direct-Pay Fee Entitlement / Allocation Decision (`38 C.F.R. § 14.636(h)–(i)`) & Executed Fee Agreement**

## Gate 1: Effective-Date & Staged-Rating Audit (`38 U.S.C. § 5110`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.114, 3.400`)

Cross-check every granted Diagnostic Code's effective date against the [Master Effective-Date & Staged-Rating Treatise](../docs/effective-dates-and-staged-ratings-treatise.md):

- [ ] **1.1 "Date of C&P Exam" Error (*Swain v. McDonald*, `27 Vet. App. 219 (2015)`):** Did the rater lazily assign the date of the VA/private DBQ examination instead of the date the claim was received (`§ 5110(a)(1)`) or the earlier date medical/lay evidence shows the disability or worsening arose?
- [ ] **1.2 Unconsumed Intent to File (`VA Form 21-0966`, `38 C.F.R. § 3.155(b)`):** Did an active `ITF` filed within 1 year prior to the `526EZ` / `0995` go unapplied—or did an earlier claim consume the first `ITF` while a second `ITF` remained valid?
- [ ] **1.3 Increased-Rating `1-Year` Pre-Claim Lookback (`§ 5110(b)(3)` `[former § 5110(b)(2)]`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.400(o)(2)`):** On any increased-rating grant (including initial `TDIU` on already-SC conditions!), did VAMC, private clinical, or lay employment records show an ascertainable increase in severity **within the `1 year` *preceding* the claim/ITF**?
- [ ] **1.4 `1-Year` Post-Discharge (`§ 5110(b)(1)`) / `1-Year` Post-Death (`§ 5110(d)`) / `1-Year` Post-`§ 1151` Injury (`§ 3.400(i)`) Lookbacks:** Verify day-after-discharge, first-of-month-of-death, or date-of-malpractice-injury backdating when filed within 1 year.
- [ ] **1.5 Liberalizing Law (`§ 5110(g)`; `§ 3.114(a)`), PACT Act (`Aug. 10, 2022`), *Nehmer* (`§ 3.816`) & `§ 3.156(c)` Service Records:** Verify raters did not assign the `20-0995` Supplemental Claim date when `§ 3.114(a)` (`up to 1 year` pre-claim back to statutory enactment), `PACT Act § 1305` prior-DIC re-evaluation, *Nehmer* Footnote 1 (`>= Sept. 25, 1985`), or `§ 3.156(c)(3)` original-claim reconsideration controlled!
- [ ] **1.6 Staged Ratings Across Multi-Year Appeals (*Fenderson v. West*, `12 Vet. App. 119 (1999)`; *Hart v. Mansfield*, `21 Vet. App. 505 (2007)`; *Singleton v. Shinseki*, `659 F.3d 1332 (Fed. Cir. 2011)`):** Did VA assign a low initial stage (`10%` or `30%`) for earlier years and `70%`/`100%` only recently despite longitudinal records supporting higher earlier stages—or stage *downward* without affirmative medical evidence and adequate `§ 7104(d)(1)` reasons or bases (*Singleton*)?
- [ ] **1.7 Silent Omission Check (*Hamill v. Collins*, `166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. 2026)`):** Compare every contention on the `526EZ` / `0995` / `NOD` against the decision's `§ 5104(b)` findings—any unaddressed issue remains legally pending!

## Gate 2: Combined Math (`§ 4.25`), Bilateral Factor (`§ 4.26(d)`), `TDIU` & Mandatory `SMC(k)–(t)` Audit

- [ ] **2.1 Whole-Person Combined Table (`38 C.F.R. § 4.25`):** Recompute every historical epoch independently (`x.5` rounds **up** to the next `10%`, e.g., `94.5% -> 95% -> 100%`; `94.4% -> 94% -> 90%`).
- [ ] **2.2 Bilateral Factor Inclusion vs. `§ 4.26(d)` Exclusion (`eff. Apr. 16, 2023`, `88 FR 22914`):** Verify paired upper- or lower-extremity SC disabilities received the `+10%` bilateral factor—**AND** for periods `>= Apr. 16, 2023`, verify VA excluded a bilateral pair under `§ 4.26(d)` if including it paradoxically dropped `95%` (`100%`) down to `94%` (`90%`)!
- [ ] **2.3 Inferred `TDIU` (`§ 4.16(a)–(b)`) & Mandatory `SMC` Sweep ([`SMC Audit Worksheet`](smc-and-ancillary-audit-worksheet.md)):**
  - If combined `< 100%` (`>= 60%` single or `>= 70%` combined with one `40%+`) and evidence shows unemployability, did VA adjudicate or defer `TDIU`?
  - Did the code sheet award **every mandatory statutory `SMC`**: **`SMC(k)`** (`ED`/`FSAD`, breast tissue/radiation, extremity `LOU`, aphonia, single eye, deafness), **Statutory `SMC(s)`** (`100%` single disability or *Bradley* single-disability `TDIU` + `>= 60%` independent SC), **`SMC(l)`–`SMC(o)`** + ***Barry* (`101 F.4th 1348`)** multi-step `§ 3.350(f)(3)–(4)` bumps, or **`SMC(t)`** (*Laska*, `37 Vet. App. 460`)?

## Gate 3: Dependency (`§ 3.401(b)`) & Ancillary Benefit Unlocks ([Playbook 13](../playbooks/13-ancillary-grants-sah-auto-clothing-dea-spina-bifida.md))

- [ ] **3.1 Dependents (`38 U.S.C. § 1115`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.401(b)`):** Whenever an award first crosses **`>= 30%` combined** (or retroactively backdates `>= 30%`), verify spouse, minor children, `18–23` school-children (`VA Form 21-674`), `§ 3.356` helpless children, and dependent parents (`VA Form 21P-509`) are paid from the **exact `>= 30%` effective date**! **Action:** If missing, file **`VA Form 21-686c` within `1 year` of the rating notification letter** (`§ 3.401(b)(3)`) to capture `100%` of multi-year retroactive dependency back pay!
- [ ] **3.2 Spouse Aid & Attendance Add-On (`38 U.S.C. § 1115(1)(E)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.351(a)(2)`):** At `>= 30%` combined, if the veteran's **spouse** needs regular A&A (nursing home, dementia, ADL assistance), file `VA Form 21-2680` + `21-686c` for an extra `~$195+/month` (pro-rated from `30%`–`100%`).
- [ ] **3.3 `100% P&T` / `TDIU P&T` Ancillary Trigger Packet *(Excluding `§ 1151`-Only Grants per [Playbook 17](../playbooks/17-section-1151-va-malpractice-and-ftca-interplay.md))!*:**
  - **`Chapter 35` `DEA` (`VA Form 22-5490`):** Confirm the code sheet explicitly grants `Basic Eligibility to Dependents' Educational Assistance` with the **earliest `100% P&T` effective date**; advise spouse/children to file **within `1 year` of P&T notice (`38 C.F.R. § 21.4131`)** to lock in retroactive tuition/stipend semesters!
  - **`CHAMPVA` (`VA Form 10-10d`)**, **`VALife` (`38 U.S.C. § 1922B`)**, **State `100% P&T` Property-Tax Exemption** (have client take the VA Summary of Benefits letter to the County Tax Assessor immediately—many states bar retroactive tax-year refunds!), and **Federal Student Loan Total & Permanent Disability (`TPD`) Discharge**.
- [ ] **3.4 Catastrophic Grants:** Screen **`SAH`/`SHA`/`TRA` (`VA Form 26-4555`, `~$117,000+`)**, **`Ch. 39` Auto (`VA Form 21-4502` / `10-1394`, `~$25,000+` + `Pub. L. 117-333` 30-yr/10-yr repeat eligibility)**, and ***Sursely* Multiple Clothing Allowances (`VA Form 10-8678`, `Aug. 1` annual cycle)**.

## Gate 4: Military Retiree (`CRDP`/`CRSC`), Severance (`§ 3.700`), Drill Pay & Incarceration Withholding Audit ([Playbook 20](../playbooks/20-backpay-offsets-crdp-crsc-severance-recoupment.md))

Inspect every dollar withheld in the Award Letter's `Withholding` column:

- [ ] **4.1 Military Retired Pay & `CRDP` / `CRSC` Check (`10 U.S.C. §§ 1413a, 1414`):**
  - **`20+` Year / `TERA` / Gray-Area Retiree Rated `>= 50%` (or `100% TDIU`):** Confirm `CRDP` triggered automatically; calendar a **`45-day` `DFAS AEW` follow-up** until VA & DFAS release the held retroactive compensation!
  - **Chapter 61 Medical Retiree (`< 20` Years Service) OR Any Combat Retiree:** Remember `< 20-yr` Ch. 61 retirees get **ZERO `CRDP`** (`10 U.S.C. § 1414(b)(2)`)! Immediately file/amend **`DD Form 2860` (`CRSC`)** with the veteran's military branch to claim **uncapped retroactive tax-free `CRSC` arrears** under ***Soto v. United States*, `605 U.S. 360 (2025)`** ([Playbook 06](../playbooks/06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md))!
- [ ] **4.2 Disability Severance Pay (`10 U.S.C. § 1212`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.700(a)(3)`) & Separation Pay (`§ 3.700(a)(5)`) Three-Prong Check:**
  - **Prong A (Combat Non-Recoupment):** Did the veteran separate under Ch. 61 **on or after `Jan. 28, 2008`** for a disability incurred in a **combat zone** or **combat-related operations** (`§ 1212(d)(2)`)? If YES, VA recoupment must be **`$0.00`**—challenge any withholding immediately!
  - **Prong B (Non-Severance Isolation Math):** For `DSP` (`§ 3.700(a)(3)`—unlike `ISP`/`SSB` under `§ 3.700(a)(5)`), verify VA withheld **at most** the lesser of `(1)` the initial standalone severance rating or `(2)` `Total Combined Rate minus Non-Severance Combined Rate` (e.g., `$0/mo` withheld when `70%` non-severance + `10%` severance still rounds to `70%`!).
  - **Prong C (Post-Sept. 30, 1996 After-Tax Net Cap):** For *any* `DSP`, `ISP`, `SSB`, or Readjustment Pay paid **after `Sept. 30, 1996`**, verify VA deducted federal income taxes withheld at separation (`Pub. L. 104-201, § 653`) rather than recouping the gross `DD-214` figure!
- [ ] **4.3 Reserve Drill Pay (`§ 3.654`) & Felony Incarceration (`§ 3.665`):**
  - Confirm drill-pay adjustments waive **only `1/30th` per drill/AT day** (e.g., `63 days/yr`), NOT a full-year award suspension.
  - If incarcerated `> 60 days` post-felony conviction (*Mulder*), verify no reduction occurred during pre-trial detention or **halfway house / work-release (`§ 3.665(b)`)**, file **`VA Form 21-0788` dependent apportionment (`§ 3.665(e)`)** immediately for spouse/children, and verify day-of-release full restoration (`§ 3.665(i)`).

## Gate 5: `38 U.S.C. § 5904(d)` `20%` Direct-Pay Fee Base & `EAJA` Same-Work Offset Audit

- [ ] **5.1 Mandatory `30-Day` Filing Compliance (*Ravin v. Wilkie*, `956 F.3d 1346 (Fed. Cir. 2020)`):** Confirm VBMS reflects timely filing of `VA Form 21-22a` and the compliant `<= 20%` direct-pay fee agreement within `30 days` of execution (`38 C.F.R. § 14.636(g)(3)`), and that all compensated work occurred *after* an initial AOJ decision on the claim (`§ 5904(c)(1)`).
- [ ] **5.2 Recompute Exact `"Past-Due Benefits"` Base (`38 C.F.R. § 14.636(h)(3)`):**
  - Sum `(Newly Awarded Monthly Rate [incl. SMC & Dependents] − Prior Paid Monthly Rate)` from the granted effective date through the date of the award decision (`up to date of award implementation` per VA fee-calculator rules).
  - **`DFAS AEW` Hold Check:** If `100%` of retro was temporarily held pending a `DFAS AEW` on a `CRDP`-eligible retiree, do **not** close the fee ledger until DFAS returns the `AEW` and VA issues the supplemental **Fee Release** disbursing `20%` of the restored net cash!
  - **Incarceration vs. Debt-Invalidation Distinctions:** Under ***Snyder v. Nicholson*, `489 F.3d 1213 (Fed. Cir. 2007)`**, `§ 5313` incarceration reductions do *not* shrink the gross `"past-due benefits awarded"` base (payable from non-apportioned retro); conversely, under ***Gumpenberger v. Wilkie*, `973 F.3d 1379 (Fed. Cir. 2020)`**, invalidating a VA debt yields direct-pay fees **only** on dollars VA actually withheld and restored in cash (*note also nonprecedential* *Jackson v. McDonald*, `635 F. App'x 858 (Fed. Cir. 2015) (nonprecedential)`).
- [ ] **5.3 Mandatory `EAJA` + `§ 5904` Same-Work Client Refund (`Pub. L. 102-572, § 506(c)`, `106 Stat. 4513`):**
  - Did the firm previously receive an **`EAJA` fee (`28 U.S.C. § 2412(d)`)** for the CAVC appeal that remanded this exact claim, and does the `§ 5904(d)` fee agreement cover that **same work** (as opposed to distinct post-remand agency advocacy cleanly segregated by contract, or where EAJA is offset)? If EAJA and `§ 5904` overlap for the same work, **calculate and disburse the lesser-of-the-two refund to the veteran immediately** upon receipt of the `§ 5904` payment and log the trust/operating check in the file!
