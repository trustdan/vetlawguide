# Historical & Modern VA Forms, Service Records & Rating Code Sheet Lexicon (1945–2026)

Deciphering a 50-year-old C-file requires recognizing obsolete form numbers, military archival documents, and internal VA Rating Code Sheet shorthand.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Always Obtain Every Historical Rating Code Sheet:** VA routinely mailed claimants only the cover letter (and, after 1990, the Rating Decision narrative) while keeping the internal **Rating Code Sheet** inside the C-file / VBMS eFolder. Only the Rating Code Sheet displays exact 4-digit Diagnostic Codes, effective dates, `38 C.F.R. § 4.25` combined math, `§ 4.26` bilateral factors, protection flags, `(Nehmer granted)` tags, and SMC letter/hospitalization codes!

Compare **every symptom written by the veteran** against Rating Code Sheet #1—omitted lines may be unadjudicated pending claims (*Shea v. Wilkie*). |

## 2. Official Military & Archival Records Triggering `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)`

* **`AGO Form 53-55` / `NAVPERS 553` / `WD AGO Form 38`:** WWII separation and clinical physical reports (predating the 1950 `DD Form 214`).
* **`DD Form 214` & `DD Form 215`:** Certificate of Release or Discharge from Active Duty (`1950–present`) and official correction/amendment adding omitted awards (Purple Heart, CIB, CAR, Vietnam Service Medal).
* **`DA Form 20` / `DA Form 2-1`:** Army *Enlisted Qualification Record* (`Vietnam/Cold War era`)—frequently proves exact unit assignments, MOS, Republic of Vietnam / Thailand / Korean DMZ dates, and combat campaigns (e.g., Tet Counteroffensive) omitted from a terse `DD-214`!
* **Inpatient Clinical Packets vs. Outpatient Health Jackets:** Military hospitals filed **inpatient surgical/ward charts** separately by hospital and year at NPRC—often absent when the RO first requested STRs!
* **Morning Reports, Muster Rolls, Operational Diaries, Ship Deck Logs & JSRRC / CRUR Reports:** Official unit records qualifying under `§ 3.156(c)(1)(i)` **even when they corroborate unit combat/exposure without naming the individual veteran**.
* **`NA Form 13055` & Surgeon General's Office (`SGO`) Extracts:** NPRC 1973 St. Louis Fire loss certification and reconstructed hospital admission punch-card extracts (`§ 3.156(c)(1)(iii)`).

## 3. Decoding Legacy & Modern Rating Code Sheet Shorthand

* **Hyphenated / Analogous Diagnostic Codes (`38 C.F.R. §§ 4.20, 4.27`):**
  * Prior to modern VBMS formatting, raters typed **`7399-7346`** (or `7346-7319` / `5299-5257`). A **`xx99-xxxx`** code means an **unlisted condition rated by analogy (`§ 4.20`)** to the second 4-digit Diagnostic Code (e.g., pre-May 2024 GERD rated analogously to Hiatal Hernia `7399-7346`).
  * Residual Etiology Hyphenation (`§ 4.27`): E.g., **`5317-7804`** or **`8520-5243`** denotes the etiological link + functional evaluation code.
* **`SC` vs. `NSC` vs. `1151`:**
  * `SC` = Service-Connected; `NSC` = Non-Service-Connected (used for pension or noted to deny linkage—audit `NSC` diagnoses on 1960s–1990s code sheets for missed `§ 3.309(a)` chronic or `§ 3.816` *Nehmer* conditions!); `38 U.S.C. § 1151` = Disability caused by VA medical care/voc rehab (paid "as if" service-connected).
* **Bilateral Factor (`38 C.F.R. § 4.26`):**
  * Paired service-connected disabilities affecting **both upper extremities** or **both lower extremities** combine first (`§ 4.25`), then receive **`+10%` of their combined value** before combining with non-bilateral disabilities.
  * **April 16, 2023 Exclusion Rule (`88 FR 22914`, `38 C.F.R. § 4.26(d)`):** If including a bilateral disability in the bilateral factor calculation paradoxically *lowers* the rounded combined evaluation (e.g., `94.4% -> 90%` instead of `94.6% -> 95% -> 100%`), VA must **exclude** that bilateral disability from the bilateral factor and combine it separately to maximize the veteran's combined rating!
* **Common SMC Code-Sheet Designators (`38 U.S.C. § 1114`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.350`):**
  * **`SMC (K-1)`, `(K-2)`, `(K-3)`:** Statutory add-on (`§ 1114(k)`) for anatomical loss or **loss of use** of one creative organ, one foot, one hand, both buttocks, blindness of one eye (light perception only), or complete organic aphonia (up to 3 `K` awards stack on `0%–100%` or higher SMC subject to statutory rate caps).
  * **`SMC (S-1)` (`§ 1114(s)`):** Statutory Housebound (`100%` single disability [or *Bradley* single-disability TDIU] + `>= 60%` separate combined SC disabilities) OR Housebound-in-Fact.
  * **`SMC (L)` through `(O)` & Intermediate `(f)` Steps:** Aid & Attendance (`L`), anatomical/loss-of-use extremity/blindness tiers (`L½`, `M`, `M½`, `N`, `N½`, `O`). Under ***Barry v. McDonough*, 101 F.4th 1348 (Fed. Cir. 2024)**, `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(f)(3)` (half-step for separate `50%+` disability) and `§ 3.350(f)(4)` (full-step for separate `100%` disability) allow **multiple cumulative steps** up to `SMC(o)`!
  * **`SMC (R-1)` / `(R-2)` / `(T)`:** Higher-level Aid & Attendance (`R-1`), skilled nursing/medical care (`R-2`), and Traumatic Brain Injury (`TBI`) Aid & Attendance (`SMC(t)`, paid at the `R-2` rate without needing `SMC(o)` prerequisites, and without needing licensed health-care professional care after *Laska v. McDonough*, `37 Vet. App. 460 (2024)`! See [Master SMC Treatise & Step Math](smc-complete-treatise-and-step-math.md), [Playbook 12](../playbooks/12-aid-and-attendance-smc-l-r1-r2-and-smc-t.md), [Playbook 13](../playbooks/13-ancillary-grants-sah-auto-clothing-dea-spina-bifida.md), and [`SMC & Ancillary Audit Worksheet`](../checklists/smc-and-ancillary-audit-worksheet.md)).
