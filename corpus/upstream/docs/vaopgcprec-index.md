# Master Binding VA General Counsel Precedent Opinion (`VAOPGCPREC`) Digest (`1989–Present`)

Under **`38 U.S.C. § 7104(c)`** (`former 38 U.S.C. § 4004(c)`, see [Three-Era Title 38 Statutory Crosswalk](title-38-statutory-crosswalk.md)) and **`38 C.F.R. §§ 2.6(e)(8), 14.507`**, written legal opinions designated as precedent opinions by the VA General Counsel (`VAOPGCPREC`) occupy **Tier 4** of the Veterans Law hierarchy of authorities ([authority-verification-log.md](authority-verification-log.md)).

Unlike the `M21-1` Adjudication Procedures Manual—which binds Regional Office (`AOJ`) raters operationally but does **not** bind the Board of Veterans' Appeals (`38 C.F.R. § 20.105`)—an active `VAOPGCPREC` opinion **legally binds both Regional Offices and the Board of Veterans' Appeals** unless and until it is superseded by statute/regulation, invalidated by the Federal Circuit/CAVC/Supreme Court, or formally withdrawn by VA OGC.

---

## 1. Bitemporal CUE Mechanics & The *George v. McDonough* Mirror Rule

Because `VAOPGCPREC` opinions are binding substantive law within VA under `38 U.S.C. § 7104(c)`, they cut **both ways** in Clear and Unmistakable Error (`CUE`) litigation ([procedural-vehicles-piercing-finality.md](procedural-vehicles-piercing-finality.md); [board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md](../checklists/board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md)):

1. **Offensive CUE Sword (`cue-and-open-claim`):** If an active, binding `VAOPGCPREC` opinion was in force on the date of a final RO or Board decision (from its internal OGC issuance date forward under `38 C.F.R. § 14.507(b)`), and undisputed facts of record triggered its rule—for example, refusing separate knee instability (`DC 5257`) and limitation-of-motion (`DC 5260`/`5261`) ratings after `VAOPGCPREC 23-97`, or rejecting a progressive hereditary disease as a "congenital defect" after `VAOPGCPREC 82-90`—**failure to apply that extant `VAOPGCPREC` is textbook CUE** (`38 U.S.C. §§ 5109A, 7111`).
2. **Defensive CUE Shield (*George v. McDonough*, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)` & `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)` — `open-claim-only-george-restricted`):** Conversely, if an RO or Board decision *faithfully followed* a restrictive `VAOPGCPREC` that was active and binding on the date of the decision—for example, `VAOPGCPREC 6-99` barring single-disability TDIU from satisfying the `100%` prong of `SMC(s)` prior to *Bradley v. Peake*, `22 Vet. App. 280 (2008)`—a later court invalidation or OGC withdrawal **cannot** establish CUE against that prior final decision (`VAOPGCPREC 25-95`; *Jordan v. Nicholson*, `401 F.3d 1296 (Fed. Cir. 2005)`). To capture retroactive back pay across a pre-withdrawal era, practitioners **must** unlock an open claim via `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(b)` (*Lang*), `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)` service records, or an unadjudicated pending claim!

---

## 2. Citator Status Taxonomy

Every opinion in this digest has been Shepardized against `va.gov/ogc/precedentopinions.asp`, *Federal Register* withdrawal notices, Title 38 statutory amendments, and CAVC/Federal Circuit precedent:

* **`Active-Binding`:** Remains in full force on all VA adjudicators and the Board under `38 U.S.C. § 7104(c)` and `38 C.F.R. § 14.507`.
* **`Active-Binding (Modified / Clarified)`:** Core holding remains binding, while a subsidiary prong was refined by later regulation or appellate precedent.
* **`Invalidated in Part by Court / Superseded by Regulation`:** A controlling Federal Circuit or CAVC decision struck down a central legal premise, prompting a corrective `38 C.F.R.` amendment.
* **`Withdrawn by VA OGC`:** Formally stripped of precedential status by VA OGC notice published in the *Federal Register*—**never cite as active law**.
