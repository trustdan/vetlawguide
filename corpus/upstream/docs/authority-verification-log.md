# Authority Hierarchy, Versioning Policy & Citation Verification Log

Synthesized and cross-verified from dual Deep Research audits ([deep-research.md](../knowledge/inbox/deep-research.md)) and primary Federal Register / judicial checks.

---

## 1. Hierarchy of Authorities in Veterans Law

1. **Positive Statutory Text (`Title 38 U.S.C.` & Public Laws; pre-1959 `Veterans' Regulations`):** Supreme controlling authority.
2. **Enacted `38 C.F.R.` Regulations (Edition in Force on Decision Date):** Binding substantive law unless invalidated—and under *George v. McDonough*, 596 U.S. 740 (2022), even a *later-invalidated* regulation defeats CUE if the adjudicator faithfully applied it while it was in force.
3. **Controlling Judicial Precedent:** U.S. Supreme Court -> U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit -> precedential panel/en banc decisions of the U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims (CAVC).
   * **Nonprecedential Warning (`CAVC Rule 30(a)`):** Single-judge CAVC memorandum decisions (e.g., *Marion v. Collins*) are **not binding precedent**.
4. **VA General Counsel Precedent Opinions (`VAOPGCPREC`):** Binding on **all VA officials, ROs, and the Board** (`38 U.S.C. § 7104(c)`; `38 C.F.R. § 14.507`), though reviewable by the Federal Circuit/CAVC.
5. **Adjudication Procedures Manual (`M21-1`):** Binding operational instructions for AOJ/RO raters, **not** binding on the Board or courts (`38 C.F.R. § 20.105`), and frequently reorganized on KnowVA—always record the exact retrieval date and historical change memo.

## 2. Mandatory Temporal-Versioning Rules

* **Publication Date != Effective Date:** Always verify the **effective date** in the Federal Register preamble:
  * **1945 VASRD:** Titled "1945 Schedule", **effective April 1, 1946** (`38 C.F.R. § 3.952`; Part 4 App. A).
  * **Auditory & Tinnitus (`§§ 4.85–4.87`):** Maryland CNC & puretone tables published Nov. 18, 1987 (`52 FR 44117`), **effective December 18, 1987**; `§ 4.86` exceptional patterns & `"recurrent"` tinnitus published May 11, 1999 (`64 FR 25202`), **effective June 10, 1999**; `DC 6260` Note (2) single `10%` rule published May 14, 2003 (`68 FR 25822`), **effective June 13, 2003**.
  * **Mental Disorders (`former § 4.132` -> `§ 4.130`):** `70%` psychoneurotic tier & `DC 9411` codified Jan. 4, 1988 (`53 FR 21`), **effective February 3, 1988**; DSM-IV unification, `§ 4.129` `50%` rule & `DC 9520–9521` published Oct. 8, 1996 (`61 FR 52695`), **effective November 7, 1996**; DSM-5 update published & **effective August 4, 2014** (`79 FR 45093`; finalized `80 FR 14308`, Mar. 19, 2015).
  * **Endocrine System (`§ 4.119`):** Era 2 published May 7, 1996 (`61 FR 20440`), **effective June 6, 1996**; Era 3 overhaul published Nov. 2, 2017 (`82 FR 50802`), **effective December 10, 2017**.
  * **Eye & Vision (`§§ 4.75–4.79`):** Era 2 overhaul published Nov. 10, 2008 (`73 FR 66543`), **effective December 10, 2008**; Era 3 clinic-visit overhaul published Apr. 10, 2018 (`83 FR 15316`), **effective May 13, 2018**.
  * **Hemic & Lymphatic Systems (`§ 4.117`):** `AL` Amyloidosis (`DC 7717`) published Feb. 8, 2012 (`77 FR 6466`), **effective March 9, 2012**; comprehensive overhaul published Oct. 29, 2018 (`83 FR 54250`), **effective December 9, 2018** (corrected Oct. 11, 2022 at `87 FR 61248`).
  * **Infectious, Immune & Nutritional (`§§ 4.88a–4.88b`):** `§ 4.88a` / `DC 6354` CFS published & **effective November 29, 1994** (`59 FR 60902`); comprehensive overhaul published June 18, 2019 (`84 FR 28227`), **effective August 11, 2019**.
  * **Muscle Injuries (`§§ 4.55–4.73`):** Published June 3, 1997 (`62 FR 30235`), **effective July 3, 1997**.
  * **Spine IVDS vs. General Formula:** IVDS published Aug. 22, 2002 (`67 FR 54345`), **effective Sept. 23, 2002**; General Rating Formula published Aug. 27, 2003 (`68 FR 51454`), **effective Sept. 26, 2003**.
  * **`38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)` Revision:** Proposed June 20, 2005 (`70 FR 35388`); Final Rule published Sept. 6, 2006 (`71 FR 52455`), **effective October 6, 2006**.
  * **Scars (`§ 4.118`):** Interim overhaul published July 31, 2002 (`67 FR 49590`), **effective August 30, 2002**; major 8-characteristic/painful-scar-count overhaul published Sept. 23, 2008 (`73 FR 54708`), **effective October 23, 2008** (clarified Jan. 20, 2012 at `77 FR 2909`); skin/scars update published July 13, 2018 (`83 FR 32592`), **effective August 13, 2018**.
  * **Standardized Forms / End of Informal Claims:** Published Sept. 25, 2014 (`79 FR 57660`), **effective March 24, 2015**.
  * **Respiratory System (`§§ 4.96–4.97`):** Published Sept. 5, 1996 (`61 FR 46720`), **effective October 7, 1996** (`§ 4.96(d)` PFT rules added Sept. 6, 2006 at `71 FR 52457, 52459`, **effective October 6, 2006**).
  * **Cardiovascular System (`§§ 4.100–4.104`):** Era 2 (`LVEF + METs`) published Dec. 11, 1997 (`62 FR 65207`), **effective January 12, 1998** (`DC 7122` Cold Injury published July 14, 1998 at `63 FR 37778`, **effective August 13, 1998**); Era 3 (`METs` General Formula) published Sept. 30, 2021 (`86 FR 54089`), **effective November 14, 2021** (corrected Nov. 9, 2021 at `86 FR 62095` and Nov. 29, 2021 at `86 FR 67654`).
  * **Musculoskeletal (`§ 4.71a` / `§ 4.73`):** Published Nov. 30, 2020 (`85 FR 76453`) and corrected Feb. 4, 2021 (`86 FR 8142` renumbering Plantar Fasciitis from `5285` to **`DC 5269`**), **effective February 7, 2021**.
  * **Bilateral Factor Exclusion (`§ 4.26(d)`):** Published Apr. 14, 2023 (`88 FR 22914`), **effective April 16, 2023**.
  * **Digestive System (`§ 4.114`):** Published Mar. 20, 2024 (`89 FR 19735`), **effective May 19, 2024**.
* **Never Project Modern Text Backward in CUE:** Obtain the print/govinfo CFR edition (govinfo covers 1996–present; HeinOnline / Federal Register covers 1936–1995) before filing any CUE motion.
