# Master Special Monthly Compensation (`SMC`) Legal Treatise & Multi-Step Rate Architecture (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(k)–(t)`)

While standard schedular evaluations (`10%–100%`, `38 U.S.C. § 1114(a)–(j)`) compensate average impairment in civil earning capacity (`38 U.S.C. § 1155`), **Special Monthly Compensation (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(k)–(t)`)** is Congress's supra-schedular statutory system compensating **non-economic anatomical loss, functional loss of use (`LOU`), loss of bodily integrity, housebound confinement, and personal caregiving dependency**. Under official 2026 VA compensation tables (effective December 1, 2025, veteran alone), monthly tax-free compensation climbs from **`$3,938.58/month`** at `100%` schedular (`§ 1114(j)`) to **`$6,877.12/month`** at `SMC(o)`/`(p)` and **`$11,271.67/month`** (over **`$135,260/year` tax-free** before dependency add-ons) at `SMC(r)(2)` and `SMC(t)`.

### B. Why SMC Is Mandatory & Inferred by Law (*Payne*, *Akles*, *Bradley*, *Buie*)

Unlike initial service-connection claims (`38 U.S.C. § 5101(a)`), **entitlement to SMC never requires a separate, explicit claim** once the underlying disability is before VA for adjudication:
1. ***Payne v. Derwinski*, `1 Vet. App. 85, 87 (1990)` & *Akles v. Derwinski*, `1 Vet. App. 118, 120–21 (1991)`:** Whenever a claimant seeks compensation or an increased evaluation and the medical evidence or rating percentages of record reasonably raise potential entitlement to `38 U.S.C. § 1114` (`former § 314`) / `38 C.F.R. § 3.350`, VA has an **affirmative, mandatory duty** to infer, develop, and adjudicate SMC—or provide adequate written reasons or bases (`38 U.S.C. § 7104(d)(1)`) explaining why SMC is not warranted. VA policies requiring claimants to "specify" SMC on an application form are unlawful (*Akles*, `1 Vet. App. at 121`).
2. ***Bradley v. Peake*, `22 Vet. App. 280, 294 (2008)` & *Buie v. Shinseki*, `24 Vet. App. 242, 250–51 (2010)` *(decided Mar. 23, 2010, amended Apr. 21, 2011)*:** SMC is an **ancillary evaluation issue** part and parcel of every claim for disability compensation and every duty-to-maximize audit, regardless of the chronological sequence in which individual disabilities were service-connected or increased.

### C. Piercing Finality & Effective-Date Mechanics on Omitted SMC

When an audit of historical Rating Code Sheets ([VA Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md)) reveals missed SMC, match the era and factual posture to the lowest-burden procedural vehicle ([Procedural Vehicles Piercing Finality](procedural-vehicles-piercing-finality.md), [Playbook 04](../playbooks/04-unadjudicated-tdiu-informal-claims-smc.md)):

* **1-Year Increased-Rating Lookback (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(b)(2)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.400(o)(2)`):** Because SMC is legally a rate increase on existing service-connected disabilities (*Akles*; *Bradley*), a newly filed claim or `VA Form 21-2680` establishing LOU, housebound status, or A&A takes an effective date up to **1 year prior to claim receipt** if it is factually ascertainable that the statutory criteria were met during that 1-year lookback window.
* **Mathematical / Undebatable RO or Board CUE (`38 U.S.C. §§ 5109A, 7111`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)` — `cue-and-open-claim`):** When the contemporaneous Rating Code Sheet or undisputed C&P examination *already* established every statutory element—e.g., schedular `100%` + independent disabilities combining to `>= 60%` with zero `SMC(s)` award, or `DC 7528` radical prostatectomy / `DC 5165` foot amputation with zero `SMC(k)` award—VA's omission is undebatable **CUE** yielding full decades-long retroactivity (*remembering `VAOPGCPREC 12-95`'s July 21, 1992 *Bell* constructive-possession cutoff for unassociated VAMC notes*).
* **Legacy Non-Finality (`< Feb. 19, 2019`): `§ 3.156(b)` (*Lang*) vs. Implicit Denial:** Under *Deshotel v. Nicholson*, `457 F.3d 1258 (Fed. Cir. 2006)`, a Legacy rating decision adjudicating the underlying disability may implicitly deny reasonably raised SMC if context alerted a reasonable claimant; however, if VAMC clinical records relevant to the rated condition were generated within **1 year *after*** a post-July 21, 1992 Legacy RO decision and VA never issued a new-and-material determination, **finality never attached** under `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(b)` (*Lang v. Wilkie*, `971 F.3d 1348 (Fed. Cir. 2020)`). Similarly, newly associated STRs or military clinical charts trigger full reconsideration back to the original claim under **`38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)`** ([Playbook 03](../playbooks/03-service-department-records-38-cfr-3-156c.md)).
* **AMA Explicit-Notice Non-Finality (`>= Feb. 19, 2019` — *Hamill v. Collins*, `166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. 2026)`):** Under `38 U.S.C. § 5104(b)`, the implicit-denial doctrine is **abolished** in all Appeals Modernization Act (`AMA`) decisions. Whenever an AMA rating decision grants or increases a disability while **staying completely silent** on reasonably raised `SMC(k)`, `SMC(l)`, `SMC(s)`, `SMC(t)`, or *Barry* step bumps, **that SMC issue was never denied and remains legally pending** anchored to the original AMA claim / Intent to File!

## 2. `SMC(k)` Add-On Architecture (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(k)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(a)`)

Unlike `SMC(l)` through `SMC(t)`—which replace standard `§ 1114(a)–(j)` percentage pay—**`SMC(k)` is a flat-rate monthly add-on (`$139.87/month` per award in 2026)** payable on top of `0%` through `100%` schedular/TDIU evaluations, `SMC(s)`, or `SMC(l)` through `SMC(n½)` awards.

After **`Pub. L. 107-330, § 102`** (`116 Stat. 2821`, **effective December 6, 2002**) broadened `§ 1114(k)` to cover `>= 25%` breast tissue loss or breast radiation treatment, VA **removed separate subparagraph `(a)(7)`** (`68 FR 55466, 55467`, Sept. 26, 2003, retroactive eff. Dec. 6, 2002) and placed the breast criteria directly into the **introductory paragraph of `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(a)`**, leaving numbered subparagraphs `(a)(1)` through `(a)(6)`.

### B. The `3-k` Maximum (`K-1`, `K-2`, `K-3`) & Statutory Rate Ceilings (`§ 1114(k)`)

A veteran may receive up to **three concurrent `SMC(k)` awards (`K-1`, `K-2`, `K-3` = `$419.61/month` in 2026)** for distinct qualifying losses (because four extremity losses or paired eye/limb losses automatically convert into higher `SMC(l)`–`(o)` base tiers). However, `38 U.S.C. § 1114(k)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(a)` enforce **two strict statutory rate ceilings**:

1. **Ceiling 1 — Combined with `0%–100%` (`§ 1114(a)–(j)`) or Housebound `SMC(s)` (`§ 1114(s)`): Capped at `SMC(l)` (`$4,900.83` in 2026):**
   * When `1` to `3` `SMC(k)` awards are added to a `0%–100%` schedular/TDIU rating (`$3,938.58` at `100%`) or to **`SMC(s)` (`$4,408.53`)**, total compensation **cannot exceed the `SMC(l)` rate (`$4,900.83`)**.
   * **2026 Arithmetic Check:** Even at `SMC(s)` + `3k`, `$4,408.53 + ($139.87 × 3) = $4,828.14`, which sits **`$72.69/month` below `SMC(l)` (`$4,900.83`)**—meaning **all three `SMC(k)` awards pay out 100% in full on top of both `100%` and `SMC(s)`**!
2. **Ceiling 2 — Combined with `SMC(l)` Through `SMC(n½)`: Capped at `SMC(o)` (`$6,877.12` in 2026):**
   * When `1` to `3` `SMC(k)` awards are added to a base or intermediate rate between `SMC(l)` (`$4,900.83`) and `SMC(n½)` (`$6,514.44`), total compensation **cannot exceed `SMC(o)` (`$6,877.12`)** (provided the `(k)` condition is **not** one of the conditions already used to establish the `(l)`–`(n½)` base!).
   * *(Example: `SMC(n½)` `$6,514.44` + `3k` `$419.61` = `$6,934.05`, which hits the `SMC(o)` cap and is truncated to **`$6,877.12`**; whereas `SMC(n½)` + `1k` = `$6,654.31` and `SMC(n½)` + `2k` = `$6,794.18` pay in full—and **`SMC(n½) + 1k` + Regular A&A unlocks `SMC(r)(1)` (`$9,826.88`)** under `38 U.S.C. § 1114(r)`!)*
   * **No `SMC(k)` Above `SMC(o)`:** Once a veteran reaches `SMC(o)`, maximum `SMC(p)`, `SMC(r)(1)`, `SMC(r)(2)`, or `SMC(t)`, `SMC(k)` no longer adds extra dollars (`§ 1114(k)`).

## 3. Loss of Use (`LOU`) Legal Standard (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.350(a)(2), 4.63` & *Tucker v. West*)

Thousands of veterans with intact limbs—suffering from severe diabetic/radicular peripheral neuropathy, gunshot nerve/muscle trauma, failed total joint arthroplasties, or complex regional pain syndrome (`CRPS`)—legally qualify for **`SMC(k)`** (single hand/foot) or **`SMC(l)`–`(m)`** (bilateral feet/hands) without amputation.

### A. The Functional Prosthesis-Equivalence Standard (*Tucker v. West*, `11 Vet. App. 369 (1998)`)

Under `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.350(a)(2)` and `4.63` (verbatim text in [`38-cfr-3.350-3.352-smc-evolution.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.350-3.352-smc-evolution.md)), **Loss of Use (`LOU`)** of a hand or foot exists whenever:
> *"no effective function remains other than that which would be equally well served by an amputation stump at the site of election below elbow or knee with use of a suitable prosthetic appliance."*

In ***Tucker v. West*, `11 Vet. App. 369, 372–73 (1998)`**, the CAVC reversed a Board denial that focused merely on whether the veteran retained *some* motion or weight-bearing ability without comparing that residual function to a prosthetic limb:
1. **Mandatory Comparative Inquiry:** Adjudicators and C&P examiners **cannot** deny LOU merely because a joint moves or the veteran walks short distances with a cane/AFO brace; they **must explicitly compare** actual remaining function against what a below-elbow (`hand`: **grasping, pinching, manipulation**) or below-knee (`foot`: **balance, propulsion, weight-bearing push-off**) prosthesis could accomplish (*Tucker*, `11 Vet. App. at 373–74`; and under *Payne* and *Akles*, severe stacked joint ratings—such as `DC 5257/5260/5261 + 5258/5259` knee stacking under *Lyles v. Shulkin*, `29 Vet. App. 107, 118–19 (2017)`—can reasonably raise `§ 3.350` LOU, noting under *Lyles* and `38 C.F.R. § 4.68` that once extremity LOU SMC is awarded, separate schedular musculoskeletal ratings for that same lost-use extremity's manifestations are subsumed and cannot be stacked on top!).
2. **Per Se / Presumptive LOU Rules (`38 C.F.R. § 4.63(a)–(b)`):**
   * **`§ 4.63(a)` Structural / Ankylosis Per Se LOU:** `(i)` **Extremely unfavorable complete ankylosis of the knee**, `(ii)` **complete ankylosis of two major joints** of an extremity (e.g., ankle + knee, or wrist + elbow), or `(iii)` **shortening of a lower extremity of `3½ inches (8.9 cm)` or more** **must** be taken as LOU of the hand or foot!
   * **`§ 4.63(b)` Common Peroneal Nerve / Foot-Drop Per Se LOU:** **Complete paralysis of the external popliteal (common peroneal) nerve (`DC 8521`)** with consequent **foot drop**, accompanied by characteristic organic changes including **trophic and circulatory disturbances** (skin thinning/ulceration, hair loss, temperature/color changes, muscle atrophy), **must** be taken as LOU of the foot! *(Crucial nuance: Even without trophic changes or complete peroneal nerve severance, functional inability to balance and propel still establishes LOU under the general *Tucker* comparative test!)*
3. **Upper-Tier LOU Escalators (`SMC(m)` & `SMC(n)`):**
   * **Preventing Natural Knee or Elbow Action (`§ 1114(m)`; `§ 3.350(c)(1)(ii)–(iii)`):** When LOU extends above the foot/hand to involve knee or elbow pathology (or above-knee / above-elbow amputation) such that natural knee/elbow action would be prevented with prostheses in place, bilateral leg LOU jumps from **`SMC(l)` (`$4,900.83`)** to **`SMC(m)` (`$5,408.55`)**!
   * **Amputation Prerequisite Exception at `SMC(n)` (`§ 1114(n)`; `§ 3.350(d)`):** At `SMC(n)` (`$6,152.64`), actual **anatomical amputation** so near the hip/shoulder as to prevent prosthetic use is required for *lower* extremities (`§ 3.350(d)(2)–(3)`—with a strict caveat that if reamputation at a higher level would allow a prosthesis, `(n)` is not met), **EXCEPT** that **functional LOU of both arms** preventing natural elbow action (`§ 3.350(d)(1)`) and **blindness without light perception in both eyes** (`§ 3.350(d)(4)`) achieve **`SMC(n)` without amputation**!

### B. Anatomical Loss vs. Preexisting Non-Service-Connected Paralysis ([`VAOPGCPREC 30-97`](vaopgcprec-index.md))

Under binding **`VAOPGCPREC 30-97`** (`62 FR 63604`, Dec. 1, 1997), `38 U.S.C. § 1114(l)–(n)` states **"anatomical loss *or* loss of use"** in the disjunctive. Therefore, where a veteran with **preexisting non-service-connected (`NSC`) paraplegia** (who already lacked lower-extremity function) subsequently suffers bilateral hip disarticulation compensable under `38 U.S.C. § 1151` (or service connection), the veteran is **unconditionally entitled to `SMC(n)`** based on **anatomical loss**, regardless of prior NSC loss of use!

### B. *Barry v. McDonough* (`101 F.4th 1348 (Fed. Cir. 2024)`) & Iterative `§ 3.350(f)(3)–(5)` Step Math

For decades prior to May 16, 2024, VA's `M21-1` manual unlawfully capped `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(f)(3)` and `(f)(4)` at **one single bump**, no matter how many additional severe `50%+` or `100%` disabilities a catastrophically disabled veteran suffered. In ***Barry v. McDonough*, `101 F.4th 1348, 1353–56 (Fed. Cir. May 16, 2024)`**, the Federal Circuit reversed the CAVC and held that the unambiguous text of `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(f)(3)` (and parallel `(f)(4)`) **mandates a cumulative step increase for *each* separate, independent qualifying disability or combination**, constrained only by the statutory `SMC(o)` / `§ 1114(p)` dollar ceiling (`$6,877.12` in 2026)!

#### Exact Mechanics of the Five `§ 3.350(f)` Step Rules:
1. **`§ 3.350(f)(1)` & `(f)(2)` (Anatomical / Sensory Intermediates):** Specific asymmetric limb-level combinations (e.g., `1` foot + `1` leg preventing knee action = `SMC(l½)`) and bilateral blindness + unilateral/bilateral deafness steps (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(p)`).
2. **`§ 3.350(f)(3)` — Half-Step Bump (`+½ Step` per Independent `>= 50%` Single or Combined Disability):**
   * **Trigger:** Each additional single permanent SC disability—**or `38 C.F.R. § 4.25` combination** of permanent SC disabilities (e.g., `30% + 30% = 51% -> 50%`!)—independently ratable at **`50%` or more** (and < `100%`), separate and distinct from the base `SMC(l)–(n)` disability(ies).
   * **Step Effect:** Advances `(l) -> (l½) -> (m) -> (m½) -> (n) -> (n½) -> (o)` (`+1` half-step per independent `50%+` unit!).
   * **Non-Consumption Packing Rule:** Never waste two separate `50%` disabilities by combining them together into one `80%` unit (`1` half-step); under *Barry* and VA's duty to maximize (*Buie*), **each standalone `50%`, `60%`, `70%`, or `80%` permanent disability triggers its own separate `+½` step**, while leftover `<50%` permanent ratings (`40%`, `30%`, `20%`, `10%`) combine via `§ 4.25` (and `§ 4.26` bilateral factor if paired!) to form additional `>= 50%` half-step bundles!
3. **`§ 3.350(f)(4)` — Full-Step Bump (`+1 Full Step` per Independent `100%` Schedular Disability):**
   * **Trigger:** Each additional single permanent SC disability independently ratable at **`100%` schedular** (*explicitly `"apart from any consideration of individual unemployability"`—**TDIU (`§ 4.16`) CANNOT trigger `(f)(4)`**!*), separate and distinct from the base `SMC(l)–(n)` disability(ies).
   * **Step Effect:** Advances a full statutory tier—e.g., `(l) -> (m) -> (n) -> (o)` or `(l½) -> (m½) -> (n½) -> (o)`!
4. **`§ 3.350(f)(5)` — Three-Extremity Bump:** Anatomical loss or LOU of **three extremities** entitles the veteran to the next higher rate (whether statutory or intermediate), capped at `§ 1114(p)` (`$6,877.12`).

### C. Anti-Pyramiding & The Double-Counting Bar (*Amberman* & *Breniser*)

Both `38 C.F.R. § 4.14` (general VASRD anti-pyramiding) and the explicit text of `38 U.S.C. § 1114(o)` (`"no condition being considered twice"`) and `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(f)(3)–(4)` (`"separate and distinct and involve different anatomical segments or bodily systems from the conditions establishing entitlement under 38 U.S.C. 1114(l) through (n)"`) strictly prohibit **double-counting the same disability or functional manifestation**:

1. ***Amberman v. Shinseki*, `570 F.3d 1377, 1380–81 (Fed. Cir. 2009)`:** Under `38 C.F.R. § 4.14`, two defined diagnoses cannot be compensated twice if they share overlapping symptomatology/manifestations; anti-pyramiding focuses on **functional manifestations**, not diagnostic labels.
2. ***Breniser v. Shinseki*, `25 Vet. App. 64, 74–77 (2011)`:** Landmark CAVC precedent defining the boundary between `SMC(l)` and `SMC(o)`/`SMC(r)(1)`:
   * **The Illegal Double-Count (*Breniser* Trap):** Suppose a veteran has bilateral lower-extremity LOU (`SMC(l)` under `§ 1114(l)`), and *because of that exact same bilateral lower-extremity paralysis*, cannot dress, bathe, or transfer without Regular Aid & Attendance. The veteran **cannot** claim a *second* `SMC(l)` rate for A&A under `§ 3.350(e)(1)(ii)` to jump to `SMC(o)` and `SMC(r)(1)`—nor can the veteran use the underlying `40%–80%` lower-extremity radiculopathy/amputation ratings a second time to fuel a `§ 3.350(f)(3)–(4)` step bump! Once lower-extremity LOU establishes base `SMC(l)`, those lower-extremity ratings are **consumed**.
   * **The Lawful Dual-Rate Unlock (`SMC(o)` -> `SMC(r)(1)`):** By contrast, if the veteran has `(1)` bilateral lower-extremity LOU (`SMC(l)` #1) **AND** `(2)` **either** loss of anal and bladder sphincter control (`38 C.F.R. § 3.350(e)(2)` `Code O-2`), **OR** an **independent need for Regular A&A (`SMC(l)` #2) caused *solely* by a separate, distinct SC disability** (e.g., SC Parkinson's upper-limb tremor/dementia, SC `100%` PTSD/cognitive hazard incapacity, or SC blind/cardiopulmonary helplessness wholly independent of the legs), *"no condition [is] considered twice"*—entitling the veteran to **`SMC(o)` (`§ 3.350(e)(1)(ii)`) + A&A = `SMC(r)(1)` (`$9,826.88/mo`)**!

### D. Bitemporal *George v. McDonough* Gate on *Barry* (`open-claim-only-george-restricted` for Pre-May 16, 2024 CUE)

Under ***George v. McDonough*, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)`** and **`38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`** ([`VAOPGCPREC 25-95`](vaopgcprec-index.md)), a judicial decision overturning VA's longstanding regulatory interpretation **cannot** establish CUE against a prior **final** RO or Board decision rendered before May 16, 2024 (`open-claim-only-george-restricted`). Consequently:
* **Final Pre-May 16, 2024 Decisions:** Do **not** waste a one-shot Board CUE motion (`§ 20.1409(c)`, see [`Board CUE Pre-Filing Gate`](../checklists/board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md)) attacking a final 2015 single-step award solely under *Barry*. Instead, screen aggressively for **open-claim / non-finality vehicles**—`§ 3.156(b)` *Lang* 1-year post-decision VAMC records, `§ 3.156(c)` newly associated military clinical/STR records, AMA `§ 5104(b)` *Hamill* silent omissions (`>= Feb. 19, 2019`), or pending Legacy/AMA appeals—where ***Barry* applies retroactively across the entire open period**!
* **Post-May 16, 2024 Decisions & New Supplemental Claims:** Any decision issued **on or after May 16, 2024** that caps `(f)(3)`/`(f)(4)` at a single bump commits **reversible error (and CUE if final)**; for closed pre-2024 files with no finality exception, file an immediate `VA Form 20-0995` / increase claim invoking `§ 5110(b)(2)`'s 1-year lookback where worsening occurred.

## 5. Housebound `SMC(s)` (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(s)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(i)`)

`SMC(s)` (`$4,408.53/month` in 2026—**`+$469.95/month` (`+$5,639/year`)** above `100%`) requires **first** a **single** service-connected disability rated as total (`100%`), **plus** **either** Statutory `60%` (`§ 1114(s)(1)`) **or** Factual Housebound (`§ 1114(s)(2)`).

### A. Prong 1 (`§ 1114(s)(1)` & `§ 3.350(i)(1)`): Statutory `"Total + 60%"` Housebound

*(Note: Despite its nickname, a veteran qualifying under `§ 1114(s)(1)` **need NOT be confined to the home**—he or she may travel freely!)*
1. **Single-Disability TDIU Satisfies the `"Rated as Total"` Prong (*Bradley v. Peake*, `22 Vet. App. 280 (2008)`):**
   * Invalidating [`VAOPGCPREC 6-99`](vaopgcprec-index.md) (formally **withdrawn at `75 FR 11229`, Mar. 10, 2010**), *Bradley* held that **`38 C.F.R. § 4.16` TDIU attributable *solely* to ONE single service-connected disability** satisfies `"a service-connected disability rated as total"` under `§ 1114(s)`, provided remaining separate SC disabilities independently combine to `>= 60%`!
   * **The Multi-Disability TDIU Bar (*Buie*, `24 Vet. App. at 250–51`; *Gazelle v. Shulkin*, `868 F.3d 1006 (Fed. Cir. 2017)`):** Conversely, a TDIU grant predicated on the combined effect of *multiple* SC disabilities **cannot** satisfy the single-total-disability prong—**unless** medical/vocational development (or an `M21-1` *Bradley* audit under *Buie*'s duty to maximize) proves that **one single SC condition (`e.g., 70% PTSD` alone) independently caused unemployability** while the remaining SC conditions independently combine to `>= 60%`!
   * **Bitemporal CUE Guardrail:** Pure mathematical omission of `SMC(s)` on a **schedular `100% + 60%`** Code Sheet is **`cue-and-open-claim` safe back to 1960** (`Pub. L. 86-663`). By contrast, attacking a final **June 7, 1999 – Nov. 25, 2008** RO/Board denial of *TDIU-based* `SMC(s)` via CUE is **barred by `VAOPGCPREC 6-99` + *George v. McDonough*** (`open-claim-only-george-restricted`).
2. **Combined-Table Math & Separate Bodily Systems (*Gazelle v. Shulkin*, `868 F.3d 1006, 1010–12 (Fed. Cir. 2017)`):**
   * Under *Gazelle*, multiple separate disabilities under `§ 1114(s)(1)` must combine to **`60%` or more under VA's `38 C.F.R. § 4.25` Combined Ratings Table** (including the `§ 4.26` bilateral factor if paired independent extremities are present—e.g., `50% + 20% = 60%`, whereas `30% + 30% = 51% -> 50%` fails, **not** `30 + 30 = 60` arithmetic addition!).
   * Under `§ 3.350(i)(1)`, the `60%+` disabilities must be separate and distinct from the `100%` disability (note: a temporary `100%` surgical convalescent or active-cancer rating under `§§ 4.29, 4.30` or `DC 7528` **does** trigger temporary statutory `SMC(s)` during every month the `100%` + `60%` ratings run concurrently!).

### B. Prong 2 (`§ 1114(s)(2)` & `§ 3.350(i)(2)`): Factual `"Permanently Housebound"`

When a veteran has a single `100%` schedular (or single-disability TDIU) evaluation **without** `60%` in additional ratings—or where all disabling manifestations stem from that single `100%` condition (e.g., severe agoraphobic `100%` PTSD, severe `100%` COPD/heart failure, or `100%` ALS/MS not yet needing A&A)—`§ 1114(s)(2)` awards `SMC(s)` if the veteran is **"permanently housebound"** (`substantially confined as a direct result of service-connected disabilities to his or her dwelling and the immediate premises, or, if institutionalized, to the ward or clinical areas`, reasonably certain to continue for life).
* ***Howell v. Nicholson*, `19 Vet. App. 535, 540 (2006)`:** Reversing VA's hyper-literal reading, the CAVC held that **"leaving one's house for medical purposes cannot, by itself, serve as the basis for finding that one is not substantially confined"** under `§ 1114(s)(2)`! Document on `VA Form 21-2680` and lay declarations that the veteran leaves the home/yard *only* for medical treatment or only with extraordinary third-party transport/escort.
