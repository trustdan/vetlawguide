# Master Chronological Matrix of Veterans Law (1945–2026)

Because both **Clear and Unmistakable Error (CUE)** and **Historical Pending Claim** analyses require applying the exact statutory and `38 C.F.R.` text in force on the date of the rating action, use this chronological matrix to anchor every C-file audit to the **Law of the Era**.

---

## Era-by-Era Master Matrix

| Era / Date | Statutory or CFR Change | Key Precedent / Authority | What Changed | Retroactive Trap vs. Opportunity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Apr 1, 1946** | **1945 Edition of the VASRD** took effect | Veterans' Reg. No. 1(a); `38 C.F.R. § 3.952`; `Part 4, App. A` | Established modern 4-digit Diagnostic Code (DC) architecture, muscle group classifications (Groups I–XXIII), and qualitative muscle/scar/orthopedic standards. | **Opportunity:** Pre-1997 muscle wound rules (`§§ 4.47–4.56`) treated through-and-through gunshot/shrapnel wounds generously; raters routinely misrated them as `0%` scars (`DC 7800–7805`). |
| **Sept 2, 1958** *(eff. Jan 1, 1959)* | **Pub. L. 85-857** — Codification of Title 38 U.S.C. & Savings Clauses | `72 Stat. 1105`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.953, 3.957(c)` | Consolidated scattered veterans' statutes into positive law as **Title 38, U.S. Code** and enacted a statutory savings protection for compensation payable on **Dec. 31, 1957**. | **Trap & Shield:** Pre-1959 decisions cite Veterans' Regulations (e.g., *Vet. Reg. 1(a)*); meanwhile, `§§ 3.953 / 3.957(c)` bar severing compensation payable on Dec. 31, 1957 absent fraud, material misrepresentation, or CUE! |
| **Oct 15, 1962** *(eff. Dec 1, 1962)* | **Pub. L. 87-825** — Effective Date & Finality Overhaul | `38 U.S.C. § 3010` (now `§ 5110`); `38 C.F.R. § 3.400` | Standardized effective-date ceilings, 1-year post-discharge filing rules (`§ 5110(b)(1)`), 1-year post-increase factual ascertainability (`§ 5110(b)(2)`), and formal BVA SOC/appeal procedures (`Pub. L. 87-666`). | **Opportunity:** Introduced Statement of the Case (SOC) mandatory requirement; if a veteran filed a timely NOD post-1962 and VA never issued an SOC, the appeal **remained legally pending for decades**! |
| **1962–1988** | **Broad Informal Claim & Hospital Report Era** | `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.155`, `3.157(b)` | Any written communication showing intent to claim benefits operated as an informal claim; VAMC/military hospital admissions and VA outpatient exam reports **automatically** constituted informal claims for increase (`§ 3.157(b)`) once SC was established. | **Goldmine Opportunity:** A `0%` SC rating from 1968 followed by a 1975 VAMC surgical/clinic note showing worsening triggered a legally pending increased-rating claim in 1975—no form required! |
| **Sept 25, 1985 / May 3, 1989** | ***Nehmer* Class Action & Stipulation** | *Nehmer v. U.S. Dept. of Veterans Affairs*, CV-86-6160; `38 C.F.R. § 3.816` | Voided prior denials under unlawful dioxin regulation (`38 C.F.R. § 3.311a`) and created binding retroactive effective-date rules for Agent Orange presumptive conditions added later. | **Goldmine Opportunity:** Unlike standard claims, *Nehmer* overrides finality *and* `§ 5110`—paying back to the original post-Sept 25, 1985 claim (or date disability arose) even if a different code/diagnosis label appeared on an earlier claim that documented the disease! |
| **Nov 18, 1988** | **Veterans' Judicial Review Act (VJRA)**, Pub. L. 100-687 | Created COVA/CAVC (`38 U.S.C. ch. 72`); amended `§ 5904` (attorney fees) & `§ 7104(d)(1)` | Ended 100+ years of "splendid isolation"; required written BVA "reasons or bases"; opened Federal Circuit review; introduced the short-lived **"well-grounded claim"** gatekeeper (`38 U.S.C. § 5107(a)` 1988–2000). | **Trap:** Between 1989 and Nov 2000, VA lawfully dismissed claims as "not well grounded" (*Grottveit* / *Caluza*) without providing a C&P exam—attacking a 1995 non-well-grounded denial as CUE for lack of a VA exam fails! |
| **Oct 1990 – Oct 1992** | **Foundational CAVC Precedent Wave** | *Gilbert* (1990); *Akles* (1991); *Ashley* (1992); *Russell* (1992) | Defined equipoise (`§ 5107(b)`), mandatory statutory SMC consideration, strict mailing regularity rules, and the 3-prong CUE standard. | **Opportunity:** Established judicial enforceability of `§ 3.105(a)` RO CUE reaching back to 1945+ decisions, even though BVA CUE did not yet exist (*Smith v. Brown*, 35 F.3d 1516 (Fed. Cir. 1994)). |
| **July 21, 1992** | **Constructive Possession Boundary** | *Bell v. Derwinski*, 2 Vet. App. 611 (1992); `VAOPGCPREC 12-95` | Held VA has constructive knowledge of VA-generated medical records in existence at the time of adjudication. | **Critical Temporal Cutoff:** *Bell* constructive possession applies to CUE attacks on decisions rendered **on or after July 21, 1992**, **NOT** to decisions rendered before July 21, 1992! |
| **Nov 7, 1996** | **VASRD Mental Disorders Overhaul** | `61 FR 52695`; `38 C.F.R. § 4.130` (`DC 9400–9440`) | Transitioned from DSM-III / subjective industrial impairment terms ("definite", "considerable", "severe") to DSM-IV nomenclature and unified General Rating Formula for Mental Disorders (`0/10/30/50/70/100%`). | **Trap:** Pre-Nov 7, 1996 PTSD/psychiatric ratings used `0/10/30/50/70/100%` based on "definite/considerable/severe social and industrial adaptability impairment"—never cite modern `§ 4.130` symptom lists for a 1991 CUE! |
| **July 3, 1997** | **VASRD Muscle Injuries Overhaul** | `62 FR 30235`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 4.55, 4.56, 4.73` (`DC 5301–5323`) | Modernized evaluation of muscle injuries into objective cardinal signs/symptoms, retained foreign bodies, fascia defects, and wound mechanism criteria. | **Opportunity & Trap:** Pre-July 3, 1997 `§ 4.56` had distinct phrasing on through-and-through wounds and muscle damage; verify whether the RO rated before or after July 3, 1997! |
| **Nov 21, 1997** | **Revision of Decisions on Grounds of CUE Act**, Pub. L. 105-111 | Codified `38 U.S.C. § 5109A` (RO CUE) & `§ 7111` (BVA CUE); `38 C.F.R. § 20.1400` | Overturned *Smith v. Brown* (which had barred CUE attacks on BVA decisions) by creating statutory BVA CUE review, while codifying RO CUE by statute. | **Trap:** `38 C.F.R. § 20.1409(c)` gives claimants **only ONE merits CUE motion per Board decision issue**—never file a half-baked BVA CUE motion! |
| **Nov 9, 2000** | **Veterans Claims Assistance Act (VCAA)**, Pub. L. 106-475 | `38 U.S.C. §§ 5103, 5103A`; eliminated "well-grounded claim" | Abolished the well-grounded claim requirement; created statutory notice (`§ 5103(a)`) and duty-to-assist (`§ 5103A`, including medical nexus exams under *McLendon*). | **Trap:** Under *Cook v. Principi* (2002), VCAA duty-to-assist violations **cannot** support CUE once a decision becomes final. |
| **May 2001 – Sept 2009** | **Sympathetic Reading & TDIU Integration** | *Roberson v. Principi* (2001); *Rice v. Shinseki* (2009) | Established that TDIU (`§ 4.16`) is not a separate standalone claim when raised by evidence of unemployability during an initial or increased-rating claim, and mandated sympathetic reading of pro se filings. | **Goldmine Opportunity:** Unadjudicated reasonably raised TDIU claims embedded inside pre-2015 rating claims! |
| **Aug 30, 2002 & Sept 23, 2002** | **Interim Scars (`DC 7800–7805`) & Spine IVDS (`DC 5293`) Revisions** | `67 FR 49590` (Scars); `67 FR 54345` (IVDS) | Replaced subjective burn/scar area phrasing with exact `sq. in. / sq. cm.` thresholds and transitioned IVDS (`DC 5293`) to incapacitating-episode duration counts one year before the 2003 full spine overhaul. | **Temporal Trap:** Rating decisions between Aug. 30, 2002 and Oct. 22, 2008 (`DC 7800–7805`) or Sept. 23, 2002 and Sept. 25, 2003 (`DC 5293`) sit in distinct interim CFR windows—check [`authorities/`](../authorities/README.md)! |
| **Sept 26, 2003** | **VASRD Spine Overhaul** | `68 FR 51454`; `38 C.F.R. § 4.71a` (`DC 5235–5243`) | Replaced subjective "slight / moderate / severe limitation of motion" spine ratings and ankylosis rules with goniometer **Range of Motion (ROM)** degrees + Note (1) separate neurologic/radiculopathy ratings. | **Opportunity:** Pre-2003 spine ratings rarely separated radiculopathy properly; post-2003 Note (1) mandates separate ratings for objective neurologic abnormalities (e.g., sciatic/femoral radiculopathy). |
| **Oct 6, 2006** *(2005–2006 Rulemaking)* | **Modernization of `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)`** | `71 FR 52455`; *Vigil v. Peake* (2008); *Blubaugh* (2014) | Clarified mandatory reconsideration and retroactive effective dates (`§ 3.156(c)(3)`) back to the original claim whenever newly associated official service department records form part of the grant. | **Goldmine Opportunity:** Unlocks 20–50 years of back pay when JSRRC, deck logs, morning reports, or missing inpatient STRs arrive decades after a denial—without proving CUE! |
| **Oct 23, 2008** | **VASRD Scars & Disfigurement Overhaul** | `73 FR 54708`; `38 C.F.R. § 4.118` (`DC 7800–7805`) | Overhauled head/face/neck disfigurement (`DC 7800`, 8 characteristics of disfigurement) and allowed stacking separate `10%/20%/30%` ratings under `DC 7804` based on the **count of painful or unstable scars**. | **Trap:** Pre-Oct 23, 2008 `DC 7804` awarded a single `10%` regardless of whether one or five scars were painful/unstable (*Sharp* / pre-2008 text); post-2008 counts individual painful/unstable scars! |
| **Aug 4, 2014** | **DSM-5 Adoption in `38 C.F.R. §§ 4.125, 4.130`** | `79 FR 45093` | Updated mental health nomenclature from DSM-IV (removing GAF scores) to DSM-5 while retaining the core `0/10/30/50/70/100%` occupational/social impairment tiers. | **Note:** Claims pending pre-Aug 4, 2014 vs. certified to BVA after Aug 4, 2014 follow transitional applicability rules in the preamble. |
| **Mar 24, 2015** | **Standardized Forms & Death of Informal Claims** | `79 FR 57660`; revised `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.155, 3.160`; **repealed `§ 3.157(b)`** | Eliminated informal claims and `§ 3.157(b)` VAMC report auto-claims; required standardized forms (`21-526EZ`, `21-0966` Intent to File). | **Hard Temporal Wall:** After **March 24, 2015**, VAMC treatment notes and non-form letters no longer initiate claims (*Shea v. Wilkie* applies to pre-3/24/2015 informal claims referencing medical records; *Sellers v. Wilkie* governs post-2015 forms). |
| **Aug 13, 2018** | **VASRD Skin & Scars Revision** | `83 FR 32592`; `38 C.F.R. § 4.118` | Updated systemic therapy tiers for skin conditions and clarified `DC 7801/7802/7804/7805` nonlinear vs. linear/functional scar interactions. | **Opportunity:** Systemic biologics/immunosuppressives unlocked much higher skin evaluations post-Aug 13, 2018. |
| **Feb 19, 2019** | **Appeals Modernization Act (AMA) Effective Date** | Pub. L. 115-55; `38 U.S.C. §§ 5104(b), 5104C, 5110(a)(2)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.2500` | Replaced Legacy NOD/SOC/Form 9 system with 3 lanes (Supplemental Claim, Higher-Level Review, Board Direct/Evidence/Hearing) and 1-year **continuous pursuit** rules preserving effective dates across lanes. | **Opportunity:** Continuous pursuit (`§ 3.2500(c),(h)`) keeps the original effective date alive indefinitely as long as a Supplemental Claim (with new and relevant evidence) or appeal is filed within 1 year of each decision, including within 1 year of BVA denials (though NOT within 1 year of CAVC affirmances—*see § 3.2500*!). |
| **Aug 19, 2020** | ***Lang v. Wilkie* (`38 C.F.R. § 3.156(b)` VAMC Finality Abatement)** | `971 F.3d 1348 (Fed. Cir. 2020)`; *Beraud v. McDonald*, `766 F.3d 1402 (Fed. Cir. 2014)` | Held that VAMC medical records generated within **1 year *after*** a post-July 21, 1992 Legacy RO decision are constructively received under `§ 3.156(b)`, abating finality unless VA determined whether they were new and material. | **Goldmine Opportunity:** Turns "final" 1993–2018 Legacy denials/low ratings into **still-pending open claims** whenever the veteran had VAMC treatment for that condition within 365 days after the decision! |
| **Feb 7, 2021** | **VASRD Musculoskeletal Update** | `85 FR 76453`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 4.71a, 4.73` | Replaced subjective knee instability grades with assistive-device/surgical rules (`DC 5257`), updated joint replacement convalescence/minimums, added `DC 5285` plantar fasciitis, and added `DC 5331` Compartment Syndrome (separate rating per affected muscle group). | **Trap & Opportunity:** Post-Feb 7, 2021 `DC 5257` requires prescribed assistive devices/bracing for higher knee instability tiers, while `DC 5331` allows separate muscle-group ratings. |
| **Aug 10, 2022** | **Honoring our PACT Act of 2022** | Pub. L. 117-168; `38 U.S.C. §§ 1116, 1119, 1120, 1168` | Expanded Agent Orange locations (Thailand, Laos, Cambodia, Guam, American Samoa, Johnston Atoll), added hypertension & MGUS to herbicide presumptions, created Gulf War/Post-9/11 burn pit presumptions + TERA exams. | **Distinction:** `M21-1 VIII.i.2.B.1.c` excludes hypertension & MGUS from `§ 3.816` *Nehmer* retroactivity; audit PACT grants for VA OIG's documented error of missing pre-Aug. 14, 2023 ITFs and `§ 3.114` 1-year lookbacks! |
| **Apr 16, 2023** | **Bilateral Factor Exclusion Rule (`38 C.F.R. § 4.26(d)`)** | `88 FR 22914` *(pub. Apr. 14, 2023)* | Amended `§ 4.26` so bilateral disabilities are excluded from the `10%` bilateral factor calculation whenever including them counter-intuitively rounds a veteran down from `100%` to `90%`. | **Math Check:** Prevents the notorious `94.5% vs. 95%` bilateral math penalty on or after April 16, 2023. |
| **May 16, 2024** | ***Barry v. McDonough* (`38 C.F.R. § 3.350(f)` Multi-Step SMC)** | `101 F.4th 1348 (Fed. Cir. 2024)` | Overturned VA's single-step ceiling on `§ 3.350(f)(3)–(4)`, allowing veterans at base `SMC(l)–(n)` to stack **multiple** half-step and full-step bumps (up to `SMC(o)`) for each independent `50%` or `100%` disability. | **Opportunity & CUE Guard:** Massive retroactive upside on open/pending SMC claims; pre-May 2024 final decisions face `§ 20.1403(e)` / *George* change-in-interpretation defenses if attacked solely via CUE. |
| **May 19, 2024** | **VASRD Digestive System Overhaul** | `89 FR 19735`; `38 C.F.R. § 4.114` (`DC 7206` GERD, `DC 7319` IBS) | Created standalone `DC 7206` for GERD (tied to esophageal stricture/dysphagia rather than hiatal hernia analogy `DC 7346`) and updated `DC 7319` IBS to Rome IV frequency metrics (`10%/20%/30%`). | **Trap & Protection:** Post-May 19, 2024 GERD without stricture often rates `0%` even with severe reflux—existing pre-May 19, 2024 `DC 7346` GERD ratings are protected against schedule-change reduction under `38 U.S.C. § 1155` / `38 C.F.R. § 3.951(a)`! |
| **June 12, 2025** | ***Soto v. United States* (SCOTUS)** | `605 U.S. 360 (2025)`; DoD Aug. 20, 2025 Guidance | Unanimously held that the Title 10 CRSC statute (`10 U.S.C. § 1413a`) contains its own settlement authority, **displacing the 6-year Barring Act (`31 U.S.C. § 3702`) cap** on retroactive CRSC back pay. | **Cross-System Multiplier (Title 10 DoD):** Uncaps DFAS retroactive CRSC payouts back to entitlement/retirement (with Court of Federal Claims Tucker Act track for >\$10,000 disputes, e.g., *Carey*)! |
| **Feb 4–5, 2026** | ***Hamill v. Collins* & *Cash v. Collins* (Fed. Cir.)** | `166 F.4th 1030` (`No. 24-1543`); `No. 24-1811` | *Hamill* abolished **implicit denial for AMA decisions** (`>= Feb. 19, 2019`) under `38 U.S.C. § 5104(b)`; *Cash* required Board consideration under `§ 7113(c)` of AMA "in-between" evidence already in VA's possession when identified in a Board NOD. | **Major Opportunity:** Any claim or reasonably raised issue omitted from a post-Feb 19, 2019 AMA decision was **never denied** and **remains legally pending**! |
| **Feb 17–27, 2026** | **Rescinded `38 C.F.R. § 4.10` Medication IFR** | `91 FR 7118`, **rescinded `91 FR 9712`**; *Ingram v. Collins*, `38 Vet. App. 130 (2025)` | VA briefly issued an IFR attempting to overturn *Ingram* by discounting ameliorative medication effects, then **rescinded it 10 days later (`91 FR 9712`)**. | **Audit Opportunity:** Under *Ingram*, VA cannot lower ratings for medication relief unless the DC explicitly says so; audit any rating issued Feb. 17–27, 2026 (or earlier/later) that discounted medicated symptoms! |
| **Mar–Apr 2026** | ***Freund v. Collins* CAVC Class Action** | `No. 21-4168 (Vet. App. class certified Mar. 2026)` | Certified class of ~28,000+ legacy appeals automatically closed by VACOLS sweeps (Dec. 12, 1990 – Feb. 6, 2025) with proposed VA readjudication settlement. | **Opportunity:** Audit legacy C-files where a timely NOD/appeal vanished in VACOLS without Board adjudication! |
| **Through Late 2026** | **Unfinalized `AQ72`, `AQ82` & `DC 7804` (`90 FR 46542`) Proposals** | `RIN 2900-AQ72`; `RIN 2900-AQ82`; `90 FR 46542` (Sept. 29, 2025) | Proposed eliminating standalone `10%` tinnitus (`DC 6260`), ending automatic `50%` CPAP sleep apnea (`DC 6847`), restructuring `§ 4.130` mental health, and adding an objective-pain-on-examination requirement to `DC 7804` Scars. | **Status Check:** **NOT ENACTED / NOT FINALIZED** as of late 2026—current `DC 6847` (`50%` CPAP), `DC 6260` (`10%` tinnitus), `§ 4.130`, and subjective/reported painful scar criteria under `DC 7804` remain active law! |
