# Three-Era Title 38 U.S.C. Statutory Crosswalk (`1933–Present`)

Positive statutory text stands at **Tier 1** of the Veterans Law hierarchy of authorities ([authority-verification-log.md](authority-verification-log.md)). When screening a multi-decade C-file or drafting a Clear and Unmistakable Error (`CUE`) motion under `38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)` or `38 C.F.R. § 20.1404(b)`, practitioners routinely encounter obsolete statutory citations across **three distinct codification eras**:

1. **Era A (`March 20, 1933 – December 31, 1958`):** Executive `Veterans' Regulation Nos. 1(a)–12` promulgated under the Economy Act of 1933 (`Pub. L. 73-2`) and WWII readjustment acts (`Pub. L. 78-144`, `Pub. L. 78-346`).
2. **Era B (`January 1, 1959 – May 6 / August 5, 1991`):** Positive-law enactment of `Title 38, United States Code` (`Pub. L. 85-857`, `72 Stat. 1105`), employing legacy three- and four-digit section numbers (`§§ 101–5228`) cited throughout all `1959–1991` Regional Office (`RO`), Board (`BVA`), and early Court of Veterans Appeals (`COVA`) decisions.
3. **Era C (`May 7 / August 6, 1991 – Present`):** Modern chapter-aligned four-digit `Title 38 U.S.C.` numbering enacted via `Pub. L. 102-40` (`May 7, 1991`) and `Pub. L. 102-83` (`August 6, 1991`), augmented by the `1997 CUE Act`, `2000 VCAA`, `2008 VBIA`, `2017/2019 AMA`, and `2022 PACT Act`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why Exact Era Citations Matter in CUE & Pending-Claim Litigation:** Under `38 C.F.R. § 20.1404(b)`, a Board CUE motion is subject to dismissal if it fails to identify the governing statutory or regulatory provision **extant on the date of the challenged decision** ([board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md](../checklists/board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md)). Always cite **both** the contemporaneous statutory citation and its modern bracketed equivalent—e.g., `former 38 U.S.C. § 314(s) (1976) [now 38 U.S.C. § 1114(s)]` or `Veterans' Regulation No. 1(a), Part I, par. I(a) [now 38 U.S.C. § 1110]`. Pair this statutory crosswalk with the [Master Chronological Matrix](master-chronological-matrix.md), [Procedural Vehicles for Piercing Finality](procedural-vehicles-piercing-finality.md), [VA Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md), and the [Master Binding `VAOPGCPREC` Digest](vaopgcprec-index.md).

## 1. Era A (`1933–1958`): Economy Act, `Veterans' Regulations Nos. 1(a)–12` & WWII Readjustment Era

### A. Statutory Architecture (`Pub. L. 73-2` Through `Pub. L. 85-56`)
* **Economy Act of March 20, 1933 (`Pub. L. 73-2`, Title I, `48 Stat. 8`):** Repealed the patchwork World War Veterans' Act of 1924 compensation tables and delegated sweeping authority to President Franklin D. Roosevelt to govern veterans' entitlement, rating schedules, effective dates, hospitalization, and definitions via numbered executive **Veterans' Regulations** (historically printed in `38 U.S.C.A. ch. 12A` prior to 1959).
* **1934 & WWII Restorations (`Pub. L. 73-141`, `Pub. L. 78-144`, `Pub. L. 78-346`):**
  * **Independent Offices Appropriation Act of 1934 (`Pub. L. 73-141`, `48 Stat. 509, 524`, Mar. 28, 1934):** Restored WWI presumptive service connection protections cut by the 1933 Economy Act.
  * **Act of December 20, 1941 (`Pub. L. 77-361`, `55 Stat. 847`):** Enacted strong combat evidentiary relaxation rules—the statutory ancestor of modern **`38 U.S.C. § 1154(b)`**!
  * **Act of July 13, 1943 (`Pub. L. 78-144`, `57 Stat. 554`):** Modernized presumption of soundness, line-of-duty, and wartime compensation standards in `Veterans' Regulation No. 1(a)` for WWII service ([1945–1961 Era Lens](../eras/1945-1961-post-wwii-korean-era.md)).
  * **Vocational Rehabilitation Act of 1943 (`Pub. L. 78-16`, `57 Stat. 43`, Mar. 24, 1943):** Added **Part VII** to `Veterans' Regulation No. 1(a)` (ancestor of Chapter 31 VR&E).
  * **Servicemen's Readjustment Act of 1944 ("G.I. Bill of Rights," `Pub. L. 78-346`, `58 Stat. 284`, June 22, 1944):** Added **Part VIII** to `Veterans' Regulation No. 1(a)` (education/training), created Boards of Review for Discharges and Dismissals (`§ 301`, ancestor of `10 U.S.C. § 1553` and `38 U.S.C. § 5303(b)`), and codified character-of-discharge bars (`§ 300`, ancestor of `38 U.S.C. § 5303(a)`).
  * **Veterans' Benefits Act of 1957 (`Pub. L. 85-56`, `71 Stat. 83`, June 17, 1957, eff. Jan. 1, 1958):** Interim consolidation of compensation, pension, hospitalization, and administrative provisions immediately preceding full Title 38 positive-law codification in `Pub. L. 85-857`.

## 2. Era B (`January 1, 1959 – 1991`): `Pub. L. 85-857`, the 1962 Acts & the VJRA

### A. Positive-Law Codification (`Pub. L. 85-857`) & The Dec. 31, 1957 Savings Shield
On **September 2, 1958**, Congress enacted **`Pub. L. 85-857` (`72 Stat. 1105`)**, revising, codifying, and enacting all laws administered by the Veterans' Administration into positive law as **Title 38, United States Code ("Veterans' Benefits")**, **effective January 1, 1959** (`§ 2`, `72 Stat. 1262`).
* **The `December 31, 1957` & `December 31, 1958` Statutory Savings Shields (`Pub. L. 85-56, § 2316(b)` & `Pub. L. 85-857, §§ 10–11`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.953(a)–(c)`):** Any individual receiving disability compensation on **December 31, 1957** (`§ 3.953(c)`) or receiving/entitled to Title 38 benefits on **December 31, 1958** (`§ 3.953(a)–(b)`) could **not** have that entitlement severed or terminated by reason of the 1957/1958 recodifications unless the original grant was procured by fraud, clear unmistakable error as to the laws in effect when rendered, or misrepresentation of material fact ([Playbook 05](../playbooks/05-void-rating-reductions-stabilized-ratings.md), [`38-cfr-3.105e-3.344-3.951-3.957-reductions.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.105e-3.344-3.951-3.957-reductions.md)).

### C. Special History: Transformation of the Judicial-Review Bar (`Former 38 U.S.C. § 211(a)` -> `38 U.S.C. § 511(a)–(b)`)

For 55 years (`1933–1988`), VA adjudicated claims in "splendid isolation" behind a sweeping statutory judicial-review bar. Practitioners researching pre-1991 federal cases or challenging extra-record agency actions must trace four statutory steps:

1. **Pre-1959 Origin (`Economy Act § 5` & `1940 Act`):** Section 5 of `Pub. L. 73-2` (`48 Stat. 9`, Mar. 20, 1933) and the Act of October 17, 1940 (`Pub. L. 76-866, § 11`, `54 Stat. 1197`) declared decisions of the Administrator on any question of law or fact concerning a claim for benefits "final and conclusive and no other official or any court of the United States shall have power or jurisdiction to review any such decisions."
2. **Era B Codification & 1970 Tightening (`Former 38 U.S.C. § 211(a)`):** Codified by `Pub. L. 85-857` (`72 Stat. 1114`, eff. Jan. 1, 1959) as **`38 U.S.C. § 211(a)`**, and expanded by **`Pub. L. 91-376, § 8(a)` (`84 Stat. 790`, Aug. 12, 1970)** to foreclose D.C. Circuit mandamus/APA suits challenging terminations of already-granted benefits.
   * **Supreme Court Carve-Outs Before VJRA:** In ***Johnson v. Robison*, 415 U.S. 361 (1974)**, the Supreme Court held `former § 211(a)` barred review of decisions *under* a statute administered by VA, not facial **constitutional challenges** to Acts of Congress. In ***Traynor v. Turnage*, 485 U.S. 535 (1988)**, the Court held `§ 211(a)` did not bar testing whether a VA regulation violated an independent federal statute outside VA's exclusive domain (the Rehabilitation Act).
3. **VJRA Partial Opening (`Pub. L. 100-687, §§ 101–102`, Nov. 18, 1988):** Amended `former § 211(a)` to carve out four explicit exceptions—most crucially **(i) direct Federal Circuit APA review of VA regulations and general policy interpretations (`former § 223`, `now 38 U.S.C. § 502`)** and **(ii) exclusive judicial review of Board decisions via Chapter 72 (`COVA -> Fed. Cir. -> SCOTUS`)**.
4. **1991 Repeal & Enactment of `38 U.S.C. § 511(a)–(b)` (`Pub. L. 102-83, § 2(a)`, `105 Stat. 388`, Aug. 6, 1991):** Formally **repealed `former § 211(a)`** and restated the rule at **`38 U.S.C. § 511`**:
   * **`§ 511(a)` (Channeling Rule):** The Secretary shall decide all questions of law and fact necessary to a decision under a law that affects the provision of benefits (`*Bates v. Nicholson*, 398 F.3d 1355 (Fed. Cir. 2005)`—broadly bringing attorney accreditation/fee disputes within BVA/CAVC appellate jurisdiction). Subject to `§ 511(b)`, the Secretary's decision is final and conclusive and may not be reviewed by any other official or federal district/regional circuit court (preserving District Court immunity against individual benefits FTCA/APA collateral suits).
   * **`§ 511(b)` (Four Exclusive Statutory Exceptions):** (1) Direct Federal Circuit rulemaking/interpretive review under **`§ 502`**; (2) USGLI/NSLI insurance suits in U.S. District Court under **`§§ 1975, 1984`**; (3) Chapter 37 housing/loan suits under **`ch. 37`**; and (4) **Chapter 72 CAVC / Federal Circuit / Supreme Court appeals**.

## 3. Era C (`1991 – Present`): The Two-Step 1991 Renumbering & Bidirectional Crosswalk

### A. Precision Trap: Two Separate 1991 Statutes (`May 7, 1991` vs. `August 6, 1991`)

Practitioners and even judicial opinions routinely attribute the entire 1991 Title 38 renumbering to `Pub. L. 102-83` or cite `May 7, 1991` for `Pub. L. 102-83`. Primary-source inspection of `105 Statutes at Large` reveals that Congress executed the 1991 renumbering in **two distinct Acts enacted 91 days apart**:

1. **Step 1 — `Pub. L. 102-40`, Title IV, `§ 402` (`105 Stat. 187, 238`, enacted & effective May 7, 1991):** *Department of Veterans Affairs Health-Care Personnel Act of 1991*. Section `402` renumbered **Parts IV, V, and VI** (`Chapters 51, 53, 55, 57, 59, 61, 71, 72, 73, 76, 81–85`)—converting claims (`§§ 3001–3023 -> §§ 5101–5123`), special provisions (`§§ 3101–3118 -> §§ 5301–5318`), attorneys (`§§ 3401–3405 -> §§ 5901–5905`), BVA (`§§ 4001–4009 -> §§ 7101–7109`), and COVA (`§§ 4051–4092 -> §§ 7251–7292`) on **May 7, 1991**.
2. **Step 2 — `Pub. L. 102-83`, `§§ 2, 4, 5` (`105 Stat. 378, 406`, enacted & effective August 6, 1991):** *Department of Veterans Affairs Codification Act*. Repealed and restated VA organizational/Secretary provisions (`Chapters 1, 3, 5, 7, 9`, moving `§ 210(c) -> § 501`, `§ 223 -> § 502`, `§ 211(a) -> § 511`), and in **`§ 5(a)` (`105 Stat. 406`)** renumbered every remaining benefit chapter in **Parts II and III** (`Chapters 11–42`, converting `§§ 301–363 -> §§ 1101–1163`, `§§ 401–423 -> §§ 1301–1323`, `§§ 501–562 -> §§ 1501–1562`, `§§ 601–664 -> §§ 1701–1764`, etc.) so that **the first two digits of every four-digit section equal its Chapter number**!

*(Consequently, a COVA or BVA decision issued in **June or July 1991** properly cites modern **`§ 7104` / `§ 5110`** alongside pre-August **`§ 314` / `§ 354`**!)*
