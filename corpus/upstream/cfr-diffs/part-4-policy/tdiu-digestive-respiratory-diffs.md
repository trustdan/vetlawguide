# VASRD Historical Diff: TDIU (`§ 4.16`), Digestive (`§ 4.114`) & Pending Proposals

---

## 1. Total Disability Based on Individual Unemployability (`38 C.F.R. § 4.16`)

### A. Historical Federal Register Milestones & CUE Temporal Traps
*(See full verbatim `38 C.F.R. § 4.16(a)–(c)` snapshots in [`38-cfr-4.16-tdiu-evolution.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.16-tdiu-evolution.md) and binding TDIU General Counsel opinions in [`vaopgcprec-index.md`](../../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md)).*
* **September 15, 1975 (`40 FR 42535`):** Promulgated the foundational two-paragraph `§ 4.16(a)` (`60%` single / `70%` combined with `40%` anchor + 5 single-disability aggregation categories) and `§ 4.16(b)` (extraschedular Central Office referral) structure.
* **March 1, 1989 (`54 FR 4281`, published January 30, 1989, effective March 1, 1989):** Overhauled `§ 4.16` and added **former `38 C.F.R. § 4.16(c)`**, mandating an automatic `100%` schedular evaluation whenever a veteran's *sole* compensable service-connected disability was a mental disorder rated `70%` that precluded substantially gainful employment. *(Former `§ 4.16(c)` remained in force from **March 1, 1989 through November 6, 1996**, when VA removed it via `61 FR 52695`, published Oct. 8, 1996, **effective November 7, 1996** — see [`38-cfr-4.132-mental-disorders-pre-1996.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.132-mental-disorders-pre-1996.md)).*
* **September 4, 1990 (`55 FR 31579`, published August 3, 1990, effective September 4, 1990):** Amended `38 C.F.R. § 4.16(a)` to codify that marginal employment generally exists when earned annual income does not exceed the U.S. Census Bureau poverty threshold for one person.
* **Bitemporal CUE Gate (*George v. McDonough*, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)`; `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`):** Never project post-Sept. 4, 1990 poverty-threshold text backward onto a pre-Sept. 4, 1990 CUE motion, and distinguish plain-text regulatory errors (`§ 4.16(a)(1)–(5)` math, `cue-and-open-claim`) from later judicial elaborations (*Ray*, *Ortiz-Valles*, *Cantrell*), which govern open, pending, `§ 3.156(b)–(c)`, and appealed claims.

### B. Schedular Thresholds (`§ 4.16(a)`) vs. Extraschedular Referral (`§ 4.16(b)`)
* **Schedular `§ 4.16(a)`:** Requires inability to secure or follow a **substantially gainful occupation** (*Ray v. Wilkie*, 31 Vet. App. 58 (2019) — has both an *economic* and a *non-economic* physical/mental ability component!) PLUS:
  * **One** disability rated **`60%` or more**, OR
  * **Two or more** disabilities with at least one rated **`40%` or more** and sufficient additional disability to bring the **combined rating to `70%` or more**.
* **The Overlooked Five "Single Disability" Aggregation Rules (`§ 4.16(a)(1)–(5)`):**
  For the `60%` single-disability prong (or the `40%` anchor prong!), **multiple disabilities count as ONE single disability** if they arise from:
  1. One **single common etiology** (e.g., all residuals of Diabetes Mellitus—neuropathy + retinopathy + nephropathy!),
  2. A **single accident or injury** (e.g., car crash or IED blast!),
  3. Disabilities affecting a **single bodily system** (e.g., orthopedic, digestive, respiratory, neuropsychiatric),
  4. **Multiple injuries incurred in action** (any combat wounds across *multiple* wars/engagements combine as **ONE** disability to hit `60%` or `40%`!), OR
  5. Multiple disabilities incurred as a **Prisoner of War (POW)**.
  * **CUE Goldmine:** Raters routinely deny schedular `§ 4.16(a)` TDIU because a veteran has `30%` right knee + `30%` left knee + `20%` lumbar (`64% -> 60%` combined, no single code at `40%`), completely overlooking that all three affect the **musculoskeletal system (`§ 4.16(a)(3)`)** or stem from **combat (`§ 4.16(a)(4)`)**, satisfying the **`60%` single-disability rule** on the face of the code sheet! *(Note: While `§ 4.16(a)(1)-(5)` aggregates disabilities to qualify for TDIU, it does NOT aggregate them into a "single 100% disability" for statutory `SMC(s)`—Gazelle v. Shulkin, 868 F.3d 1006 (Fed. Cir. 2017)).*
* **Marginal & Protected Employment (`§ 4.16(a)`):**
  * Income below the U.S. Census Bureau poverty threshold for **one person** (`55 FR 31579`, eff. Sept. 4, 1990) is marginal employment by law and **cannot bar TDIU** (*Ortiz-Valles v. McDonald*, 28 Vet. App. 65 (2016)).
  * Income *above* the poverty threshold **still qualifies as marginal** if earned in a **protected environment** (e.g., family business or heavily accommodated role shielded from competitive market productivity demands—*Cantrell v. Shulkin*, 28 Vet. App. 382 (2017)).
* **Extraschedular `§ 4.16(b)`:** Requires mandatory referral by the RO to the **Director, Compensation Service** whenever a veteran falls below `§ 4.16(a)` percentages yet evidence shows unemployability due to SC disabilities (*Bowling v. Principi*, 15 Vet. App. 1 (2001)). *(Note: RO/BVA lack jurisdiction to grant `§ 4.16(b)` in the first instance without Director review, and failure to refer under `§ 4.16(b)` is normally a procedural/record-weighing error rather than CUE).*

## 2. May 19, 2024 Digestive System Overhaul (`89 FR 19735`, Published March 20, 2024, Effective May 19, 2024 — `38 C.F.R. § 4.114`)

*(For the complete System 07 `DC 7200–7357` structural diff—including `§ 4.112` weight loss/parenteral nutrition, `§§ 4.113–4.114` non-combination boundaries, `DC 7326` IBD biologics, `DC 7312/7345` MELD liver rules, and `DC 7355–7357` Celiac/Dysmotility/Post-pancreatectomy—see **[`VASRD System 07 Digestive Diff`](../vasrd/07-digestive-system-4.110-4.114.md)** and verbatim **[`38-cfr-4.114-digestive-pre-and-post-2024.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.114-digestive-pre-and-post-2024.md)**).*

| Condition | Before May 19, 2024 | On or After May 19, 2024 (`89 FR 19735`, Pub. Mar. 20, 2024) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **GERD** | Rated by analogy under **`DC 7346` (Hiatal Hernia)**:<br/>• **`10%`:** Two or more symptoms of `30%` group of *less severity*.<br/>• **`30%`:** Persistently recurrent epigastric distress with dysphagia, pyrosis (heartburn), and regurgitation, accompanied by substernal or arm/shoulder pain.<br/>• **`60%`:** Symptoms of pain, vomiting, material weight loss, and hematemesis/melena with moderate anemia. | Dedicated **`DC 7206` (GERD)** — tied strictly to **esophageal stricture & dysphagia**:<br/>• **`0%`:** Documented history without daily symptoms or daily medication.<br/>• **`10%`:** Documented history of **esophageal stricture(s)** requiring daily medications to control dysphagia otherwise asymptomatic.<br/>• **`30%`:** Recurrent esophageal stricture(s) causing dysphagia with dilatation `<=2x/yr`.<br/>• **`50%`:** Recurrent/refractory stricture(s) causing dysphagia with dilatation `>=3x/yr`, steroid dilatation `>=1x/yr`, or esophageal stent.<br/>• **`80%`:** Stricture + aspiration/undernutrition/substantial weight loss + surgical treatment or PEG tube. |
| **Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)** | **`DC 7319` (Irritable Colon Syndrome):**<br/>• **`0%`:** Mild (disturbances of bowel function with occasional episodes of abdominal distress).<br/>• **`10%`:** Moderate (frequent episodes of bowel disturbance with abdominal distress).<br/>• **`30%`:** Severe (diarrhea, or alternating diarrhea and constipation, with more or less constant abdominal distress) — **Note: No `20%` tier existed!** | **`DC 7319` (IBS — Rome IV Criteria; added `20%` tier!):** Abdominal pain related to defecation over past 3 months + `>=2` of 5 stool/bloating/mucorrhea symptoms:<br/>• **`10%`:** At least **once** during previous 3 months.<br/>• **`20%`:** At least **3 days per month** during previous 3 months.<br/>• **`30%`:** At least **1 day per week** during previous 3 months. *(No `0%` tier—minimum is `10%` if frequency met!)* |

> [!WARNING]
> **Transitional Protection (`38 U.S.C. § 1155`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.951(a)`):**
> * Veterans already rated `10%`, `30%`, or `60%` for GERD under `DC 7346` before May 19, 2024 **cannot** have their rating reduced solely because `DC 7206` now requires esophageal strictures.
> * For claims **pending across May 19, 2024** (note `89 FR 19735` was published **March 20, 2024**, effective **May 19, 2024**), VA must evaluate pre-May 19, 2024 periods under `DC 7346` (`DC 7319` old text) and post-May 19, 2024 periods under whichever version (`DC 7346` vs. `DC 7206` / old vs. new `DC 7319`) is **more favorable** to the veteran!

## 3. Bilateral Factor Exclusion Rule (`38 C.F.R. § 4.26(d)`, Effective April 16, 2023)

Historically, adding the `10%` bilateral bonus (`38 C.F.R. § 4.26`) could paradoxically **hurt** veterans near the `95% -> 100%` rounding boundary due to intermediate rounding in `§ 4.25` Table I. Effective **April 16, 2023** (`88 FR 22914`, published **April 14, 2023**), VA enacted **`38 C.F.R. § 4.26(d)`**: whenever including bilateral disabilities in the `10%` bilateral factor results in a *lower* combined evaluation (e.g., `94% -> 90%` instead of `95% -> 100%`), VA **must exclude** those bilateral disabilities from the bilateral bonus calculation so the veteran receives the `100%` combined rating!

## 4. Unfinalized Proposals & Rescinded IFR Watchlist (Active Law Still Governs as of Late 2026)

Practitioners frequently encounter client or rater confusion over **proposed / unfinalized** or **rescinded** VASRD rules (see [Authority Verification Log](../../docs/authority-verification-log.md)):
* **Obstructive Sleep Apnea (`DC 6847`, `RIN 2900-AQ72`, `87 FR 8474`, Feb. 15, 2022):** **Current active law STILL awards `50%`** whenever use of a breathing assistance device such as **CPAP** is medically required (`30%` persistent daytime hypersomnolence). Proposed Feb. 2022 rule remains **unfinalized / not enacted**.
* **Tinnitus (`DC 6260`, `RIN 2900-AQ72`, `87 FR 8474`, Feb. 15, 2022):** **Current active law STILL awards a standalone `10%`** for recurrent tinnitus. Proposed Feb. 2022 rule remains **unfinalized / not enacted**.
* **Mental Disorders (`38 C.F.R. § 4.130`, `RIN 2900-AQ82`, `87 FR 8498`, Feb. 15, 2022):** Proposed domain-based WHODAS overhaul remains **unfinalized / not enacted**; `0/10/30/50/70/100%` General Rating Formula remains active law.
* **Painful Scars (`DC 7804`, `90 FR 46542`, Sept. 29, 2025):** Proposed note requiring objective evidence of pain upon examination/demonstration remains **unfinalized / not enacted**; existing `DC 7804` criteria remain active law.
* **Rescinded Medication IFR (`38 C.F.R. § 4.10`, `91 FR 7118` -> Rescinded `91 FR 9712`, Feb. 17–27, 2026):** VA's 10-day Interim Final Rule (`91 FR 7118`) attempting to overturn *Ingram v. Collins*, `38 Vet. App. 130 (2025)` was **formally rescinded on February 27, 2026 (`91 FR 9712`)**—audit any decision rendered Feb. 17–27, 2026 that discounted medicated symptoms!
