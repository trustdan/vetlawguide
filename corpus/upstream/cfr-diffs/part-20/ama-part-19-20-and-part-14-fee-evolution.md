# Part 19, Part 20, Part 14 & Part 17 Historical Diff: Legacy vs. AMA Appeals (`57 FR 4104` vs. `84 FR 138`), Board CUE `Subpart O` (`64 FR 2134`), Four-Era `§ 14.636` Fee Rules (`1862–2026`) & `§§ 17.1002/17.1005` Emergency Reimbursement (`83 FR 974` / `88 FR 10835`)

This Reference module documents the exact primary-source Federal Register citations, publication vs. effective dates, statutory triggers, and bitemporal *George v. McDonough* boundaries across four procedural and financial cornerstones of Veterans Law practice:
1. **Legacy `38 C.F.R. Parts 19 & 20` (`57 FR 4088`, `1992`) vs. AMA Overhaul (`84 FR 138`, `2019`)**;
2. **Board CUE `38 C.F.R. Part 20, Subpart O` (`§§ 20.1400–20.1411`, `64 FR 2134`, `1999` vs. `84 FR 191`, `2019`)**;
3. **Four-Era `38 C.F.R. § 14.636` (`Former § 20.609`) Attorney/Agent Fee Evolution (`1862–2026`)**; and
4. **Non-VA Emergency Medical Reimbursement `38 C.F.R. §§ 17.1002 & 17.1005` (`66 FR 36467` -> `83 FR 974` -> `88 FR 10835`)**.

## 1. Legacy `38 C.F.R. Parts 19 & 20` (`57 FR 4088`, `1992`) vs. AMA Overhaul (`84 FR 138`, `2019`)

* **Era 1 (Post-VJRA Legacy Baseline):**
  * **Federal Register Citation:** `57 FR 4088` (`Part 19` at `57 FR 4104`; `Part 20` at `57 FR 4109`)
  * **Publication Date:** `1992-02-03`
  * **Effective Date:** `1992-03-04`
  * **Transitional / Pending-Claim Rule:** Restructured legacy `38 C.F.R. Part 19` into separate AOJ appellate processing (`Part 19`) and Board Rules of Practice (`Part 20`) following `Pub. L. 100-687` (`VJRA`). Remains **actively in force today** for all un-opted appeals of AOJ decisions issued **prior to `2019-02-19`** (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.2400(b), 19.2`), including `~28,000+` `VACOLS`-swept appeals restored under ***Freund v. Collins*, `No. 21-4168 (Vet. App. class certified Mar. 2026)`**.
* **Era 2 (Appeals Modernization Act Overhaul):**
  * **Federal Register Citation:** `84 FR 138` (`Part 3` at `84 FR 167–172`; `Part 19` at `84 FR 177`; `Part 20` at `84 FR 178–193`; `RIN 2900-AQ26`)
  * **Publication Date:** `2019-01-18`
  * **Effective Date:** `2019-02-19` (`Pub. L. 115-55, § 2(x)`, `131 Stat. 1115`)
  * **Transitional / Pending-Claim Rule:** **Strict Statutory Partition (`Karnas v. Derwinski`, `1 Vet. App. 308 (1991)` Inapplicable to Structural Track):** Applies to all claims in which VA mails notice of an initial AOJ decision **on or after `2019-02-19`** (`§ 3.2400(a)(1)`), plus Legacy claims affirmatively and **irrevocably** opted into AMA via `RAMP` or within `60 days` of a post-Feb. 19, 2019 `SOC`/`SSOC` (`§ 3.2400(a)(2), (c)`).

| Structural Dimension | Era 1: **Legacy System (`57 FR 4104, 4109`, Eff. `1992-03-04`)** | Era 2: **AMA System (`84 FR 138`, Eff. `2019-02-19`, as Modified by Courts)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Post-Decision Review Architecture** | Single linear track (`38 U.S.C. § 7105` `[pre-AMA]`): `1-yr` `NOD` (optional `DRO` election under `former § 3.2600`, `66 FR 21871`, eff. `2001-06-01`) -> VA issues `SOC` (`§ 19.29`) -> `VA Form 9` Substantive Appeal. | Three modular `1-year` lanes (`38 U.S.C. § 5104C`; `§ 3.2500(c)`): `(1)` **HLR** (`VA Form 20-0996`, `§ 3.2601`), `(2)` **Supplemental Claim** (`VA Form 20-0995`, `§ 3.2501`), or `(3)` **Board Appeal** (`VA Form 10182`, `§ 20.202`). Issue-splitting across lanes permitted (`§ 3.2500(d)`). |
| **Board Appeal Initiation & Deadline** | Two-step perfection: `(1)` `NOD` within `1 year` (`VA Form 21-0958` mandatory `>= 2015-03-24`, `79 FR 57660`); `(2)` **`VA Form 9`** within **`60 days` from `SOC` mailing OR remainder of `1-year` post-decision window, whichever is later** (`§ 19.52(b)` `[former § 20.302(b)]`). | Single-step perfection (`38 U.S.C. § 7105(b)(1)`): **`VA Form 10182` (`NOD`)** filed directly with the Board within **`1 year`** of AOJ decision notice (`§ 20.202(b)`). `SOC`, `SSOC`, and `VA Form 9` abolished for AMA claims. |
| **Docket Modification Deadline** | *Not applicable* (single unified Board docket; hearing could be requested anytime prior to decision under `former § 20.700`). | **`38 C.F.R. § 20.202(c)(2)`:** File new `10182` by **later of `1 year` from AOJ decision notice OR `60 days` from Board receipt of initial `10182`** (barred if evidence/testimony already submitted). Board **cannot** decide prematurely before window expires absent waiver (***Williams v. McDonough*, `37 Vet. App. 305 (2024)`**); late switches/delays barred (***Brack v. Collins*, `No. 25-1205 (Fed. Cir. Sept. 8, 2026)`**, *aff'g* `37 Vet. App. 172`). |
| **Evidentiary Record Before the Board** | **Open Continuous Record (`former §§ 19.37, 20.800, 20.1304`):** Evidence accepted continuously throughout appeal (`90 days` post-certification under `§ 20.1304`, or afterward upon good cause / before decision). Triggered mandatory `SSOC` (`30-day` response, `§ 19.38`) unless waived under *Bernard v. Brown*, `4 Vet. App. 384 (1993)`. | **Sealed Statutory Windows (`38 U.S.C. § 7113`; `§§ 20.301–20.303`):**<br/>• **Direct (`§ 7113(a)` / `§ 20.301`):** Closed as of AOJ decision.<br/>• **Evidence (`§ 7113(c)` / `§ 20.303`):** With `10182` or within **`90 days` post-NOD receipt**.<br/>• **Hearing (`§ 7113(b)` / `§ 20.302`):** At Board hearing or within **`90 days` post-hearing**.<br/>• Board weighs window evidence **in first instance** (`§§ 20.300(a), 20.801`) without `SSOC` or *Bernard* waiver! |
| **`"In-Between"` Gap Evidence Rule** | *No gap existed*—all post-AOJ submissions entered the continuous appellate record (and `§ 3.156(b)` abated finality if submitted within `1 yr` without an `NOD`, *Beraud* / *Lang*). | • ***Cook v. McDonough*, `36 Vet. App. 175 (2023)`:** Gap submissions (between AOJ decision & `10182`, or between `10182` & Board hearing) do not auto-enter record, though VLJ/Board must give accurate exclusion/resubmission notice (`§§ 20.205(c), 20.705`).<br/>• ***Cash v. Collins*, `No. 24-1811 (Fed. Cir. Feb. 5, 2026)`:** When `VA Form 10182` **identifies or incorporates by reference** probative gap-period evidence already in `VBMS`, Board **must consider it** under `§ 7113(c)` without physical re-upload! |
| **Readjudication / Reopening Threshold** | **`"New and Material"` (`former § 3.156(a)`):** Must relate to an **unestablished fact** necessary to substantiate the claim **AND raise a reasonable possibility of substantiating the claim**. | **`"New and Relevant"` (`38 U.S.C. § 5108(a)`; `§§ 3.156(d), 3.2501(a)(1)`):** Lower threshold—not previously in actual record + **tends to prove or disprove a matter at issue** (or raises unaddressed theory). |
| **Ongoing `§ 5103A` Duty to Assist** | **Continuous** throughout AOJ and Board proceedings until final BVA decision. | **Terminates at AOJ decision notice (`§ 5103A(e)(2)`)** during HLR and BVA review—*except* mandatory **`§ 3.2601(g)` / `§ 20.802(a)` remand** for pre-decisional DTA error (`Full § 5103A` re-attaches in Supplemental Claims, `§ 3.2501(b)`). |
| **Post-BVA / Post-CAVC Continuity & Implicit Denial** | • Reopening after BVA/CAVC affirmance **reset effective date** (`§ 7291`).<br/>• **Implicit Denial (`Deshotel` / `Adams`)** extinguished unmentioned related claims. | • **`§ 5110(a)(2)(D)–(E)` & *MVA v. Sec'y*, `7 F.4th 1110 (Fed. Cir. 2021)`:** `1-yr` Supplemental Claim after **BVA OR CAVC** decision preserves initial effective date (`§ 3.2500(b)` Fed. Cir. bar & `§ 3.155` Supplemental `ITF` exclusion **invalidated**!).<br/>• ***Hamill v. Collins*, `166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. 2026)`:** Implicit denial **abolished** for AMA decisions under `38 U.S.C. § 5104(b)`! |

## 2. Board CUE `Subpart O` (`38 C.F.R. §§ 20.1400–20.1411`): `64 FR 2134` (`1999`) -> `84 FR 191` (`2019`) -> *George* (`2022`)

* **Pre-1997 Baseline:** In ***Smith v. Brown*, `35 F.3d 1516 (Fed. Cir. 1994)`**, the Federal Circuit held `38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)` applied solely to RO decisions, leaving veterans with no statutory CUE motion against final Board decisions (only discretionary Chairman reconsideration under `38 U.S.C. § 7103`). Congress responded by enacting **`Pub. L. 105-111` (`111 Stat. 2271`, enacted & effective `1997-11-21`)**, codifying `38 U.S.C. § 5109A` (RO CUE) and **`38 U.S.C. § 7111`** (Board CUE).
* **Era 1 (`Subpart O` Enactment):**
  * **Federal Register Citation:** `64 FR 2134, 2139` (`RIN 2900-AJ15`)
  * **Publication Date:** `1999-01-13`
  * **Effective Date:** `1999-02-12`
  * **Transitional / Pending-Claim Rule:** Applies retroactively to revise *any* prior final Board decision regardless of how many decades earlier the Board decision was rendered (`§ 20.1400(a)`), judged strictly against the record and law extant when that Board decision issued (`§ 20.1403(b)`).
* **Era 2 (AMA Conforming Amendments & *George* Validation):**
  * **Federal Register Citation:** `84 FR 138, 191–192` (`RIN 2900-AQ26`)
  * **Publication Date:** `2019-01-18`
  * **Effective Date:** `2019-02-19`
  * **Transitional / Pending-Claim Rule:** Updated internal `Part 20` cross-references and conformed `§ 20.1411(b)` to AMA `§ 5108` Supplemental Claims without altering substantive CUE burdens; **`§ 20.1403(e)` upheld 6–3** in ***George v. McDonough*, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)`**.

| `Subpart O` Section | Era 1 (`64 FR 2134, 2139`, Eff. `1999-02-12`) | Era 2 (`84 FR 191–192`, Eff. `2019-02-19` & *George*, `2022`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`§ 20.1400(b)`** *(Finality & Subsumption)* | All final Board merits decisions are subject to revision except issues which have been **appealed to and decided by a court of competent jurisdiction** (`CAVC`/`Fed. Cir.`); subsumes underlying RO rating decision on the same issue. | Unchanged substantively—remains the threshold jurisdictional check on [`Board CUE Pre-Filing Gate`](../../checklists/board-cue-pre-filing-gate.md). |
| **`§ 20.1403(d)(1)–(3)`** *(Examples of Non-CUE)* | Codified absolute bars against pleading `(1)` **changed medical diagnosis** without factual record error, `(2)` **Secretary's failure to fulfill the duty to assist** (*Cook v. Principi*, `318 F.3d 1334`), or `(3)` **disagreement as to how the facts were weighed or evaluated**. | Unchanged—still defeats every DTA, *McLendon*, *Nieves-Rodriguez*, and `§ 5107(b)` equipoise theory pleaded as CUE. |
| **`§ 20.1403(e)`** *(Change in Interpretation)* | *"Clear and unmistakable error does not include the otherwise correct application of a statute or regulation where, subsequent to the Board decision challenged, there has been a change in the interpretation of the statute or regulation."* | **Affirmed by U.S. Supreme Court in *George v. McDonough*, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)`:** Even when a regulation (`former § 3.304(b)` soundness rebuttal in *George*, `§ 3.350(f)` single-step cap in *Barry*, or `§ 3.352(b)(2)` in *Laska*) is later invalidated as contrary to statute, a prior final Board decision applying that regulation while in force is **immune from CUE**! |
| **`§ 20.1404(b)` & `§ 20.1409(c)`** *(Specificity & One-Shot Rule)* | • **`§ 20.1404(b)`:** Must set forth clearly and specifically the alleged factual/legal error, exact extant law, and why result would have **manifestly changed** (*non-specific motions dismissed without prejudice*).<br/>• **`§ 20.1409(c)`:** **STRICT ONE-SHOT RES JUDICATA**—once there is a final decision on a `Subpart O` motion on a given Board issue, *"that issue may not be reviewed again under this subpart."* | Unchanged (`84 FR 191` updated cross-references to `Rule 803`/`§ 20.803` etc.). **Never** file a half-researched Board CUE motion! |
| **`§ 20.1411(b)`** *(Relationship to `§ 5108`)* | Referenced `"reopening"` a previously disallowed claim based on `"new and material evidence"` under `38 U.S.C. § 5108`. | **`84 FR 192`** replaced `"reopen"` / `"new and material"` with filing a **`"supplemental claim"`** under `38 U.S.C. § 5108` (`new and relevant`), clarifying a `Subpart O` CUE motion is distinct from a `§ 5108` Supplemental Claim. |

## 3. Four-Era `38 C.F.R. § 14.636` (`Former § 20.609`) Attorney & Agent Fee Evolution (`1862–2026`)

Eligibility to charge a `38 U.S.C. § 5904` (`former § 3404`) contingency or hourly fee—and eligibility for `20%` direct VA withholding from past-due benefits (`§ 5904(d)`)—is governed by **four distinct statutory/regulatory eras**:

* **Era 1 (`1862 / 1864 – 1988-11-17`):** Act of July 14, 1862 (`12 Stat. 566, 568`, `$5` cap) & Act of July 4, 1864 (`13 Stat. 387, 389`, **`$10` cap**; `former 38 U.S.C. § 3404(c)`).
* **Era 2 (`1988-11-18 – 2007-06-19`):** `Pub. L. 100-687, § 104(a)` (`102 Stat. 4108`, enacted `1988-11-18`); implemented in **`former 38 C.F.R. § 20.609`** at **`57 FR 4088, 4115`** (pub. `1992-02-03`, eff. `1992-03-04`).
* **Era 3 (`2007-06-20 – 2019-02-18`):** `Pub. L. 109-461, § 101(c)(1), (i)` (`120 Stat. 3403, 3407–08`, enacted `2006-12-22`, **effective `2007-06-20`**); implemented in **`38 C.F.R. § 14.636`** (removing `former § 20.609`) at **`73 FR 29852, 29875`** (`RIN 2900-AM62`, pub. `2008-05-22`, **eff. `2008-06-23`**).
* **Era 4 (`2019-02-19 – Present`):** `Pub. L. 115-55, § 2(n)` (`131 Stat. 1105, 1110`, **eff. `2019-02-19`**); implemented in **`38 C.F.R. § 14.636(c)`** at **`84 FR 138, 174`** (`RIN 2900-AQ26`, pub. `2019-01-18`, **eff. `2019-02-19`**)—**partially invalidated on `2021-07-30`** in ***Military-Veterans Advocacy v. Sec'y of Veterans Affairs*, `7 F.4th 1110, 1135–41 (Fed. Cir. 2021)`**!

| Fee Rule Dimension | Era 1: **Pre-VJRA (`1864 – Nov. 17, 1988`)** | Era 2: **VJRA Post-BVA Era (`Nov. 18, 1988 – June 19, 2007`)** | Era 3: **Post-Legacy-NOD Era (`June 20, 2007 – Feb. 18, 2019`)** | Era 4: **AMA Post-Initial-Decision Era (`Feb. 19, 2019 – Present`)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Governing Statute & `38 C.F.R.`** | `former 38 U.S.C. § 3404(c)` (`13 Stat. 389`) | `38 U.S.C. § 5904(c)(1)` (`Pub. L. 100-687`); `former 38 C.F.R. § 20.609` (`57 FR 4115`) | `38 U.S.C. § 5904(c)(1)` (`Pub. L. 109-461, § 101`); `38 C.F.R. § 14.636(c)(2)` (`73 FR 29875`) | `38 U.S.C. § 5904(c)(1)` (`Pub. L. 115-55, § 2(n)`); `38 C.F.R. § 14.636(c)(1)` (`84 FR 174`) + ***MVA*, `7 F.4th 1110`** |
| **Earliest Fee-Charging Trigger** | **Capped at `$10` per claim** across all VA stages (upheld in *Walters*, `473 U.S. 305 (1985)`). Criminal misdemeanor (`§ 5905`) to charge `> $10`. | **ONLY AFTER a first final Board (`BVA`) decision** on the issue, **AND** attorney/agent had to be **retained within `1 year`** of that BVA decision (`NOD` had to be `>= Nov. 18, 1988`). | **After a Legacy Notice of Disagreement (`NOD`) is filed ON OR AFTER `June 20, 2007`** (`Pub. ## 4. Non-VA Emergency Medical Reimbursement (`38 U.S.C. § 1725`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 17.1002 & 17.1005`): `2001` -> `2018` (*Staab*) -> `2023` (*Wolfe*)

Under `38 U.S.C. § 1725` (enacted by `Pub. L. 106-117, § 111`, `113 Stat. 1553`, Nov. 30, 1999, eff. `2000-05-29` for non-service-connected emergency treatment of active `24-month` VHA-enrolled veterans, distinct from `38 U.S.C. § 1728` SC/`P&T`/`Ch. 31` emergency care), VA's Other-Health-Insurance (`OHI`) exclusion regulations underwent two major judicial invalidations and Federal Register overhauls:

* **Era 1 (Pre-*Staab* Total / Partial `OHI` Exclusion Era):**
  * **Federal Register Citations:** `66 FR 36467` (pub. `2001-07-12`, eff. `2001-08-13`) & `77 FR 23615` (pub. `2012-04-20`, eff. `2012-05-21`, implementing `Pub. L. 111-137`, retro. eff. `2009-07-19`)
  * **Rule & Judicial Invalidation:** `Pub. L. 111-137` amended `38 U.S.C. § 1725(c)(4)(D)` so VA became secondary payer while barring reimbursement for `"any copayment or similar payment"` owed by the veteran. Despite `Pub. L. 111-137`, VA retained `former 38 C.F.R. § 17.1002(f)` barring *all* reimbursement if a health-plan contract covered *any fraction* of the ER visit—**invalidated** in ***Staab v. McDonald*, `28 Vet. App. 50, 54–56 (2016)`** as frustrating Congress's `2010` amendment!
* **Era 2 (Post-*Staab* Interim Final Rule — Sweeping Deductible & Coinsurance Ban):**
  * **Federal Register Citation:** `83 FR 974` (`RIN 2900-AQ08`, *Reimbursement for Emergency Treatment*)
  * **Publication Date:** `2018-01-09`
  * **Effective Date:** `2018-01-09` *(Interim Final Rule)*
  * **Rule & Judicial Invalidation:** Amended `§ 17.1002(f)` to strike `"or in part"`, **but simultaneously amended `§ 17.1005(a)(5)`** to prohibit VA from reimbursing `"any copayment, deductible, **coinsurance**, or similar payment"`! In ***Wolfe v. Wilkie*, `32 Vet. App. 1 (2019)`** (`Rule 23` class action) and ***Wolfe v. McDonough*, `28 F.4th 1348, 1354–60 (Fed. Cir. 2022)`**, the Federal Circuit held that while fixed **copayments** and **deductibles** are `"similar payments"` barred by `38 U.S.C. § 1725(c)(4)(D)`, percentage-based **coinsurance** (`e.g., 20%` of a `$250,000` ICU/air-ambulance bill = `$50,000`!) is **NOT** a `"copayment or similar payment"`—invalidating `83 FR 974`'s coinsurance ban! *(Note: Wolfe, `28 F.4th at 1358–60` vacated CAVC mandamus because class members had an adequate direct statutory appeal pathway through BVA to CAVC).*
* **Era 3 (Post-*Wolfe* Final Rule — Coinsurance Payable; Copayments & Deductibles Excluded):**
  * **Federal Register Citation:** `88 FR 10835` (`RIN 2900-AQ08`, *Reimbursement for Emergency Treatment*)
  * **Publication Date:** `2023-02-22`
  * **Effective Date:** `2023-04-24`
  * **Transitional / Retroactive Filing Window (`§ 17.1004(f)`):** Opened a special **`1-year` retroactive claim window (through `February 22, 2024`)** for veterans whose `OHI` emergency claims met `§§ 17.1002–17.1003` criteria back to February 1, 2010!

| Patient Cost-Sharing Bucket (`§ 1725` + `OHI`) | Era 1 (`2001 / 2012 – Jan. 8, 2018`; Pre-*Staab*) | Era 2 (`Jan. 9, 2018 – Apr. 23, 2023`; `83 FR 974` IFR) | Era 3 (`Apr. 24, 2023 – Present`; `88 FR 10835` Final Rule) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fixed Copayment** (`e.g., $150 ER copay`) | Denied (total `OHI` bar, `former § 17.1002(f)`). | **Excluded (`$0`)** under `§ 17.1005(a)(5)`. | **Excluded (`$0`)** — statutorily barred by `§ 1725(c)(4)(D)`. |
| **Annual Deductible** (`e.g., $3,000 deductible`) | Denied (total `OHI` bar). | **Excluded (`$0`)** under `§ 17.1005(a)(5)`. | **Excluded (`$0`)** — upheld as `"similar payment"` in *Wolfe v. McDonough*, `28 F.4th at 1356`. |
| **Percentage Coinsurance** (`e.g., 20%` patient share = `$40,000`) | Denied under `former § 17.1002(f)` (**invalidated** in *Staab*, `28 Vet. App. 50`). | Denied under `83 FR 974` (**invalidated** in *Wolfe*, `32 Vet. App. 1` & `28 F.4th 1348`). | **100% REIMBURSABLE BY VA (`§§ 17.1002(f), 17.1005(a)(5)`)!** |
| **Uncovered Emergency Services** (`e.g., non-covered air ambulance / out-of-network balance`) | Denied if `OHI` paid any other line item (*Staab* error). | Reimbursable secondary to `OHI` (`83 FR 974`). | **Reimbursable** up to `§ 17.1005` payment schedule (**extinguishes provider balance-billing liability** under `38 U.S.C. § 1725(a)(2)(A), (e)` & `38 C.F.R. § 17.1005(a)(4)` once VA pays!). |
