## 2. `38 C.F.R. § 4.112` Weight Loss, Baseline Weight & Nutritional Support Definitions (`>= May 19, 2024`)

From `1946–July 1, 2001`, `§ 4.112` used qualitative prose (*"substantial"* vs. *"minor"* weight loss below *"normal/standard weight"*); on **July 2, 2001 (`66 FR 29486`, `RIN 2900-AK12`)**, VA first quantified `>20%` substantial and `10–20%` minor weight loss sustained `>=3 months` against a `2-year` pre-onset baseline weight. At **`89 FR 19735, 19742` (eff. May 19, 2024)**, VA reserved `§§ 4.110–4.111` and expanded [`38 C.F.R. § 4.112(a)–(d)`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.114-digestive-pre-and-post-2024.md) to add *involuntary* weight loss, diminished self-care/work quality, the **favorable Hamwi/BMI baseline fallback (`(b)`)**, **undernutrition (`(c)`)**, and **`TPN`/assisted enteral nutrition (`(d)`)**—plugging directly into `DC 7203, 7206, 7301, 7303, 7308, 7328–7329, 7345, 7355–7356`:

| `§ 4.112` Paragraph | Defined Term | Binding Post-May 19, 2024 Regulatory Standard (`89 FR 19735`) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`§ 4.112(a)`** | **Substantial Weight Loss** vs. **Minor Weight Loss** vs. **Inability to Gain Weight** | • **Substantial weight loss:** Involuntary loss **`> 20%`** of baseline weight **sustained for `3 months`** with diminished quality of self-care or work tasks.<br/>• **Minor weight loss:** Involuntary loss between **`10% and 20%`** of baseline weight **sustained for `3 months`** with GI-related symptoms, involving diminished quality of self-care/work tasks or decreased food intake.<br/>• **Inability to gain weight:** Substantial weight loss with inability to regain it despite following appropriate therapy. |
| **`§ 4.112(b)`** | **Baseline Weight** *(Favorable Fallback Rule!)* | Clinically documented **average weight for the `2-year` period preceding onset of illness**, OR (if relevant) weight recorded at the veteran's **most recent discharge physical**. **Favorable Fallback:** If neither is available or currently relevant, VA **must** use ideal body weight via either the **Hamwi formula** or **BMI tables**, **whichever is MOST FAVORABLE to the veteran**! |
| **`§ 4.112(c)`** | **Undernutrition** | Nutrient deficiency from insufficient intake or malabsorption/inability to utilize or retain nutrients, characterized by failure to maintain normal organ functions/healthy tissues (signs include subcutaneous tissue loss, edema, peripheral neuropathy, muscle wasting, weakness, abdominal distention, ascites, and below-normal BMI). |
| **`§ 4.112(d)(1)–(2)`** | **Nutritional Support (`TPN` & Assisted Enteral Nutrition)** | • **`(d)(1)` Total Parenteral Nutrition (`TPN` / hyperalimentation):** IV catheter infusion of proteins, carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, and minerals bypassing GI digestion.<br/>• **`(d)(2)` Assisted Enteral Nutrition:** Special liquid mixture delivered directly into stomach or small intestine via **nasogastric (`NG`), nasoenteral, gastrostomy (`PEG`), or jejunostomy (`J-tube`) tube**. |

## 3. Pre- vs. Post-May 19, 2024 Structural Diff Matrix: Major Digestive Conditions

See [`38-cfr-4.114-digestive-pre-and-post-2024.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.114-digestive-pre-and-post-2024.md) for verbatim historical and current `§ 4.114` schedules.

### A. GERD (`Former DC 7399-7346` -> `DC 7206`), Hiatal Hernia (`DC 7346 -> 7203`) & Barrett's (`DC 7207`)

| Dimension | Era 1: `Apr. 1, 1946 – May 18, 2024` *(Rated by Analogy under `DC 7346` Hiatal Hernia)* | Era 2: `May 19, 2024 – Present` *(`89 FR 19735` — Dedicated `DC 7206` GERD & Revised `DC 7346`)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Governing Code** | **`DC 7399-7346`** (GERD rated analogously under `DC 7346` Hiatal Hernia per `38 C.F.R. § 4.20`). | • **GERD:** Dedicated **`DC 7206`** (*Gastroesophageal reflux disease*).<br/>• **Hiatal / Paraesophageal Hernia (`DC 7346`):** Expressly directs **"Rate as esophagus, stricture of (`DC 7203`)"** (identical `80/50/30/10/0%` stricture tiers as `DC 7206`!).<br/>• **Barrett's Esophagus (`DC 7207`):** With stricture -> `DC 7203`; **without stricture** -> **`30%`** high-grade dysplasia, **`10%`** low-grade dysplasia (malignancy -> `DC 7343` `100%`). |
| **Highest Tiers (`60%` vs. `80%`/`50%`)** | **`60%`:** Symptoms of pain, vomiting, material weight loss, and **hematemesis or melena with moderate anemia**; or other symptom combinations productive of severe impairment of health. | • **`80%`:** Documented recurrent/refractory **esophageal stricture(s)** causing dysphagia + `>=1` of `(1)` aspiration, `(2)` undernutrition, or `(3)` `§ 4.112(a)` substantial weight loss (`>20%`), **AND** surgical stricture correction or **`PEG` tube**.<br/>• **`50%`:** Documented recurrent/refractory **esophageal stricture(s)** causing dysphagia requiring `(1)` **dilatation `>=3` times/yr**, `(2)` **steroid dilatation `>=1` time/yr**, OR `(3)` **esophageal stent placement**. |
| **`30%` Tier** | **`30%`:** Persistently recurrent epigastric distress with **dysphagia, pyrosis (heartburn), and regurgitation**, accompanied by **substernal or arm or shoulder pain**, productive of considerable impairment of health (*no stricture or endoscopy dilatation required!*). | **`30%`:** Documented history of recurrent **esophageal stricture(s)** causing dysphagia which requires **dilatation no more than `2` times per year (`<=2x/yr`)**. |
| **`10%` & `0%` Tiers** | • **`10%`:** Two or more of the symptoms for the `30%` evaluation of *less severity* (e.g., chronic heartburn + acid regurgitation!).<br/>• **`0%`:** Asymptomatic (`§ 4.31`). | • **`10%`:** Documented history of **esophageal stricture(s)** that requires **daily medications to control dysphagia** otherwise asymptomatic.<br/>• **`0%`:** Documented history **without daily symptoms or requirement for daily medications**. *(Note 1: Stricture findings must be documented by **barium swallow, CT, or `EGD` endoscopy**!)* |

> [!WARNING]
> **Three Critical GERD Practice Rules (`§ 1155`, *Karnas* & `DC 7206` Medication Trap):**
> 1. **Grandfathering of Pre-May 19, 2024 `10%/30%/60%` GERD Ratings (`38 U.S.C. § 1155`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.951(a)`):** Hundreds of thousands of veterans hold `10%`, `30%`, or `60%` ratings under `DC 7399-7346` based on pyrosis, regurgitation, dysphagia, and substernal/shoulder pain *without* an `EGD`-proven esophageal stricture. Under `§ 1155` and `§ 3.951(a)`, VA **cannot** reduce those running awards unless sustained medical improvement is proven **under the pre-May 19, 2024 `DC 7346` criteria** ([Playbook 05](../../playbooks/05-void-rating-reductions-stabilized-ratings.md)). **Caution:** Do *not* reflexively file a post-May 19, 2024 increased-rating claim on a `30%` GERD veteran who lacks an esophageal stricture!
> 2. **Straddling Claims (`Karnas v. Derwinski`, `1 Vet. App. 308 (1991)`):** Any GERD or `DC 7346` claim (including ITFs, Supplemental Claims under `§ 3.2500`, HLRs, Board appeals, or `§ 5104(b)` *Hamill* pending claims) pending prior to **May 19, 2024** must be evaluated under `DC 7346` prior to May 19, 2024 and under **whichever schedule (`DC 7346` vs. `DC 7206`) is more favorable** from May 19, 2024 forward!
> 3. **Medication Split (*Jones*/*Ingram* vs. *McCarroll*) & Secondary GERD Gaps:** For pre-May 19, 2024 `DC 7346` periods, `DC 7346` was **silent on medication**—so under *Jones v. Shinseki* (`26 Vet. App. 56`) and *Ingram v. Collins* (`38 Vet. App. 130`), raters **cannot** discount heartburn/regurgitation controlled by omeprazole/pantoprazole (`PPIs`) or famotidine (`H2` blockers)! By contrast, post-May 19, 2024 **`DC 7206`'s `10%` tier explicitly requires *both* documented esophageal stricture(s) *and* daily medication** to control dysphagia (*McCarroll* carve-out)—note the regulatory gap where daily `PPI`-dependent GERD *without* an `EGD` stricture lands at `0%` under `DC 7206` unless accompanied by **`DC 7207` Barrett's dysplasia (`10%/30%`)**, secondary **`DC 6516` reflux laryngitis (`10%/30%`)**, or secondary **`DC 6602` reflux-aggravated asthma**!

## 4. `38 C.F.R. §§ 4.113–4.114` Anti-Pyramiding Lock vs. Permissible Multi-Rating Stacking (CUE Goldmine)

Under [`38 C.F.R. § 4.113`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.114-digestive-pre-and-post-2024.md) and the opening paragraph of [`38 C.F.R. § 4.114`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.114-digestive-pre-and-post-2024.md), VA's categorical prohibition on combining digestive ratings is **strictly bounded by numerical Diagnostic Code listing** (*Tropf v. Nicholson*, `20 Vet. App. 317, 320 (2006)`):

| Category | Exact Diagnostic Codes | Combination & Elevation Rule (`38 C.F.R. § 4.114` & `§ 4.14`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Non-Combinable Abdominal / Visceral Block (`§ 4.114` Lock)** | **`DC 7301–7329` inclusive, `7331` *(Peritonitis)*, `7342` *(Visceroptosis)*, `7345–7350` inclusive *(Liver/Hiatal/Pancreatitis/Vagotomy/Abscess)*, `7352` *(Pancreas transplant)*, and `7355–7357` inclusive *(Celiac, Dysmotility, Post-pancreatectomy)*** *(plus `7354` rated under `7345`)* | **STRICTLY NON-COMBINABLE WITH EACH OTHER.** Assign a **single evaluation** under the DC reflecting the **predominant disability picture**, and **elevate it to the next higher evaluation** if warranted by the severity of the overall disability! *(Watch `DC 7346` Hiatal Hernia: `7346` sits inside `7345–7350`, whereas standalone **`DC 7206` GERD** sits outside it!)* |
| **2. Upper Oral / Esophageal Codes (`OUTSIDE` `§ 4.114` Lock!)** | **`DC 7200–7207`** — Mouth/Lip (`7200–7201`), Tongue loss (`7202`), **Esophageal stricture (`7203`)**, Motility/Achalasia (`7204`), Diverticulum (`7205`), **GERD (`7206`)**, and **Barrett's esophagus (`7207`)** | **EXEMPT FROM THE `§ 4.114` NON-COMBINATION LIST!** A veteran can lawfully hold **separate, combined ratings** for **`DC 7206` GERD** (or `DC 7207` Barrett's) **PLUS** a lower-GI or liver code from Group 1 (e.g., **`DC 7319` IBS (`30%`)**, **`DC 7326` Crohn's (`60%`)**, or **`DC 7312` Cirrhosis**), so long as the same symptom is not compensated twice under `38 C.F.R. § 4.14` (*Esteban v. | **3. Anorectal, Abdominal-Wall Hernia, Spleen/Transplant & Cancer Carve-Outs (`OUTSIDE` `§ 4.114` Lock!)** | • **`DC 7332`** (Rectum & anus, impairment of sphincter control `100/60/30/10/0%`)<br/>• **`DC 7333`** (Rectum & anus, stricture `100/60/30/10%`)<br/>• **`DC 7336`** (Hemorrhoids `20/10%`)<br/>• **`DC 7338`** (Inguinal/ventral/incisional hernia `60/30/20/10/0%` + `Note (1)` `100%` post-op)<br/>• **`DC 7343–7344`** (Malignant/benign neoplasms), **`7351`** (Liver transplant), **`7353`** (Anal/perianal injury) | **EXEMPT FROM THE `§ 4.114` NON-COMBINATION LIST!** Raters routinely commit **plain-text CUE (`38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)`)** by lumping **`DC 7336` Hemorrhoids (`20%`)**, **`DC 7338` Inguinal/Incisional Hernia**, or **`DC 7332` Fecal Incontinence** into `DC 7319` IBS or `DC 7304` Ulcer! Only avoid duplicating the *exact same* symptom (e.g., rectal incontinence used for `DC 7326`'s `100%` tier cannot also be rated at `100%` under `DC 7332` per `§ 4.14`, whereas `DC 7319` IBS abdominal pain/diarrhea + `DC 7336` prolapsed/thrombosed hemorrhoids + `DC 7206` GERD **stack cleanly**!). |
