# VASRD Historical Diff: Mental Disorders & PTSD (`38 C.F.R. §§ 4.125–4.130`)

Mental health evaluations evolved through four distinct historical phases (plus a pending unfinalized proposal). For the complete Body System `14` reference (`DC 9201–9521`, `§ 4.129` `50%` traumatic-discharge rule, and Eating Disorders `DC 9520–9521`), see [`14-mental-disorders-4.125-4.130.md`](14-mental-disorders-4.125-4.130.md) and [`38-cfr-4.132-mental-disorders-pre-1996.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.132-mental-disorders-pre-1996.md). Because subjective evaluation of psychiatric severity involves weighing evidence, **pure percentage CUEs (`30%` vs. `50%`) are very difficult**, whereas **unlawful reductions (`§ 3.344`)**, **`former § 4.16(c)` `1989–1996` `100%` mental CUEs**, **`§ 3.156(c)` JSRRC/combat stressor reconsiderations**, and **unadjudicated TDIU (`Roberson`/`Rice`)** are massive winners.

## Historical Evolution Matrix (`1945 – 2026`)

| Era & Effective Date | Diagnostic Framework & Codes | Rating Tiers & Substantive Criteria | Key Retroactive & CUE Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Era 1: 1945 – 1980** *(Pre-PTSD Era)* | **1945 VASRD / DSM-I & DSM-II:** Separate schedules for Psychoses (`DC 9200s`) vs. Psychoneurotic Disorders (`DC 9400` anxiety reaction, `9405` depressive reaction). **No PTSD code existed!** | Psychoneuroses often capped below `100%` in early versions unless psychotic/total industrial incapacity; evaluated via **social and industrial inadaptability** (`none 0%`, `mild 10%`, `definite 30%`, `considerable 50%`, `severe 70%`, `total 100%` after 1960s harmonization). | **Opportunity:** Combat veterans misdiagnosed with "anxiety neurosis" or "personality disorder" where missing combat records (`§ 3.156(c)`) later verify trauma, OR psychoses (`DC 9200s`) manifesting within **1 year of separation** (`38 C.F.R. § 3.309(a)` chronic disease presumption—Note: **only psychoses**, not neuroses/PTSD, are `§ 3.309(a)` 1-year chronic diseases!). |
| **Era 2: Oct. 1980 – Nov. 6, 1996** *(DSM-III & Addition of PTSD `DC 9411`)* | **DSM-III (1980) / DSM-III-R (1988):** Added **`DC 9411` (Post-traumatic stress disorder)** to `38 C.F.R. § 4.132`. Separate rating tables remained for Psychotic vs. Organic vs. Psychoneurotic (`DC 9400–9411`). | **`0%` / `10%` (Mild) / `30%` (Definite) / `50%` (Considerable) / `70%` (Severe) / `100%` (Complete)** impairment of **social and industrial adaptability**. | **Critical Trap:** Do **NOT** cite modern `§ 4.130` symptoms (like "suicidal ideation" or "panic attacks more than once a week") for pre-Nov. 7, 1996 decisions! Pre-Nov. 7, 1996 `§ 4.132` hinged on `definite / considerable / severe` industrial impairment. **Best Weapon:** `§ 3.344` unlawful reductions of `50%/70%/100%` PTSD ratings and `§ 3.157(b)` VAMC PTSD hospitalizations! |
| **Era 3: Nov. 7, 1996 – Aug. 3, 2014** (`61 FR 52695`) | **DSM-IV (`38 C.F.R. §§ 4.125, 4.130`):** Unified **ALL** mental disorders (except eating disorders `DC 9520–9521`) into **one General Rating Formula for Mental Disorders** (`0/10/30/50/70/100%`); referenced **GAF (Global Assessment of Functioning)** scores. | Introduced non-exhaustive symptom examples (*Mauerhan*, 2002):<br/>• **`30%`:** Occasional decrease in work efficiency.<br/>• **`50%`:** Reduced reliability and productivity; panic attacks `>1/wk`.<br/>• **`70%`:** Deficiencies in **most areas** (work, school, family, judgment, thinking, or mood), **suicidal ideation** (*Bankhead*!), obsessional rituals, near-continuous panic/depression.<br/>• **`100%`:** Total occupational and social impairment. | **Key Precedents:** *Mauerhan* (symptoms are examples, not rigid checklists); *Vazquez-Claudio* (symptom severity/frequency/duration drive occupational impairment); *Bankhead v. Shulkin* (presence of **suicidal ideation** strongly points to at least `70%`, though weighing nuances normally make *Bankhead* a direct-appeal remand smash rather than CUE). |
| **Era 4: Aug. 4, 2014 – Present** (`79 FR 45093`; final `80 FR 14308`) | **DSM-5 (`38 C.F.R. §§ 4.125, 4.130`):** Adopted DSM-5 diagnostic criteria and **eliminated GAF scores**; renumbered several `94xx` categories (`DC 9411` PTSD unchanged). | **Identical `0 / 10 / 30 / 50 / 70 / 100%` General Rating Formula** text to the Nov. 7, 1996 rule! | Also cross-check **July 13, 2010 PTSD Stressor Liberalization (`38 C.F.R. § 3.304(f)(3)`, `75 FR 39843` as corrected at `75 FR 41092`)** (fear of hostile military/terrorist activity confirmed by VA psychiatrist)—liberalizing rule (`§ 3.114`) effective no earlier than July 13, 2010 unless `§ 3.156(c)` unit records apply! |
| **Proposed (NOT ENACTED): `RIN 2900-AQ82`** *(Feb. 2022 Proposal)* | Proposed 5-domain WHODAS model (`cognition`, `interpersonal`, `task completion`, `life activities`, `self-care`) with **`10%` minimum floor** and elimination of total-unemployability bar to `100%` while working. | **NOT ACTIVE LAW AS OF LATE 2026.** | Never apply `RIN 2900-AQ82` to any current or historical claim; monitor Federal Register for any future Final Rule publication. |
