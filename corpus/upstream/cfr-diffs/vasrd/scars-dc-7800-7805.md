# VASRD Historical Diff: Scars & Disfigurement (`38 C.F.R. § 4.118`, `DC 7800–7805`)

Scars underwent **three** enacted modernizations—**August 30, 2002 (`67 FR 49590`)**, **October 23, 2008 (`73 FR 54708`)**, and **August 13, 2018 (`83 FR 32592`)**—plus an **unfinalized September 29, 2025 proposed rule (`90 FR 46542`)**. Always consult the verbatim four-era text in [`authorities/38-cfr-4.118-scars-evolution.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.118-scars-evolution.md) before drafting a CUE motion.

---

## Side-by-Side Evolution (`DC 7800–7805`)

| Diagnostic Code | Era 1: 1945 – Oct. 22, 2008 *(incl. Aug. 30, 2002 interim clarifications)* | Era 2: Oct. 23, 2008 – Aug. 12, 2018 (`73 FR 54708`) | Era 3: Aug. 13, 2018 – Present (`83 FR 32592`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`DC 7800`** *(Head, Face, or Neck)* | Qualitative tiers (`10/30/50%`, up to `80%` post-2002 for extremerepugnant/gross distortion): *"slight / moderate / severe / pronounced / repulsive"* disfigurement. | **Objective 8-Characteristic Matrix** (`width >= 1/4"` or `"1/2"`, elevation/depression on palpation, adherence, hypo/hyperpigmentation `>6 sq in`, abnormal texture `>6 sq in`, underlying soft tissue missing `>6 sq in`, inflexible/indurated skin `>6 sq in`) **OR Gross Distortion** of paired features (`10/30/50/80%`). | Retained 8-characteristic matrix & gross distortion (`10/30/50/80%`); clarified zone boundaries (`Zone 1`) and explicitly allowed separate evaluation of painful/unstable head/face/neck scars under `DC 7804`! |
| **`DC 7801`** *(Deep Scars, Nonlinear, Not Head/Face/Neck)* | Deep scars with **underlying soft-tissue damage** not head/face/neck based on area (`>= 6 sq in` = `10%`; `>= 12` = `20%`; `>= 72` = `30%`; `>= 144` = `40%`). | Required **nonlinear** scar + underlying soft tissue damage; aggregated qualifying scars within body zones (`10/20/30/40%` at `6/12/72/144 sq in`). | Dropped "nonlinear" label restriction in heading while clarifying **area of deep scars with underlying soft-tissue damage**; clarified aggregation across trunk/extremities. |
| **`DC 7802`** *(Superficial Scars, Nonlinear, Not Head/Face/Neck)* | Superficial scars covering **`>= 144 sq in`** (`1 sq ft`) = **`10%`** (`0%` below `144 sq in`). | Superficial nonlinear scars `>= 144 sq in` = **`10%`**. | Superficial scars not of head/face/neck `>= 144 sq in` = **`10%`**. |
| **`DC 7803`** *(Unstable Superficial Scars)* | Historically **`10%`** for superficial poorly nourished scars with repeated ulceration/breakdown (merged into `DC 7804` on **Oct. 23, 2008**!). | *[Removed & consolidated into `DC 7804`]* | *[Removed — rated under `DC 7804`]* |
| **`DC 7804`** *(Painful or Unstable Scars)* | **Single `10%` Rating Cap** for superficial tender and painful scar(s) on objective demonstration (*pre-Oct. 23, 2008*). *(Note: pre-2008 combinations of `7803` unstable + `7804` painful existed in specific eras!)* | **Count-Based Stacking Revolution!**<br/>• 1–2 painful *or* unstable scars = **`10%`**<br/>• 3–4 painful *or* unstable scars = **`20%`**<br/>• 5+ painful *or* unstable scars = **`30%`**<br/>• **+`10%` Bonus Note (1):** If one or more scars is **BOTH** unstable *and* painful, add `10%`! | Retained `1-2 = 10%`, `3-4 = 20%`, `5+ = 30%`, plus `+10%` Note (1) bonus for both painful and unstable; applies to **any** scar anywhere on the body (including `DC 7800/7801/7802` scars!). |
| **`DC 7805`** *(Other Scars / Disabling Effects)* | Linear scars and functional limitation of motion caused by scar contracture/adherence -> rate on limitation of function of affected body part (e.g., joint ROM, eye, muscle). | Rate disabling functional effects not captured in `7800–7804` under appropriate system code (plus separate `7800–7804` scar ratings per *Cullen* / *Lyles*!). | Explicitly directs rating any functional limitation (e.g., scar limiting knee flexion or damaging muscle/nerve) under the affected body system **in addition to** `DC 7800–7804`! |

## High-Yield Retroactive & CUE Rules

1. **The Pre-2008 vs. Post-2008 `DC 7804` Trap:**
   * **Before October 23, 2008:** Having four painful shrapnel scars on the legs yielded a single `10%` under `DC 7804` (unless separate body regions/deep scars `DC 7801` or `DC 7803` instability applied). Do **not** plead CUE on a 1995 rating demanding `20%` under `DC 7804` for 4 painful scars!
   * **On or After October 23, 2008:** The 2008 rule explicitly invited veterans with existing scar ratings to seek review, and for any claim pending or decided on/after **October 23, 2008**, counting 5 painful surgical/shrapnel scars on a DBQ while awarding only `10%` instead of `30%` is **textbook RO CUE**!
2. **Stacking `DC 7800/7801/7802` + `DC 7804` + `DC 53xx` Muscle (*Esteban* & *Cullen*):**
   * Under *Esteban v. Brown*, 6 Vet. App. 259 (1994) and Note (2) to `DC 7804`, a veteran with facial disfigurement (`DC 7800` at `30%`) whose facial scar is also painful on palpation (`DC 7804` at `10%`) and impairs facial muscles (`DC 5325` / CN VII `DC 8207`) receives **separate stacked evaluations** for all three!
3. **Unfinalized Proposal Alert (`90 FR 46542`, Sept. 29, 2025):**
   * On September 29, 2025, VA proposed adding a note to `DC 7804` requiring *objective evidence of pain upon examination/demonstration* rather than credible subjective/reported scar pain. **As of late 2026, `90 FR 46542` has NOT been published as a Final Rule**—existing `DC 7804` painful scar standards remain binding law!
