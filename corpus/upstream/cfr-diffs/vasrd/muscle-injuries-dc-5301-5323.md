# VASRD Historical Diff: Muscle Injuries (`38 C.F.R. §§ 4.55, 4.56, 4.73`, `DC 5301–5323`)

The watershed dividing line for all muscle injury evaluations is **July 3, 1997** (published June 3, 1997 at `62 FR 30235`, effective July 3, 1997, removing/reserving former `§§ 4.47–4.54, 4.72` and revising `§§ 4.55, 4.56, 4.69, 4.73`; the Feb. 7, 2021 musculoskeletal update `85 FR 76453` added `DC 5330` Rhabdomyolysis and `DC 5331` Compartment Syndrome to `§ 4.73` while leaving `§ 4.56` unchanged).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Current `§ 4.56(a)–(b)` Floor Rules (Effective July 3, 1997 – Present):**
> * **`38 C.F.R. § 4.56(a)`:** An open comminuted fracture with muscle or tendon damage is rated as a **severe** injury of the muscle group involved, unless muscle damage is minimal (e.g., wrist or tibia).
> * **`38 C.F.R. § 4.56(b)`:** **"A through-and-through injury with muscle damage shall be evaluated as no less than a moderate injury for each group of muscles damaged."**
> * **Historical CFR Caution:** While `62 FR 30235` noted the 1997 restructuring was largely editorial rather than a substantive tightening, **never quote post-July 3, 1997 `§ 4.56(b)` verbatim in a CUE motion attacking a pre-July 3, 1997 decision**—always pull and quote the exact historical CFR edition (`§§ 4.47–4.56`) in force on the rating date! Also avoid the shortcut *"retained metallic fragments = automatic severe"*—fragments must be assessed alongside wound mechanism, fascia/muscle loss, and cardinal signs.

## 1. Pre-July 3, 1997 vs. Post-July 3, 1997 (`38 C.F.R. § 4.56`)

| Severity Grade | Era 1: April 1, 1945 – July 2, 1997 (`1945 VASRD § 4.56`) | Era 2: July 3, 1997 – Present (`62 FR 30235`, `38 C.F.R. § 4.56(d)`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Architecture** | Emphasized **type/mechanism of combat wound** ("Factors to be considered in the evaluation of disabilities residual to healed wounds involving muscle groups due to gunshot or other trauma"). | Structured every grade into **(1) Type of Injury**, **(2) History and Complaint**, and **(3) Objective Findings**, anchored to the 5 **cardinal signs and symptoms** (`§ 4.56(c)`: loss of power, weakness, lowered threshold of fatigue, fatigue-pain, impairment of coordination/uncertainty of movement). |
| **Slight** | Simple wound of muscle **without debridement**, without prolonged infection or sloughing, healing with clean linear scar, no retained fragments causing symptoms, trivial functional effect. | **Type:** Simple wound without debridement or infection. **History:** No cardinal signs or controlled by medication. **Objective:** Linear scar, no fascia loss/hernia, no palpably defective muscle, normal strength. |
| **Moderate** | **Type (`§ 4.56(b)`):** *"Through and through or deep penetrating wounds of relatively short track by single bullet or small shell or shrapnel fragment, without explosive effect of high velocity missile, residuals of debridement or prolonged infection."* **History/Objective:** Consistent complaint of `>=1` cardinal symptom (especially lowered fatigue threshold) + T&T or deep scar + definite impairment of use/fatigue/tonus. | **Type (`§ 4.56(d)(2)`):** *"Through and through or deep penetrating wound of short track from a single bullet, small shell or shrapnel fragment, **without** explosive effect of high velocity missile, residuals of debridement, or prolonged infection."* **Plus `§ 4.56(b)` floor:** *"A through-and-through injury with muscle damage shall be evaluated as no less than a moderate injury for each group of muscles damaged."* |
| **Moderately Severe** | **Type (`§ 4.56(c)`):** T&T or deep penetrating wound by high-velocity missile or large/multiple low-velocity fragments, or compound fracture, **or wound requiring extensive debridement or prolonged infection** + prolonged hospitalization + loss of deep fascia/muscle substance. | **Type (`§ 4.56(d)(3)`):** T&T or deep penetrating wound by small high-velocity missile or large low-velocity missile, **with debridement, prolonged infection, or sloughing of soft parts, and intermuscular scarring** + prolonged hospitalization + loss of deep fascia/muscle substance + positive strength/endurance impairment. |

## 2. Why Verbatim `§ 4.56` Text Comparison Wins CUEs (See [`authorities/38-cfr-4.56-muscle-injuries-1946-to-present.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.56-muscle-injuries-1946-to-present.md))

Look closely at the verbatim text of **Moderate** vs. **Moderately Severe** across both eras:
* In **BOTH** the pre-July 3, 1997 (`§ 4.56(b)–(c)`) and post-July 3, 1997 (`§ 4.56(d)(2)–(3)`) regulations, `without` in *Moderate* modifies all three qualifiers (*"without explosive effect of high velocity missile, residuals of debridement, or prolonged infection"*), whereas **surgical debridement** sits in **Moderately Severe**!
* Moreover, on July 3, 1997, VA added explicit `§ 4.56(b)` (*"no less than a moderate injury for each group of muscles damaged"* for any through-and-through muscle injury).
* **CUE Strategy for WWII / Korea / Vietnam / Desert Storm (`1946-04-01` – `1997-07-02`):** Quote the verbatim **pre-July 3, 1997** `38 C.F.R. § 4.56` text from [`38-cfr-4.56-muscle-injuries-1946-to-present.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.56-muscle-injuries-1946-to-present.md)! Whenever the C-file documented a `T&T` wound through a muscle belly with lowered fatigue threshold/weakness (or surgical **debridement** + fascia loss -> *Moderately Severe*!), and the RO assigned `0%` scar (`DC 7804/7805`) while omitting `DC 5301–5323`, the contemporaneous text exposes the misclassification cleanly.

## 3. High-Value Muscle Group Rating Percentages (`38 C.F.R. § 4.73`)

Always check **Dominant (Major) vs. Non-Dominant (Minor)** hand on `DC 5301–5309` (`38 C.F.R. § 4.69`)!

| Muscle Group & Code | Anatomical Function / Location | Slight | Moderate | Mod. Severe | Severe |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Group I (`DC 5301`)** | Extrinsic shoulder girdle (`trapezius`, `levator scapulae`, `serratus magnus`) | `0%` | `10%` / `10%` | `20%` (Maj) / `10%` (Min) | `40%` (Maj) / `30%` (Min) |
| **Group III (`DC 5303`)** | Intrinsic shoulder (`deltoid`) | `0%` | `20%` (Maj) / `10%` (Min) | `30%` (Maj) / `20%` (Min) | `50%` (Maj) / `40%` (Min) |
| **Group IV (`DC 5304`)** | Shoulder rotation (`supraspinatus`, `infraspinatus`, `teres minor`, `subscapularis` — rotator cuff!) | `0%` | `10%` / `10%` | `20%` / `20%` | `40%` (Maj) / `30%` (Min) |
| **Group V (`DC 5305`)** | Elbow flexors (`biceps`, `brachialis`, `brachioradialis`) | `0%` | `10%` / `10%` | `30%` (Maj) / `20%` (Min) | `40%` (Maj) / `30%` (Min) |
| **Group XI (`DC 5311`)** | Posterior lower leg / calf & plantar flexion (`gastrocnemius`, `soleus`, `tibialis posterior`) | `0%` | **`10%`** | **`20%`** | **`30%`** |
| **Group XII (`DC 5312`)** | Anterior lower leg / dorsiflexion (`tibialis anterior`, extensor digitorum) | `0%` | **`10%`** | **`20%`** | **`30%`** |
| **Group XIII (`DC 5313`)** | Posterior thigh / hamstrings (`biceps femoris`, `semimembranosus`, `semitendinosus`) | **`10%`** *(even Slight!)* | **`20%`** | **`30%`** | **`40%`** |
| **Group XIV (`DC 5314`)** | Anterior thigh / quadriceps (`rectus femoris`, `vastus lateralis/medialis`) | `0%` | **`10%`** | **`20%`** | **`40%`** |
| **Group XVII (`DC 5317`)** | Gluteal group (`gluteus maximus`, `medius`, `minimus`) | `0%` | **`20%`** | **`40%`** | **`50%`** *(or SMC!)* |
| **Group XIX (`DC 5319`)** | Abdominal wall (`rectus abdominis`, `obliques`) | `0%` | **`10%`** | **`20%`** | **`30%`** |
| **Group XX (`DC 5320`)** | Spinal muscles (`erector spinae`, thoracolumbar/cervical musculature) | `0%` | **`10%`** / `10%` | **`20%`** / `20%` | **`40%`** (Thor-Lumb) / `30%` (Cerv) |

> [!TIP]
> **Look at `DC 5313` (Posterior Thigh / Hamstrings), `DC 5317` (Gluteal Group) & `DC 5331` (Compartment Syndrome):** Under `DC 5313`, **even a SLIGHT injury pays `10%`**, and *Moderate* pays **`20%`**! Under `DC 5317` (buttock shrapnel/GSW wounds—exceedingly common in Vietnam/Korea!), *Moderate* jumps straight from `0%` to **`20%`**, and *Moderately Severe* jumps to **`40%`**! And effective **February 7, 2021** (`85 FR 76453`), **`DC 5331` (Compartment Syndrome)** explicitly mandates evaluating **each affected muscle group separately** under `38 C.F.R. § 4.25` (e.g., separate anterior `DC 5312` + posterior `DC 5311` lower-leg compartments after fasciotomy)!
