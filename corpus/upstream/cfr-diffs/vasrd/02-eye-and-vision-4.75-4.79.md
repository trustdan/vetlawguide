## 2. Three-Era General Rating Formula Evolution (`1946` vs. `2008` vs. `2018`)

See [`38-cfr-4.75-4.79-eye-evolution.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.75-4.79-eye-evolution.md) for verbatim Era-1, Era-2, and Era-3 regulatory text.

### A. Side-by-Side Structural & Threshold Matrix

| Dimension | Era 1: `Apr. 1, 1946 – Dec. 9, 2008` *(Former `§§ 4.75–4.84a`)* | Era 2: `Dec. 10, 2008 – May 12, 2018` *(`73 FR 66543`, pub. Nov. 10, 2008)* | Era 3: `May 13, 2018 – Present` *(`83 FR 15316`, pub. Apr. 10, 2018)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Governing Rule** | Condition-specific schedules (`DC 6000–6009` rated `10%` during *"active pathology"* unilateral or bilateral, else on visual residuals). | **General Rating Formula for Diagnostic Codes 6000 through 6009:** Rate on **visual impairment (`§§ 4.75–4.78`)** OR **total duration of incapacitating episodes** over past `12 months`, whichever is higher. | **General Rating Formula for Diseases of the Eye:** Rate on **visual impairment (`§§ 4.75–4.78`)** OR **number of clinic treatment visits** over past `12 months`, whichever is higher. |
| **`60%` Tier** | Visual acuity / field residuals only. | Incapacitating episodes totaling **at least `6 weeks`** during past `12 months`. | Documented incapacitating episodes requiring **`7 or more` (`>= 7`) treatment visits** for an eye condition during past `12 months`. |
| **`40%` Tier** | Visual acuity / field residuals only. | Incapacitating episodes totaling **at least `4 weeks`, but `< 6 weeks`**, during past `12 months`. | Documented incapacitating episodes requiring **at least `5` but `< 7` (`5–6`) treatment visits** during past `12 months`. |
| **`20%` Tier** | Visual acuity / field residuals only (`10%` active pathology cap). | Incapacitating episodes totaling **at least `2 weeks`, but `< 4 weeks`**, during past `12 months`. | Documented incapacitating episodes requiring **at least `3` but `< 5` (`3–4`) treatment visits** during past `12 months`. |

| **Dedicated Retinal / Neuro-Visual Codes** | Pooled under `DC 6006` Retinopathy, `DC 6026` Optic atrophy, or analogous `DC 6080`. | Pooled under `DC 6006` Retinopathy/maculopathy; no standalone diabetic retinopathy, AMD, or post-chiasmal code. | **Added `DC 6040` (Diabetic retinopathy)**, **`DC 6042` (Retinal dystrophy: RP, wet/dry AMD, cone-rod)**, and **`DC 6046` (Post-chiasmal disorders)**. |

### B. The Post-May 13, 2018 Anti-VEGF Injection & Biologic Goldmine (`cue-and-open-claim`)
* **Why `83 FR 15316` Transformed Retinal & Uveitic Ratings:** Prior to May 13, 2018, veterans with **proliferative diabetic retinopathy (`DC 6040`)**, **neovascular ("wet") macular degeneration (`DC 6042`)**, **retinal vein occlusion (`DC 6006`)**, or **autoimmune uveitis (`DC 6000`)** who received monthly or bimonthly **intravitreal anti-VEGF injections** (*aflibercept [Eylea], ranibizumab [Lucentis], bevacizumab [Avastin], faricimab [Vabysmo]*) or **focal/panretinal laser photocoagulation** preserved `20/30` or `20/40` central acuity and were routinely stuck at **`0%` or `10%`** because outpatient injections did not cause "4 to 6 weeks" of continuous incapacitation.
* **Exact Post-May 13, 2018 Audit Rule:** Under Note (1) and Note (2) to `§ 4.79` (`>= May 13, 2018`), **every clinic visit where an intravitreal/periocular injection, laser procedure, surgical intervention, or systemic immunosuppressant/biologic administration/adjustment is performed for treatment purposes counts as `1` qualifying treatment visit**:
  * Every `8 weeks` (`6` injection visits/year) -> **`40%`** (`5–6` visits);
  * Every `4 to 6 weeks` (`7–12` injection visits/year, or bilateral alternating-visit injections!) -> **`60%`** (`>= 7` visits)!
* **Kill-Switch Screen:** Purely **diagnostic, monitoring, refraction, OCT imaging, or intraocular-pressure checkup visits** where no active treatment procedure is performed or initiated **do NOT count** under Note (1).

## 3. Central Visual Acuity (`§§ 4.75–4.76`, `DC 6061–6066`) & Four High-Yield Audit Traps

Under `38 C.F.R. § 4.75(b)`, every VA visual impairment examination **must** be conducted by a **licensed optometrist or ophthalmologist** using standard Snellen test types (`20 feet / 6 meters` distance; `14 inches / 35 cm` near) or equivalent.

### A. Trap 1 — Unilateral SC Eye Presumed `20/40` (`§ 4.75(c)`) vs. `38 U.S.C. § 1160(a)(1)` Paired-Organ Unlock!
* **Default Unilateral Rule (`38 C.F.R. § 4.75(c)–(d)`):** When visual impairment of **only one eye** is service-connected, the visual acuity of the **non-service-connected (`NSC`) eye must be considered `20/40`** when reading across `Table V` (`DC 6063–6066`), capping unilateral functional visual impairment at **`30%`** (`DC 6064` LPO or `DC 6065` `5/200` paired with `20/40` = `30%`; `DC 6063` anatomical loss paired with `20/40` = `40%`, or **`50%`** if unable to wear an ocular prosthesis under `§ 4.75(e)`).
* **The `§ 1160(a)(1)` / `§ 3.383(a)(1)` Paired-Organ Exception (`cue-and-open-claim`):** Whenever the veteran has **compensable or noncompensable service-connected blindness/impairment in one eye** (`<= 20/200` or peripheral field `<= 20°` SC eye) AND suffers **non-service-connected (`NSC`) blindness in the other eye** (not due to willful misconduct) reducing the NSC eye to:
  1. Central visual acuity of **`20/200` or less**, **OR**
  2. Peripheral visual field widest diameter of **`20 degrees` or less**,
  **`38 U.S.C. § 1160(a)(1)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.383(a)(1)` override `§ 4.75(c)` and mandate rating BOTH eyes together "as if" service-connected**—jumping the veteran from `30%` unilateral all the way to **`70%`–`100%` + `SMC(l)`–`(n)` + `Ch. 35` DEA** (*Kimberlin v. Brown*, `5 Vet. App. 174 (1993)`)!

### B. Trap 2 — The `> 3.0 Diopters` Anisometropia Uncorrected-Acuity Rule (`38 C.F.R. § 4.76(b)(1)`)
* Normally, central visual acuity is rated on **best-corrected** distance vision.
* **Critical Regulatory Carve-Out (`§ 4.76(b)(1)`):** When the corrective lens power (spherical equivalent) required to correct distance vision in the **poorer eye differs by MORE than `3.0 diopters`** from the lens required for the **better eye** (*and* the difference is **not** due to congenital or developmental refractive error—e.g., post-traumatic cataract extraction, unilateral corneal surgery/scar, scleral buckle), and the poorer eye (or both eyes) is SC, the rater **MUST evaluate the poorer eye using EITHER its uncorrected OR corrected visual acuity, whichever yields the higher combined rating**! Raters routinely overlook refraction diopter boxes on the Eye DBQ, creating clean **RO CUE** (`>= Dec. 10, 2008`).

### C. Trap 3 — Corneal Contact-Lens Tolerance (`§ 4.76(b)(2)`), Near-Vision Step-Down (`§ 4.76(b)(3)`) & Intermediate Rounding (`§ 4.76(b)(4)`)
1. **Corneal Irregular Astigmatism & Contact Lenses (`§ 4.76(b)(2)` — Crucial for `DC 6035` Keratoconus & `DC 6036` Corneal Transplant):** Even if rigid gas-permeable (`RGP`) or scleral contact lenses correct a keratoconus eye to `20/25`, VA may rate on contact-lens acuity **ONLY if the veteran *"customarily wears contact lenses"*!** If corneal pain, dry eye, or graft ectasia prevents customary contact-lens wear, VA **must** rate on **spectacle-corrected** acuity!
2. **Near-Vision Discrepancy (`§ 4.76(b)(3)`):** If corrected near vision (`14 in.`) is **`2 or more` scheduled steps worse** than corrected distance vision (verified by `>= 2` recordings + clinical explanation, e.g., posterior subcapsular cataract or accommodative palsy), rate the eye at **one step poorer** than measured corrected distance vision!
3. **Intermediate Acuity Rounding (`§ 4.76(b)(4)`):** Any Snellen measurement falling *between* two listed Table V benchmarks (`20/40`, `20/50`, `20/70`, `20/100`, `20/200`, `15/200`, `10/200`, `5/200`, `LPO`) **must** be rounded to the poorer Snellen benchmark permitting the **higher evaluation** (e.g., `20/60 -> 20/70`; `20/80 -> 20/100`; `20/150 -> 20/200`).

### D. Trap 4 — Cataract Pseudophakia (`DC 6027`) vs. True Aphakia (`DC 6029`)
* **`DC 6027` (Cataract of any type):** Preoperatively, rate under the General Rating Formula. Postoperatively, if an **intraocular replacement lens (`IOL` — pseudophakia)** is present, rate under the General Rating Formula (acuity/field residuals or post-op treatment visits—**no** automatic `30%` minimum!).
* **`DC 6029` (Aphakia or dislocation of crystalline lens — NO replacement `IOL`):** When the natural lens is absent or dislocated **without** an implanted `IOL`, evaluate based on visual impairment, **elevate the resulting visual acuity level by ONE STEP poorer**, and enforce a **mandatory `30%` minimum evaluation** (unilateral or bilateral)!

## 4. Visual Fields (`§ 4.77`, `DC 6080–6081`, `DC 6046`) & Muscle Function / Diplopia (`§ 4.78`, `DC 6090`)

### A. Visual Field Perimetry Standards (`§ 4.77`) & `DC 6080–6081` Equivalent Acuity Rules
* **Approved Perimetry Equipment (`§ 4.77(a)–(b)`):** Examiners must use **Goldmann kinetic perimetry** OR automated perimetry with simulated kinetic Goldmann capability (**Humphrey Model 750**, **Octopus Model 101**, or later versions of these perimetric devices), testing **`III/4e`** target isopter (`IV/4e` for aphakic/pseudophakic eyes not well adapted to contact lens/IOL), documenting results for **at least `16` meridians `22½ degrees` apart for each eye** (`§ 4.77(a)`), averaging remaining field across the **`8` principal meridians `45 degrees` apart** (`§ 4.77(b)`), and—when additional testing is indicated—conducting either a **tangent screen** or a **`30-degree` threshold visual field with Goldmann `III` stimulus size**.
* **Combining Visual Field Loss + Acuity Loss in the Same Eye (`§ 4.77(c)` & `DC 6080`):** Under **`38 C.F.R. § 4.77(c)`**, when **both** decreased visual acuity and a visual field defect are present in one or both eyes and are service-connected, VA must **separately evaluate** the visual acuity and the visual field defect (using `DC 6080`'s standalone percentage or its equivalent Snellen acuity `20/50` down to `5/200` on `DC 6065–6066`) **and combine them under `38 C.F.R. § 4.25`** (subject to `§ 4.75(d)`'s `30%` unilateral visual-impairment cap)!
  * **Homonymous hemianopsia** *(loss of same right or left half of field in both eyes — classic post-TBI/stroke **`DC 6046` Post-chiasmal disorder** residual!)*: **`30%`**.
  * **Loss of temporal half OR inferior half:** **`30%` bilateral** \| **`10%` unilateral** *(or evaluate affected eye as **`20/70`**)*.
  * **Loss of nasal half OR superior half:** **`10%` bilateral** \| **`10%` unilateral** *(or evaluate affected eye as **`20/50`**)*.
  * **Concentric Contraction (`Remaining Average Field Radius`):**
    * **`<= 5°`:** **`100%` bilateral (+ `SMC(l)` under `§ 3.350(b)(2)`!)** \| **`30%` unilateral** *(or evaluate as **`5/200`**)*;
    * **`6° to 15°`:** **`70%` bilateral** \| **`20%` unilateral** *(or evaluate as **`20/200`**)*;
    * **`16° to 30°`:** **`50%` bilateral** \| **`10%` unilateral** *(or evaluate as **`20/100`**)*;
    * **`31° to 45°`:** **`30%` bilateral** \| **`10%` unilateral** *(or evaluate as **`20/70`**)*;
    * **`46° to 60°`:** **`10%` bilateral** \| **`10%` unilateral** *(or evaluate as **`20/50`**)*.
  * **`DC 6081` (Scotoma, unilateral):** Centrally located scotoma of any size, OR scotoma affecting at least one-quarter (`quadrantanopsia`) of the visual field -> **`10%`** *(combine or substitute acuity if higher)*.

### B. Diplopia (`§ 4.78`, `DC 6090`) — Single-Eye Assignment & Mandatory Step-Up Multiplier
* **Goldmann `III/4e` Diplopia Mapping (`DC 6090`):** Mapped within `40°` of primary gaze (**Note:** occasional diplopia or diplopia **correctable with prism spectacles** is rated **`0%`** under `§ 4.31`):
  * **Central `20 degrees`:** Equivalent to **`5/200`** (`30%` unilateral when paired with `20/40`);
  * **`21° to 30°`:** **Down = `15/200`** (`20%`) \| **Lateral = `20/100`** (`10%`) \| **Up = `20/70`** (`10%`);
  * **`31° to 40°`:** **Down = `20/200`** (`20%`) \| **Lateral = `20/70`** (`10%`) \| **Up = `20/40`** (`0%`).
* **The `§ 4.78(b)(1)` One-to-Three Step Elevation Rule (`cue-and-open-claim`):**
  1. Assign an evaluation for diplopia to **ONLY ONE EYE** (never `DC 6090` bilaterally!).
  2. When the veteran has **BOTH diplopia AND decreased visual acuity or a visual field defect**, adjust the corrected visual acuity of the poorer eye (or the SC eye, if unilateral) poorer by:
     * **`+1 step` poorer** if `DC 6090` diplopia equals **`20/70` or `20/100`**;
     * **`+2 steps` poorer** if `DC 6090` diplopia equals **`20/200` or `15/200`**;
     * **`+3 steps` poorer** if `DC 6090` diplopia equals **`5/200`** (capped at `5/200`, i.e., step-ups cannot fabricate `LPO`!).
