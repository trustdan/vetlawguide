# VASRD System 10 Historical Diff: Hemic & Lymphatic Systems, December 9, 2018 Overhaul (`83 FR 54250` / `87 FR 61248`), Leukemias/Myeloma/Lymphomas, AL Amyloidosis & MGUS (`38 C.F.R. § 4.117`, `DC 7700–7725`)

This Reference module catalogs the Federal Register transitions (`1946–2026`), pre- vs. post-December 9, 2018 (`83 FR 54250`) Diagnostic Code (`DC 7700–7725`) restructurings, hematologic malignancy post-treatment floors (`6 months` vs. `2 years` vs. `5 years` vs. permanent `100%`), toxic-exposure presumptive hooks (`Nehmer`, Camp Lejeune, Radiation, and the January 10, 2025 `38 C.F.R. § 3.320b` burn-pit IFR), and ***George v. McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) / `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`** bitemporal boundaries governing **Body System 10 (The Hematologic and Lymphatic Systems)** under `38 C.F.R. § 4.117`.

*(For verbatim historical and current regulatory text of `38 C.F.R. § 4.117`, see [`38-cfr-4.117-hemic-lymphatic-evolution.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.117-hemic-lymphatic-evolution.md), [`38-cfr-3.105e-3.344-3.951-3.957-reductions.md`](../../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.105e-3.344-3.951-3.957-reductions.md), and [`38-cfr-4.1-4.31-general-policy-snapshots.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.1-4.31-general-policy-snapshots.md); for Agent Orange `§ 3.816` *Nehmer* class retroactivity on B-cell leukemias, Hodgkin's, NHL, Multiple Myeloma, and AL Amyloidosis vs. PACT `§ 404` MGUS exclusions and `10 U.S.C. § 1413a` CRSC *Soto* recovery, see [Playbook 06 (*Nehmer* Herbicide Presumptions & CRSC)](../../playbooks/06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md); for the January 10, 2025 `38 C.F.R. § 3.320b` (`90 FR 1894`) burn-pit hematologic cancer presumptions, see [Playbook 15 (Gulf War MUCMIs, Burn Pits & PACT/TERA)](../../playbooks/15-gulf-war-mucmi-burn-pits-pact-tera.md); for Camp Lejeune, radiation, and `§ 3.309(a)` 1-year chronic blood/lymphatic presumptions, see [Playbook 07 (Statutory Presumptions)](../../playbooks/07-statutory-presumptions-chronic-tropical-pow-als.md); for hereditary blood disorders (`DC 7714` Sickle Cell Anemia), see [Soundness, Aggravation & Hereditary Diseases Treatise](../../docs/service-connection-direct-soundness-aggravation.md) and [Master `VAOPGCPREC` Index](../../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md); for tuberculous adenitis (`DC 7710`) and HIV/infectious hematologic overlaps, see [`System 04 Infectious & Immune Diff`](04-infectious-immune-nutritional-4.88b.md); for pernicious anemia (`DC 7722`) neurocognitive/neuropathy cross-hooks, see [`System 13 Neurological Diff`](13-neurological-tbi-nerves-4.120-4.124a.md) and [`System 14 Mental Disorders Diff`](14-mental-disorders-4.125-4.130.md); and see [Authority Verification Log](../../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Anemias (`Former DC 7700` -> `DC 7720–7723`)** | Single blanket **`DC 7700` (Anemia, hypochromic-microcytic and megaloblastic)** rated strictly by static **`gm/100ml` hemoglobin ladder** + clinical signs (`<= 5g = 100%`; `<= 7g = 70%`; `<= 8g = 30%`; `<= 10g = 10%`; `> 10g` asymptomatic `= 0%`). | **`DC 7700` REPEALED.** Replaced by four etiology/treatment-driven codes:<br/>• **`DC 7720` Iron deficiency anemia** (`30%/10%/0%` IV infusions vs. oral iron; secondary blood-loss excluded per Note!)<br/>• **`DC 7721` Folic acid deficiency** (`10%/0%`)<br/>• **`DC 7722` Pernicious / Vitamin B12 deficiency anemia** (`100%/10%` + separate CNS/neuropathy!)<br/>• **`DC 7723` Acquired hemolytic anemia** (`100%/60%/30%/10%/0%` immunosuppressives/transplant). | **Liberalization for Medicated Veterans:** Under Era 2, a veteran on lifelong B12 injections or oral iron whose medicated hemoglobin normalized above `10 g/dL` got `0%`; under Era 3 `DC 7720–7722`, continuous oral iron, high-dose folate, or B12 injections/nasal/sublingual therapy earns **`10%`** regardless of normalized hemoglobin! |

## 3. Hematologic Malignancies, Post-Treatment Convalescent Floors & *Foster* Notice Traps

### A. Side-by-Side Mandatory `100%` Duration & Post-Treatment Re-Evaluation Matrix

A recurrent RO error in System 10 is **confusing the post-treatment mandatory `100%` duration across different hematologic malignancies** (not every blood cancer drops after `6 months`!), or terminating `100%` via an automated diary date without a C&P exam and **`38 C.F.R. § 3.105(e)`** notice:

| DC | Malignancy / Plasma Cell Disorder | `100%` Trigger & Mandatory Duration Floor | Post-Floor Examination, `§ 3.105(e)` Notice (*Foster*) & Residual Stacking |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`7703`** | **Leukemia** *(Acute myeloid `AML`, acute lymphoblastic `ALL`, hairy cell, symptomatic `CLL` Rai I–IV; excludes `CML` `7719` & Rai 0 `CLL`)* | **`100%`** during active disease or treatment phase **+ `6 months` beyond cessation** of any surgical, radiation, antineoplastic chemotherapy, or other therapeutic procedures. *(Rai Stage 0 asymptomatic `CLL` / `MBL` = **`0%`** post-Dec. 9, 2018).* | **Mandatory VA examination** at `6 months` post-treatment cessation; any reduction requires **`60-day` `§ 3.105(e)` notice** (*Foster v. McDonough*, `34 Vet. App. 338 (2021)`, though `§§ 3.343/3.344` do not apply). Rate chronic chemo-induced peripheral neuropathy (`DC 8520`/`8515`), cardiomyopathy (`DC 7020`), cytopenias, fatigue, and psychiatric depression (`§ 4.130`) separately! |
| **`7709`** | **Hodgkin's lymphoma** | **`100%`** with active disease or during treatment phase **+ `6 months` after discontinuance** of surgical, radiation, chemotherapy, or other therapeutic procedures. | **Mandatory VA examination** at `6 months` + `§ 3.105(e)` notice. Audit post-mantle-radiation **secondary hypothyroidism (`DC 7903`)**, **coronary/valvular heart disease (`DC 7005`/`7000`)**, **pulmonary fibrosis (`DC 6825`)**, and bleomycin/vinblastine neuropathy! |
| **`7715`** | **Non-Hodgkin's lymphoma (`NHL`)** *(Includes indolent/follicular, diffuse large B-cell, mantle cell, cutaneous T-cell mycosis fungoides, Waldenström's macroglobulinemia)* | **`100%`** when there is active disease, during a treatment phase, **OR with indolent and non-contiguous phase of low-grade `NHL`** **+ `TWO (2) YEARS` after discontinuance** of surgical, radiation, chemotherapy, or other therapeutic procedures! | **CRITICAL `2-YEAR` FLOOR TRAP:** Unlike `DC 7703`/`7709` (`6 months`), `DC 7715` guarantees **`100%` for `2 full years` post-treatment**—AND low-grade/indolent non-contiguous `NHL` in `"watch and wait"` remains **`100%` indefinitely** (unlike Rai 0 `CLL`)! Cutting `NHL` at 6 months is undebatable **RO CUE** (`cue-and-open-claim`). |

Even when `MGUS` or smoldering myeloma is rated `0%`, **separately rate** paraproteinemic neuropathy, light-chain renal disease (`DC 7530`), pathologic vertebral/rib fractures, and secondary mood disorder. |

Mar. 9, 2012; retained `83 FR 54250`). | **NO future examination scheduled.** Immediately audit for **`38 U.S.C. § 1114(s)` (`SMC(s)`)** (*Bradley* statutory `100% + 60%`) or **`SMC(l)–(r)`** by separately evaluating amyloid restrictive cardiomyopathy (`DC 7020`), nephrotic syndrome (`DC 7502`), autonomic/peripheral neuropathy, and GI malabsorption ([Playbook 12](../../playbooks/12-aid-and-attendance-smc-l-r1-r2-and-smc-t.md))! |
| **`7724`** | **Solitary plasmacytoma** *(Osseous or extramedullary)* | **`100%`** during active disease or treatment phase (including radiation, surgery, chemo, or autologous stem-cell transplant) **+ `6 months` after discontinuance** (`Note (1)`). | **Mandatory VA examination** at `6 months` + `§ 3.105(e)` notice; rate skeletal/neurological/radiation residuals separately, and monitor closely (`~50%+` risk) for progression to **`DC 7712` Multiple Myeloma (`100%`)**! |

### B. Toxic-Exposure Matrix Across `DC 7703–7725` (`Nehmer` vs. PACT `§ 404` vs. `§ 3.320b` Burn Pits)

* **Agent Orange / Herbicide Exposure (`38 U.S.C. § 1116`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.309(e), 3.816`):**
  * **Full `§ 3.816` *Nehmer* Class Conditions (Footnote 1 & 2 Retroactive Back Pay + Uncapped `10 U.S.C. § 1413a` CRSC per *Soto v. United States*, `605 U.S. 360 (2025)`):** All **Chronic B-cell Leukemias** (`CLL` including hairy cell leukemia — `DC 7703`), **Hodgkin's Lymphoma (`DC 7709`)**, **Non-Hodgkin's Lymphoma (`DC 7715`, plus `§ 3.313` Vietnam waters/shore)**, **Multiple Myeloma (`DC 7712`)**, and **`AL` Amyloidosis (`DC 7717`)** ([Playbook 06](../../playbooks/06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md)).
  * **Non-*Nehmer* PACT `§ 404` Addition (`>= Aug. 10, 2022`):** **`MGUS` (`DC 7712` `0%`)** is presumptive for herbicide-exposed cohorts effective August 10, 2022 (`§ 3.114(a)`), **without** `§ 3.816` *Nehmer* class retroactivity—**EXCEPT** that if medical records show `MGUS` converted into **symptomatic Multiple Myeloma (`DC 7712` — `ASH`/`IMWG` `>= 60%` clonal bone marrow plasma cells, free light-chain ratio `>= 100`, `> 1` MRI focal lesion, or `CRAB` hypercalcemia/renal/anemia/bone lesions)** or **`AL` Amyloidosis (`DC 7717`)**, re-plead immediately as a **full *Nehmer* class claim**!
* **Gulf War & Post-9/11 Burn-Pit Fine Particulate Presumptions (`38 C.F.R. § 3.320b`, `90 FR 1894`, eff. Jan. 10, 2025):**
  * Effective **January 10, 2025**, VA added **acute leukemias, chronic leukemias (`DC 7703` & `DC 7719` — ALL types, myeloid and lymphoid!), multiple myeloma (including `MGUS`, `DC 7712`), myelodysplastic syndromes (`DC 7725`), and myelofibrosis (`DC 7718`)** as presumptive diseases for Persian Gulf (`>= Aug. 2, 1990`) and Post-9/11 (`>= Sept. 11, 2001` incl. Afghanistan, Djibouti, Syria, Uzbekistan) veterans ([Playbook 15](../../playbooks/15-gulf-war-mucmi-burn-pits-pact-tera.md)).
* **Camp Lejeune Contaminated Water (`38 C.F.R. § 3.307(a)(7)(iii)`):** `>= 30` cumulative days (`Aug. 1, 1953 – Dec. 31, 1987`) triggers presumptive SC for **adult leukemia (`7703`/`7719`), aplastic anemia and other myelodysplastic syndromes (`7716`/`7725`), multiple myeloma (`7712`), and non-Hodgkin's lymphoma (`7715`)** ([Playbook 07](../../playbooks/07-statutory-presumptions-chronic-tropical-pow-als.md)).
