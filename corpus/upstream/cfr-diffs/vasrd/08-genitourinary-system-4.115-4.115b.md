# VASRD System 08 Historical Diff: Genitourinary System Two-Era Dysfunction Matrices (`1994` Creatinine/`BUN` vs. `Nov. 14, 2021` `eGFR`/Albuminuria), Voiding Pad/Frequency Traps, Prostate/Bladder Cancers (`DC 7528` & `§ 3.320a`) & `ED`/`SMC(k)` (`38 C.F.R. §§ 4.115–4.115b`, `DC 7500–7545`)

This Reference module catalogs the Federal Register transitions (`1946–2026`), the **February 17, 1994 (`59 FR 2523`)** and **November 14, 2021 (`86 FR 54081`, `RIN 2900-AQ71`)** `38 C.F.R. §§ 4.115–4.115b` overhauls, two-era **Renal Dysfunction** (`BUN`/creatinine/HTN vs. `GFR`/`eGFR` + `ACR` albuminuria), **Voiding Dysfunction** (absorbent pads vs. frequency vs. obstructed voiding predominant-area ceiling), **Urinary Tract Infection** (`UTI`), Diagnostic Code (`DC 7500–7545`) schedules, `DC 7528` malignancy discontinuance protections (*Tatum* & *Foster*), `DC 7522` Erectile Dysfunction & `SMC(k)` rules (*Williams*, *Payne*, `VAOPGCPREC 5-89`), toxic-exposure GU cancer presumptions (`Nehmer`, Camp Lejeune, PACT Act, and the **January 2, 2025 `38 C.F.R. § 3.320a` burn-pit IFR**), and ***George v. McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) / `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`** bitemporal boundaries governing **Body System 08 (The Genitourinary System)**.

## 2. Two-Era `38 C.F.R. § 4.115a` Dysfunction Matrices

See [`38-cfr-4.115a-4.115b-genitourinary-evolution.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.115a-4.115b-genitourinary-evolution.md) for verbatim Era-2 (`1994–2021`) and Era-3 (`>= Nov. 14, 2021`) text.

### A. Renal Dysfunction: `1994–2021` (`BUN`/Creatinine/Edema/HTN) vs. `>= Nov. 14, 2021` (`GFR`/`eGFR` & `ACR`)

| Rating Tier | Era 2: `Feb. 17, 1994 – Nov. 13, 2021` *(`59 FR 2523` — Crucial for *Nehmer* / Legacy Audits!)* | Era 3: `Nov. 14, 2021 – Present` *(`86 FR 54081` — `GFR` Sustained `>= 3 Consecutive Months` in Past `12 Mos`!)* |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`100%`** | Requiring **regular dialysis**, OR precluding more than sedentary activity from one of: `(a)` persistent edema and albuminuria; `(b)` **`BUN > 80 mg%`**; `(c)` **`creatinine > 8 mg%`**; OR `(d)` markedly decreased function of kidney or other organ systems, especially cardiovascular. | • **Chronic kidney disease (`CKD`) with `GFR < 15 mL/min/1.73 m²`** for at least `3` consecutive months during the past `12` months; **OR**<br/>• Requiring **regular routine dialysis**; **OR**<br/>• **Eligible kidney transplant recipient**. |
| **`80%`** | Persistent edema and albuminuria with **`BUN 40 to 80 mg%`**; OR **`creatinine 4 to 8 mg%`**; OR generalized poor health characterized by lethargy, weakness, anorexia, weight loss, or limitation of exertion. | **`CKD` with `GFR 15 to 29 mL/min/1.73 m²`** (`CKD Stage 4`) for at least `3` consecutive months during the past `12` months. |
| **`60%`** | **Constant albuminuria with some edema**; OR **definite decrease in kidney function**; OR **hypertension at least `40%` disabling under `DC 7101`**! | **`CKD` with `GFR 30 to 44 mL/min/1.73 m²`** (`CKD Stage 3b`) for at least `3` consecutive months during the past `12` months. |
| **`30%`** | **Albumin constant or recurring with hyaline and granular casts or red blood cells**; OR **transient or slight edema OR hypertension at least `10%` disabling under `DC 7101`**! | **`CKD` with `GFR 45 to 59 mL/min/1.73 m²`** (`CKD Stage 3a`) for at least `3` consecutive months during the past `12` months *(Note: `GFR 45–59` sustained `>=3` consecutive months warrants **`30%` on `GFR` alone** without needing albuminuria or edema!)*. |
| **`0%`** | **Albumin and casts with history of acute nephritis**; OR **hypertension noncompensable (`0%`) under `DC 7101`**. | **`GFR 60 to 89 mL/min/1.73 m²`** (`CKD Stage 2`) **AND** at least ONE of the following for `>=3` consecutive months during past `12` months: `(1)` structural kidney abnormalities (*cystic, obstructive, or glomerular*); `(2)` **albumin/creatinine ratio (`ACR`) `>= 30 mg/g`** *(microalbuminuria/proteinuria)*; OR `(3)` recurrent red blood cell (`RBC`), white blood cell (`WBC`), or granular casts. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Three High-Dollar Renal Dysfunction Audit Rules (`§ 4.115`, `§ 4.115a` Note & `38 U.S.C. § 1160(a)(2)`):**
> 1. **`eGFR` Lab Goldmine in Diabetic (`DC 7913 -> 7541`) & Hypertensive (`DC 7101 -> 7502/7534`) Veterans:** Every VAMC routine Basic/Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (`BMP`/`CMP`) prints calculated **`eGFR`** alongside serum creatinine, and `§ 4.115a`'s Note explicitly accepts **`eGFR` / creatinine-based approximations**! Whenever VAMC labs show `eGFR 45–59` (`30%`), `30–44` (`60%`), `15–29` (`80%`), or `<15` (`100%`) across `>=3` consecutive months in an Agent Orange Diabetic or PACT Hypertensive veteran, **file/audit secondary `DC 7541` / `DC 7502` immediately** (remembering `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(b)` *Lang* constructive VAMC lab receipt in legacy claims)!
> 2. **The Unchanged `38 C.F.R. § 4.115` Nephritis–Heart Non-Combination Bar (and Its Two Carve-Outs!):** **`86 FR 54081` (eff. Nov. 14, 2021) did NOT amend `38 C.F.R. § 4.115`**! Under `§ 4.115`, ratings for **heart disease and any form of nephritis still CANNOT be combined**—instead, assign one predominant evaluation (elevating if warranted, or under `DC 7507` arteriolar nephrosclerosis evaluating under whichever system predominates)—**EXCEPT in two explicit carve-outs where heart and renal ratings DO combine separately:** `(1)` **absence of one kidney (`DC 7500`)** is the sole renal disability, **OR `(2)` chronic renal disease requires regular dialysis (`100%`)**! *(Note: Standalone `DC 7101` Hypertension without nephritis—or coexisting with non-nephritic voiding codes—combines normally, and pre-Nov. 14, 2021 `30%` or `60%` renal awards anchored on `DC 7101` `10%`/`40%` hypertension with normal `GFR >= 60` remain **statutorily grandfathered under `38 U.S.C. § 1155`**!)*
> 3. **Paired-Kidney Statutory Service Connection (`38 U.S.C. § 1160(a)(2)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.383(a)(2)`):** If a veteran has service-connected loss or kidney disease of **one** kidney (e.g., `DC 7500` unilateral nephrectomy for trauma/cancer at `30%` minimum) and subsequently develops non-service-connected `CKD`/failure in the **other** kidney (not due to willful misconduct), VA **must** compensate the combined bilateral renal impairment (`60%/80%/100%`) *as if* both kidneys were service-connected!

### B. Voiding Dysfunction (`60/40/30/20/10/0%` — The Predominant-Area Trap!) & `UTI` (`30/10/0%`)

Under [`38 C.F.R. § 4.115a`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.115a-4.115b-genitourinary-evolution.md), **Voiding Dysfunction** remained structurally consistent across `1994` and `2021`, divided into **three sub-tracks**—while **Urinary Tract Infection (`UTI`)** gained explicit stent/nephrostomy (`30%`) and `6-month` suppressive-therapy (`10%`) rules on November 14, 2021 (`86 FR 54081`):

| Sub-Track | Rating Tiers & Exact Verbatim Thresholds (`38 C.F.R. § 4.115a`) | Critical Practitioner & CUE Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Urine Leakage / Continual Urine Leakage / Post-Surgical Urinary Diversion / Stress Incontinence** *(Highest Ceiling: **`60%`**!)* | • **`60%`:** Requiring the **use of an appliance** *(e.g., external Texas/condom catheter, urostomy/ileal conduit bag, artificial urinary sphincter)*, **OR** the **wearing of absorbent materials which must be changed MORE THAN `4` TIMES PER DAY (`>4/day`)**.<br/>• **`40%`:** Requiring the wearing of absorbent materials which must be changed **`2 to 4` times per day**.<br/>• **`20%`:** Requiring the wearing of absorbent materials which must be changed **less than `2` times per day** *(i.e., `1` pad/liner per day!)*. | • **Lay Competence Goldmine:** A veteran (and spouse) is **100% competent** to testify to how many adult diapers/pads (`Depends`, `Guards`, liners) are changed per day! Post-radical prostatectomy (`DC 7528`) veterans are routinely lowballed at `10%/20%` frequency when `2–4` pads/day (`40%`) or `>4` pads/day (`60%`) is documented in VAMC Prosthetics pad prescriptions!<br/>• **Do NOT Let Examiners Require a Physician "Prescription" for OTC Pads:** The regulation requires *"requiring the wearing of absorbent materials"*, not a formal VA Prosthetics order. |
| **2. Urinary Frequency** *(Highest Ceiling: **`40%`**)* | • **`40%`:** Daytime voiding interval **less than `1 hour` (`<1 hr`)**, **OR** awakening to void **`5 or more` times per night (`>=5x/night` nocturia)**.<br/>• **`20%`:** Daytime voiding interval **between `1 and 2 hours`**, **OR** awakening to void **`3 to 4` times per night**.<br/>• **`10%`:** Daytime voiding interval **between `2 and 3 hours`**, **OR** awakening to void **`2` times per night**. | • **Disjunctive (`"or"`) Rule:** Nocturia alone (`>=5x/night = 40%`; `3–4x/night = 20%`; `2x/night = 10%`) satisfies the tier even if daytime intervals are completely normal! |
| **3. Obstructed Voiding** *(Highest Ceiling: **`30%`**)* | • **`30%`:** Urinary retention requiring **intermittent or continuous catheterization**.<br/>• **`10%`:** Marked obstructive symptomatology (*hesitancy, slow or weak stream, decreased force of stream*) **WITH one or more of**: `(1)` **post-void residuals (`PVR`) `> 150 cc`**, `(2)` uroflowmetry with markedly diminished peak flow rate (**`< 10 cc/sec`**), `(3)` recurrent `UTIs` secondary to obstruction, OR `(4)` stricture disease requiring periodic dilatation every `2 to 3 months`.<br/>• **`0%`:** Obstructive symptomatology with or without stricture disease requiring dilatation `1 to 2` times per year. | • **Indwelling Foley vs. Appliance (`30%` vs. `60%`):** Watch the distinction between clean **intermittent self-catheterization** for retention (`30%` obstructed voiding) vs. requiring an external/suprapubic **urinary drainage appliance/bag** for continual leakage/diversion (`60%` urine leakage)! |
| **4. Urinary Tract Infection (`UTI`)** *(Updated Nov. 14, 2021 at `86 FR 54081`)* | • **Poor renal function:** Rate as **renal dysfunction** (`30%–100%`).<br/>• **`30%`:** Recurrent symptomatic infection requiring **drainage by stent or nephrostomy tube** `[added Nov. 14, 2021]`, **OR** requiring **`> 2` hospitalizations per year**, **OR** requiring **continuous intensive management**.<br/>• **`10%`:** Recurrent symptomatic infection requiring **`1–2` hospitalizations per year**, **OR** **suppressive drug therapy lasting `6 months` or longer** *(pre-2021 text said `"intermittent intensive management"` / long-term suppressive therapy without the strict `6-month` cutoff!)*.<br/>• **`0%` `[added Nov. 14, 2021]`:** Recurrent symptomatic infection not requiring hospitalization, needing suppressive drug therapy **`< 6 months`**. | • **The `§ 4.115a` Predominant-Area Trap (`Kill-Switch`):** Opening text of `§ 4.115a` mandates that where diagnostic codes refer to these dysfunction areas, **ONLY the predominant area of dysfunction shall be considered for rating purposes** (*unless* distinct disabilities have non-overlapping symptoms under `§ 4.14`!). Thus, a veteran with `DC 7527` BPH who has *both* `3–4x/night` nocturia (`20%`) *and* wears `2–4` pads/day (`40%`) receives **ONE `40%` rating** (not `40% + 20%`)—always audit to make sure VA selected the **HIGHEST** dysfunction track (`60%` pads over `20%` frequency)! |

## 3. High-Value Diagnostic Code Audits (`38 C.F.R. § 4.115b`, `DC 7500–7545`)

### A. `DC 7528` Malignant Neoplasms of the Genitourinary System (Prostate, Bladder, Ureter, Kidney & Testicular Cancers)

1. **Schedular `100%` & Mandatory `6-Month` Post-Treatment Exam Floor:**
   * Under **`DC 7528`**, active malignancy of any genitourinary organ (prostate, urinary bladder, ureter, renal pelvis, kidney/renal cell carcinoma, testis, penis, urethra) warrants **`100%`**—and following the cessation of surgical, X-ray, antineoplastic chemotherapy, hormone/androgen-deprivation (`Lupron`/`Zoladex` — watch active vs. palliative continuous `ADT`!), or other therapeutic procedure, **the `100%` evaluation continues with a mandatory VA examination `6 months` following cessation of treatment**! *(Remember to check *Bradley v. Peake* statutory **`SMC(s)` housebound** whenever `DC 7528` is `100%` and other SC disabilities combine to `>= 60%`!)*
2. ***Tatum v. Shinseki* (`2010`) vs. *Foster v. McDonough* (`2021`) Procedural Protection Split:**
   * **Running `100%` Awards (*Foster v. McDonough*, `34 Vet. App. 338 (2021)`):** When a veteran is *currently receiving* a running `100%` rating under `DC 7528`, VA **cannot** terminate that `100%` rating without `(1)` conducting the mandatory post-6-month VA examination showing no local recurrence or metastasis, **AND `(2)` issuing `38 C.F.R. § 3.105(e)` 60-day advance notice** of the proposed discontinuance (with the lower residual rating taking effect `60 days` after the *final* decision notice, first of the following month!). However, *Foster* clarified that `DC 7528` post-treatment step-downs are regulatory *"discontinuances"*, so `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.343` and `3.344` sustained-improvement rules do **not** apply.
   * **Retroactive Staged Ratings (*Tatum v. Shinseki*, `24 Vet. App. 139 (2010)`):** When VA grants initial service connection *retroactively* (e.g., an Agent Orange claim adjudicated two years after radical prostatectomy) and assigns staged ratings in a single decision, `§ 3.105(e)` prospective notice does not apply to historical closed stages—provided the medical record affirmatively establishes remission 6 months post-treatment.
3. **Mandatory Post-Prostatectomy / Post-Radiation Residual Triad (`Voiding` + `DC 7522 ED` + `SMC(k)` + `Digestive DC 7332`!):**
   * Whenever `DC 7528` steps down from `100%` after radical prostatectomy, `TURP`, external-beam radiation, or brachytherapy, audit for **four concurrent entitlements**:
     1. **Voiding Dysfunction (`20%` / `40%` / `60%`):** Stress incontinence pad count (`2–4/day = 40%`, `>4/day` or sphincter/clamp appliance `= 60%`) or radiation cystitis frequency!
     2. **Secondary Erectile Dysfunction (`DC 7522`):** `0%` on/after Nov. 14, 2021 (`20%` **prior to Nov. 14, 2021** under *Williams v. Wilkie*, `30 Vet. App. 134 (2018)` whenever radical prostatectomy caused internal anatomical distortion!).
     3. **Mandatory Statutory `SMC(k)` (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(k)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(a)`):** Loss of use of a creative organ—**even while `DC 7528` is at `100%`**, `SMC(k)` stacks on top of `100%` (and on top of `SMC(s)`!)!
     4. **Radiation Proctitis / Fecal Incontinence (`DC 7332` / `DC 7326`):** Pelvic radiation frequently causes chronic radiation proctitis/bleeding or anal sphincter weakness, which stacks **completely separately** under [System 07 (`§ 4.114`)](07-digestive-system-4.110-4.114.md)!
4. **Four Overlapping Toxic-Exposure Presumptive Gateways for `DC 7528` Cancers:**
   * **Agent Orange / Herbicide (`38 U.S.C. § 1116`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.309(e)` — FULL `§ 3.816` *NEHMER* RETROACTIVITY!):** Covers **Prostate Cancer** AND **Bladder Cancer** (`Pub. L. 116-283, § 9109`, eff. Jan. 1, 2021 — audit all prior Vietnam, Blue Water Navy, Thai base, Korean DMZ, Guam, Johnston Atoll, and C-123 denials and survivor `§ 3.816(f)` estates!) ([Playbook 06](../../playbooks/06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md)).
   * **January 2, 2025 Burn-Pit GU Cancer IFR (`38 C.F.R. § 3.320a`, `90 FR 23, 30`, eff. Jan. 2, 2025):** Establishes presumptive SC for **urinary bladder cancer, ureter cancer (including ureteric orifice and urachus), and related genitourinary cancers** for Gulf War (`>= Aug. 2, 1990` SWA/Somalia) and Post-9/11 (`>= Sept. 11, 2001` Afghanistan, Syria, Djibouti, Uzbekistan, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Yemen) veterans!
   * **PACT Act `38 U.S.C. § 1120(b)(2)(D), (H)` (`Pub. L. 117-168, § 406`, eff. Aug. 10, 2022):** Establishes presumptive SC for **reproductive cancers of any type** (prostate, testicular, penile) and **kidney cancer** for burn-pit-exposed veterans ([Playbook 15](../../playbooks/15-gulf-war-mucmi-burn-pits-pact-tera.md)).
   * **Camp Lejeune Contaminated Water (`38 C.F.R. § 3.307(a)(7)`):** `>= 30 days` (`Aug. 1, 1953 – Dec. 31, 1987`) establishes presumptive SC for **Kidney Cancer** and **Bladder Cancer** ([Playbook 07](../../playbooks/07-statutory-presumptions-chronic-tropical-pow-als.md)).
