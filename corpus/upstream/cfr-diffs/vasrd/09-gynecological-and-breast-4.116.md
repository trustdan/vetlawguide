# VASRD System 09 Historical Diff: Gynecological Conditions & Disorders of the Breast (`83 FR 15068`), Breast `SMC(k)`, Endometriosis (`DC 7629`) & `FSAD` (`DC 7632`) (`38 C.F.R. § 4.116`, `DC 7610–7632`)

This Reference module catalogs the three-era Federal Register evolution (`1946` -> `May 22, 1995` -> `May 13, 2018`), statutory `SMC(k)` creative-organ and breast-tissue/radiation expansions (`VAOPGCPREC 5-89`, `Pub. L. 106-419`, `Pub. L. 107-330`), complete Diagnostic Code (`DC 7610–7632`) criteria, PACT Act `38 U.S.C. § 1120(b)(2)(H)` reproductive-cancer presumptions, and ***George v. McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) / `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`** bitemporal boundaries governing **Body System 09 (Gynecological Conditions and Disorders of the Breast)** under `38 C.F.R. § 4.116`.

## 2. Three-Era Structural Diff & Complete `DC 7610–7632` Comparison Matrix

See **[`38-cfr-4.116-gynecological-breast-evolution.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.116-gynecological-breast-evolution.md)** for verbatim regulatory snapshots and introductory **`§ 4.116 Notes 1 & 2`**:
* **`§ 4.116 Note 1` (Non-Compensable Physiological States vs. Compensable Complications):** Natural menopause, primary amenorrhea, and pregnancy/childbirth are **not** disabilities for rating purposes; however, **chronic disease, injury, or surgical complications of pregnancy/childbirth** (e.g., obstetric `DC 7621` pelvic organ prolapse, `DC 7624–7625` rectovaginal/urethrovaginal fistulas, chronic `3rd/4th-degree` perineal tear fecal/urinary incontinence, emergency cesarean adhesions/hysterectomy, or postpartum depression `§ 4.130`) incurred during active service **ARE fully compensable**!
* **`§ 4.116 Note 2` (Mandatory `§ 3.350` SMC Audit):** Directs adjudicators to review **`38 C.F.R. § 3.350`** for Special Monthly Compensation whenever evaluating anatomical loss or loss of use of one or more creative organs or breast tissue.

## 3. High-Yield `SMC(k)`, Analogous Pleading & Post-Cancer Unbundling Rules

### A. The Dual-`SMC(k)` (`K-2`) Stacking Rule: Creative Organ (`§ 3.350(a)(1)`) + Breast Loss/Radiation (`§ 3.350(a)`)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DISTINGUISH SINGLE CREATIVE-ORGAN `SMC(k)` FROM SEPARATE BREAST `SMC(k)` (`K-1` vs. `K-2`):**
> 1. **Within Creative Organs (`§ 3.350(a)(1)` = Max `1` `SMC(k)`):** Because `38 U.S.C. § 1114(k)` specifies *"anatomical loss or loss of use of **one or more** creative organs"*, concurrent SC hysterectomy (`DC 7618`), bilateral oophorectomy (`DC 7619`), and `FSAD` (`DC 7632`) authorize **only ONE `SMC(k)`** (`K-1`). Similarly, unilateral and bilateral breast tissue loss/radiation authorize **one** breast `SMC(k)`.
> 2. **Creative Organ + Breast Loss/Radiation = TWO `SMC(k)` Awards (`K-2` = `$279.74/mo` in 2026!):** In `38 U.S.C. § 1114(k)` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(a)`, *"anatomical loss of 25 percent or more of tissue from a single breast or both breasts in combination (including loss by mastectomy or partial mastectomy) or when breast tissue has been subjected to radiation treatment"* is a **separate statutory category** from *"one or more creative organs"*. Therefore, a woman veteran with **`DC 7630`/`7626` breast lumpectomy + radiation** (or prophylactic/therapeutic `BRCA`/cancer mastectomy + oophorectomy, or `DC 7626` breast radiation + SSRI-induced `DC 7632` `FSAD` / `DC 7620` chemo-induced ovarian failure!) is legally entitled to **TWO concurrent `SMC(k)` awards (`K-2`)** ([Master SMC Treatise](../../docs/smc-complete-treatise-and-step-math.md))!

### B. Navigating the `DC 7629` Endometriosis Laparoscopy Gate (`DC 7610–7615` & `§ 4.20` *Webb* Pivot)

Modern gynecologists routinely diagnose endometriosis clinically via transvaginal ultrasound, pelvic MRI, and empiric GnRH agonist/antagonist (`elagolix`/`Orilissa`, `relugolix`/`Myfembree`, `leuprolide`/`Lupron`) or continuous progestin response without subjecting the patient to invasive diagnostic laparoscopy. Yet until VA finalizes **`RIN 2900-AS39` (`90 FR 47266`, Oct. 1, 2025 — proposed / not finalized)**, the `Note` to `DC 7629` states: *"Diagnosis of endometriosis must be substantiated by laparoscopy."*
* **Practitioner Pleading Contract:** Never let an RO deny service connection altogether when surgical laparoscopy was not performed! Under ***Saunders v. Wilkie*, `886 F.3d 1356 (Fed. Cir. 2018)`** (functional impairment from chronic pain is a compensable disability even absent a specific pathological confirmation) and ***Webb v. McDonough*, `71 F.4th 1377 (Fed. Cir. 2023)`** (`38 C.F.R. § 4.20` analogous ratings cannot be defeated by demanding literal structural prerequisites unique to a listed code), **plead in the alternative**:
  1. Direct evaluation of chronic pelvic pain, dysmenorrhea, menorrhagia, or ovarian/uterine dysfunction under **`DC 7613` / `DC 7615`** (`10%` continuous hormonal/analgesic treatment; `30%` symptoms not controlled by continuous treatment); **AND**
  2. Separate secondary evaluation of dyspareunia under **`DC 7632` (`0%` + `SMC(k)`)**, secondary voiding frequency/dysuria under **`§ 4.115a`**, secondary IBS/dyschezia under **`DC 7319`** (`10%–30%`), iron-deficiency anemia from menorrhagia under **`DC 7720`** (*`former DC 7700` pre-Dec. 9, 2018*; note `DC 7720 Note` rates chronic blood-loss anemia via the underlying bleeding condition), and secondary depression/anxiety under **`§ 4.130`**.

### C. `DC 7630` Breast Cancer: Adjuvant Endocrine Therapy (`Tamoxifen` / Aromatase Inhibitors) & Residual Unbundling

1. **The `6-Month` Post-Treatment Exam, *Foster* Notice & Adjuvant Hormone Therapy Dispute:**
   * Under `DC 7630` (and `DC 7627`), the `100%` evaluation continues *"beyond the cessation of any surgical, radiation, antineoplastic chemotherapy or **other therapeutic procedure**"* until a mandatory VA C&P examination **`6 months` after discontinuance**, governed by **`38 C.F.R. § 3.105(e)` `60-day` advance notice** (***Foster v. McDonough*, `34 Vet. App. 338 (2021)`**—any premature reduction without a post-6-month exam AND `§ 3.105(e)` notice is **void ab initio**; [Playbook 05](../../playbooks/05-void-rating-reductions-stabilized-ratings.md)).
   * **Audit Alert on `5–10 Year` Adjuvant Endocrine / Targeted Regimens:** Hormone-receptor-positive (`ER+`/`PR+`) and `HER2+` breast cancers are routinely treated for `1–10 years` post-surgery/radiation with oral Selective Estrogen Receptor Modulators (**`tamoxifen`**), Aromatase Inhibitors (**`anastrozole`/`Arimidex`, `letrozole`/`Femara`, `exemestane`/`Aromasin`**), ovarian-suppression **`goserelin`/`Zoladex` or `leuprolide`/`Lupron`**, `CDK4/6` inhibitors (**`abemaciclib`/`Verzenio`, `ribociclib`/`Kisqali`**), or anti-`HER2` immunotherapy (**`trastuzumab`/`Herceptin`, `pertuzumab`**). ROs frequently attempt to cut `100%` `6 months` after surgery/radiation by labeling tamoxifen/aromatase inhibitors "preventive/prophylactic." Always obtain an oncologist nexus statement explaining whether the agent is prescribed as **active systemic antineoplastic / cytostatic therapy to eradicate micrometastatic disease** (*sustaining `100%` under `DC 7630`!*), **AND**—even if `100%` eventually steps down—separately rate **every drug-induced side effect**: aromatase-inhibitor **polyarthralgia (`§ 4.71a` / `§ 4.59`)**, **secondary osteoporosis (`DC 5013`)**, **chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy (`DC 8515/8520` + `§ 4.26` bilateral factor)**, **chemo-induced ovarian failure (`DC 7620 = 20% + SMC(k)`)**, and **tamoxifen endometrial thickening/polyps (`DC 7613/7628`)**!
2. **Mandatory Post-Mastectomy & Reconstructive Flap Unbundling (`DC 7630 Note` & *Esteban*):**
   * The `Note` to `DC 7630` (`83 FR 15068`) expressly commands raters to evaluate non-overlapping residuals **in addition to `DC 7626` (`30%–80%`) + `SMC(k)`**:
     * **Post-Mastectomy / Sentinel-Node Upper-Extremity Lymphedema:** **`DC 7121`** (`10% / 20% / 40% / 100%` under `§ 4.104`, [Cardiovascular Diff](06-cardiovascular-system-4.100-4.104.md)) + annual *Sursely* compression-sleeve clothing allowance if rigid/damaging (`§ 3.810`).
     * **Shoulder / Arm Limitation of Motion & Nerve Injury:** **`DC 5201`** arm limitation (`20%–40%`), **`DC 8518`** long thoracic nerve (`winged scapula`), **`DC 8519`** thoracodorsal / intercostobrachial post-mastectomy neuropathic pain syndrome!
     * **Reconstructive Muscle-Flap Donor Site Residuals (`TRAM`, `DIEP`, `Latissimus Dorsi` Flap):** Separate muscle-group ratings for abdominal wall (`DC 5319` Group XIX hernia/weakness) or back/shoulder (`DC 5302` Group II latissimus dorsi) harvesting ([Muscle Injuries Diff](muscle-injuries-dc-5301-5323.md)), **PLUS** painful/unstable donor & chest scars under **`DC 7801/7802` + `DC 7804`** ([Scars Diff](scars-dc-7800-7805.md))!

## 4. Toxic Exposure Presumptions: PACT Act (`38 U.S.C. § 1120`), Camp Lejeune, Radiation & Male Breast Cancer Reversal

1. **PACT Act Burn-Pit Presumption (`38 U.S.C. § 1120(b)(2)(H)`, eff. Aug. 10, 2022):**
   * Establishes presumptive service connection (`0%` floor, zero manifestation deadline) for **`"reproductive cancer of any type"`** in Persian Gulf (`>= Aug. 2, 1990`) and post-9/11 (`>= Sept. 11, 2001`) covered veterans—unconditionally encompassing **female breast cancer (`DC 7630`)**, **ovarian, fallopian tube, uterine/endometrial, cervical, vaginal, and vulvar cancers (`DC 7627`)**, plus **`38 U.S.C. § 1305` / `§ 3.33` retroactive survivor DIC re-evaluations** ([Playbook 08](../../playbooks/08-survivor-dic-direct-and-1318-hypothetical.md), [Playbook 15](../../playbooks/15-gulf-war-mucmi-burn-pits-pact-tera.md)).
2. **The Male Breast Cancer Sub-Regulatory Window & Preservation Rule (`89 FR 51224` -> Sept. 30, 2025):**
   * On **June 17, 2024 (`89 FR 51224`)**, VA sub-regulatorily designated **male breast cancer (`DC 7630`)** as a presumptive PACT Act reproductive cancer (retroactive to Aug. 10, 2022), then **prospectively rescinded** that sub-regulatory presumption effective **September 30, 2025** (preserving all claims granted or pending under prior guidance while denying new post-Sept. 30, 2025 filings as non-presumptive).
   * **Litigation Preservation:** For any male breast cancer claim denied after September 30, 2025, **always preserve both** `(a)` a statutory challenge that `38 U.S.C. § 1120(b)(2)(H)`'s plain text (*`"reproductive cancer of any type"`*) already covers male breast cancer as a matter of statute, **and** `(b)` direct toxic-exposure causation under **`38 U.S.C. § 1168` `TERA`** (`PFAS`/`AFFF`, `TCE`/`PCE` solvents, ionizing radiation, burn pits; [Playbook 15](../../playbooks/15-gulf-war-mucmi-burn-pits-pact-tera.md))!
3. **Camp Lejeune (`38 C.F.R. § 3.307(a)(7)`, `§ 3.309(f)`) & Ionizing Radiation (`§§ 3.309(d), 3.311(b)(2)`):**
   * **Breast cancer (`DC 7630`, female and male)** is a presumptive **Camp Lejeune contaminated-water disease** (`>= 30` cumulative days, Aug. 1, 1953 – Dec. 31, 1987; while female infertility and miscarriage are covered for `38 U.S.C. § 1787` healthcare/reimbursement and direct `TERA`/water-exposure nexus compensation claims; [Playbook 07](../../playbooks/07-statutory-presumptions-chronic-tropical-pow-als.md)) and a primary **radiogenic malignancy** under `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.309(d)(2)(ii)` and `3.311(b)(2)` (alongside **ovarian cancer** under `§ 3.311(b)(2)`).
