McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) / `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`** bitemporal boundaries governing **Body System 15 (Dental and Oral Conditions)**.

### B. Controlling `DC 9905` Notes, Measurement Traps, `§ 4.59` Painful Motion & Secondary Bruxism

1. **Mandatory `DC 9905 Notes (1)–(3)` (`82 FR 36080`):**
   * **`Note (1)` (Non-Combination of Vertical + Lateral):** Ratings for limited interincisal vertical movement **shall NOT be combined** with ratings for limited lateral excursion (`0–4 mm = 10%`); assign whichever single evaluation is higher.
   * **`Note (2)` (Normal Unassisted Vertical Opening = `35 to 50 mm`):** Examiners must measure **maximum *unassisted* vertical opening** (inter-incisal gap in millimeters using a millimeter ruler/Boley gauge—**never** passive examiner-forced prying of the jaw!).
   * **`Note (3)` (Mechanically Altered Diet Definition & Physician-Verification Gate):** Mechanically altered foods are foods altered by **blending, chopping, grinding, or mashing** so they are easy to chew and swallow (`4` recognized texture levels: **full liquid, puree, soft, and semisolid**). **Crucial Gate:** *"To warrant elevation based on mechanically altered foods, the use of texture-modified diets **must be recorded or verified by a physician**"* (or treating dentist/oral surgeon—a veteran's uncorroborated lay statement of eating soft soup/smoothies without provider chart documentation/verification will fail at the Board; **always have the treating dentist or PCP record the texture-modified diet in the clinical treatment plan before the C&P exam!**).
2. **The `38 C.F.R. §§ 4.40, 4.45 & 4.59` Painful-Motion `10%` Floor & *DeLuca*/*Mitchell* Flare-Ups:**
   * The temporomandibular joint is a **synovial musculoskeletal joint**! Even when a veteran's unassisted interincisal opening measures **`>= 35 mm`** (`"normal"` under `Note (2)`) and lateral excursion exceeds `4 mm`, **actually painful motion (`§ 4.59`)** during jaw opening, closing, lateral excursion, or mastication (*Burton v. Shinseki*, `25 Vet. App. 1 (2011)`; *Petitti v. McDonald*, `27 Vet. App. 415 (2015)`) **mandates at least the minimum compensable `10%` evaluation** under `DC 9905`—and examiners must also estimate additional millimeter loss during masticatory **flare-ups** and repetitive motion (`§§ 4.40, 4.45`; [System 01 Diff](01-musculoskeletal-joints-arthritis-implants-4.71a.md))!
   * **Why Even a `10%` `DC 9905` Award Is Worth Tens of Thousands of Dollars:** Crossing from `0%` to **`10%` under `DC 9905`** immediately qualifies a veteran (even one whose combined VA rating is `< 100%`) for **Class I Lifetime Comprehensive VA Outpatient Dental Care (`38 C.F.R. § 17.161(a)`)**—covering **all** oral health, crowns, implants, root canals, and prosthodontics for life, **not just the TMJ**!
3. **The Secondary Bruxism Intermediate-Step Doctrine (`PTSD` / `MDD` / `TBI` / `SSRIs` -> Bruxism -> `DC 9905` TMD + Tooth Attrition):**
   * Under `M21-1` (`Part V, Subpart iii, Ch. 14`), **bruxism** (involuntary sleep/awake teeth grinding and jaw clenching) has **no standalone Diagnostic Code** and is treated by VA as a *symptom* rather than an independent compensable disability when claimed in isolation.
   * **The Winning Secondary Strategy ([Playbook 14](../../playbooks/14-secondary-service-connection-aggravation-obesity.md)):** Plead bruxism (triggered by **SC PTSD hyperarousal, MDD/anxiety, TBI, or SSRI-induced bruxism** from `sertraline`/`fluoxetine`/`venlafaxine`) as the **causal intermediate step** (`38 C.F.R. § 3.310(a)`) producing **`DC 9905` Temporomandibular Disorder (`10%–50%`)**, secondary **cervicogenic/tension/migraine headaches (`DC 8100`, `10%–50%`)**, and secondary **tinnitus (`DC 6260`, `10%`)**! Once `DC 9905` reaches **`>= 10%`**, **Class I (`§ 17.161(a)`)** automatically unlocks full VA restorative dental care for every bruxism-fractured or worn-down tooth!

## 3. Complete `DC 9900–9918` Structural Diff Table (`1994` vs. `2017`) & `§ 4.150 Notes (1)–(2)`

### A. Governing `§ 4.150 Notes (1) & (2)` (`82 FR 36080`)
* **`§ 4.150 Note (1)` (Objective Imaging Rule):** Ratings under `DC 9900–9918` requiring bony loss or nonunion must be confirmed by **diagnostic imaging studies** (panoramic `Panorex`, periapical/bitewing X-ray, cone-beam `CBCT`, `CT`, or `MRI`).
* **`§ 4.150 Note (2)` (Mandatory Multi-System Unbundling under `§ 4.25`):** Expressly commands adjudicators to **separately evaluate and combine (`38 C.F.R. § 4.25`)** any coexisting **loss of vocal articulation / speech (`DC 6519` + `SMC(k)` organic aphonia `§ 3.350(a)(6)`), loss of smell (`DC 6275` anosmia `10%`), loss of taste (`DC 6276` ageusia `10%`), neurological / cranial nerve impairment (`DC 8205` Trigeminal CN V sensory/motor, `DC 8207` Facial CN VII paralysis, `DC 8212` Hypoglossal CN XII tongue paralysis), facial scars/disfigurement (`DC 7800` / `DC 7804`), and respiratory/sinus dysfunction (`§ 4.97`)**!

## 4. Compensable VASRD (`§ 4.150`) vs. VHA Outpatient Dental Care (`38 U.S.C. § 1712`, `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.381 & 17.161`) & *Mays v. Brown*

Because standard VHA health care (`38 U.S.C. § 1710`) **excludes** routine dental care for most `< 100%` veterans, unlocking VHA outpatient dental eligibility under **`38 U.S.C. § 1712`** and **`38 C.F.R. § 17.161`** is often worth **$20,000–$80,000+ in lifetime implants, crowns, prosthodontics, and periodontal care**. Under ***Mays v. Brown*, `5 Vet. App. 302, 306 (1993)`**, **every claim for dental disability compensation automatically raises a claim for `§ 3.381` / `§ 17.161` outpatient dental eligibility**:

| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
