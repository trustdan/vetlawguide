McDonald* & *Johnson v. Shulkin*), specialized dermatologic schedules (`DC 7807–7833`), and ***George v. McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) / `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`** bitemporal boundaries governing **Body System 11 (The Skin — Non-Scar Disorders)** under `38 C.F.R. § 4.118`.

## 3. *Johnson v. Shulkin*, *Warren v. McDonald*, & The 2018 Biologics / Systemic Therapy Revolution

### A. Mandatory Citator Disambiguation (`862 F.3d 1351` vs. `762 F.3d 1362`; `28 Vet. App. 194` vs. `214`)

> [!WARNING]
> **DO NOT CONFUSE THE TWO *JOHNSON* LITIGATIONS OR THE TWO *WARREN* PAGES:**
> 1. **Skin Topical vs. Systemic Therapy (*Richard E. Johnson*):** Cite ***Johnson v. Shulkin*, `862 F.3d 1351 (Fed. Cir. 2017)`**, which **reversed** ***Johnson v. McDonald*, `27 Vet. App. 497 (2016)`** (`DC 7806` topical triamcinolone cream). Do **NOT** confuse this case with *Yancy Johnson*'s `38 C.F.R. § 3.321(b)(1)` collective extraschedular litigation in *Johnson v. McDonald*, `762 F.3d 1362 (Fed. Cir. 2014)` and *Johnson v. McDonald*, `28 Vet. App. 136 (2016) (en banc)`!
> 2. **Illustrative `"Such As"` Systemic Therapy (*Warren*):** Cite ***Warren v. McDonald*, `28 Vet. App. 194 (2016)`** for the substantive `DC 7806` holding that `"systemic therapy such as corticosteroids or other immunosuppressive drugs"` is illustrative rather than exhaustive (`28 Vet. App. 214` is a separate procedural jurisdiction opinion).

### B. Era-2 (`2002–2018`) vs. Era-3 (`>= Aug. 13, 2018`) Therapy Rules & Biologic `60%` Goldmine

1. **The Pre-August 13, 2018 Doctrine (*Warren* & *Johnson v. Shulkin*):**
   * Under *Warren*, `28 Vet. App. at 199–201`, any *systemic* treatment similar in nature to corticosteroids or immunosuppressives satisfied Era-2 `DC 7806`.
   * Under *Johnson v. Shulkin*, `862 F.3d at 1354–56`, the Federal Circuit held that localized **topical** corticosteroid application (e.g., triamcinolone, hydrocortisone, clobetasol, fluocinonide, or tacrolimus cream/ointment) is **topical therapy** (`0%` therapy prong), **NOT** systemic therapy—*unless* the factual record proves a topical steroid was applied on such a massive, body-wide scale that systemic bloodstream absorption treated the body as a whole.
2. **The August 13, 2018 Route-of-Administration Bright Line (`38 C.F.R. § 4.118(a)`, `83 FR 32592`):**
   * Effective **August 13, 2018**, VA codified bright-line definitions in `§ 4.118(a)`:
     * **Systemic therapy:** Treatment administered **through any route *other than* the skin**—specifically oral, subcutaneous/intramuscular injection, intravenous infusion, suppository, intranasal, **plus** therapeutic **phototherapy, photochemotherapy, or `PUVA`** (`UVB`/narrowband `UVB`). *(Note: Systemic antibiotic/antifungal/antiviral courses count as systemic therapy when prescribed to treat the SC skin disorder—e.g., oral doxycycline/minocycline, oral terbinafine/itraconazole for `DC 7813`, or oral valacyclovir!)*
     * **Topical therapy:** Treatment administered **through the skin** (creams, ointments, lotions, gels, foams, medicated shampoos, intralesional skin injections confined locally, or localized topical `UV` wand). Regardless of drug class (even potent Class I topical clobetasol or topical JAK inhibitor ruxolitinib/`Opzelura` cream), skin-applied medication is strictly **`0%` topical therapy** on or after August 13, 2018!
3. **The Modern Biologics & Oral Small-Molecule `30%` / `60%` Goldmine:**
   * Modern dermatology treats moderate-to-severe **atopic dermatitis (`DC 7806`)**, **plaque psoriasis (`DC 7816`)**, **hidradenitis suppurativa (`DC 7820`/`7828`)**, and **bullous pemphigoid (`DC 7815`)** with maintenance **subcutaneous/IV biologics** and **oral targeted systemic immunomodulators**:
     * **Biologics (Subcutaneous / IV):** `dupilumab` (**Dupixent**), `tralokinumab` (**Adbry**), `lebrikizumab` (**Ebglyss**), `adalimumab` (**Humira**), `etanercept` (**Enbrel**), `infliximab` (**Remicade**), `secukinumab` (**Cosentyx**), `ixekizumab` (**Taltz**), `brodalumab` (**Siliq**), `ustekinumab` (**Stelara**), `risankizumab` (**Skyrizi**), `guselkumab` (**Tremfya**), `tildrakizumab` (**Ilumya**), `bimekizumab` (**Bimzelx**), `rituximab` (**Rituxan**).
     * **Oral Immunosuppressives / Retinoids / JAK-TYK2 / PDE4 Inhibitors:** `apremilast` (**Otezla**), `deucravacitinib` (**Sotyktu**), `upadacitinib` (**Rinvoq**), `abrocitinib` (**Cibinqo**), `methotrexate`, `cyclosporine`, `mycophenolate mofetil`, `azathioprine`, `acitretin` (**Soriatane**), `isotretinoin`, oral `prednisone`, and clinical **narrowband `UVB` / `PUVA`**.
   * **Crucial Audit Rule (`0%` BSA Still Equals `60%`!):** Because the General Rating Formula is explicitly disjunctive (*"At least one of the following"*), a veteran maintained year-round on every-2-week `Dupixent`/`Humira`, monthly `Cosentyx`, or every-12-week `Skyrizi`/`Stelara` injections (which maintain continuous therapeutic drug levels across the entire 12-month period) **mandatorily qualifies for `60%`** (`30%` if `>= 6 weeks` total duration)—**even when the C&P examiner observes `0%` visible skin lesions on exam day because the biologic worked!** Assigning `0%` or `10%` based on cleared BSA while ignoring continuous biologic maintenance is textbook **RO CUE (`cue-and-open-claim`)**!
4. **The Overlooked *Sursely* Annual Clothing Allowance (`38 U.S.C. § 1162`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.810`):**
   * Even when a veteran receives only **`0%` or `10%`** because treatment is strictly *topical*, **every prescribed topical ointment/cream/lotion** (coal tar, anthralin, calcipotriene, tacrolimus, petrolatum-based triamcinolone/clobetasol, benzoyl peroxide, tretinoin) that **irremediably stains or damages outer garments** unlocks an annual tax-free **Clothing Allowance (`VA Form 10-8678`)**—and under ***Sursely v. Peake*, `551 F.3d 1351 (Fed. Cir. 2009)`**, **multiple** annual clothing allowances are payable if distinct SC skin/orthopedic conditions damage distinct types of outer garments ([Playbook 13](../../playbooks/13-ancillary-grants-sah-auto-clothing-dea-spina-bifida.md))!
