McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) / `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`** bitemporal gates governing **Body System 05 (The Respiratory System)** under `38 C.F.R. §§ 4.96–4.97`.

## 3. `38 C.F.R. § 4.96(a)` Anti-Pyramiding Matrix vs. Upper-Respiratory Carve-Outs (CUE Goldmine)

### A. Exact Scope of the `§ 4.96(a)` Single-Rating Lock
Under [`38 C.F.R. § 4.96(a)`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.96-4.97-respiratory-evolution.md), VA's categorical ban on combining coexisting respiratory ratings applies **strictly** to the Diagnostic Code ranges enumerated in the text (*Tropf v. Nicholson*, `20 Vet. App. 317, 320 (2006)`):

| DC Block | Covered Conditions | `§ 4.96(a)` Combination Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`DC 6502–6524`** *(Nose, Sinuses, Larynx & Pharynx)* | Deviated septum (`6502`), Nasal loss (`6504`), **Sinusitis (`6510–6514`)**, Laryngitis (`6515–6516`), Laryngectomy/Aphonia (`6518–6519`), Stenosis (`6520`), Pharynx (`6521`), **Allergic/Bacterial/Granulomatous Rhinitis (`6522–6524`)** | **EXEMPT FROM `§ 4.96(a)`!** May be rated **separately** from each other AND **separately** alongside any lower-respiratory or Sleep Apnea code (`DC 6600–6847`), subject only to `38 C.F.R. § 4.14` against compensating the *exact same* symptom twice (*Esteban v. Brown*, `6 Vet. App. 259 (1994)`). |
| **`DC 6600–6817` & `DC 6822–6847`** *(Trachea/Bronchi, TB, Vascular, Mycotic, Interstitial, Restrictive, Sarcoidosis & Sleep Apnea)* | Bronchitis (`6600`), Bronchiectasis (`6601`), **Asthma (`6602`)**, Emphysema (`6603`), **COPD (`6604`)**, TB (`6701–6731`), Pulmonary Vascular (`6817`), Actinomycosis/Mycotic (`6822–6839`), **Interstitial (`6825–6833`)**, **Restrictive (`6840–6845`)**, **Sarcoidosis (`6846`)**, **Sleep Apnea (`6847`)** | **STRICTLY NON-COMBINABLE WITH EACH OTHER.** Assign **ONE single rating** under the DC reflecting the **predominant disability**, with **elevation to the next higher evaluation** where the severity of the overall respiratory disability warrants such elevation. |
| **`DC 6819–6820`** *(Malignant & Benign Respiratory Neoplasms)* | Lung/bronchial cancer, pleural mesothelioma (`6819`), laryngeal/tracheal/sinus carcinoma (`6819`), benign respiratory neoplasms (`6820`) | • **With lung or pleural involvement:** **Cannot** combine `6819` & `6820` with each other OR with `DC 6600–6817` / `6822–6847`.<br/>• **WITHOUT lung or pleural involvement** *(e.g., upper-airway paranasal sinus or laryngeal neoplasm)*: **OUTSIDE** the `§ 4.96(a)` lower-lung combination bar! |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **High-Yield PACT Act Triple-Stacking Audit (`cue-and-open-claim`):**
> A Gulf War / Post-9/11 burn-pit veteran (`38 U.S.C. § 1120`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.320`) can lawfully hold **concurrent, separate schedular ratings** for:
> 1. **`DC 6510–6514` Chronic Sinusitis** (up to `50%` — facial pain/tenderness, headaches, purulent sinus discharge/antibiotics) **+**
> 2. **`DC 6522` Allergic/Vasomotor Rhinitis** (`30%` for nasal polyps or `10%` nasal passage obstruction) **+**
> 3. **ONE Lower/Sleep Code**—e.g., **`DC 6847` Sleep Apnea (`50%` CPAP)** *or* **`DC 6602` Asthma (`30%/60%`)** (merged into a single predominant lower-respiratory rating under `§ 4.96(a)`, e.g., `6847-6602` at `50%` or elevated to `100%` if warranted).
>
> **Plain-Text CUE Trigger:** Whenever a post-Oct. 7, 1996 Rating Code Sheet subsumes `DC 6510–6514` Sinusitis or `DC 6522` Rhinitis into `DC 6602` Asthma or `DC 6847` Sleep Apnea by misreading `§ 4.96(a)` as covering *"all respiratory codes,"* attack the final decision via **RO CUE (`38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)`)** (`cue-and-open-claim`). Also note that **`DC 6732` (Pleurisy, tuberculous, active or inactive)** sits in the gap between `6817` and `6822`, and extrapulmonary manifestations of **`DC 6846` Sarcoidosis** (ocular, cutaneous, cardiac, neurologic) are rated separately outside `§ 4.96(a)`!

### B. Controlling Precedent on Predominant-DC Elevation: *Urban v. Shulkin* (`2017`)
* In ***Urban v. Shulkin*, `29 Vet. App. 82, 87–89 (2017)`**, a veteran service-connected for both **`DC 6847` Obstructive Sleep Apnea (`50%` CPAP)** and **`DC 6602` Asthma (`30%`)** argued he should be elevated from `50%` to `100%` (or `60%` under `DC 6602`). The CAVC held that `38 C.F.R. § 4.96(a)`'s phrase *"elevation to the next higher evaluation where the severity of the overall disability warrants such elevation"* is evaluated **using the criteria of the predominant Diagnostic Code alone** (there, `DC 6847`, whose next higher tier above `50%` is `100%` requiring chronic respiratory failure with `CO2` retention, cor pulmonale, or tracheostomy), rather than mixing criteria across non-predominant codes.
* **Practitioner Takeaway:** When `DC 6847` (`50%`) and `DC 6602` (`60%` for `>=3` systemic steroid courses/yr) coexist, **`DC 6602` (`60%`) becomes the predominant disability**, and severe coexisting CPAP-dependent OSA can be argued on direct appeal to warrant elevation to `DC 6602`'s next higher tier (`100%`)—or referral under `38 C.F.R. § 3.321(b)(1)` / `§ 4.16(a)` TDIU ([Playbook 19](../../playbooks/19-extraschedular-3.321b1-analogous-4.20-and-temp-total.md)). *(Note: Challenging a pre-2017 final decision based on *Urban* nuances is `open-claim-only-george-restricted` under `§ 20.1403(e)`).*

## 4. `38 C.F.R. § 4.96(d)` Pulmonary Function Testing (`PFT`) & Discrepancy Rules

Subsection [`38 C.F.R. § 4.96(d)`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.96-4.97-respiratory-evolution.md) governs PFT administration and metric selection across **`DC 6600, 6603, 6604` (COPD/Bronchitis/Emphysema)**, **`DC 6825–6833` (Interstitial Lung Disease)**, and **`DC 6840–6845` (Restrictive Lung Disease)** (and informs `DC 6602` spirometry):

1. **When PFTs Are Excused (`§ 4.96(d)(1)(i)–(iv)`):** PFTs are required to evaluate `DC 6600, 6603, 6604, 6825–6833, and 6840–6845` *except*:
   * `(i)` When maximum exercise capacity test results are of record and are **`20 ml/kg/min` or less** (`< 15 ml/kg/min = 100%`; `15–20 ml/kg/min = 60%`);
   * `(ii)` When **pulmonary hypertension** (documented by echocardiogram or cardiac catheterization), **cor pulmonale**, or **right ventricular hypertrophy** has been diagnosed (`= 100%`);
   * `(iii)` When there have been one or more episodes of **acute respiratory failure** (`= 100%`); OR
   * `(iv)` When **outpatient oxygen therapy** is required (`= 100%`). *(See also `§ 4.96(d)(3)` when an examiner states why PFTs are not a valid indication of respiratory functional impairment in a particular case).*
2. **Mandatory `DLCO(SB)` Rule (`§ 4.96(d)(2)`):** If the `DLCO(SB)` (diffusion capacity of the lung for carbon monoxide by single-breath method) test is **not** in the record, evaluate on alternative criteria **only so long as the examiner states why `DLCO(SB)` would not be useful or valid** in that particular case. Omitting `DLCO(SB)` without that medical explanation renders a C&P exam legally inadequate on appeal!
3. **Post-Bronchodilator & `> 100%` Rules (`§ 4.96(d)(4)–(5), (7)`):** Post-bronchodilator studies are required unless pre-bronchodilator PFTs are normal or the examiner states why post-bronchodilator testing should not be done (`(d)(4)`). Evaluate using **post-bronchodilator** results *unless* post-bronchodilator results are **poorer** than pre-bronchodilator results, in which case pre-bronchodilator values govern (`(d)(5)`). Under `(d)(7)`, if **both `FEV-1` and `FVC` exceed `100%`**, do not assign a compensable rating based on a decreased `FEV-1/FVC` ratio.
4. **The Discrepancy Resolution Rule (`§ 4.96(d)(6)` — Major Remand/Audit Trigger):**
   * Whenever a veteran's `FEV-1%`, `FEV-1/FVC`, `FVC%`, and `DLCO(SB)%` point to **different percentage tiers** (e.g., `FEV-1 = 72%` [`10%`], `FEV-1/FVC = 65%` [`30%`], and `DLCO(SB) = 51%` [`60%`]), **`§ 4.96(d)(6)` strictly forbids the rater or Board from mechanically picking the lowest (or highest) number on lay intuition**.
   * Instead, VA **must** *"use the test result that the examiner states most accurately reflects the level of disability."* For emphysema (`DC 6603`) and interstitial fibrosis (`DC 6825`), alveolar-capillary gas-exchange impairment makes **`DLCO(SB)`** clinically superior to `FEV-1`; if the examiner left the DBQ rationale blank and the rater picked the lower `FEV-1`, remand/HLR for a `§ 4.96(d)(6)` opinion (or apply `§ 4.7` higher evaluation if both metrics are equally valid!).

### B. Three High-Yield Sinusitis & Rhinitis Litigation Traps
1. **"Radical Surgery" vs. "Repeated Surgeries" (`DC 6510–6514` `50%` Tier):**
   * **Radical Surgery:** Refers to open, obliterative procedures—classically a **Caldwell-Luc radical antrostomy**, Lynch frontalethmoidectomy, or osteoplastic frontal sinus obliteration—paired with **chronic osteomyelitis**. Routine outpatient **Functional Endoscopic Sinus Surgery (`FESS`)**, balloon sinuplasty, septoplasty, or endoscopic polypectomy is **not** *"radical surgery"* on its own.
   * **Repeated Surgeries (`>= 2` FESS/Endoscopic Procedures):** However, **two or more `FESS` or sinus procedures** satisfy **"repeated surgeries"** under the *second* `50%` pathway whenever the veteran continues to suffer *"near-constant sinusitis characterized by headaches, pain and tenderness of affected sinus, and purulent discharge or crusting"* (*Tropf v. Nicholson*, `20 Vet. App. 317 (2006)`; *cf. Williams v. Wilkie*, `30 Vet. App. 134, 138 (2018)` (applying ordinary dictionary meaning to undefined VASRD terms)).
2. **Incapacitating vs. Non-Incapacitating Episodes (`DC 6510–6514` Note):**
   * Under the Sinusitis **Note**, an **"incapacitating episode"** strictly requires **bed rest AND treatment by a physician** (prescribed/directed bed rest + `4 to 6 weeks` of prolonged antibiotics for `10%/30%`). Most modern outpatient urgent-care prescriptions (`7–14 days` of amoxicillin-clavulanate or doxycycline without physician-prescribed bed rest) fail the *"incapacitating"* prong—so practitioners **must** pivot to the **non-incapacitating episode** pathway (`3–6/year = 10%`; **`> 6/year = 30%`**), proving frequency through competent lay flare-up logs, OTC nasal irrigation/steroid records, and clinical histories!
   * Under ***Murphy v. Shinseki*, `26 Vet. App. 510, 514–16 (2014)`**, if an RO grants an intervening higher sinusitis rating while an appeal is pending, the Board errs if it ignores the intervening grant or effectively reduces an established sinusitis rating without complying with `38 C.F.R. § 3.344` material-improvement protections ([Playbook 05](../../playbooks/05-void-rating-reductions-stabilized-ratings.md)).
3. **Recurrent vs. Resected Polyps (`DC 6522`):** If a veteran has a history of `30%` nasal polyps removed via polypectomy (`FESS`), VA **cannot** reduce `DC 6522` from `30%` to `10%/0%` without `38 C.F.R. § 3.105(e)` 60-day notice and a post-surgical exam confirming sustained absence of recurrent polyps (especially given high polyp recurrence rates in burn-pit/aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease!).

### B. Doctrinal Deep Dives: Medication Rules, Constrictive Bronchiolitis, CPAP & Respiratory Cancers

1. **`DC 6602` Asthma & `DC 6846` Sarcoidosis vs. *Ingram v. Collins* (`2025`):**
   * Because `DC 6602` (`10%/30%/60%/100%`) and `DC 6846` (`30%/60%`) **explicitly incorporate medication regimens into their evaluation tiers**, they fall within the ***McCarroll v. McDonald*, `28 Vet. App. 267 (2016) (en banc)`** carve-out rather than ***Jones v. Shinseki*, `26 Vet. App. 56 (2012)`** or ***Ingram v. Collins*, `38 Vet. App. 130 (2025)`**—meaning a veteran whose PFTs are normalized (`FEV-1 = 88%`) by a daily Advair/Symbicort inhaler receives **`30%`** based on the medication tier itself, rather than speculative unmedicated PFTs!
   * Conversely, codes that are **silent on medication**—such as **`DC 6604` COPD**, **`DC 6825` Pulmonary Fibrosis**, and **`DC 6522` Rhinitis**—remain governed by *Ingram v. Collins* (VA's reactive Feb. 17, 2026 `§ 4.10` IFR at `91 FR 7118` was **rescinded ab initio** Feb. 27, 2026 at `91 FR 9712`).
2. **Constrictive (Obliterative) Bronchiolitis — Current `§ 4.20` Analogous Strategy (*Webb*):**
   * Although **constrictive bronchiolitis** is an express burn-pit presumptive illness under **`38 U.S.C. § 1120(b)(2)(B)`** and `38 C.F.R. § 3.320`, VA's proposed dedicated **`DC 6605`** (`RIN 2900-AQ72`, `89 FR 74162`, Sept. 12, 2024 SNPRM) remains **proposed / not finalized**.
   * Today, constrictive bronchiolitis is rated **by analogy (`38 C.F.R. § 4.20`)**—typically `6699-6600`, `6699-6604`, `6699-6602`, or `6899-6825`. Crucially, small-airways constrictive bronchiolitis often presents with **normal spirometry (`FEV-1 > 80%`)** despite crippling exertional dyspnea (`VO2 max <= 20 ml/kg/min` or air-trapping on high-resolution expiratory CT)! Under ***Webb v. McDonough*, `71 F.4th 1377 (Fed. Cir. 2023)`**, raters cannot mechanically assign `0%` solely because spirometry is normal when cardiopulmonary exercise testing (`CPET` / `VO2 max`), inhaled steroid therapy (`6699-6602`), or `§ 3.321(b)(1)` extraschedular criteria demonstrate severe functional loss ([Playbook 15](../../playbooks/15-gulf-war-mucmi-burn-pits-pact-tera.md), [Playbook 19](../../playbooks/19-extraschedular-3.321b1-analogous-4.20-and-temp-total.md)).
3. **`DC 6847` Sleep Apnea `50%` `"Requires Use of Breathing Assistance Device"` Nuances:**
   * The `50%` threshold turns on **medical necessity**—documented when a treating sleep physician, pulmonologist, dentist, or C&P examiner **prescribes or certifies that a breathing assistance device is medically required** (`CPAP`, `BiPAP`, `APAP`, `ASV`, `Inspire` hypoglossal nerve stimulator, or a custom prescription **oral mandibular advancement device** prescribed specifically as a breathing-assistance alternative to CPAP!).
   * If a device is medically required and prescribed, inability to tolerate the mask due to claustrophobia, PTSD nightmares, nasal obstruction (`DC 6522`), or aerophagia does **not** negate medical necessity so long as a clinician confirms the veteran still medically requires positive-airway/breathing-assistance therapy.
   * **Secondary SC Workhorse:** Sleep Apnea (`DC 6847`) is **NOT** a PACT Act `§ 1120` presumptive condition—making secondary service connection (`38 C.F.R. § 3.310(a)–(b)`) to SC **`DC 6522` Rhinitis / `DC 6510` Sinusitis** (upper-airway nasal resistance!) or SC **PTSD / Orthopedic conditions via `VAOPGCPREC 1-2017` Obesity** one of the highest-volume pathways in Veterans Law ([Playbook 14](../../playbooks/14-secondary-service-connection-aggravation-obesity.md)).
4. **`DC 6819` Malignant Neoplasms of the Respiratory System (Herbicide & PACT Presumptive Powerhouse):**
   * **Presumptive Triggers:** Herbicide exposure (`38 U.S.C. § 1116` / `38 C.F.R. § 3.309(e)` — lung, bronchus, larynx, trachea cancers -> **full `§ 3.816` *Nehmer* class retroactivity**!) AND Burn-Pit/PACT exposure (`38 U.S.C. § 1120` — **respiratory cancer of any type** and **head cancer of any type**, including paranasal sinus, nasal cavity, pharyngeal, laryngeal, tracheal, bronchial, lung, and pleural mesothelioma!).
   * **Mandatory `100%` + `6-Month` Post-Treatment Floor (`DC 6819` Note):** Assign **`100%`** during active malignancy or any surgical, X-ray, antineoplastic chemotherapy, immunotherapy, or radiation procedure, **continuing for `6 months` following cessation of treatment**. Thereafter, VA **MUST schedule a mandatory C&P examination** to determine residuals (`DC 6844` post-lobectomy/pneumonectomy PFTs, radiation fibrosis `DC 6825/6845`, `DC 6518` total laryngectomy `100% + SMC(k)`, dysphonia, painful thoracotomy scars `DC 7804`, or chemo peripheral neuropathy `DC 8520`) **AND issue `60-day` pre-reduction notice under `38 C.F.R. § 3.105(e)`** (*Foster v. McDonough*, `34 Vet. App. 338 (2021)`—note that while omitting the mandatory exam or `§ 3.105(e)` notice renders a reduction **void ab initio**, *Foster* held `§§ 3.343(a)/3.344` stabilized-rating rules do not apply to temporary cancer Note discontinuances) ([Playbook 05](../../playbooks/05-void-rating-reductions-stabilized-ratings.md))!
