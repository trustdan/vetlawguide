# VASRD Historical Diff: Spine & Musculoskeletal Joints (`38 C.F.R. § 4.71a`)

Orthopedic claims turn on three VASRD overhauls—**September 23, 2002 (Interim Spine IVDS `DC 5293`, `67 FR 54345`)**, **September 26, 2003 (Full Spine General Rating Formula, `68 FR 51454`)**, and **February 7, 2021 (Musculoskeletal System, `85 FR 76453`, corrected Feb. 4, 2021 at `86 FR 8142`)**—plus timeless functional-loss and anti-pyramiding rules that generate clean **math/code-sheet CUEs**. For verbatim historical spine text across all three eras, see [`38-cfr-4.71a-spine-evolution.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.71a-spine-evolution.md); for complete `DC 5000–5284` joint, arthritis, prosthetic-implant, amputation, and foot/knee stacking schedules, see [`01-musculoskeletal-joints-arthritis-implants-4.71a.md`](01-musculoskeletal-joints-arthritis-implants-4.71a.md) and [`38-cfr-4.71a-joints-arthritis-pre-and-post-2021.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.71a-joints-arthritis-pre-and-post-2021.md).

## 1. Spine Evolution: Pre-Sept. 26, 2003 vs. Post-Sept. 26, 2003 (`DC 5235–5244`)

*(Note: On Sept. 26, 2003, old spine codes `DC 5285–5295` were renumbered to `DC 5235–5243`! When `85 FR 76453` Nov. 30, 2020 initially attempted to reuse `DC 5285` for Plantar Fasciitis, VA formally corrected Plantar Fasciitis to **`DC 5269`** at `86 FR 8142` Feb. 4, 2021 so `5285` remained reserved for legacy vertebral-fracture corporate records!)*

| Feature | Era 1: 1945 – Sept. 25, 2003 *(Old `DC 5285–5295`)* | Era 2: Sept. 26, 2003 – Present *(General Rating Formula for Spine, `DC 5235–5244`)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lumbar / Cervical Limitation of Motion & Vertebral Fracture** | Old **`DC 5292`** (Lumbar), **`DC 5291`** (Dorsal `0/10/10%`), & **`DC 5290`** (Cervical) used qualitative adjectives:<br/>• **Lumbar (`5292`):** Slight `10%` / Moderate `20%` / Severe `40%` (Ankylosis `5289`: Fav `40%` / Unfav `50%`)<br/>• **Cervical (`5290`):** Slight `10%` / Moderate `20%` / Severe `30%`<br/>• **Vertebral Fracture (`former DC 5285`):** Added **`+10%` for demonstrable deformity of vertebral body** on X-ray! | **Objective Goniometer ROM Cutoffs:**<br/>• **`10%`:** Thoracolumbar flexion `>60° to 85°` (Cervical `>30° to 40°`), combined ROM, muscle spasm/guarding/localized tenderness not altering gait.<br/>• **`20%`:** Thoracolumbar flexion **`>30° to 60°`** (Cervical `>15° to 30°`), OR combined ROM `<=120°`, OR spasm/guarding severe enough to cause **abnormal gait or abnormal spinal contour**.<br/>• **`40%` (Thoracolumbar) / `30%` (Cervical):** Forward flexion **`30° or less`** (Cervical `15° or less`), OR favorable ankylosis.<br/>• **`50%` / `40%`:** Unfavorable ankylosis of entire thoracolumbar / cervical spine (`100%` whole spine). |
| **Intervertebral Disc Syndrome (IVDS)** | Old **`DC 5293`:** Rated `0/10/20/40/60%` based on recurring attacks, sciatic radiation, and mild/moderate/severe/pronounced persistent symptoms (**included neurologic manifestations inside `5293`** unless rated higher separately!). | **`DC 5243` ( updated Sept. 23, 2002 & Sept. 26, 2003):** Rated **EITHER** under the General Rating Formula (ROM) **OR** on total duration of **incapacitating episodes** requiring **physician-prescribed bed rest** over past 12 months (`>=1 wk = 10%`, `>=2 wks = 20%`, `>=4 wks = 40%`, `>=6 wks = 60%`), whichever yields the higher overall result (*Chavis*, 2021)! |
| **Radiculopathy / Peripheral Nerves** | Often bundled into `DC 5293` IVDS prior to 2002/2003 unless peripheral nerve rating alone exceeded IVDS. | **Note (1) Mandatory Separate Rating:** **ANY associated objective neurologic abnormality** (e.g., lower-extremity sciatic `DC 8520` / femoral `DC 8526` radiculopathy, bowel/bladder sphincter impairment) **MUST be rated separately**! |

## 2. February 7, 2021 Musculoskeletal Overhaul (`85 FR 76453`)

| Condition / Code | Before Feb. 7, 2021 | On or After Feb. 7, 2021 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Knee Instability (`DC 5257`)** | Subjective grades of recurrent subluxation or lateral instability: **Slight `10%`**, **Moderate `20%`**, **Severe `30%`**. | Objective surgical/brace tiers:<br/>• **`10%`:** Sprain/incomplete ligament tear/repair with persistent instability **without** prescription of assistive device/bracing.<br/>• **`20%`:** Persistent instability **with** physician-prescribed brace *or* assistive device (`cane/walker`).<br/>• **`30%`:** Persistent instability requiring **BOTH** prescribed brace **AND** assistive device (or unrepairable multi-ligament instability). |
| **Total Joint Replacements (`DC 5054` Hip, `5055` Knee)** | **12 months** (`1 year`) of temporary `100%` convalescent rating post-arthroplasty (`13 months` pre-2015 calculations), then `30%` minimum. | Reduced temporary `100%` post-surgical convalescence from 1 year down to **4 months** following arthroplasty; retained **`30%` minimum** evaluation thereafter (`50%/60%+` for chronic severe residuals). |
| **Plantar Fasciitis (`DC 5269`, corrected from `5285` at `86 FR 8142`)** | Rated by analogy (typically `DC 5276` flatfoot `0/10/20/30/50%` or `DC 5284` other foot injury). | Dedicated **`DC 5269`** (`86 FR 8142`, Feb. 4, 2021, eff. Feb. 7, 2021): `10%` (unilateral/bilateral otherwise), `20%` (unilateral, no relief from both non-surgical & surgical treatment, or surgical non-candidate per Note 2), `30%` (bilateral, no relief from both non-surgical & surgical treatment, or surgical non-candidate per Note 2; `40%` actual loss of use of foot under `DC 5167` + `SMC(k)`!). |

## 3. Three Classic Orthopedic Code-Sheet CUEs (`38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)`)

1. **Ignoring `38 C.F.R. § 4.59` Painful Motion Minimum (`0%` Joint Error):**
   * Under `38 C.F.R. § 4.59` (and *Lichtenfels v. Derwinski*, `1 Vet. App. 484 (1991)` / *Burton v. Shinseki*, `25 Vet. App. 1 (2011)` / *Petitti v. McDonald*, `27 Vet. App. 415 (2015)` / *Southall-Norman v. McDonald*, `28 Vet. App. 346 (2016)`), **actually painful, unstable, or malaligned joints** supported by objective evidence of painful motion are entitled to **at least the minimum compensable rating (`10%`)** for the joint—even if goniometer flexion/extension numbers fail to hit the `10%` schedular ROM cutoff! When a C&P exam explicitly records objective painful motion of a shoulder (`20%` minimum under `DC 5201`!), hip, knee, or ankle, and the rater assigns `0%`, audit immediately for **RO CUE** *(Note `§ 20.1403(e)` / George v. McDonough bitemporal gate: plain-text arthritic painful-motion CUE is safe since 1946/Lichtenfels, whereas non-arthritis `§ 4.59` CUE predating Burton (2011) or `DC 5276` CUE predating Southall-Norman (2016) is open-claim-only)*!
2. **Failure to Stack Knee Codes (`VAOPGCPREC 23-97`, `9-98` & `9-2004`):**
   * Binding VA General Counsel Opinions hold that a veteran with **both** knee instability (`DC 5257` — `VAOPGCPREC 23-97` & `9-98`) **and** painful/limited flexion (`DC 5260`) **and** limited extension (`DC 5261` — `VAOPGCPREC 9-2004` & *Lyles v. Shulkin*, `29 Vet. App. 107 (2017)`) is entitled to **separate concurrent ratings** without violating `§ 4.14` anti-pyramiding!
3. **Post-Sept. 26, 2003 Spine Goniometer Math Error or Omitted Radiculopathy:**
   * DBQ records thoracolumbar active forward flexion of `25°` (`<= 30°` = **`40%`**) and rater mistakenly awards `20%` -> **undebatable RO CUE**.
   * Post-Feb. 19, 2019 AMA spine DBQ documents sciatic radiculopathy and AMA rating decision silently omits it -> **still legally pending under *Hamill v. Collins* (2026)**!
