# VASRD System 12 Historical Diff: Endocrine System, December 10, 2017 Overhaul (`82 FR 50802`), Diabetes Mellitus (`DC 7913`), Thyroid 6-Month Rule & Multi-System Secondary Cascades (`38 C.F.R. § 4.119`, `DC 7900–7919`)

This Reference module catalogs the Federal Register overhauls (`1946` -> `June 6, 1996` -> `Dec. 10, 2017`), three-era Diagnostic Code (`DC 7900–7919`) matrices, `38 U.S.C. § 1155` grandfathering rules, cumulative vs. disjunctive rating canons (*Camacho*/*Middleton* vs. *Tatum*), and ***George v. McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) / `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`** bitemporal boundaries governing **Body System 12 (The Endocrine System)** under `38 C.F.R. § 4.119`.

### B. High-Yield Thyroid Litigation & CUE Rules (`§ 1155`, *Tatum*, *Nehmer* & Residual Unbundling)

1. ***Tatum v. Shinseki*, `23 Vet. App. 152, 156 (2009)` (Pre-Dec. 10, 2017 `DC 7903` Non-Successive Rule):**
   * In Era 2 (`June 6, 1996 – Dec. 9, 2017`), raters routinely denied `30%` or `60%` under `DC 7903` unless the veteran exhibited *every single* listed symptom (e.g., fatigability *and* constipation *and* mental sluggishness for `30%`). In *Tatum*, the CAVC held that Era-2 `DC 7903` criteria are **variable rather than strictly successive**, meaning **`38 C.F.R. § 4.7` ("more nearly approximates") applies**—a veteran with fatigability and weight gain or mental disturbance could qualify for `30%` or `60%` without exhibiting every symptom in that tier!
2. **The `Pub. L. 116-283` *Nehmer* Staged-Rating Goldmine (`Pre-Dec. 10, 2017` vs. `Post-Dec. 10, 2017`):**
   * On **January 1, 2021**, `Pub. L. 116-283, § 9109` added **Hypothyroidism (`DC 7903`)** to `38 U.S.C. § 1116(a)(2)` as a covered Agent Orange presumptive disease. Under `38 C.F.R. § 3.816`, any Vietnam or Blue Water Navy veteran (or `§ 3.816(f)` survivor/estate) whose prior hypothyroidism claim—or prior claim where VAMC/private labs in the file already showed hypothyroidism ("Footnote 1" diagnostic lookback)—was received between **September 25, 1985 and December 9, 2017** is entitled to a retroactive effective date in **Era 1 or Era 2**!
   * **Crucial Audit Check:** For the entire staged period **prior to December 10, 2017**, VA **must** apply Era-2 `DC 7903` (`10%` minimum for daily levothyroxine/Synthroid, `10%` under `DC 7900` for daily methimazole, or `30%/60%` under *Tatum*). Furthermore, once that pre-Dec. 10, 2017 running award is established, **`38 U.S.C. § 1155` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.951(a)`** bar VA from arbitrarily dropping the rating to `0%` on December 10, 2017 solely because the rating schedule changed ([Playbook 05](../../playbooks/05-void-rating-reductions-stabilized-ratings.md))!
3. **Post-December 10, 2017 Initial Diagnosis Trap & Mandatory Multi-System Unbundling:**
   * For claims with effective dates **on or after December 10, 2017**, VA awards `30%` under `DC 7903` (or `DC 7900`) *only* during the **`6 months` immediately following initial medical diagnosis**—meaning a veteran first diagnosed with hypothyroidism in 2015 who files a non-*Nehmer* PACT/TERA claim in 2024 receives **`0%` under `DC 7903`**.
   * **The Practitioner Counter-Move (`82 FR 50802` Mandatory Residual Cascade):** Under post-2017 `DC 7903`, VA **cannot** stop at `0%` when chronic hypothyroid symptoms persist despite levothyroxine replacement. Every chronic organ manifestation **must** be separately rated without triggering `§ 4.14` pyramiding:
     * **Psychiatric / Cognitive (`§ 4.130`, `DC 9434`/`9440`):** Secondary depressive disorder, anxiety, or cognitive slowing (`10%–70%+`, [Mental Health Diff](mental-health-dc-9400-9411.md)).
     * **Digestive (`§ 4.114`, `DC 7319`):** Chronic hypothyroid constipation / irritable bowel syndrome (`10%–30%`).
     * **Cardiovascular (`§ 4.104`, `DC 7009` / `7002` / `7101` / `7008`):** Symptomatic sinus bradycardia, pericardial effusion, secondary hyperlipidemia/atherosclerosis, or hyperthyroid heart disease (`DC 7008`).
     * **Neurological (`§ 4.124a`, `DC 8515` / `8520`):** Myxedematous carpal tunnel syndrome (`DC 8515`) or peripheral paresthesias (`DC 8520` + `§ 4.26` bilateral factor).
     * **Skin / Hair (`§ 4.118`, `DC 7806` / `7831`):** Severe xerosis/dermatitis or alopecia ([Skin Diff](11-skin-dermatitis-psoriasis-biologics-4.118.md)).
     * **Obesity Intermediate Step (`VAOPGCPREC 1-2017` / *Walsh*):** Hypothyroid metabolic weight gain -> obesity intermediate step -> **Obstructive Sleep Apnea (`DC 6847` CPAP = `50%`)** and weight-bearing knee/lumbar aggravation (`§ 3.310(b)`, [Playbook 14](../../playbooks/14-secondary-service-connection-aggravation-obesity.md))!

## 3. Diabetes Mellitus (`DC 7913`): Cumulative Criteria Trap & Note (1) Secondary Cascade

### A. Three-Era `DC 7913` Criteria Comparison (`1946` vs. `1996` vs. `2017`)

| Rating | Era 1: 1946 – June 5, 1996 | Era 2: June 6, 1996 – Dec. 9, 2017 (`61 FR 20446`) | Era 3: Dec. 10, 2017 – Present (`82 FR 50802`) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`100%`** | **Severe:** Large insulin doses (`>80` units), persistent glycosuria/acidosis, progressive loss of weight and strength. | Requiring **more than one daily injection of insulin**, restricted diet, and regulation of activities (avoidance of strenuous occupational and recreational activities) with episodes of ketoacidosis or hypoglycemic reactions requiring **`>= 3` hospitalizations/yr** or **weekly visits** to a diabetic care provider, **plus** either progressive loss of weight and strength **or** compensable complications if separately evaluated. | Unchanged substantive text (*"Requiring more than one daily injection of insulin, restricted diet, and regulation of activities..."* + `>= 3` hospitalizations/yr or weekly provider visits + progressive weight/strength loss OR compensable complications). |
| **`60%`** | **Moderately severe:** Large insulin requirement, restricted diet, careful regulation of activities, poor control. | Requiring **insulin**, restricted diet, and regulation of activities with episodes of ketoacidosis or hypoglycemic reactions requiring **`1 or 2` hospitalizations/yr** or **twice a month visits** to a diabetic care provider, **plus** complications that would not be compensable if separately evaluated. | Clarified first clause to: *"Requiring **one or more daily injection(s) of insulin**, restricted diet, and regulation of activities..."* (+ `1–2` hospitalizations/yr or twice-monthly visits + noncompensable complications). |
| **`40%`** | **Moderate:** Substantial insulin dosage and restricted diet, with fair sugar control. | Requiring **insulin**, **restricted diet**, **and** **regulation of activities**. | *"Requiring **one or more daily injection(s) of insulin**, **restricted diet**, **and** **regulation of activities**."* |
| **`20%`** | **Mild (with insulin/oral):** Small insulin dose or oral hypoglycemic agent and restricted diet. | Requiring **insulin and restricted diet**, **or**; **oral hypoglycemic agent and restricted diet**. | *"Requiring **one or more daily injection(s) of insulin and restricted diet**, **or**; **oral hypoglycemic agent and restricted diet**."* |
| **`10%`** | **Mild:** Manageable by restricted diet alone, good sugar control. | Manageable by **restricted diet only**. | *"Manageable by **restricted diet only**."* |

### B. Controlling Appellate Precedents on `DC 7913` (*Camacho*, *Middleton* & *Jackson*)

* **Strictly Cumulative & Successive Criteria (*Camacho* & *Middleton*):**
  * In ***Camacho v. Nicholson*, `21 Vet. App. 360, 366–67 (2007)`**, the CAVC held that `DC 7913`'s `40%` tier uses `"and"` conjunctively, requiring **all three elements**: `(1)` daily insulin, `(2)` restricted diet, **and** `(3)` **medically necessary** `"regulation of activities"`—defined parenthetically in the `100%` tier as ***avoidance of strenuous occupational and recreational activities*** (voluntary exercise to *promote* health does **not** satisfy avoidance of strenuous activities, and a physician/PA/NP must establish that avoiding strenuous occupational *and* recreational exertion is medically necessary to prevent hypoglycemia/hyperglycemia!).
  * In ***Middleton v. Shinseki*, `727 F.3d 1172, 1178 (Fed. Cir. 2013)`**, the Federal Circuit affirmed that `DC 7913` is a **successive, cumulative rating schedule**, barring a veteran from invoking **`38 C.F.R. § 4.7`** (*"more nearly approximates"*) to leapfrog to `40%` or `60%` when daily insulin injections are absent—even if the veteran satisfies hospital/provider visit counts!
  * **Modern GLP-1 / GIP Non-Insulin Injectable Trap:** Under `82 FR 50802`'s explicit text (*"one or more daily injection(s) of **insulin**"*), veterans treated exclusively with oral agents (`metformin`, `empagliflozin`/`Jardiance`, `dapagliflozin`/`Farxiga`, `sitagliptin`/`Januvia`, `glipizide`) **or non-insulin GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist injections** (`semaglutide`/`Ozempic`, `dulaglutide`/`Trulicity`, `liraglutide`/`Victoza`, `tirzepatide`/`Mounjaro`) **without insulin** cap at **`20%`** under `DC 7913` *(Note: Insulin pump continuous subcutaneous infusion delivers continuous daily insulin and satisfies the daily insulin requirement)*.
* **Medication Contemplation Boundary (*Jackson v. McDonough* vs. *Ingram v. Collins*):**
  * In ***Jackson v. McDonough*, `37 Vet. App. 87 (2023)`**, the CAVC held that because `DC 7913` explicitly incorporates insulin and oral hypoglycemic agents into its rating tiers, VA properly evaluates the medicated disability picture under `DC 7913` without speculating about unmedicated diabetic severity. **Crucial Distinction:** Under ***Ingram v. Collins*, `38 Vet. App. 130 (2025)`**, secondary diabetic complications rated under Diagnostic Codes **silent on medication**—most notably **diabetic peripheral neuropathy (`DC 8520` / `8515`)** treated with gabapentin, pregabalin (`Lyrica`), or duloxetine (`Cymbalta`)—**cannot** be discounted for medication relief!

### C. `DC 7913` Note (1) Mandatory Separate Complications Cascade (`DeLisio` Scope Rule)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **`DC 7913` Note (1) & *DeLisio v. Shinseki*, `25 Vet. App. 45 (2011)`:**
> * **Note (1)** mandates: *"Evaluate compensable complications of diabetes **separately unless they are part of the criteria used to support a 100-percent evaluation**. Noncompensable complications are considered part of the diabetic process under diagnostic code 7913."* (Note (2) bars ordering a glucose tolerance test solely for rating purposes once diabetes is diagnosed).
> * Under ***DeLisio*** and `M21-1`, a claim for `DC 7913` Diabetes (or a `DC 7913` increased-rating claim) **reasonably encompasses** any diabetic complication shown by medical evidence during the pendency of the claim! Even when `DC 7913` sits at `20%`, unbundling Note (1) complications routinely drives combined ratings to **`80%–100% + SMC(k) + SMC(s)`** and triggers **`38 C.F.R. § 4.16(a)(1)` single-disability aggregation** (all secondary diabetic complications combine as **one** disability to clear the `40%` or `60%` schedular TDIU gate!).
