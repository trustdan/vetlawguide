## 1. Functional Loss, Painful Motion & Medication Canons (`38 C.F.R. §§ 4.40, 4.45, 4.59, 4.10`)

Every musculoskeletal joint evaluation rests on four interlocking regulations ([Verbatim Snapshot](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.71a-joints-arthritis-pre-and-post-2021.md)): **`§ 4.40` (Functional loss)**, **`§ 4.45` (The joints)**, **`§ 4.59` (Painful motion)**, and **`§ 4.10` (Functional impairment)**.

### A. Functional Loss on Repetition, Flare-Ups & C&P Testing (`§§ 4.40, 4.45, 4.59`)

| Controlling Precedent | Regulatory Hook | Doctrinal Rule | Bitemporal CUE vs. Open-Claim Gate (`§ 20.1403(e)`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ***DeLuca v. Brown*, `8 Vet. App. 202, 205–07 (1995)`** | `§§ 4.40, 4.45, 4.71a` | Diagnostic codes predicated on limitation of motion (`DC 5201`, `5206–5207`, `5251–5252`, `5260–5261`, `5271`, `5235–5243`) are **not** capped by single-pass passive goniometer arcs: VA must evaluate **additional functional loss** caused by **weakness, fatigability, incoordination, or pain on repetitive use** and express that loss in terms of equivalent additional ROM degrees lost. | **Open-Claim / Appeal Remand Engine:** An examiner's failure to record *DeLuca* repetitive-use loss is an **inadequate-exam / duty-to-assist error**, which **cannot** constitute CUE under ***Cook v. Principi*, `318 F.3d 1334 (Fed. Cir. 2002) (en banc)`** (*unless* the C&P report already quantified the exact post-repetition/flare ROM degrees meeting a higher tier and the rater ignored them!). |
| ***Mitchell v. Shinseki*, `25 Vet. App. 32, 40–44 (2011)`** | `§§ 4.40, 4.45` | Pain **alone** does not automatically equal the limitation-of-motion degrees at which pain begins! For pain during motion (e.g., knee flexion pain starting at `90°` with full movement to `130°`) to trigger a higher ROM tier (`DC 5260` `<= 45°`), the medical/lay evidence must show pain causes **functional loss equivalent to inability to perform movement beyond the compensable ROM cutoff** (e.g., pain preventing weight-bearing, squatting, or repetitive flexion past `45°`). | **`open-claim-only-george-restricted`:** Defeats naive CUE claims arguing *"pain began at `30°` flexion = automatic `20%` `DC 5260`"* where full active motion persisted without equivalent functional loss. |
| ***Sharp v. Shulkin*, `29 Vet. App. 26, 32–36 (2017)`** | `§§ 4.40, 4.45` | C&P examiners **must elicit flare-up frequency, duration, and functional impairment** from the veteran and provide a medical estimate of additional ROM loss during flares—even when the exam is **not** conducted during an active flare—unless the examiner explains why **medical knowledge as a whole** (not personal reluctance or lack of contemporaneous observation) renders any estimate speculative. | **Open-Claim Only (*Cook* CUE Bar):** Classic Board/CAVC remand hook against boilerplate *"cannot estimate flare-up loss without mere speculation because veteran not examined during flare"* DBQs. |
| ***Correia v. McDonald*, `28 Vet. App. 158, 166–70 (2016)`** | Final sentence of `§ 4.59` | To be adequate, a joint examination **must** (where feasible) test and record ROM across **four modalities** on **both the affected and opposite undamaged joint**: **(1) active motion**, **(2) passive motion**, **(3) weight-bearing**, and **(4) nonweight-bearing**. | **Open-Claim Only (*Cook* CUE Bar):** Never plead a *Correia* missing-modality DBQ defect as RO/Board CUE—file a Supplemental Claim, HLR, or Board/CAVC remand! |

### B. `38 C.F.R. § 4.59` Minimum Compensable Rating, *Saunders* Pain-Only SC & *Ingram* Medication Silence

`38 C.F.R. § 4.59` declares: *"It is the intention to recognize actually painful, unstable, or malaligned joints, due to healed injury, as entitled to at least the minimum compensable rating for the joint."* Five controlling precedents govern its reach:

1. **Not Limited to Arthritis (***Burton v. Shinseki*, `25 Vet. App. 1, 4–6 (2011)`**):** Although `§ 4.59` mentions arthritis in its opening sentences, its painful-motion rule applies across **all musculoskeletal joint and periarticular conditions** (sprains, strains, rotator cuff tendinopathy, meniscal tears, bursitis, surgical residuals), with or without radiographic arthritis.
2. **Objective Evidence of Painful Motion — No ROM Loss Required (***Petitti v. McDonald*, `27 Vet. App. 415, 424–26 (2015)`**):** `§ 4.59` does **not** require goniometer ROM loss reaching a schedular cutoff; it requires **satisfactory/objective evidence of actually painful motion** (e.g., examiner-observed grimacing, wincing, crepitus with tenderness on movement, guarding, spasm, or consistent documented painful arc), triggering at least the **minimum compensable rating** for that joint (`20%` under `DC 5201` shoulder; `10%` under hip/knee/ankle/elbow/wrist codes).
3. **Applies Even to Non-ROM Codes & Codes With a `0%` Tier (***Southall-Norman v. McDonald*, `28 Vet. App. 346, 351–54 (2016)`**):** `§ 4.59` is **not** restricted to range-of-motion diagnostic codes, nor is it defeated by the presence of a `0%` tier in the selected code (specifically holding `§ 4.59` applies to bilateral pes planus under **`DC 5276`**, elevating a `0%` mild flatfoot rating to the **`10%` minimum compensable tier** when objective painful motion of the foot/subtalar/tarsal joints is present!). *(Exception: If a Diagnostic Code has **no compensable tier at all**—such as `DC 5230` ring or little finger limitation of motion (`0%` max)—`§ 4.59` cannot invent a `10%` tier unless ankylosis or multi-joint arthritis applies).*
4. **Undiagnosed Functional Pain as a Compensable `"Disability"` (***Saunders v. Wilkie*, `886 F.3d 1356, 1361–68 (Fed. Cir. 2018)`**):** Overturning *Sanchez-Benitez v. West*, the Federal Circuit held that **pain alone—even absent a diagnosed underlying pathology—constitutes a `"disability"` for service connection under `38 U.S.C. § 1110`** whenever the pain causes functional impairment diminishing earning capacity.
5. **Baseline Unmedicated Joint Severity (***Ingram v. Collins*, `38 Vet. App. 130 (2025)`**):** Where a musculoskeletal Diagnostic Code does **not** explicitly factor medication into its criteria (true of virtually all joint ROM and instability codes in `§ 4.71a`), examiners and adjudicators **cannot discount the veteran's baseline joint pain, flare-up severity, or ROM loss** based on relief from prescription or over-the-counter medications (`NSAIDs`, meloxicam, celecoxib, intra-articular corticosteroid/hyaluronic-acid injections, gabapentin, or biologics).

> [!CAUTION]
> **Quarantine & Bitemporal Gate (*George v. McDonough*, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)` & Rescinded `91 FR 7118`):**
> * **Rescinded `§ 4.10` IFR Quarantine:** On February 17, 2026, VA promulgated an Interim Final Rule at **`91 FR 7118`** attempting to rewrite `38 C.F.R. § 4.10` to evaluate disabilities at their medicated level; **10 days later, VA formally rescinded `91 FR 7118` *ab initio* at `91 FR 9712` (Feb. 27, 2026)**. Never cite `91 FR 7118` as active law, and audit any musculoskeletal rating issued between February 17 and February 27, 2026!
> * ***George* CUE Split:** Attacking a **pre-2011** non-arthritis `0%` rating via *Burton*, a **pre-2016** `DC 5276` `0%` rating via *Southall-Norman*, a **pre-2018** pain-only denial via *Saunders*, or a **pre-2025** medicated-ROM decision via *Ingram* as standalone **CUE** is **`open-claim-only-george-restricted`** (`38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`). By contrast, assigning `0%` to an **X-ray-confirmed arthritic major joint with undisputed objective painful motion** has violated plain regulatory text and ***Lichtenfels v. Derwinski*, `1 Vet. App. 484 (1991)`** for decades (**`cue-and-open-claim`**)!

## 2. Multi-Joint, Degenerative & Traumatic Arthritis + Fibromyalgia (`DC 5002–5025`)

### A. Multi-Joint Active Inflammatory Arthritis (`DC 5002`) vs. Degenerative / Post-Traumatic Arthritis (`DC 5003` & `DC 5010`)

Effective **February 7, 2021 (`85 FR 76453`)**, VA retitled `DC 5002` from *"Arthritis rheumatoid (atrophic) As an active process"* to ***"Multi-joint arthritis (except post-traumatic and gout), 2 or more joints, as an active process"*** (encompassing rheumatoid, psoriatic, reactive, enteropathic, and seronegative inflammatory polyarthropathies; `§ 3.309(a)` 1-year chronic disease).

| Feature | `DC 5002` (Multi-Joint Inflammatory Arthritis) | `DC 5003` (Degenerative Arthritis) & `DC 5010` (Post-Traumatic Arthritis) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Evaluation Pathways** | Rated **EITHER** *(whichever is higher—never combined!)*:<br/>**1. Active Process:**<br/>• **`100%`:** Constitutional manifestations associated with active joint involvement, **totally incapacitating**.<br/>• **`60%`:** Less than criteria for `100%` **with weight loss and anemia** productive of severe impairment of health, OR **4 or more** incapacitating exacerbations/year.<br/>• **`40%`:** Symptom combinations productive of **definite impairment of health** objectively supported by examination, OR **3 or more** incapacitating exacerbations/year.<br/>• **`20%`:** **1 or 2** exacerbations/year in a well-established diagnosis.<br/>**OR 2. ### B. Major Joints vs. Minor Joint Groups (`38 C.F.R. § 4.45(f)`) & The *Lichtenfels* Asymmetry

Under **`38 C.F.R. § 4.45(f)`** ([Verbatim Snapshot](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.71a-joints-arthritis-pre-and-post-2021.md)), practitioners must strictly distinguish **Major Joints** from **Minor Joint Groups** when applying `DC 5003`:
* **Six Paired Major Joints (`12` total):** **Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist, Hip, Knee, and Ankle** *(Note: Hands and Feet as a whole are NOT "major joints"—their constituent joints are minor joint groups!).*
* **Minor Joint Groups:**
  1. Multiple involvement of **interphalangeal (`DIP`/`PIP`), metacarpophalangeal (`MCP`), and carpal** joints of the upper extremities *(each affected hand's multiple involved joints = `1` minor joint group)*.
  2. Multiple involvement of **interphalangeal, metatarsophalangeal (`MTP`), and tarsal** joints of the lower extremities *(each affected foot's multiple involved joints = `1` minor joint group)*.
  3. **Lumbosacral, sacroiliac, cervical, and dorsal (thoracic) spine** segments *(vertebrae count as minor joint groups for `DC 5003` when noncompensable under `DC 5235–5243`)*.
* **The *Lichtenfels* Single-Joint vs. Multi-Joint Asymmetry (***Lichtenfels v. Derwinski*, `1 Vet. App. 484 (1991)`**):**
  * **Painful Motion (`§§ 4.40 / 4.59`):** Painful motion of even **ONE single major joint** (e.g., right knee alone) earns **`10%`** (`20%` for shoulder under `DC 5201`)—and painful motion of **three** major joints (e.g., right knee, left knee, right ankle) earns **three separate `10%` ratings (`10% + 10% + 10%` + `§ 4.26` bilateral factor = `30%`)**!
  * **Non-Painful X-Ray Arthritis (`DC 5003` Fallback):** Conversely, if a veteran has X-ray osteoarthritis in **four** major joints with full, **completely pain-free** motion (`0%` each), `DC 5003` awards **only ONE single combined `10%` rating** (`20%` with occasional incapacitating exacerbations)—**never** `10%` per non-painful joint!

### C. Bone/Soft-Tissue Codes (`DC 5013–5024`) & Fibromyalgia (`DC 5025`)

* **`DC 5013–5024` Cross-Hook (`85 FR 76453`):** Osteoporosis (`5013`), Osteomalacia (`5014`), Benign bone neoplasm (`5015`), Paget's disease / osteitis deformans (`5016`), Gout (`5017`, rated under **`DC 5002`**!), Intermittent hydrarthrosis (`5018`), Bursitis (`5019`), Synovitis (`5020`), Myositis (`5021`), Periostitis (`5022`), Heterotopic ossification / myositis ossificans (`5023`), and Tenosynovitis / tendinopathy (`5024`) are rated **as degenerative arthritis (`DC 5003`)** on limitation of motion / `§ 4.59` painful motion of affected joints.
* **Fibromyalgia (`DC 5025`, enacted May 7, 1996 at `61 FR 20438`):** Requires **widespread musculoskeletal pain and tender points**, with or without associated fatigue, sleep disturbance, stiffness, paresthesias, headache, irritable bowel symptoms, depression, anxiety, or Raynaud's-like symptoms:
  * **`40%`:** Constant, or nearly so, **AND refractory to therapy**.
  * **`20%`:** Episodic, with exacerbations often precipitated by environmental or emotional stress or overexertion, **present more than one-third of the time**.
  * **`10%`:** **That require continuous medication for control** *(Notice `DC 5025` `10%` explicitly mentions continuous medication, whereas `40%` requires refractoriness to therapy—an explicit statutory/schedular medication exception recognized in Ingram!)*.
  * **Gulf War `§ 3.317` MUCMI & Anti-Pyramiding Interplay:** Fibromyalgia is one of the three named presumptive **Medically Unexplained Chronic Multisymptom Illnesses (`MUCMIs`)** for Southwest Asia / `38 U.S.C. § 1117` service ([Playbook 15](../../playbooks/15-gulf-war-mucmi-burn-pits-pact-tera.md)). If associated IBS (`DC 7319`), migraines (`DC 8100`), or depression/anxiety (`§ 4.130`) are **NOT** used to justify the `DC 5025` fibromyalgia tier (because widespread pain/tender points alone satisfy `40%` constant/refractory), separate ratings for those comorbid conditions are permissible under `§ 4.14` (*Esteban v. Brown*, `6 Vet. App. 259 (1994)`).

### B. The *Hudgens v. McDonald* (`823 F.3d 630`) Partial Knee Replacement Timeline (`Pre-July 16, 2015` vs. `80 FR 42040`)

1. **Pre-July 16, 2015 Unamended `DC 5055`:** Prior to July 16, 2015, `DC 5055` covered *"Knee replacement (prosthesis)"* without specifying `"total"` vs. `"partial"`. In ***Hudgens v. McDonald*, `823 F.3d 630, 637–39 (Fed. Cir. 2016)`**, the Federal Circuit held that the unamended regulation did **not** unambiguously exclude **partial / unicompartmental knee arthroplasties** (and `11` of `14` Board decisions had granted `DC 5055` to partial replacements), resolving interpretive doubt in the veteran's favor!
2. **July 16, 2015 Interpretive/Final Rule (`80 FR 42040`):** On **July 16, 2015** (`80 FR 42040`, effective and applicable to claims filed on or after July 16, 2015), VA added an explanatory Note to `DC 5051–5056` stating `"prosthetic replacement"` means a **total** replacement of the named joint (*except* `DC 5054` hip, which covers total replacement of the femoral head *or* acetabulum), later reinforced in `85 FR 76453` (Note 3).
3. **Practitioner Audit Rule:** For any claim or continuous appeal **pending prior to July 16, 2015**, a partial/unicompartmental knee replacement qualifies for the **`1-year` (`13-month`) `100%` rating AND the permanent `30%` minimum floor** under *Hudgens*! For partial replacements performed under claims filed **on or after July 16, 2015**, claim **`38 C.F.R. § 4.30`** (`1` to `3+` months temporary `100%` surgical convalescence) followed by stacked knee residual codes (`DC 5257 + 5260 + 5261`). *(Note: Under George v. McDonough, attacking a final pre-2016 RO/Board denial of DC 5055 for a partial knee replacement via standalone CUE is barred by `§ 20.1403(e)`).*

### B. Elbow & Forearm Multi-Plane Stacking (`DC 5205–5213`)

Unlike the shoulder (`DC 5201`), the elbow/forearm has **three separate movement-plane Diagnostic Codes** (`Normal` = `0°` extension to `145°` flexion; `85°` supination / `80°` pronation per `§ 4.71` Plate I):
* **`DC 5206` (Forearm, limitation of flexion):** Flexion limited to `45°` (`50%` Maj / `40%` Min) \| `55°` (`40% / 30%`) \| `70°` (`30% / 20%`) \| `90°` (`20% / 20%`) \| `100°` (`10% / 10%`) \| `110°` (`0% / 0%`).
* **`DC 5207` (Forearm, limitation of extension):** Extension limited to `110°` (`50% / 40%`) \| `100°` (`40% / 30%`) \| `90°` (`30% / 20%`) \| `75°` (`20% / 20%`) \| `60°` (`10% / 10%`) \| `45°` (`10% / 10%`). *(Note **`DC 5208`**: Flexion limited to `100°` **and** extension to `45°` = **`20% / 20%`**, beating `10% + 10% = 19%` when no bilateral factor applies, though separate `10% + 10%` may combine favorably with `§ 4.26`—assign whichever is higher!)*
* **`DC 5213` (Supination and pronation, impairment of):** Bone fusion/synostosis (`40% / 30%` unfavorable; `30% / 20%` favorable); pronation lost beyond middle of arc (`30% / 20%`) or beyond last quarter (`20% / 20%`); supination to `30°` or less (`10% / 10%`).
* **Triple-Plane Elbow Stacking Rule (`§ 4.14` / *Esteban* / *Brady*):** Separate ratings under **`DC 5206` (flexion) + `DC 5207` (extension) + `DC 5213` (supination/pronation)** are permissible on the **same arm** whenever goniometer findings establish distinct limitations in each anatomical plane (mirroring `VAOPGCPREC 9-2004`; however, `§ 4.59` painful motion **cannot** be used twice to award `10% + 10%` where goniometer motion is noncompensable in both planes—that double-counts the same joint pain under *Brady v. Brown*, `4 Vet. App. 203 (1993)`!).

### C. Wrist, Hand & Digits (`DC 5214–5230`, `§§ 4.63–4.71`) & `38 C.F.R. § 4.69` Hand Dominance Traps

* **Wrist (`DC 5214–5215`):** `DC 5214` Ankylosis (`50%/40%` unfavorable, `40%/30%` intermediate, `30%/20%` favorable); **`DC 5215` Limitation of motion** (`10%` Maj / `10%` Min for dorsiflexion `< 15°`, palmar flexion limited in line with forearm, OR objective painful motion under `§ 4.59`).
* **Digits (`DC 5216–5230`):** Evaluated across multi-digit ankylosis (`DC 5216–5223`), single-digit ankylosis (`DC 5224` Thumb `20%/20%` or `10%/10%`; `DC 5225` Index `10%/10%`; `DC 5226` Long `10%/10%`; `DC 5227` Ring or Little `0%/0%`), and single-digit limitation of motion (`DC 5228` Thumb opposition gap > `2` inches = `20%/20%`, `1–2` inches = `10%/10%`; `DC 5229` Index/Long `>1` inch fingertip-to-transverse-crease gap or `>30°` extension loss = `10%/10%`; `DC 5230` Ring/Little = `0%/0%`). Under `85 FR 76453`, multi-digit combinations impairing thumb-to-finger opposition/grasp convert directly under `DC 5216–5223` Notes (and complete loss of effective grasping/pinching triggers **`70%/60%` Hand Loss of Use (`DC 5125`) + `SMC(k)`** under `38 C.F.R. § 4.63`!).
* **`38 C.F.R. § 4.69` Dominant-Hand (`Major` vs. `Minor`) Audit Rules (`62 FR 30235`, eff. July 3, 1997):**
  1. **Single Dominant Hand Mandate:** *"Only one hand shall be considered dominant."* Assigning `Minor` percentages to a veteran's documented dominant arm on `DC 5121–5125`, `5200`, `5201` (`25°/45°`), `5202`, `5205–5207`, `5213–5214`, `5301–5309`, or `8510–8519` is an **undebatable RO/Board CUE** (`cue-and-open-claim`).
  2. **The Post-Injury / Post-Amputation "Dominance Flip" Trap:** When a naturally right-handed veteran suffers a devastating injury, nerve palsy, or amputation of the right hand/arm and adapts over years to write and eat with the left hand, C&P examiners frequently check *"Left-handed"* on modern DBQs—causing raters to **unlawfully downgrade the injured/amputated originally dominant right arm to `Minor`!** Handedness for the primary injured limb is governed by **pre-injury natural dominance** evidenced in entrance/service records (`DD 2808` / `SF 88`), NOT forced post-injury compensatory adaptation! *(And if the opposite minor hand breaks down from compensatory overuse, claim it as secondary under `§ 3.310(a)`!).*
  3. **Ambidextrous Reversal Rule:** Conversely, when evidence or VA testing establishes genuine **ambidexterity**, `§ 4.69` explicitly mandates: *"The injured hand, or **the most severely injured hand**, of an ambidextrous individual will be considered the dominant hand for rating purposes"*—meaning if the opposite hand later becomes more severely disabled, `Major` designation shifts to whichever hand yields the higher combined schedule!

## 5. Lower Extremity — Hip (`DC 5250–5255`), Knee Multi-Code Stacking (`DC 5256–5263`) & Ankle/Foot (`DC 5269–5284`)

### A. Hip & Thigh Triple-Plane Stacking (`DC 5250–5255`)

Like the elbow, the hip joint separates movement across **three independent anatomical planes** (`Normal` = `125°` flexion, `30°`/`0°` extension, `45°` abduction, `25°` adduction, `40°` external/`60°` internal rotation per `§ 4.71` Plate II):
1. **`DC 5251` (Thigh, limitation of extension):** Extension limited to `5°` = **`10%`**.
2. **`DC 5252` (Thigh, limitation of flexion):** Flexion limited to `10°` (**`40%`**) \| `20°` (**`30%`**) \| `30°` (**`20%`**) \| `45°` (**`10%`**).
3. **`DC 5253` (Thigh, impairment of — abduction, adduction & rotation):** Loss of abduction beyond `10°` = **`20%`**; cannot cross legs (adduction) **or** cannot toe-out more than `15°` (rotation), affected leg = **`10%`**.
4. **Triple-Plane Hip Stacking Rule:** Under `§ 4.14` (*Esteban*) and `M21-1` V.iii.1.B, a veteran with goniometer-confirmed loss in multiple planes can receive **up to THREE concurrent ratings on a single hip (`DC 5251` `10%` + `DC 5252` `10–40%` + `DC 5253` `10–20%`)**! *(Again, `§ 4.59` painful motion without schedular ROM loss yields a single `10%` hip rating, not `10% x 3`).* Also check **`DC 5255` (Femur, impairment of)**: fracture nonunion w/ loose motion (`60%` / `40%` w/ brace), or malunion with marked knee/hip disability (`30%`), moderate (`20%`), slight (`10%`).

### B. The Knee Multi-Stacking Goldmine (`DC 5257 + 5260 + 5261 + 5258/5259`)

No joint in the VASRD produces more under-rated code sheets—or cleaner CUEs—than the knee (`DC 5256–5263`):

### C. Ankle (`DC 5270–5274`) & Foot Architecture (`DC 5269, 5276–5284`)

* **Ankle (`DC 5270–5274`):** `DC 5270` Ankylosis (`40%` plantar flexion `> 40°` or dorsiflexion `> 10°` w/ deformity; `30%` `30°–40°`; `20%` `< 30°`); **`DC 5271` Limited motion** (`20%` Marked / `10%` Moderate—with explicit `< 5°` / `< 10°` and `< 15°` / `< 30°` cutoffs added **Feb. 7, 2021** at `85 FR 76453`); `DC 5272` Subastragalar/tarsal ankylosis (`20%`/`10%`); `DC 5273` Os calcis/astragalus malunion (`20%`/`10%`); `DC 5274` Astragalectomy (`20%`).
* **Foot Schedule (`DC 5269, 5276–5284`) & The `§ 4.26` Bilateral Factor Trap:**
  * **`DC 5276` (Flatfoot / Pes Planus, acquired):**
    * **Pronounced** *(marked pronation, extreme tenderness of plantar surfaces, marked inward displacement/spasm of Achilles tendon, **not improved by orthopedic shoes or appliances**)*: **`50%` Bilateral** \| **`30%` Unilateral**.
    * **Severe** *(objective evidence of marked deformity—pain on manipulation/use accentuated, indication of swelling on use, characteristic callosities)*: **`30%` Bilateral** \| **`20%` Unilateral**.
    * **Moderate** *(weight-bearing line over/medial to great toe, inward bowing of Achilles, pain on manipulation/use)*: **`10%` Bilateral or Unilateral**.
    * **Mild** *(symptoms relieved by built-up shoe or arch support)*: **`0%`** *(elevated to **`10%`** under `§ 4.59` if objective painful motion exists per Southall-Norman, `28 Vet. App. at 352`!)*.
  * **CRITICAL `§ 4.26` Bilateral Factor Trap on Foot Codes:**
    1. **Single Bilateral-Tier Code (`DC 5276`, `DC 5278` Claw Foot `50%/30%`, `DC 5269` Plantar Fasciitis `30%/20%`, `DC 5279` Metatarsalgia `10%`):** When rated as a **single bilateral evaluation** with *no other* compensable lower-extremity SC disability, VA does **NOT** add a standalone `+10%` `§ 4.26` bilateral bonus onto that single number (because bilateral involvement is already built into `50%` vs. `30%`). **HOWEVER**, if the veteran holds **any separate compensable SC disability on either lower extremity** (e.g., `10%` right knee `DC 5260`), the `50%` bilateral `DC 5276` rating **DOES enter the `§ 4.26` bilateral-factor pool** (`50%` + `10%` = `55%` + `5.5%` bilateral bonus = `60.5% -> 60%`)!
    2. **Two Separate Unilateral Foot Ratings (`DC 5284` Foot Injuries, Other: `30%` Severe, `20%` Moderately Severe, `10%` Moderate; `DC 5283` Tarsal/Metatarsal Malunion; `DC 5280` Hallux Valgus `10%`; `DC 5281` Hallux Rigidus `10%`):** Because `DC 5284` has **no** built-in bilateral tier, bilateral foot injuries rated as **`DC 5284` Right (`30%`) + `DC 5284` Left (`30%`)** **AUTOMATICALLY trigger the `+10%` `§ 4.26` bilateral factor** (`30% + 30% = 51% + 5.1% = 56.1% -> 60%`!)—frequently **beating** a `30%` bilateral `DC 5269` plantar fasciitis rating!
  * **Multi-Code Foot Stacking (`38 C.F.R. § 4.14`; *Esteban v. Brown*, `6 Vet. App. 259, 261–62 (1994)`):** A veteran with pes planus (`DC 5276`) **and** distinct non-overlapping foot pathologies—such as hallux valgus (`DC 5280`), hammer toes (`DC 5282`), Morton's metatarsalgia (`DC 5279`), tarsal malunion (`DC 5283`), or Achilles/ankle limitation (`DC 5271`)—is entitled to **separate ratings** so long as the exact same symptom (e.g., plantar tenderness) is not compensated twice (`§ 4.14`; note `DC 5281` Note explicitly bars combining hallux rigidus with `DC 5278` claw foot), capped per foot/below-knee at **`40%`** under `38 C.F.R. § 4.68`! *(Where pain on prolonged standing is uncompensated by `DC 5276`'s manipulation/use criteria, also evaluate `§ 3.321(b)(1)` extraschedular referral per **Yancy v. McDonald**, `27 Vet. App. 484, 490–96 (2016)`).*
  * **Pre- vs. Post-Feb. 7, 2021 Plantar Fasciitis (`DC 5269`):** Under `DC 5269` (`86 FR 8142`), a veteran whose bilateral plantar fasciitis was **never recommended for surgery** caps out at **`10%`** (even if orthotics/injections fail!), whereas pre-Feb. 7, 2021 analogous rating under `DC 5276` allowed **`30%` or `50%` bilateral** without surgery! Always audit pre-Feb. 7, 2021 pending claims for protected higher `5299-5276` evaluations, and for post-2021 claims check **Note (2)** (*recommended for surgery, but medically not a surgical candidate -> `20%/30%`*) or **`40%` actual Loss of Use of the foot + `SMC(k)`**!

### B. Mechanics & Exceptions of the `38 C.F.R. § 4.68` Amputation Rule Ceiling

`38 C.F.R. § 4.68` (*Amputation rule*, [Verbatim Snapshot](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.71a-joints-arthritis-pre-and-post-2021.md)) mandates:
> *"The combined rating for disabilities of an extremity shall not exceed the rating for the amputation at the elective level, were amputation to be performed. For example, the combined evaluations for disabilities below the knee shall not exceed the **40 percent** evaluation, diagnostic code **5165**. This 40 percent rating may be further combined with evaluation for disabilities above the knee but not to exceed the above the knee amputation elective level."*

1. **How the Stepwise Distal-to-Proximal Cap Works:**
   * **Below-the-Knee Cap (`40%` under `DC 5165`):** If a veteran has multiple SC disabilities strictly **below the right knee**—e.g., `DC 5271` ankle (`20%`) + `DC 5276` unilateral pes planus (`30%`) + `DC 8521` common peroneal nerve (`20%`)—their raw combination (`55% -> 60%`) is **capped at `40%`** (`DC 5165` below-knee amputation elective level; *unless* knee-action control is lost per `DC 5164` = `60%`).
   * **Combining Below-Knee (`40%`) With Knee / Thigh Disabilities (`60% / 80% / 90%` Cap):** That capped `40%` below-knee block **then combines** with SC disabilities at or above the knee on the same leg—e.g., `DC 5257` knee instability (`20%`) + `DC 5261` extension (`30%`) + `DC 8520` sciatic nerve (`40%`)—up to the applicable above-knee elective amputation ceiling (`60%` middle/lower thigh `DC 5162`, `80%` upper third `DC 5161`, or `90%` hip disarticulation `DC 5160`). *(Painful stump neuroma after amputation receives the rating for the **elective site of reamputation** under `§ 4.68`, plus `§ 4.67` pelvic-girdle rules).*
2. **CRITICAL EXCEPTIONS THAT PIERCE OR SIT OUTSIDE `§ 4.68` (High-Value CUE / Audit Goldmines):**
   * **Proximal Spine & Trunk Disabilities Are NEVER Capped by `§ 4.68`:** Thoracolumbar/cervical spine ratings (`DC 5235–5243`), pelvic fractures (`DC 5236`), and gluteal/pelvic girdle muscles (`DC 5317`) are **not** distal extremity disabilities and combine freely on top of the `§ 4.68` extremity cap!
   * **Skin/Scars (`DC 7800–7805`) & Vascular Systemic Sequelae:** Painful/unstable superficial scars (`DC 7804`) or deep soft-tissue defects not solely affecting extremity propulsion, and systemic secondary conditions, require careful audit so raters do not unlawfully sweep non-musculoskeletal body systems under `§ 4.68`.
   * **Mandatory Presumptive Secondary Cardiovascular Disease (`38 C.F.R. § 3.310(c)`):** Whenever a veteran has a service-connected **unilateral amputation of one lower extremity at or above the knee** (`DC 5160–5162`) OR **bilateral amputations of both lower extremities at or above the ankles** (`DC 5160–5165`), **ischemic heart disease (`DC 7005`) or ANY other cardiovascular disease** developing thereafter **MUST be held proximately due to the amputation as a matter of law** ([`§ 3.310` Snapshot](../../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.310-secondary-aggravation-evolution.md), [Playbook 14](../../playbooks/14-secondary-service-connection-aggravation-obesity.md))! Denying secondary SC (or DIC under `§ 1310`) for IHD, hypertension, stroke, or heart failure in a qualifying `AKA` or bilateral `BKA` amputee is an **undebatable CUE (`cue-and-open-claim`)**!
   * **Multiple Annual Clothing Allowances (`38 U.S.C. § 1162`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.810` & *Sursely v. Peake*, `551 F.3d 1351 (Fed. Cir. 2009)`):** Whenever a veteran wears **multiple** prosthetic limbs or rigid orthopedic braces (e.g., bilateral hinged knee braces under `DC 5257`, an ankle-foot orthosis `AFO` + knee brace, or prosthesis + spinal brace) that each independently wear out or tear distinct articles of outer clothing (or uses skin medication staining garments), *Sursely* entitles the veteran to **multiple annual clothing allowances**—never just one ([Playbook 13](../../playbooks/13-ancillary-grants-sah-auto-clothing-dea-spina-bifida.md))!
