| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **`60%` Evaluation** | Following typical coronary occlusion/thrombosis, OR with history of **substernal anginal pain on mild exertion** / *NYHA Class III* marked limitation of physical activity (less than ordinary activity causes dyspnea, fatigue, or angina). | • **More than one (`> 1`) episode of acute congestive heart failure (`CHF`) in the past year**, **OR**<br/>• Workload of **`> 3 METs` to `5 METs`** results in dyspnea, fatigue, angina, dizziness, or syncope, **OR**<br/>• **Left ventricular dysfunction with an ejection fraction (`LVEF`) of `30 to 50 percent` (`30%–50%`)**! | • Workload of **`3.1–5.0 METs`** results in **heart failure symptoms**.<br/>*(**DELETED:** Standalone `LVEF 30%–50%` and `> 1` episode of acute CHF in past year!)* |

### B. Three Multi-Six-Figure Audit & Litigation Goldmines Across the Three Eras

1. **The `1998–2021` `LVEF` & `ECG/X-Ray LVH` CUE Goldmine (`cue-and-open-claim`):**
   * Between **January 12, 1998 and November 13, 2021**, VA cardiac DBQs routinely recorded interview-estimated `> 7–10 METs` (`10%`) for veterans on cardiac meds while VAMC cardiology notes, nuclear stress tests (`SPECT`/`MUGA`), catheterizations, chest X-rays, `12-lead ECGs` (*Sokolow-Lyon / Cornell voltage LVH*), or transthoracic echocardiograms in the C-file (or constructively in VAMC records under *Bell v. Derwinski*) documented:
     * **`LVEF < 30%`** -> Mandatory **`100%`** (+ check *Bradley* `SMC(s)` statutory housebound!);
     * **`LVEF 30%–50%`** (e.g., `LVEF 45%` or `"45–50%"` under `§ 4.7` / `§ 4.3`!) -> Mandatory **`60%`**; or
     * **Left ventricular hypertrophy (`LVH`) or chamber dilatation** on **ECG, chest X-ray, or echo** -> Mandatory **`30%`**.
   * Because the Era-2 criteria were strictly **disjunctive (`"or"`)** (*Tatum v. Shinseki*, `23 Vet. App. 152 (2009)`), assigning `10%` based on `METs` when `LVEF 45%` or `LVH` was undisputedly in the record is **undebatable plain-text RO CUE (`38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)`)**!
2. **Transitional Protection (`38 U.S.C. § 1155` / `§ 3.951(a)`) & Straddling Claims (`Nov. 14, 2021`):**
   * **Running Pre-Nov. 14, 2021 Ratings Cannot Be Reduced by the Rule Change:** Under **`38 U.S.C. § 1155`** and **`38 C.F.R. § 3.951(a)`**, no readjustment of the VASRD may be used to reduce any veteran's disability rating in effect on the effective date (`Nov. 14, 2021`) unless actual medical improvement is proven **under the *prior* (`1998–2021`) criteria** (e.g., proving `LVEF` actually improved above `50%` on sustained examination under `§ 3.344`!). Any post-2021 reduction of a grandfathered `60%` (`LVEF 40%`) rating to `10%` based solely on `> 7 METs` is **void ab initio** ([Playbook 05](../../playbooks/05-void-rating-reductions-stabilized-ratings.md)).
   * **Pending Claims Straddling Nov. 14, 2021:** Under the `86 FR 54089` applicability clause and *Karnas v. Derwinski*, `1 Vet. App. 308 (1991)`, any claim filed *before* November 14, 2021 and pending on/after that date **must** be evaluated under Era-2 (`LVEF` / `CHF` / `ECG LVH`) for all periods prior to Nov. 14, 2021, and retains the higher Era-2 evaluation from Nov. 14, 2021 forward unless Era-3 criteria yield an even higher rating!
3. **Contraindicated Exercise `METs` Testing & Non-SC Orthopedic Confounders:**
   * When SC knee/spine disability or amputation prevents treadmill testing, the examiner estimates cardiac `METs` from clinical interview of daily activities (`1 MET = sitting quietly; 3 METs = light housework/slow flat walking; 5 METs = climbing 1 flight of stairs/light yardwork; 7 METs = jogging/carrying heavy loads`). Watch for DBQ examiners erroneously writing `"No METs limit"` when the veteran avoids exertion *because* of angina/dyspnea, or conversely confusing orthopedic pain with cardiac dyspnea—demand a pharmacologic nuclear stress test + cardiology interview isolating **cardiac-symptom-limited `METs`**!

## 4. Hypertensive Vascular Disease (`DC 7101`) vs. Hypertensive Heart Disease (`DC 7007`)

### A. `DC 7101` Schedule & Note (1)–(3) Traps (`38 C.F.R. § 4.104`)

| Rating | Verbatim `DC 7101` Threshold ([Snapshot](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.100-4.104-cardiovascular-evolution.md)) | Controlling Precedential & Evidentiary Rules |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`60%`** | Diastolic pressure **predominantly `130` or more** | *"Predominantly"* looks to the preponderance/majority pattern of readings across the rating period—raters cannot cherry-pick a single normal clinic BP to defeat predominantly elevated outpatient/VAMC readings. |
| **`40%`** | Diastolic pressure **predominantly `120` or more** | Systolic pressure alone maxes out at **`20%`** under `DC 7101` (`>= 200`); `40%` and `60%` require diastolic elevation (`>= 120` / `>= 130`). |
| **`20%`** | Diastolic pressure **predominantly `110` or more**, **OR** systolic pressure **predominantly `200` or more** | Isolated systolic hypertension (`>= 200` mmHg) reaches **`20%`** even when diastolic pressure is `< 100`. |
| **`10%`** | Diastolic pressure **predominantly `100` or more**, **OR** systolic pressure **predominantly `160` or more**, **OR** minimum evaluation for an individual with a **history of diastolic pressure predominantly `100` or more who requires continuous medication for control** | • **Note (1) Initial-Diagnosis-Only Rule:** Hypertension or isolated systolic hypertension must be confirmed by readings taken **`2 or more times` on at least `3 different days`** *to establish initial diagnosis for SC*. Once SC is granted, VA **cannot** demand 3-day multi-reading confirmation on increased-rating exams!<br/>• ***McCarroll v. McDonald*, `28 Vet. App. 267 (2016) (en banc)`:** Because `DC 7101`'s `10%` tier **explicitly addresses continuous medication**, *Jones v. Shinseki* (`26 Vet. App. 56`) and *Ingram v. Collins* (`38 Vet. App. 130`) do **not** apply to award `20%+` based on speculative unmedicated blood pressure. Conversely, if pre-medication STRs or historical medical records show diastolic readings predominantly `>= 100` and the veteran now takes lisinopril/amlodipine, **`10%` is guaranteed** even if current medicated BP is `118/74`! *(Rescinded Feb. 2026 `§ 4.10` IFR `91 FR 7118` — rescinded `91 FR 9712` — did not alter `DC 7101`).* |
| **`0%`** | Diagnosed SC hypertension (`>= 140/90` on 3 days, or medicated without historical diastolic `>= 100` or current `>= 160/100`) | Still unlocks **massive secondary SC & survivor DIC value** (see Pivot below)! |

### B. The `DC 7101 -> DC 7007` Upgrade Pivot & Secondary End-Organ Cascade
* **Mandatory Upgrade Pivot (`DC 7101` Note (2) & (3)):** Because most medicated veterans with `DC 7101` Hypertension are capped at `0%` or `10%` under *McCarroll*, **every `DC 7101` audit MUST check VAMC imaging and labs for end-organ damage**:
  1. **`DC 7007` Hypertensive Heart Disease (`30%` / `60%` / `100%`):** The instant an echocardiogram or cardiac MRI confirms **concentric left ventricular hypertrophy (`LVH`) or left atrial/ventricular dilatation** (a textbook consequence of chronic hypertension!), or cardiac symptoms occur at `<= 7.0 METs`, the veteran qualifies for **`30%` (or `60%/100%`) under `DC 7007` Hypertensive Heart Disease**! Under **`DC 7101` Note (3)** (`71 FR 52460`), *"Evaluate hypertension separately from hypertensive heart disease and other types of heart disease"* (*DeLisio v. Shinseki*, `25 Vet. App. 45 (2011)` scope of claim; Note (2) merges secondary isolated systolic hypertension into aortic insufficiency or hyperthyroidism).
  2. **Secondary Renal Dysfunction (`DC 7502` / `7530` Hypertensive Nephropathy):** Evaluate secondary hypertensive kidney disease **separately** (`30%`–`100%` under post-Nov. 14, 2021 `eGFR` / albuminuria criteria)!
  3. **Secondary Cerebrovascular Stroke (`DC 8007–8009`):** **`100%`** for 6 months + neurological/speech/extremity residuals (`10%` minimum; [System 13 Diff](13-neurological-tbi-nerves-4.120-4.124a.md)).
  4. **Secondary Hypertensive Retinopathy (`DC 6006`) & Erectile Dysfunction (`DC 7522` + `SMC(k)`):** Antihypertensive beta-blockers/thiazides and vascular endothelial damage routinely cause compensable `SMC(k)` loss of use of a creative organ!
* **PACT Act `§ 404` vs. *Nehmer* Trap:** Remember that **`DC 7005` Ischemic Heart Disease** (`75 FR 53202`) **IS** a `38 C.F.R. § 3.816` *Nehmer* condition (paying decades of retroactive back pay + `10 U.S.C. § 1413a` CRSC uncapped under *Soto v. United States*, `605 U.S. 360 (2025)`!), whereas **PACT Act `§ 404` Hypertension (`DC 7101`)** is **excluded** from `§ 3.816` *Nehmer* retroactivity (`>= Aug. 10, 2022` liberalizing effective date under `§ 3.114(a)`, except for PACT `§ 1305` survivor DIC re-evaluations) ([Playbook 06](../../playbooks/06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md), [Playbook 08](../../playbooks/08-survivor-dic-direct-and-1318-hypothetical.md)).

## 5. Diseases of the Arteries & Veins: Aneurysms (`DC 7110`), `PAD` (`DC 7114`), Varicose Veins (`DC 7120`) & Cold Injury (`DC 7122`)

### A. Peripheral Arterial Disease (`DC 7114`, `DC 7115` Buerger's, `DC 7117` Raynaud's Phenomenon vs. `DC 7124` Raynaud's Disease)

On **November 14, 2021 (`86 FR 54089`)**, VA replaced Era-2 subjective claudication yardage (`<25 yds` / `<100 yds`) in **`DC 7114` Peripheral Arterial Disease (`PAD`)** (and cross-referenced `DC 7115` Thromboangiitis obliterans) with **four objective vascular hemodynamic metrics**—with **`Note (2)` formally corrected on November 9, 2021 at `86 FR 62095, 62096` (eff. Nov. 14, 2021)** so examiners do *not* falsely assign `0%` when calcified arteries skew `ABI`:

| Rating *(Per Extremity!)* | Ankle/Brachial Index (`ABI`) | OR Ankle Pressure (`AP`) | OR Toe Pressure (`TP`) | OR Transcutaneous `O2` (`TcPO2`) | Era-2 (`1998–2021`) Comparison *(Pre-Nov. 14, 2021)* |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **`100%`** | **`<= 0.39`** | **`< 50 mm Hg`** | **`< 30 mm Hg`** | **`< 30 mm Hg`** | Ischemic limb pain at rest, **OR** deep ischemic ulcers/trophic changes + **`ABI <= 0.4`**. |
| **`60%`** | **`0.40 – 0.53`** | **`50 – 65 mm Hg`** | **`30 – 39 mm Hg`** | **`30 – 39 mm Hg`** | Claudication at **`< 25 yards`** on level grade at 2 mph + persistent coldness/trophic changes **OR** **`ABI <= 0.5`**. |
| **`40%`** | **`0.54 – 0.66`** | **`66 – 83 mm Hg`** | **`40 – 49 mm Hg`** | **`40 – 49 mm Hg`** | Claudication at **`25–100 yards`** + trophic changes **OR** **`ABI <= 0.7`**. |
| **`20%`** | **`0.67 – 0.79`** | **`84 – 99 mm Hg`** | **`50 – 59 mm Hg`** | **`50 – 59 mm Hg`** | Claudication on walking **`> 100 yards`**, diminished peripheral pulses, **OR** **`ABI <= 0.9`**. |
| **`0%`** | **`>= 0.80`** *(asymptomatic; see Note (2) calcified-vessel override!)* | `>= 100 mm Hg` | `>= 60 mm Hg` | `>= 60 mm Hg` | Asymptomatic (`ABI > 0.9`). *(Note: Era-2 granted `20%` for `ABI 0.80–0.90`—check `§ 3.951(a)` grandfathering!)* |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Critical `DC 7114`, `DC 7110` & Raynaud's Audit Rules (`86 FR 54089` & `86 FR 62095`):**
> 1. **Diabetic / CKD Non-Compressible Calcified Vessel Trap (`Note (2)`, `86 FR 62095`):** In veterans with **Diabetes Mellitus (`DC 7913` — Agent Orange!)** or chronic kidney disease, medial arterial calcification (*Mönckeberg sclerosis*) makes ankle arteries stiff and non-compressible, producing **falsely normal or supra-normal `ABI` readings (`> 1.00` or `> 1.30`)** despite critical limb ischemia! Under corrected **Note (2) (`86 FR 62095, 62096`, Nov. 9, 2021)**, `ABI` is used *unless* the examiner states that `AP`, `TP` (Toe Pressure), or `TcPO2` is needed because `ABI` does not sufficiently reflect the severity of the veteran's `PAD`—and in all other cases where multiple tests exist, VA **must evaluate based on the test that provides the HIGHEST impairment value**!
> 2. **Bypass/Graft Residuals (`Note (3)`) & Separate Per-Extremity Ratings + `§ 4.26` Bilateral Factor (`Note (4)`):** Evaluate residuals of aortic and large arterial bypass surgery or arterial graft as `PAD` (`Note (3)`), evaluate **each affected lower or upper extremity separately**, and apply the **`38 C.F.R. § 4.26` `10%` bilateral factor** whenever both legs (or both arms) are compensable (`Note (4)`)! Also check **`§ 4.30` post-surgical convalescence (`100%`)** and **`SMC(k)`** if PAD culminates in toe/foot/leg amputation.
> 3. **Aortic Aneurysm (`DC 7110`):** **`100%`** if **`>= 5.0 cm` diameter**, symptomatic (precluding exertion), OR requiring surgical/endovascular (`EVAR`/`TEVAR`) correction—crucially, under `DC 7110` Note, **when surgery is required, the `100%` evaluation begins on the date a physician recommends surgical correction** (not waiting until hospital admission!), continuing through `6 months` post-discharge with a mandatory VA exam + `§ 3.105(e)` notice (*Foster*); **`0%`** if `< 5.0 cm` and asymptomatic (`60%` if precluded from exertion pre-2021).
> 4. **Raynaud's Split (`86 FR 54089`):** Strictly distinguish **`DC 7117` Raynaud's syndrome (secondary Raynaud's phenomenon)** (`100%` `>=2` digital ulcers + auto-amputation of `>=1` digit + attacks; `60%` `>=2` digital ulcers + attacks; `40%` characteristic attacks at least daily; `20%` `4–6`x/wk; `10%` `1–3`x/wk) from **`DC 7124` Raynaud's disease** (primary idiopathic vasospasm, which has **ONLY `10%` and `0%` tiers**: **`10%`** for characteristic attacks associated with trophic change(s) such as tight, shiny skin; **`0%`** without trophic changes!).
> 5. **Varicose Veins (`DC 7120`) & Post-Phlebitic Syndrome (`DC 7121`):** `DC 7120` cross-references **`DC 7121`**, rated **`100%/60%/40%/20%/10%/0%` per extremity** (+ `§ 4.26` bilateral factor) based on persistent edema, stasis pigmentation/eczema, intermittent vs. persistent ulceration, and relief by elevation/compression hosiery.

### B. Cold Injury Residuals (`DC 7122`, `63 FR 37778`, eff. August 13, 1998; Expanded `86 FR 54089`, eff. November 14, 2021)

Enacted for **Korean War (Chosin Reservoir), WWII (Battle of the Bulge / Ardennes / Aleutians), Mountain/Arctic Warfare, and peacetime winter field-training** frostbite, trench foot, and immersion foot survivors (`38 U.S.C. § 1112(b)(3)(L)` FPOW cold-injury presumption; [Playbook 07](../../playbooks/07-statutory-presumptions-chronic-tropical-pow-als.md)):

| Rating *(Per Affected Body Part!)* | `DC 7122` Cold Injury Criteria (`63 FR 37778`, eff. Aug. 13, 1998; expanded Nov. 14, 2021 at `86 FR 54089, 54097`) |
| :---: | :--- |
| **`30%`** | Arthralgia or other pain, numbness, or cold sensitivity **plus TWO OR MORE (`>= 2`)** of the following: `(1)` tissue loss, `(2)` nail abnormalities, `(3)` color changes, `(4)` locally impaired sensation, `(5)` hyperhidrosis, `(6)` X-ray abnormalities (osteoporosis, subarticular punched-out lesions, or osteoarthritis), *plus `[added Nov. 14, 2021]`*: `(7)` anhydrosis, `(8)` atrophy or fibrosis of affected musculature, `(9)` flexion or extension deformity of distal joints, `(10)` volar fat pad loss in fingers or toes, `(11)` avascular necrosis of bone, `(12)` chronic ulceration, or `(13)` carpal or tarsal tunnel syndrome. |
| **`20%`** | Arthralgia or other pain, numbness, or cold sensitivity **plus ONE (`1`)** of the objective signs above (`1–6` pre-Nov. 14, 2021; `1–13` on/after Nov. 14, 2021). |
| **`10%`** | **Arthralgia or other pain, numbness, OR cold sensitivity alone** *(subjective cold sensitivity or numbness of each affected foot/hand/ear/nose warrants `10%` PER PART without needing objective tissue/nail changes!)* |

> [!TIP]
> **Multi-Rating Stacking Under `DC 7122` Notes (1) & (2) (`cue-and-open-claim` since Aug. 13, 1998):**
> * **Note (2) — Separate Per-Part Evaluation + Bilateral Factor:** Evaluate **each affected part separately** (e.g., Right Foot `30%` + Left Foot `30%` + Right Hand `20%` + Left Hand `20%` + Bilateral Ears `10%` + Nose `10%`) and apply the **`38 C.F.R. § 4.26` `10%` bilateral factor** to paired extremities!
> * **Note (1) — Mandatory Separate Residual Stacking:** Separately diagnose and evaluate **`(a)` Peripheral Neuropathy (`DC 8520/8720` / `8515` — omitting sensory/pain symptoms already used to elevate `DC 7122` above `10%`)**, **`(b)` Amputations of digits/feet (`DC 5160–5173` + `SMC(k)`/`(l)`)**, **`(c)` Squamous cell carcinoma in cold-injury scars (`DC 7818`)**, and **`(d)` Secondary fungal infections (`DC 7813` onychomycosis/tinea pedis) or vascular thrombosis** whenever non-duplicative under `§ 4.14`!

## 6. Regulatory Watchlist & Quarantine (`91 FR 7118` — Rescinded `91 FR 9712`)

> [!CAUTION]
> **QUARANTINE NOTICE — DO NOT CITE AS ACTIVE LAW (`AGENTS.md` Law 6):**
> * **Rescinded `38 C.F.R. § 4.10` Medication Interim Final Rule (`91 FR 7118`, Feb. 17, 2026 — Rescinded `91 FR 9712`, Feb. 27, 2026):** VA's February 2026 attempt to mandate rating disabilities in their medicated state across all VASRD schedules was **rescinded ab initio** after 10 days.
>   * **Cardiovascular Impact:** As verified above, **`DC 7101` Hypertension** (`10%`), **`DC 7010` SVT** (`10%`), and the **General Rating Formula for Diseases of the Heart** (`10%`) *already* explicitly mention continuous medication in their text (*McCarroll v. McDonald*, `28 Vet. App. 267 (2016) (en banc)`). By contrast, cardiovascular codes that are **silent on medication**—such as **`DC 7114` PAD** (vasodilators/cilostazol), **`DC 7117` Raynaud's syndrome** (calcium-channel blockers reducing vasospastic attack frequency!), and **`DC 7122` Cold Injury** (gabapentin/analgesics masking cold pain)—are governed strictly by ***Ingram v. Collins*, `38 Vet. App. 130 (2025)`**, forbidding raters from discounting unmedicated attack frequency or baseline severity!
> * **20-Year Protection Reminder (`38 U.S.C. § 110`; *Salgado v. Brown*, `4 Vet. App. 316 (1993)`):** Any Era-1 or Era-2 cardiovascular rating continuously in force for `>= 20 years` (even during periods when military retired pay was elected prior to `CRDP`/`CRSC`!) is **permanently immune from reduction below its lowest 20-year level** absent fraud.
