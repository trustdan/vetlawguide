## 2. Service-Connection Gate (`38 C.F.R. § 3.385`), Delayed-Onset Nexus (*Hensley*) & Soundness (*McKinney*)

### A. Current Disability Threshold (`38 C.F.R. § 3.385`) vs. Delayed-Onset Acoustic Trauma (*Hensley v. Brown*)
Under `38 C.F.R. § 3.385`, impaired hearing constitutes a compensable/service-connectable **"disability"** for VA purposes when an audiometric examination by a state-licensed audiologist (`§ 4.85(a)`) shows **any one** of three disjunctive criteria in the affected ear:
1. Auditory threshold of **`40 decibels (dB)` or greater** at **any** of the five frequencies **`500, 1000, 2000, 3000, or 4000 Hz`** *(note: `500 Hz` counts for `§ 3.385` service connection even though `500 Hz` is excluded from the `1000–4000 Hz` `§ 4.85(d)` rating average; by contrast, isolated `6000 Hz` or `8000 Hz` notches alone do NOT satisfy `§ 3.385`!)*; **OR**
2. Auditory thresholds of **`26 dB` or greater** at **at least three (`>= 3`)** of the five frequencies **`500, 1000, 2000, 3000, or 4000 Hz`**; **OR**
3. Speech recognition score using the **Maryland CNC Test** of **less than `94 percent` (`< 94%`)**.

* **The *Hensley* Separation-Audiogram Rule (*Hensley v. Brown*, `5 Vet. App. 155, 159–60 (1993)`):** VA examiners and raters frequently commit legal error by denying service connection solely because the veteran's **separation audiogram** was "normal" (did not yet cross `§ 3.385` thresholds). Under *Hensley*, **`§ 3.385` establishes ONLY whether the veteran has a *current* hearing disability at the time of the claim—it is NOT a requirement that `§ 3.385`-level hearing loss have manifested during active service!** A veteran with a sub-`§ 3.385` separation audiogram (or no separation audiogram at all, e.g., in-service high-frequency `4000–6000 Hz` threshold shift, combat artillery/flight-line exposure conceded under `38 U.S.C. § 1154(a)–(b)`) establishes direct service connection (`§ 3.303(a)`) via a medical nexus opinion linking current sensorineural loss to in-service acoustic trauma, reinforced by credible lay continuity (*Dalton*; *Jandreau*).

### B. Presumption of Soundness on Entrance Audiograms (*McKinney v. McDonald*, `28 Vet. App. 15 (2016) (en banc)`)
* Prior to 2016, VA routinely denied `DC 6100` claims on the theory that an entrance audiogram recorded elevated decibel numbers (e.g., `30 dB` at two frequencies or `45 dB` at `6000 Hz`) that did **not** meet `§ 3.385`, yet treated those sub-`§ 3.385` numbers as "noted preexisting hearing loss" shifting the `§ 1153` aggravation burden onto the veteran.
* In ***McKinney v. McDonald*, `28 Vet. App. 15, 27–31 (2016) (en banc)`**, the full CAVC abolished that trap: because pre-entrance audiometric readings that do **not** cross `38 C.F.R. § 3.385` are **legally not a "hearing disability"** under VA's own regulation, an entrance exam recording sub-`§ 3.385` thresholds **does NOT "note" a preexisting hearing defect**! Consequently, the veteran enjoys the **full `38 U.S.C. § 1111` presumption of soundness**, requiring **VA** to prove by **clear and unmistakable evidence** *both* that a hearing disability preexisted service *and* that service noise did not aggravate it (*Wagner v. Principi*, `370 F.3d 1089 (Fed. Cir. 2004)`; [Soundness Treatise](../../docs/service-connection-direct-soundness-aggravation.md)).

## 3. Hearing Loss Rating Evolution (`§§ 4.85–4.86`, `DC 6100`): Puretone/CNC Tables & The `§ 4.86` CUE Goldmine

See [`38-cfr-4.85-4.87-auditory-tinnitus-evolution.md`](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.85-4.87-auditory-tinnitus-evolution.md) for the complete verbatim `Table VI`, `Table VIa`, and `Table VII` matrices.

### A. Mechanical Operation of `38 C.F.R. § 4.85` (`>= Dec. 18, 1987`, `52 FR 44117`)
1. **Un-Aided State-Licensed Audiologist Exam (`§ 4.85(a)`):** Testing **must** be conducted **without hearing aids** by a state-licensed audiologist using **controlled Maryland CNC (`Consonant-Nucleus-Consonant`) 50-word lists** and **puretone audiometry**.
2. **Puretone Threshold Average (`PTA`, `§ 4.85(d)`):** Sum the puretone decibel thresholds at **`1000, 2000, 3000, and 4000 Hz` ONLY** and **divide by `4`** (rounding `.5` or `.75` to the nearest whole integer; `500 Hz` is **never** included in the `§ 4.85(d)` `PTA`! Under VA audiology exam protocol / `M21-1` `V.iii.2.B` and `Table VIA`'s `105+` ceiling, if threshold at any of the four frequencies exceeds `100 dB` or registers "no response," it is recorded as **`105 dB`**).
3. **`Table VI` vs. `Table VIa` (`§ 4.85(b)–(c)`; *Lendenmann v. Principi*, `3 Vet. App. 345, 349 (1992)`):**
   * **Default (`Table VI`):** Intersect the ear's **Maryland CNC Speech Discrimination `%`** (`92–100%`, `84–90%`, `76–82%`, `68–74%`, `60–66%`, `52–58%`, `44–50%`, `36–42%`, `0–34%`) with its **`PTA`** (`0–41 dB` up to `98+ dB`) to obtain a mechanical Roman Numeral **`I` (normal) through `XI` (profound)** (*Lendenmann*; `Table VIA` splits `98–104 dB` [`X`] and `105+ dB` [`XI`]).
   * **Mandatory `Table VIa` Puretone-Only Override (`§ 4.85(c)`):** When the audiologist certifies that the Maryland CNC speech test is **not appropriate**—due to **language difficulties (non-native English speaker), inconsistent/unreliable speech discrimination scores, cognitive/aphasic barriers, or conductive/mixed pathology rendering CNC invalid**—OR when triggered by **`§ 4.86`**, the rater **must** use **`Table VIa`** (puretone average alone)!
4. **Combining Both Ears on `Table VII` (`§ 4.85(e)`):** Intersect the **Better Ear** Roman Numeral (horizontal row `I–XI`) with the **Poorer Ear** Roman Numeral (vertical column `I–XI`) to assign a single **`0%` to `100%`** bilateral disability evaluation under `DC 6100` *(no `§ 4.26` bilateral factor applies because `Table VII` already combines both ears into a single `DC 6100` code)*.

### B. The `38 C.F.R. § 4.86(a)–(b)` Exceptional Patterns CUE Goldmine (`>= June 10, 1999`, `64 FR 25202`)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why `§ 4.86` Is One of the Highest-Yield Code-Sheet CUEs in Veterans Law (`cue-and-open-claim`):** Veterans with severe noise-induced "ski-slope" high-frequency loss often score `92%–96%` on quiet-booth Maryland CNC testing (trapping them at Roman Numeral `I` or `II` = **`0%`** on `Table VI`!). Effective **June 10, 1999 (`64 FR 25202`)**, VA enacted `§ 4.86(a)–(b)` specifically to rescue these veterans—and evaluated **each ear separately**:
> 1. **Pattern A (`§ 4.86(a)` — Flat Severe `>= 55 dB` Pattern):** When the puretone threshold at **EACH of the four frequencies (`1000, 2000, 3000, AND 4000 Hz`) is `55 dB or more`**, determine the ear's Roman numeral from **BOTH `Table VI` and `Table VIa`** and assign **whichever Roman numeral is HIGHER**!
> 2. **Pattern B (`§ 4.86(b)` — Steep Ski-Slope `1000 -> 2000 Hz` Drop + Mandatory `+1` Numeral Bump!):** When the puretone threshold is **`30 dB or less` at `1000 Hz` AND `70 dB or more` at `2000 Hz`**, determine the ear's Roman numeral from **BOTH `Table VI` and `Table VIa`** (taking whichever is **higher**), **AND THEN ELEVATE THAT NUMERAL TO THE NEXT HIGHER ROMAN NUMERAL** (e.g., `IV -> V`, capped at `XI`)!
>
> *Audit Check:* Pull every post-June 10, 1999 `0%` or `10%` `DC 6100` audiogram. Whenever raters plugged `1000–4000 Hz` numbers into `Table VI` while forgetting `§ 4.86(a)` or `(b)`'s `Table VIa` + `+1` Roman numeral elevation, file an immediate **RO CUE motion (`38 C.F.R. § 3.105(a)`)**!

### C. Extraschedular Limits (*Martinak*, *Doucette*, *King*) vs. Paired-Organ Hearing (`§ 1160(a)(3)`) & `SMC(k)`/`SMC(o)`
* **Mechanical Tables & Narrow `§ 3.321(b)(1)` Extraschedular Path:** Under ***Martinak v. Nicholson*, `21 Vet. App. 447, 453–55 (2007)`**, audiologists must still record the functional effects of hearing loss in daily life/work (`§ 4.10`). However, in ***Doucette v. Shulkin*, `28 Vet. App. 366, 371–72 (2017)`** and ***King v. Shulkin*, `29 Vet. App. 174 (2017)`**, the CAVC held that `§ 4.85` **already contemplates difficulty hearing and understanding speech** (including in background noise or group settings); therefore, `§ 3.321(b)(1)` extraschedular referral is triggered **only** by exceptional functional effects *outside* difficulty hearing—such as **otalgia (ear pain), aural fullness/pressure, dizziness/vertigo, or severe occupational/social sequelae not captured by secondary codes** ([Playbook 19](../../playbooks/19-extraschedular-3.321b1-analogous-4.20-and-temp-total.md)).
* **Two-Era Unilateral SC Ear (`VAOPGCPREC 32-97` / *Boyer*) vs. `38 U.S.C. § 1160(a)(3)` (`§ 3.383(a)(3)`) Paired-Organ Unlock:**
  1. **When Unilateral SC Ear Is `0%` (`Roman Numeral I–IX` when paired with `I` on `Table VII`):** Under **`VAOPGCPREC 32-97`** and ***Boyer v. West*, `210 F.3d 1351 (Fed. Cir. 2000)`**, an opposite non-service-connected (`NSC`) ear must be fixed at Roman Numeral `I` (normal) *(except prior to Dec. 6, 2002 where *both* the SC ear and NSC ear had **total deafness**)*.
  2. **Post-December 6, 2002 `§ 1160(a)(3)` Paired-Organ Breakthrough (`Pub. L. 107-330, § 103`, `116 Stat. 2821`; `69 FR 48149`, Aug. 9, 2004):** Effective **December 6, 2002**, Congress overrode the "total deafness" barrier! Whenever a veteran has **`(a)` SC hearing impairment in one ear compensable at `10% or more`** (`Roman Numeral X or XI` when paired with `I` on `Table VII`) **AND `(b)` NSC hearing impairment in the other ear that meets `38 C.F.R. § 3.385`** (not due to willful misconduct), VA **must evaluate BOTH ears together on `Table VII` "as if" service-connected**!
* **Statutory `SMC(k)` Total Bilateral Deafness (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(k)`, `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(a)(5)`) & `SMC(o)` Deaf-Blind Escalators:** Whenever bilateral SC (or `§ 1160(a)(3)` paired-organ) hearing loss reaches **`100%` under `Table VII`** (`Roman Numeral XI` bilaterally — deemed by `§ 3.350(a)(5)` under `Pub. L. 88-20` to establish *"deafness of both ears, having absence of air and bone conduction"*), VA **must** award mandatory **`SMC(k)`** (`+ $139.87/mo` in 2026) on top of `100%`! Furthermore, bilateral deafness rated `>= 60%` (one or both ears SC) combined with `<= 20/200` bilateral SC blindness jumps straight to **`SMC(o)` (`$6,877.12/mo`)** under `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(e)(1)(iii)` ([`System 02 Eye Diff`](02-eye-and-vision-4.75-4.79.md); [Master SMC Treatise](../../docs/smc-complete-treatise-and-step-math.md)).

## 4. Tinnitus (`DC 6260`): Three-Era Evolution, The *Smith* CUE Kill-Switch & *Fountain*/*Charles* Merits Wins

### A. Three-Era Regulatory Text Comparison (`1946` vs. `1999` vs. `2003`)

| Era | Effective Window & FR Citation | Verbatim `DC 6260` Criteria & Notes ([Snapshot](../../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.85-4.87-auditory-tinnitus-evolution.md)) | Key Doctrinal Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Era 1** | `Apr. 1, 1946 – June 9, 1999` *(1945 VASRD)* | **`6260` Tinnitus:** *"Persistent as a symptom of head injury, concussion or acoustic trauma .......... **`10%`**"* | Required persistent ringing **plus** head-injury, concussion, or acoustic-trauma etiology (*upheld against statutory challenge in **Wanner v. Principi**, `370 F.3d 1124 (Fed. Cir. 2004)`*). |
| **Era 2** | `June 10, 1999 – June 12, 2003` *(`64 FR 25202`, pub. May 11, 1999)* | **`6260` Tinnitus, recurrent .......... `10%`**<br/>• *Note (1):* Separate evaluation combinable with `6100, 6200, 6204`, etc., unless tinnitus already supports that code (`6205`).<br/>• *Note (2) [now Note (3)]:* Objective tinnitus rated under underlying cause. | **Major Liberalization:** Eliminated the head-injury/concussion/acoustic-trauma prerequisite—unlocking `10%` for recurrent tinnitus from **ototoxic drugs (aspirin/NSAIDs, aminoglycosides, cisplatin, quinine/antimalarials), barotrauma, hypertension, TMJ, or idiopathic in-service onset**! |
| **Era 3** | `June 13, 2003 – Present` *(`68 FR 25822`, pub. May 14, 2003, `RIN 2900-AK86`)* | Added **Note (2):** *"Assign **only a single evaluation** for recurrent tinnitus, whether the sound is perceived in one ear, both ears, or in the head."* *(Redesignated objective-tinnitus note as Note (3)).* | Codified `VAOPGCPREC 2-2003` (`May 22, 2003`) confirming a strict **`10%` single-rating ceiling** regardless of bilateral perception. |

### B. CRITICAL CUE TRAP WARNING: *Smith v. Nicholson*, `451 F.3d 1344 (Fed. Cir. 2006)`!

> [!CAUTION]
> **DO NOT FILE BILATERAL TINNITUS (`10% + 10%`) CUE MOTIONS (`AGENTS.md` Law 5 & Law 6):**
> * Outdated secondary blogs occasionally cite ***Smith v. Nicholson*, `19 Vet. App. 63 (2005)`**, in which the CAVC *briefly* held that pre-June 13, 2003 `DC 6260` and `38 C.F.R. § 4.25(b)` required dual `10% + 10%` ratings for bilateral tinnitus.
> * **THE FEDERAL CIRCUIT REVERSED THAT HOLDING IN *Smith v. Nicholson*, `451 F.3d 1344, 1350–51 (Fed. Cir. 2006)`**, deferring to VA's binding interpretation in [`VAOPGCPREC 2-2003`](../../docs/vaopgcprec-index.md) (`69 FR 25178`) that **even under pre-June 13, 2003 regulations, `DC 6260` authorized ONLY ONE `10%` evaluation** whether perceived in one ear, both ears, or the head!
> * **Practitioner Pivot:** Never waste a one-shot Board CUE (`§ 7111`) or RO CUE motion demanding bilateral `10% + 10%` tinnitus. Instead, capture `30%–70%+` in secondary compensation by filing **`38 C.F.R. § 3.310(a)` secondary claims** for **somatic symptom disorder / depression / anxiety (`§ 4.130`, `30%–70%`)** or **migraine headaches (`DC 8100`, `30%–50%`)** proximately caused or aggravated by relentless `DC 6260` tinnitus ([Playbook 14](../../playbooks/14-secondary-service-connection-aggravation-obesity.md))!

### C. High-Value Merits & Effective-Date Weapons on `DC 6260` (*Charles*, *Jandreau*, *Fountain*)
1. **Lay Competence to Self-Diagnose & Report Tinnitus (*Charles v. Principi*, `16 Vet. App. 370, 374 (2002)`; *Jandreau v. Nicholson*, `492 F.3d 1372, 1377 (Fed. Cir. 2007)`):** Subjective tinnitus has no objective audiometric test; ringing in the ears is a classic observable symptom that **a lay veteran is 100% competent to perceive and report** both in service and continuously since discharge, triggering VA's `§ 5103A(d)` duty to provide an exam (*McLendon v. Nicholson*, `20 Vet. App. 79 (2006)`) even when STRs are completely silent!
2. **Tinnitus as a `38 C.F.R. § 3.309(a)` Chronic Nervous System Disease (*Fountain v. McDonald*, `27 Vet. App. 258, 265–72 (2015)`):** Even after *Walker v. Shinseki*, `708 F.3d 1331 (Fed. Cir. 2013)` restricted `38 C.F.R. § 3.303(b)` chronicity/continuity of symptomatology to `§ 3.309(a)` chronic diseases, the CAVC held in ***Fountain*** that **tinnitus associated with acoustic trauma (or nervous-system pathology) IS an `"organic disease of the nervous system"` under `38 C.F.R. § 3.309(a)`**—preserving both the **1-year post-discharge `10%` presumptive window** AND **`§ 3.303(b)` continuity of symptomatology** without requiring a separate medical nexus opinion if credible lay testimony establishes continuous ringing since service!

## 6. Regulatory Watchlist & Quarantine Notice (`RIN 2900-AQ72` — Proposed / Not Finalized)

> [!CAUTION]
> **QUARANTINE NOTICE — DO NOT CITE AS ACTIVE LAW (`AGENTS.md` Law 6):**
> * **`RIN 2900-AQ72` (`87 FR 8474`, published February 15, 2022 — Proposed Rule, Not Finalized):** VA published a controversial **proposed** rule (*Schedule for Rating Disabilities—Ear, Nose, Throat, and Audiology Disabilities...*) proposing to **eliminate `DC 6260` as a standalone `10%` compensable code** and instead evaluate tinnitus solely as a symptom within an underlying diagnosed disease (`0%` if underlying hearing loss is non-compensable!). Following massive Congressional and VSO opposition (and subsequent Unified Agenda deferrals), **proposed rule `RIN 2900-AQ72` (`87 FR 8474`) remains unfinalized and not enacted.** Standalone `DC 6260` (`10%`) remains **100% active binding law** for all claims and appeals. *(Note: Even if VA ever enacted a future final rule, any running `10%` `DC 6260` award in effect prior to that effective date would be statutorily grandfathered against reduction under `38 U.S.C. § 1155` and `38 C.F.R. § 3.951(a)`!).*
> * **Rescinded `38 C.F.R. § 4.10` Medication IFR (`91 FR 7118`, Feb. 17, 2026 — Rescinded `91 FR 9712`, Feb. 27, 2026):** VA's reactive medication Interim Final Rule was **rescinded ab initio** after 10 days; under ***Ingram v. Collins*, `38 Vet. App. 130 (2025)`**, because `DC 6204` (vestibular vertigo) and `DC 6205` (Meniere's) are silent on medication, examiners and raters **cannot** discount baseline vertigo/staggering severity for ameliorative meclizine, scopolamine, diazepam, or diuretic therapy!
