## 2. August 11, 2019 Overhaul (`84 FR 28227`): General Rating Formula & Complete Infectious Code Table (`DC 6300–6335`)

### A. The General Rating Formula for Infectious Diseases (`38 C.F.R. § 4.88b`)
Effective **August 11, 2019 (`84 FR 28227`)**, VA replaced repetitive acute/inactive phrasing across most bacterial, viral, rickettsial, and parasitic codes with a single two-step architecture:
1. **For Active Disease:** **`100%`** evaluation.
2. **After Active Disease Has Resolved:** Rate at **`0%` for the infection**, AND **rate any residual disability that is attributable to the infection separately under the appropriate body system** (`38 C.F.R. § 4.88b`).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Post-Infection Residual Unbundling Mandate (`cue-and-open-claim` >= Aug. 11, 2019):** When an acute tropical, tick-borne, foodborne, or Gulf War infection resolves to `0%` under `DC 6300–6335`, VA **cannot** stop at `0%` if chronic post-infectious sequelae persist! Every `DC 6300–6335` Note explicitly cross-references signature post-infectious organ damage (matching the presumptive residual table in **`38 C.F.R. § 3.317(d)`**), which must be separately coded and combined under `§ 4.25`.

## 3. Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (`38 C.F.R. § 4.88a` & `DC 6354`): Diagnostic Gate & Disjunctive `"Or"` Goldmine

### A. Strict Three-Prong Diagnostic Prerequisite (`38 C.F.R. § 4.88a(a)(1)–(3)`)
Before a veteran can be rated under `DC 6354` (including as a **presumptive Gulf War `MUCMI` under `38 U.S.C. § 1117` / `38 C.F.R. § 3.317(a)(2)(i)(B)(1)`**), a valid diagnosis of **Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (`CFS` / `ME/CFS`)** for VA purposes requires **ALL THREE** elements:
1. **Six-Month `< 50%` Onset Threshold (`§ 4.88a(a)(1)`):** New onset of debilitating fatigue severe enough to reduce daily activity to **less than `50 percent`** of the usual level for **at least `6 months`** *(note: once diagnosed, current rating severity can fluctuate across `10%–100%` under `DC 6354`)*; **AND**
2. **Clinical Exclusion Gate (`§ 4.88a(a)(2)`):** Exclusion—by history, physical examination, and laboratory tests—of all other clinical conditions that may produce similar symptoms *(watch for C&P examiners lazily blaming fatigue on controlled hypothyroidism, CPAP-treated OSA, or mild depression without checking whether `CFS` post-exertional malaise, tender lymph nodes, sore throat, and migratory arthralgias exist independently!)*; **AND**
3. **At Least `6` of the `10` Listed Clinical Criteria (`§ 4.88a(a)(3)(i)–(x)`):** `(i)` acute onset of the condition; `(ii)` low-grade fever; `(iii)` nonexudative pharyngitis; `(iv)` palpable or tender cervical or axillary lymph nodes; `(v)` generalized muscle aches or weakness; `(vi)` **fatigue lasting `24 hours` or longer after exercise** *(post-exertional malaise)*; `(vii)` headaches (of a type, severity, or pattern different from the pre-morbid state); `(viii)` migratory joint pains; `(ix)` neuropsychologic symptoms; `(x)` sleep disturbance.

### B. Primary-Source-Verified `DC 6354` Schedule & The Disjunctive `"Or"` Trap

| Rating | Prong 1: Nearly Constant Routine Daily Activity Restriction | **OR** Prong 2: Waxing/Waning Physician-Prescribed Incapacitation *(See `Note`!)* / Meds |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`100%`** | Debilitating fatigue, cognitive impairments (such as inability to concentrate, forgetfulness, or confusion), or a combination of other signs and symptoms which are **nearly constant and so severe as to restrict routine daily activities almost completely and which may occasionally preclude self-care**. | *(No incapacitation-weeks alternative at `100%`; evaluate `SMC(l)` Aid & Attendance or *Bradley* `SMC(s)` whenever `100%` is met!)* |
| **`60%`** | Nearly constant symptoms which **restrict routine daily activities to less than `50 percent` (`< 50%`) of the pre-illness level** *(i.e., `> 50%` loss of routine daily activity!)* | **OR** wax and wane, resulting in periods of incapacitation of **at least `6 weeks` (`>= 6 wks`) total duration per year**. |
| **`40%`** | Nearly constant symptoms which **restrict routine daily activities from `50 to 75 percent` of the pre-illness level** *(i.e., a `25%–50%` restriction of routine daily activity!)* | **OR** wax and wane, resulting in periods of incapacitation of **at least `4` but less than `6 weeks` (`4 to < 6 wks`) total duration per year**. |
| **`20%`** | Nearly constant symptoms which **restrict routine daily activities by less than `25 percent` (`< 25%` restriction) of the pre-illness level** | **OR** wax and wane, resulting in periods of incapacitation of **at least `2` but less than `4 weeks` (`2 to < 4 wks`) total duration per year**. |
| **`10%`** | *(Waxing/waning without nearly constant daily restriction)* | Wax and wane resulting in periods of incapacitation of **at least `1` but less than `2 weeks` (`1 to < 2 wks`) total duration per year**; **OR symptoms controlled by continuous medication**. |

> [!WARNING]
> **THE #1 `DC 6354` LITIGATION & CUE TRAP — Physician-Prescribed Bed Rest Is NOT Required for Prong 1 (`20%` / `40%` / `60%` / `100%`):**
> * Under `DC 6354` **`Note`**, *"incapacitation exists only when a licensed physician prescribes bed rest and treatment"* (modern medicine rarely prescribes multi-week strict bed rest for `ME/CFS`!).
> * RO raters routinely misread that `Note` to deny **`40%` or `60%`** whenever no doctor prescribed bed rest. Under ***Tatum v. Shinseki*, `23 Vet. App. 152 (2009)`**, the semicolon **`; or`** separating Prong 1 (*nearly constant restriction of routine daily activities to `< 50%` [`60%`] or `50–75%` [`40%`] of pre-illness level*) from Prong 2 (*waxing/waning incapacitation*) is **strictly disjunctive**! A veteran whose `CFS` DBQ or lay/clinical record establishes nearly constant debilitating fatigue and brain fog cutting routine daily activities below `50%` of pre-illness capacity is legally entitled to **`60%`** with **ZERO** days of prescribed bed rest!
> * **Anti-Pyramiding (`§ 4.14`) Comorbidity Check:** Can a Gulf War veteran hold separate ratings for **`DC 6354` `CFS` + `DC 5025` Fibromyalgia (`10/20/40%`) + `DC 8100` Migraines + `DC 9411/9434` PTSD/Depression + `DC 6847` Sleep Apnea**? **YES**, so long as the medical examiner delineates which distinct manifestations support each code without counting the *exact same* fatigue/cognitive/sleep symptom twice (*Esteban v. Brown*, `6 Vet. App. 259 (1994)`; *Joyner v. McDonald*, `766 F.3d 1393 (Fed. Cir. 2014)`).

## 4. Immune Disorders (`DC 6350` Lupus & `DC 6351` HIV) & FPOW Nutritional Deficiencies (`DC 6313–6315`)

### A. Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (`DC 6350`): Flare Schedule vs. Multi-Organ Residual Math
Under **`DC 6350` Note** (`38 C.F.R. § 4.88b`), VA **MUST** calculate the veteran's disability under **BOTH** methods below and award **whichever method results in the HIGHER evaluation** (never combining `DC 6350` itself with SLE organ residuals or `DC 7809` Discoid Lupus):
1. **Method A — `DC 6350` Exacerbation Tiers:**
   * **`100%`:** Acute, with frequent exacerbations, producing severe impairment of health.
   * **`60%`:** Exacerbations **lasting a week or more, `2 or 3 times` per year**.
   * **`10%`:** Exacerbations **once or twice a year** OR symptomatic during the past `2 years`. *(Notice: `DC 6350` has **NO `30%` or `40%` tier**—jumping straight from `10%` to `60%`!)*
2. **Method B — Combined Multi-Organ Residual Stack (`§ 4.25`):** Combine separate evaluations across every affected organ system: **Lupus nephritis (`DC 7502`/`7530`: `30/60/80/100%`)** + **Inflammatory lupus polyarthritis (`DC 5002`/`5003` + `§ 4.26` bilateral factor)** + **Cutaneous/discoid lupus or systemic biologics (`DC 7809`/`7806` — belimumab/anifrolumab/azathioprine/mycophenolate = `60%` under `§ 4.118`!)** + **Pericarditis/pleuritis (`DC 7002`/`6845`)** + **Neuropsychiatric lupus (`DC 9326`/`9434` or seizures `8910`)** + **Autoimmune hemolytic anemia / `ITP` (`DC 7723`/`7705`)** + **Secondary Raynaud's phenomenon (`DC 7117`)** + **Hydroxychloroquine (`Plaquenil`) bull's-eye maculopathy (`§ 4.79`)**! Whenever Method B combines to `70%+`, `80%`, `90%`, or `100%` (+ *Bradley* `SMC(s)` when one organ hits `100%` and others hit `60%`!), Method B crushes a `60%` `DC 6350` flare rating!

### B. HIV-Related Illness (`DC 6351`) & Modern Antiretroviral (`ART`) Strategy
* **Verbatim `DC 6351` Tiers (`84 FR 28227`):**
  * **`100%`:** `AIDS` with recurrent opportunistic infections (`Note (3)` — *Pneumocystis*, *Toxoplasma*, `CMV`, `MAC`, cryptococcosis, Kaposi's sarcoma, etc.) OR secondary diseases afflicting multiple body systems; HIV-related illness with debility and progressive weight loss.
  * **`60%`:** Refractory constitutional symptoms, diarrhea, and pathological weight loss; **OR minimum rating following development of any `AIDS`-related opportunistic infection or neoplasm**!
  * **`30%`:** Recurrent constitutional symptoms, intermittent diarrhea, and use of approved medication(s) (`Note (1)` includes research-protocol medications at an accredited medical institution); **OR minimum rating with `T4` (`CD4`) cell count `< 200`**.
  * **`10%`:** Following development of HIV-related constitutional symptoms; **`T4` cell count between `200 and 500`**; **use of approved `ART` medication(s)**; OR evidence of depression or memory loss with employment limitations.
  * **`0%`:** Asymptomatic following initial HIV diagnosis, without approved medication (`T4 > 500`).
* **Crucial `Note (2)` Alternative Stacking Rule:** In the modern `ART` era (`Biktarvy`, `Triumeq`, `Dovato`), most virally suppressed veterans have `CD4 > 500`, placing `DC 6351` alone at **`10%`** ("use of approved medication(s)" per `Note (1)`)—unless they **ever historically suffered an `AIDS`-defining opportunistic infection or neoplasm (`Note (3)`)**, which locks in a **permanent `60%` minimum floor**! Even more importantly, **`Note (2)`** directs raters to **separately evaluate definite secondary/opportunistic malignancies (`DC 7715` NHL `100%`), `ART`-induced tenofovir renal tubulopathy (`DC 7530`), osteoporosis/avascular necrosis (`DC 5013`/`5255`), peripheral neuropathy (`DC 8520`), chronic diarrhea, HIV-associated neurocognitive disorder (`DC 9301`), or major depressive disorder (`DC 9434` — `30%/50%/70%/100%` under `§ 4.130` instead of `10%` under `6351`!)** whenever separate ratings yield a higher combined evaluation!

### C. FPOW Nutritional Deficiencies (`DC 6313` Avitaminosis, `DC 6314` Beriberi & `DC 6315` Pellagra)
For **Former Prisoners of War (`FPOWs`)** (`38 U.S.C. § 1112(b)(2)–(3)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.309(c)` — `Avitaminosis`, `Beriberi` [including **beriberi heart disease**, which encompasses **any** POW who suffered localized edema during captivity!], `Pellagra`, and any other nutritional deficiency; [Playbook 07](../../playbooks/07-statutory-presumptions-chronic-tropical-pow-als.md)):
* **`DC 6313` Avitaminosis & `DC 6315` Pellagra (`10/20/40/60/100%`):** **`10%`** nonspecific anorexia/weight loss/weakness/irritability; **`20%`** stomatitis, achlorhydria, OR diarrhea; **`40%`** stomatitis + diarrhea + symmetrical dermatitis; **`60%`** all `40%` symptoms + mental symptoms & impaired bodily vigor; **`100%`** marked mental changes, moist dermatitis, inability to retain nourishment, exhaustion, and cachexia.
* **`DC 6314` Beriberi (Thiamine `B1` Deficiency — Active vs. Chronic Residual Goldmine!):**
  * **Active Disease:** **`100%`** congestive heart failure, anasarca, or **Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome**; **`60%`** cardiomegaly, OR peripheral neuropathy with footdrop or thigh/calf muscle atrophy; **`30%`** peripheral neuropathy with absent knee/ankle jerks and sensory loss, OR weakness/fatigue/leg stiffness.
  * **Post-Captivity Chronic Residual Stacking (`Note`):** Rate chronic **"wet beriberi" cardiomyopathy/heart disease (`DC 7000/7020` under `§ 4.104`)** AND **"dry beriberi" bilateral lower/upper-extremity peripheral neuropathy (`DC 8520` + `§ 4.26` bilateral factor, plus `SMC(k)` if complete footdrop!)** **separately** under their respective body systems!
