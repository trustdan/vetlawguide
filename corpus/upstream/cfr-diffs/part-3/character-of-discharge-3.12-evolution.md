# Part 3 Historical Diff: Character of Discharge Evolution (`38 U.S.C. § 5303` & `38 C.F.R. § 3.12` Pre- vs. Post-June 25, 2024)

This Reference module tracks the eighty-year statutory evolution of Character of Discharge (`COD`) bars (`1944–Present`), maps a clause-by-clause comparison between pre-June 25, 2024 `38 C.F.R. § 3.12(a)–(k)` and the **June 25, 2024 Final Rule (`89 FR 32361`, `RIN 2900-AQ95`, published April 26, 2024, effective June 25, 2024)** (`§ 3.12(a)–(l)`), and defines the strict bitemporal boundary between prospective `38 C.F.R. § 3.114(a)` reopenings and the **five retroactive pre-2024 pathways**.

*(For verbatim pre- and post-June 25, 2024 text of `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.12, 3.13, and 3.354`, see [`38-cfr-3.12-3.13-3.354-cod-insanity.md`](../../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.12-3.13-3.354-cod-insanity.md); for operational pleading strategy, see [Playbook 10 (COD, Insanity, `§ 3.13` & Upgrades)](../../playbooks/10-character-of-discharge-insanity-upgrades.md), [Playbook 11 (Guard/Reserve ADT/IADT & LOD)](../../playbooks/11-guard-reserve-adt-iadt-and-line-of-duty.md), [Playbook 03 (`§ 3.156(c)` Service Records)](../../playbooks/03-service-department-records-38-cfr-3-156c.md), and the [COD & Guard/Reserve Intake Audit Checklist](../../checklists/cod-and-guard-reserve-intake-audit.md)).*

## 1. Chronological Statutory & Regulatory Architecture (`1944–Present`)

Under `38 U.S.C. § 101(2)`, basic eligibility as a `"veteran"` requires discharge or release `"under conditions other than dishonorable."` In *Garvey v. Wilkie*, `972 F.3d 1333, 1337–41 (Fed. Cir. 2020)`, the Federal Circuit confirmed that `38 U.S.C. § 5303(a)` sets mandatory **statutory** floors while `38 U.S.C. §§ 101(2) and 501(a)` empower VA to promulgate supplemental **regulatory** bars in `38 C.F.R. § 3.12(d)`.

| Era & Effective Date | Enacting Authority | Core Structural Changes & Litigation Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Era 1: `June 22, 1944 – Dec. 31, 1958`**<br/>*(WWII Readjustment Origin)* | *Servicemen's Readjustment Act of 1944* ("G.I. Bill"), `Pub. L. 78-346, §§ 300–301` (`58 Stat. 284, 286`, June 22, 1944); `1946` VA regs (`11 FR 8731`) | • **`§ 300`:** Enacted the original five statutory bars—conscientious objector refusing duty/uniform/orders, sentence of a general court-martial (`GCM`), officer resignation for the good of the service, deserter, and alien requesting release during hostilities—while carving out the statutory **insanity exception**.<br/>• **`§ 301`:** Created military Discharge Review Boards (`DRBs`, ancestor of `10 U.S.C. § 1553`). Simultaneously, VA codified the administrative COD bars (`escape trial by GCM`, `mutiny/spying`, `moral turpitude`, `willful and persistent misconduct`, and `aggravated homosexual acts`). |
| **Era 2: `Jan. 1, 1959 – Oct. 7, 1977`**<br/>*(Title 38 Positive-Law Codification)* | `Pub. L. 85-857` (`72 Stat. 1105, 1230`, Sept. 2, 1958, **eff. Jan. 1, 1959**); `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.12, 3.13(a)–(b)` (`26 FR 1566`, Feb. 24, 1961; `28 FR 123`, Jan. 4, 1963) | • Codified `"under conditions other than dishonorable"` in **`38 U.S.C. § 101(2)`**, retirement-without-break protection in **`38 U.S.C. § 101(18)`** (paired with regulatory `38 C.F.R. § 3.13(a)–(b)` conditional-discharge rules), and statutory bars + insanity (`§ 3.354`) in **`former 38 U.S.C. § 3103(a)–(b)`** (see [Three-Era Statutory Crosswalk](../../docs/title-38-statutory-crosswalk.md)).<br/>• **Pre-Oct. 8, 1977 Upgrade Rule (`former § 3.12(f)`, `now § 3.12(g)`):** Any regular `10 U.S.C. § 1553` DRB upgrade to Honorable or General issued **before October 8, 1977** set aside `(c)` and `(d)` bars **except `(c)(2)` (`GCM`)** and `1977 SDRP` / Proclamation `4313` upgrades. |
| **Era 3: `Oct. 8, 1977 – May 6, 1991`**<br/>*(`Pub. L. 95-126` Overhaul & Uncharacterized Era)* | `Pub. L. 95-126` (`91 Stat. 1106`, enacted & **eff. Oct. 8, 1977**); `43 FR 15153, 15154` (Apr. 11, 1978); `49 FR 44099` (Nov. 2, 1984, uncharacterized separations `>= Oct. 1, 1982`) | Enacted four transformative changes alongside the Carter-era Vietnam `SDRP` / Proclamation `4313` clemency review:<br/>1. **Added the `6th` Statutory Bar (`former § 3103(a)`, `now § 5303(a)` / `§ 3.12(c)(6)`):** Barred benefits for an OTH discharge issued for **continuous AWOL of `>= 180 days`**, *unless* VA finds **"compelling circumstances"** warranting the prolonged absence.<br/>2. **Post-Oct. 8, 1977 `DRB` Gate (`former § 3103(e)(1)–(2)`, `now § 5303(e)(1)–(2)` / `§ 3.12(h)–(i)`):** Restricted `10 U.S.C. § 1553` DRB upgrades issued **on or after October 8, 1977** (`§ 5303(e)(1)` / `§ 3.12(h)`) and `SDRP` programs (`§ 5303(e)(2)` / `§ 3.12(i)`) so DRB upgrades set aside **`(d)` regulatory bars ONLY (never `(c)` statutory bars!)**, and only if issued on an individual case basis under published, historically consistent uniform standards (`10 U.S.C. § 1552` BCMR upgrades under `§ 3.12(f)` remained conclusive over *both* `(c)` and `(d)`!).<br/>3. **Multi-Enlistment Unconditional-Discharge Rescue (`Pub. L. 95-126, § 3`, `91 Stat. 1108` -> `38 U.S.C. § 101(18)(B)` & `38 C.F.R. § 3.13(c)`):** Statutory safeguard deeming a service member **unconditionally discharged or released** upon completing an obligated enlistment where an intervening reenlistment prevented issuance of a `DD-214`.<br/>4. **Chapter 17 Healthcare (`Pub. L. 95-126, § 2`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.360`) & Oct. 1, 1982 `ELS` (`§ 3.12(l)(1)`):** Preserved Chapter 17 care for non-`§ 5303(a)`/`BCD` line-of-duty disabilities and made **Entry Level Separations (`ELS`)** automatically "under conditions other than dishonorable." |
| **Era 4: `May 7 / Aug. 6, 1991 – June 24, 2024`**<br/>*(Modern `§ 5303` & Judicial Review Era)* | `Pub. L. 102-40, § 402(b)(1)` (`105 Stat. 187, 238`, **May 7, 1991**); `Pub. L. 102-83` (`105 Stat. 378`, **Aug. 6, 1991**) | • **`Pub. L. 102-40` (May 7, 1991)** renumbered `former 38 U.S.C. § 3103` to **`38 U.S.C. § 5303`** (`Pub. L. 102-83` conformed Secretary references Aug. 6, 1991).<br/>• CAVC and Federal Circuit decisions defined `§ 3.12(d)(4)` minor-offense protections (*Cropper*, *Stringham*), broad non-criminal `§ 3.354` insanity (*Zang*, *Gardner*, `VAOPGCPREC 20-97`), GCM clemency limits (`VAOPGCPREC 10-96`), and `§ 3.12(d)` validity (*Garvey*). |
| **Era 5: `June 25, 2024 – Present`**<br/>*(`89 FR 32361` Regulatory Overhaul)* | **Final Rule, `89 FR 32361–32373`** (`RIN 2900-AQ95`, proposed July 10, 2020 at `85 FR 41471`; RFI Sept. 9, 2021 at `86 FR 50513`; **published Apr. 26, 2024, effective June 25, 2024**) | • Retitled `§ 3.12` (*Benefit eligibility based on character of discharge*); **eliminated former `§ 3.12(d)(5)`** (homosexual acts); bifurcated `§ 3.12(d)` into `(d)(1)` (non-exempt) and `(d)(2)` (exempt); replaced subjective minor-offense guesswork in `(d)(2)(ii)` with **objective `2-year` / `5-year` *Manual for Courts-Martial* (`MCM`) spacing and punishment rules**; created unified **`§ 3.12(e)` Compelling Circumstances Exception** covering `(c)(6)` AWOL, `(d)(2)(i)` moral turpitude, and `(d)(2)(ii)` willful & persistent misconduct; and redesignated former `(e)–(k)` as **`(f)–(l)`**. *(No subsequent 2025 or 2026 NPRM or IFR has altered `§ 3.12`).* |

## 3. Bitemporal & Effective-Date Trap Matrix: `89 FR 32361` Floor vs. Five Retroactive Pre-2024 Pathways

> [!CAUTION]
> **THE JUNE 25, 2024 LIBERALIZING-LAW CEILING (`38 U.S.C. § 5110(g)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.114(a)`):** Under the applicability clause of `89 FR 32361` (`Apr. 26, 2024`) and `38 C.F.R. § 3.114(a)`, when a previously final COD denial is overcome **solely** because of the liberalized June 25, 2024 criteria (e.g., applying `§ 3.12(e)` compelling circumstances to a `(d)(2)` bar or applying the new `2-year`/`5-year` MCM spacing rules), **the effective date CANNOT precede June 25, 2024** (`prospective-liberalizing-3.114`)! Even if the veteran files within 1 year of June 25, 2024 (`§ 3.114(a)(1)`) or files years later (`§ 3.114(a)(3)` `1-year` lookback), `§ 5110(g)` forbids any award date prior to the effective date of the liberalizing regulation (**June 25, 2024**), and *George v. McDonough*, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)` / `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)` bars citing `89 FR 32361` as CUE against a pre-June 25, 2024 decision!

Whenever a veteran has an earlier denied claim or prolonged disability stretching back years or decades before June 25, 2024, **never settle for a June 25, 2024 effective date** without screening all **five retroactive pre-2024 pathways** below ([Procedural Vehicles for Piercing Finality](../../docs/procedural-vehicles-piercing-finality.md)):
