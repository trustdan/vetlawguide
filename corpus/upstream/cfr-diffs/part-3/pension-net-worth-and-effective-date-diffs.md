# Part 1 & Part 3 Historical Diff: Effective Dates (`§§ 3.155, 3.400(o)(2), 3.2500`), Severance Recoupment (`§ 3.700`), Wartime Pension Net Worth (`§§ 3.274–3.279`) & Waiver Deadlines (`§ 1.963(b)`) (`1962–2026`)

This Reference module maps the side-by-side statutory and `38 C.F.R. Parts 1 & 3` regulatory transitions (`1962–2026`) across five high-dollar financial domains: `(1)` increased-rating lookbacks and Intent-to-File (`ITF`) standardization (`§§ 3.155, 3.400(o)(2)`), `(2)` Legacy finality vs. AMA continuous pursuit (`§ 3.2500`), `(3)` military separation and disability severance recoupment (`§ 3.700(a)(2), (a)(3) & (a)(5)`), `(4)` the **October 18, 2018 Wartime Pension net-worth and `36-month` lookback overhaul (`83 FR 47246`, `§§ 3.274–3.279`)**, and `(5)` the **December 29, 2022 -> January 26, 2026 (`90 FR 54234`) overpayment waiver deadline alignment (`§ 1.963(b)`, `former § 1.963(b)(2)`)**.

## 2. `38 C.F.R. § 20.1103` (Legacy) vs. `38 C.F.R. § 3.2500` (AMA Continuous Pursuit, `84 FR 170`)

| Feature / Procedural Stage | Legacy System (`< Feb. 19, 2019`; `38 C.F.R. § 20.1103`) | AMA System (`>= Feb. 19, 2019`; `38 U.S.C. § 5110(a)(2)(A)–(E)` & `38 C.F.R. § 3.2500`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enacting Authority** | `Pub. L. 87-666` (`1962`); `38 U.S.C. §§ 7105, 7291`; `38 C.F.R. § 20.1103` | `Pub. L. 115-55` (`131 Stat. 1105`); **`84 FR 138, 170–171`** (pub. Jan. 18, 2019, **eff. Feb. 19, 2019**); modified by ***MVA v. Sec'y*, `7 F.4th 1110 (Fed. Cir. 2021)`** |
| **Post-AOJ Continuity** | Single linear track: `1-year` NOD -> VA issues `SOC` -> `60-day` / remainder-of-1-year `VA Form 9` Substantive Appeal. | Choice of three `1-year` lanes (`§ 5110(a)(2)(A)–(C)`; `§ 3.2500(c)`): `0996` HLR, `0995` Supplemental Claim (`new & relevant`), or `10182` Board NOD. |
| **Post-BVA Denial Continuity** | Appeal to CAVC within `120 days` (`§ 7266`). Filing a `§ 5108` reopening at the RO **forfeited** the original claim's effective date (effective date reset to reopening unless `§ 3.156(c)` or CUE applied). | Appeal to CAVC within `120 days` **OR** file a **`§ 5108` Supplemental Claim (`20-0995`) within `1 year` of BVA decision (`§ 5110(a)(2)(D)`; `§ 3.2500(c)(3), (h)(1)`)**—**preserving the initial effective date**! *(HLR barred after BVA).* |
| **Post-CAVC Affirmance** | **FINALITY BREAK (`38 U.S.C. § 7291`):** CAVC affirmance makes the BVA denial final; any later reopening gets **only the date of reopening** (absent `§ 3.156(c)` or CUE). | **CONTINUOUS PURSUIT PRESERVED (`§ 5110(a)(2)(E)`):** Filing a `§ 5108` Supplemental Claim (`new & relevant`) within **`1 year` after CAVC decision** preserves the initial effective date even after a CAVC affirmance! |
| **Concurrent Federal Circuit Appeal (`§ 3.2500(b)`)** | Barred concurrent RO adjudication of the exact issue pending before Fed. Cir. (*Cerullo*). | `84 FR 170` originally barred concurrent Supplemental Claims during Federal Circuit appeals (`§ 3.2500(b)`), creating a fatal gap because `§ 5110(a)(2)` lacks a post-Fed.-Cir. clause. ***MVA v. Sec'y*, `7 F.4th at 1134` INVALIDATED `§ 3.2500(b)`**—counsel **can and must** file a protective `20-0995` / `21-0966` within `1 year` of the CAVC decision while appealing to the Federal Circuit! |

## 4. `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.274–3.279`: Needs-Based Wartime Pension Net Worth, `36-Month` Lookback & `UME` Overhaul (`83 FR 47246`, Eff. Oct. 18, 2018)

On **September 18, 2018**, VA published **`83 FR 47246–47275` (`RIN 2900-AO73`, effective October 18, 2018)**, replacing nearly sixty years of subjective "corpus of estate" pension adjudication (`38 U.S.C. §§ 1522, 1543`) with objective Medicaid-aligned rules ([`38-cfr-3.274-3.279-3.353-1.965-pension-competency-waivers.md`](../../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.274-3.279-3.353-1.965-pension-competency-waivers.md); [Playbook 21](../../playbooks/21-overpayment-waivers-incompetency-and-wartime-pension.md)):

| Pension Element & `38 C.F.R.` Section | Era 1: **Pre-October 18, 2018** (`Former §§ 3.274–3.276`) | Era 2: **On or After October 18, 2018** (`83 FR 47246`, `RIN 2900-AO73`, `§§ 3.274–3.279`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Net Worth Standard (`§ 3.274`)** | **Subjective Multi-Factor Corpus Test:** No fixed dollar cap (informal RO rules of thumb `$50k–$80k` routinely reversed on appeal); weighed liquid assets against income, family expenses, medical costs, and **table life expectancy** (`whether corpus would be consumed during claimant's lifetime`). | **Bright-Line Medicaid `CSRA`-Indexed Cap (`§ 3.274(a)–(b)`):** Indexed annually on Dec. 1 by Social Security COLA (`$123,600` on Oct. 18, 2018 -> **`$163,699` for `Dec. 1, 2025 – Nov. 30, 2026`**).<br/>• **Formula (`§ 3.274(b)`):** **`Net Worth = Countable Assets + Annual Income for VA Purposes (IVAP)`** (after deducting annualized `§ 3.278` unreimbursed medical expenses; if `UMEs` reduce `IVAP` below `$0`, `IVAP` counts as `$0`, never negative!). |
| **Primary Residence & Lot Exclusion (`§ 3.275`)** | Excluded single-family dwelling plus `"such contiguous land as is reasonable"` based on local zoning/custom without a strict acreage cap. | **`2-Acre` Residential Lot Cap (`§ 3.275(b)(1)`):** Excludes primary residence (even if claimant moved into assisted living / nursing care!) **plus residential lot area up to `2 acres` (`87,120 sq. ft.`)**. Marketable value of land **exceeding `2 acres`** counts as an asset *unless* excess land is legally/physically **unmarketable** (zoning ban on subdivision, landlocked). Also excludes personal effects and daily family transportation vehicles (`(b)(2)`). |
| **Asset Transfers & Lookback Window (`§ 3.276`)** | **ZERO LOOKBACK / ZERO PENALTY:** Uncompensated gifts or irrevocable trust/annuity transfers made **even `1 day` before filing** removed assets from net worth so long as the claimant completely relinquished ownership and control (`VAOPGCPREC 33-97`)! | **PROSPECTIVE-ONLY `36-Month` Lookback (`§ 3.276(a)–(b)`):**<br/>1. **100% Exemption for Pre-Oct. 18, 2018 Transfers (`§ 3.276(b)`):** VA **strictly disregards ALL asset transfers made BEFORE October 18, 2018**!<br/>2. **36-Month Window & `"Covered Asset"` Threshold (`§ 3.276(a)(2)–(3)`):** Reviews uncompensated transfers (gifts, irrevocable trusts, immediate annuities lacking residual liquidation value) within `36 months` preceding claim receipt—**PENALIZING ONLY the `"covered asset"` portion** (the dollar amount by which pre-transfer net worth would have exceeded the bright-line cap! If a claimant had `$100,000` total net worth and gifted `$50,000`, `"covered asset" = $0` and **penalty = `0 months`**!). |
| **Transfer Penalty Divisor & `5-Year` Cap (`§ 3.276(e)`)** | *Not applicable (no transfer penalty existed prior to Oct. 18, 2018).* | • **Single Uniform Monthly Divisor (`§ 3.276(e)(1)`):** Regardless of whether claimant is a single veteran, married veteran, or surviving spouse, divide `"covered asset"` amount by **`MAPR for a Veteran with 1 Dependent in need of Aid & Attendance ÷ 12`** (**`$34,488 ÷ 12 = $2,874/month`** for `Dec. 1, 2025 – Nov. 30, 2026`; round down fractional cents/months per `(e)(1)`, starts first day of month after last transfer per `(e)(2)`!).<br/>• **Maximum Penalty Cap (`§ 3.276(e)`):** Capped at **`5 years` (`60 months`)**.<br/>• **Cure Window (`§ 3.276(e)(5)`):** Full/partial return of transferred assets within **`60 days`** of penalty notice (with `90-day` proof window) recalculates or extinguishes the penalty! |
| **Deductible Medical, Assisted-Living & Care Expenses (`§ 3.278`)** | Governed by scattered `former § 3.272(g)` and `M21-1` instructions leading to inconsistent RO disallowances of assisted-living room-and-board and family-caregiver payments. | **Codified `UME` Catalog (`§ 3.278(c)–(d)`):** Deducts unreimbursed medical/custodial expenses exceeding `5%` of baseline `MAPR`:<br/>• **Nursing Home / Memory Care / Assisted Living (`(d)(3)`):** `100%` of facility charges (including **meals and lodging**) are deductible as `UMEs` whenever the facility provides or contracts for health care or **custodial care** (assistance with **`>= 2` Activities of Daily Living [`ADLs` under `§ 3.278(b)(2)`: bathing or showering, dressing, eating, toileting, transferring, and ambulating within the home or living area]** OR supervision due to cognitive/physical impairment), OR when a `physician/PA/CNP/CNS` states in writing that the individual must reside in the protected facility to obtain third-party/family care (`(d)(3)(iv)(B)`)!<br/>• **In-Home Attendant (`(d)(2)`):** Deductible (even for an unlicensed family caregiver!) when claimant needs `A&A`/`Housebound` or has a `physician/PA/CNP/CNS` written statement of need for health care or custodial care. *(Cross-ref `§ 3.279` consolidating statutory exclusions).* |

| Historical Error / Audit Scenario | Bitemporal Classification | Why It Succeeds (or Fails) & Optimal Remedy |

| **Recouping Combat-Zone `DSP` on a Chapter 61 Separation `>= Jan. 28, 2008` (`10 U.S.C. § 1212(d)(2)`)** | **`cue-and-open-claim`** | Unambiguous statutory prohibition since **Jan. 28, 2008** (`Pub. L. 110-181, § 1646`). Audit `DD-214` separation date (`>= Jan. 28, 2008`) + PEB combat code; demand full refund of illegally withheld compensation via CUE / administrative audit ([Playbook 20](../../playbooks/20-backpay-offsets-crdp-crsc-severance-recoupment.md)). *(Kill-Switch: Fails if separated `<= Jan. 27, 2008`).* |
