# Part 3 Historical Diff: Gulf War (`§ 3.317`), Particulate Matter & PACT Act (`§§ 3.320–3.320b`) & VA Malpractice (`§§ 3.358, 3.361–3.363`) Evolution (`1934–2026`)

This Reference module tracks the primary-source statutory and `38 C.F.R. Part 3` evolution across three major non-herbicide compensation regimes—**Persian Gulf War illnesses (`§ 3.317`)**, **fine particulate matter / burn pits (`§§ 3.320, 3.320a, 3.320b`)**, and **VA medical/rehabilitation injuries (`§§ 3.358, 3.361–3.363`)**—isolating exact **Federal Register publication vs. effective dates**, **statute-vs.-CFR lag periods**, and ***George v. McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) / `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)` bitemporal CUE boundaries**.
