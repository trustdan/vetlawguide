# Part 3 Historical Diff: Special Monthly Compensation (`SMC(k)` Through `SMC(t)`) Statutory & `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.350, 3.352` Evolution (`1933–2026`)

This Reference module maps the 93-year statutory and regulatory evolution of **Special Monthly Compensation (`SMC`)** from `Veterans' Regulation No. 1(a)` (`1933`) through `former 38 U.S.C. § 314` (`1959–1991`) and modern **`38 U.S.C. § 1114(k)–(t)`** (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.350, 3.352, 3.552`), isolating exact **Federal Register publication vs. effective dates** and ***George v. McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) / `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)` bitemporal CUE gates**.

*(For verbatim regulatory text of `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.350` and `3.352`, see [`38-cfr-3.350-3.352-smc-evolution.md`](../../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.350-3.352-smc-evolution.md); for doctrinal rate-step architecture, see [Master SMC Legal Treatise & Step Math](../../docs/smc-complete-treatise-and-step-math.md); for operational pleading, see [Playbook 12 (`SMC(l)`, `SMC(r)(1)–(2)` & `SMC(t)`)](../../playbooks/12-aid-and-attendance-smc-l-r1-r2-and-smc-t.md), [Playbook 13 (Ancillary Grants)](../../playbooks/13-ancillary-grants-sah-auto-clothing-dea-spina-bifida.md), [Playbook 04 (Unadjudicated TDIU & Mandatory SMC)](../../playbooks/04-unadjudicated-tdiu-informal-claims-smc.md), [`Surviving Spouse & Helpless Child Evolution Diff`](survivor-marriage-dependency-evolution.md), [`SMC & Ancillary Audit Worksheet`](../../checklists/smc-and-ancillary-audit-worksheet.md), [VA Forms & Code-Sheet Lexicon](../../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md), and [Authority Verification Log](../../docs/authority-verification-log.md)).*

## 2. Side-by-Side Structural Threshold Diffs (`Pre- vs. Post-Judicial / Statutory Shifts`)

### A. `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(f)(3)–(4)` Intermediate & Full-Step Bumps (`Pre-Barry` vs. `Post-Barry`, May 16, 2024)

| Dimension | Pre-May 16, 2024 VA Practice (`M21-1` Single-Bump Cap) | Post-May 16, 2024 Controlling Law (*Barry v. McDonough*, `101 F.4th 1348`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`§ 3.350(f)(3)` (`½`-Step per `50%+` Independent SC Disability or Combination)** | Capped veteran at **one single `½`-step** regardless of whether they had `2`, `3`, or `4` separate `50%+` SC disabilities/combinations. | **Unlimited cumulative `½`-steps** (one `½`-step for **each** separate, independent `50%–90%` permanent SC disability **and** for any `§ 4.25` combination of leftover `< 50%` permanent SC disabilities combining to `>= 50%`), capped at **`SMC(o)`**. |
| **`§ 3.350(f)(4)` (`1` Full Step per `100%` Independent Schedular SC Disability)** | Capped veteran at **one single full step** AND prohibited combining an `(f)(3)` `½`-step with an `(f)(4)` full step. | **Cumulative full steps + `(f)(3)`/`(f)(4)` stacking** for each separate `100%` schedular (*not `§ 4.16` TDIU*) SC disability (e.g., base `SMC(l)` A&A + independent `100%` PTSD `[+1 step -> M]` + independent `50%` Sleep Apnea `[+½ step -> M½]` + independent `50%` Radiculopathy `[+½ step -> N]`, capped at **`SMC(o)`**—which in turn unlocks **`SMC(r)(1)`** under `§ 3.350(h)` if A&A is present!). |
| **Unchanged Anti-Pyramiding Floor (*Breniser* & *Amberman*)** | Prohibited counting the base `SMC(l)–(n)` disability twice. | **Strictly preserved:** Under *Breniser v. Shinseki*, `25 Vet. App. 64 (2011)` and *Amberman v. Shinseki*, `570 F.3d 1377 (Fed. Cir. 2009)`, a disability used to establish base `SMC(l)–(n)` **cannot also** power an `(f)(3)` or `(f)(4)` bump! |

### B. `SMC(r)(2)` vs. `SMC(t)` Care Thresholds After *Laska v. McDonough* (`37 Vet. App. 460 (2024)`)

| Requirement | `SMC(r)(2)` (`38 U.S.C. § 1114(r)(2)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.352(b)(1)`) | `SMC(t)` Pre-*Laska* (`83 FR 20735`, `June 7, 2018 – Sept. 5, 2024`) | `SMC(t)` Post-*Laska* (`>= Sept. 6, 2024`, Controlling Statutory Meaning of `§ 1114(t)`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monthly Dollar Rate** | Top `SMC` tier (`~$11,000+/mo`) | Identical to **`SMC(r)(2)`** | Identical to **`SMC(r)(2)`** |
| **Underlying `SMC(o)` / `(n½ + k)` Prerequisite?** | **YES** (must first reach `SMC(o)` or maximum `(p)`/`(n½ + k)` rate + A&A under `§ 1114(r)`) | **NO** (requires SC Traumatic Brain Injury residuals only) | **NO** (requires SC Traumatic Brain Injury residuals only) |
| **Level of Daily Care Required** | **Higher level of care (`§ 3.352(b)(1)`):** Daily personal health-care services by (or under regular supervision of) a **licensed health-care professional** | Unlawfully demanded **`§ 3.352(b)(2)` "higher level of care"** (daily skilled/professional services) | **Regular Aid & Attendance ONLY (`§ 3.352(a)`):** ADLs, cognitive/supervisory cueing, or protection from daily hazards provided by a **family caregiver or lay attendant**—**NO skilled/professional care required**! |
| **"But-For" Institutionalization Prong** | Absent skilled care, would require **hospital, nursing home, or other residential institutional care** | Same | Absent **regular A&A**, would require **hospitalization, nursing home care, or other residential institutional care** (including assisted-living / TBI group-home supervision). |
