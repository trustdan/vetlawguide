# Part 3 Historical Diff: Surviving Spouse Marriage Validity, Multi-Era Remarriage Cutoffs (`§ 3.55`) & Helpless Child Status (`§ 3.356`)

This Reference module compares the statutory and `38 C.F.R. Part 3` status thresholds governing **surviving spouse recognition** (`38 U.S.C. §§ 101(3), 103(a)–(d), 1304, 1318(c)`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.1(j), 3.50–3.55`) and **helpless child dependency** (`38 U.S.C. §§ 101(4)(A)(ii), 103(e), 1313–1314, 3562`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.55(b), 3.57, 3.356, 3.707, 21.3023`).

*(For verbatim `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.22` and `3.1000–3.1010` snapshots, see [`38-cfr-3.22-dic-hypothetical-evolution.md`](../../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.22-dic-hypothetical-evolution.md) and [`38-cfr-3.1000-3.1010-accrued-substitution.md`](../../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.1000-3.1010-accrued-substitution.md); for operational litigation strategy, see [Playbook 08 (DIC & `§ 1318`)](../../playbooks/08-survivor-dic-direct-and-1318-hypothetical.md), [Playbook 09 (Accrued, Substitution & *Nehmer*)](../../playbooks/09-accrued-benefits-substitution-nehmer-estates.md), [Playbook 06 (*Nehmer* `§ 3.816`)](../../playbooks/06-nehmer-herbicide-presumptions-crsc.md), and the [48-Hour Survivor Post-Mortem Triage Checklist](../../checklists/survivor-post-mortem-triage-gate.md)).*

## 1. Surviving Spouse Validity, Cohabitation & Duration Thresholds (`38 C.F.R. §§ 3.1(j), 3.50–3.54`)

### A. Choice-of-Law Validity & Same-Sex Marriage Evolution (`38 U.S.C. § 103(c)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.1(j)`)

| Rule / Era | Governing Authority | Substantive Threshold & Temporal Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Place-of-Residence Choice of Law** | `38 U.S.C. § 103(c)` [pre-1991 unchanged section number; see [Statutory Crosswalk](../../docs/title-38-statutory-crosswalk.md)]; `38 C.F.R. § 3.1(j)` | A marriage is valid if valid under the law of the place where the parties **resided at the time of marriage** OR where the parties **resided when the right to benefits accrued** (includes ceremonial, common-law, tribal, and proxy marriages if recognized by that jurisdiction). |
| **Post-*Windsor* / Pre-*Obergefell* Interim (`June 26, 2013 – June 25, 2015`)** | *United States v. Windsor*, `570 U.S. 744 (2013)`; `VAOPGCPREC 3-2014`; `VAOPGCPREC 4-2014` | Executive Branch ceased enforcing DOMA § 3 (`Sept. 4, 2013`), but `38 U.S.C. § 103(c)` state-residency language temporarily blocked VA recognition where couples resided in states banning same-sex marriage both at marriage and accrual. |
| **Post-*Obergefell* Nationwide Recognition (`June 26, 2015 – Present`)** | *Obergefell v. Hodges*, `576 U.S. 644 (June 26, 2015)` | Eliminated all state-residency marriage bans nationwide under `§ 103(c)` and `§ 3.1(j)`. *(Bitemporal CUE Note: Under George v. McDonough, `596 U.S. 740 (2022)` and `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`, Obergefell cannot be used to prove CUE in a final pre-June 2015 denial that applied extant § 103(c) law—use liberalizing/supplemental pathways or Instruction `01-22` below (`open-claim-only-george-restricted`)).* |
| **Same-Sex Duration-Gap Relief (`Oct. 11, 2022 – Present`)** | Secretary Instruction `01-22` (`87 FR 68904`, pub. Nov. 17, 2022, eff. Oct. 11, 2022) | Where unconstitutional state/federal bans prevented a same-sex couple from marrying early enough to satisfy the **1-year** DIC/pension duration rule (`§§ 1304, 1318(c), 1541(f)`) or **8-year** DIC kicker (`§ 1311(a)(2)`), VA counts a documented pre-*Obergefell* **"marriage-type" relationship** toward marriage duration! |

### B. Deemed Valid Marriage vs. Continuous Cohabitation Rescue (`38 U.S.C. §§ 101(3), 103(a)`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.52, 3.53`)

| Doctrine | Statute & CFR | Four Mandatory Elements / Evidentiary Standard | Controlling Precedent & Fatal Traps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Deemed Valid Marriage** | `38 U.S.C. § 103(a)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.52(a)–(d)` | Attempted marriage invalid due to a **legal impediment** is deemed valid if:<br/>1. Married `>= 1 year` before death, OR **any period** if a child was born of/before the purported marriage (`§ 3.54(d)`),<br/>2. Claimant entered marriage **without knowledge of the impediment** (`§ 3.52(b)`),<br/>3. Continuous cohabitation to death (`§ 3.52(c)` / `§ 3.53`), AND<br/>4. **No rival legal surviving spouse** files and establishes entitlement to gratuitous death benefits (`§ 3.52(d)`). | • ***Dedicatoria v. Brown*, `8 Vet. App. 441 (1995)`:** Ignorance of the impediment is tested **solely at the moment the purported marriage was contracted** (discovering the veteran's prior undissolved marriage *later* does NOT defeat `§ 3.52`!).<br/>• ***Colon v. Brown*, `9 Vet. App. 104 (1996)`:** "Legal impediment" includes a jurisdiction's **non-recognition of common-law marriage**, provided the claimant genuinely lacked knowledge that a ceremony/license was legally required.<br/>• ***Lamour v. Peake*, `544 F.3d 1317 (Fed. Cir. 2008)`:** VA must adjudicate `§ 103(a)`/`§ 3.52` sympathetically alongside applicable `VAOPGCPREC` opinions. |
| **Continuous Cohabitation & Separation Without Fault** | `38 U.S.C. § 101(3)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.53(a)–(b)` | Required for *all* surviving spouse claims (`§ 3.50(b)(1)`). Living apart at the veteran's death **does NOT break continuity** if:<br/>• Separation was due to **misconduct of, or procured by, the veteran without fault on the surviving spouse's part** (e.g., veteran's PTSD volatility, domestic abuse, substance abuse, desertion, infidelity), OR<br/>• Separation was by **mutual consent** for health, medical institutionalization, nursing care, work, or convenience with **no intent by the surviving spouse to desert**. | • ***Gregory v. Brown*, `5 Vet. App. 108 (1993)`:** Invalidated VA's former continuing-fault rule; **absence of fault on the surviving spouse's part is judged strictly at the time the separation occurred** (post-separation conduct while estranged does not retroactively create fault at separation!).<br/>• **`§ 3.53(b)` Presumption:** The surviving spouse's statement as to the reason for separation **shall be accepted** in the absence of contradictory information in the file; state desertion laws do not control. |

### C. Critical Statutory Diff: Marriage Duration Rules (`§ 1304` Direct DIC vs. `§ 1318(c)` Total-Disability DIC)

> [!WARNING]
> **DO NOT CONFUSE `38 U.S.C. § 1304` WITH `38 U.S.C. § 1318(c)`:** When a marriage lasted **less than 1 year** before the veteran's death and no child was born to the couple, the **15-year post-discharge rule** rescues `§ 1310` service-connected-death DIC (`§ 1304` / `§ 3.54(c)(1)`) but **DOES NOT EXIST** under `§ 1318(c)` (`§ 3.22(d)`) total-disability non-SC-death DIC!

| Benefit Program | Governing Statute & CFR | Qualifying Marriage Date / Duration Alternatives | Definition of "Child Born" (`38 C.F.R. § 3.54(d)`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Service-Connected Death DIC (`§ 1310`)** | `38 U.S.C. § 1304` [pre-1991 `§ 404`]; `38 C.F.R. § 3.54(c)` | Any **ONE** of three paths:<br/>1. Married **before or within `15 years`** after termination of the period of service in which the injury/disease causing death was incurred or aggravated (`§ 3.54(c)(1)`), OR<br/>2. Married for **`1 year` or more** (`§ 3.54(c)(2)`), OR<br/>3. Married for **any period of time** if a **child was born** of the marriage or born to them before the marriage (`§ 3.54(c)(3)`). | Biological child born on or after the marriage date, or born to the veteran and spouse before the marriage (includes a fetus advanced to the point of gestation required to constitute a birth under the law of the jurisdiction where the fetus was delivered; excludes stepchildren or adopted children for `§ 3.54(d)` waiver purposes). |
| **`10-Year` / `5-Year` / `1-Year POW` Total-Disability DIC (`§ 1318`)** | `38 U.S.C. § 1318(c)` [pre-1991 `§ 418(c)`]; `38 C.F.R. § 3.22(d)` | Only **TWO** paths (**NO 15-year post-service path!**):<br/>1. Married to the veteran for **`1 year` or more** immediately preceding the veteran's death, OR<br/>2. Married for **any period of time** if a **child was born** of the marriage or born to them before the marriage. | Same `§ 3.54(d)` definition (also review Secretary Instruction `01-22`, `87 FR 68904` if same-sex marriage bans prevented 1-year duration). |

## 2. Multi-Era Remarriage Termination, Restoration & Age-Cutoff Diff (`38 U.S.C. § 103(d)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.55(a)`)

| Era & Effective Window | Controlling Public Law & Statute | Effect of Remarriage (or "Holding Out") on DIC & Ancillaries | Restoration / Age-Retention Rules & Critical Temporal Traps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Era 1: `Pre-Jan. 1, 1971`**<br/>*(Pre-`Pub. L. 91-376`)* | `Pub. L. 85-857` (`72 Stat. 1105`, eff. Jan. 1, 1959); `former 38 U.S.C. §§ 101(3), 103(d)` | Remarriage—or living with another person and openly holding oneself out as their spouse ("inferred remarriage")—**permanently terminated** widow(er) status. | **Permanent bar** unless the purported remarriage was **void** or **annulled** (`§ 3.55(a)(1)`). Death or divorce of the second spouse did *not* restore DIC or pension. |
| **Era 2: `Jan. 1, 1971 – Oct. 31, 1990`** | `Pub. L. 91-376, § 4` (`84 Stat. 787`, enacted Aug. 12, 1970, **eff. Jan. 1, 1971**); `38 U.S.C. § 103(d)` | Remarriage or holding out terminated DIC/pension while ongoing, **subject to full restoration** upon termination. | **Full Restoration Era:** Termination of remarriage by **death or divorce** (free of fraud/collusion) OR cessation of open cohabitation/holding-out **restored both DIC and Survivors Pension** (`§ 3.55(a)(2)`). |
| **Era 3: `Nov. 1, 1990 – Sept. 30, 1998`**<br/>*("OBRA 1990 Dark Era")* | `OBRA 1990`, `Pub. L. 101-508, § 8004` (`104 Stat. 1388-343`, Nov. 5, 1990); narrowed by `Pub. L. 102-86, § 502` (Aug. 14, 1991) & `Pub. L. 102-568, § 103` (Oct. 29, 1992) | Repealed `§ 103(d)` restoration for claims filed after **Oct. 31, 1990**. | **Grandfather Rescue Exceptions (`§ 3.55(a)(2)`):** Restoration *remained available* during `1990–1998` if:<br/>1. Claimant was a **former surviving spouse whose remarriage had already terminated before Nov. 1, 1990**, OR<br/>2. Legal divorce/annulment proceedings were **commenced before Nov. 1, 1990**! |
| **Era 4: `Oct. 1, 1998 – Dec. 31, 2003`** | `TEA-21`, `Pub. L. 105-178, § 8207` (`112 Stat. 495`, June 9, 1998, **eff. Oct. 1, 1998**); `Pub. L. 106-117, § 502` (**eff. Dec. 1, 1999**); `Pub. L. 107-330, § 101` (**eff. Feb. 4, 2003**) | Remarriage barred DIC while ongoing (except CHAMPVA at **age `55+`** eff. Feb. 4, 2003 under `Pub. L. 107-330`), **but restoration upon termination returned for DIC**. | • **Oct. 1, 1998 (`§ 3.55(a)(3)`):** Termination of remarriage by death, divorce, or annulment (or cessation of cohabitation) **restored Chapter 13 DIC** prospectively from Oct. 1, 1998.<br/>• **Dec. 1, 1999 (`§ 3.55(a)(4)`):** Restored `CHAMPVA (§ 1781)`, `DEA (ch. 35)`, and `VA Home Loan (ch. 37)` upon termination.<br/>• **TRAP:** **Survivors Pension (`ch. 15`) WAS NEVER RESTORED** post-OBRA 1990 unless an Era 3 pre-Nov. 1, 1990 grandfather exception or void/annulled rule applies! |
| **Era 5: `Jan. 1, 2004 – Jan. 4, 2021`**<br/>*(`Age 57` DIC Retention Era)* | `Veterans Benefits Act of 2003`, `Pub. L. 108-183, § 101` (`117 Stat. 2651`, enacted Dec. 16, 2003, **eff. Jan. 1, 2004**); `38 U.S.C. § 103(d)(2)(B)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.55(a)(10)` | Remarriage **on or after attaining age `57`** **NO LONGER BARS** `Chapter 13 DIC`, `CHAMPVA (§ 1781)`, `DEA (ch. 35)`, or `VA Home Loan (ch. 37)`! | **Pre-Dec. 16, 2003 Transition Cutoff Trap (`Pub. L. 108-183, § 101(e)` / `§ 3.55(a)(10)(ii)`):**<br/>• Spouses who remarried at age `57+` *before* **Dec. 16, 2003** had to submit a written application received by VA **before December 16, 2004** (*Frederick v. Shinseki*, `684 F.3d 1263 (Fed. Cir. 2012)`, *rev'g* `24 Vet. App. 335 (2011)` — strictly enforcing `§ 101(e)`'s 1-year filing bar against pre-Dec. 16, 2003 remarriages!).<br/>• By contrast, spouses who remarried at age `57+` **on or after Dec. 16, 2003 / Jan. 1, 2004** face **NO 1-year filing bar** (`38 U.S.C. § 103(d)(2)(B)`; `§ 3.55(a)(10)(i)`). |
| **Era 6: `Jan. 5, 2021 – Present`**<br/>*(`Age 55` DIC Retention Era)* | `Johnny Isakson and David P. Roe, M.D. Act of 2020`, `Pub. L. 116-315, § 2009` (`134 Stat. 4932, 4978`, enacted & **eff. Jan. 5, 2021**); `38 U.S.C. § 103(d)(2)(B)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.55(a)(9)(iii)` (`87 FR 68360, 68363`, pub. & eff. **Nov. 15, 2022**) | Remarriage **on or after attaining age `55`** (occurring **on or after January 5, 2021**) **NO LONGER BARS `Chapter 13 DIC`** (or `CHAMPVA § 1781`)! | **THREE CRITICAL TRAPS (`Pub. L. 116-315, § 2009` & `87 FR 68360`):**<br/>1. **No Pre-2021 Grandfathering (`§ 3.55(a)(9)(iii)`):** VA (`87 FR 68360, 68363` & `M21-1 VII.i.2`) applies the age-`55` DIC rule strictly to remarriages occurring **on or after January 5, 2021**. A spouse who remarried at age `55` or `56` in `2018` remains barred from DIC while that marriage continues (whereas a spouse remarrying at age `55` on `Jan. 6, 2021` retains DIC!). *(Appellate Preservation Note: `Pub. L. 116-315 § 2009` amended the second sentence of `§ 103(d)(2)(B)` directly without an explicit pre-enactment cutoff clause unlike `Pub. L. 108-183 § 101(e)`—preserve statutory-text challenges on direct appeal against `87 FR 68360` if encountered).*<br/>2. **22-Month Regulatory Lag Audit (`Jan. 5, 2021 – Nov. 14, 2022`):** Because VA did not update `38 C.F.R. § 3.55(a)(9)` until **November 15, 2022** (`87 FR 68360`), raters and VLJs during `2021–2022` (or citing stale pre-Nov. 2022 CFR editions) sometimes denied post-Jan. 5, 2021 age-`55+` remarried spouses by quoting unamended `§ 3.55(a)(10)` age-`57` text—a **textbook CUE / statutory error** under controlling `38 U.S.C. § 103(d)(2)(B)` ([Authority Verification Log](../../docs/authority-verification-log.md))!<br/>3. **`DEA` & `Home Loan` Still Require Age `57` (`§ 3.55(a)(10)`):** `Pub. L. 116-315 § 2009` inserted `"chapter 13 or"` into the second sentence of `§ 103(d)(2)(B)` alongside `§ 1781`, leaving `Chapter 35 DEA` and `Chapter 37 Home Loan` governed by the **age `57`** rule (`§ 103(d)(2)(B)` sentence 1 & `§ 3.55(a)(10)`)! |

## 3. Helpless Child Dependency & Lifetime DIC (`38 U.S.C. §§ 101(4)(A)(ii), 103(e), 1313–1314, 3562`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.55(b), 3.57, 3.356, 21.3023`)

A veteran's biological, legally adopted, or qualifying stepchild (`38 C.F.R. § 3.57`) who became **permanently incapable of self-support prior to attaining age `18`** qualifies for **lifetime** dependency additions (`§ 1115`), supplemental DIC (`§ 1314(c)`), or standalone surviving-child DIC (`§ 1313` / `§ 1318`).

| Legal Dimension | Governing Authority | Controlling Standard & Operational Audit Traps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pre-Age-`18` Permanent Incapacity Standard** | `38 U.S.C. § 101(4)(A)(ii)`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.356(a)–(b)`; *Dobson v. Brown*, `4 Vet. App. 443 (1993)` | • **`§ 3.356(a)` Origin Cutoff:** The physical or mental defect causing permanent incapacity for self-support **must have existed before the child's `18th` birthday** (even if the formal diagnostic label—e.g., schizophrenia, autism spectrum disorder, severe bipolar I, intellectual disability, refractory epilepsy—crystallized shortly after age 18, provided pre-18 medical, school IEP/504, or lay records prove incapacity existed prior to 18).<br/>• **`§ 3.356(b)` Functional Benchmark:** Evaluated solely on whether the child can **earn their own reasonable support through their own competitive efforts**. Intermittent, casual, sheltered-workshop, heavily accommodated family employment, or post-18 college/special-education enrollment **does NOT automatically defeat** `§ 3.356` permanent incapacity! *(Under Dobson, Board determinations under `§ 3.356` are factual findings reviewed for clear error—build a thorough pre-18 functional record).* |
| **Marriage Termination Trap for Helpless Children** | `38 U.S.C. § 103(e)`; `Pub. L. 101-508, § 8004`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.55(b)(1)–(2), 3.400(u)` | **SEVERE ASYMMETRY WITH SURVIVING SPOUSES:** While Congress restored remarried surviving spouses' DIC in `1998` (`TEA-21`), **Congress NEVER repealed the `OBRA 1990` marriage-restoration ban for children (`38 U.S.C. § 103(e)`)!**<br/>• Any valid marriage of a helpless child terminated **on or after November 1, 1990** (unless divorce proceedings began before Nov. 1, 1990, `§ 3.55(b)(2)`) **permanently extinguishes** VA child status—even if the child later divorces or is widowed!<br/>• **Sole Rescue (`§ 3.55(b)(1)`):** Child status survives/resumes **only** if the child's marriage was **void *ab initio*** (e.g., lack of mental capacity to contract marriage under state law!) or **annulled** by a court of competent jurisdiction free of fraud/collusion (`§ 3.400(u)`). |
| **`Chapter 35` DEA vs. Helpless Child Concurrency & Sequencing Trap** | `38 U.S.C. § 3562`; `38 C.F.R. §§ 3.707(a), 21.3023(a)–(c)` | • **Recognized Helpless Child Concurrency (`38 C.F.R. § 21.3023(b)`):** Unlike regular `18–23` school-attending children, an adjudicated **helpless child (`§ 3.356`) MAY receive `Chapter 35` DEA educational assistance or special restorative training CONCURRENTLY with disability compensation, DIC, or pension** (barring duplicate same-parental-line multi-parent awards on/after June 9, 1960). Raters who terminate an adjudicated helpless child's DIC upon `Ch. 35` DEA payment commit clear legal error!<br/>• **CRITICAL SEQUENCING TRAP (`§ 3562` & `§ 21.3023(a), (c)`):** For a regular `18–23` schoolchild (`§ 21.3023(a)`), negotiating the first `Ch. 35` DEA check/EFT is an **irrevocable lifetime bar** to future schoolchild `18–23` dependency/DIC payments (`§ 21.3023(c)(1)`, save VA misinformation under `(c)(2)`). Therefore, if a disabled 18-year-old commences `Ch. 35` DEA **before** `§ 3.356` helpless-child status is established—and VA later denies `§ 3.356`—the child is **permanently locked out** of schoolchild DIC/dependency rates! Always file and document `§ 3.356` helpless-child status (`VA Form 21-534EZ` / `21-686c` + pre-18 IEP/medical records; see [Forms Lexicon](../../docs/forms-and-codesheet-lexicon.md)) **before** negotiating a `Ch. 35` DEA payment, and frame any post-18 `Ch. 35` enrollment as accommodated or **Subchapter V Special Restorative Training** so examiners do not mischaracterize school attendance as competitive employability. |
