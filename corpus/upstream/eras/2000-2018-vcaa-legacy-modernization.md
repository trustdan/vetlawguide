# Era Lens 4: VCAA & Legacy Modernization Era (2000–2018)

## 1. Regulatory & Statutory Inflection Points
* **Veterans Claims Assistance Act of 2000 (`Pub. L. 106-475`, `114 Stat. 2096`, Nov. 9, 2000):** Eliminated the "well-grounded claim" barrier and codified `38 U.S.C. § 5103` (VCAA notice) and `§ 5103A` (duty to assist, including low-threshold medical nexus exams under `38 C.F.R. § 3.159(c)(4)` and *McLendon v. Nicholson*, 20 Vet. App. 79 (2006)).
* **2006 Expansion of Paid Representation (`Pub. L. 109-461`, `120 Stat. 3403`, enacted Dec. 22, 2006):** For NODs filed on or after **June 20, 2007**, accredited attorneys and agents could charge fees right after the RO decision/NOD rather than waiting for a final BVA decision.
* **2006 `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)` Overhaul (`71 FR 52455`, published Sept. 6, 2006, eff. Oct. 6, 2006):** Clarified mandatory retroactive effective dates under `§ 3.156(c)(3)` back to the previously denied claim (see [`38-cfr-3.156c-service-records-evolution.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.156c-service-records-evolution.md)).
* **Major VASRD & Presumptive Amendments (Publication vs. Effective Dates):**
  * **August 30, 2002 & September 23, 2002:** Interim Scars (`38 C.F.R. § 4.118`, `67 FR 49590`, published July 31, 2002, **effective Aug. 30, 2002**, converting area thresholds to `sq. in. / sq. cm.`) and Interim Spine IVDS (`DC 5293`, `67 FR 54345`, published Aug. 22, 2002, **effective Sept. 23, 2002**, incapacitating-episode duration formula).
  * **September 26, 2003:** Spine Formula (`38 C.F.R. § 4.71a`, `68 FR 51454`, published Aug. 27, 2003, **effective Sept. 26, 2003**) — goniometer ROM degrees + separate radiculopathy ratings under Note (1) (see [`38-cfr-4.71a-spine-evolution.md`](../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.71a-spine-evolution.md)).
  * **October 23, 2008:** Scars & Disfigurement (`38 C.F.R. § 4.118`, `73 FR 54708`, published Sept. 23, 2008, **effective Oct. 23, 2008**; clarified Jan. 20, 2012 at `77 FR 2909`) — 8 disfigurement characteristics under `DC 7800` and count-based painful/unstable scar ratings under `DC 7804` (see [`38-cfr-4.118-scars-evolution.md`](../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.118-scars-evolution.md)).
  * **July 12, 2010:** PTSD Stressor Liberalization (`38 C.F.R. § 3.304(f)(3)`, `75 FR 39843`, **published July 13, 2010, effective July 12, 2010**) — VA/contract psychiatrist confirmation of fear of hostile military or terrorist activity eliminates JSRRC corroboration for non-combat veterans in theater.
  * **August 31, 2010:** *Nehmer* Herbicide Expansion (`75 FR 53202`, published & **effective Aug. 31, 2010**) — added **Ischemic Heart Disease (IHD)**, **Parkinson's disease**, and **hairy cell / B-cell leukemias** to `38 C.F.R. § 3.309(e)`.
  * **August 4, 2014:** DSM-5 Mental Health Transition (`79 FR 45093`, interim final rule published & **effective Aug. 4, 2014**; adopted final Mar. 19, 2015, `80 FR 14308`).
  * **August 13, 2018:** Skin & Scars Revision (`83 FR 32592`, published July 13, 2018, **effective Aug. 13, 2018**).

## 2. The March 24, 2015 Hard Wall (Death of Informal Claims)
Effective **March 24, 2015** (`79 FR 57660`, published Sept. 25, 2014 — see [`38-cfr-3.155-3.157b-informal-claims-pre-2015.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.155-3.157b-informal-claims-pre-2015.md)), VA abolished the decades-old informal claim regime:
* **Before March 24, 2015:** `21-4138` statements, letters from VSOs/congressional offices, and VAMC hospital/outpatient reports (`§ 3.157(b)` for already-SC conditions) triggered valid informal claim effective dates.
* **On or After March 24, 2015:**
  * Claims **must** be filed on prescribed standardized forms (`VA Form 21-526EZ` for initial/increase; `VA Form 21-0966` Intent to File preserving up to 1 year to submit the complete form).
  * `38 C.F.R. § 3.157(b)` was **completely repealed**. VAMC medical records dated March 24, 2015 or later **never** initiate an informal increased-rating claim on their own.
  * **Pre-2015 Informal-Claim Scope vs. Post-2015 Forms (*Shea v. Wilkie* vs. *Sellers v. Wilkie*):** Note that *Shea v. Wilkie*, 926 F.3d 1362, 1370 (Fed. Cir. 2019) is **not** an exception to the post-2015 Intent-to-File requirement—it construed **pre-March 24, 2015 `38 C.F.R. § 3.155(a)`** informal-claim scope (holding that where a pre-2015 filing refers to specific medical records containing a reasonably ascertainable diagnosis, it raises an informal claim for that condition). For **post-March 24, 2015** standardized forms (`21-526EZ`), *Sellers v. Wilkie*, 965 F.3d 1328 (Fed. Cir. 2020) governs: a claimant must minimally identify the sickness, disease, or injuries for which benefits are sought on the prescribed form rather than relying on unreferenced medical records.

## 3. Screening Opportunities & Bitemporal Traps
* **The `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(b)` 1-Year Post-Decision VAMC Goldmine (*Beraud* & *Lang*):** Even after March 24, 2015 killed informal claims, **every Legacy rating decision issued before February 19, 2019** remained subject to `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(b)`! Under *Beraud v. McDonald*, 766 F.3d 1402 (Fed. Cir. 2014) and *Lang v. Wilkie*, 971 F.3d 1348 (Fed. Cir. 2020), if a VAMC generated relevant clinical records within **1 year *after*** a Legacy RO decision and VA never issued a determination on whether those records were new and material, **that Legacy decision never became final**!
* **Bitemporal *George v. McDonough* Gate (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`; `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`):** Because `§ 3.156(b)` (*Lang*) or `§ 3.156(c)` keeps/reopens the historical claim on direct merits rather than CUE, intervening judicial reinterpretations (such as *Bradley v. Peake* (2008) on TDIU-anchored `SMC(s)` or *Barry v. McDonough* (2024) on multi-step `§ 3.350(f)` SMC) apply **retroactively without triggering the `§ 20.1403(e)` change-in-interpretation CUE bar**!
* **Audit the Feb–March 2015 Window:** Any VAMC exam, hospital admission, `21-4138`, or eBenefits incomplete saved application initiated *before* March 24, 2015 is governed by the old informal-claim rules.
* **Legacy Implicit Denial Still Applies:** For any rating decision issued **prior to February 19, 2019** (and non-RAMP), VA can still raise *Deshotel*/*Adams*/*Hampton*/*Steele* implicit denial if a related pending claim was omitted. Check *Hamill v. Collins*, `166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. 2026)` once you cross February 19, 2019!
