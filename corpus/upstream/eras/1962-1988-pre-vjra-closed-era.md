# Era Lens 2: Pre-VJRA Closed Era & Vietnam Adjudications (1962–1988)

## 1. Regulatory & Statutory Landscape
* **"Splendid Isolation" (`former 38 U.S.C. § 211(a)`):** Until November 18, 1988, VA benefit decisions were **completely insulated from federal judicial review** (save for constitutional challenges to statutes), and attorneys were capped at a **$10 fee** (`former 38 U.S.C. § 3404(c)`), leaving veterans represented almost exclusively by overburdened VSOs or pro se. Note also that throughout `1962–1988`, Title 38 carried pre-1991 `Pub. L. 85-857` section numbers (`§ 310` for wartime SC, `§ 314` for compensation/SMC rates, `§ 3010` for effective dates as reformed by `Pub. L. 87-825`, `76 Stat. 948`, enacted Oct. 15, 1962, **effective Dec. 1, 1962**, and `§ 4005` for NOD/SOC appeals).
* **Creation of the NOD -> SOC -> Form 9 Pipeline (`Pub. L. 87-666`, `76 Stat. 553`, enacted Sept. 19, 1962, eff. Jan. 1, 1963):** Established the mandatory **Statement of the Case (SOC)**. Once a claimant filed a written Notice of Disagreement (NOD) within 1 year of a rating notice, VA was **statutorily required** to issue an SOC before the claimant's 60-day clock (or remainder of the 1-year period) to file a Substantive Appeal (`VA Form 1-9` / `Form 9`) could even begin to run!
* **Broad Informal Claims & `38 C.F.R. § 3.157(b)`:** Throughout 1962–1988, `38 C.F.R. § 3.157(b)` (see [`38-cfr-3.155-3.157b-informal-claims-pre-2015.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.155-3.157b-informal-claims-pre-2015.md)) automatically converted **every VA hospital admission report or VA outpatient examination report** regarding an already service-connected condition into an **informal claim for an increased rating** on the date of admission/examination.
* **Recognition of PTSD (`DC 9411`, Effective April 11, 1980):** Following DSM-III (1980), VA added Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (`DC 9411`) to `38 C.F.R. § 4.132` effective **April 11, 1980** (see [`38-cfr-4.132-mental-disorders-pre-1996.md`](../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.132-mental-disorders-pre-1996.md)). Prior Vietnam psychiatric claims were often mischaracterized as non-compensable "personality disorders" or "situational maladjustment" despite documented combat trauma.

## 2. Signature C-File Goldmines of the 1962–1988 Era
1. **Unperfected NODs Missing an SOC:** Audit every post-1962 letter from the veteran expressing dissatisfaction with a rating or denial. If it met the broad pre-AMA definition of an NOD (`former 38 C.F.R. § 19.118` / `§ 20.201` — "disagreement and desire for appellate review") and **VA never issued an SOC**, the 1960s/1970s/1980s rating **never became final** (*Tablazon v. Brown*, 8 Vet. App. 359 (1995))!
2. **Dormant `§ 3.157(b)` VAMC Reports:** Whenever a Vietnam or Korean War veteran held *any* service-connected disability (including `0%`), check if a VAMC hospital summary (`VA Form 10-1000`) or outpatient report (`10-2545` / progress notes) **physically entered the C-file** (or was received by VA) showing worsening without a follow-on rating action.
3. **Missing Inpatient Clinical Records vs. Outpatient Health Record (`§ 3.156(c)`):** During the Vietnam War, field hospital and evac hospital **inpatient clinical packets** (e.g., 24th Evac Hospital, USS *Sanctuary*, USS *Repose*, Yokosuka/Clark AFB surgical records) were stored separately at NPRC from the thin outpatient Dental/Health jacket (`DD Form 722`). Thousands of 1970s Vietnam claims were denied or rated `0%` because VA only requested the outpatient jacket! Associating those inpatient clinical files today triggers **`38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)`** (see [`38-cfr-3.156c-service-records-evolution.md`](../authorities/part-3/38-cfr-3.156c-service-records-evolution.md)) back to the 1970s claim!
4. ***Nehmer* Class Claims (Sept. 25, 1985 Forward):** Any claim received or pending on or after **September 25, 1985** from a qualifying herbicide-exposed veteran for a condition later added to `38 C.F.R. § 3.309(e)` (including ischemic heart disease, Parkinson's, B-cell leukemias, prostate/respiratory cancers, peripheral neuropathy, bladder cancer, hypothyroidism, parkinsonism—noting `M21-1 VIII.i.2.B.1.c` excludes chloracne, hypertension, and MGUS from `§ 3.816` class retroactivity) must be audited under **`38 C.F.R. § 3.816`**.
5. **Unlawful Rating Reductions (`38 C.F.R. § 3.344`):** In the 1970s and 1980s, ROs routinely cut `50%` or `70%` stabilized (5+ years) psychiatric or orthopedic ratings based on a single cursory C&P exam without reviewing the entire medical history or finding sustained improvement under the ordinary conditions of life and work. Under *Brown v. Brown*, 5 Vet. App. 413 (1993), such reductions are **void *ab initio***—restoring the prior rating back to the date of reduction without needing to prove CUE!

## 3. Critical Bitemporal & Evidentiary Traps
* **No *Bell* Constructive Possession Before July 21, 1992:** For a **CUE** motion attacking a 1962–1988 decision, you **cannot** rely on VAMC records that stayed strictly inside the VAMC medical center and were never placed in the VARO claims folder (`VAOPGCPREC 12-95`, `60 FR 43186`, Aug. 18, 1995). Look instead to whether those VAMC records triggered an unadjudicated **`§ 3.157(b)` informal claim**!
* ***George v. McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) & `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)` Gate:** Later judicial invalidation of a regulation cannot establish CUE against a final 1962–1988 decision (which is why *Nehmer* class stipulations under `38 C.F.R. § 3.816` were uniquely necessary to override final `§ 3.311a` denials!). Whenever a 1962–1988 decision is kept **open** via an unperfected NOD (*Tablazon*), pending `§ 3.157(b)` claim, `§ 3.156(c)` service record reconsideration, or *Brown* void *ab initio* reduction, `§ 20.1403(e)` does **not** apply.
