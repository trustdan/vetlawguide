# Era Lens 3: The VJRA Revolution (1988–2000)

## 1. Regulatory & Statutory Landscape
* **Creation of Judicial Review (`Pub. L. 100-687`, `102 Stat. 4105`, Nov. 18, 1988):** Established the U.S. Court of Veterans Appeals (renamed CAVC on Mar. 1, 1999 by `Pub. L. 105-368`), mandated BVA "reasons or bases" (`former § 4004(d)(1)` -> `§ 7104(d)(1)`), and authorized paid attorney representation following a first BVA final decision (if NOD was filed on or after Nov. 18, 1988); written RO decision notices (`former § 3004` -> `38 U.S.C. § 5104`) followed via `Pub. L. 101-237, § 115` (effective **Feb. 1, 1990**).
* **Two-Step 1991 Title 38 Renumbering (`Pub. L. 102-40` & `Pub. L. 102-83`):** Early COVA opinions (`1–2 Vet. App.`) cite pre-1991 section numbers that Congress renumbered in two stages during 1991:
  * **May 7, 1991 (`Pub. L. 102-40, § 402`, `105 Stat. 187`):** Renumbered administrative, claims, and appellate chapters (`§ 3007 -> § 5107`, `§ 3010 -> § 5110`, `§ 3404 -> § 5904`, `§ 4004 -> § 7104`, `§ 4051 -> § 7251`).
  * **August 6, 1991 (`Pub. L. 102-83, §§ 4–5`, `105 Stat. 378`):** Codified Department organization (`former § 211(a)` superseded by `§ 511(a)`) and renumbered substantive benefit chapters (`§ 310 -> § 1110`, `§ 314 -> § 1114`, `§ 410 -> § 1310`, `§ 521 -> § 1521`).
* **The "Well-Grounded Claim" Trapdoor (Nov. 18, 1988 – Nov. 8, 2000):** Under former `38 U.S.C. § 5107(a)` (`§ 3007(a)`), a claimant bore the threshold burden of submitting a "well-grounded" (plausible) claim—including **competent medical nexus evidence** (*Grottveit v. Brown*, 5 Vet. App. 91 (1993); *Caluza v. Brown*, 7 Vet. App. 498 (1995), *aff'd*, 78 F.3d 604 (Fed. Cir. 1996))—**before VA's duty to assist (including providing a C&P exam) ever attached**!
* **July 21, 1992 *Bell* Cutoff:** Decisions issued **on or after July 21, 1992** are charged with **constructive possession** of VA medical records generated before the decision (*Bell v. Derwinski*, 2 Vet. App. 611 (1992); `VAOPGCPREC 12-95`, `60 FR 43186`, Aug. 18, 1995).
* **1989–1997 Major Regulatory & VASRD Overhauls (Always Separate Publication vs. Effective Dates!):**
  * **March 1, 1989 & September 4, 1990 (`38 C.F.R. § 4.16` TDIU):** `54 FR 4281` (published Jan. 30, 1989, **effective March 1, 1989**, adding former `§ 4.16(c)`) and `55 FR 31579` (published Aug. 3, 1990, **effective September 4, 1990**, codifying the Census poverty-threshold marginal employment rule in `§ 4.16(a)`).
  * **December 31, 1990 (`38 C.F.R. § 3.655` Missed C&P Exams):** Published Nov. 29, 1990 (`55 FR 49521`), **effective December 31, 1990**, distinguishing original claims from increased-rating/reopened claims.
  * **November 7, 1996 (`38 C.F.R. § 4.130` Mental Disorders):** Published Oct. 8, 1996 (`61 FR 52695`), **effective November 7, 1996** (adopting DSM-IV, eliminating former `§ 4.132` and former `§ 4.16(c)` — see [`38-cfr-4.132-mental-disorders-pre-1996.md`](../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.132-mental-disorders-pre-1996.md)).
  * **July 3, 1997 (`38 C.F.R. §§ 4.55, 4.56, 4.73` Muscle Injuries):** Published June 3, 1997 (`62 FR 30235`), **effective July 3, 1997** (modernizing `DC 5301–5323` — see [`38-cfr-4.56-muscle-injuries-1946-to-present.md`](../authorities/part-4/38-cfr-4.56-muscle-injuries-1946-to-present.md)).
* **1997 CUE Act (`Pub. L. 105-111`, `111 Stat. 2271`, Nov. 21, 1997):** Created statutory BVA CUE (`38 U.S.C. § 7111`; implemented at `38 C.F.R. §§ 20.1400–20.1411`, `64 FR 2134`, published Jan. 13, 1999, **effective Feb. 12, 1999**) and codified RO CUE (`§ 5109A`).

## 2. High-Yield Opportunities in 1988–2000 Decisions
1. **Post-July 21, 1992 *Bell* Constructive Possession CUEs:** If an RO rated a condition at `10%` in 1994 while VAMC orthopedic or surgical notes from 1993 (not yet printed into the paper C-file) documented ankylosis, statutory housebound criteria, or loss of use of a foot (`SMC(k)`), those VAMC records **were constructively before the rater**!
2. **Anti-Pyramiding Errors After *Esteban* (1994) & *DeLuca* (1995):** Raters continued lumping painful scars, muscle group damage, and peripheral nerve injuries into one low code despite *Esteban v. Brown*, 6 Vet. App. 259 (1994), or failing to account for functional loss due to pain/flare-ups on joint exams under *DeLuca v. Brown*, 8 Vet. App. 202 (1995) (note: *DeLuca* inadequacy of exam is a duty-to-assist error, *not* CUE, unless the C&P report *itself* documented the reduced painful ROM in degrees that warranted a higher schedular rating!).
3. **Bypassing 1990s "Not Well Grounded" Denials via `§ 3.156(c)`:** While you rarely win CUE against a 1995 "not well grounded" denial, **many 1990s claims were deemed "not well grounded" only because in-service STRs or combat unit logs were missing from the C-file**! If STRs, deck logs, or JSRRC records were later associated, **`38 C.F.R. § 3.156(c)`** completely sweeps aside the 1995 "not well grounded" denial and restores the 1995 effective date!

## 3. Critical Era & Bitemporal Traps
* **Never Attack Lack of a C&P Exam in 1990–2000 as CUE:** Double kill-switch—both because pre-VCAA law denied exams to non-well-grounded claims (*Grottveit*) and because duty-to-assist errors can never constitute CUE (*Cook v. Principi*, 318 F.3d 1334 (Fed. Cir. 2002) (en banc)).
* ***Karnas v. Derwinski* Transitional Rule (1996/1997 VASRD Changes):** If a claim was pending across the **Nov. 7, 1996** mental health change or **July 3, 1997** muscle injury change, VA was required under *Karnas v. Derwinski*, 1 Vet. App. 308 (1991) to apply whichever version of the regulation was **more favorable** to the veteran (for the period after the amendment's effective date, while applying only the old rule prior to the amendment's effective date).
* ***George v. McDonough* (`596 U.S. 740 (2022)`) & `38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)` Gate:** Under `§ 20.1403(e)`, a post-decision judicial reinterpretation (e.g., *Bradley v. Peake* in 2008 or *Barry v. McDonough* in 2024) applies to **open/pending** 1990s claims (including Legacy claims kept non-final under *Lang* `§ 3.156(b)` or *Tablazon* missing SOCs), whereas attacking a **final** 1990s decision via CUE must rest on regulatory/statutory commands that were settled at the time of that decision.
