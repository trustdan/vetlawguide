# Era Lens 5: AMA, PACT Act & Modern Precedent Era (2019–2026)

## 1. Appeals Modernization Act (AMA) Architecture (Effective Feb. 19, 2019)
* **Three Review Lanes (`Pub. L. 115-55`, `131 Stat. 1105`; `38 U.S.C. § 5104C`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.2500`, `84 FR 138`, published Jan. 18, 2019, effective Feb. 19, 2019):**
  1. **Supplemental Claim (`VA Form 20-0995`):** Requires "new and relevant" evidence (`38 U.S.C. § 5108`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(d)`, a lower threshold than Legacy "new and material"); triggers full duty to assist.
  2. **Higher-Level Review (`VA Form 20-0996`):** Closed-record de novo review by senior adjudicator; no new evidence permitted, though HLR "duty to assist error" return converts the claim back into a Supplemental development lane!
  3. **Board Appeal (`VA Form 10182`):** Direct Review (closed record), Evidence Submission (90-day window), or Hearing (testimony + 90-day post-hearing evidence window).
* **Continuous Pursuit (`38 C.F.R. § 3.2500(c) & (h)`):** Protects the original effective date continuously so long as a valid lane election is filed within **one year** of each AOJ decision or **within one year of a Board denial** (`Supplemental Claim`).
  * **CRITICAL TRAP:** Continuous pursuit **stops at the CAVC/Fed. Cir.** Filing a `20-0995` Supplemental Claim within 1 year of a **CAVC affirmance** does **not** preserve the earlier effective date (*see* `38 U.S.C. § 5110(a)(2)(D)-(E)`). Always evaluate whether to file a Supplemental Claim within 1 year of the *Board* decision or seek CAVC vacatur/remand!

## 2. Landmark Judicial Shifts (2020–2026) & Bitemporal Scope
* ***Lang v. Wilkie*, 971 F.3d 1348 (Fed. Cir. 2020):** VAMC clinical records generated within 1 year *after* a Legacy RO rating decision are constructively received under `38 C.F.R. § 3.156(b)`, preventing finality from attaching unless VA adjudicated whether they were new and material.
* ***George v. McDonough*, 596 U.S. 740 (2022):** Later judicial invalidation or reinterpretation of a VA regulation (`38 C.F.R. § 20.1403(e)`) does **not** make prior reliance on that regulation "clear and unmistakable error" (establishing the core bitemporal split between `open-claim-only-george-restricted` doctrines and `cue-and-open-claim` doctrines).
* ***Arellano v. McDonough*, 598 U.S. 1 (2023):** `38 U.S.C. § 5110(b)(1)` (the 1-year post-discharge filing window for discharge-day effective dates) is **not subject to equitable tolling**.
* ***Barry v. McDonough*, 101 F.4th 1348 (Fed. Cir. May 16, 2024):** Overturned VA's single-step ceiling on `38 C.F.R. § 3.350(f)(3)–(4)`, holding veterans at base `SMC(l)–(n)` can receive **multiple** half-step and full-step intermediate SMC increases (up to `SMC(o)`) for each independent `50%` or `100%` disability *(open-claim / continuous-pursuit / `§ 3.156(b)–(c)` safe; CUE on final pre-May 2024 ratings faces `§ 20.1403(e)` / George defenses)*.
* ***Bufkin v. Collins*, 604 U.S. 369 (2025):** CAVC reviews the Board's approximate-balance determination under the `38 U.S.C. § 5107(b)` benefit-of-the-doubt standard for clear error.
* ***Ingram v. Collins*, 38 Vet. App. 130 (2025) & Rescinded `38 C.F.R. § 4.10` Medication IFR (`91 FR 7118`, rescinded `91 FR 9712`, Feb. 17–27, 2026):** Reaffirmed that VA **cannot discount disability ratings based on the ameliorative effects of medication** unless the Diagnostic Code explicitly accounts for medication. VA issued an Interim Final Rule on Feb. 17, 2026 (`91 FR 7118`) attempting to reverse *Ingram*, **rescinded it on Feb. 27, 2026 (`91 FR 9712`)**, and abandoned its Federal Circuit appeal (`No. 25-1972`) on Mar. 30, 2026—flag any rating rendered **Feb. 17–27, 2026** (or earlier/later) that discounted symptoms due to medication!
* ***Soto v. United States*, 605 U.S. 360 (June 12, 2025):** Unanimously abolished the 6-year Barring Act (`31 U.S.C. § 3702`) cap on retroactive **Title 10 DoD Combat-Related Special Compensation (CRSC)** (`10 U.S.C. § 1413a`), followed by **Aug. 20, 2025 DoD guidance** and parallel Court of Federal Claims Tucker Act claims (e.g., *Christopher Carey v. United States* for >\$10,000 arrears).
* ***Hamill v. Collins*, 166 F.4th 1030 (Fed. Cir. Feb. 4, 2026) (`No. 24-1543`) & *Cash v. Collins*, No. 24-1811 (Fed. Cir. Feb. 5, 2026):** *Hamill* abolished the **implicit denial doctrine** for all AMA-era decisions (`>= Feb. 19, 2019`) under `38 U.S.C. § 5104(b)` (noting in fn. 6 that within 1 year of a silent AMA decision, HLR or Board appeal is the proper remedy over mandamus). *Cash* held under `38 U.S.C. § 7113(c)` that a Board NOD (`VA Form 10182`) identifying probative "in-between" AMA evidence already in VA's possession compels Board consideration without physical resubmission.
* ***Freund v. Collins*, No. 21-4168 (Vet. App. class certified Mar. 2026):** Landmark CAVC class action covering ~28,000+ legacy appeals improperly closed by VACOLS automated sweeps (Dec. 12, 1990 – Feb. 6, 2025) despite timely appellate posture.

## 3. PACT Act (`Pub. L. 117-168`, `136 Stat. 1759`, Aug. 10, 2022) & Effective Dates
* **Expanded Herbicide Locations & Conditions (`38 U.S.C. §§ 1116, 1116A`):** Added Thailand (any U.S./Royal Thai base), Laos, Cambodia (Mimot/Krek), Guam, American Samoa, Johnston Atoll, plus **hypertension** and **MGUS**.
* **Gulf War / Post-9/11 Burn Pit Presumptions (`38 U.S.C. § 1120`) & TERA (`§ 1168`):** Added 23+ respiratory and cancer presumptions and mandated Toxic Exposure Risk Activity (TERA) medical nexus exams.
* **Retroactive Effective Date Nuances & OIG Error Pattern:**
  * Under `38 C.F.R. § 3.114` (liberalizing law) and PACT Act transition rules, claims filed (or covered by an Intent to File) by **August 14, 2023** could receive an effective date back to **August 10, 2022** (assuming disability existed then). **VA OIG documented \$20.4 million in underpayments** caused by raters assigning claim-receipt dates rather than `Aug. 10, 2022` or `§ 3.114(a)` 1-year lookbacks!
  * **Crucial *Nehmer* Exclusions vs. Inclusions:** `M21-1 VIII.i.2.B.1.c` explicitly **excludes hypertension, MGUS, and chloracne** from `38 C.F.R. § 3.816` *Nehmer* class retroactivity. Conversely, check whether **RVN / Blue Water Navy (`Procopio` / `Pub. L. 116-23`)** claims for classic *Nehmer* `§ 3.309(e)` conditions qualify for full `§ 3.816` retroactivity back to post-1985 denied claims, OR whether `§ 3.156(c)` newly associated unit/deck records carry even a PACT-granted claim back to an original denial (*Emerson*)!

## 4. Regulatory & VASRD Status Check (Enacted vs. Unfinalized Proposals Through Late 2026)
* **ENACTED — Musculoskeletal (`85 FR 76453`, published Nov. 30, 2020, eff. Feb. 7, 2021):** Objective assistive-device rules for knee instability (`DC 5257`), updated prosthetic minimums, added plantar fasciitis (`DC 5285`), and added Compartment Syndrome (`DC 5331`, each affected muscle group rated separately).
* **ENACTED — Bilateral Factor Exclusion (`88 FR 22914`, published Apr. 14, 2023, eff. April 16, 2023):** Added `38 C.F.R. § 4.26(d)` excluding bilateral disabilities from the `10%` bilateral bonus whenever including them causes combined math to round down from `100%` to `90%`.
* **ENACTED — Digestive System (`89 FR 19735`, published Mar. 20, 2024, eff. May 19, 2024):** Created `DC 7206` for GERD (esophageal stricture/dysphagia model) and updated `DC 7319` IBS (`10%/20%/30%` Rome IV frequency model). Pre-May 19, 2024 ratings are protected against schedule-based reductions (`38 U.S.C. § 1155`; `38 C.F.R. § 3.951(a)`).
* **ENACTED — Character of Discharge (`38 C.F.R. § 3.12`, `89 FR 32361`, published Apr. 26, 2024, eff. June 25, 2024):** Overhauled `§ 3.12(d)` regulatory bars and created the **"compelling circumstances"** exception for prolonged AWOL and moral turpitude discharges.
* **NOT FINALIZED (Still Proposed as of Late 2026):**
  * **Sleep Apnea (`DC 6847`) & Tinnitus (`DC 6260`) (`RIN 2900-AQ72`, `87 FR 8474`, Feb. 15, 2022):** Proposed rule eliminating standalone `10%` tinnitus and `50%` CPAP sleep apnea ratings has **NOT** been published as a Final Rule. Current criteria (`10%` standalone `DC 6260`; `50%` CPAP `DC 6847`) **remain active law**.
  * **Mental Disorders (`38 C.F.R. § 4.130`, `RIN 2900-AQ82`, `87 FR 8498`, Feb. 15, 2022):** Proposed domain-based WHODAS overhaul (`10%` minimum floor) has **NOT** been published as a Final Rule. Existing `§ 4.130` (`0/10/30/50/70/100%`) remains active law.
  * **Painful Scars (`DC 7804`, `90 FR 46542`, Sept. 29, 2025):** Proposed note requiring objective evidence of pain upon examination/demonstration has **NOT** been published as a Final Rule. Existing `DC 7804` painful scar criteria remain active law.
